## Objective

Find here the answers to the most frequent questions about Managed Backup for VMware, and the steps to follow for common problems.

## Order and delivery

### My infrastructure does not appear in the order

Only eligible infrastructures are offered. An infrastructure is missing in three cases. It is already protected by Managed Backup for VMware, including during the 35 days that follow a termination. It is qualified SecNumCloud. It is located in a region that is not covered. Support can tell you the exact reason for a given infrastructure.

### My service has been "Deploying" for a long time

The service is usually available within 2 hours (indicative time). After that, contact support and give the name of your infrastructure: nothing is billed until the service is delivered.

If the delivery fails, the service shows the **Error** badge on your dashboard. Contact support and give the name of your infrastructure. Nothing is billed.

### Does my infrastructure have enough resources?

The backup server and its proxy use at least 10 vCPU, 19 GB of memory and 530 GB of disk in the datacenter chosen at order time. Check this capacity before you order (see [Getting started with Managed Backup for VMware](/guides/storage-and-backup/managed-backup-for-vmware/getting-started)).

### I ordered without choosing any machine, is that a problem?

No. The service is delivered with its three empty backup jobs. You only need to tag your machines in vSphere. Nothing is billed until a machine is backed up.

If no machine is protected, you receive a reminder three days after delivery, then a second one ten days after, which announces the termination date. Thirty days after delivery, if no machine is protected, the service is terminated automatically: access to the VBR is closed and nothing is billed. There is no 35-day window and no reactivation in this case: to protect your infrastructure, you need to order again (see [Terminating and reactivating a service](/guides/storage-and-backup/managed-backup-for-vmware/terminate#automatic-termination-of-a-service-that-was-never-used)).

### A machine chosen at order time is not protected

If a machine was deleted or could not be tagged during delivery, it appears as unprotected in the VM list of your OVHcloud Control Panel. You only need to set the tag on it in vSphere (see [Protecting your virtual machines](/guides/storage-and-backup/managed-backup-for-vmware/tag-vm)).

## VBR access

### The link to my initial password no longer works

This link is single-use and has a limited validity. Request a reset from your dashboard: <code className="action">Reset the VBR password</code> (see [Resetting the password of your VBR](/guides/storage-and-backup/managed-backup-for-vmware/monitor-protection#reset-the-password-of-your-vbr)).

### I lost my password

OVHcloud does not keep it and cannot send it to you. Use the reset: your VBR sends an e-mail to the address of your OVHcloud account, with a temporary password to change at login.

### I cannot find how to create a backup job

This is normal: jobs are created and managed by OVHcloud. To choose what is backed up, set a tag on the machine in vSphere.

### Can I have an administrator account on my VBR?

No. The service is fully managed by OVHcloud, and the account you receive at delivery is the only account provided.

## Machine protection

### A tagged machine appears as unprotected

1. Check that the tag belongs to the `OvhManagedBackup` category.
2. Check its spelling: `standard`, `advanced` or `premium`, in lowercase.
3. Wait one hour: the OVHcloud Control Panel is updated every hour.

An unknown or misspelled tag is treated as no tag.

### A protected machine has no last backup date

It has not been backed up yet: it will be at the next run of the jobs, which start every evening at 10 pm. To back it up right away, start an on-demand backup from your VBR.

### The last backup date of a machine is old

Its latest backups have probably failed. You can see the details of a failure in the console of your VBR. The last backup date in your OVHcloud Control Panel is the signal to watch. Your VBR also sends you its backup notifications by e-mail, to the address of the OVHcloud account that ordered the service. A machine with no successful backup over a period is not billed for that period. If the problem persists, contact support.

### I restored a machine, what should I check?

After a restore, check the tag of the restored machine in vSphere and set it again if needed.

### My machine is 8 TB or more

Machines of 8 TB or more are not supported: Managed Backup for VMware does not back them up. A machine of this size can delay the backups of your other machines. They remain visible in the order list, but cannot receive a Backup plan.

Do not put a tag on them in vSphere. If one of them has a tag, remove it: OVHcloud does not remove it automatically, and a weekly e-mail reminds you.

### Can I change the secondary site?

No, the secondary site is final once the order is placed. If you have a specific need, contact support.

### Can I change the backup time?

No, the jobs start every evening at 10 pm and this schedule is set by OVHcloud. You can, however, start an on-demand backup at any time.

### Can I restore to another infrastructure?

No, a machine is always restored to its original infrastructure.

## Billing

### A machine is billed although I removed its tag

The billing period keeps the highest plan and tier reached. A machine whose tag is removed during a period is still billed for that period, then not at all afterwards.

### Why is my machine not billed on the size of its disks?

Because the price follows the size of the machine's backup, never the space allocated to its disks.
