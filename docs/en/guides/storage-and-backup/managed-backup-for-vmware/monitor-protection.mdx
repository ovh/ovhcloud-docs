## Objective

Your OVHcloud Control Panel brings together, on a single dashboard, all the infrastructures protected by Managed Backup for VMware and all their virtual machines.

**This guide explains how to read your dashboard, check that a machine is backed up, access your VBR and reset its password.**

## Requirements

- Have at least one Managed Backup for VMware service.
- Be logged in to your <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Control Panel</ManagerLink>.

## Instructions

### Access the dashboard

In the <code className="action">Hosted Private Cloud</code> universe, open <code className="action">Backup</code> > <code className="action">Managed Backup for VMware</code>. The dashboard opens directly on your whole subscription. It has two tabs: <code className="action">Dashboard</code> and <code className="action">Billing</code>.

*The **General information** card shows the number of protected and unprotected machines, the secondary site and the **Reset the VBR password** link.*

*The **VM list** shows one row per machine, with its Backup plan, its restore points and its status.*

### The General information card

- **Protected virtual machines**: the number of protected virtual machines.
- **Unprotected virtual machines**: the number of virtual machines that are not protected.
- **Secondary site**: the secondary site of the infrastructure, in read-only mode.
- <code className="action">Reset the VBR password</code>: the link that starts the reset of the VBR password (see below).

### The Subscription card

- **Infrastructures**: your protected infrastructures, each with its status (for example **Active**).
- **Created on**: the creation date of the service.
- <code className="action">Open the VBR console</code>: the link that gives access to the console of your VBR (see below).
- <code className="action">Terminate a backup service</code>: the action that stops the protection of an infrastructure (see [Terminating and reactivating a service](/guides/storage-and-backup/managed-backup-for-vmware/terminate)).

### The virtual machine list

Below the cards, the **VM list** shows all the machines of all your infrastructures. The **All infrastructures** selector limits the display to one infrastructure, and the **Search** field looks up a machine by name. The list has no other filter.

| Column | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Name** | The exact name of the machine in vSphere, with its identifier underneath. |
| **Infrastructure** | The infrastructure where the machine is located. |
| **Backup plan** | The plan that matches its tag: Standard, Advanced or Premium. A dash if the machine has no tag. |
| **Restore points** | The number of restore points available for this machine. |
| **Last restore point** | The date of its last backup. A dash if the machine has never been backed up. |
| **Status** | **Protected** or **Not protected**. A machine without a valid tag is not protected. |

:::info
**Spot a machine that is no longer backed up**

The OVHcloud Control Panel does not show an error status for each machine. Sort the list on the **Last restore point** column: the oldest dates and the machines that have never been backed up come first. A date older than the others, or a dash, flags a machine to check. You can find the details of a failure in the console of your VBR. Your VBR also sends you its backup notifications by e-mail, to the address of the OVHcloud account that ordered the service.
:::

The information is updated every hour. A tag set in vSphere therefore appears in the list within the hour.

Machines of 8 TB or more are not supported: Managed Backup for VMware does not back them up. A machine of this size can delay the backups of your other machines. Do not tag it, and remove its tag if it has one (see [Machines of 8 TB or more](/guides/storage-and-backup/managed-backup-for-vmware/tag-vm#virtual-machines-of-8-tb-or-more)).

### The details of a machine

Click the name of a machine to open its details in a side panel. It shows:

- **The badge at the top of the panel:** **Backup plan** followed by the plan (for example **Backup plan Standard**) for a tagged machine, or **No tag** for a machine without a tag.
- **What to do:** for a tagged machine, backups run automatically. For a machine without a tag, nothing is backed up until you set one of the three tags on it in vSphere. The tag is taken into account at the next check.
- **Restore points**: the number of restore points, or **No restore point**.
- **Backup plan**: the plan of the machine, or **None**.
- **Size** and **Size band**: the size of its backup and its size band, on which its price is calculated. The space allocated to its disks is never taken into account. For a machine that has never been backed up, the screen shows **Not measured yet** and **Unknown**.
- **Last restore point**: the date of the last backup, or **Never backed up**.
- **Reference**: the reference of the machine, to give to support if needed.

### Open the console of your VBR

1. Click <code className="action">Open the VBR console</code>. The **VBR console** window opens.
2. In the **Infrastructure** field, choose the infrastructure. Each infrastructure has its own VBR.
3. Copy the identifier shown next to **Login** with the copy button, then click <code className="action">Open the VBR web interface</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="VBR console window with the infrastructure selector, the Login with a button to copy it and the Open the VBR web interface link" src="/images/storage-and-backup/managed-backup-for-vmware/monitor-protection/03-vbr-console.png" loading="lazy" />

*The window shows the identifier of the selected VBR, with a button to copy it, and the link to its web interface.*

The action is not available while the service is being deployed.

### Reset the password of your VBR

OVHcloud does not store the password you choose. If you lose it, request a reset:

1. In the **General information** card, click <code className="action">Reset the VBR password</code>.
2. In the **Reset the VBR password** window, choose in **Infrastructure** the one whose password you lost. The matching **Login** is displayed.
3. Click <code className="action">Send the e-mail</code>. The <code className="action">Cancel</code> button closes the window without sending anything.
4. Open the reset e-mail, sent by your VBR to the address of the OVHcloud account that ordered the service, log in with the temporary password, then choose a new password.

- The reset only applies to the selected VBR, and does not change the rights of the account.
- The VBR password reset remains available during the 35 days that follow a termination, so that you can still restore.
- The reset is not available during deployment, or if another reset is already in progress on this VBR.

### Protect another infrastructure

In the **VM list**, click <code className="action">Add an infrastructure</code> to start a new order. It runs from start to finish, like the first one (see [Getting started with Managed Backup for VMware](/guides/storage-and-backup/managed-backup-for-vmware/getting-started)): an infrastructure that is already protected is not offered. The new service appears on the same dashboard.

### What is managed elsewhere

- **Backup jobs:** they are created and scheduled by OVHcloud.
- **Tags:** the choice of the Backup plan is made in vSphere.
- **Storage:** the price of each machine includes it, so there is nothing to size.
- **The secondary site:** it is chosen at order time and does not change afterwards.
