## Objective

All the reliability of Veeam to protect your workloads, without the complexity. Managed Backup for VMware backs up the virtual machines of your Managed VMware vSphere infrastructure.

**Your VBR, our operations:** manage your backups from a dedicated Veeam Backup & Replication (VBR) server, while OVHcloud handles the deployment, sizing and maintenance of the whole backup stack. You stay in control of your backups, restores and protection monitoring. You never operate a server, manage a licence or set up your backup policies by hand.

You choose the protection level of each virtual machine, from Standard for the essentials up to Premium with its long-term retention. You can change it at any time, machine by machine, by changing its tag in vSphere. The service is billed per billing period, with no minimum commitment.

**This guide explains how to order the service, connect to your VBR and start protecting your virtual machines.**

## Requirements

- Have an active [Managed VMware vSphere](/links/hosted-private-cloud/vmware) infrastructure.
- Have access to the vSphere interface of your infrastructure, to add the backup tags.
- Have, in one of the datacenters of your infrastructure, the resources needed by the backup server (see below).

### Eligible infrastructures

You can order the service for an infrastructure that meets all of these conditions:

- The infrastructure is located in a region covered by the backup platform (Europe and Canada).
- The infrastructure is not SecNumCloud qualified.
- The infrastructure is not already protected by a Managed Backup for VMware service.

Only eligible infrastructures are offered when you order. If an infrastructure does not appear, contact support to find out why.

### Resources used in your infrastructure

The backup server is deployed **in your infrastructure**, in the datacenter you choose when you order. It therefore uses part of its compute and storage resources, like your own virtual machines. OVHcloud installs, monitors and maintains it. You have nothing to administer.

| Component | vCPU | Memory | Disk |
|-----------|------|--------|------|
| Backup server (VBR) | 8 | 16 GB | 500 GB |
| Backup proxy (VBR proxy) | 2 | 3 GB | 30 GB |
| **Minimum required in the datacenter** | **10** | **19 GB** | **530 GB** |

:::info
These values correspond to the backup server and one proxy. If your fleet needs more proxies, contact support and OVHcloud deploys more. Each additional proxy uses 2 vCPU, 3 GB of memory and 30 GB of disk.

:::

## Instructions

### Step 1: understanding who does what

| Where | What you do there |
|-------|-------------------|
| **OVHcloud Control Panel** | Order the service and monitor your protected machines. Open the VBR console (<code className="action">Open the VBR console</code>) and reset its password (<code className="action">Reset the VBR password</code>). Check your billing (<code className="action">Billing</code> tab). Terminate (<code className="action">Terminate a backup service</code>) or reactivate a service (<code className="action">Reactivate the service</code>). |
| **vSphere** | Choose the protection level of each virtual machine by adding a tag: `standard`, `advanced` or `premium`. |
| **Web console of your VBR** | Start an on-demand backup, restore a full machine or files, check the status of your backups. |
| **OVHcloud** | Deploy and maintain the VBR and its proxies, create and schedule the backup jobs, store, encrypt and replicate your backups. |

Each protected infrastructure is a **service**, with its own VBR. All your services together form your **subscription**, which you follow on a single dashboard.

### Step 2: choosing your Backup plans

Each virtual machine is protected by one of the three Backup plans. You can mix them within the same infrastructure.

| Feature | Standard | Advanced | Premium |
|---------|----------|----------|---------|
| Encryption | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Retention | 14 days | 35 days | 35 days |
| Copy in the secondary site | No | Yes | Yes |
| Immutability | No | 14 days | 14 days |
| On-demand backup and file-level restore (FLR) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Instant Recovery | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| GFS long-term retention: 12 months of coverage (11 monthly points) | No | No | Yes |

To learn more about these guarantees, read the [Security and guarantees of your backups](/guides/storage-and-backup/managed-backup-for-vmware/security) guide.

### Step 3: ordering the service

Click <code className="action">Order</code> on the product home page if you do not have a service yet, or <code className="action">Add an infrastructure</code> on the dashboard otherwise. You can also open the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/private-cloud/managed-backup-for-vmware/order">order page</ManagerLink> directly. The order has two steps, with the <code className="action">Next</code> and <code className="action">Previous</code> buttons.

#### 1. Infrastructure, sites and virtual machines

1. **Choose the infrastructure to protect.** In the **Infrastructure** card, open the **Select an Infrastructure** field and choose the infrastructure. The service takes its name, so you have nothing to enter.
2. **Choose the datacenter of the backup server** (**Backup server datacenter**). It hosts the backup server and its proxy, and the capacity needed is shown below the field. You cannot change this choice after you order.
3. **Check the primary site** (**Primary Site**). It matches the location of your infrastructure and cannot be changed.
4. **Choose the secondary site** (**Secondary Site**). This is the region where your Advanced and Premium backups are copied, within your geographic zone: see [the sites offered depending on the location of your infrastructure](/guides/storage-and-backup/managed-backup-for-vmware/security#copy-to-a-secondary-site). The region recommended by OVHcloud (**Recommended**) is preselected. You cannot change this choice after you order.
5. **Give a Backup plan to your virtual machines (optional).** You can give a plan to each machine or the same plan to all of them, and compare the plans with the <code className="action">Compare Backup plans</code> link. You can also click <code className="action">skip this step</code> and tag your machines later in vSphere.

:::warning
**Virtual machines of 8 TB or more**

Virtual machines of 8 TB or more are **not protected** by Managed Backup for VMware. See also [Protecting your virtual machines](/guides/storage-and-backup/managed-backup-for-vmware/tag-vm#virtual-machines-of-8-tb-or-more).

:::

#### 2. Summary, estimate and terms

1. **Check the summary** (**Order summary**). It lists the infrastructure, the primary and secondary sites, the datacenter of the backup server and the number of virtual machines that have a Backup plan.
2. **Read the amount estimate** (**Estimated monthly amount, at most**). It is shown plan by plan, then as a total. It is a **maximum amount**, not your invoice: the invoice then follows the machines you tag or untag (see [Understanding your billing](/guides/storage-and-backup/managed-backup-for-vmware/billing)).
3. **Accept the terms** (**Terms and conditions**). Tick the box that confirms you have read the listed contracts. The confirm button stays disabled until the box is ticked, including when you order for an additional infrastructure.
4. **Confirm the order** with the <code className="action">Confirm the order</code> button.

### Step 4: following the delivery

After you confirm, your service appears on your dashboard with the **Deploying** badge. It is usually available within 2 hours (indicative time). You can start tagging your virtual machines in vSphere about 30 minutes after you order.

You receive two separate emails:

1. When you confirm, an **order confirmation**, with no credentials, because the backup server does not exist yet.
2. On delivery, a **"your service is ready"** email, with the address of your VBR, your account login and a **one-time link** to your initial password.

A message on your dashboard tells you when you can tag your machines in vSphere.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Dashboard banner telling you that you can tag your machines in vSphere" src="/images/storage-and-backup/managed-backup-for-vmware/getting-started/01-tag-banner.png" loading="lazy" />

*The **You can tag your machines in vSphere** banner appears on the dashboard when the VBR is ready.*

:::info
**When can you tag your machines?**

You can tag your machines about 30 minutes after you order. After that, the tag in vSphere is what counts, and it is taken into account within the hour. The full process is explained in the [Protecting your virtual machines](/guides/storage-and-backup/managed-backup-for-vmware/tag-vm) guide.

:::

:::warning
**If the delivery fails**

The service shows the **Error** badge on your dashboard, instead of **Deploying**. Contact support and give the name of your infrastructure. You do not receive the "your service is ready" email, nor any access to the VBR. Nothing is billed.

:::

### Step 5: connecting to your VBR

1. **Get your initial password** by opening the one-time link in the delivery email. This link is valid for a limited time, so open it quickly.
2. **Open the VBR console.** On the dashboard, click <code className="action">Open the VBR console</code>. The **VBR console** window opens. Choose the infrastructure in the **Infrastructure** field, then copy the login shown in **Login** with the copy button.
3. **Sign in.** Click <code className="action">Open the VBR web interface</code>. The page opens in a new tab. Enter the login given in your delivery email and the initial password in the **Username** and **Password** fields, then click <code className="action">Sign in</code>.
4. **Choose a new password.** You are asked for it the first time you sign in. OVHcloud does not keep it. If you lose it, use the [password reset](/guides/storage-and-backup/managed-backup-for-vmware/monitor-protection#resetting-the-password-of-your-vbr).

   <img className="thumbnail" alt="VBR Change password screen with two masked password fields and the Confirm button" src="/images/storage-and-backup/managed-backup-for-vmware/getting-started/02-vbr-change-password.png" loading="lazy" />

   *The **Change password** screen at first sign-in. Fill in the fields then click <code className="action">Confirm</code>.*

:::info
**What your account allows**

Your VBR account lets you start an on-demand backup, restore a full virtual machine or files, and check the status of your backups. OVHcloud still manages the creation and editing of backup jobs: the vSphere tag decides what is protected.

:::

### Step 6: protecting your virtual machines

A machine is backed up only if it has a tag. In vSphere, add the tag of its Backup plan to the machine: `standard`, `advanced` or `premium`. Only one tag per machine, taken into account within the hour.

The procedure is detailed in the [Protecting your virtual machines](/guides/storage-and-backup/managed-backup-for-vmware/tag-vm) guide.

:::info
**If no machine is protected**

Three days after delivery, then ten days after, you receive a reminder email. The reminder at ten days announces the termination date of your service. Thirty days after delivery, if no machine is protected, the service is terminated automatically: access to the VBR is closed and nothing is billed. To protect your infrastructure, you then need to order again (see [Terminating and reactivating a service](/guides/storage-and-backup/managed-backup-for-vmware/terminate#automatic-termination-of-a-service-that-was-never-used)).

:::

### Protecting another infrastructure

Each infrastructure is a separate service, with its own VBR, its own credentials and its own billing. To protect another one, click <code className="action">Add an infrastructure</code> on the dashboard. You start a full order again, in which you accept the terms and choose the secondary site for this infrastructure. Your existing services keep running while the new one is deployed.
