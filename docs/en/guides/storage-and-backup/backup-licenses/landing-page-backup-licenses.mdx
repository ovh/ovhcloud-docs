## Backup Licenses guides

The **Backup Licenses** product lets you rent Veeam licences from OVHcloud for your own **Veeam Backup & Replication** (VBR) server, whether it is hosted at OVHcloud or in your own infrastructure. Choose your licence level, scale up or down without a long-term commitment, and let OVHcloud manage the licence and the storage while you keep full control of your Veeam server. The licence is applied and renewed automatically once your VBR server is connected to the OVHcloud **Veeam Service Provider Console** (VSPC), and each licence includes a managed Vault — 500 GB of Object Storage — to store your backup data. Backup Licenses replaces the Veeam Enterprise offer.

Whether you are ordering your first licence, migrating from Veeam Enterprise, managing your backup storage or troubleshooting a connection, the guides below cover the whole product.
