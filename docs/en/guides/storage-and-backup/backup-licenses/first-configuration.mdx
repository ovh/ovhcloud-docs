## Objective

The **Backup Licenses** product lets you rent Veeam licences from OVHcloud for your own **Veeam Backup & Replication** (VBR) server, whether it is hosted at OVHcloud or in your own infrastructure. The licence is applied automatically to your VBR once it is connected to the OVHcloud **Veeam Service Provider Console** (VSPC).

**This guide explains how to order your first Backup Licenses licence and connect your VBR server to the OVHcloud VSPC.**

## Requirements

- An OVHcloud account, and access to the OVHcloud Control Panel.
- A **Veeam Backup & Replication** server, installed and operational, whose **public IP address** you know.
- Access to the VBR console with an account that has **local Administrator** rights or the **Veeam Backup Administrator** role.

:::info
Read the "[Known restrictions](/guides/storage-and-backup/backup-licenses/restrictions)" guide before ordering: your VBR version and operating system determine which licence editions are available to you.

:::

## Instructions

To set up your first licence:

- Choose the licence edition that matches your needs.
- Order the licence from the OVHcloud Control Panel, entering your VBR name and IP address.
- Wait for the delivery email containing your connection details.
- Open the required network flows on your firewall.
- Connect your VBR server to the OVHcloud VSPC.
- Check that the licence has been applied.

### Step 1: Choose your licence edition

Backup Licenses offers 4 editions. Pricing is expressed **per server, VM, database or 500 GB block of storage** protected, and up-to-date prices are displayed when ordering in the Control Panel.

| Edition | Compatibility | Recommended for | Included features |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Veeam Enterprise Plus** | VBR V12 and V13, **Windows only** | Existing Windows environments that do not need the advanced Veeam Data Platform features. | Backup and restore · 500 GiB Vault fully managed by OVHcloud · Security baseline |
| **Veeam Data Platform (VDP) Foundation** | VBR V13, **Windows or Linux** | Small businesses with a limited fleet (5 to 30 workloads) and simple backup/restore needs. | Backup and restore · 500 GiB Vault fully managed by OVHcloud · Security and permission control |
| **Veeam Data Platform (VDP) Advanced** | VBR V13, **Windows or Linux** | Small and medium businesses with a large fleet (30 to 150 workloads) needing fine-grained monitoring and proactive alerting. | Everything in Foundation · Cyber security, observability and AI |
| **Veeam Data Platform (VDP) Premium** | VBR V13, **Windows or Linux** | Mid-market and large enterprises, public sector and regulated environments with an extended fleet (150 to 2000+ workloads). | Everything in Advanced · DR orchestration and compliance |

:::info
For the full feature breakdown of each edition, see the [official Veeam Data Platform edition comparison](https://www.veeam.com/products/veeam-data-platform/enterprise-packaging-pricing-options.html?ad=packaging-feature_comparison#comparison).

:::

:::warning
If your VBR server runs on a **Veeam Software Appliance** (Linux), only the **VDP** editions are available. The Veeam Enterprise Plus edition is offered on Windows only.

:::

### Step 2: Order your licence

From the OVHcloud Control Panel, open the <code className="action">Storage and backups</code> section, then <code className="action">Backup Licenses</code>. In the <code className="action">Linked servers</code> tab, click <code className="action">Add a server</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Linked servers tab of the Backup Licenses product" src="/images/storage-and-backup/backup-licenses/control-panel/bl_home_en.png" loading="lazy" />

On the <code className="action">Licence type</code> step, choose the licence to associate with your VBR server: **Veeam Enterprise Plus**, or **Veeam Data Platform** followed by the **Foundation**, **Advanced** or **Premium** tier.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Choosing the Backup Licenses licence type" src="/images/storage-and-backup/backup-licenses/control-panel/bl_order_en.png" loading="lazy" />

On the <code className="action">VBR Server</code> step, enter your server details:

- **Service name**: the identifier for your VBR server in the OVHcloud Control Panel. This name is only used to identify your server in our interfaces: it can differ from the machine's actual hostname.
- **Public IP address**: the public IP address of your VBR server. It is used to allow traffic between our infrastructure and your server.
- **Veeam Client IP address**: optional. The public IP address of the machine where your Veeam Client runs, if it does not run directly on the VBR.
- **My Veeam server is behind a NAT**: enable this option if your server's public IP differs from its local IP on the internal network.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Setting up the VBR server during the order" src="/images/storage-and-backup/backup-licenses/control-panel/bl_order_2_en.png" loading="lazy" />

Finally, click <code className="action">Order</code> to confirm the order.

### Step 3: Receive the delivery email

Once the order is complete, you will receive a **delivery email** containing everything you need to connect:

| Item | Description |
|---|---|
| **Cloud gateway URL** | The DNS address of the OVHcloud cloud gateway, in the form `vspc-adv-cgw…`. This is the address your VBR will contact. |
| **Port** | The port used for the connection: `6180`. |
| **Company Tenant** | The account used to connect your VBR to the VSPC. |
| **Company Owner** | The account used to log in to the VSPC portal and monitor your backups. |

:::warning
Keep this email: it contains the credentials required for the following steps and for connecting any additional VBR server.

:::

### Step 4: Open the network flows

Before connecting your VBR, make sure the server firewall — and your network firewall — allows the following two flows:

- **Outbound**: from your VBR server to the OVHcloud cloud gateway provided in the delivery email (`vspc-adv-cgw…`), on **port 6180**.
- **Inbound**: from our infrastructure's IP address to your VBR server, **without restriction**. In Europe, this address is `79.137.44.43`.

:::warning
If either flow is blocked, your VBR server cannot be connected, or will not appear correctly in the VSPC. See the "[Troubleshooting guide](/guides/storage-and-backup/backup-licenses/troubleshooting)" if you run into an issue.

:::

### Step 5: Connect your VBR server to the VSPC

The steps below are carried out **from the Veeam Backup & Replication console**, not from the VSPC portal.

#### 5.1 Open the Veeam Backup & Replication console

Log in to the server where Veeam Backup & Replication is installed (or to a machine where the VBR console is deployed), then launch the console:

- On a **Windows** installation: connect to `localhost`.
- On a **Veeam Software Appliance**: enter the VBR server address and the credentials of an account with the Veeam Backup Administrator role.

:::info
If your VBR runs on a Veeam Software Appliance (Linux), remote data collection must be enabled in the **Veeam Host Management Console**. See the Veeam documentation "[Connecting Linux Veeam Backup & Replication Servers](https://helpcenter.veeam.com/docs/vac/provider_admin/vbr_connect_linux.html)" for more information.

:::

#### 5.2 Start adding the service provider

In the ribbon at the top of the console, click <code className="action">Add Service Provider</code>.

You can also go through the navigation pane:

1. Open the <code className="action">Backup Infrastructure</code> view.
2. Locate the <code className="action">Service Providers</code> node.
3. Right-click <code className="action">Service Providers</code>, then click <code className="action">Add Service Provider</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Adding a service provider from the VBR console" src="/images/storage-and-backup/backup-licenses/vbr/bl_vbr_add_service_provider.png" loading="lazy" />

#### 5.3 Enter the service provider settings

On the <code className="action">Service Provider</code> step of the wizard:

1. **DNS name or IP address**: enter the OVHcloud cloud gateway URL received in the delivery email (`vspc-adv-cgw…`).
2. **Port**: enter the port provided by OVHcloud, `6180`.
3. **Description**: optional, a description to identify this provider (for example "OVHcloud — Backup Licenses").
4. Tick the <code className="action">Allow this Veeam Backup & Replication installation to be managed by the service provider</code> checkbox.

:::danger
The <code className="action">Allow this Veeam Backup & Replication installation to be managed by the service provider</code> checkbox is **mandatory**. Without it, the management agent is not installed, your server does not appear in the VSPC, and the licence cannot be assigned to it.

:::

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Service Provider step of the wizard, with the management checkbox ticked" src="/images/storage-and-backup/backup-licenses/vbr/bl_wizard_1.png" loading="lazy" />

Click <code className="action">Next</code> to move on to the next step.

#### 5.4 Enter the credentials

On the <code className="action">Credentials</code> step, enter the **Company Tenant** credentials received in the delivery email:

- **User**: the Company Tenant login.
- **Password**: the password associated with this account.

The certificate presented by the OVHcloud gateway is verified automatically, then click <code className="action">Apply</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Credentials step of the wizard" src="/images/storage-and-backup/backup-licenses/vbr/bl_wizard_2.png" loading="lazy" />

#### 5.5 Complete the wizard

The wizard saves the service provider and enables remote management.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Apply step of the wizard" src="/images/storage-and-backup/backup-licenses/vbr/bl_wizard_3.png" loading="lazy" />

Click <code className="action">Next</code>, review the summary, then click <code className="action">Finish</code> on the last step.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Wizard summary with the Finish button" src="/images/storage-and-backup/backup-licenses/vbr/bl_wizard_4.png" loading="lazy" />

The console then runs a <code className="action">Service Provider Rescan</code> job, which must complete with the `Success` status.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Service Provider Rescan job completed successfully" src="/images/storage-and-backup/backup-licenses/vbr/bl_wizard_5.png" loading="lazy" />

The OVHcloud service provider then appears in the <code className="action">Service Providers</code> list of your VBR console.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="OVHcloud service provider added in the VBR console" src="/images/storage-and-backup/backup-licenses/vbr/bl_service_provider_added.png" loading="lazy" />

The management agent is deployed on your VBR server and the connection to the OVHcloud VSPC is established.

:::warning
On a VBR server running **Windows**, enrolling the server takes **at least 15 minutes** after the wizard completes. During this time, it is normal for your server not to appear in the VSPC yet, and for the licence not to be applied yet. Wait for this delay to elapse before troubleshooting.

:::

### Step 6: Check the licence on your VBR

Once the connection is established, check that the licence provided by OVHcloud is correctly in place:

1. In the Veeam Backup & Replication console, open the main menu and click <code className="action">License</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="License entry in the VBR console main menu" src="/images/storage-and-backup/backup-licenses/vbr/bl_show_license_menu.png" loading="lazy" />

2. In the <code className="action">License Information</code> window, check the following:

| Field | Expected value |
|---|---|
| **Status** | `Valid` (not `Expired`, `Invalid` or `Not Installed`) |
| **Type** | The licence type: `Rental` |
| **Package** | The edition you ordered, for example `Foundation` or `Enterprise Plus` |
| **Licensed to** | The organisation associated with the licence: `OVH` |
| **Points** | The number of usable points, consistent with your order |
| **Expiration date** | The licence expiry date |

<img className="thumbnail" alt="License Information window in the VBR console" src="/images/storage-and-backup/backup-licenses/vbr/bl_license.png" loading="lazy" />

If the status is `Valid` and the capacity matches your order, the licence has been applied correctly.

### Step 7: Check the connection from the VSPC

You can also check the connection from the VSPC portal, using the **Company Owner** account received in the delivery email:

1. Log in to the VSPC portal. The access link is available in the <code className="action">General information</code> tab of the Backup Licenses product, under <code className="action">VSPC access</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="General information tab with the VSPC access link" src="/images/storage-and-backup/backup-licenses/control-panel/bl_information_en.png" loading="lazy" />

2. Go to the <code className="action">Managed Computers</code> section, <code className="action">Backup Servers</code> tab.
3. Check that your VBR server appears with the `Healthy` status.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="List of backup servers in the VSPC" src="/images/storage-and-backup/backup-licenses/vspc/bl_vspc_backup_server.png" loading="lazy" />

You can also check the licence assignment from <code className="action">Configuration</code> > <code className="action">License Usage</code>, <code className="action">Veeam Backup & Replication</code> tab: your server must appear there with the `Valid` status, its expiry date and the number of points allocated.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="License Usage view in the VSPC" src="/images/storage-and-backup/backup-licenses/vspc/bl_vspc_license_key.png" loading="lazy" />

Once enrolment is complete, your server also appears in the <code className="action">Linked servers</code> tab of your Control Panel, along with its licence, VBR version and operating system.

## Important points

- **Do not connect the same VBR server twice** to the VSPC with different credentials: this would skew the data displayed.
- If **MFA** (multi-factor authentication) is enabled on the VBR server, it may appear as `Inaccessible` in the VSPC. See [Veeam KB 4431](https://www.veeam.com/kb4431) for the resolution procedure.
- If your VBR is the primary node of a **high availability cluster**, the VSPC registers it as a cluster node: the secondary node must then be connected separately.
