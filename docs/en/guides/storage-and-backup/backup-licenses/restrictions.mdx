## Objective

**This guide details the known restrictions and limitations of the Backup Licenses product that you should be aware of before ordering your licence.**

## Known restrictions

### Veeam Backup & Replication version

- Our infrastructure only supports Veeam Backup & Replication servers running **version 12 or higher**. A VBR server on an earlier version cannot be connected to the OVHcloud Veeam Service Provider Console (VSPC), and therefore cannot be assigned a licence.

### Operating system and available editions

- VBR servers running on **Linux** (Veeam Software Appliance) only support **Veeam Data Platform (VDP)** licences: Foundation, Advanced or Premium.
- The **Veeam Enterprise Plus** edition is only available for VBR servers running on **Windows**.

The table below summarises the possible combinations:

| VBR operating system | Veeam Enterprise Plus | Veeam Data Platform (Foundation / Advanced / Premium) |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| **Windows** | Yes | Yes |
| **Linux** (Veeam Software Appliance) | No | Yes |

:::info
See the "[Ordering and configuring your first licence](/guides/storage-and-backup/backup-licenses/first-configuration)" guide for details of each edition and its features.

:::

### VBR servers licensed through Veeam Enterprise

- A VBR server whose licence is still managed by the former OVHcloud **Veeam Enterprise** offer cannot receive a Backup Licenses licence: its licence is controlled by a Veeam Backup Enterprise Manager server, which prevents the VSPC from applying a new one.
- You must therefore **cancel your Veeam Enterprise service before connecting your VBR server to the VSPC**, then wait 24 hours. The full procedure is described in the "[Migrating from Veeam Enterprise to Backup Licenses](/guides/storage-and-backup/backup-licenses/migration-veeam-enterprise)" guide.
