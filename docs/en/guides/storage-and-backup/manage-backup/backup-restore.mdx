## Objective

Your Veeam Backup & Replication (VBR) backup server is dedicated to your infrastructure. You access it through its web console to start backups and restore your data, without opening a ticket.

**This guide explains how to start an on-demand backup and how to restore a full virtual machine, files, or start a machine directly from its backup.**

## Requirements

- Have an active Managed Backup for VMware service.
- Have the login of your VBR account and its password (see [Getting started with Managed Backup for VMware](/guides/storage-and-backup/manage-backup/overview)).
- Have at least one virtual machine that is tagged and backed up.

## Instructions

### What your account allows

| You can | Managed by OVHcloud |
|---------|---------------------|
| • Start an on-demand backup.<br />• Restore a full virtual machine.<br />• Restore files or folders.<br />• Start a machine from its backup (Instant Recovery).<br />• Check the status of your backups and their restore points. | • The backup jobs: creation, editing and deletion.<br />• The configuration of the VBR, its proxies and the storage.<br />• The deletion of an immutable backup. |

To choose what is backed up, use the vSphere tags (see [Protecting your virtual machines](/guides/storage-and-backup/manage-backup/tag-vm)).

### How your backups are scheduled

- **Three backup jobs** are created on your VBR at delivery, one per Backup plan: Standard, Advanced and Premium. Each machine joins the job of its tag.
- **The jobs start every evening at 10 pm.**
- **An on-demand backup** runs immediately, including outside this schedule.

### Sign in to the VBR console

The VBR interface is in English. The labels below are the ones shown on screen.

1. On your dashboard, click <code className="action">Open the VBR console</code> and choose your infrastructure. The **Veeam Backup & Replication** sign-in page opens.
2. In **Username**, enter the login given in your delivery e-mail. In **Password**, enter the initial password. Click <code className="action">Sign in</code>. Tick **Remember me** only on a personal computer.
3. At first sign-in, the **Change password** screen asks you to choose a new password. Fill in the two fields, check the **Password requirements** link if needed, which describes the rules to follow, then click <code className="action">Confirm</code>. OVHcloud does not keep this password, so write it down in a safe place.
4. The **Overview** page appears. The left menu gives you access to <code className="action">Jobs</code> and <code className="action">Backups</code> (under **Home**), then to <code className="action">Logs & Events</code> (under **Audit**).

### Start an on-demand backup

This is useful right after you tag a machine, or before a sensitive operation (update, application migration).

1. Sign in to the console (see [Sign in to the VBR console](#sign-in-to-the-vbr-console)).
2. In the left menu, under **Home**, click <code className="action">Jobs</code>, then open the <code className="action">Backup Jobs</code> tab. You see one job per Backup plan. The job name ends with `standard`, `advanced` or `premium`.
3. Tick the box of the job for the Backup plan you want.
4. In the action bar, click <code className="action">Start</code>. The job backs up all the machines that have this tag.

   <img className="thumbnail" alt="VBR Jobs page, Backup Jobs tab, with the jobs ticked and the Start button in the action bar" src="/images/storage-and-backup/manage-backup/backup-restore/01-vbr-jobs-start.png" loading="lazy" />

   *The **Jobs** page, **Backup Jobs** tab. In the screenshot, the three jobs are ticked. For a single backup, tick only the job you want.*

5. Follow the result in the columns of the list:
   - **Status** shows the state of the job. **Stopped** means it is not running at that moment.
   - **Last Run** shows how long ago the job last ran.
   - **Last Result** shows the result: **Success** for a successful backup, **Warning** for a warning to read in the job details.
6. Open the job details to read its sessions. The history at the top of the page shows the latest runs. The **Session Details** area lists the actions of the session in the **Job View** view. The **Workload View** view shows the result machine by machine.
7. Check that a new restore point appears (see [Check the status of your backups](#check-the-status-of-your-backups)).

### Restore a full virtual machine

Restoring a whole machine uses the **Entire VM Restore** wizard.

1. From the <code className="action">Backups</code> page, select the machine to restore and its restore point, then start the full machine restore.
2. The wizard shows its steps on the left: **Virtual Machines**, **Restore Mode**, **Host**, **Datastore**, **Folder**, **Network**, **Reason** and **Summary**. Click <code className="action">Next</code> to move forward, <code className="action">Previous</code> to go back, <code className="action">Cancel</code> to quit.
3. At the **Restore Mode** step, choose:
   - **The original location**, to replace the existing machine.
   - **Another location or other settings**, under another name, to keep the current machine.
4. At the **Host** and **Datastore** steps, indicate the host and the storage that will host the restored machine.
5. At the **Folder** step, the original folder of the machine is selected by default. To change it, select the machine then click <code className="action">Folder</code>. To restore the machine under another name, click <code className="action">Name</code>. The **Change VM Settings** panel opens. It offers **Set name to**, **Add prefix** and **Add suffix**. Confirm with <code className="action">OK</code>.

   <img className="thumbnail" alt="Entire VM Restore wizard at the Folder step with the Change VM Settings panel open to rename the machine" src="/images/storage-and-backup/manage-backup/backup-restore/02-vbr-entire-vm-restore.png" loading="lazy" />

   *The **Entire VM Restore** wizard, **Folder** step, with the **Change VM Settings** panel open.*

6. At the **Network**, **Reason** and **Summary** steps, check the network, enter the reason for the restore if needed, then review the summary before you start the restore.
7. Follow the restore until its final report.

:::warning
**Check the tag after a restore**

After a restore, check the tag of the restored machine in vSphere and add it again if needed. A machine without a tag is neither backed up nor billed.

:::

A restore always goes to the original infrastructure of the machine. Restoring to another infrastructure is not available.

### Restore files or folders

1. From the <code className="action">Backups</code> page, select the machine then start a guest file restore (*Guest File Restore*). The **Browse** page of the machine opens.
2. Next to **Restore Point**, click <code className="action">Select restore point</code>. The **Select Restore Point** dialog shows a calendar. The days that have restore points are active. The **Available restore points** list shows the time of each point and its type: **Full** for a full backup, **Increment** for an incremental backup. Choose a point, or click <code className="action">Select latest restore point</code> to take the most recent one. The **Mount server** field stays on **Automatic**. Click <code className="action">Select</code>.
3. Browse the tree of the restore point. The left tree shows the machine, and the right list shows the folders and files with their type, size, dates and owner. The **Search** field helps you find a name. Tick the files or folders you want.

   <img className="thumbnail" alt="Browsing the files of a backed-up machine with the Search, Restore and Download bar and the list of folders" src="/images/storage-and-backup/manage-backup/backup-restore/03-vbr-file-level-restore.png" loading="lazy" />

   *Browsing the files of the mounted restore point.*

4. Click <code className="action">Restore</code> to restore the selection to the original machine, or <code className="action">Download</code> to download it.
5. When you are done, click <code className="action">Exit File-level Restore</code>, at the top left, to close the file restore session.

The console gives you an operation report, which you can keep to document the incident.

### Start a machine from its backup (Instant Recovery)

Instant Recovery starts a machine directly from a restore point, without waiting for its data to be copied. It is the fastest way to bring a service back online.

1. From the <code className="action">Backups</code> page, select the machine and start an instant restore. The **Instant Recovery to VMware vSphere** wizard opens. Its steps are **Machines**, **Restore Mode**, **Reason** and **Summary**.
2. At the **Machines** step, tick the machine to restore. The **Restore Point** column shows the selected point. <code className="action">Add</code> adds other machines and <code className="action">Remove</code> removes some.
3. To change the restore point, click <code className="action">Restore Point</code>. The **Restore Points** panel lists the available points with their type (**Increment** or **Full**). Select one and click <code className="action">OK</code>.
4. Click <code className="action">Next</code>. At the **Restore Mode** step, choose **Restore to the original location** for an identical restore, or **Restore to a new location or with different settings** to customise the location and settings.
5. Click <code className="action">Next</code>. At the **Reason** step, you can enter the reason for the restore. The **Summary** step recaps your choices. Review them then start the restore.
6. Once the machine has started, it appears in the left menu, under **Action Required** > **Instant Recovery**. The list shows its state (**Mounted**) and the restore point used. Check that the machine works as expected.
7. To finish the restore, select the machine then click <code className="action">Migrate to Production</code>. If you do not need the machine, click <code className="action">Stop Publishing</code> to end the session without migrating it.

   <img className="thumbnail" alt="List of machines in Instant Recovery with the Migrate to Production, Stop Publishing and Properties buttons" src="/images/storage-and-backup/manage-backup/backup-restore/04-vbr-instant-recovery-migrate.png" loading="lazy" />

   *The **Instant Recovery** list under **Action Required**.*

8. The **Quick Migration** wizard opens. Its steps are **Virtual Machines**, **Destination**, **Datastore**, **Transfer** and **Summary**. At the **Destination** step, choose the host or cluster (**Host or cluster**), the resource pool (**Resource Pool**) and the folder (**VM folder**) with the <code className="action">Choose</code> buttons.
9. At the **Transfer** step, the selection of the source and target proxies (**Source proxy** and **Target proxy**) is automatic by default. The **Force Veeam transport usage** box forces the disks to be moved by the Veeam transfer engine, even if VMware Storage vMotion is available. Finish with <code className="action">Next</code>, then confirm the summary.

:::warning
**Instant Recovery is a temporary solution, not a lasting way to run**

During the session, the machine runs from the backup storage, not from your production storage. Its performance is reduced. It cannot be moved (vMotion). It is not covered by the automatic maintenance of the platform.

Do not leave a machine running in Instant Recovery: as soon as it is checked, migrate it to the production storage and end the session. If you do not need the machine, end the session without migrating it. The duration of the session is not monitored, so remember to end it yourself.

:::

### Restore from the secondary site

Advanced and Premium backups are copied to the secondary site chosen at order time. The points copied to the secondary site stay restorable. If the primary site is unavailable, contact support, who will guide you: OVHcloud rebuilds the backup server (see [Security and guarantees](/guides/storage-and-backup/manage-backup/security)).

### How long your restore points stay available

| Backup plan | Retention | Immutability | Monthly points |
|-------------|-----------|--------------|----------------|
| Standard | 14 days | None | No |
| Advanced | 35 days | 14 days | No |
| Premium | 35 days | 14 days | 11 monthly points, about one year |

A restore point keeps the Backup plan under which it was written: if you change the tag of a machine, its old points keep their original retention.

### Check the status of your backups

Three pages of the console give you the status of your backups.

- **Overview** summarises the activity. The **Protection Overview** card counts the sessions of the last 24 hours (**Total Sessions**) and splits them into **Success**, **Warning** and **Failed**.
- **Backups** lists the backups present on your VBR, by job. Expand a job with the arrow to the left of its name to see one line per machine. The column whose header is truncated to **Restor...** shows the number of restore points of each machine.

  <img className="thumbnail" alt="VBR Backups page with the jobs expanded, one line per machine and the column with the number of restore points" src="/images/storage-and-backup/manage-backup/backup-restore/05-vbr-backups-restore-points.png" loading="lazy" />

  *The **Backups** page, jobs expanded. The columns show, among other things, the creation date and the number of restore points.*

- **Logs & Events**, **Session Logs** tab, lists the sessions of the last 24 hours (**Last 24 Hours**) with the job name, the session type, its status (**Success** or **Warning**) and its start and end times. The **Type in a name** and **Status** fields let you filter the list.

Your VBR sends you its backup notifications by e-mail, to the address of the OVHcloud account that ordered the service. The last backup date shown in your OVHcloud Control Panel remains the signal to watch: a date older than that of the other machines, or no date, flags a machine to check. The details of a failure are available in the console of your VBR.
