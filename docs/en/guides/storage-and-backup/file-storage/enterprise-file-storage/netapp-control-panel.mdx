## Objective

Enterprise File Storage services can be managed [via the OVHcloud API](/guides/storage-and-backup/file-storage/enterprise-file-storage/netapp-quick-start) or in the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>.

**This guide explains how to manage Enterprise File Storage volumes and snapshots in the OVHcloud Console.**

## Requirements

- An Enterprise File Storage service in your OVHcloud account. The service can be ordered from the [product page](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/storage-solutions/enterprise-file-storage/) or from the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>.

[[cpnav:storage-enterprise-file-storage]]

## Instructions <a name="instructions"></a>

In the OVHcloud Console, open **Bare Metal Cloud**, click <code className="action">Enterprise File Storage</code> under **Storage** in the left-hand menu, then select your service from the list.

The <code className="action">Overview</code> tab displays the **General information** and the **Service configuration** of your service (with a shortcut to [create a volume](#create_volume)), its **Network configuration**, its **Subscription** details and its **Tags**.

:::info
Please visit the [concepts page](/guides/storage-and-backup/file-storage/enterprise-file-storage/netapp-concepts) for detailed information on the technical properties of Enterprise File Storage.

:::


### Managing volumes <a name="manage_volume"></a>

Switch to the <code className="action">Volumes</code> tab. The table lists all volumes you have created for the selected service. Click on a volume name to open its [management page](#modify_volume).

To perform an action on a volume, tick the box of its row, then open the <code className="action">Actions (1)</code> menu above the table:

- **Edit volume**: Opens the ["Information" tab](#modify_volume) of the volume.
- **Resize volume**: Opens a window in which you enter the **New size** of the volume, then click <code className="action">Resize</code>.
- **Manage snapshots**: Opens the ["Snapshots" tab](#snapshots) of the volume.
- **Manage IP Access (ACL)**: Opens the ["ACL" tab](#access_control) of the volume.
- **Replicate volumes**: Replicates the selected volumes to another Enterprise File Storage service.
- **Delete volume**: Deletes this volume after you confirm the action in the window that opens.

#### Creating a volume <a name="create_volume"></a>

Click on the button <code className="action">Create a volume</code>. In the window that opens, you can enter a **Name** and a **Description** (optional), choose the **Protocol**, optionally select a snapshot in **Create from a snapshot**, set the **Size** and, optionally, the **Mount path**. Click <code className="action">Create a volume</code> to confirm.

#### Modifying a volume <a name="modify_volume"></a>

Click on a volume name in the table to open the management page of this volume.

The <code className="action">Information</code> tab displays your volume's details (**Volume**, **Storage** and **Usage overview** sections) and the **Volume connection** section, with the mount path and the steps to connect to the volume.

From this tab, you can also change the volume's name (<code className="action">Edit name</code>) and description (<code className="action">Edit description</code>), resize it (<code className="action">Resize the volume</code>) and change its snapshot reserve (<code className="action">Edit snapshot reserve</code>).

The <code className="action">NFS connections</code> tab lists the NFS connections to the volume, and the <code className="action">Performance</code> tab displays its performance metrics.

#### Deleting a volume <a name="delete_volume"></a>

You can delete a volume by clicking <code className="action">Delete volume</code> in its <code className="action">Information</code> tab, or by ticking its box in the <code className="action">Volumes</code> tab and selecting <code className="action">Delete volume</code> in the <code className="action">Actions (1)</code> menu.

Confirm the deletion by typing `DELETE` in the confirmation field, then click the <code className="action">Delete volume</code> button.

### Managing volume snapshots <a name="snapshots"></a>

:::info
Volume snapshots use storage capacity from your Enterprise File Storage offer. By default, 5% of a volume storage capacity is reserved for snapshots.
:::

The <code className="action">Snapshots</code> tab of a volume lists all the snapshots that have been created for it. You can filter them by **Type**.

:::info
You can also access snapshot from your mount point using the commands provided in the [NetApp documentation](https://library.netapp.com/ecmdocs/ECMP1196991/html/GUID-36DC110C-C0FE-4313-BF53-1C12838F7BBD.html).
:::

In the **Snapshot policy** section of this tab, you can view the [Snapshot Policy](#snapshot_policy) applied to the volume and its rules (<code className="action">View the rules</code>).

To apply another policy, click <code className="action">Manage snapshot policy</code>, select a policy in **Available policies**, then click <code className="action">Apply policy</code>. The `default` policy cannot be selected manually.

In order to configure your [Snapshot Policies](#snapshot_policy), navigate back to your service and open the <code className="action">Policies</code> tab.

#### Creating a snapshot <a name="create_snapshot"></a>

To create a new `manual` snapshot of the volume's current state, click <code className="action">Take a snapshot</code> in the <code className="action">Snapshots</code> tab (or in the <code className="action">Information</code> tab).

In the window that opens, you can enter a **Name** and a **Description** for the snapshot (optional). Click <code className="action">Take a snapshot</code> to start snapshot creation.

#### Modifying a snapshot <a name="modify_snapshot"></a>

To change the name or description of a `manual` snapshot, click the <code className="action">...</code> button in its row, then click on <code className="action">Edit</code>. In the window that opens, choose a new name and/or description, then click <code className="action">Confirm</code>.

#### Deleting a snapshot <a name="delete_snapshot"></a>

:::warning
`System` snapshots can not be deleted.
Snapshots with type `system` are essential for the proper function of your Enterprise File Storage service.
:::

A snapshot can be deleted by clicking the <code className="action">...</code> button on its row inside the <code className="action">Snapshots</code> tab, then selecting <code className="action">Delete</code>. In the window that opens, click <code className="action">Delete snapshot</code>.

#### Restore a volume using one of its snapshots <a name="restore_volume"></a>

:::warning
Please note that once a volume is restored using a snapshot copy, any subsequent files or snapshots will be lost.
When a volume is restored, all data in the volume is replaced with the snapshot’s data. The action is irreversible.
:::

You can restore a volume using its most recent snapshot by clicking <code className="action">Restore last snapshot</code> in the <code className="action">Snapshots</code> tab. In the window that opens, type the name of the volume to confirm, then click <code className="action">Restore last snapshot</code>. If the most recent snapshot is an `automatic` one, it is first saved as a `manual` snapshot before the volume is restored.

:::info
To restore a volume using a snapshot from before the latest snapshot, you must delete snapshots until the desired snapshot becomes the latest one.
:::

#### Holding automatic snapshots <a name="hold_snapshot"></a>

An `Automatic` snapshot, created by a [Snapshot Policy](#snapshot_policy) can be held by clicking the <code className="action">...</code> button on its row in the <code className="action">Snapshots</code> tab and then selecting <code className="action">Save</code>.

After the holding operation is complete, the snapshot type will change to `manual` and it will no longer be rotated by a Snapshot Policy.

You can also create a new volume from a `manual` snapshot: click the <code className="action">...</code> button on its row, then <code className="action">Create a volume</code>.

#### Managing volume ACLs <a name="access_control"></a>

Access control to volumes works via IP address restrictions. Since none are configured by default, the first step with new volumes is to define IP addresses or ranges from which access will be authorised.

Open the <code className="action">ACL</code> tab of the volume and click <code className="action">Add a rule</code>. In the **Add an ACL rule** panel, enter an **IP address** or an address block (CIDR), select the **Access level** (`Read only` or `Read/Write`), then click <code className="action">Add</code>.

To remove a volume access, click the <code className="action">...</code> button on its row, then <code className="action">Delete rule</code>.

### Managing snapshot policies <a name="snapshot_policy"></a>

:::info
By default, every volume is protected by the `default` Snapshot Policy which will create Snapshots of the volume at regular intervals.
:::

Adding policies allows you to schedule the creation of snapshots for all of your volumes.

Switch to the <code className="action">Policies</code> tab of your service. The table lists all policies created for the selected service.

A default policy is already in place which cannot be altered. To add your own, click on the button <code className="action">Create a policy</code>.

On the **Create a snapshot policy** page, enter a **Policy name** and a **Policy description**. Then click <code className="action">Add a rule</code> to add one or more rules to the policy.

Fill in the fields to specify the **Minute(s)**, **Hour(s)**, **Day(s) of the month**, **Month(s)** and **Day(s) of the week** of the snapshot creation, the **Prefix** needed for naming the snapshots, and the number of **Copies to keep**. By expanding the **Example** section you can view two sets of policy rules with an explanation of their outcome.

Click on the confirmation icon of the row to add the new rule. Once all rules are added, click on <code className="action">Create a policy</code>.

[Select a volume](#manage_volume) and go to its <code className="action">Snapshots</code> tab in order to [apply your policies](#snapshots).

To remove a policy, click the <code className="action">...</code> button on its row, then <code className="action">Delete</code>.
