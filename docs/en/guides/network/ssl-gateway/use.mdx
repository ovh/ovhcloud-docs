## Objective

Once your SSL Gateway is ordered, you configure it in the OVHcloud Console.

**This guide explains how to attach your domains to an SSL Gateway, define the servers it forwards the traffic to, configure its network settings, and follow its operations and statistics.**

## Requirements

- An [SSL Gateway](/guides/network/ssl-gateway/order), Free or Advanced offer
- Access to the DNS zone of each domain you want to serve through the gateway

## Introduction

[SSL Gateway](/links/web/ssl-gateway) is a managed HTTPS front end for your website. It sits in front of your own servers, called backend servers: it holds the SSL certificate, handles the TLS connection with your visitors' browsers and protects the traffic against DDoS attacks, then forwards the requests to your servers.

Your gateway's offer determines what you can configure:

| Feature | Free | Advanced |
|---|---|---|
| Domains | 3: 1 domain, its `www` and 1 subdomain | Up to 1,000 domains and subdomains, including domains of 4 levels and more |
| Backend servers | 1 | Up to 3, with the traffic balanced across them |
| Dedicated IP addresses | No | Yes |
| Cipher profile | Intermediate, fixed | Modern or Intermediate |
| HSTS (HTTP Strict Transport Security) | No | Yes |
| Allowed sources | No: every source can reach the gateway | Yes |
| Editable reverse DNS | No | Yes |
| <code className="action">Statistics</code> tab | No | Yes |
| EV (extended validation) certificate | No | Optional (Sectigo) |

## Instructions

### Opening your SSL Gateway

{/* CP-STEPS-START:open-gateway */}

The list shows each of your gateways with its **Name / ID**, **Region**, **Offer**, **IPv4** address and **Status**. Click the name of the gateway you want to configure.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="SSL Gateway list with the Name / ID, Region, Offer, IPv4 and Status columns" src="/images/network/ssl-gateway/use-ssl-gateway/console/ssl-gateway-list.png" loading="lazy" />

The gateway's page opens on the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, which shows four cards:

- **General information**: the gateway's ID, region, reverse DNS, IPv4 and IPv6 addresses, and the **Outbound IPv4** ranges the gateway uses to reach your servers;
- **Subscription**: the offer, the creation date, the renewal mode and the renewal date;
- **Network configuration**: the gateway's TLS and access settings;
- **Backend**: the servers the gateway forwards the traffic to.

The other tabs are <code className="action">Domains</code>, <code className="action">Statistics</code> (Advanced offer only) and <code className="action">Operations</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Overview tab of an Advanced SSL Gateway with the General information, Subscription, Network configuration and Backend cards" src="/images/network/ssl-gateway/use-ssl-gateway/console/overview-advanced.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:open-gateway */}

### Adding and removing domains

For a domain to be validated, its DNS records must point to the gateway: an A record to the gateway's IPv4 address and, if the gateway has an IPv6 address, an AAAA record to it. Both addresses are shown in the **General information** card. If OVHcloud manages the domain's DNS zone, refer to our guides on [A records](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-a-record-creation) and [AAAA records](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-aaaa-record-creation).

{/* CP-STEPS-START:domains */}

To attach a domain, open the <code className="action">Domains</code> tab and click <code className="action">Add domain</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Domains tab of an SSL Gateway with the Add domain button and the delete icon in a domain's row" src="/images/network/ssl-gateway/use-ssl-gateway/console/domains-tab.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Domain** field, enter the hostname you want to serve over HTTPS, for example `shop.example.com`, then click <code className="action">Add domain</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Add a domain window with the Domain field" src="/images/network/ssl-gateway/use-ssl-gateway/console/add-domain-dialog.png" loading="lazy" />

DNS validation can take up to 72 hours. Until it ends, the domain's status reads **Creating**.

With the Free offer, a gateway holds 3 domains: 1 domain, its `www` and 1 subdomain. Once 3 domains are attached, <code className="action">Add domain</code> is disabled.

To remove a domain, click the delete icon in its row, then <code className="action">Delete</code>. The domain is detached from the gateway and is no longer served over HTTPS.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:domains */}

### Configuring the backend servers

The **Backend** card of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab shows where the gateway forwards the traffic it receives: the IP address and the port of your servers.

{/* CP-STEPS-START:backend */}

**Free offer**: the gateway forwards the traffic to 1 server. Click <code className="action">Edit backend</code>, enter the server's **IP address** and the **Port** it listens on for the gateway's traffic, then click <code className="action">Save</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Edit backend panel of a Free SSL Gateway with the IP address and Port fields" src="/images/network/ssl-gateway/use-ssl-gateway/console/edit-backend-drawer.png" loading="lazy" />

**Advanced offer**: the gateway balances the traffic across up to 3 servers. Click <code className="action">Add a server</code>, enter its **IP address** and **Port**, then click <code className="action">Add</code>. To edit or delete a server, use the menu in its row.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Add a server panel of an Advanced SSL Gateway with the IP address and Port fields" src="/images/network/ssl-gateway/use-ssl-gateway/console/add-server-drawer.png" loading="lazy" />

The IP address must be your server's public IPv4 address: the gateway cannot reach a private address.

Each change runs as an operation and is applied in a few moments. You can follow it in the <code className="action">Operations</code> tab.

The gateway reaches your servers from the IP ranges listed under **Outbound IPv4** in the **General information** card. Allow these ranges on your servers' firewalls.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:backend */}

### Configuring the network settings

{/* CP-STEPS-START:network */}

In the **Network configuration** card, click <code className="action">Edit network configuration</code>. You can set:

- **Cipher profile**: the cipher suites the gateway offers browsers. <code className="action">Modern</code> keeps the strongest, for recent clients; <code className="action">Intermediate</code> also serves older ones.
- <code className="action">Backend HTTPS</code>: encrypts the traffic from the gateway to your servers too, not only from browsers to the gateway.
- <code className="action">HTTPS redirect</code>: redirects every HTTP request to HTTPS.
- <code className="action">HSTS</code>: tells browsers to reach your domains over HTTPS only on their next visits.
- **Allowed sources**: the IPv4 or IPv6 addresses or blocks allowed to reach the gateway. Enter an address or a block, for example `203.0.113.0/24`, then click <code className="action">Add</code>, once per source. To remove a source, click the cross next to it. With none listed, every source can reach the gateway.

Then click <code className="action">Save</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Edit network configuration panel with the Cipher profile list, the Backend HTTPS, HTTPS redirect and HSTS boxes, and the Allowed sources field" src="/images/network/ssl-gateway/use-ssl-gateway/console/network-configuration-drawer.png" loading="lazy" />

With the Free offer, the cipher profile stays **Intermediate**, and HSTS and allowed sources are not available. If a Free gateway still holds such settings, saving a change resets them to the Free values: Intermediate, HSTS disabled, no source restriction.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:network */}

### Editing the reverse DNS

{/* CP-STEPS-START:reverse */}

With the Advanced offer, you can set the reverse DNS of the gateway: in the **General information** card, click the pencil icon next to **Reverse DNS**, enter the hostname in the **Reverse DNS** field, then click <code className="action">Save</code>. With the Free offer, the reverse DNS cannot be edited.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Edit reverse DNS panel with the Reverse DNS field" src="/images/network/ssl-gateway/use-ssl-gateway/console/reverse-dns-drawer.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:reverse */}

### Following operations

{/* CP-STEPS-START:operations */}

Every change to the gateway, such as adding or deleting a domain or a server, or an upgrade, runs as an operation. The <code className="action">Operations</code> tab lists them with their **ID**, **Action**, **Status**, **Progress** and **Created on**. To see an operation's details, click <code className="action">See details</code> in its row.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:operations */}

### Viewing statistics

{/* CP-STEPS-START:statistics */}

With the Advanced offer, the <code className="action">Statistics</code> tab shows the gateway's traffic. Under **Period**, choose a duration, from <code className="action">1h</code> to <code className="action">1M</code> (one month). The tab shows the average and the peak number of requests and connections per second over the period. Above the chart, choose <code className="action">Requests/s</code> or <code className="action">Connections/s</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Statistics tab of an Advanced SSL Gateway with the period selector, the average and peak values, and the requests chart" src="/images/network/ssl-gateway/use-ssl-gateway/console/statistics-tab.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:statistics */}

### Managing the offer and the subscription

{/* CP-STEPS-START:subscription */}

The **Subscription** card shows the offer, the creation date, the renewal mode and the renewal date. Click <code className="action">Manage my subscription</code> to change the renewal. To cancel the service, click <code className="action">Terminate</code>, then <code className="action">Terminate</code> again in the confirmation window: you then receive an email to confirm the termination.

<Region zones={['eu']}>

When your gateway's offer can be upgraded, the **Subscription** card also shows <code className="action">Upgrade</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Overview tab of a Free SSL Gateway with Upgrade in the Subscription card" src="/images/network/ssl-gateway/use-ssl-gateway/console/overview-free.png" loading="lazy" />

Click <code className="action">Upgrade</code>. The **Upgrade to Advanced** window lists what the Advanced offer adds (up to 1,000 domains and subdomains, domains of 4 levels and more, a dedicated IP address, load balancing across up to 3 backend servers), the amount due today, prorated, and the amount then charged every month.

The <code className="action">Pay now with my default payment method</code> box is ticked by default. To pay on the order page instead, which opens in a new tab, untick it. Then click <code className="action">Upgrade</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Upgrade to Advanced window with the amounts, the IP address warning and the Pay now with my default payment method box" src="/images/network/ssl-gateway/use-ssl-gateway/console/upgrade-dialog.png" loading="lazy" />

:::warning
The Advanced offer runs on dedicated IP addresses. When the upgrade ends, your gateway gets a new IPv4 and IPv6 address, and the old ones stop serving traffic at once. Point your domains to the new addresses, sent by email and shown in the **General information** card: until then, your website cannot be reached through the gateway.

:::

The gateway keeps its ID and configuration. The upgrade runs as an operation you can follow in the <code className="action">Operations</code> tab.

</Region>

{/* CP-STEPS-END:subscription */}

### Renewing the SSL certificate

The gateway's SSL certificate is a DV (domain validation) certificate issued by Let's Encrypt and renewed automatically. With the Advanced offer, a Sectigo EV certificate is available as an option.

To renew the certificate on demand, use the following API call:
