## Objective

[SSL Gateway](/links/web/ssl-gateway) secures the connections to your website: it places a managed HTTPS front end, with an SSL certificate, in front of your own servers (VPS, dedicated server, etc.).

:::warning
SSL Gateway is not compatible with [OVHcloud web hosting](/links/web/hosting) plans. To use an SSL certificate with a web hosting plan, read our guide [Web Hosting - How to configure an SSL certificate](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ssl-on-webhosting).

:::

**This guide explains how to order an SSL Gateway.**

## Requirements

- A [domain name](/links/web/domains) or subdomain that already points to your website's server
- Access to the domain name's DNS zone

[[cpnav:network-ssl-gateway-console]]

## Instructions

### Ordering

On the **SSL Gateway** page, click <code className="action">Order an SSL Gateway</code>. The order form has 4 steps.

**1. Offer**: choose <code className="action">Free</code> or <code className="action">Advanced</code>.

- **Free**: 1 domain, its `www` and 1 subdomain, domains of up to 3 levels, a shared IP address, 1 backend server, and a Let's Encrypt certificate renewed automatically, with no monthly fee.
- **Advanced**: up to 1,000 domains and subdomains, domains of 4 levels and more, a dedicated IP address, and load balancing across up to 3 backend servers.

Both offers are billed monthly, with no setup fee and no commitment.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Order an SSL Gateway page with the Free and Advanced offers, the Domain step and the order summary" src="/images/network/ssl-gateway/order-ssl-gateway/console/order-offer.png" loading="lazy" />

**2. Domain**: in the **Domain** field, enter the domain or subdomain your SSL Gateway serves over HTTPS, then click <code className="action">Check</code>. The domain must already point to your website: under **Backend IP**, the form shows the IPv4 address it points to, where your SSL Gateway forwards the traffic. If there are several, choose one.

:::warning
- With the Free offer, the domain can have up to 3 levels, such as `www.domain.tld`. For a deeper subdomain, such as `blog.www.domain.tld`, choose Advanced.
- The form refuses a domain that points to an address already used by another OVHcloud service (Load Balancer, CDN, web hosting or SSL Gateway). Point it to your own server's address, then check again.
- With the Advanced offer, you can add up to 2 more backend servers after delivery, in the **Backend** card of your SSL Gateway. Refer to our guide [Configure and manage an SSL Gateway](/guides/network/ssl-gateway/use).

:::

**3. Location**: choose <code className="action">Roubaix (eu-west-rbx-a)</code> or <code className="action">Beauharnois (ca-east-bhs-a)</code>.

**4. DNS**: if you manage the DNS zone of the domain at OVHcloud, you can tick <code className="action">I manage this domain's DNS zone and authorise OVHcloud to update the required DNS record automatically.</code> The record is then updated for you, and the order summary shows **DNS update**: Automatic. Otherwise, you point the domain to your SSL Gateway yourself after ordering (see below).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Domain, Location and DNS steps of the SSL Gateway order form after a domain check, with the backend IP, the 3-day deadline and the HTTPS notice" src="/images/network/ssl-gateway/order-ssl-gateway/console/order-domain-location-dns.png" loading="lazy" />

Check your choices in the order summary, then click <code className="action">Continue order</code>. The order review opens in a new tab: follow the steps until you pay for your order. Your SSL Gateway appears on the **SSL Gateway** page once it is delivered.

### Pointing your domain name to the SSL Gateway

If you did not tick the DNS box, point your domain name or subdomain to the IP address of your SSL Gateway within 3 days after ordering: our email gives you the address. Without it, the order is cancelled.

:::info
**Case 1: your DNS zone is managed by the OVHcloud shared DNS servers**

- If your NIC handle is an *administrator* or *technical* contact of this DNS zone, modify the zone in the <ManagerLink console to="/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>.
- Otherwise, ask the person in charge of this DNS zone to modify it.

If necessary, refer to our guide [How to add a DNS A record for a domain name](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-a-record-creation).

:::

:::info
**Case 2: your DNS zone is not managed by the OVHcloud shared DNS servers**

Modify the IP address in your DNS zone in your DNS provider's interface, or on your own DNS server.

:::

From delivery, plain HTTP already flows through your SSL Gateway. HTTPS starts once your domain points to the SSL Gateway and its certificate is issued, usually within 15 minutes.

:::warning
A change to your DNS zone can take up to 24 hours to take effect, because Internet service providers keep DNS records in their caches.

:::
