## Objective

With the Content Delivery Network (CDN), you can optimise your website’s response times for all of your users. Whenever a user visits your website, a volume of traffic is generated. This uses a portion of your quota (a limit on the total volume of incoming and outgoing traffic for your CDN).

**This guide will provide detailed information on how to manage your CDN’s quota.**

## Requirements

- an [OVHcloud Content Delivery Network (CDN)](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/network/cdn/) solution

[[cpnav:network-cdn|en]]

## Instructions

### Order your quota.

{/* CP-STEPS-START:order-quota */}

When you place an order, it comes with a **1 TB** quota allowance for traffic. It is important to note that this quota is not renewed monthly with your solution. Once you have used up this quota allowance (regardless of duration), you will need to add more quota allowance.

The traffic you have left is shown in the **Overview** tab of your CDN, in the **Usage** section, under **Traffic volume**. If you need additional quota, click <code className="action">Add an add-on</code> in this section. In the **Add an add-on** window, select **Traffic volume** from the **Add-on** list, choose the amount of bandwidth from the **Option** list, tick **I have read and accept the terms and conditions of sale**, then click <code className="action">Add</code>. The payment page opens so that you can complete your order.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Overview tab of a CDN with the Usage section" src="/images/network/content-delivery-network-infrastructure/quota/overview-usage.png" loading="lazy" />

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Add an add-on window with a Traffic volume option" src="/images/network/content-delivery-network-infrastructure/quota/add-addon.png" loading="lazy" />

You can view the prices for quota allowance on our [website](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/network/cdn/).

You are automatically notified when you have **100 GB** of quota remaining, so that you have time to order more bandwidth in advance. If you run out of quota allowance, the `bypass` is automatically enabled until you order more quota.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:order-quota */}

### Traffic billed on the CDN solution.

:::info
You are billed for all outgoing traffic on your CDN.  

:::

{/* CP-STEPS-START:traffic-example */}

Your CDN's traffic is made up of two types of requests, which you can follow in the <code className="action">Statistics</code> tab (**Bandwidth** and **Cache performance**):

- **Files that have already been cached**: the CDN responds directly to requests for these files.

- **Files called from the CDN's back end**: the cache rules applied to your domain, managed in the <code className="action">Cache Rules</code> tab, determine which files are called from the back end, and which files are cached.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:traffic-example */}

Whether the files are stored in the cache or called from the back-end through the CDN, **the traffic generated will be subtracted from your quota allowance in both instances.** For this reason, we recommend creating a specific subdomain that will be called for files to be cached, and keeping the domains pointed to your CDN’s back-end for all other files.
