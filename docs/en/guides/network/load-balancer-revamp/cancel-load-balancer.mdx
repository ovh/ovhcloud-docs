## Objective

Cancelling ends your Load Balancer service: either immediately, or when its current period expires, depending on how you cancel it.

**This guide explains how to cancel an OVHcloud Load Balancer from the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink> or with the OVHcloud API.**

:::danger
Cancelling a Load Balancer is irreversible once the termination is confirmed. When the service is terminated, its IP address, frontends, server farms, health probes, routes, and SSL certificates are permanently deleted. Any traffic still pointing at the Load Balancer IP address will stop being distributed to your backend servers. Make sure you have redirected production traffic elsewhere before you proceed.
:::

## Requirements

- An active <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud account</ManagerLink>
- An existing OVHcloud Load Balancer service in your account
- The administrative rights required to cancel a service

## Introduction

How a cancellation takes effect depends on the path you use in the OVHcloud Console. Terminating the Load Balancer from the **Subscription** section of its <code className="action">Overview</code> tab is immediate and irreversible. Cancelling the subscription from the **Manage my services** page shows the expiry date of the service. In both cases the configuration is removed when the service is terminated, so plan your traffic migration in advance.

Before cancelling, confirm that no DNS records, applications, or upstream services still rely on the Load Balancer IP address. For background on how the service is structured, see the [What is OVHcloud Load Balancer](/guides/network/load-balancer-revamp/what-is-load-balancer) guide.

## Instructions

You can cancel your Load Balancer from the OVHcloud Console or with the OVHcloud API. Choose the channel that matches your workflow.

{/* CP-STEPS-START:cancel-service */}
<Tabs>
<Tab label="Console">

The OVHcloud Console offers two ways to terminate a Load Balancer. Both start from the **Subscription** section of your Load Balancer's <code className="action">Overview</code> tab.

**From the Subscription section**

1. In the **Subscription** section, click <code className="action">Terminate</code>. The **Terminate the service** window opens.
2. In the **Confirmation** field, type `terminate`, then click <code className="action">Terminate</code>.

:::warning
This action is irreversible.
:::

**From the Manage my services page**

1. In the **Subscription** section, click <code className="action">Manage my subscription</code>. The **Manage my services** page opens in a new browser tab.
2. On the row of your Load Balancer (type **Load balancing IP**), click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button, then select <code className="action">Cancel my subscription</code>. The **Terminate service** window opens.
3. Answer the two mandatory questions: the reason for the termination, and **What do you plan to do?**
4. Click <code className="action">Terminate</code> to confirm.

:::warning
You will lose all data associated with this service.
:::

</Tab>
<Tab label="OVHcloud API">

Cancelling a service with the OVHcloud API is a two-step flow: you request the termination, then confirm it with the token you receive.

1. Request the termination of the service. This call returns a confirmation token, usually sent by email.

<Api version="v1" section="/ipLoadbalancing" method="POST" route={"/ipLoadbalancing/\{serviceName\}/confirmTermination"} />

| Parameter | Required | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| `serviceName` | Yes | The unique identifier of your Load Balancer service. | `loadbalancer-a1b2c3d4` |
| `token` (body) | Yes | The confirmation token received after requesting the termination. | `xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx` |
| `reason` (body) | No | The reason for the cancellation, used for feedback. | `MIGRATED_TO_ANOTHER_OVH_PRODUCT` |

2. Confirm the termination by submitting the token with the call above. Once the call succeeds, the termination is scheduled according to the service expiration settings.

:::info
To review or adjust when the service stops renewing, inspect the service information with `GET /ipLoadbalancing/{serviceName}/serviceInfos`.
:::

</Tab>
<Tab label="Terraform">

Service cancellation is generally handled outside Terraform. Removing the `ovh_iploadbalancing` resource from your configuration and running `terraform destroy` deletes the managed resources but does not necessarily cancel the underlying service subscription. Cancel the service from the OVHcloud Console or with the OVHcloud API to stop renewal.

</Tab>
</Tabs>
{/* CP-STEPS-END:cancel-service */}

### Verify the cancellation

After confirming, verify that the cancellation was recorded.

{/* CP-STEPS-START:verify-cancellation */}
- In the OVHcloud Console, open your Load Balancer and, in the **Subscription** section of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, review the **Renewal mode** and **Renewal date** of the service.
- With the API, call `GET /ipLoadbalancing/{serviceName}/serviceInfos` and check the expiration and renewal fields.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:verify-cancellation */}

:::warning
Until the termination date is reached, traffic continues to be distributed if the Load Balancer is still configured. Redirect your production traffic away from the Load Balancer IP address before the service is terminated to avoid an outage.
:::
