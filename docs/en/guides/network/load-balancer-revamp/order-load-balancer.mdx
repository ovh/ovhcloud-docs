## Objective

The OVHcloud Load Balancer distributes incoming traffic across several backend servers to improve the availability and scalability of your applications. Before you can configure frontends, server farms, and probes, you must order a Load Balancer service and select its size and location.

**This guide explains how to order an OVHcloud Load Balancer from the OVHcloud Console, then how to locate the new service once it is delivered.**

## Requirements

- An active <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud account</ManagerLink>
- A clear understanding of the size and availability zone you need — see [Availability and zones](/guides/network/load-balancer-revamp/availability-and-zones)
- Reviewed the [prerequisites and limitations](/guides/network/load-balancer-revamp/prerequisites-and-limitations) for the service

## Instructions

This section walks you through the order in the OVHcloud Console: starting it, choosing the size and zone, confirming, and finding the delivered service.

### Step 1 - Start the order

{/* CP-STEPS-START:start-order */}
In the OVHcloud Console, the **Load Balancer** page lets you start a new order. In the top bar, select <code className="action">Bare Metal Cloud</code>, open <code className="action">Network</code> > <code className="action">Load Balancer</code> in the left menu, then click <code className="action">Order a Load Balancer</code> to open the order page.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:start-order */}

### Step 2 - Choose the size and availability zone

{/* CP-STEPS-START:choose-size-zone */}
The order page guides you through configuring your new service. Select the options that match your needs, then click <code className="action">Continue</code>.

| Option | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Choose your pack | Determines the capacity of the Load Balancer. **Load Balancer - Pack 1** and **Load Balancer - Pack 2** differ by included traffic, number of farms and frontends, SLA, and vRack support (Pack 2). Choose the pack that matches your expected traffic. |
| Region | Determines where the Load Balancer is deployed: choose the region from the **Region** drop-down list. For some regions, the entry also names the zone. A single-zone deployment runs in one zone; a multi-zone deployment spreads the service across several zones for higher resilience. Once the service is delivered, you can add zones with <code className="action">Add a zone</code> in the **General information** section of its <code className="action">Overview</code> tab. |

For guidance on choosing between a single-zone and a multi-zone deployment, refer to [Availability and zones](/guides/network/load-balancer-revamp/availability-and-zones) and [High availability](/guides/network/load-balancer-revamp/high-availability).

:::info
The exact packs and pricing are presented on the order page.
:::
{/* CP-STEPS-END:choose-size-zone */}

### Step 3 - Confirm and pay for the order

{/* CP-STEPS-START:confirm-order */}
Review your configuration, then complete the order.

1. In the summary panel of the order page, verify the selected product, the **Commitment** period and the total.
2. Click <code className="action">Continue order</code>.
3. Accept the contracts and conditions, then confirm the order and complete the payment.

After payment, the service is provisioned. Delivery is not always immediate.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:confirm-order */}

:::warning
The Load Balancer is billed as a service. Cancelling it later follows the cancellation flow described in [Cancel a Load Balancer](/guides/network/load-balancer-revamp/cancel-load-balancer).
:::

### Step 4 - Locate your new service

{/* CP-STEPS-START:locate-service */}
Once the order is delivered, the Load Balancer appears in the OVHcloud Console.

1. Log in to the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>.
2. In the top bar, select <code className="action">Bare Metal Cloud</code>, then open <code className="action">Network</code> > <code className="action">Load Balancer</code> in the left menu.
3. Select your service from the **Load Balancer** list. It opens on its <code className="action">Overview</code> tab.

From the service page, you can manage the configuration through the <code className="action">Overview</code>, <code className="action">Frontends</code>, <code className="action">Server farms</code>, <code className="action">SSL</code>, <code className="action">Network</code>, <code className="action">Logs</code>, <code className="action">Statistics</code>, <code className="action">Operations</code> and <code className="action">Quotas</code> tabs.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:locate-service */}

To confirm the service is present and read its properties, you can also list your Load Balancer services through the OVHcloud API.

<Api version="v1" section="/ipLoadbalancing" method="GET" route="/ipLoadbalancing" />

This call returns the service names of every Load Balancer on your account. Use a service name with the following call to read the properties of a specific service.

<Api version="v1" section="/ipLoadbalancing" method="GET" route={"/ipLoadbalancing/\{serviceName\}"} />

| Parameter | Required | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| `serviceName` | Yes | The unique identifier of your Load Balancer service. | `loadbalancer-abcdef1234567890` |

The OVHcloud API cannot order a Load Balancer with a single call. Ordering through the API uses the order and cart endpoints, in this order:

<Api version="v1" section="/order" method="POST" route="/order/cart" />

<Api version="v1" section="/order" method="POST" route={"/order/cart/\{cartId\}/assign"} />

<Api version="v1" section="/order" method="POST" route={"/order/cart/\{cartId\}/ipLoadbalancing"} />

<Api version="v1" section="/order" method="POST" route={"/order/cart/\{cartId\}/ipLoadbalancing/options"} />

<Api version="v1" section="/order" method="POST" route={"/order/cart/\{cartId\}/checkout"} />

To order with infrastructure-as-code instead, use the `ovh_iploadbalancing` resource of the OVHcloud Terraform provider.
