## Objective

Layer 2 (L2) mode extends your network to OVHcloud at the **data-link layer**: your vRack is bridged to your equipment "as-is", with no routing involved. Because the link is transparent to VLANs (802.1q), L2 is the simplest way to interconnect an existing network with an OVHcloud vRack — well suited to "lift & shift" migrations without IP changes, or to applications that need Layer 2 adjacency.

L2 is available on **OVHcloud Connect Direct only** (OVHcloud Connect Provider is Layer 3 only). It is a **strictly point-to-point** service: one PoP to one availability zone, with only one OVHcloud Connect L2 per vRack.

## Overview

For the complete L2 service description — how Ethernet frames are forwarded, VLAN transparency, topology rules and redundancy — see [Introduction to OVHcloud Connect](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/introduction#layer-2-l2-service). For the full list of L2 capabilities and unsupported features, see [Prerequisites and limitations](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/limits).

This guide covers the configuration itself.

## Configure the OVHcloud side

You can configure the OVHcloud side in L2 mode either from the **OVHcloud Control Panel** or with the **OVHcloud API**. Both methods achieve the same result. Afterwards, extend your VLANs over the link from your own equipment.

:::warning
Switching a PoP configuration between L2 and L3 requires deleting the whole configuration. Choose the mode carefully before you start.
:::

### Using the OVHcloud Control Panel

Open your solution from the <code className="action">Bare Metal Cloud</code> > <code className="action">Network</code> > <code className="action">OVHcloud Connect</code> section, then:

1. **Associate a vRack** (if not already done): click <code className="action">Attach a vRack</code> and select an existing vRack.
2. **Add a PoP configuration**: in the `PoP configuration` table, click <code className="action">Configure port</code> to open the **Add a PoP configuration** form, set the **Type** to **L2**, and confirm.
3. **Add an availability zone (AZ) configuration**: under the `AZ configuration` section, click <code className="action">Add an AZ</code>, select an availability zone, and confirm.

L2 mode has no routing configuration — that applies to L3 only.

### Using the OVHcloud API

#### Step 1 — Identify your interface ID

<Api version="v1" section="/ovhCloudConnect" method="GET" route={"/ovhCloudConnect/\{serviceName\}/interface"} />

#### Step 2 — Create the PoP configuration (L2)

<Api version="v1" section="/ovhCloudConnect" method="POST" route={"/ovhCloudConnect/\{serviceName\}/config/pop"} />

Provide the `interfaceId` obtained above and set `type` to `l2`.

#### Step 3 — Add the availability zone (datacenter) configuration

For L2, only the availability zone (datacenter) ID is required.

<Api version="v1" section="/ovhCloudConnect" method="POST" route={"/ovhCloudConnect/\{serviceName\}/config/pop/\{popId\}/datacenter"} />

Provide the `datacenterId` of the availability zone you want to reach.

## Configure your equipment

On your side, extend the VLANs you want to reach OVHcloud over the OVHcloud Connect link — no routing is required. If you use two links to the same PoP for redundancy, configure **LACP** to aggregate them.

Auto-negotiation is not supported on the OVHcloud Connect link: disable it on your interface and force the port speed to match the ordered link.

## Verify

- Confirm the port shows as **up** in the OVHcloud Control Panel.
- Confirm your services in the vRack can reach your on-premises network over the expected VLANs.
- For a Layer 2 diagnostic, retrieve the list of MAC addresses seen on the service — see [Monitor your OVHcloud Connect](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/monitor).
