## Objective

OVHcloud Connect Edge provides a fully managed, private connection from a customer site to the OVHcloud services attached to your vRack, including the last-mile access. This guide walks you through ordering the service and connecting your site.

**In this guide, you will learn how to:**

- Check whether your site is eligible for OVHcloud Connect Edge.
- Order the service and choose your connection options.
- Have the line installed and, optionally, receive a managed device (uCPE).
- Connect your site and verify connectivity to your OVHcloud services.

## Requirements

- An OVHcloud account.
- An OVHcloud vRack to which your services are attached.
- A site located in an area eligible for OVHcloud Connect Edge.
- Access to the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Control Panel</ManagerLink>.

### Step 3 — Line installation and device delivery

Once the order is validated, OVHcloud arranges the installation of the last-mile access at your site. OVHcloud aims to set up the link within **12 weeks** of order validation (best efforts). Installation takes place during an appointment scheduled in business hours: you must be present (or represented) and provide access to the premises, along with any authorisations needed for the cabling work.

At the appointment, the fibre and the terminating equipment are installed and the optical link is checked. If you ordered a uCPE, it is delivered to you separately; you install it yourself, following the OVHcloud documentation.

### Step 4 — Connect your site

Connect your site to OVHcloud Connect Edge.

- **With the uCPE:** plug the device into your network. It is pre-configured and establishes the connection to OVHcloud automatically, with no BGP configuration required on your side.
- **Without the uCPE:** configure a BGP session on your own router towards OVHcloud, over the /30 subnet provided, using your ASN and the OVHcloud ASN.
