## General

### What is OVHcloud Connect?

OVHcloud Connect (OCC) is a dedicated, private network connection between your infrastructure and OVHcloud. It bypasses the public internet, providing **lower latency, higher bandwidth, and improved security** compared to VPN or internet-based connections.

See [What is OVHcloud Connect?](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/introduction) for a full overview.

### What is the difference between Direct and Provider?

| | OVHcloud Connect Direct | OVHcloud Connect Provider |
|---|---|---|
| Physical connection | You manage the cross-connect at the PoP | Provider manages the last-mile connection |
| Speed | 1, 10 or 100 Gbps | 50 Mbps to 50 Gbps |
| Lead time | Depends on the cross-connect — delivered on light detection, or 60 days after the order | As soon as the service key is provisioned by your provider |
| Best for | Organisations with colocation presence | Multi-cloud, remote sites, faster deployment |

Delivery times are indicative and **non-guaranteed** (per the OVHcloud Connect contract).

### Which providers are supported?

OVHcloud Connect Provider works with several third-party network providers — see the [Providers](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/providers) guide for the current list.

### Where is OVHcloud Connect available?

OVHcloud Connect is available in major PoP locations across Europe, North America, and Asia-Pacific. See [PoP locations and regions](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/pop-locations-regions) for the full list.

### Can OVHcloud host my router?

No. OVHcloud does not host customer networking equipment. To use OVHcloud Connect Direct, you must have your **own rack space (a bay)** in a data centre where OVHcloud has a point of presence, with your router installed and operated by you. You can then order an OVHcloud Connect Direct service and a cross-connect between your bay and the OVHcloud PoP within that facility.

If you do not have a presence in an OVHcloud PoP, use [OVHcloud Connect Provider](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/order-provider) instead — your provider handles the last-mile connectivity on your behalf.

## Ordering & billing

### How do I order OVHcloud Connect?

- **Direct**: [Order a Direct connection](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/order-direct)
- **Provider**: [Order a Provider connection](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/order-provider)

You can order through the OVHcloud Control Panel, the API, or Terraform.

### How long before my OVHcloud Connect service is delivered?

**Direct:**

- OVHcloud provides the **Letter of Authorization (LOA)** after the order (delivery times are objectives, not guarantees).
- You then have **60 days** to finalise the interconnection: order the cross-connect from your data centre operator, and physically connect your equipment to the OVHcloud patch panel.
- The service is delivered when OVHcloud detects light on the port, or 60 days after the order even if no light is detected (the service is then considered operational and **billing starts**), or by manual arrangement with an OVHcloud agent.

**Provider:**

- OVHcloud generates a **service key** immediately after the order.
- The service is delivered as soon as your provider consumes the service key and provisions the virtual circuit — typically within **minutes to hours** for on-demand providers.

### How is OVHcloud Connect billed?

OVHcloud Connect is billed monthly based on:

- **Connection type** (Direct or Provider)
- **Bandwidth** (Direct: 1, 10 or 100 Gbps; Provider: 50 Mbps to 50 Gbps)
- **Number of services**

Provider connections may also incur charges from your provider. Check the [OVHcloud pricing page](/links/network/ovhcloud-connect) for current rates.

### Can I change the bandwidth of my connection?

Yes. You can change the bandwidth from the OVHcloud Control Panel using the <code className="action">Change bandwidth</code> action on the PoP configuration. Note that for a **Direct** connection, moving to a different physical port speed (for example 1 Gbps to 10 Gbps) may still require new optics and a new cross-connect. Plan any change during a maintenance window to limit disruption.

### How do I cancel a service?

- **Direct**: [Cancel a Direct connection](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/cancel-direct)
- **Provider**: [Cancel a Provider connection](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/cancel-provider)

:::warning
**Important:** Always remove the provider virtual circuit and BGP configuration before cancelling the OVHcloud service to avoid routing issues.
:::

## Technical

### Which BGP ASN can I use?

You can use any BGP ASN, except the values reserved by OVHcloud per region: **65501** (Europe), **65502** (Canada), or **65519** (Asia). Your side and the OVHcloud-side routing instance must use different ASNs — for example, AS35540 is a valid choice. Do not use the reserved region values as your ASN (a private ASN in the 64512–65534 range is a common choice for your own side).

### What is the maximum number of BGP prefixes?

OVHcloud supports a maximum of **100 prefixes** per BGP session. Aggregate your routes to stay within this limit.

### Does OVHcloud Connect support IPv6?

No. IPv6 is **not supported** on OVHcloud Connect L3. Use IPv4 for your BGP peering and routed traffic.

### What MTU is supported?

Jumbo frames up to **9000 bytes** are supported. Make sure the MTU is configured consistently on both sides of the connection.

### Can I use OVHcloud Connect with Hosted Private Cloud (VMware)?

Yes. Associate your OVHcloud Connect service with a vRack, then add your Hosted Private Cloud service to the same vRack. VMware VMs can use the private connection via vRack-attached port groups.

### Can I use OVHcloud Connect with Public Cloud instances?

Yes. Attach your Public Cloud project to the same vRack as your OVHcloud Connect service. Assign a vRack private network to your instances to route traffic through the OCC link.

### What happens if my BGP session goes down?

If you have a single OVHcloud Connect link, traffic between your network and OVHcloud will stop flowing until BGP is re-established. For production workloads, always use a [resilient architecture](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/multi-az) with two links and BGP failover.

### Can I use BFD (Bidirectional Forwarding Detection)?

Yes. BFD is **enabled by default** on all BGP sessions. It significantly reduces failover times to under 1 second. Make sure BFD is also enabled and correctly configured on your own router.

## Connecting to other clouds

### Can I use OVHcloud Connect to reach other public clouds?

OVHcloud Connect links your network to your OVHcloud vRack. Connectivity to other clouds (such as AWS, Azure, or GCP) is handled by third-party providers and is out of scope for OVHcloud Connect. Refer to the relevant provider's documentation for their own interconnection products.

### Do I need a separate vRack for each OVHcloud Connect service?

No. You can associate multiple OVHcloud Connect services with the **same vRack**, allowing your connected networks to communicate through the shared vRack.

## Resilience & SLA

### What SLA does OVHcloud Connect offer?

The availability SLA depends on your configuration:

| Configuration | Monthly availability SLA |
|---|---|
| 1 connection on 1 PoP | No commitment |
| 2 connections on 1 PoP (Local redundant connection) | No commitment |
| 2 connections on 2 PoPs (High Availability – Level 1) | 99.9% |
| 4 connections on 2 PoPs to 2 datacentres (High Availability – Level 2) | 99.99% |

The SLA covers the PoP-to-Datacenter path only and excludes the third-party Virtual Circuit (Provider). See [SLAs](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/slas) for full details and conditions.

### How do I set up a resilient connection?

Order two OVHcloud Connect services at **different PoPs**, associate both with the same vRack, and configure BGP failover with Local Preference and AS-path prepending.

Tutorials:

- [On-premises resilient](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/resilient-architecture)
- [AWS resilient](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/resilient-architecture)
- [Azure resilient](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/resilient-architecture)
- [GCP resilient](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/resilient-architecture)

### How long does BGP failover take?

BFD is enabled by default on all BGP sessions and brings failover down to **under 1 second**. Make sure BFD is also enabled on your own router so both sides detect the failure quickly.

## Troubleshooting

### My connection is ordered but not active

| Possible cause | Action |
|---|---|
| Cross-connect not completed (Direct) | Contact your colocation provider; check LOA status |
| Provider virtual circuit not provisioned (Provider) | Check provider portal; ensure the service key is correct |
| BGP not configured | [Configure BGP](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/l3-bgp) |
| vRack not associated | [Associate vRack](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/associate-vrack) |

### I can see the BGP session but have no reachability

- Verify that **routes are being exchanged** (on your router or via the OVHcloud API).
- Ensure **subnets are correct** and non-overlapping.
- Check for **firewall rules** blocking traffic on either side.
- Verify **VLAN tagging** matches between your equipment and OVHcloud.

### Traffic is slow or experiencing packet loss

- Check interface errors and CRC counts on your router.
- Verify the **bandwidth** of your OVHcloud Connect matches your traffic load.
- Look for **MTU mismatches** causing fragmentation.
- Use the [monitoring guide](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/monitor) to check link health.

### How do I open a support ticket?

1. Log in to the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Control Panel</ManagerLink> and open the **Help Centre** (linked from your dashboard).
2. Start a new support request.
3. Select your **OVHcloud Connect** service as the affected service.
4. Include your service name, error details, and any diagnostic output.

See [Incident follow-up](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/incident-followup) for detailed escalation procedures.

## Automation

### Can I manage OVHcloud Connect with Terraform?

Yes. Use a recent version of the [OVHcloud Terraform provider](https://registry.terraform.io/providers/ovh/ovh/latest) to manage OVHcloud Connect resources. See [Automation](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/automation) for examples.

### Is there an API for OVHcloud Connect?

Yes. The OVHcloud API provides full lifecycle management:

- **EU endpoint**: `https://eu.api.ovh.com/v1/ovhCloudConnect/`
- **CA endpoint**: `https://ca.api.ovh.com/v1/ovhCloudConnect/`

Explore the API at [api.ovh.com/console](https://api.ovh.com/console/#/ovhCloudConnect).

See [Automation](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/automation) for SDK and API examples.
