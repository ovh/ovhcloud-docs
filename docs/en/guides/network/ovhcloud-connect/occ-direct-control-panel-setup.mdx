## Objective

OVHcloud Connect Direct is the self-managed option: you own the physical cross-connect at an OVHcloud PoP and manage both Layer 2 and Layer 3 yourself, without a third-party provider. Choose it when your equipment (or a circuit) already reaches a data centre where OVHcloud has a PoP. This quick start covers the end-to-end path — from ordering to a configured link — so that by the end you have a private connection carrying traffic to your OVHcloud resources.

## Before you begin

Make sure you have:

- ✅ An **OVHcloud account** with billing configured
- ✅ Equipment (or a circuit) in a **data centre where OVHcloud has a PoP** (see PoP Locations{/* TODO(occ-revamp): restore this link before the final merge, once the revamp guide lands → [PoP Locations](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/pop-locations-regions) */})
- ✅ A **device** with single-mode fibre optic interfaces
- ✅ For **Layer 3**: a planned **IP addressing scheme** (peering IPs and prefixes) and a public or private **ASN**
- ✅ For **Layer 2**: the **VLANs** you want to extend to OVHcloud (no ASN or IP plan required)

## Steps overview

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Overview of the steps to set up OVHcloud Connect Direct from the OVHcloud Console" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel-setup/fig-1.svg" loading="lazy" />

### Step 1 — Order OVHcloud Connect Direct

{/* CP-STEPS-START:order-occ-direct */}
1. On the OVHcloud Connect page of the OVHcloud Console, click <code className="action">Order an OVHcloud Connect</code>. The OVHcloud Connect product page opens on the OVHcloud website, in a new tab.
2. Under **OVHcloud Connect Direct**, click <code className="action">Order now</code>.
3. On the **Configure your OVHcloud Connect Direct** page, choose:
    - the **Bandwidth**: 1, 10 or 100 Gbit/s guaranteed;
    - the **Number of ports**: 1 or 2;
    - the **Point of presence**: select its region (Europe, North America or Asia/Oceania), then your PoP;
    - the **Commitment period**: no commitment, 12 months or 24 months.
4. Click <code className="action">Continue order</code>, then complete your order. Keep your technical details at hand: your ASN, contact information, and any special instructions for the data centre.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:order-occ-direct */}

For detailed ordering steps, see Order OVHcloud Connect Direct{/* TODO(occ-revamp): restore this link before the final merge, once the revamp guide lands → [Order OVHcloud Connect Direct](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/order-direct) */}.

### Step 2 — Receive the LOA

After your order is validated, OVHcloud sends you a **Letter of Authorization (LOA)** by email. This document contains:

- The data centre and rack/cage reference for OVHcloud's equipment
- The port designation on OVHcloud's side
- Instructions for the data centre operator

### Step 3 — Install the physical cross-connect

Give the LOA to your **data centre operator** (or handle it yourself if you manage your own cage). They will install a fibre-optic patch cable between your router port and OVHcloud's designated port.

This step may take a few days depending on the data centre's processes.

### Step 4 — Associate with your vRack

Link your OVHcloud Connect service to your **vRack** so that your OVHcloud resources (servers, VMs, etc.) can communicate over the private connection.

See Associate an OVHcloud Connect to your vRack{/* TODO(occ-revamp): restore this link before the final merge, once the revamp guide lands → [Associate an OVHcloud Connect to your vRack](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/associate-vrack) */}.

### Step 5 — Configure your service in the OVHcloud Console

{/* CP-STEPS-START:configure-service */}
Once the physical link is up, open your service in the OVHcloud Console: select <code className="action">Bare Metal Cloud</code> (or <code className="action">Private Cloud</code>) in the top bar, go to <code className="action">Network</code> > <code className="action">OVHcloud Connect</code>, then select your service in the list. You configure it from the <code className="action">Configure</code> tab.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:configure-service */}

#### Associate a vRack

{/* CP-STEPS-START:associate-a-vrack */}
If you have not done so already, attach a vRack: on the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, click <code className="action">Attach a vRack</code> in the **No vRack attached** message, or click <code className="action">Attach</code> next to **vRack** in the **Settings** section. In the **Attach a vRack** window, select an existing vRack from the drop-down list, then click <code className="action">Attach</code>. The **vRack attachment in progress** message then shows on the service page: attaching the vRack can take a few minutes.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:associate-a-vrack */}

#### Add a PoP configuration

{/* CP-STEPS-START:add-a-pop-configuration */}
In the <code className="action">Configure</code> tab, go to the **PoP Configuration** section. On the row of your port, click <code className="action">Configure</code> to open the **Add PoP configuration** window, then set the **Layer type**:

- **L2** — select **L2** (selected by default) and click <code className="action">Add</code>. This is a transparent point-to-point link (one PoP, one availability zone).

- **L3** — select **L3**, enter the following information, then click <code className="action">Add</code>:

| Information | Description |
|---|---|
| Customer ASN | Your BGP AS number, configured on your router located in the PoP |
| OVHcloud ASN | The AS number configured on the OVHcloud Connect routers located in the PoP |
| Peering subnet (/30) | A /30 IPv4 block, used for the link between your router and the OVHcloud router in the PoP |
{/* CP-STEPS-END:add-a-pop-configuration */}

#### Add an availability zone (AZ) configuration

{/* CP-STEPS-START:add-an-az-configuration */}
In the **Availability Zone configuration** section, click <code className="action">Add AZ configuration</code>. In the **Add AZ configuration** window, select the **Datacentre** (the availability zone) and (for L3) enter:

| Information | Description |
|---|---|
| OVHcloud ASN | The AS number configured on the OVHcloud Connect routers in the AZ (may differ from the PoP ASN) |
| Subnet | A private subnetwork configured in your vRack in the selected AZ (an IPv4 block of /28 or larger) |

Click <code className="action">Add</code> to confirm. If your service has several PoP configurations, the window also asks you to choose the **PoP configuration**.

In L2, a service connects one PoP to a single availability zone: once one AZ is configured, <code className="action">Add AZ configuration</code> is disabled.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:add-an-az-configuration */}

#### Add a routing configuration

{/* CP-STEPS-START:add-a-routing-configuration */}
Next to the name of the availability zone, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button, then select <code className="action">Add a route</code> to add a routing configuration. If the zone has no route yet, you can also click <code className="action">Add a route</code> directly in its routing table. In the **Add a route** window, set the **Route type** to **BGP** or **Static**:

- **BGP** — enter the **Neighbour ASN** (your ASN) and the **Neighbor IP** (the BGP neighbour IP of your router in the AZ, within the AZ subnetwork).

- **Static** — enter the **Target subnet** (a prefix in CIDR notation) and the **Next hop** (a gateway IP within the subnetwork range).

Click <code className="action">Add</code> to confirm.

You can add several routing configurations in the same availability zone, with up to 20 static routes per data centre.

Then, on **your router**, configure the matching BGP session (or static routes) and advertise the prefixes you want to reach from OVHcloud. For detailed routing guidance, see Configure OCC L3 with BGP{/* TODO(occ-revamp): restore this link before the final merge, once the revamp guide lands → [Configure OCC L3 with BGP](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/l3-bgp) */} or Configure OCC L3 with static routes{/* TODO(occ-revamp): restore this link before the final merge, once the revamp guide lands → [Configure OCC L3 with static routes](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/l3-static) */}.

:::info
The routing configuration applies to **Layer 3** only. In **Layer 2** mode there is no routing configuration — once the L2 PoP configuration and the availability zone configuration are active, your VLANs are bridged transparently. See Configure OCC L2{/* TODO(occ-revamp): restore this link before the final merge, once the revamp guide lands → [Configure OCC L2](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/l2) */}.
:::
{/* CP-STEPS-END:add-a-routing-configuration */}

### Step 6 — Test and verify

Run these checks to confirm everything is working:

{/* CP-STEPS-START:test-and-verify */}
| Check | How |
|---|---|
| **Physical link** | In the OVHcloud Console, confirm that the **Link status** column of the **PoP Configuration** section (<code className="action">Configure</code> tab) shows **Up**, and that the port is up on your router. |
| **BGP session** | In the OVHcloud Console, confirm that the **BGP status** column of the **PoP Configuration** section shows **Up**, and that the session is **Established** on your router. |
| **Route exchange** | Confirm routes from OVHcloud appear in your routing table and vice versa. |
| **Connectivity** | Ping a known OVHcloud resource (e.g. a VM in your vRack) from your on-premises network. |
| **Traceroute** | Run `traceroute` to confirm traffic takes the private path (not the Internet). |
{/* CP-STEPS-END:test-and-verify */}

The **BGP session** and **route exchange** checks apply to Layer 3. For **Layer 2**, confirm the port is up and that your VLANs reach your OVHcloud resources (you can also run a MAC-address diagnostic — see [Monitor](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-monitor)).

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | What to check |
|---|---|
| Port shows "down" | Physical cable, SFP/transceiver compatibility, data centre cross-connect status |
| BGP session stuck in "Active" or "Idle" | Peer IP addresses, ASN configuration, firewall rules (TCP port 179) |
| No routes exchanged | BGP prefix filters, route-map configuration, maximum-prefix limits |
| Cannot ping OVHcloud resources | vRack association, VLAN ID, subnet configuration in AZ |

If you cannot resolve the issue, open a support ticket{/* TODO(occ-revamp): restore this link before the final merge, once the revamp guide lands → [open a support ticket](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/incident-followup) */} with your BGP output, interface status, and traceroute results.
