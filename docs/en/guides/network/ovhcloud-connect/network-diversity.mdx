## Objective

Network diversity lets you connect your OVHcloud Connect links to two separate physical devices within the same Point of Presence (PoP). By placing two connections on different devices, you avoid any single point of failure at the PoP.

**This guide explains how to choose the sector of an OVHcloud Connect service when you order it, and how to build a redundant pair of connections.**

## Requirements

- An active OVHcloud account
- A [vRack](/links/network/vrack) to attach your OVHcloud Connect services to
- For physical redundancy: two OVHcloud Connect services ordered on the same PoP

[[cpnav:network-ovhcloud-connect]]

## Introduction

{/* DRAFT: To verify — confirm with the product team that a sector is a group of devices (the order page says "two separate physical devices within the same PoP") */}

Each OVHcloud PoP where network diversity is available is split into two groups of physical network devices, called **sectors**: Sector A and Sector B. When you order an OVHcloud Connect service, you can choose which sector it connects to.

- **One connection:** you can leave the choice to OVHcloud.
- **Two connections on the same PoP:** place one on Sector A and the other on Sector B. If a device fails in one sector, the connection on the other sector keeps carrying your traffic.

Network diversity is available for OVHcloud Connect **Provider** and OVHcloud Connect **Direct**, at no extra cost on most PoPs.

## Instructions

This section shows how to select a sector during the order, then how to check the sector of an existing service.

### Step 1: Select a sector when ordering

The sector is chosen in the **Diversity** section of the OVHcloud Connect order page.

1. Go to the [OVHcloud Connect page](/links/network/ovhcloud-connect) and click the order button for **OVHcloud Connect Provider** or **OVHcloud Connect Direct**.
2. Select the provider (Provider only), the bandwidth and, for Direct, the number of ports.
3. Select a **Point of Presence**. The **Diversity** section appears once a PoP is selected.
4. In the **Diversity** section, select one of the following options:

| Option | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| <code className="action">No preference</code> | Selected by default. OVHcloud assigns the sector automatically. |
| <code className="action">Sector A</code> | The connection is placed on Sector A. Ideal for physical redundancy with an OVHcloud Connect service on Sector B. |
| <code className="action">Sector B</code> | The connection is placed on Sector B. Ideal for physical redundancy with an OVHcloud Connect service on Sector A. |

5. Check the order summary: the **Diversity** line shows the option you selected.
6. Select a commitment period, then click <code className="action">Continue order</code> to finalise the order.

{/* DRAFT: To screenshot — Diversity section of the Provider configurator (PoP PA3 selected, the three options and the order summary) */}

> [!INFO]
> If the **Diversity** section does not appear after you select a PoP, sector selection is not available for this offer on this PoP. The sector is then assigned automatically.

:::warning
The sector cannot be changed from the OVHcloud Control Panel after the order. Changing the sector of an existing connection is still in development. Choose the sector carefully when you order.
:::

### Step 2: Build a redundant pair of connections

To protect your connectivity against the failure of a single device, order two OVHcloud Connect services on the same PoP:

1. Order the first service and select <code className="action">Sector A</code> in the **Diversity** section.
2. Order the second service on the **same PoP** and select <code className="action">Sector B</code>.

Each service is then configured independently: attach it to your vRack and add its PoP configuration as described in our guides for [OVHcloud Connect Provider](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-provider-control-panel) and [OVHcloud Connect Direct](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-direct-control-panel).

> [!TIP]
> With OVHcloud Connect Provider, the two connections also have to reach two distinct infrastructures on the provider side. Check with your provider how to place your two virtual circuits accordingly. [UNVERIFIED: product team]

### Step 3: Check the sector of a service

The sector of each service is displayed in the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Control Panel</ManagerLink>.

1. Open the **OVHcloud Connect** section and click the name of your service.
2. In the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, the **Settings** card shows the **Sector** of the service:

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `A` | The service is connected to Sector A. |
| `B` | The service is connected to Sector B. |
| `A/B` | The service has two ports (OVHcloud Connect Direct), one connected to each sector. |
