## Objective

OVHcloud Connect Provider is the provider-managed option: a third-party network provider builds the physical link to an OVHcloud PoP on your behalf, so you do not need to be present in the same data centre as OVHcloud. It is always a Layer 3 service. This quick start covers the end-to-end path — from ordering to a configured link — so that by the end you have a connection carrying traffic to your OVHcloud resources.

## Before you begin

Make sure you have:

- ✅ An **OVHcloud account** with billing configured
- ✅ A contract or account with a **supported provider** (see Providers{/* TODO(occ-revamp): restore this link before the final merge, once the revamp guide lands → [Providers](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/providers) */})
- ✅ A **router** that supports BGP peering (OVHcloud Connect Provider is always a Layer 3 service)
- ✅ A planned **IP addressing scheme** and **ASN**

## Steps overview

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Overview of the steps to set up OVHcloud Connect Provider from the OVHcloud Console" src="/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-provider-control-panel-setup/fig-1.svg" loading="lazy" />

### Step 1 — Order OVHcloud Connect Provider

{/* CP-STEPS-START:order-occ-provider */}
1. On the OVHcloud Connect page of the OVHcloud Console, click <code className="action">Order an OVHcloud Connect</code>. The OVHcloud Connect product page opens on the OVHcloud website, in a new tab.
2. Under **OVHcloud Connect Provider**, click <code className="action">Order now</code>.
3. In the **Configure your OVHcloud Connect Provider** step, choose your **provider**, the guaranteed **bandwidth** (from 50 Mbit/s to 5 Gbit/s) and the **Point of Presence** (the PoPs offered depend on the provider), then click <code className="action">Continue order</code>.
4. Review pricing and complete the order.
5. OVHcloud generates a **service key** for your provider. Once your service is listed in the OVHcloud Console, you can find this key on its <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, in the **Settings** tile, under **Service Key** (**No service key generated** is shown while there is none; click <code className="action">Regenerate</code> to generate a new one).
{/* CP-STEPS-END:order-occ-provider */}

For detailed steps, see Order OVHcloud Connect Provider{/* TODO(occ-revamp): restore this link before the final merge, once the revamp guide lands → [Order OVHcloud Connect Provider](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/order-provider) */}.

### Step 2 — Share the service key

Give the **service key** to your provider. This key allows the provider to identify and activate the connection to OVHcloud on your behalf.

In your provider's portal, create the connection to OVHcloud and enter the service key. See the Providers{/* TODO(occ-revamp): restore this link before the final merge, once the revamp guide lands → [Providers](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/providers) */} guide.

### Step 3 — Provider provisions the connection

The provider sets up the physical and logical link between their infrastructure and OVHcloud's PoP. This is usually fast (minutes to hours for on-demand providers), but timing varies.

You can track the connection status in both the **OVHcloud Console** and your **provider's portal**.

### Step 4 — Configure your service in the OVHcloud Console

{/* CP-STEPS-START:configure-service */}
Once your service shows as "Active" in the **Status** column of the OVHcloud Connect list, open it in the OVHcloud Console: select <code className="action">Bare Metal Cloud</code> (or <code className="action">Private Cloud</code>) in the top bar, go to <code className="action">Network</code> > <code className="action">OVHcloud Connect</code>, then select your service. The service opens on its <code className="action">Overview</code> tab; you configure it from the <code className="action">Configure</code> tab. OVHcloud Connect Provider is always a **Layer 3** service.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:configure-service */}

#### Associate a vRack

{/* CP-STEPS-START:associate-a-vrack */}
On the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, click <code className="action">Attach a vRack</code> in the **No vRack attached** message (or, in the **Settings** tile, click <code className="action">Attach</code> next to **vRack**). In the **Attach a vRack** window, select an existing vRack from the drop-down list, then click <code className="action">Attach</code>. The **vRack attachment in progress** message then shows on the service page: attaching the vRack can take a few minutes.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:associate-a-vrack */}

See Associate an OVHcloud Connect to your vRack{/* TODO(occ-revamp): restore this link before the final merge, once the revamp guide lands → [Associate an OVHcloud Connect to your vRack](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/associate-vrack) */}.

#### Add a PoP configuration (L3)

{/* CP-STEPS-START:add-a-pop-configuration */}
On the <code className="action">Configure</code> tab, in the **PoP Configuration** section, click <code className="action">Configure</code> on the row of your port (its **Configuration status** reads **Not Configured**) to open the **Add PoP configuration** window. There is no layer type to select: a provider service supports L3 only. Enter the following information, then click <code className="action">Add</code>:

| Information | Description |
|---|---|
| Customer ASN | Your BGP AS number, configured on your router located in the PoP |
| OVHcloud ASN | The AS number configured on the OVHcloud Connect routers located in the PoP |
| Peering subnet (/30) | A /30 IPv4 block, used for the link between your router and the OVHcloud router in the PoP |
{/* CP-STEPS-END:add-a-pop-configuration */}

#### Add an availability zone (AZ) configuration

{/* CP-STEPS-START:add-an-az-configuration */}
In the **Availability Zone configuration** section, click <code className="action">Add AZ configuration</code>. In the **Add AZ configuration** window, select the availability zone in the **Datacentre** list, enter the following information, then click <code className="action">Add</code>:

| Information | Description |
|---|---|
| OVHcloud ASN | The AS number configured on the OVHcloud Connect routers in the AZ (may differ from the PoP ASN) |
| Subnet | A private subnetwork configured in your vRack in the selected AZ (an IPv4 block of /28 or larger) |

If your service has several PoP configurations, the window also asks you to choose the **PoP configuration**.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:add-an-az-configuration */}

#### Add a routing configuration

{/* CP-STEPS-START:add-a-routing-configuration */}
Next to the name of the availability zone, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button, then select <code className="action">Add a route</code>. If the zone has no route yet, you can also click <code className="action">Add a route</code> directly in its routing table. In the **Add a route** window, set the **Route type** to **BGP** or **Static**:

- **BGP** — enter your ASN in **Neighbour ASN** and the **Neighbor IP** (the BGP neighbour IP of your router in the AZ, within the AZ subnetwork).

- **Static** — enter the **Target subnet** (a prefix in CIDR notation) and the **Next hop** (a gateway IP within the subnetwork range).

Click <code className="action">Add</code> to confirm.

You can add several routing configurations in the same availability zone, with up to 20 static routes per data centre.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:add-a-routing-configuration */}

### Step 5 — Verify BGP session and routing

OVHcloud Connect Provider is always a Layer 3 service — BGP peering is established between your router (or your provider's router, depending on your provider offering) and the OVHcloud PoP. Verify in the OVHcloud Console and in your provider's portal that the session is established and routes are being exchanged.

See Configure OCC L3 with BGP{/* TODO(occ-revamp): restore this link before the final merge, once the revamp guide lands → [Configure OCC L3 with BGP](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/l3-bgp) */} for detailed guidance.

### Step 6 — Test and verify

{/* CP-STEPS-START:test-and-verify */}
| Check | How |
|---|---|
| **Connection status** | Shows "Active" in the **Status** column of the OVHcloud Connect list in the OVHcloud Console, and in the provider portal |
| **BGP session** | Established on your router; in the OVHcloud Console, the **BGP status** column of the **PoP Configuration** section (<code className="action">Configure</code> tab) shows "Up" ("Down" if the session is failing) |
| **Routes** | Your prefixes visible on OVHcloud side; OVHcloud routes visible on your side |
| **Ping** | Ping an OVHcloud resource from your network |
| **Traceroute** | Verify traffic goes through the private link, not the Internet |
{/* CP-STEPS-END:test-and-verify */}

## Troubleshooting

{/* CP-STEPS-START:troubleshooting */}
| Issue | What to check |
|---|---|
| Service stuck at the "Waiting for the provider" step (while a service is being delivered, it is listed in the <code className="action">Being delivered</code> tab of the OVHcloud Connect list) | Verify the service key was entered correctly on the provider's side |
| Provider shows "Active" but the **BGP status** of your PoP configuration in the OVHcloud Console shows "Down" | Contact OVHcloud support — there may be a provisioning delay |
| BGP session not establishing | Check peering IPs, ASN, and VLAN ID on your router, and the ASNs and peering subnet in the **PoP Configuration** section of the OVHcloud Console |
| High latency or packet loss | Check provider's network status page; run traceroute to identify where delays occur |
{/* CP-STEPS-END:troubleshooting */}
