## Objective

With OVHcloud Connect, you can link your company network to your private OVHcloud vRack network, without creating a VPN tunnel through the internet. This will give you a quicker, more stable connection with guaranteed bandwidth. 

**This guide will show you how to set up the OVHcloud Connect Provider solution via the OVHcloud Console**

## Requirements

:::warning
To ensure correct operation of this service, you must be aware of the [technical capabilities and limitations of the OVHcloud Connect solution](/guides/network/ovhcloud-connect/occ-limits) and configure your network devices accordingly.

:::

- An [OVHcloud Connect Provider solution](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/network/ovhcloud-connect/)
- An OVHcloud [vRack](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/network/vrack/)

[[cpnav:network-ovhcloud-connect]]

## Instructions

### Step 1: Ordering your solution

Once you have ordered your OVHcloud Connect Provider solution, you will receive an order confirmation via email, along with a service key.

Depending on the provider you have chosen, go to their portal to log in via the link provided in the order confirmation email. Then enter your service key and confirm the order presented to you.

{/* CP-STEPS-START:order-solution */}

Next, check the activation status of your solution in the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>, in the **Status** column of the **OVHcloud Connect** list. Your solution's status should have changed to "Active".

{/* CP-STEPS-END:order-solution */}

### Step 2: Associating a vRack

{/* CP-STEPS-START:associate-vrack */}

Select your service in the **OVHcloud Connect** list.

You will need to link a vRack to your solution first. On the **Overview** tab of your service, click <code className="action">Attach a vRack</code>, select an existing vRack from the drop-down list, then click <code className="action">Attach</code>.

The service page then shows **vRack attachment in progress**: the attachment can take a few minutes.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:associate-vrack */}

### Step 3: Adding a PoP configuration

:::warning
The OVHcloud Connect Provider solution requires an L3 level configuration.

:::

{/* CP-STEPS-START:add-pop-configuration */}

Once you have connected your vRack, open the <code className="action">Configure</code> tab. In the **PoP Configuration** section, click <code className="action">Configure</code> on the row of your port. In the **Add PoP configuration** window, there is no layer type to choose: an OVHcloud Connect Provider service is configured in L3.

You will then need to enter the following, then click <code className="action">Add</code>:

| Information    | Description |
|:-------:|:------:|
| Customer ASN    | Your AS BGP number, which is configured on your router located in the PoP |
| OVHcloud ASN     | The OVHcloud AS number that will be configured on the OVHcloud Connect routers located in the PoP |
| Peering subnet (/30)    | A size /30 IPv4 block, used for the link between your router and the OVHcloud Connect router located in the PoP |

The row of the port then shows **L3** in the **Layer** column, with the **Customer ASN**, the **OVHcloud ASN**, the **Subnet** and the **BGP status** of the configuration.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:add-pop-configuration */}

### Step 4: Adding an availability zone configuration 

{/* CP-STEPS-START:add-az-configuration */}

When your PoP configuration has been set, click <code className="action">Add AZ configuration</code> in the **Availability Zone configuration** section.

In the **Add AZ configuration** window, select an availability zone from the **Datacentre** dropdown list, then enter the information required and click <code className="action">Add</code>.

| Information    | Description |
|:-------:|:------:|
| OVHcloud ASN    | The OVHcloud AS number that will be configured on the OVHcloud Connect routers located in the AZ. This number may be different from the ASN chosen for the PoP |
| Subnet  |  A private subnetwork configured in your vRack in the selected AZ. This can be an IPv4 block of size /28 or higher |

You can add additional availability zone configurations by clicking on the same <code className="action">Add AZ configuration</code> button, at the top of the section.

You must also add a routing configuration.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:add-az-configuration */}

##### **Adding a routing configuration**

{/* CP-STEPS-START:add-routing-configuration */}

Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button on the desired availability zone, then <code className="action">Add a route</code>. If the availability zone has no route yet, you can also click the <code className="action">Add a route</code> button displayed in it.

In the **Add a route** window, choose the **Route type** between **Static** and **BGP**.

If you choose the BGP type, then enter the required information:

| Information    | Description |
|:-------:|:------:|
| Neighbour ASN    | Your AS BGP number, which is configured on your router located in the AZ |
| Neighbor IP    | IP address of the BGP neighbour of your router in the AZ. This address must be part of the subnet specified in the **Availability Zone configuration** section |

If you choose the Static type, enter the required information:

| Information    | Description |
|:-------:|:------:|
| Target subnet    | A prefix using CIDR notation |
| Next hop    | IP address acting as gateway in the subnet range |

Then click <code className="action">Add</code>.

You can add multiple routing configurations within the same availability zone: they are listed in its **Routing** table.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:add-routing-configuration */}

### Deleting resources

Each resource (PoP or AZ) can be deleted individually, but deleting a parent resource such as AZ or POP will automatically delete all the subresources.

Recursive removal is slower than sequential removal of each resource.

:::info
If an AZ configuration is shared between two or more OVHcloud Connect services, removing the PoP configuration from a single OVHcloud Connect service will not affect the AZ resource.

:::

#### Deleting a Routing Configuration

{/* CP-STEPS-START:delete-routing-configuration */}

To delete a routing configuration, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column of the route to delete, in the **Routing** table of its availability zone, then click <code className="action">Remove the route</code>. In the **Remove the route** window, click <code className="action">Remove</code>.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:delete-routing-configuration */}

#### Deleting an AZ configuration

{/* CP-STEPS-START:delete-az-configuration */}

To delete an AZ configuration, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button on the AZ configuration to delete, then click <code className="action">Remove AZ configuration</code>. In the **Remove the datacenter** window, click <code className="action">Remove</code>.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:delete-az-configuration */}

:::info
Deleting an AZ configuration will delete the related routing configurations.

:::

#### Deleting a PoP configuration

{/* CP-STEPS-START:delete-pop-configuration */}

To delete a PoP configuration, in the **PoP Configuration** section, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column of the port, then click <code className="action">Remove the configuration</code>. In the **Remove the configuration** window, click <code className="action">Remove</code>.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:delete-pop-configuration */}

:::info
Deleting a PoP configuration will delete the AZ and routing configurations.

:::
