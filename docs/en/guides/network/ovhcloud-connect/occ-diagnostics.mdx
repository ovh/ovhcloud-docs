## Objective

With OVHcloud Connect, you can link your company network to your private OVHcloud vRack network, without creating a VPN tunnel through the internet. This will give you a quicker, more stable connection with guaranteed bandwidth. 

**This guide will show you how to get a status report of your OVHcloud Connect services via the OVHcloud Console.**

## Requirements

- An [OVHcloud Connect solution](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/network/ovhcloud-connect/) with a valid POP configuration.

## List of available diagnostics

### Layer 3 mode

{/* CP-STEPS-START:diagnostics-list-layer-3 */}

In Layer 3 mode, the **Check BGP peering** window offers three diagnostics:

- **Default**: Fetches the BGP session states and all related information.
- **Routes**: Fetches the routing table learned by OVHcloud via BGP.
- **Advertised-Routes**: Fetches the routing table advertised by OVHcloud via BGP.

## Instructions

### Layer 3 mode

{/* CP-STEPS-START:layer-3-diagnostic */}

Open the service for which you want to get a diagnosis, then click the <code className="action">Configure</code> tab.

In the **PoP Configuration** section, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column of your Layer 3 port, then select <code className="action">Check BGP peering</code>. The same entry is also available in the <code className="action">⋮</code> menu of each BGP route, in the **Routing** table of an availability zone.

A window will open. Select the type of diagnostic you wish to use, and click <code className="action">Launch diagnostic</code>.

You can now access the list of your diagnostics by opening the <code className="action">Diagnostic</code> tab. If a diagnostic you have just launched is not listed yet, click <code className="action">Refresh</code>.

Each diagnostic is listed with its **ID Diagnostic**, its **Date**, its **Name**, its **Scope** and its **Status**.
To access the content of a diagnostic, once its status is **Done**, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column of its row. You can select either <code className="action">View the result</code> to open its content in the **Diagnostic result** window, or <code className="action">Download</code> to get a *.txt* file with the same content.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:layer-3-diagnostic */}

## Limits

- **Retention time of diagnostics**: Only diagnostics initiated **within the last seven days** are accessible. We recommend you download them and properly archive them, in case you need future access.

- **Maximum number of diagnostics**: On a 24 hour period, you can initiate a maximum of 10 diagnostics per type of diagnostic, and per service. For example, if you have two OVHcloud Connect services configured in your OVHcloud Console, both configured in Layer 3 mode, you can theoretically launch 10 of each diagnostic type per service, for a total of 60.<br/>
This limit has been set in order to restrict the amount of resources used by the OVHcloud infrastructures, as the diagnostics are launched in real-time.
