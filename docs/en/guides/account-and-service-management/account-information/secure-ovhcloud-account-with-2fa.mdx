## Objective

OVHcloud offers tools to enhance the security of your account and services.
You can enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on your OVHcloud account. This two-factor authentication is added to your username/password pair, and is managed from a device you own: a phone, tablet, or security key.

**Find out more about the two-factor authentication methods available, and how to enable them.**

This guide will help you:

- [Understand the different two-factor authentication methods](#instructions)
- [Enable your first two-factor authentication method](#enabling-2fa)
- [Find out how to log in with two-factor authentication](#login-2fa)
- [Find out what to do if your phone/tablet/key is lost/stolen/damaged](#lost-device)
- [Find out how to disable two-factor authentication completely](#disable-2fa)

## Requirements

- A phone (for the SMS method), a smartphone or tablet (for the mobile application method), or a Universal Second Factor (U2F) security key
- An understanding of the [password management recommendations for accessing your account](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/manage-ovh-password)

[[cpnav:account-security-console]]

## Instructions <a name="instructions"></a>

You can enable one or more two-factor authentication methods to secure and control access to the OVHcloud Console.

We offer four different methods (click on the tabs below to view their explanations):

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="SMS">
    ![2FA sms](/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/secure-ovhcloud-account-with-2fa/sms.svg)<br/>

For this method, you will need to enter your mobile number.
    A single-use code is sent to you via SMS each time you try to log in to your OVHcloud account.
    
    The main advantage of this method is that codes are sent to a device not attached to your computer. If your system is compromised, e.g. by malware, your account will remain secure.
    However, you must have sufficient network coverage to receive SMS messages.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Authenticator app">
    ![2FA OTP](/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/secure-ovhcloud-account-with-2fa/app.svg)<br/>

For this method, you will need to install an **OTP** application on your Android or iOS smartphone or tablet.
    Many OTP applications exist (no OTP applications have been developed by OVHcloud) and can be downloaded from the Google Play Store for Android or from the Apple Store for iOS. The following two applications are free:
    
    - FreeOTP for Android
    - OTP Auth for iOS
    
    Once the application is linked to your OVHcloud account, the application generates a single-use code that is valid for a short period of time (a few seconds) each time you try to log in.
    
    :::tip
    **Advantages of this method**:
    
    - Once you have linked the application to your account for the first time, you no longer need to be connected to the internet on your smartphone/tablet for the codes to be generated.
    - You can use a single OTP application for all of your services or sites that require two-factor authentication.

    :::

  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Security key">
    ![2FA U2F](/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/secure-ovhcloud-account-with-2fa/key.svg)<br/>

For this method, you must have a **U2F** physical key that you plug into a USB port on your computer each time you log in to your OVHcloud account. The authentication is then carried out automatically. 
    
    This method offers a higher level of security as it is based on an independent security equipment, totally separate from your computer, smartphone or tablet, and which is less exposed to the risk of hacking.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Backup codes">
    ![2FA codes](/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/secure-ovhcloud-account-with-2fa/code.svg)<br/>

When you configure two-factor authentication (via **SMS**, **Authenticator app** or **Security key**) for the first time, you can generate 10 **single-use backup codes** in the OVHcloud Console.
    
    This two-factor authentication method complements an already activated method (via **SMS**, **Authenticator app** or **Security key**). It cannot be activated alone.
    
    Each time you try to log in, you can enter one of the 10 single-use codes.
    It is essential to always have at least 1 remaining backup code. Remember to regenerate them via the OVHcloud Console if you have used all of them, or if you have lost them.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>


### Step 1 - Enable your first two-factor authentication method <a name="enabling-2fa"></a>

{/* CP-STEPS-START:enable-2fa */}

Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/account/security-and-login/login">Security and login</ManagerLink> page (side menu <code className="action">My account</code> > <code className="action">Security and login</code>). In the **Sign-in and 2FA** tab, the **Authentication methods** section lists the three available methods, **Authenticator app**, **SMS** and **Security key**, each with its own <code className="action">Add method</code> button.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="2FA status and Authentication methods section, with an Add method button for each method" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/secure-ovhcloud-account-with-2fa/console/2fa-methods-enabled.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:enable-2fa */}

**Click on the tab corresponding to the method of your choice:**

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="SMS">

{/* CP-STEPS-START:enable-2fa-sms */}

    In the **SMS** section, click <code className="action">Add method</code>.

    In the **Set up SMS authentication** window, check the country code shown beside the **Phone number** field (for example, +33 for a mobile phone in France), then enter your mobile phone number in this field. You can also add a description for this phone number in the **Description (optional)** field.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Set up SMS authentication window with the Phone number and Description fields" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/secure-ovhcloud-account-with-2fa/console/2fa-sms-setup.png" loading="lazy" />

    Click <code className="action">Send code</code>. A validation code is then sent by SMS to the number you have given. Enter this code in the field provided, then click <code className="action">Verify and enable</code>.

    Two-factor authentication is now enabled. You can also add other numbers.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:enable-2fa-sms */}

  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Authenticator app">

{/* CP-STEPS-START:enable-2fa-mobile-app */}

    In the **Authenticator app** section, click <code className="action">Add method</code>.

    In the **Set up authenticator app** window, a QR code is generated: scan it via your OTP application. If your OTP application does not offer this option, enter the key shown under **Or enter this key manually:** into the OTP application.

    Your application then generates a single-use code. Enter this code in the field provided. You can also add a description for this authentication method. Then click <code className="action">Verify and enable</code>.

    Two-factor authentication is now enabled.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:enable-2fa-mobile-app */}

  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Security key">

{/* CP-STEPS-START:enable-2fa-security-key */}

    In the **Security key** section, click <code className="action">Add method</code>. In the **Set up security key** window, add a description for your key in the **Description (optional)** field if you wish, then click <code className="action">Register key</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Set up security key window with the Description field and the Register key button" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/secure-ovhcloud-account-with-2fa/console/2fa-security-key-setup.png" loading="lazy" />

    Plug in your security key when prompted. If it has a button, press it.

    :::warning
    A pop-up window will open, prompting you to validate the key. If you don't see this window appear, make sure your browser isn't blocking pop-ups.

    :::

    Once the key is recognized, two-factor authentication is enabled.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:enable-2fa-security-key */}

  </Tab>
</Tabs>


Once you have added the first method, you can also **add one or two other methods** to give you multiple ways of logging in to your account.

### Step 2 - Save backup codes <a name="codes"></a>

{/* CP-STEPS-START:save-backup-codes */}

After adding your first authentication method, generate your backup codes: in the **Backup codes** section of the **Sign-in and 2FA** tab, below the **Authentication methods** section, click <code className="action">Generate new codes</code>. The **Your backup codes** window then displays 10 single-use **backup codes**.

**Please keep them safe**. We recommend saving them in a password manager, such as [KeePass](https://keepass.info/) or [Bitwarden](https://bitwarden.com/) (both applications are free). You can also download them with the <code className="action">Download codes</code> button. Once your codes are saved, click <code className="action">I have saved my codes</code>.

You can regenerate or delete your backup codes from the **Backup codes** section, with the <code className="action">Regenerate codes</code> and <code className="action">Delete codes</code> buttons:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Backup codes section with the Regenerate codes and Delete codes buttons" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/secure-ovhcloud-account-with-2fa/console/2fa-backup-codes.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:save-backup-codes */}

:::warning
It is **strongly recommended that you save these backup codes** and ensure that they are valid.
If you don't have a security code and your phone/smartphone/tablet or security key is lost or stolen, access to your OVHcloud Console and your services may be blocked.

:::


### Step 3 - Log in to the OVHcloud Console with two-factor authentication <a name="login-2fa"></a>

Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/">login page for the OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink> and enter your ID (or primary email address) and password.

The login screen displays the last two-factor authentication method used or entered. If you would like to use another method, click the <code className="action">Try another method</code> button.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="2FA" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/secure-ovhcloud-account-with-2fa/2024-007-log-in-01.png" loading="lazy" style={{ maxWidth: '400px' }} />

All the methods you have enabled will then appear, including the backup codes method.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="2FA" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/secure-ovhcloud-account-with-2fa/2024-007-log-in-02.png" loading="lazy" style={{ maxWidth: '400px' }} />

By choosing this last method, you just need to enter one of your backup codes.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="2FA" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/secure-ovhcloud-account-with-2fa/2024-007-log-in-03.png" loading="lazy" style={{ maxWidth: '400px' }} />

### What do I do if I lose one of my devices, or if it stops working? <a name="lost-device"></a>

If your device (mobile phone/smartphone/security key) is lost, stolen or is no longer working, you can:

- Use the active [backup codes](#codes) that you have backed up.
- Use another two-factor authentication device, if you have enabled several.
- [Disable two-factor authentication](#disable-2fa).

:::warning
If one of your devices has been lost or stolen, this can compromise the security of your OVHcloud account.
Once you regain access to your OVHcloud Console, you must **remove this device from the list of devices used for two-factor authentication**.

See the next chapter of this guide for details on removing a device.

:::


#### Remove a device linked to two-factor authentication <a name="delete-device"></a> 

:::warning
Before removing a device, and in order not to block access to your account, please check that you have:

- Another functional device
- Another functional two-factor authentication method
- Valid backup codes

:::


{/* CP-STEPS-START:remove-device */}

To remove a device, go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/account/security-and-login/login">Security and login</ManagerLink> page. In the **Authentication methods** section, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column of the device you want to delete, then click <code className="action">Remove</code>.

In the **Remove authentication method** window that opens, a final validation code is requested if the device is a phone number or an authenticator app with the status **Enabled**: for a phone number, the code is sent to it by SMS when the window opens; for an authenticator app, the application generates it. Enter this code, then click <code className="action">Remove</code> to complete the deletion. For a security key, or a device whose status is not **Enabled**, no code is requested: click <code className="action">Remove</code>.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:remove-device */}

If you no longer have access to a phone number or an authenticator app with the status **Enabled** that you want to remove, you cannot remove it yourself from the OVHcloud Console: removing it requires a code from that device.
In this case, **contact our support teams directly** by [creating a ticket from the Help Center](https://help.ovhcloud.com/csm?id=csm_get_help) or by following the process described [below](#2FA-deletion).

:::warning
Removing a single device does not disable two-factor authentication on your OVHcloud account, unless it is the last one.

:::


### Disabling two-factor authentication completely <a name="disable-2fa"></a>

#### If you still have access to your OVHcloud Console

{/* CP-STEPS-START:disable-2fa-with-access */}

To disable two-factor authentication completely on your OVHcloud account, you will need to delete **all** of the devices entered: two-factor authentication is disabled as soon as the last one is removed.

To remove each device, please refer to the dedicated [part of this guide](#delete-device).

Deleting your backup codes is a separate action, which does not disable two-factor authentication. If you also want to delete them, click <code className="action">Delete codes</code> in the **Backup codes** section. In the **Delete backup codes** window, enter one of your backup codes, then click <code className="action">Delete</code>.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:disable-2fa-with-access */}

#### If you no longer have access to your OVHcloud Console <a name="2FA-deletion"></a>

If you no longer have valid devices or valid backup codes, then you must request for two-factor authentication to be disabled by providing proof of identity documents corresponding to your OVHcloud account.

First, go to <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/">the login page of the OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>.

Enter your OVHcloud ID and password to access the two-factor authentication step. Then click the <code className="action">I have lost access to my mobile phone, U2F security key or emergency codes</code> button.<br/>
If you do not see this button, click the <code className="action">Try another method</code> button, then <code className="action">I have lost access to my mobile phone, U2F security key or emergency codes</code>.

The following interface allows you to upload and send to our teams the necessary documents to disable two-factor authentication on your account.

:::warning
- Please ensure that all your documents are correct and readable before sending.
- **Accepted formats**: jpg, jpeg, pdf, png. The maximum file size for each document is 10 MB.
- If there are invalid documents, this procedure will be cancelled and you will need to perform a new procedure.

:::


Within 72 hours, you will receive confirmation by email that two-factor authentication has been disabled.

<details>

<summary>Needed documents</summary>


|OVHcloud account type|Needed documents|
|---|---|
|Private individual|- Proof of identity (identity card, driving licence, passport) mentioning full name, date of birth and expiry date, in the name of the OVHcloud account holder|
|Company|- Company registration documents<br/><br/>- Proof of identity (identity card, driving licence, passport) mentioning full name, date of birth and expiry date, in the name of the OVHcloud account holder, or in the name of a person authorized to represent the company.|

</details>

:::tip
For security purposes, once access to your account has been restored, we recommend that you re-enable two-factor authentication as soon as possible.
