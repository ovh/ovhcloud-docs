## Objective

You can use **Single Sign-On** (SSO) to connect to your OVHcloud account. To enable these connections, your account and your Okta accounts have to be configured using SAML (*Security Assertion Markup Language*) authentications.

**This guide explains how to associate your OVHcloud account with an external Okta service.**

## Requirements

- Being an administrator of an Okta service
- An [OVHcloud account](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/ovhcloud-account-creation)

[[cpnav:iam-saml-sso-console]]

## Instructions

:::info
In order for a service provider (i.e. your OVHcloud account) to establish an SSO connection with an identity provider (i.e. your Okta service), the key is to establish a mutual trust relationship by registering the SSO connection in both services.

:::


### Registering OVHcloud into Okta

Your Okta service acts as an identity provider. Requests to authenticate your OVHcloud account will only be accepted if you have first declared it as a trusted third party.

This means that it must be added to `Applications`.

Log in to the Okta administration interface with your administrator account.

Go to <code className="action">Applications</code> then again <code className="action">Applications</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Add SAML Application, Step 1" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/ovhcloud-account-connect-saml-okta/OKTA_add_application_step1.png" loading="lazy" />

Click <code className="action">Create App Integration</code> and select <code className="action">SAML 2.0</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Add SAML Application, Step 2" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/ovhcloud-account-connect-saml-okta/OKTA_add_application_step2.png" loading="lazy" />

In the "General Settings" step, add a name for this application, **OVHcloud** for example, and a logo if you want. Click <code className="action">Next</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Add SAML Application, Step 3" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/ovhcloud-account-connect-saml-okta/OKTA_add_application_step3.png" loading="lazy" />

In the step "Configure SAML", complete the `Single sign-on URL` and `Audience URI` fields with the values for your region:

- EU region: **Single sign-on URL**: `https://www.ovhcloud.com/eu/auth/saml/acs` and **Audience URI**: `https://www.ovhcloud.com/eu/auth/`
- CA region: **Single sign-on URL**: `https://www.ovhcloud.com/ca/auth/saml/acs` and **Audience URI**: `https://www.ovhcloud.com/ca/auth/`

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Add SAML Application, Step 4" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/ovhcloud-account-connect-saml-okta/OKTA_add_application_step4.png" loading="lazy" />

Then set the following `Attribute Statements`:

- **Name**: `http://schemas.xmlsoap.org/ws/2005/05/identity/claims/upn` and **Value**: `user.login`
- **Name**: `http://schemas.xmlsoap.org/ws/2005/05/identity/claims/emailaddress` and **Value**: `user.email`
- **Name**: `http://schemas.xmlsoap.org/ws/2005/05/identity/claims/name` and **Value**: `user.displayName`

Set these `Group Attribute Statements`:

- **Name**: `groups` and **Filter**: `Matches regex:.*` (Adapt the filter if you want to be more specific)

Click <code className="action">Next</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Add SAML application, step 5" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/ovhcloud-account-connect-saml-okta/OKTA_add_application_step5.png" loading="lazy" />

In the "Feedback" step, select the according option and click <code className="action">Finish</code>.

Then open the application and go to the "Assignments" tab and assign users or groups to the application.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Assign users" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/ovhcloud-account-connect-saml-okta/OKTA_add_user.png" loading="lazy" />

Before going to the next section, go to the "Sign On" tab, and access to the **Metadata URL** and save the provided XML file.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Retrieve metadata" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/ovhcloud-account-connect-saml-okta/OKTA_retrieve_metadata.png" loading="lazy" />

Your Okta service now trusts OVHcloud as a service provider. The next step is to ensure that the OVHcloud account trusts your Okta as an identity provider.

### Registering Okta into the OVHcloud account and configuring the connection

{/* CP-STEPS-START:sso-connection */}

To add Okta as a trusted identity provider, you need to provide the identity provider metadata. Open the <code className="action">SSO</code> tab of the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/iam/identities/sso">Identities</ManagerLink> page and click the <code className="action">Connect via SSO</code> button.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="SSO tab of the Identities page with the Connect via SSO button" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/ovhcloud-account-connect-saml-okta/console/access_to_the_IAM_menu_03.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Connect via SSO** panel, fill in the XML metadata of your Okta service in the **XML metadata** field. Enter `http://schemas.xmlsoap.org/ws/2005/05/identity/claims/upn` as the **User attribute name** and `Group` as the **Group attribute name**.

You can keep local users by leaving the **Keep OVHcloud users active** switch enabled, as it is by default. If you disable it, your local users will no longer be able to log in with their password.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Connect via SSO panel with the attribute names and XML metadata filled in" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/ovhcloud-account-connect-saml-okta/console/ovhcloud_add_federation.png" loading="lazy" />

Click <code className="action">Add</code>. You should now find Okta as your identity provider on the **SSO** tab, and the default groups on the **User groups** tab.

The **Configuration** and **Connection information** sections of the **SSO** tab show the details of the SSO, including the **SSO service URL**.

The <code className="action">Edit SSO</code> and <code className="action">Delete SSO</code> buttons in the **Configuration** section allow you to update or delete the SSO.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:sso-connection */}

{/* CP-STEPS-START:declare-groups */}

Your Okta service is now considered a trusted identity provider. However, you still need to add groups to your OVHcloud account.

:::warning
If you try to connect via SSO at this point, you will probably receive a `Not in valid groups` error message.

That is because your OVHcloud account checks whether the authenticating user belongs to an existing group on the account.

:::

You must then assign **roles** to Okta user groups at OVHcloud. Otherwise, your OVHcloud account does not know what the user is allowed to do and, by default, no rights are assigned.

On the <code className="action">Identities</code> page, open the <code className="action">User groups</code> tab. Then click the <code className="action">Create a group</code> button and fill in the fields of the **Create a group** panel:

- **Name**: Group name within Okta
- **Role**: Level of rights granted to this group
- **Description**: Optional description of the group

<img className="thumbnail" alt="User groups tab of the Identities page with the Create a group button" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/ovhcloud-account-connect-saml-okta/console/ovhcloud_user_management_groups_1.png" loading="lazy" />

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Create a group panel with the Name, Role and Description fields" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/ovhcloud-account-connect-saml-okta/console/ovhcloud_user_management_groups_2.png" loading="lazy" />

Click <code className="action">Create</code>. You can then verify that the group is added to your OVHcloud account in the list on the **User groups** tab.

When you later log in with a user from the **Intern** group, your OVHcloud account will recognise that the user has the **Read-only** role specified by their group.

Warning: if you give the **None** role, you will need to assign permissions to this group via the [IAM policies](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/iam-policy-ui).

You will then be able to log out of your account and log back in with your Okta as an identity provider.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:declare-groups */}

### Connecting via SSO

On <ManagerLink to="/">the OVHcloud login page</ManagerLink>, enter your [login](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/ovhcloud-account-creation#what-is-my-nic-handle) followed by **/idp** without a password and click the <code className="action">Login</code> button.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Connection to OVHcloud federation" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/ovhcloud-account-connect-saml-okta/ovhcloud_federation_login_1.png" loading="lazy" />

You are then redirected to your Okta login page. Enter the login and password for a user of your Okta, then click the <code className="action">Sign in</code> button.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="OVHcloud Federation login Redirection Okta" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/ovhcloud-account-connect-saml-okta/OKTA_login.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-START:connecting-via-sso */}

You are now logged in with the same customer ID, but through your Okta user.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:connecting-via-sso */}
