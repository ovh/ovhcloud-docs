## Objective

When you created your OVHcloud account, you specified a contact email address. If you want to share or delegate the management of your messages related to your customer account, you can add new contact email addresses and configure rules to manage these messages.

**This guide explains how to add additional email contact addresses to your OVHcloud account and configure notification delivery rules.**

## Requirements

[[cpnav:account-messages]]

## Instructions

### Messages

{/* CP-STEPS-START:my-messages */}

From the <code className="action">Messages</code> tab of the **Communications** page, you can view all messages that have been sent to your contact email address, sorted by date. Above the table, use the **All priorities** and **All categories** drop-down lists to filter your messages by priority and category, and the **Start date** and **End date** fields to display only the messages sent within a given period. You can also search your messages using the **Search** field.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Messages tab of the Communications page, with the All priorities filter open" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/manage-messages/console/messages-01.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:my-messages */}

### Contacts

{/* CP-STEPS-START:contacts-1 */}

From the <code className="action">Contacts</code> tab, you can find the reference email address of the OVHcloud account, which cannot be removed or modified from this tab. To change it, go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/account/security/auth/password">Password & Email</ManagerLink> tab of the **Authentication** page.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:contacts-1 */}

:::info
If you no longer have access to your main contact email address and do not have a backup email address, you will need to follow [this procedure](https://www.ovh.co.uk/cgi-bin/en/procedure/procedureChangeEmail.cgi) to request a change from our teams.

:::


In addition to your default contact, you can add new contact email addresses to your OVHcloud account.

{/* CP-STEPS-START:contacts-2 */}

- Click the <code className="action">Add contact</code> button. In the **Add a contact** window, fill in the **Email address** and **Description** fields, then click <code className="action">Add</code>. A validation code will then be sent to this email address.

- Click on the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the new contact to display the options:
    - <code className="action">Validate</code>: Allows you to enter the validation code sent to the new contact by email.
    - <code className="action">Resend validation</code>: Allows you to resend an email containing a validation code to this contact.
    - <code className="action">Delete</code>: Allows you to delete this contact.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Contacts tab with the Add contact button and the open menu of a pending contact" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/manage-messages/console/contacts-01.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:contacts-2 */}

### Rules

{/* CP-STEPS-START:delivery-settings-1 */}

From the <code className="action">Rules</code> tab, you can create rules to organise the delivery of messages to your contact email addresses.

- Click the <code className="action">Add rule</code> button. On the **Add routing rule** page, enter a name for the rule in the **Rule name** field. In the **Condition 1** section, select the categories and priority levels of the messages from the **Categories** and **Priorities** lists, then the contacts that will receive them from the **Contact means** list, which only shows validated contacts. Then click <code className="action">Create</code>.

- Click on the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of a rule to access the options:
    - <code className="action">Edit</code> the rule. This is also how you enable or disable it **without deleting it**: on the **Edit routing rule** page, tick the <code className="action">Active</code> checkbox to enable the rule or untick it to disable it, then click <code className="action">Save</code>.
    - <code className="action">Delete</code> the rule.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Rules tab with the Add rule button and the open menu of a rule" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/manage-messages/console/rules-01.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:delivery-settings-1 */}

Rules are applied based on two criteria: the category and the priority of the message.

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| Account | Communications related to your customer account settings, preferences, access or credentials. |
| Billing | Communications related to financial transactions, such as invoices, payments, renewals, refunds or billing enquiries. |
| Billing (invoices & documents) | Communications related to billing documents, such as invoices, payments, renewals, refunds or credit notes. |
| Incident | Notifications about unexpected service disruptions, outages or recovery updates. |
| Maintenance | Communications about scheduled or emergency maintenance, upgrades or infrastructure changes that may affect service availability. |
| Product | Communications related to the services you have subscribed to, such as features, usage, status and updates. |
| Security | Messages related to account or system security, such as breaches, suspicious activity, password changes, two-factor authentication or phishing alerts. |

| Priority | Description |
|---|---|
| Low | Recurring communications with no impact. |
| Medium | Unusual communications that pose no risk. |
| High | Communications to read as a priority, since your services may be affected. |

You can create your rules one by one; they will all be applied when a message is sent to your account.

{/* CP-STEPS-START:delivery-settings-2 */}

You can also create a rule that includes multiple conditions that will be applied in sequence. To do this, when configuring a rule, click on the <code className="action">Add a condition</code> button. You can add as many conditions as needed.  
By default, if a condition applies, the process stops. If you want the process to continue applying the following conditions, tick the <code className="action">Continue to next rule</code> checkbox under the condition concerned. Every condition except the last one has this checkbox.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Add routing rule page with two conditions and the Continue to next rule checkbox" src="/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/manage-messages/console/rules-02.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:delivery-settings-2 */}
