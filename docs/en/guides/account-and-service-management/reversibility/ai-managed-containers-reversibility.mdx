## Objective

This document describes the reversibility policy for the AI Managed Container product covering the OVHcloud AI Deploy and AI Training offers.

This policy aims to implement the general reversibility principles and our compliance with the SWIPO IaaS Code of Conduct for cloud providers.

## List of features

Features of the product line fall into three categories:

1. **Core features** for which we guarantee migration capacity.
2. **OVHcloud implementations** that require adaptation to a new migration environment.
3. **Specific features** that cannot be guaranteed for migration as they are related to the OVHcloud environment or involve custom developments.

## List of architectures

The product is based on managed Kubernetes clusters, with full orchestration of AI workloads (deployment, scaling, monitoring). Architectures support horizontal and vertical auto-scaling, integrated load balancing, high availability, GPU/CPU integration for training and inference and access to persistent storage services (Block storage, Object Storage). CI/CD integration, multi-model management, and access governance are native.

## Cost and fees

Billing is on a pay-per-use basis and stops when you no longer consume resources. No cancellation fees apply: deleting the service will stop the billing immediately. Any associated OVHcloud credits cannot be transferred.
Once the service is cancelled, OVHcloud releases its resources, making data retrieval impossible. Clients are responsible for exporting their configurations, manifests, and images before termination, as all resources are released upon deletion.

## Data retention after contract termination

After service deletion or contract termination, OVHcloud permanently deletes all images, models, logs, metrics and configurations stored in its environment. Automatic backups and logs are also deleted. It is therefore imperative to export all the necessary data before deletion, as you cannot restore it after these actions.
