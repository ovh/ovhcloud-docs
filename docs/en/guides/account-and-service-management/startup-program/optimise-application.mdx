## Objective

OVHcloud's Startup Program offers numerous benefits to support startups in their growth. 

**To maximize your chances of acceptance, follow these steps to prepare and complete your application.**

## Requirements

- An [OVHcloud account](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/ovhcloud-account-creation).

[[cpnav:account-profile-console]]

## Instructions

### Step 1 - Update your OVHcloud account

Ensure your OVHcloud account is correctly configured before submitting your application.

{/* CP-STEPS-START:update-account */}

On the <ManagerLink console to="/#/customer/account/profile">Profile</ManagerLink> page, set your account type to `Company` and complete the required information. These details are edited in two sections of the page.

In the **Account administrator** section (**Account holder** on a private individual's account), click <code className="action">Edit</code>. In the **Edit administrator** panel, select <code className="action">Company</code> from the **Account type** drop-down list and complete the personal information of the account holder: **First name**, **Last name** and **Phone**. Click <code className="action">Save</code>: your changes apply straight away.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Edit administrator panel with Company selected as the account type" src="/images/account-and-service-management/startup-program/01-optimise-application/console/edit-administrator-account-type.png" loading="lazy" />

Use a professional email address associated with your company domain. The email address is not edited in this panel: click <code className="action">Change email</code> to change it from the **Security and login** page. Please also enter a backup email address (preferably a personal email address): on the same page, click <code className="action">Modify</code> next to **Backup email**.

In the **Company** section, click <code className="action">Edit</code> and complete your company's details: the **SIRET** (only for accounts in France), the **Tax ID** (VAT number) and the **Head office address**. For a company in France, look it up by its SIRET in the **Find your company** search: the search fills in these details. Then click <code className="action">Save</code>.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:update-account */}

Refer to this guide for more information: [Securing my OVHcloud account and managing my personal information](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/all-about-username).

### Step 2 - Add a valid payment method

A valid payment method is required to ensure the continuity of your services. It will be used for non-eligible products during your participation in the program and for all products after the program ends.

Refer to this guide for more information: [Managing payment methods](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-payment-methods).

{/* CP-STEPS-START:add-payment-method */}

{/* CP-STEPS-END:add-payment-method */}

### Step 3 - Carefully complete the Startup Program application form

To allow us to effectively review your application, complete the form with accuracy and detail.

**Essential information to provide:**

- Company name
- Website
- Social media (LinkedIn, X)
- Description of your project and business model
- Server infrastructure needs
- Stage of development of your project

**Precision and completeness**: Any missing or incorrect information could result in your application being rejected or delays if updates are required.

### Processing time

Applications are reviewed within 7 days. To expedite the process, ensure all required information is provided during the initial submission.

By following these steps, you will significantly increase your chances of being accepted into OVHcloud’s Startup Program.
