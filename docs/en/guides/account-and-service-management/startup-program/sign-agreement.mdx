## Objective

To participate in the OVHcloud Startup Program, signing the contract is an essential step. Once your application is accepted, you will receive an email asking you to sign the contract in the OVHcloud Console. 

**This guide explains how to access and sign this contract so that your credits are activated quickly.**


## Requirements

Your Startup Program application must have been accepted.

[[cpnav:account-contracts-console]]

## Instructions

### Signing the contract in the OVHcloud Console

You can access the contract directly in your customer account.

{/* CP-STEPS-START:sign-contract */}

Make sure to log in with the account you used to apply for the Startup Program, then go to the <ManagerLink console to="/#/customer/account/contracts">Contracts</ManagerLink> page of the OVHcloud Console.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Contracts page of the OVHcloud Console, with the table of contracts" src="/images/account-and-service-management/startup-program/02-sign-agreement/console/contracts-list.png" loading="lazy" />

Look for your Startup Program contract in the table: a contract awaiting your agreement has the status **To confirm**.

Click the name of the contract to open its page, where you can review it and download it with <code className="action">Download PDF</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Contract page in the OVHcloud Console with its text and the Download PDF link" src="/images/account-and-service-management/startup-program/02-sign-agreement/console/contract-detail.png" loading="lazy" />

On the page of your Startup Program contract, tick the <code className="action">I have read and accept this contract.</code> checkbox, then click <code className="action">Accept</code> to validate the contract acceptance.

After signing, your credits will be credited to your account within 48 business hours, and you will be able to fully benefit from the program's advantages.

Your signed contract will remain available on the **Contracts** page for reference at any time, with the status **Accepted on** followed by the date.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:sign-contract */}
