## OVHcloud Startup Program

The [OVHcloud Startup Program](/links/transversal/startup-program) helps startups build and scale their server infrastructure on OVHcloud. Once your application is accepted and your contract signed, you receive free credits valid for 12 months, and our Startup Program Managers support you throughout the program.

As a member of the Startup Program, you can:

- Pay for eligible products with your credits, such as Public Cloud and Hosted Private Cloud
- Request technical consultations to review and evolve your infrastructure
- Contact a Startup Program Manager for advice at any stage
- Meet our teams at startup events
