## Objective

As part of your professional activities or out of interest on the subject, you may need to calculate the carbon footprint of your services.

**Find out how to retrieve the monthly carbon footprint of your OVHcloud services.**

## Requirements

- Being the billing contact for the service(s) you would like to create a carbon footprint for. For more information, see [our guide to managing contacts](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/managing-contacts).

[[cpnav:account-dashboard]]

**The carbon footprint calculation is available for the following services:**

- [Dedicated Server](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/bare-metal/) (Advance, Game, Scale, High Grade, Storage)
- [Eco Dedicated Server](https://eco.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/) (Rise, Kimsufi, So you Start)
- [VMware on OVHcloud](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/)
- [Public Cloud](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/public-cloud/)

## Instructions

:::info
Carbon footprint data is not yet available for 3AZ architectures and Local Zones.

:::

Please note the following information:

- You cannot generate a carbon footprint report for the current month.
- When using the OVHcloud API, whether you enter a date at the beginning, middle or end of a month, the carbon footprint report always covers whole months.
- No carbon footprint report can be generated for more than the last 24 months.
- No carbon footprint can be generated before the date of implementation of the feature for each OVHcloud service (see table below).

### Retrieving the monthly carbon footprint report for the previous month via the OVHcloud Control Panel

1. On the <ManagerLink to="/#/account/useraccount/dashboard">My account</ManagerLink>, scroll down to the **Useful links** section in the left-hand column, then click on the <code className="action">My carbon footprint</code> tab.
1. On the new page that appears, click <code className="action">Download my [Month] [Year] footprint</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Carbon footprint" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/right-column/carbon-footprint/my-carbon-footprint.png" loading="lazy" />

You can recover the carbon footprint of the previous month for your eligible services every month.

If you need the carbon footprint for a month prior to the month preceding the current month, or a report covering several months, you will have to use our APIs to retrieve it.

### Retrieving a carbon footprint report prior to the previous month via our APIs

By default, OVHcloud APIs are made available to developers or integrators to associate features, such as those not present in the OVHcloud Control Panel, directly with their applications or solutions.

#### Step 1 - Access the OVHcloud APIs

- Go to our <ApiLink>OVHcloud API</ApiLink> website.
- On the page that pops up, click <code className="action">Explore the OVHcloud API</code>.
- On the left-hand side of the new page that appears, go to the form to the right of the <code className="action">v1</code> field, then select/enter the choice `/me`.
- From the list of APIs that appear below in the left-hand column, locate and click the API call matching the format you want: **POST /me/carbonCalculator/pdf** for a report in PDF format, or **POST /me/carbonCalculator/csv** for a report in CSV format. You can also access them directly via the API calls below:

<Api version="v1" section="/me" method="POST" route={"/me/carbonCalculator/pdf"} />

<Api version="v1" section="/me" method="POST" route={"/me/carbonCalculator/csv"} />

- On the right-hand side of the page, you will then see the API with its fields to complete.
- Click the button in the top right-hand corner labeled <code className="action">Authenticate</code>, then the <code className="action">Login with OVHcloud SSO</code> button.
- The <ManagerLink to="/#/account/useraccount/dashboard">OVHcloud Control Panel</ManagerLink> login page will open.
- Log in with your customer credentials, then click <code className="action">Authorize</code> to use the OVHcloud APIs with your services.
- You will then be automatically redirected to the previous page of the API call you selected.

#### Step 2 - Request the carbon footprint generation and retrieve the requested Task ID

Two API calls are available, depending on the format you want. The `taskID` you obtain at this step will produce either a PDF or a CSV, according to the call you selected in step 1. Select the corresponding tab below.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="PDF format">
    The **POST /me/carbonCalculator/pdf** API call generates a report covering a single month.

    Replace the current date that appears in the API sidebar with a date in the month you want the report for. Please respect the following date format:

    ```bash
    {
      "month": "YYYY-MM-DD"
    }
    ```

    The day is ignored: whichever day you enter, the report covers the full month. The month requested cannot be the current month, nor go back further than 24 months.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="API" src="/images/assets/screens/api/post-me-carboncalculator-pdf.png" loading="lazy" />

    Once you have chosen the correct date, click the blue <code className="action">EXECUTE</code> button.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="CSV format">
    The **POST /me/carbonCalculator/csv** API call generates a report covering a range of months.

    Replace the two dates that appear in the API sidebar with the month the calculation starts from and the month it stops at. Please respect the following date format:

    ```bash
    {
      "endMonth": "YYYY-MM-DD",
      "startMonth": "YYYY-MM-DD"
    }
    ```

    The day is ignored: whichever day you enter, the report covers full months, one month being the minimum. `endMonth` must be later than `startMonth`, and `startMonth` cannot go back further than 24 months.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="API" src="/images/assets/screens/api/post-me-carboncalculator-csv.png" loading="lazy" />

    Once you have entered both dates, click the blue <code className="action">EXECUTE</code> button.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Whichever call you used, if everything has been done correctly, a `taskID` will appear in the <code className="action">RESPONSE</code> window when you scroll down the page below the <code className="action">EXECUTE</code> button.

For example, if your OVHcloud account ID is `aa00000-ovh` and you requested a report in PDF format for January 2025, then you will get the following result:

```bash
{
  "taskID": "aa00000-ovh_202501"
}
```

Copy only the obtained value which must be equivalent to the value of our example `aa00000-ovh_202501` (without copying the quotation marks (`"`)).

#### Step 3 - Retrieve the carbon footprint report in PDF or CSV format

Thanks to the `taskID` value previously retrieved — from the PDF call or from the CSV call — you can now retrieve the carbon footprint of your services.

To do this, stay on our <ApiLink>OVHcloud API</ApiLink> website and perform the following actions:

- On the left-hand side of the page, go to the form to the right of the <code className="action">v1</code> field, then select/enter the choice <code className="action">/me</code>.
- From the list of API calls that appear below, locate and click the following API: **GET /me/carbonCalculator/task/&#123;taskID&#125;**. You can also access it directly via the API call below:

<Api version="v1" section="/me" method="GET" route={"/me/carbonCalculator/task/\{taskID\}"} />

- On the right-hand side of the page, you will then see the API with a form to fill in.

Complete the form in the `PATH PARAMETERS` section as follows:

- `taskID`: Copy here the `taskID` value retrieved earlier in step 2, whichever format you requested.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="API" src="/images/assets/screens/api/get-me-carboncalculator-task-taskid.png" loading="lazy" />

Once you have entered the value for your `taskID` correctly, click the blue <code className="action">EXECUTE</code> button.

The following result appears in the <code className="action">RESPONSE</code> window when you scroll down the page below the <code className="action">EXECUTE</code> button:

```bash
{
  "link": "Find here the complete URL to download the carbon footprint",
  "status": "SUCCESS",
  "taskID": "aa00000-ovh_202501"
}
```

In this result, copy the entire URL in HTTPS (**without the quotation marks**) on the right-hand side of the comment `"link":`, then paste it into the address bar of your web browser to start downloading the carbon footprint in the format you requested.

Your web browser will automatically download the file, and may display it directly in the case of a PDF report.

:::info
Depending on your browser’s configuration, the file may not be downloaded and displayed properly. If this is the case, check your browser configuration, then reload the page.

:::

Once you have opened the file, you will find the following items.

**In the PDF report:**

- A summary table of CO2 emissions by category for the requested month.
- A summary table of CO2 emissions by category between the beginning of the calendar year and the requested month.
- A table detailing the values by type of product subscribed.
- A graph showing CO2 emissions by category.

**In the CSV report:**

- A summary of CO2 emissions per physical server, or per server range (PCI), for the requested month or months.

:::warning
The link generated is only valid for 24 hours. Make sure to download the carbon footprint from your browser once the link is open.

:::
