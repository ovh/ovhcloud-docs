## Objective

**In the OVHcloud Console, you can save and manage different payment methods.**

## Requirements

- A valid payment method

[[cpnav:billing-payment-methods]]

## Instructions <a name="payment_methods"></a>

{/* CP-STEPS-START:cp-access */}

Open the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/payment-methods">My payment methods</ManagerLink> page.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Account menu of the OVHcloud Console, with My payment methods under Quick access" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/right-menu/my-payment-methods.png" loading="lazy" />

The **Payment methods** page opens on its **My payment methods** tab. Your default payment method is shown in the **Default payment method** section, above a table listing the other payment methods saved to your customer account. You can:

- add a payment method.
- change your default payment method.
- modify your payment method description.
- delete a payment method.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:cp-access */}
### Register a payment method

When you place your first order, you are asked to save a payment method to ensure that your service can be renewed via automatic payments.

This payment method is then used by default for all of your renewals and is offered to pay for new orders.

You can save other payment methods so that they are suggested when you place new orders and used by default for future payments.

{/* CP-STEPS-START:register-payment-method */}

You can save two different payment method types:

- Credit card
- PayPal® account

To do this, simply click <code className="action">Add a payment method</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="My payment methods tab, with the Add a payment method button" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-payment-methods/console/managepaymentmethods2.png" loading="lazy" />

Choose the payment method you want to use.

Follow the steps for saving a payment method. In the first step, you are asked to check the option <code className="action">I want to choose this as my default payment method as soon as it is validated</code>, so that it is used for future orders and automatic payments.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:register-payment-method */}
#### Credit card

{/* CP-STEPS-START:credit-card */}

To register a new bank card, you will be redirected to our payment provider's secure interface. A small amount of money is deducted from your bank account to check your card details and their validity.

No amount will be charged and your credit card will be activated in a few minutes.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:credit-card */}
If a credit card payment is refused, see our [Billing and payment FAQ](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/faq-billing#card-payment-refused).

#### PayPal account

{/* CP-STEPS-START:paypal-account */}

Select <code className="action">PayPal</code> as your payment method, then click on the button <code className="action">PayPal</code>. A popup window will then open to log in to your PayPal® account and register it as an authorized payment method with OVHcloud.

Your PayPal® account will be activated within a few minutes.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:paypal-account */}
### Change your default payment method

{/* CP-STEPS-START:change-default-payment-method */}

Payments for your services' renewal bills are always taken via your default payment method. If you would like to change it, you will need to add a new payment method in the OVHcloud Console.

To do this, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, to the right of the new payment method, then <code className="action">Make this the default payment method</code>.

> **How do I replace my default payment method with another one?**
>
> - Step 1: add the new payment method
> - Step 2: set the new payment method as the default payment method
> - Step 3: delete the old payment method
>

{/* CP-STEPS-END:change-default-payment-method */}
### Delete a payment method

{/* CP-STEPS-START:delete-payment-method */}

If you no longer want to use one of your payment methods, you can delete it by clicking the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of it: in the **Actions** column of the table or, for your default payment method, in the **Default payment method** section. Then click <code className="action">Delete</code> and, in the **Delete a payment method** window, click <code className="action">Confirm</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Menu of the default payment method, with Edit description and Delete" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-payment-methods/console/managepaymentmethods4.png" loading="lazy" />

If you would like to delete all of your payment methods, all of your services must be set to [manual renewal](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/automatic-renewal#auto-vs-manual).

{/* CP-STEPS-END:delete-payment-method */}
#### Delete a payment method via the OVHcloud API

You can delete a payment method via the API by connecting to <ApiLink>OVHcloud API</ApiLink>.

Start by getting the payment method’s ID:

<Api version="v1" section="/me" method="GET" route={"/me/payment/method"} />

Then delete the payment method using the ID you obtained in the previous step:

<Api version="v1" section="/me" method="DELETE" route={"/me/payment/method/\{paymentMethodId\}"} />

{/* CP-STEPS-START:delete-via-api-edit-description */}

:::info
For more information, please refer to our guide [Getting started with the OVHcloud API](/guides/manage-and-operate/api/first-steps).

If you experience any difficulties finding your payment methods via the OVHcloud API, use the <code className="action">Edit description</code> function by clicking the <code className="action">⋮</code> button next to your payment method on the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/payment-methods">My payment methods</ManagerLink> page. In the **Modify the description** window, enter the new description, then click <code className="action">Save the description</code>.

:::

{/* CP-STEPS-END:delete-via-api-edit-description */}
### Prepaid account <a name="prepaid-account"></a>

#### What is the prepaid account?

{/* CP-STEPS-START:what-is-prepaid-account */}

The *prepaid account* is added to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/payment-methods">My payment methods</ManagerLink> page as soon as it is created. It allows you to credit your customer account in advance and use these funds to pay for your orders and renewal invoices.

:::info
To pay for a new order using the credit on your prepaid account, the purchase order amount must be less than or equal to the available credit.
:::

By crediting your account regularly, you will ensure that your services will never be interrupted for default on their [automatic renewal](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/automatic-renewal#auto-vs-manual).

To do this, simply go to the **Payment methods** page of the OVHcloud Console:

- Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/payment-methods">My payment methods</ManagerLink> page.
- Select the <code className="action">My prepaid account</code> tab.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="My prepaid account tab of the Payment methods page, with its balances and transactions" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-payment-methods/console/prepaid-account.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:what-is-prepaid-account */}
#### How does it work?

The prepaid account is used as a priority to pay for your automatic renewals, until the available funds are exhausted.

When you have services set to *automatic renewal*, your bill will be deducted from your prepaid account as soon as possible.

If you do not have enough funds, your prepaid account balance does not go negative: the remaining amount becomes a debt, pending payment, which you must settle with another payment method.

If you have a valid payment method saved in your customer account, this amount will be automatically deducted within 24 hours and your balance reset. This will not affect the status of your services.

{/* CP-STEPS-START:how-does-it-work */}

However, if you have not saved a payment method, you will need to pay the balance within 7 days from <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/balance">the OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink> to avoid any service interruptions.

If you do not have a registered payment method, we recommend setting an **alert threshold**, to ensure that you have sufficient funds for your next bills. You will be alerted by email when your account balance falls below this threshold.

In the <code className="action">My prepaid account</code> tab, click <code className="action">Set up an alert</code> in the **Alert threshold** section. In the **Set up an alert** window, enter the amount in the **Alert threshold amount (EUR)** field, then click <code className="action">Set threshold</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Set up an alert window, with the alert threshold amount field and Set threshold" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-payment-methods/console/warning_prepaid_account.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:how-does-it-work */}
If the credit available on your prepaid account falls below the defined limit, you will be sent a notification email immediately.

#### How do I credit my prepaid account?

{/* CP-STEPS-START:credit-prepaid-account */}

In the <code className="action">My prepaid account</code> tab, click on the <code className="action">Credit</code> button.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="My prepaid account tab, with the Credit button under the prepaid account amount" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-payment-methods/console/credit-prepaid-account.png" loading="lazy" />

In the window that pops up, enter the amount you want to credit, click on <code className="action">Next</code>, then <code className="action">Order</code>.

On the purchase order that appears, select the payment method of your choice and pay for your order.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:credit-prepaid-account */}
#### How do I request a transfer of a credit note or a refund?

{/* CP-STEPS-START:request-refund */}

When an order is cancelled, the refund is made, where possible, to your original payment method, unless you paid by bank transfer. In other cases, depending on the amount and the type of request, it is credited to your prepaid account.

You can request a transfer of the credit notes and refunds available on your prepaid account.

However, amounts that you credited to your prepaid account yourself (for example, a bank transfer made twice) cannot be refunded from the OVHcloud Console. To get them back, contact us by [creating a support ticket](/links/support-contact).

:::warning
Before making your request, check that you have a [valid payment method](#register-a-payment-method) saved in your customer account.
:::

To do this:

- go to the <code className="action">My prepaid account</code> tab;
- in the **Transactions on my prepaid account** section, find the row corresponding to the credit note or refund in question;
- click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, to the right of this row, then <code className="action">Request a refund</code>;
- in the window that pops up, click <code className="action">Confirm request</code>.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:request-refund */}
