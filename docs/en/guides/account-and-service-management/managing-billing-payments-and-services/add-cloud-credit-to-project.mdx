## Objective

With the *cloud credit* option you can allocate a specific amount to your Public Cloud project which will serve as the default payment method for the [billing of the project](/guides/public-cloud/cross-functional/analyze-billing).<br/> This means that cloud credit is debited first and any outstanding amount will then be paid by other available payment methods. (Please consult the [Public Cloud billing guides](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/best-practices) to learn more about this topic.)

**This guide explains how to add credit on to your Public Cloud account using a voucher, or by generating a purchase order.**

## Requirements

- A [Public Cloud project](/links/public-cloud/public-cloud) in your OVHcloud account

[[cpnav:publiccloud-credits-vouchers]]

## Instructions

{/* CP-STEPS-START:open-credits-vouchers */}
In the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, click <code className="action">Cost center</code>, then the <code className="action">Credits & vouchers</code> tab. The buttons of this tab are not available for a project in discovery mode.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:open-credits-vouchers */}

:::warning
Note that credit added in this section cannot be transferred or refunded. It does not have monetary value, and any credit that has not been used within 13 months of purchase will be lost.

:::

### Adding credit

{/* CP-STEPS-START:add-credit */}
Click on the button <code className="action">Buy Public Cloud credit</code>.

In the **Buy Public Cloud credit** window, enter the amount you wish to add, in your currency (ex VAT), in the **Amount to credit** field. Click on <code className="action">Generate a purchase order</code> to confirm.

A purchase order will then be generated. Click on the link in the green notification box to view it in a new tab and initiate the payment.

Once the payment is processed, your credit will appear in the <code className="action">Credits & vouchers</code> tab.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:add-credit */}

### Redeeming a voucher

{/* CP-STEPS-START:redeem-voucher */}
Click on the button <code className="action">Use a voucher</code>.

In the **Use a voucher** window, enter your voucher code in the **Your voucher** field and click on <code className="action">Activate</code>.

The voucher balance will appear in the <code className="action">Credits & vouchers</code> tab.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:redeem-voucher */}

:::info
Vouchers are generally valid for only one month; you should use the voucher as soon as possible. The voucher balance will be applied before the Public Cloud credit.

:::
