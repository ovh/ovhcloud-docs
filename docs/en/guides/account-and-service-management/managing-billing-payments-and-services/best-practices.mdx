## Objective

This guide contains the essential elements for managing your account and the billing of your OVHcloud services.

**Find the best practices to adopt in order to avoid any service interruptions or irregularities in your OVHcloud account.**

## Requirements

- Active OVHcloud services in your OVHcloud account

## Instructions

### Account settings

#### Account security

[Create a strong password](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/manage-ovh-password#generate-a-strong-password) which will ensure your OVHcloud account's privacy.

[Enable two-factor authentication](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/secure-ovhcloud-account-with-2fa) to prevent any fraudulent access.

For the security measures to be efficient, make sure your email addresses (main and backup) and your mobile number are **up-to-date and accessible**.

#### Personal information

Your personal information (postal address, email address, etc.) must be up-to-date in your customer account to guarantee the security and proper functioning of your services.

Verify your personal information on the <ManagerLink to="/#/account/useraccount/infos">My profile</ManagerLink> page.

For more information, read the guide [Securing my OVHcloud account and managing my personal information](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/all-about-username#how-to-manage-your-personal-details).

Ensure your email address is always **up to date, accessible and secured**. Email messages from our services will be sent to this address, including the ones meant to reset your Control Panel's password.

:::warning
Add a **backup email** to your personal information to prevent any loss of access to your <ManagerLink to="/#/account/useraccount/infos">OVHcloud Control Panel</ManagerLink>.

:::

Emails sent by OVHcloud are also visible on the <ManagerLink to="/#/communication">My messages</ManagerLink> page.

#### Automatic renewal

:::info
In order to prevent any service interruption and for the good functioning of the auto-renewal, [register a payment method](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-payment-methods) in your <ManagerLink to="/#/billing/payment/method">OVHcloud Control Panel</ManagerLink>.

Check regularly that your payment method is **valid**.

:::

For more information, please follow the instructions of these guides:

- [Managing renewal for OVHcloud services](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/automatic-renewal)
- [Managing payment methods](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-payment-methods)

If you decide not to activate automatic renewal on your services, you will receive notifications prompting you to make a **manual payment** to either renew your service in advance or pay your pending bill.

### Order follow-up

Did you know that you can [track your orders](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/ovh-orders) from the <ManagerLink to="/#/billing/orders">My orders</ManagerLink> page?

You will find out the steps of delivery and you will know if actions are required from you.

### Service termination

If you want to stop using one of your services, you can either cancel it, set its renewal to manual mode or delete it immediately from the <ManagerLink to="/#/billing/autorenew/services">My offers and services</ManagerLink> page. Click on the <code className="action">...</code> button corresponding to the service you want to act on, then click on one of the following buttons:

- <code className="action">Configure renewal</code>: On the following screen, you'll be able to switch the `Renewal type` of your service to `Manual`. Then click on <code className="action">Next</code> and <code className="action">Confirm</code>. Before the expiration date of your service, you will receive several notifications by email. If you don't renew your service, it will be **permanently** deleted.
- <code className="action">Cancel my subscription</code>: On the following screen, specify the reasons for your cancellation request, then click on <code className="action">Confirm</code>. On the expiration date of your service, it will be **permanently** deleted.
- <code className="action">Delete immediately</code>: On the following screen, click on <code className="action">Confirm</code>. You will be sent a confirmation email. After your validation, your service will be **immediately and permanently** deleted.

To get more information about the termination of your services, consult the [How to cancel your OVHcloud services](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/how-to-cancel-services) guide.

### Account closure

If you want to definitely close your OVHcloud personal account, first check that it does not contain any active services, pending invoices or ongoing orders.<br/>
Then, use [this form](https://www.ovh.co.uk/personal-data-protection/exercise-your-rights) to ask for the permanent deletion of your account. Select to exercise the "Right of deletion" from the list of proposals.
