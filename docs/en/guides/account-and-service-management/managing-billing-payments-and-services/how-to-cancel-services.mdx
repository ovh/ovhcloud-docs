## Objective

You want to cancel one or more OVHcloud services. This guide will show you how to cancel your subscription via the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/manage-services">My offers and services</ManagerLink> page.

- First read the section "[What happens when an OVHcloud service is cancelled?](#consequences)".
- Follow the steps described in the section "[How do I cancel my subscription?](#terminate)".

### What happens when an OVHcloud service is cancelled? <a name="consequences"></a>

The “termination” of an OVHcloud service implies that it will be **permanently** discontinued at the end of the current subscription (with an exception, however, for [domain names](#domain)).
You can cancel your service or [stop a cancellation request](#cancel) up to 24 hours before the subscription end date.

If this is an email solution, a server or a web hosting plan, all of the data concerned will be deleted **without any possibility of recovery**.<br/>
If it is a domain name, it will be recirculated and can be **ordered by other customers**.

Before cancelling a service, you must be **certain**:

- The operation will concern a service that you no longer want to use.
- That you have a **functional** solution to replace it.
- That you have backed up all of the data concerned.

<a name="domain"></a>

:::info
**Domain name cancellation**

Following your termination and the end of the current subscription, your generic domain name (.com, .org, .net, etc.) will remain reserved for a "grace period" (approximately 7 days) and will then receive the "redemption" status (approximately 35 to 40 additional days). This means that only you will be able to renew it during these periods. For more details, please read the [FAQ](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/automatic-renewal#faq) at the bottom of our guide “[How to renew my OVHcloud services](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/automatic-renewal)”.

:::

## Requirements

- You must be the "administrative contact" of the service to be cancelled (for more information, see [this guide](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/managing-contacts#definition)).
- Your OVHcloud services to be cancelled are active (not suspended due to payment default).

## Instructions

The cancellation procedure is identical for the majority of OVHcloud services.

However, there are **special cases** that are detailed [below](#specific-cases) in this guide.

### How do I cancel my subscription? <a name="terminate"></a>

{/* CP-STEPS-START:cancel-subscription */}

To cancel your service subscription, open the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/manage-services">My offers and services</ManagerLink> page.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Account menu of the OVHcloud Console, with My offers and services in Quick access" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/right-menu/my-solutions-and-services.png" loading="lazy" />

Next, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, to the right of the service you want to cancel, then <code className="action">Cancel my subscription</code> (<code className="action">Cancel subscription</code> for a service under commitment).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Menu of a service in the Actions column, with Cancel my subscription" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/right-column/my-solutions-and-services/cancel-en.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Terminate service** window, select the main reason for your termination and, if you wish, explain it in the optional text field. Answer the question **What do you plan to do?**, then click <code className="action">Terminate</code>.

:::warning
When your service is terminated, you will lose all data associated with it.
:::

:::info
Learning your opinion is essential for us in order to develop our services to suit your needs and expectations.

Please tell us more about your personal experience with OVHcloud by filling in the cancellation form. We thank you in advance for your contribution to the improvement of our products.

:::

Your service will be cancelled on the date shown in the **Effective date** column of the table on the **Manage my services** page. If you do not see the status **Termination requested**, please refresh the page.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Service with termination requested in the services table: status, renewal and effective date" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/right-column/my-solutions-and-services/cancellation-request.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:cancel-subscription */}

### Special cases <a name="specific-cases"></a>

#### MX Plan email <a name="mxplan"></a>

Most MX Plan services are linked to a web hosting plan. If this is the case and you would like to delete this email option, follow [the instructions in this guide](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/activate-email-hosting#deleting-the-email-solution-linked-to-your-web-hosting-plan).

**Warning**: Deleting an MX Plan will permanently delete all of the data associated with it, and will not result in any refunds.

If your MX Plan email solution was ordered independently of any hosting plan, follow [the procedure detailed above](#terminate).

#### Email Pro <a name="emailpro"></a>

{/* CP-STEPS-START:cancel-email-pro */}

An [Email Pro](/links/web/email-pro) service is cancelled account by account. In the **Subscription** section of the platform's <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, click <code className="action">Manage my service accounts</code>. On the **Service management** page that opens, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button of each account, then <code className="action">Terminate at expiration</code>. The account will be suspended on its expiry date. To cancel the whole platform, first delete all its accounts and associated domains, then click <code className="action">Terminate service</code> in the **Subscription** section of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:cancel-email-pro */}

For details, follow the instructions in our guide "[Managing the billing for your Email Pro accounts](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/manage-billing-emailpro#deleting-accounts)".

#### Hosted Exchange <a name="hosted"></a>

{/* CP-STEPS-START:cancel-hosted-exchange */}

A [Hosted Exchange](/links/web/emails-hosted-exchange) service is cancelled account by account. In the **Subscription** section of the platform's <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, click <code className="action">Manage my service accounts</code>. On the **Service management** page that opens, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button of each account, then <code className="action">Terminate at expiration</code>. The account will be suspended on its expiry date. To cancel the whole platform, first delete all its accounts and associated domains, then click <code className="action">Terminate service</code> in the **Subscription** section of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:cancel-hosted-exchange */}

For details, follow the instructions in our guide "[Managing the billing for Exchange accounts](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/manage-billing-exchange#deleting-accounts)".

#### Private Exchange <a name="private"></a>

{/* CP-STEPS-START:cancel-private-exchange */}

[Private Exchange](/links/web/emails-private-exchange) accounts are renewed monthly only: delete them from the <code className="action">Email account</code> sub-tab of the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab (<code className="action">⋮</code> button of the account, then <code className="action">Delete this account</code>), with no prior cancellation. Only configured accounts can be deleted. To cancel the whole platform, first delete its accounts and associated domains, then click <code className="action">Terminate service</code> in the **Subscription** section of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:cancel-private-exchange */}

For details, follow the instructions in our guide "[Managing the billing for Exchange accounts](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/manage-billing-exchange#deleting-accounts-1)".

<Region zones={['eu']}>
#### Microsoft 365 (CSP1) <a name="office-csp1"></a>

{/* CP-STEPS-START:cancel-microsoft-365-csp1 */}

For your [Microsoft 365 (CSP1)](/links/web/ms365) solution, delete a licence from the <code className="action">Licenses</code> tab of your licence group (<code className="action">⋮</code> button of the user concerned, then <code className="action">Delete</code>), or cancel the whole group from the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/manage-services">My offers and services</ManagerLink> page (<code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, then <code className="action">Cancel my subscription</code>). The current month's consumption is billed at the end of the month.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:cancel-microsoft-365-csp1 */}

For details, follow the instructions in our guide "[How to manage Microsoft 365 licence groups at OVHcloud](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-office/office365-csp1#manage-your-subscriptions)".

#### Office 365 Reseller (CSP2) <a name="office-csp2"></a>

{/* CP-STEPS-START:cancel-office-365-reseller-csp2 */}

For your [Office 365 Reseller (CSP2)](/links/web/ms365-reseller) solution, in the <code className="action">Licenses</code> tab, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button of a subscription, then <code className="action">Delete subscription</code> to cancel the subscription and all its licences. The <code className="action">Change quantity</code> entry of the same menu lets you change the number of licences.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:cancel-office-365-reseller-csp2 */}

For details, follow the instructions in our guide "[Managing a group of OVHcloud Office 365 Reseller licences (CSP2)](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-office/office365-csp2#manage-your-subscriptions)".
</Region>

#### SMS <a name="sms"></a>

There is no cancellation procedure for [SMS offers](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/sms/). An SMS account does not generate a monthly subscription or billing. SMS credits have an unlimited lifetime.

If you would like to delete an SMS account, please create a support request from the OVHcloud Console.

#### VMware on OVHcloud <a name="hostedprivatecloud-vmware"></a>

{/* CP-STEPS-START:cancel-vmware-on-ovhcloud */}

For your [VMware on OVHcloud](/links/hosted-private-cloud/vmware) solution, click <code className="action">Cancel the service</code> in the **Subscription** section of your service's dashboard. Then confirm via the link you receive by email, which is valid for 72 hours. All data is deleted as soon as you confirm. As any month started is due, make your request from the 25th of the month onwards.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:cancel-vmware-on-ovhcloud */}

For details, follow the instructions in our guide "[How to cancel your Private Cloud offer](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/cancel-private-cloud)".

#### Public Cloud <a name="publiccloud"></a>

{/* CP-STEPS-START:delete-public-cloud-services */}

Each [Public Cloud](/links/public-cloud/public-cloud) service can be deleted from the OVHcloud Console. Click <code className="action">Public Cloud</code> in the top bar to open your project.

Select a service on the left, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, to the right of the service concerned, then <code className="action">Delete</code>, as in the example below for an instance:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Menu of an instance in the Actions column of the Instances page, with Delete" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/how-to-cancel-services/console/pci-deletion-en.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:delete-public-cloud-services */}

OVHcloud Public Cloud is based on the principle of *Pay-as-you-go* (you can find more details on our page [information on Public Cloud billing method](/guides/public-cloud/cross-functional/analyze-billing)). At the end of the month, you only pay for the actual resource usage time.

You can also [view your current resource usage](/guides/public-cloud/cross-functional/analyze-billing#view-your-current-resource-usage) in the OVHcloud Console, and receive email alerts if your resource usage is projected to exceed a certain threshold.

## How do I stop my subscription cancellation? <a name="cancel"></a>

:::warning
You can revoke a cancellation request up to 24 hours before the subscription end date.

:::

{/* CP-STEPS-START:stop-cancellation */}

To abort a cancellation request, open the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/manage-services">My offers and services</ManagerLink> page.

Next, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, to the right of the service for which a cancellation request is being made, then <code className="action">Stop cancellation</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Menu of a service whose termination is requested, with Stop cancellation" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/right-column/my-solutions-and-services/cancel-termination-en.png" loading="lazy" />

Finally, in the **Cancel termination** window, click <code className="action">Confirm cancellation</code>.

A termination of a cancellation procedure is effective immediately. Please refresh the **Manage my services** page if you still see the **Termination requested** status.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:stop-cancellation */}

## FAQ

<details>

<summary>I cannot cancel a service. What should I do?</summary>

If you are unable to cancel a service (the service's ⋮ menu does not offer <code className="action">Cancel my subscription</code>), it may be one of the specific cases detailed [previously in this guide](#specific-cases).
Also check that the following conditions are met:

- You must be the “administrative contact” for the service. For more information, see [this guide](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/managing-contacts#definition).
- The service is not suspended, mainly because of a bill that has not been settled. Check that you are up to date in the [payments](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/invoice-management#pay-bills) and [renewals](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/automatic-renewal#actions) of your services.

If you are still unable to cancel your service, contact our support teams by opening a ticket from the [Help Centre](https://help.ovhcloud.com/csm?id=csm_cases_requests).

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I cancel all my services?</summary>

You must terminate each service individually. You cannot cancel several services at once, or request a global cancellation from our customer support teams.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I delete my OVHcloud account?</summary>

Closing your OVHcloud customer account and deleting your personal data is only possible if:

{/* CP-STEPS-START:delete-account-prerequisites */}

1. You no longer have any services listed on the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/manage-services">My offers and services</ManagerLink> page. Otherwise, you will need to request their termination and wait for their deletion.
2. You do not have any invoices awaiting payment. Otherwise, you will need to pay for them on the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/balance">My balance</ManagerLink> page, by clicking <code className="action">Settle all my balance</code>.
3. You have no pending orders. Check it on the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/orders">My orders</ManagerLink> page.
4. Your prepaid account has no credit balance. Check it in the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/payment-methods/prepaid-account">My prepaid account</ManagerLink> tab of the **Payment methods** page.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:delete-account-prerequisites */}

If the above conditions are met, you can request to delete your OVHcloud account and the related data by following these steps:

1. Log in to the [OVHcloud Help Centre](https://help.ovhcloud.com/csm?id=csm_get_help).
2. Click the <code className="action">Create ticket</code> button.
3. Select the reason `Manage your OVHcloud customer account`.
4. Specify `I want to close my OVHcloud account` and follow the steps described.

</details>
