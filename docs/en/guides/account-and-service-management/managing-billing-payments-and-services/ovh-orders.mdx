## Objective

When you place an order, you can track and interact with it from the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/orders">My orders</ManagerLink> page.

**This guide explains how to manage orders via the OVHcloud Console.**

:::info
Depending on your place of residence and its local legislation, as well as the products concerned, some details may vary from the information provided in this guide, and some information may not apply to your situation. If you have any doubts, please refer to the OVHcloud contracts (available in the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/manage-services/contracts">Contracts</ManagerLink> tab of the **Manage my services** page).

:::

## Requirements

- At least one OVHcloud product order

## Instructions

### Purchase order

The purchase order is created when you place your order. It lists the products ordered, their price and any reductions you may receive.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="OVH purchase order" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/managing-ovh-orders/order01.png" loading="lazy" />

|Number|Description|
|---|---|
|1|Purchase order number, creation date and expiration date. If it has expired, you will have to reorder.|
|2|Your billing details. You must be logged in to view and pay your purchase order.|
|3|Payment methods saved to your customer account. For more information, please refer to our guide [Managing payment methods](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-payment-methods).|
|4|Payment methods available. You will only be offered payment methods that are authorised in the account’s country of origin, or those linked to the account type you have registered.|

You can view your purchase order at any time on the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/orders">My orders</ManagerLink> page, as explained below.

### Access purchase orders via the OVHcloud Console

{/* CP-STEPS-START:access-purchase-orders */}

Open the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/orders">My orders</ManagerLink> page from the left-hand menu of the OVHcloud Console, or from its home page: in the **Most recent order** section, click <code className="action">View my orders</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="OVHcloud Console home page, with the Most recent order section and View my orders" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/managing-ovh-orders/console/huborders.png" loading="lazy" />

The page lists the orders you have placed:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="My orders page, with the table of the orders placed" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/right-column/initials/order-tab.png" loading="lazy" />

On this page, you will see the following information:

- the date the purchase order was created
- the order number
- the order status
- the purchase order amount, including VAT
- the order's expiration date

The main order statuses are listed below:

|Status|Definition|
|---|---|
|Payment not received|This status means that the payment has not yet been received for the purchase order. If you do not pay, the order will expire on the date indicated on the purchase order. After this period, if you want to make this purchase, you will simply have to place an order again.|
|Confirmation|Your order is in the process of being verified manually by our internal services (working days only). The validation will be complete once this step is finalised. If the operation cannot be finalised, you will be sent an email request to provide supporting documents.|
|Being processed|Your order is in the process of being delivered — please wait. Depending on the service you have ordered, this step may take from a few minutes to several days (for example, for telephony, domain name transfers or customised servers).|
|Complete|You have received your order and your access details have been sent to you by email (if access details are required).|

The **Status** column can also show **Cancelling...**, **Cancelled**, **Awaiting documents**, **Order expired** or **Error**.

You can also view the purchase order in HTML format by clicking the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, to the right of your order, then <code className="action">View</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Menu of an order in the Actions column, with Order tracking and View" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/managing-ovh-orders/console/order04.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:access-purchase-orders */}

### Track your order

{/* CP-STEPS-START:track-order */}

After generating your purchase order, you can track it from the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/orders">My orders</ManagerLink> page:

- Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, to the right of your order.
- Select <code className="action">Order tracking</code>. Order tracking can also be displayed by clicking the status of an order in the **Status** column.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Menu of an order in the Actions column, with Order tracking highlighted" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/managing-ovh-orders/console/order05b.png" loading="lazy" />

The tracking page of your order opens, allowing you to follow it in four steps: **Your order has been processed**, **Your order is confirmed**, **The product is being delivered** and **The product is available**. Below the steps, the **Your order history** section lists the dated events of your order, and the **Order summary** section lists the products ordered.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Tracking page of an order, with its four steps completed" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/managing-ovh-orders/console/order06.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:track-order */}

### Apply the right of withdrawal

Article 14.1.2 of our [General Terms of Service](https://contract.eu.ovhapis.com/1.0/pdf/contrat_genServices-gb.pdf) states that "*Pursuant to the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, the Client enjoys the statutory right of cancellation. Client may exercise this right, without having to justify its reasons nor pay any penalties other than any return fees, within a period of fourteen (14) days from the day after the Order.*"

The terms and conditions for the right of withdrawal can be found in the remainder of that article.

:::warning
The right of withdrawal applies only to individuals and applies only to new orders. You cannot therefore argue:

- for renewals;
- if your OVHcloud customer account is [different from Private](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/all-about-username#changing-the-account-type).

In addition, the right of withdrawal does not apply to unpaid orders, as they are automatically cancelled when the purchase order expires.

Finally, it does not apply to certain services (notably [domain names](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/domains/) and [Sectigo SSL certificates](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/web-hosting/options/ssl/)). When you order these products, you are therefore asked to expressly waive your right of withdrawal, in accordance with article L.221-28 of the French Consumer Code.

:::

{/* CP-STEPS-START:apply-right-of-withdrawal */}

If the eligibility criteria for the right of withdrawal are met, you can apply it directly from the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/orders">My orders</ManagerLink> page:

- Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, to the right of your order.
- Select <code className="action">Cancel the order</code>.

To confirm the order cancellation, tick the box <code className="action">Yes, I want to cancel that order.</code>, then click <code className="action">Confirm</code>.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:apply-right-of-withdrawal */}

:::info
You will be refunded the amount initially paid, minus the days of services consumed on a pro rata basis, within 30 days of the withdrawal request.

:::
