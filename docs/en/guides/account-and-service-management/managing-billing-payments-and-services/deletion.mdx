## Objective

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you can exercise the right to delete your OVHcloud account and all the services it contains.

**This guide explains how to immediately and irreversibly delete your OVHcloud services as part of a GDPR data deletion request.**

## Requirements

- You must have verified that all your data has been backed up.

[[cpnav:billing-services-console]]

## Instructions

{/* CP-STEPS-START:list-services */}

To list your services, open the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/manage-services">My services</ManagerLink> page.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:list-services */}

:::danger
The steps described below explain how to delete OVHcloud services **immediately** and **irreversibly**.

It is your responsibility to check in advance that you have a backup of your data and/or that you have a replacement service.

Unless otherwise stated in the product-specific instructions, deleting an OVHcloud service does not entitle you to a refund.

:::

### Web Cloud

**Click on the title corresponding to the desired product to expand the instructions.**

<details>

<summary>Domain name</summary>

{/* CP-STEPS-START:delete-domain-name */}

1. Open the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/manage-services">My services</ManagerLink> page.
1. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, to the right of the concerned domain name, then <code className="action">Cancel my subscription</code>. In the **Terminate service** window, select the <code className="action">Cancel service now.</code> option, then click <code className="action">Yes, cancel</code>.
1. A confirmation email is automatically sent to the main email address of the OVHcloud account. Click on the link in this email to confirm the immediate deletion.

A period of 45 to 60 days, depending on the domain extension, may be observed before the service is effectively deleted.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:delete-domain-name */}

</details>

<details>

<summary>Web Hosting</summary>

:::danger
Deleting a web hosting service results in the permanent deletion of all its data.

:::

{/* CP-STEPS-START:delete-web-hosting */}

1. Open the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/manage-services">My services</ManagerLink> page.
1. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, to the right of the concerned hosting plan, then <code className="action">Cancel my subscription</code>.
1. In the **Terminate service** window, select the main reason for your termination, answer the question **What do you plan to do?**, then click <code className="action">Terminate</code>. Your hosting plan will be terminated on its expiry date.

For an older web hosting plan, the menu offers <code className="action">Delete web hosting plan immediately</code> instead of <code className="action">Cancel my subscription</code>. Click it, then click <code className="action">Terminate</code> in the **Delete my web hosting plan immediately** window. A confirmation email is automatically sent to the main email address of the OVHcloud account. Click on the link in this email to confirm the immediate deletion.

A period of 45 days may be observed before the service is effectively deleted.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:delete-web-hosting */}

:::info
**Pro rata refund**

If you delete your hosting plan before its expiry date, the unused period is refunded to you. This refund is only issued on your explicit request, and provided that all outstanding amounts have been settled.

The refunded amount is calculated pro rata to the remaining period, applying the rate corresponding to the time actually elapsed:

- deletion during the first 12 months: 12-month plan rate;
- deletion between month 13 and month 24: 24-month plan rate;
- deletion between month 25 and month 48: 48-month plan rate.

For more information, please refer to article 6.3 of the [Specific Terms of Service for Web Hosting](https://storage.gra.cloud.ovh.net/v1/AUTH_325716a587c64897acbef9a4a4726e38/contracts/445cd16-contrat_partWeb-GB-4.0.pdf).
:::

<details>

<summary>Zimbra</summary>

:::danger
Deleting your Zimbra Mail service results in the permanent deletion of all emails and data it contains.

:::

{/* CP-STEPS-START:delete-zimbra */}

1. Open <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/email/zimbra">Zimbra</ManagerLink> in the OVHcloud Console and select the concerned platform.
1. Click the **Email account** tab. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of each email account, then <code className="action">Terminate</code>. In the window that opens, click <code className="action">Terminate</code>: the account and all of its data will be permanently erased when its subscription ends.
1. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of each email account to delete, then <code className="action">Delete</code>. In the **Delete account** window, click <code className="action">Delete</code>.
1. Once all email accounts are deleted, click the **Associated domains** tab. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of each domain, then <code className="action">Delete</code>. In the **Delete domain** window, click <code className="action">Delete</code>.
1. Once these actions are completed, you can create a [support ticket via the help center](https://help.ovhcloud.com/csm?id=csm_get_help) to request an accelerated deletion of the service.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:delete-zimbra */}

</details>

<details>

<summary>Email Pro</summary>

:::danger
Deleting your Email Pro service results in the permanent deletion of all emails and data it contains.

:::

{/* CP-STEPS-START:delete-email-pro */}

1. Open <ManagerLink to="/#/web/email_pro">Email Pro</ManagerLink> in the OVHcloud Control Panel and select the concerned platform.
1. Click on the <code className="action">Email accounts</code> tab. Click on the <code className="action">...</code> button to the right of each email account to delete and then on <code className="action">Reset this account</code>.
1. Once all email accounts are deleted, click on the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab. Click on the <code className="action">...</code> button to the right of each domain and then on <code className="action">Delete this domain</code>.
1. When the associated domains have been deleted, click on the <code className="action">Actions</code> button in the top right and then on <code className="action">Cancel</code>. Follow the steps described.
1. Once these actions are completed, you can create a [support ticket via the help center](https://help.ovhcloud.com/csm?id=csm_get_help) to request an accelerated deletion of the service.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:delete-email-pro */}

</details>

<summary>Microsoft Exchange</summary>

:::danger
Deleting your Exchange service results in the permanent deletion of all emails and data it contains.

1. Once these actions are completed, you can create a [support ticket via the help center](https://help.ovhcloud.com/csm?id=csm_get_help) to request an accelerated deletion of the service.

### Bare Metal Cloud

<details>

<summary>Virtual Private Server (VPS)</summary>

:::danger
The immediate deletion of a VPS results in the permanent and irrevocable deletion of all its data.

:::

{/* CP-STEPS-START:delete-vps */}

1. Open <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/bare-metal/vps">VPS</ManagerLink> in the OVHcloud Console and select the concerned VPS.
1. In the **Subscription** section, click <code className="action">Terminate</code>. In the **Cancel your subscription** window, select the <code className="action">Cancel service now.</code> option, then click <code className="action">Confirm</code>. This option is only offered for a VPS without commitment: if your VPS offer carries a commitment, the window only offers to end the commitment and the service on the expiry date, or to switch to a commitment-free subscription.
1. A confirmation email is automatically sent to the main email address of the OVHcloud account. Click on the link in this email to confirm the immediate deletion.

A period of 30 days may be observed before the service is effectively deleted.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:delete-vps */}

</details>

<details>

<summary>Dedicated Server</summary>

:::danger
The deletion of a dedicated server results in the permanent and irrevocable deletion of all its data.

:::

{/* CP-STEPS-START:delete-dedicated-server */}

1. Open <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/bare-metal/dedicated-server">Dedicated servers</ManagerLink> in the OVHcloud Console and select the concerned server.
1. In the **Subscription** section, click <code className="action">Terminate</code>. In the window that opens, click <code className="action">Terminate</code>.
1. A confirmation email is automatically sent to the main email address of the OVHcloud account. Click on the link in this email to confirm the deletion.
1. Once these actions are completed, you can create a [support ticket via the help center](https://help.ovhcloud.com/csm?id=csm_get_help) to request an accelerated deletion of the service.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:delete-dedicated-server */}

</details>

<details>

<summary>vRack</summary>

{/* CP-STEPS-START:delete-vrack */}

1. Open <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/network/vrack">vRack</ManagerLink> in the OVHcloud Console and select the concerned vRack.
1. You must remove all services from a vRack in order to delete it. Click the **Attached services** tab, then click <code className="action">Detach</code> on the row of each service. In the **Detach service from vRack** window, click <code className="action">Detach</code>.
1. Once all services are detached from the vRack, click the **Overview** tab. In the **Subscription** section, click <code className="action">Terminate</code>. In the **Terminate vRack** window, click <code className="action">Terminate</code>.
1. A confirmation email is automatically sent to the main email address of the OVHcloud account. Click on the link in this email to confirm the deletion.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:delete-vrack */}

</details>

### Public Cloud

<details>

<summary>Public Cloud Project</summary>

:::danger
The immediate deletion of a Public Cloud project results in the permanent and irrevocable deletion of all its services, data, resources and settings.

:::

{/* CP-STEPS-START:delete-public-cloud-project */}

1. Open <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/public-cloud/project">Public Cloud</ManagerLink> in the OVHcloud Console and select the concerned project.
1. In the project's side menu, click <code className="action">Project Management</code>, then the **Settings** tab. In the **Delete project** section, click <code className="action">Delete project</code>. In the **Cancel project** window, click <code className="action">Yes, cancel</code>. A project with a Savings Plan cannot be deleted from the OVHcloud Console: the window offers <code className="action">Contact Customer Support</code> instead, to ask our Customer Service team how to delete it.
1. A confirmation email is automatically sent to the main email address of the OVHcloud account. Click on the link in this email to confirm the immediate deletion.

A period of 30 days may be observed before the services are effectively deleted.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:delete-public-cloud-project */}

</details>

### Telecom

<details>

<summary>SMS</summary>

There is no cancellation procedure for SMS offers. An SMS account does not generate any monthly subscription or billing. SMS credits have an unlimited lifespan.

To delete an SMS account, we invite you to create a [support ticket via the help center](https://help.ovhcloud.com/csm?id=csm_get_help).

</details>
