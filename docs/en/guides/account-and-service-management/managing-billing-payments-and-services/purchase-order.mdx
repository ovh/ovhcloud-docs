## Objective

## Instructions

### Purchase Order (PO) number

When purchasing goods or services, companies are required to validate purchase orders. Typically, this validation is done by returning a document (paper or digital format) on the company's letterhead that recalls the goods or services purchased and specifying an order number. This document is usually called **Purchase Order** and is abbreviated to **PO**.

For the company issuing the PO, the purpose of this document is to control purchases of goods and services from its suppliers, including by requiring that all invoices issued include a PO Number.

There are essentially two types of orders, described below.

#### Closed order / Purchase Order (PO)

This is a document confirming the order of goods or services for a fixed quantity and for a fixed duration.

For OVHcloud, the document must contain at least the following information:

- Company name of the contractor
- Order number
- Type(s) of services ordered
- Quantities
- Unit prices
- Effective date
- End date

#### Open order / Blanket Purchase Order (BPO)

This is a document confirming the order of goods or services for a variable quantity with a maximum amount and for a fixed duration.

For OVHcloud, the document must contain at least the following information:

- Company name of the contractor
- Order number
- Type(s) of services ordered
- Unit prices
- Total order amount
- Effective date
- End date

### How to enter a Purchase Order (PO) reference in the OVHcloud Console

{/* CP-STEPS-START:enter-po-reference */}

Open the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/orders">My orders</ManagerLink> page.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="OVHcloud Console home page, with the View my orders link under Most recent order" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/purchase-order/console/huborders.png" loading="lazy" />

Click the <code className="action">My internal references</code> tab and then click the <code className="action">Add an internal reference</code> button (<code className="action">Create an internal reference</code> if you have none yet).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="My internal references tab, with the Add an internal reference button" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/purchase-order/console/internalreference01.png" loading="lazy" />

You can use two different wordings, depending on whether you want to display the notation `Internal reference` **or** the notation `Purchase order` on your invoices.

Select either <code className="action">Create your internal reference</code> or <code className="action">Create your purchase order</code>.

Give your internal reference / purchase order a name in the field provided, enter a **start date** and an **end date** (the end date is excluded) then click <code className="action">Validate</code>.

:::info
You cannot create 2 internal references / purchase orders over the same time period.

:::

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Create an internal reference page, with the two wordings, the name field and the dates" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/purchase-order/console/internalreference02.png" loading="lazy" />

This reference will then appear on your next invoices, for the time period that you have set.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="OVHcloud invoice with its internal reference line highlighted" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/purchase-order/internalreference03.png" loading="lazy" />

From the <code className="action">My internal references</code> tab, you can edit or disable a reference that has already been created by clicking the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the reference concerned, in the **Actions** column.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Edit and Disable options of a reference, opened from its ⋮ button" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/purchase-order/console/internalreference04.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
If you want to disable or edit one reference for another **on the same time interval**, you must use the <code className="action">Edit</code> option to change the time interval of the first reference.

:::

{/* CP-STEPS-END:enter-po-reference */}
