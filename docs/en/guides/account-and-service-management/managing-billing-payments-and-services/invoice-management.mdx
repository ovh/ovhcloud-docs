## Objective

OVHcloud provides a space for you to view, manage and pay your various bills.

**Find out how to manage bills in the OVHcloud Console.**

:::info
Depending on your place of residence and its local legislation, as well as the products concerned, some details may vary from the information provided in this guide, and some information may not apply to your situation. If you have any doubts, please refer to the OVHcloud contracts (available in the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/manage-services/contracts">Contracts</ManagerLink> tab of the **Manage my services** page).

## Instructions

{/* CP-STEPS-START:instructions-my-bills */}

:::info
Each invoice is sent to you by email and accessible via a clickable link. You can then access it on the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/invoices">My bills</ManagerLink> page. All invoices are also available from the homepage of your OVHcloud Console.

:::

{/* CP-STEPS-END:instructions-my-bills */}
### Understanding your bill

Your OVHcloud service bill is sent to you following the payment of an order or a renewal. It summarises the amounts corresponding to the purchase or renewal of your products, as well as their periods of validity.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Bill detail" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/invoice-management/invoice_ovh.png" loading="lazy" />

|Number|Description|
|---|---|
|1|Invoice information: Reference, issue date, purchase order to which it refers, payment type and NIC handle concerned|
|2|Billing contact information|
|3|Subscription: A description of the service and the period billed|
|4|Description: The billed service reference|
|5|Quantity: The number of units of the service billed for|
|6|Unit price (ex. VAT) of a product|
|7|Total amount (incl. taxes) of the invoice|

### The billing section in your OVHcloud Console

#### View and manage bills

{/* CP-STEPS-START:view-manage-bills */}

To view your invoices, open the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/invoices">My bills</ManagerLink> page.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Account menu of the OVHcloud Console, with My bills in the Quick access list" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/right-menu/my-bills.png" loading="lazy" />

You will then land on the **Invoices** page, which summarises your bills:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Invoices page of the OVHcloud Console, with the table of invoices" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/invoice-management/console/billing_section.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
If an invoice is pending payment (`Unpaid` status), the amount due will be shown in red in the **Balance** column.

:::

On each row of the table, you will see the following information:

- the invoice number (**Invoice #**)
- the order number (**Order #**)
- the issue date (**Date**)
- its **Amount excl. tax**
- its **Amount incl. tax**
- the **Balance** to be settled
- the invoice **Status** (`Unpaid` or `Paid`)

By clicking the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, to the right of an invoice, you can also access these functions:

- <code className="action">View invoice</code>: The bill opens in a new tab in your browser.
- <code className="action">PDF</code>: A downloadable PDF version of your invoice will be generated automatically.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Menu of an invoice in the Actions column, with View invoice and PDF" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/invoice-management/console/actions_choices.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
If one of your bills is awaiting payment, you can pay it on the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/balance">Balance</ManagerLink> page.

:::

{/* CP-STEPS-END:view-manage-bills */}
##### **Filters**

{/* CP-STEPS-START:filters */}

Several filters are available. Click <code className="action">Filter</code> above the table to display them:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Filter options of the Invoices page, with the Column, Condition and Value fields" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/invoice-management/console/sort_filters.png" loading="lazy" />

To find a specific invoice, you can enter its reference, the associated order number or its issue date.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:filters */}
##### **Export**

{/* CP-STEPS-START:batch-actions */}

Using the <code className="action">Export</code> menu, you can export your invoice summary in *.csv* or *.pdf* format. The files created will show their amount, reference and issue date. For each export, a download link is sent to you by email.

If you want to export all of your invoices, click <code className="action">Export</code>, then select <code className="action">Export all as CSV</code> or <code className="action">Export all as PDF</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Export menu of the Invoices page, with Export all as CSV and PDF" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/invoice-management/console/export_all.png" loading="lazy" />

If you want to export only selected invoices, use the corresponding check boxes in the first column of the table to select the invoices you want to export. Two new actions, <code className="action">Export selection in CSV format</code> and <code className="action">Export selection in PDF format</code>, will then be available in the <code className="action">Export</code> menu.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="One invoice selected and the Export menu, with Export selection in CSV and PDF format" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/invoice-management/console/export_selection.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:batch-actions */}
#### Pay your bills <a name="pay-bills"></a>

{/* CP-STEPS-START:pay-bills */}

To pay bills that are awaiting payment, open the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/balance">Balance</ManagerLink> page and click <code className="action">Settle all my balance</code>. To settle a single amount, use <code className="action">Settle my balance</code> on its row. In the window that opens, click <code className="action">Pay balance</code>.

A [settlement purchase order](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/ovh-orders#purchase-order) will then be created. Once the payment has been made, your account will be balanced again.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:pay-bills */}
#### Request invoice cancellation

:::info
To request that your service be cancelled on the expiry date, follow the instructions in [this guide](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/how-to-cancel-services).

:::

{/* CP-STEPS-START:request-invoice-cancellation */}

If you have received an invoice that you do not consider to be legitimate and you wish to request a refund, or exercise your [right of withdrawal](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/ovh-orders#apply-the-right-of-withdrawal), you can submit a claim via the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>, subject to our [General Terms of Service](https://contract.eu.ovhapis.com/1.0/pdf/contrat_genServices-gb.pdf). Click the button with your initials in the top right-hand corner of your screen, then click <code className="action">Support</code> in the **Quick access** list. On the **My support requests** page, click <code className="action">Create ticket</code>.

In the window that opens, click <code className="action">Use the AI Assistant</code> to describe your request with the AI Assistant, or <code className="action">No</code> to open the help centre's ticket form in a new tab.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:request-invoice-cancellation */}
### Track your payments

{/* CP-STEPS-START:track-payments */}

You can find the payment history in <code className="action">My bills</code> if you click on the <code className="action">Payment tracking</code> tab. You can then link each invoice to the associated payment. To do so, click a payment's reference in the **Reference** column: the **Payment Details** page that opens lists the invoices settled by that payment.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Payment tracking tab with the CSV and Filter buttons and the table of payments" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/invoice-management/console/payment_tracking.png" loading="lazy" />

In this section, you can also export your payment receipts in *.csv* format, by clicking on <code className="action">CSV</code>. You can also use several filters via the <code className="action">Filter</code> button.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:track-payments */}
:::info
If you notice a discrepancy between a payment and the total amount listed on a bill, it means that an account balance automatically reduced the amount debited.

:::
