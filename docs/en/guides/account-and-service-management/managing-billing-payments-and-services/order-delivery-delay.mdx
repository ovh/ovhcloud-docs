## Objective

**You want to track your order and find out the delivery delay.**

## Instructions

### Tracking your order

#### Accessing order tracking

Open the <ManagerLink to="/#/billing/orders">My orders</ManagerLink> page.

A summary of your past orders appears, showing the creation date, the number, and the status of your order.

Here are the possible statuses and what they mean:

| Status | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Unpaid** | Your order has not been validated: payment has not yet been recorded. If you paid by bank transfer, the status will only change once the funds are actually received. |
| **Confirmation** | Your order is being manually verified by our teams (24 to 48 business hours). |
| **Being processed** | The service is being made available. The delay varies depending on the product ordered. |
| **Complete** | Your service has been delivered to you. |

Click <code className="action">...</code> to the right of the order, then <code className="action">Order tracking</code>, to settle your purchase order and allow delivery to proceed.

#### Order in "Confirmation"

During this step (24 to 48 business hours), two outcomes are possible:

- Action is required from you: an email will ask you for additional information or documents. Without a response from you, the order cannot be delivered.
- Your order is validated: it automatically moves to the next step and is delivered to you.

:::info
If your account or your order does not meet all the required security guarantees, it will be cancelled.
:::

#### Order "Being processed"

The delivery delay depends on the type of service ordered:

**Telephony**

Check the status of the withdrawal period in your purchase order tracking.

- If the period is active: delivery takes place after 14 days.
- If the period has been disabled: the service is delivered as soon as possible.

**Domain name (creation)**

Check the progress in your Control Panel, under the <code className="action">Web Cloud</code> > <ManagerLink to="/#/web/ongoing-operations">Ongoing operations</ManagerLink> section (domain names section).

If additional information or documents are needed, you will be notified in Ongoing operations or by email (subject: "OVHcloud – Action required on your part").

**Domain name (transfer)**

Transfer delays vary depending on the extension. See our **[Domain name transfer delays FAQ](/guides/web-cloud/domains/faq-domain-transfer-delays)** to find the delay for your extension, and our guide **[Tracking the transfer of your domain name](/guides/web-cloud/domains/track-domain-transfer)** to track the progress of your transfer.

**VPS and dedicated servers**

The delay shown on the website is indicative: it depends on availability and any customisation made when ordering. Feel free to contact our support team for more details.

:::warning
If your order remains stuck on "Being processed", wait 24 hours after the order was created and payment validated before contacting support or opening a support ticket about it.
:::

#### Order "Complete"

Your service has been delivered to you. For any questions about how to use or configure it, contact our support team.
