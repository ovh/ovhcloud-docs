## Objective

OVHcloud offers you the ability to commit to your Dedicated Servers and VPS for a period of time, in exchange for a discount that depends on the specific product you subscribe to. When you take out a longer commitment, you can pay for it up front for the whole period.

**This guide explains how to start, manage, and cancel a commitment on your Dedicated Servers and VPS via the OVHcloud Console.**

:::info
Commitments are a legacy feature. If your service is already under a commitment, you can still manage it as described in this guide. Commitments are billed **annually and up front only** — monthly commitment payments are no longer offered.

:::

:::info
Not all Dedicated Server and VPS ranges are eligible for a commitment. If your service is eligible, the commitment option appears in its **Subscription** section, as described below; if it is not, the option is not shown.

:::

## Requirements

- An active Dedicated Server or VPS in your OVHcloud account.
- You are the [administrative contact](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/managing-contacts#definition) of the service concerned.
- You have a valid payment method in your OVHcloud account (see our guide [Managing your payment methods](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-payment-methods)).

## Instructions

:::info
In this guide, we use a Dedicated Server as an example. However, the process is the same for a VPS.

:::

{/* CP-STEPS-START:instructions */}

:::info
The commitment button is in the **Subscription** section of the service's **Overview** tab. For a dedicated server **without** a commitment, as for one **already under** a commitment, it is labelled <code className="action">Manage my subscription</code>. It opens the server's **Subscription** tab, and the steps that follow are identical in both cases.

:::

{/* CP-STEPS-END:instructions */}

### Starting a commitment <a name="starting"></a>

{/* CP-STEPS-START:start-commitment */}

Use the side menu to access <code className="action">Dedicated servers</code> or <code className="action">VPS</code>.

Select the name of your service from the list displayed. Scroll down to the **Subscription** section of the **Overview** tab and click <code className="action">Manage my subscription</code>.

You will be taken to the service's **Subscription** tab, where you can select your commitment.

:::warning
- Different servers may have different options for commitment periods; your options may not match the example below.
- You may choose to commit to a longer term for a larger discount.
- If you do nothing at all, your commitment will automatically renew for the same price and terms.
- Once you have a commitment in place, you can only cancel it early by paying the remainder of what you owe on the commitment. To do so, [contact OVHcloud support](/links/support-contact).

:::

Make your choices in the **Subscription** tab:

1. **Minimum usage duration** — choose the commitment duration (for example, 1 year or 2 years). A longer duration gives a larger discount.
2. **Payment method** — choose how often you pay, for example 1 year upfront or monthly. The options available depend on the server.
3. **My offer** — review the summary. Your default payment method will be billed on the payment dates it shows, and the service will be renewed automatically at the frequency it states (for example, every 12 months).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Subscription tab of a dedicated server without commitment: Minimum usage duration and My offer" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-commitments/console/subscription-tab-no-commitment.png" loading="lazy" />

Click <code className="action">Validate</code> to process your commitment.

Once you are finished, your new commitment will begin on your next renewal date. You can view your renewal date in the **Subscription** section of the service's **Overview** tab.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:start-commitment */}

### Renewing or extending a commitment <a name="managing"></a>

{/* CP-STEPS-START:renew-commitment */}

When a service is already under a commitment, the **My offer** section of its **Subscription** tab shows the commitment end date under **End of current commitment** (for example, `1 Feb 2028`).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Subscription tab of a dedicated server under commitment, with End of current commitment" src="/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/manage-commitments/console/subscription-tab-committed.png" loading="lazy" />

To renew or extend the commitment before its end date, open the service, scroll down to the **Subscription** section of the **Overview** tab, and click <code className="action">Manage my subscription</code>. You will be taken to the service's **Subscription** tab.

:::warning
- You can only commit for a period that is longer than the remaining time on your current commitment.
- You may choose to commit to a longer term for a larger discount.
- If you do nothing at all, your commitment will automatically renew for the same price and terms.
- Once you have a commitment in place, you can only cancel it early by paying the remainder of what you owe on the commitment. To do so, [contact OVHcloud support](/links/support-contact).

:::

By extending your commitment, you can maintain your discount for the new commitment period. You can also increase your commitment duration. The **Subscription** tab offers the same steps as for a service without commitment, shown above:

1. **Minimum usage duration** — choose the new commitment duration.
2. **Payment method** — choose how often you pay, for example monthly or 6 months upfront (the options depend on the server).
3. **My offer** — review the summary. From your next renewal date onwards, your default payment method will be billed for the service period until the end of the new commitment.

Click <code className="action">Validate</code> to process your commitment.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:renew-commitment */}

### Checking whether a service is under commitment <a name="checking"></a>

There are two ways to check whether one of your services is currently under a commitment.

{/* CP-STEPS-START:check-commitment */}

**In the OVHcloud Console**

Open the service and look at the following information:

- In the **My offer** section of the **Subscription** tab, **End of current commitment** shows the date your commitment ends; it only appears when a commitment is active.
- In the **Subscription** section of the **Overview** tab, the **Renewal mode** field shows the renewal type applied to the service (for example, `Automatic renewal`).

**On your invoice**

A commitment is also reflected in the corresponding invoice, through the billing period and line items that cover it. You can consult your invoices from the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/invoices">My bills</ManagerLink> page (see our guide [Managing your OVHcloud bills](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/invoice-management)).

{/* CP-STEPS-END:check-commitment */}

### Canceling commitments <a name="canceling"></a>

If you already have a commitment, you can cancel it. The cancellation becomes effective at the **end of the commitment period**.

:::info
**Automatic renewal of a commitment**

If you take no action before the end of the commitment period, your commitment **and its associated discount are automatically renewed** for a new period of the same duration, at the same price and terms. To stop the commitment from renewing, you must cancel it before the end of the current period.

:::

To cancel a commitment **early** — that is, before the end of the commitment period — you must [contact OVHcloud support](/links/support-contact). Early cancellation requires you to pay the remainder of what you owe on the commitment.

{/* CP-STEPS-START:cancel-commitment */}

To cancel the underlying service at the end of its commitment period, open the service, scroll down to the **Subscription** section of the **Overview** tab, click <code className="action">Terminate</code>. In the window that opens, select <code className="action">Terminate the service</code>, then confirm with <code className="action">Confirm my choice</code>. For the full cancellation procedure, follow our guide:

{/* CP-STEPS-END:cancel-commitment */}

- [How to cancel your OVHcloud services](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/how-to-cancel-services)

## FAQ

<details>

<summary>Can I still take out a new commitment?</summary>

Commitments are a legacy feature. If your service already has a commitment, you can continue to manage and renew it as described in this guide. Availability of new commitments depends on your commercial zone and the product concerned.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I pay for my commitment on a monthly basis?</summary>

No. Commitments are billed **annually and up front**. There is no monthly payment option for commitments.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What happens if I do nothing before the end of my commitment period?</summary>

Your commitment and its associated discount are **automatically renewed** for a new period of the same duration, at the same price and terms. No action is required on your part to keep the commitment and the discount.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I cancel a commitment before its expiry date?</summary>

A commitment cancellation is normally effective at the **end of the commitment period**. To cancel a commitment before its expiry date, you must [contact OVHcloud support](/links/support-contact) and pay the remainder of what you owe on the commitment.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Which services are eligible for a commitment?</summary>

Commitments are available for eligible Dedicated Servers and VPS. Eligibility depends on the specific product range. If your service is eligible, the commitment option appears in its **Subscription** section; if it is not, the option is not shown.

</details>
