## Objective

Do you have a question about a payment, an invoice, or renewing your services?

**Find the answers to the most frequently asked questions about billing and managing your OVHcloud account in this guide.**

## FAQ

### Payment methods

<details>

<summary>Can I pay by bank transfer?</summary>

Yes, payment by bank transfer is accepted. Allow **3 business days** for it to be processed.

To make your transfer, use the bank details below. Be sure to include **your order number and your customer ID** in the transfer reference. Without these references, we will not be able to match your payment to your order.

:::info

**Bank details for OVH SAS**

- **Bank**: HSBC
- **IBAN**: FR76 3005 6005 0305 0300 0004 147
- **BIC code**: CCFRFRPP

:::

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why is my credit card rejected when I try to register it?</summary>

To register a credit card as a payment method, 3 conditions must be met:

- **3D Secure** must be enabled on your card. If this is not the case, contact your bank;
- the card must already have been **activated**, by an initial payment or withdrawal;
- the cardholder's details must **match those on your customer account**.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why does my payment method stay "pending validation"?</summary>

Your payment method stays pending validation until you have confirmed its addition with your bank. A manual check may also be in progress on our side.

In this case, wait for the check to complete, or add another payment method.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why has my payment method been disabled, or why does it no longer appear?</summary>

For security reasons, a payment method is disabled after several unsuccessful payment attempts, or following a negative response from your bank or PayPal.

You can add another payment method or contact support to request that it be reactivated.

</details>

<details>

<summary>My order was not validated, but an amount was debited. Why?</summary>

If your order was unsuccessful or was cancelled, the amount shown on your bank account corresponds to a **card authorisation hold**, not a final payment. It will disappear automatically:

- within 15 days for a credit card payment;
- within 21 days for a PayPal payment.

</details>

### Gift vouchers

<details>

<summary>How do I use my gift voucher, and how long is it valid?</summary>

To use your gift voucher, **add it to your purchase order**.

If you received it by email, that message contains all the information you need, including its validity period.

If the gift voucher corresponds to a refund, it is available on your prepaid account.

</details>

### Vouchers

<details>

<summary>How do I use my voucher?</summary>

Once added to your prepaid account, your voucher is used automatically when your consumption is billed. No action is required on your part.

:::tip

A gift voucher and a voucher do not work the same way: a gift voucher must be added to a purchase order, whereas a voucher is deducted automatically from your consumption.

:::

</details>

### Invoices and purchase orders

<details>

<summary>Where can I find my invoice?</summary>

If your company is subject to e-invoicing, your invoice is available on your approved platform. In this case, your Control Panel only gives you access to duplicates.

For all other customers, invoices are available in the "My invoices" section of your Control Panel.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I amend my invoice?</summary>

No, an invoice that has already been issued cannot be amended.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I dispute an invoice or request its cancellation?</summary>

Any claim must relate to an invoice that has already been settled. For more information, open a support ticket or contact support.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why do I have a €0 purchase order?</summary>

A €0 purchase order can have two causes:

- certain actions carried out on your customer account require a purchase order in order to keep a record of the changes;
- it may be the renewal of one of your free services.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why do I have several purchase orders for the same automatic renewal?</summary>

During an automatic renewal, a first purchase order is created: OVHcloud advances the renewal fee, which generates your initial invoice.

A second purchase order is then created to debit the corresponding amount from your payment method.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why do the prices shown on the website differ from those in my Control Panel?</summary>

The offers available on our website are not necessarily identical to the services you have already subscribed to. Our offers or prices may have changed since you subscribed. You are notified by email of any change to our offers or prices.

Prices may also differ depending on whether it is an initial subscription or a renewal.

For more information, open a support ticket or contact support.

</details>

### Renewing your services

<details>

<summary>Why has my service switched to manual renewal?</summary>

When no payment method is registered on your customer account, your services automatically switch to manual renewal. You are notified of this by email.

You are then sent several reminders by email before your service's expiry date, so that you can renew it before any suspension.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why can't I switch some services to manual renewal?</summary>

Some services do not offer manual renewal:

- **Telephony (VoIP) and internet access**: renewal is always automatic;
- **Public Cloud projects**: these are billed based on consumption. To stop all billing, you must delete the project;
- **Email Pro and Exchange**: as these services are linked to Microsoft, they cannot be switched to manual renewal;
- **Licences**: a licence can only be active or cancelled.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why can't I change the renewal period for some services?</summary>

Depending on the service, the renewal period is fixed:

- **Domain names and web hosting**: annual renewal only, switching to a monthly period is not possible;
- **Telephony**: monthly renewal only, with no option to change;
- **Public Cloud**: your consumption is billed exclusively each month.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I reactivate a service that was not renewed in time, or whose invoice was paid late?</summary>

Yes. A service that expired due to a missed manual renewal, or whose invoice was paid late, can be reactivated as long as it has not been deleted. However, a few exceptions apply:

- **Domain names**: reactivation fees may be charged;
- **VoIP lines**: beyond 3 months of suspension, reactivation is no longer possible;
- **Internet access (fibre and xDSL)**: once cancellation has been processed, it is no longer possible to reverse it.

</details>

### Cancellation

<details>

<summary>I cancelled my service, but it still appears in my Control Panel. Why?</summary>

After a cancellation request, your service remains active until its expiry date. It then remains visible until it is suspended, then deleted.

If you requested a deletion, remember to confirm your choice via the email you received.

:::tip

For more information on deletion delays, see our guide **[Deleting a service](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/deletion)**.

:::

</details>

### Managing contacts and domain names

<details>

<summary>What is the difference between a transfer, a contact change, a holder change, and an account update?</summary>

- **Transfer**: moving your domain name from one registrar to another;
- **Contact change**: changing the customer account that manages the service;
- **Holder change**: changing the information of the domain name's legal owner;
- **Account update**: changing the information shown on your invoices and your contact details.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why can't I change the contacts for my service?</summary>

For certain services, such as **Email Pro** and **Exchange accounts**, the contact change must be carried out by our technical teams. Contact support for more information.

For **Zimbra plans**, contacts cannot be changed.

</details>

### Logging in to the Control Panel

<details>

<summary>I'm not receiving the password reset email or login code. What should I do?</summary>

1. Check your spam/junk folder.
2. Make sure you are checking the inbox of the email address associated with your customer account.
3. If you still have not received anything, clear your browser's cache and cookies, or use a private browsing window.
4. Submit your request again: you should receive the email containing the reset link or code.
5. If you are still unable to log in, contact support.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why can't I log in with my email address?</summary>

If your email address is associated with several customer accounts, this can cause a conflict when logging in.

In this case, we recommend logging in with your **customer ID** (NIC handle) instead.

</details>
