## Objective

A domain name transfer refers to moving a domain name from one registrar to another. Depending on whether the domain name is coming to OVHcloud or leaving it, the tracking procedure differs.

**Find out how to track your domain name transfer, incoming or outgoing, and how to cancel a transfer in progress.**

## Requirements

- Be the domain name holder or have their authorisation.
- Have initiated a domain name transfer, incoming or outgoing, with OVHcloud.
- Have access to the OVHcloud Control Panel associated with the domain name in question.

## Instructions

### Tracking an incoming transfer (to OVHcloud)

Once your transfer order is validated and payment is received, the process starts. You can track its progress from the <ManagerLink to="/#/web/ongoing-operations">Ongoing operations</ManagerLink> page.

| Step | What happens | Delay |
|---|---|---|
| Transfer validation | The current registrar validates, ignores, or refuses the request | 24h (validation) to 5 days (no response) |
| Effective transfer | The domain name is registered with OVHcloud | Immediate after validation |
| Holder verification | OVHcloud automatically compares the holder in the registry and the one indicated when ordering | Automatic, right after the transfer |

:::info
The total delay varies significantly depending on the extension. For a .fr, for example, the transfer takes **a minimum of 8 unavoidable days**, and up to **22 days** if the current registrar objects. For most gTLDs (.com, .net, .org...), the transfer can be completed in 5 days if no action is required. Always check the delay specific to your extension before worrying about a "stuck" transfer.
:::

:::warning
If the transfer code was not provided when ordering, you can add it directly from the <ManagerLink to="/#/web/ongoing-operations">Ongoing operations</ManagerLink> page to validate the transfer.
:::

### Tracking an outgoing transfer (from OVHcloud)

After lifting the `Protection against domain name transfer` and retrieving your AUTH/INFO code, tracking is mainly done **with your new registrar**, who drives the request.

- Depending on the extension, you may receive an email from OVHcloud asking you to confirm or refuse the outgoing transfer (see next section).
- The transfer can take up to **10 days**, and this delay also varies depending on the extension and the registry concerned.

:::info
As long as you have not relocked your domain name, it remains transferable for seven days after the protection is lifted. After this period, if no transfer request has been received by the new registrar, the protection is automatically reactivated.
:::

### Confirming or refusing a request received by email

For a holder change or an outgoing transfer, OVHcloud may send a confirmation email (a "Form of Authorization", or FOA) containing a validation link.

| Your action | Effect on an outgoing transfer |
|---|---|
| You click <code className="action">Accept</code> | The transfer is sped up |
| You click <code className="action">Reject</code> | The transfer is **cancelled** |
| You do nothing | The transfer is finalised automatically after a few days (usually 5) |

:::warning
Always make sure you are using the link in the **most recent email**: if a new request is initiated, previous links become invalid.
:::

### Cancelling an outgoing transfer (in progress to another registrar)

:::warning
**Cancelling an outgoing transfer is not done from the OVHcloud Control Panel.** This request must be made directly **to your current registrar** (the one the domain name is going to be registered with), as they are the ones with the authority to validate or refuse the transfer request. OVHcloud, as the current registrar, cannot unilaterally interrupt or cancel a request that is being validated with the registry.
:::

Contact your current registrar to find out their refusal/cancellation procedure, especially if the validation period (5 days by default) has not yet elapsed.

### Cancelling an incoming transfer

For an incoming transfer, cancelling is much easier on your end:

- Reply <code className="action">Reject</code> to the confirmation email received from OVHcloud, if you receive one;
- Or, in the <code className="action">Web Cloud</code> section, go to the <ManagerLink to="/#/web/ongoing-operations">Ongoing operations</ManagerLink> page to request the cancellation of the transfer operation. This will cancel your request and the amount paid will be refunded to you.
