### Understand how the transfer works

**When does the transfer delay start?**

The delay starts as soon as your transfer request is validated, meaning once the transfer code (AUTH code) is correct and the domain name is unlocked at your current registrar.

**Why is my transfer taking longer than expected?**

Several reasons can extend the transfer delay:

- the transfer code entered is incorrect or has expired;
- the domain name is still locked at your current registrar;
- an email validation is pending from the domain holder or the administrative contact;
- the extension's registry is processing the request more slowly than usual.

:::tip
Before starting your transfer, check that the domain name holder's email address is valid. You can check it using a WHOIS tool.
:::

**Will my website and emails be interrupted during the transfer?**

No, as long as your domain name's DNS servers remain unchanged during the transfer. We recommend not changing your DNS configuration until the transfer is finalised. If needed, you can contact support to speed up your transfer.
