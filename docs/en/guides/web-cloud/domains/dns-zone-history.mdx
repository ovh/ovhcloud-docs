## Objective

The **D**omain **N**ame **S**ystem (**DNS**) zone of a domain name is its configuration file. It is made up of technical information, called *DNS records*. The DNS zone is, in a way, like a switching centre.

For more information, please refer to the following guides:

- [Everything you need to know about DNS servers](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-server-general-information)
- [Everything you need to know about DNS zones](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-general-information)
- [Everything you need to know about DNS records](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-records)

For various reasons, you may need to apply an older DNS configuration to your domain name.

DNS management is now made easier thanks to the history of your DNS zones.

**Find out how to view, compare, download and restore your DNS zone backups.**

## Requirements

- Access to manage the domain name concerned.

## Instructions

:::info
**Backup limits**

Your DNS zone backups are subject to the following limitations:

- We keep a maximum of 200 backups for the same DNS zone.
- Once a backup is more than 31 days old, it is automatically deleted, with the exception of the **5 most recent backups** made.

**If your DNS zone has been deleted**

When a DNS zone is deleted from your OVHcloud Control Panel (manual deletion, service expiry or any other reason), an email is sent to the service's **administrator contact** at the time of deletion. Its subject line reads `[aa00000-ovh] [OVH-DNS] Deletion of your DNS Zone domain.tld` and contains a link to retrieve the contents of your former DNS zone.

This link is only valid for **30 days** from the date the email was sent. After this period, the configuration of the deleted DNS zone is permanently lost.

If you cannot find this email in your mailbox, you can view it in your OVHcloud Control Panel, in the <code className="action">My messages</code> section, which contains all the messages sent to your contact email address. For more information, consult our guide "[How to manage messages in your OVHcloud Control Panel](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/manage-messages)".

To check or change the administrator contact of a service, consult our guide "[Managing contacts for your services](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/managing-contacts)".

:::

### Open your DNS zone history

Above the table, open the <code className="action">Actions on my zone</code> menu and click <code className="action">View the history of my DNS zone</code>.

A new page lists the history of your DNS zone backups, from the most recent to the oldest. The current version of your DNS zone is at the top of the table, marked **Current**. Each row provides a <code className="action">View</code>, a <code className="action">Download</code> and a <code className="action">Retrieve</code> action, and the <code className="action">Compare versions</code> button lets you compare two versions.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="DNS zone history page" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/nx-history.png" loading="lazy" />

**Click on the action of your choice to view the details.**

<details>

<summary>View a DNS zone</summary>

To view the content of a version, identify the corresponding row in the table, then click <code className="action">View</code> in the **View** column. The records of that DNS zone version are displayed. Click <code className="action">Close</code> to return to the DNS zone history page.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Content of a DNS zone version" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/nx-history-view.png" loading="lazy" />

</details>

<details>

<summary>Download a DNS zone</summary>

To download a version, identify the corresponding row in the table, then click <code className="action">Download</code> in the **Download** column. The DNS zone is downloaded in `.txt` format.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Restore a DNS zone</summary>

To replace your current DNS zone with an older one, identify the row corresponding to the version you want to restore (check the date on the left of the row), then click <code className="action">Retrieve</code> in the **Retrieve** column.

In the confirmation window that appears, check that the date shown matches the version you want to restore. As the warning indicates, the current DNS zone (at the top of the history list) will be deleted and replaced by the version you restore. Click <code className="action">Retrieve</code> to confirm, or <code className="action">Cancel</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="DNS zone restore confirmation window" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/nx-history-restore.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
Modifying or restoring a DNS zone causes a propagation delay of **4** to **24** hours to be fully taken into account on the DNS network.

:::

</details>

<details>

<summary>Compare two DNS zones</summary>

To compare two versions, click <code className="action">Compare versions</code> above the table. On the page that opens, choose the versions to compare from the <code className="action">Base version</code> and <code className="action">Modified version</code> drop-down lists: by default, the base version is the most recent one and the modified version the one just before it.

Both versions are displayed side by side, line by line, and a colour code highlights the differences:

- In the <code className="action">Base version</code> pane, on the left, the lines that differ are highlighted in **red**.
- In the <code className="action">Modified version</code> pane, on the right, the corresponding lines are highlighted in **green**.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Comparing two versions of a DNS zone" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/nx-history-compare.png" loading="lazy" />

</details>
