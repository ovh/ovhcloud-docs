## Objective

The **D**omain **N**ame **S**ystem (**DNS**) zone of a domain name is its configuration file. It is made up of technical information, called *DNS records*. The DNS zone is, in a way, like a switching centre.

For more information, please refer to the following guides:

- [Everything you need to know about DNS servers](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-server-general-information)
- [Everything you need to know about DNS zones](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-general-information)
- [Everything you need to know about DNS records](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-records)

For various reasons, you may need to apply an older DNS configuration to your domain name.

DNS management is now made easier thanks to the history of your DNS zones.

**Find out how to view, compare, download and restore your DNS zone backups.**

## Requirements

- Access to manage the domain name concerned.

[[cpnav:web-domain-dns-zone-console]]

## Instructions

:::info
**Backup limits**

Your DNS zone backups are subject to the following limitations:

- We keep a maximum of 200 backups for the same DNS zone.
- Once a backup is more than 31 days old, it is automatically deleted, with the exception of the **5 most recent backups** made.

**If your DNS zone has been deleted**

When a DNS zone is deleted from your OVHcloud account (manual deletion, service expiry or another reason), an email is sent to the service's **administrator contact** at the time of deletion. Its subject has the form `[aa00000-ovh] [OVH-DNS] Deletion of your DNS Zone domain.tld` and it contains a link to retrieve the content of your old DNS zone.

This link is only valid for **30 days** from the date the email was sent. After this period, the configuration of the deleted DNS zone is permanently lost.

If you cannot find this email in your mailbox, you can view it on the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/communications">My messages</ManagerLink> page, which lists all the messages sent to your contact email address. In the OVHcloud Console, you can access it by clicking the button with your initials at the top right of the screen, then <code className="action">My messages</code>. For more information, read our guide "[How to manage messages in your OVHcloud Control Panel](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/manage-messages)".

To check or change the administrator contact of a service, consult our guide "[Managing contacts for your services](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/managing-contacts)".

:::

### Open your DNS zone history

Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">Domains & DNS</ManagerLink> page, choose the domain name concerned, then click the <code className="action">DNS Zone</code> tab. Your DNS zone history is displayed in the **My DNS zone history** card, at the bottom of the tab.

On the left of this card, the list of your DNS zone backups goes from the most recent to the oldest, each identified by its date: the current version is at the top, marked **Current version**. The content of the selected version is displayed on the right. The <code className="action">Compare</code> button, in the header of this content, lets you compare two versions.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="My DNS zone history card, list of versions and content of the current version" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/history.png" loading="lazy" />

**Click on the action of your choice to view the details.**

<details>

<summary>View a DNS zone</summary>

To view the content of a version, click its date in the list on the left. The records of this version of the DNS zone are displayed on the right, in text format. The copy button, at the top right of this content, lets you copy it.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Previous version of the DNS zone selected in the history and its content" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/history-view.png" loading="lazy" />

</details>

<details>

<summary>Download a DNS zone</summary>

To download a version, click its date in the list on the left, then the download button at the top right of its content. The DNS zone is downloaded in `.txt` format.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Restore a DNS zone</summary>

To replace your current DNS zone with an older one, click the version you want to restore in the list on the left (check its date carefully), then <code className="action">Restore</code> at the top right of its content. This button does not appear for the current version.

In the **Restore DNS zone** window that appears, check that the date shown matches the version you want to restore. As the window states, the current configuration of your DNS zone (at the top of the history list) will be replaced by the version you restore. Click <code className="action">Restore</code> to confirm, or <code className="action">Cancel</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Restore DNS zone window and date of the version to restore" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/history-restore.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
Modifying or restoring a DNS zone causes a propagation delay of **4** to **24** hours to be fully taken into account on the DNS network.

:::

</details>

<details>

<summary>Compare two DNS zones</summary>

To compare two versions, click the first version to compare in the list, then <code className="action">Compare</code> in the header of its content (this button appears as soon as the history contains at least two versions). The list of versions remains displayed. The selected version is displayed on the left; on the right, choose the version to compare it with from the drop-down list above the column. By default, this is the version preceding the selected one.

The two versions are displayed side by side, line by line, and a colour code highlights the differences:

- On the left, the lines that differ are highlighted in **red**.
- On the right, the corresponding lines are highlighted in **green**.

By default, only the lines that differ are displayed: the **Differences only** option, above the comparison, hides identical lines. Disable it to display all lines. Click <code className="action">Exit comparison</code> to return to the content of the selected version.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Comparison of two versions of a DNS zone, differences highlighted" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/history-compare.png" loading="lazy" />

</details>
