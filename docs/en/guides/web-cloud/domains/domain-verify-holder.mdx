## Objective

Have you received an email from OVHcloud stating that action is required for your domain name? Does the same email indicate that the contact details (contact information) relating to your domain name holder must be corrected or completed?

**Find out how to correct or complete the contact information of a domain name holder after receiving an alert email from OVHcloud.**

### Why did I receive a contact verification email from OVHcloud?

ICANN, the organization responsible for governing domain names globally, or registries, organizations responsible for managing extensions, require the validation of holder information when a domain name is created, transferred, or whenever associated contacts are updated.

Verification of holder contact details can occur at any time during a domain name's lifecycle, including for domain names purchased several years ago. Thus, each domain concerned must undergo verification of the holder's contact details within 15 days of receiving the verification email, whether following a purchase, transfer, modification of contact details, or a periodic verification requested by ICANN or the registry.

### What happens if the holder's email or contact information is not verified within the timeframe?

If verification is not completed within the specified timeframe, the domain name is automatically suspended and becomes temporarily unavailable until verification is completed, potentially resulting in the deletion of the domain name.
In this case, OVHcloud will not be able to provide any refund.

## Requirements

- You have a [domain name](/links/web/domains) registered with OVHcloud.
- You have received an email from OVHcloud stating that action is required regarding your domain name holder contact details.
- Check the following points to ensure that this is not a fraudulent email:
    - The subject of the email contains your NIC handle (e.g.: `aa00000-ovh`) and your domain name (e.g.: `domain.tld`).

## Instructions

### 1 - Holder email verification after purchase or modification of the holder's contact email for a domain name

After the order, the domain name holder will receive an email from OVHcloud to validate their email address and confirm it is reachable.

When clicking on <code className="action">Confirm your email address</code>, a new page will open with a link directly validating the holder's email address.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Validation email CGI" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/general-information/validation-email-CGI.png" loading="lazy" />

### 2 - Correct or complete the contact details of the domain name holder as an administrator

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">Domains & DNS</ManagerLink> page, then choose the domain name concerned. The domain name's <code className="action">Overview</code> tab is displayed.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Domains & DNS page and list of the account's domain names" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/listing.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    First check that your domain name is affected by the procedure: a banner states "Your domain name is subject to a contact data verification procedure." (or, if it is already suspended, "Your domain name is suspended as part of a contact data verification procedure."). The operation to carry out also appears in the **To monitor** section.

    In the **Contacts** section, then click <code className="action">Edit</code> under **Owner**.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Contacts section of the Overview and Edit link under Owner" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/general-information/contacts-owner.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    The **Edit holder** panel is displayed.

    :::warning
    Any change to the holder's first name, last name, organisation, legal status or email address is considered a **change of holder**. In the panel, these fields are locked (padlock icon); depending on the extension, other fields, such as the country, may be locked too.

    If your changes **only** concern information other than that mentioned above, make them directly, then click <code className="action">Save</code>. In this specific case, there is no need to start a change of holder request. For this operation, you do not need to confirm the changes by email.

    Otherwise, to start the change of holder request, click the <code className="action">change of holder procedure</code> link at the top of the panel.

    :::

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Edit holder panel and locked fields" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/general-information/edit-holder-drawer.png" loading="lazy" />

    Find more details about the change of holder in our guide: "[Domain name - How do I change the holder?](/guides/web-cloud/domains/trade-domain)".
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### 3 - What to do if the holder contact's email address is no longer accessible?

#### General case

If the email address associated with your domain name is incorrect or inaccessible (for example, due to a typo), you should [contact OVHcloud support](/links/support).

Please note that the domain's holder will have to prove their identity again by providing the required documents according to their type (individual, company, association, etc.).

:::info
The list of documents below is not exhaustive. Other documents may be requested depending on your situation or the requirements of the relevant registry.

:::

**Documents to provide according to the type of holder:**

- **Individual**:
    - Valid identity document (front and back): national identity card, passport (pages containing the photograph and personal information as well as the signature page), residence permit or driving licence
- **Company**
    - Proof of entity existence: company registration extract (less than 3 months old), trade register extract, business licence, or any equivalent document.
    - Valid identity document (front and back) of the legal representative (business owner, president or managing director): national identity card, passport, residence permit or driving licence.
- **Non-profit organisation**
    - Valid identity document (front and back) of the legal representative (president, manager or director of the association): national identity card, passport, residence permit or driving licence
    - Legal proof of existence of the organisation.
    - Minutes of the last general meeting.
- **Public administration**
    - Administrative certificate (or equivalent official document).
    - Valid identity document (front and back) of the public administration representative: national identity card, passport, residence permit or driving licence.

Our teams will help you in the next steps to verify your domain.

#### Special case - Modification of the holder contact by the administrator without assistance (example of .fr)

For some extensions (for example: .fr, .it, .es), the domain administrator can go directly to the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink> and change the holder contact's address by following the steps described in step 2 of this page.

### 4 - Relaunch the operation to verify the contact details of the domain name holder

:::warning
If the corrections in the first part of this guide required the initialization of a change of holder for the domain name, complete the change of holder **before** continuing with the actions described in this guide.

:::

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **4** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/operations">Ongoing operations</ManagerLink> page, then find the **Contact correction** operation for the domain name concerned. If needed, filter the list on <code className="action">To be processed</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Ongoing operations page, status filters and Actions column" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/ongoing-operations.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    Click the button in the **Actions** column, to the right of the operation concerned. The operation panel opens.

    You can also open this panel from the **To monitor** section of the domain name's <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, by clicking the operation.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    In the panel, click <code className="action">Relaunch</code>.

    If any of the holder's information still needs to be corrected, the panel displays it: complete it, then save it. The operation is then relaunched automatically once your changes have been saved.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 4">
    It takes a few minutes for the operation to be finalised. Refresh the **Ongoing operations** page where the contact correction operation for your domain name was located.

    If the operation has completed successfully, it now shows the **Closed** status.

    If this is not the case, a correction is necessary in the domain name holder's contact details. In this case, please follow this guide from the beginning to resolve the issue.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
