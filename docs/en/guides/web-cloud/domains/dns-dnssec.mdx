## Objective 

A DNS server hosts one or more DNS zones. A DNS zone contains the DNS configuration of a domain name. This configuration links your domain name to the various services associated with it (hosting server for your website, servers for your custom email addresses with your domain name, etc.).

In some cases, data streams that pass through DNS servers can be intercepted by hackers.  
To achieve this, they manipulate the DNS server cache to apply their own DNS configuration to your domain name. This is called *cache poisoning*.
This way, they can redirect incoming traffic for your domain name to their websites and email addresses.

The **D**omain **N**ame **S**ystem **SEC**urity Extensions (**DNSSEC**) protect your domain name's DNS configuration against *cache poisoning* by verifying and authenticating DNS responses.

**This guide explains how to enable DNSSEC for your domain name to protect it against *cache poisoning*.**

For more information on how **DNSSEC** works, please visit our page "[Understanding DNSSEC](/links/web/domains-dnssec)".

You can also refer to our guides on [OVHcloud DNS servers](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-server-general-information) and on [editing an OVHcloud DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit) if you would like more information on these topics.

## Requirements

- A domain name with an extension compatible with DNSSEC

## Instructions

To check if your domain name uses the OVHcloud DNS configuration, click on the tabs below to view each of the **2** steps.

**These 2 steps are only valid if your domain name is registered with OVHcloud.** Otherwise, you will need to check with your domain name registrar.

If the DNS server names end with *ovh.net* (with the exception of the *snds2.ovh.net* server), *ovh.ca* or *anycast.me*, your domain name will use OVHcloud DNS servers.

:::info
The activation/deactivation of **DNSSEC** takes **24** hours to be effective.

If you would like to change the DNS servers associated with your domain name at a later stage, the DNS servers will only be modified on the OVHcloud side after the **DNSSEC** has been disabled. After this, an additional period of **24** to **48** hours will be required for the DNS propagation of the modification.

In total, the modification of a domain name's DNS servers with the **DNSSEC** active solution will be fully effective after **48** at **72** hours.

:::

You can enable **DNSSEC** in three scenarios detailed below.

### Case 1 - Your domain name is registered with OVHcloud and uses OVHcloud DNS servers

To enable (or disable) the **DNSSEC** solution for your domain name, click on the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">Domains & DNS</ManagerLink> page, then choose the domain name concerned. The domain name's <code className="action">Overview</code> tab is displayed.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Domains & DNS page and list of the account's domain names" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/listing.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    Click the <code className="action">DNS Zone</code> tab. The **DNSSEC** card shows the status of **DNSSEC** on your domain name, on the **Zone signing** line: **Enabled** or **Disabled**.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="DNSSEC card of the DNS Zone tab with DNSSEC enabled" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dnssec/dnssec-enabled.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    To enable **DNSSEC**, click <code className="action">Enable DNSSEC</code>, then confirm in the **Enable DNSSEC** window by clicking <code className="action">Enable DNSSEC</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="DNSSEC card of the DNS Zone tab with DNSSEC disabled and the Enable DNSSEC button" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dnssec/dnssec-disabled.png" loading="lazy" />

    To disable **DNSSEC**, click <code className="action">Disable DNSSEC</code>, then confirm the change in the **Disable DNSSEC** window by clicking <code className="action">Disable DNSSEC</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Disable DNSSEC window" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dnssec/dnssec-disable-window.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Case 2 - Your domain name is registered with OVHcloud and does not use OVHcloud DNS servers

In this situation, contact the service provider managing your domain name's DNS configuration to ask them for the DNSSEC activation settings ("Key Tag" / "Algorithm" / "Public key (encoded in base64)"). You do not need to ask for the "Flags" setting: the Console sets it to 257.

Once you have retrieved these 3 settings, click on the tabs below to view each of the **4** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">Domains & DNS</ManagerLink> page, then choose the domain name concerned. The domain name's <code className="action">Overview</code> tab is displayed.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Domains & DNS page and list of the account's domain names" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/listing.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    Click the <code className="action">DNS Zone</code> tab. The **DNSSEC** card lets you add and modify **DS records**. It is only available if your domain name has an OVHcloud DNS zone: without a DNS zone, the tab shows the order form for a DNS zone instead, and you cannot add a DS record.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    Below the **DS records**, click <code className="action">Add a DS record</code>.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 4">
    In the **Add a DS record** panel, fill in the 3 fields **Key Tag**, **Algorithm** (drop-down list) and **Public Key** with the data provided by your current provider. The **Flags** value is automatically set to 257, the value required by the registry.

    To add another record (4 at most), click <code className="action">Add a record</code>. Once you have filled in the fields, click the <code className="action">Validate</code> button.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Case 3 - Your domain name is not registered with OVHcloud and uses OVHcloud DNS servers

:::warning
Before you proceed, please check with your domain name's current registrar to make sure that there are no DNSSEC options already enabled for it.

:::

Unlike **case 2**, you will need to retrieve the DNSSEC activation settings ("Key Tag" / "Flag" / "Algorithm" / "Public key (encoded in base64)") from the OVHcloud side.

To do this, you will need to use the [OVHcloud APIs](/guides/manage-and-operate/api/first-steps) and perform the following actions:

- Go to our website <ApiLink>OVHcloud API</ApiLink>.
- On the page that pops up, middle-click <code className="action">Explore the OVHcloud API</code>.
- On the new page that appears, and on the left-hand side of the page, use the dropdown menu to the right of the form <code className="action">v1</code>, then select/enter the choice `/domain`.
- From the list of APIs that appears below in the left-hand column, locate and click on the following node: **POST /domain/zone/&#123;zoneName&#125;/dnssec**. You can also click on this link to access it:

<Api version="v1" section="/domain" method="POST" route={"/domain/zone/\{zoneName\}/dnssec"} />

- On the right-hand side of the page, you will then see the various forms to fill in.
- Click the button in the top right-hand corner labeled <code className="action">Authenticate</code>, then the <code className="action">Login with OVHcloud SSO</code> button.
- The login interface for your <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud account</ManagerLink> will open.
- Log in to your account, then click <code className="action">Authorize</code> to use the OVHcloud API with the services in your OVHcloud account.
- You will then be automatically redirected to the previous page of the **POST /domain/zone/&#123;zoneName&#125;/dnssec** API, where you will now be authenticated.
- On the right-hand side of the page, you will then see the form to fill in.
- Fill in the form in the `PATH PARAMETERS` section as follows:
- `zoneName`: Enter the domain name concerned (e.g. `domain.tld`).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="API" src="/images/assets/screens/api/post-domain-zone-zonename-dnssec.png" loading="lazy" />

Once you have filled in the form, click on the blue <code className="action">EXECUTE</code> button in the bottom right-hand corner of the previously filled-in section.

After a few minutes, you will receive an email from OVHcloud to the contact email address of your OVHcloud DNS zone.  
This email will contain the 4 parameters ("Key Tag" / "Flag" / "Algorithm" / "Public key (encoded in base64)") required to activate DNSSEC with your domain name registrar.

:::tip
Check your spam folder if you have not received the email within an hour.

:::

Finally, contact your domain name registrar with the 4 settings to enable the DNSSEC option for them.
