## Objective

You are the holder of a domain name with OVHcloud and want to connect it to a Google Site. This guide explains how to prepare and configure your OVHcloud DNS zone for your Google Site.

**Find out how to connect your OVHcloud domain name to a Google Site.**

[[fragment:support-scope]]

:::warning
Google Site support does not have access to your OVHcloud domain name settings and therefore cannot advise you on the information you will need to provide.
:::

## Requirements

- A [domain name](/links/web/domains) registered with OVHcloud.
- The [necessary permissions to manage](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/managing-contacts) the domain name.
- A Google Site and be its owner.

[[cpnav:web-domain-dns-zone]]

## Instructions

Before following the steps in this guide, we recommend reading our guide on [Editing an OVHcloud DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit).

:::warning
Your DNS zone may already be preconfigured or linked to a web hosting plan. We will see how to identify each DNS record needed to connect to your Google Site. Some will need to be deleted to avoid conflicts with the DNS records required in this configuration. Others will just need to be modified or created. For a better understanding, we will use the domain name "**domain.tld**" as an example. Replace it with your domain name during configuration.

:::

### 1. Configure your Google Site

:::warning
Only the owner of a Google Site can connect it to a domain name. If necessary, find out how to [change the owner of the Google site](https://support.google.com/sites/answer/97934).

:::

First, prepare your Google Site by following the instructions in the **Configure a custom domain** section from [**this page of Google support**](https://support.google.com/sites/answer/9068867?hl=en#zippy=).

### 2. Configure your DNS records on your OVHcloud account

Click the tabs below to view each of the **5** steps in sequence.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">Domains & DNS</ManagerLink> page, choose the domain name concerned, then click the <code className="action">DNS Zone</code> tab.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="DNS Zone tab listing a domain name's records" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/zone-overview.png" loading="lazy" />

    The table lists the DNS records of the selected domain name.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    **Configuring the A records**

    **1 - Identification:** click <code className="action">Filters</code> above the table, then select <code className="action">A</code> in the **Record type** list.

    :::info
    The **Record type** list of the <code className="action">Filters</code> button only offers the record types present in your DNS zone. If a type does not appear in the list, your zone does not contain any record of that type.

    :::

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="DNS zone filtered on A records" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/filter-a.png" loading="lazy" />

    Find the existing "A" records for your domain name alone (subdomain `@`, example: `domain.tld.`) and for the "www" subdomain (example: `www.domain.tld.`).

    **2 - Deletion:** delete all existing "A" records for the "www" subdomain. If more than 4 "A" records exist for the domain name alone, delete the excess records to keep only 4. For each record to be deleted, on the row concerned, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, then <code className="action">Delete</code>, and confirm in the **Delete DNS entry** window.

    **3 - Modification:** modify each "A" record kept for the domain name alone: on the row concerned, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, then <code className="action">Modify</code>. The row expands into an edit form. Replace the **Target (IPv4)** with one of the 4 IPv4 addresses of Google Site (a different address for each record):

    - `216.239.32.21`
    - `216.239.34.21`
    - `216.239.36.21`
    - `216.239.38.21`

    Click <code className="action">Modify</code> to confirm.

    **4 - Addition:** if fewer than 4 "A" records existed, create the missing records. Click <code className="action">Create a record</code> above the table, then select `A — Point to an IPv4` in the **Type** list. Enter `@` in the **Subdomain** field, then enter each IPv4 address not yet assigned in the **Target (IPv4)** field. Click <code className="action">Add</code>.

    As the values of these IP addresses may change, check the official documentation for [the value of A records](https://support.google.com/a/answer/2579934?hl=en&ref_topic=2721296).

    Then proceed to step 3.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    **Deleting AAAA records**

    **1 - Identification:** click <code className="action">Filters</code> above the table, then select <code className="action">AAAA</code> in the **Record type** list. If the list does not offer <code className="action">AAAA</code>, your zone does not contain any record of this type: go directly to step 4.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Filters button and Record type list of the DNS zone" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/filter-type.png" loading="lazy" />

    Find the existing "AAAA" records for your domain name alone (example: `domain.tld.`) and for the "www" subdomain (example: `www.domain.tld.`).

    **2 - Deletion:** delete all the "AAAA" records identified (domain name alone and "www" subdomain) to avoid a conflict with the new DNS records. For each record, on the row concerned, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, then <code className="action">Delete</code>, and confirm in the **Delete DNS entry** window.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 4">
    **Configuring the TXT record**

    **1 - Identification:** click <code className="action">Filters</code> above the table, then select <code className="action">TXT</code> in the **Record type** list.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="DNS zone filtered on TXT records" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/filter-txt.png" loading="lazy" />

    Find the existing "TXT" records for your domain name alone (example: `domain.tld.`) and for the "www" subdomain (example: `www.domain.tld.`).

    **2 - Deletion:** delete all the "TXT" records identified (domain name alone and "www" subdomain) to avoid a conflict with the new DNS records. For each record, on the row concerned, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, then <code className="action">Delete</code>, and confirm in the **Delete DNS entry** window.

    **3 - Addition:** create a TXT verification record. Click <code className="action">Create a record</code> above the table, then select `TXT — Free text (verification, SPF...)` in the **Type** list (**Extended fields** section). Fill in the **Subdomain** and **Value** fields with the information on the "[TXT record values](https://support.google.com/a/answer/2716802?hl=en&ref_topic=2716886)" page of the official documentation. Usually, the **Subdomain** field contains `@` and the **Value** field is of type `google-site-verification=XXXXXXXXXXXX`. Click <code className="action">Add</code>.

    Then proceed to step 5.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 5">
    **Configuring the CNAME record**

    **1 - Identification:** click <code className="action">Filters</code> above the table, then select <code className="action">CNAME</code> in the **Record type** list.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="DNS zone filtered on CNAME records" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/filter-cname.png" loading="lazy" />

    Find the existing "CNAME" records for the "www" subdomain (example: `www.domain.tld.`).

    **2 - Deletion:** if several "CNAME" records exist for the "www" subdomain, delete all but one. For each record to be deleted, on the row concerned, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, then <code className="action">Delete</code>, and confirm in the **Delete DNS entry** window.

    **3 - Modification:** if a "CNAME" record exists for the "www" subdomain, on the row concerned, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, then <code className="action">Modify</code>. The row expands into an edit form. Replace only the **Target (hostname)** with `ghs.googlehosted.com.`: if you omit the final dot, the Console adds it automatically. Click <code className="action">Modify</code> to confirm.

    If no "CNAME" record exists for the "www" subdomain, click <code className="action">Create a record</code> above the table, then select `CNAME — Alias to another domain` in the **Type** list, enter `www` in the **Subdomain** field and `ghs.googlehosted.com.` in the **Target (hostname)** field. Click <code className="action">Add</code>.

    As these values may change, check them on the "[CNAME record values](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/support/troubleshooting/cname-record-values?hl=en)" page of the official documentation.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The DNS zone is now configured to point to your Google Site.

:::info
It may take up to 48 hours to verify your domain name.

:::

If you are using an OVHcloud email solution, or planning to subscribe to one of [our email solutions](/links/web/emails), you will also need to prepare your DNS zone accordingly. Read our guide on [configuring an MX record](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-mx).
