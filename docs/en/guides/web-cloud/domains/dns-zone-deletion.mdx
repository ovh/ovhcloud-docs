## Objective

The **DNS** (**D**omain **N**ame **S**ystem) zone is a domain name's configuration file. It consists of DNS **records** that map the domain name to various services and functions.

For more information on DNS zones and servers, see the following guides: 

- [Everything you need to know about DNS servers](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-server-general-information)
- [Everything you need to know about DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-general-information)
- [Everything you need to know about DNS records](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-records)

For example, you may need to delete a DNS zone for your domain name at OVHcloud in the following cases (non-exhaustive list):

- You are using an active DNS zone for your domain name with a provider other than OVHcloud.
- You no longer use the domain name associated with the DNS zone at OVHcloud.
- You have migrated your services to another provider and you want to cancel your OVHcloud services.

:::info
The creation/modification/deletion of a DNS zone in the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink> is free of charge.

:::

**This guide explains how to delete an OVHcloud DNS zone for your domain name via the OVHcloud Console.**

## Requirements

- You have a DNS zone in your OVHcloud account.
- You have sufficient rights on the DNS zone to be deleted. You can find more information in our guide "[Managing contacts for your services](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/managing-contacts)".

[[cpnav:billing-services]]

:::info
Deleting a DNS zone will not delete the domain name associated with it. You will not lose your domain name by deleting a DNS zone associated with it.

:::

## Instructions

:::warning
Before you proceed, check that the DNS zone you want to delete is no longer used by your domain name.

If you delete the active DNS zone for your domain name, your online services (website, email addresses, etc.) will be interrupted.

Perform a [WHOIS](/links/web/domains-whois) query to find out whether your domain name's active DNS zone is the one hosted at OVHcloud.

If the active DNS zone for your domain name is the one at OVHcloud, and you would like to replace it with a DNS zone hosted elsewhere, please read our guide on [Modifying the DNS servers for an OVHcloud domain name](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-server-edit) before deleting any DNS zones.

:::

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/manage-services">My offers and services</ManagerLink> page. Click the <code className="action">Filter</code> button and select the **DNS zones** type to display only your DNS zones. Then click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the DNS zone to cancel, then <code className="action">Cancel my subscription</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="My offers and services page filtered on DNS zones and actions menu of a DNS zone" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/account/manage-services-dns-zone-actions.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    In the **Terminate service** window that appears, specify the reason for your cancellation request and your project, then click <code className="action">Terminate</code>.

    The window points out that cancellation leads to the loss of all data associated with the DNS zone.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    Your service will be cancelled on the **effective date** shown in the **Effective date** column of the table on the "My offers and services" page. If you do not see the "Termination requested" status appear, refresh the page.

    :::info
    If you want to delete immediately a DNS zone that is not associated with any OVHcloud domain name (it appears as **Zone only** in the list on the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">Domains & DNS</ManagerLink> page), click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button on its row, then <code className="action">Terminate</code>. In the **Terminate service** window that appears, select the **Terminate immediately** option and click <code className="action">Terminate now</code>, then confirm in the **Immediate termination** window by clicking <code className="action">Terminate now</code> again. This deletion is immediate and irreversible.

    For a DNS zone associated with a domain name, carry out the 3 steps to request cancellation on the effective date, then contact OVHcloud support by creating a support ticket from the [Help Centre](/links/support-contact).
    In the ticket, specify the concerned DNS zone and clearly state your wish to delete it immediately without waiting for the date of effect.

    :::

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Actions menu of a zone-only DNS zone in the Domains & DNS list" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/zone-only-actions.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
