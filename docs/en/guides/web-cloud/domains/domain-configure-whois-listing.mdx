## Objective

WHOIS, short for "Who is?", is a protocol that allows you to access different information related to a domain name.

For example, you can find the following information (non-exhaustive list):

- Contact details of the holder (subscriber), the administrator and the technical contact of the service.
- Indication of availability for purchase.
- Creation, expiry, last modification and renewal dates.
- Status (created, blocked, expired, etc.).
- etc.

When using your domain name, you may find that you need to show or hide certain information in WHOIS.

**Find out how to show or hide certain information contained in a domain name's WHOIS from the OVHcloud Console.**

## Requirements

- A [domain name](/links/web/domains)

[[cpnav:web-domains-console]]

:::warning
Some registries (organizations that manage domain name extensions, such as *.com*, *.net*, etc.) do not authorize the masking or display of certain information contained in WHOIS.
Registries define the display rules and determine what information the WHOIS extension they manage should or should not contain.

Therefore, it is possible that despite your wishes in terms of display or masking in the WHOIS of your domain name, your request will not be taken into account.

:::

## Instructions

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">Domains & DNS</ManagerLink> page, then choose the domain name concerned. The domain name's <code className="action">Overview</code> tab is displayed.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Domains & DNS page and list of the account's domain names" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/listing.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    In the **Contacts** section of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, the **Data protection** line shows the status of your domain name's data protection in the WHOIS. Click <code className="action">Manage RDDS protection</code>.

    If the registry managing your domain name's extension requires the information to be displayed, the **Data protection** line shows **Disabled**, with the message "The registry managing this domain extension does not allow custom data display management.": the <code className="action">Manage RDDS protection</code> button does not appear and this setting cannot be changed.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Contacts section of the Overview tab and Data protection line" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/general-information/data-protection-entry.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    In the **Configure data protection (RDDS)** panel that opens, choose for each contact offered (owner, administrator, technical, etc.) whether to show or hide their information in the WHOIS, within the limits of the actions allowed by the registry managing the extension:

    - **Box ticked**: the contact's information is displayed in the WHOIS.
    - **Box unticked**: the contact's information is hidden (anonymised with the mention "REDACTED").

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Configure data protection panel and contacts visible in the WHOIS" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/general-information/data-protection-drawer.png" loading="lazy" />

    The contacts offered depend on your domain name's extension.

    Once you have made your choices, click the <code className="action">Validate</code> button.

    Changes may take up to **48** hours to take effect. These changes will only be applied **if authorized by the registry managing your domain name extension**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
