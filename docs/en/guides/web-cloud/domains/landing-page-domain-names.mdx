## What is a domain name?

A **domain name** is the unique, human-readable address of your online presence: `mydomain.ovh`, for example. It replaces the technical IP address of your servers with a name that is easy to remember and share, both for a website and for your email addresses.

A domain name is made up of two parts, read from right to left:

- the **extension** (or TLD, *Top-Level Domain*), the suffix that follows the last dot: `.ovh`, `.com`, `.fr`…;
- the **domain** (the *label*), the name you choose and reserve: `mydomain`.

You can then add **sub-domains** in front of your domain (`www.mydomain.ovh`, `blog.mydomain.ovh`) to distinguish your different services.

The domain name is therefore the **gateway** to your online presence: without it, your visitors would have to remember the technical IP address of your server to access your website.

## The lifecycle of a domain name

From registration to renewal, a domain name goes through three main stages. At any time, you can also transfer it. Select a stage to access the corresponding starting guide.

<DomainLifecycle />

### Register

A domain name is obtained from a registrar such as OVHcloud, for a renewable period. You can reserve a new domain or [transfer to OVHcloud](/guides/web-cloud/domains/transfer-incoming-generic-domain) a domain already registered elsewhere. The [domain name & DNS FAQ](/guides/web-cloud/domains/faq-domain-dns) answers the most common questions.

### Configure

Once the domain is registered, you decide what it points to: a website, an email address, or a [redirection](/guides/web-cloud/domains/redirect-domain-name). All of this relies on its [DNS configuration](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns/landing-page-dns). You can also [create sub-domains](/guides/web-cloud/domains/domain-create-subdomains) (such as `blog.mydomain.ovh`) to organise your different services.

### Renew

A domain name is reserved for a defined period: without renewal, it expires and becomes available again to other people. Enable [automatic renewal](/guides/web-cloud/domains/autorenew-domain-name) to never lose your address through a simple oversight.

### Transfer

You remain free to choose at any time: [transfer your domain to OVHcloud](/guides/web-cloud/domains/transfer-incoming-generic-domain) from another registrar, or conversely [transfer it to another registrar](/guides/web-cloud/domains/transfer-outgoing-domain).
