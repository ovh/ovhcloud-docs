## Objective

A **domain name transfer** refers to the process of moving a domain name from one registrar to another. For example, if you have ordered a domain name on our website, OVHcloud is its current registrar. An outgoing domain name transfer must be initiated by the new registrar.

In order to prevent unauthorised domain name transfers, domain names are usually locked, indicated by the status *clientTransferProhibited*. This protection must be lifted in the OVHcloud Console before starting a transfer.

**This guide explains how to prepare your domain name for an outgoing transfer.**

:::warning
If the domain name in question should stay registered at OVHcloud but modified in terms of its management or holder information, an outgoing domain name transfer is not the appropriate procedure.

To transfer the domain name management to another OVHcloud customer account, a **change of contacts** must be made instead. The procedure is described in [this guide](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/managing-contacts).

If you also need to change the **domain name holder**, you should do so **before** you change the domain name contacts. To do this, use our [instructions for changing the holder of a domain name](/guides/web-cloud/domains/trade-domain).

:::

## Requirements

- A [domain name](/links/web/domains) registered with OVHcloud
- You have the authority to request the transfer of the domain name: the holder and/or the people managing it must be informed of the transfer request.
- The registration of the domain name in question was at least 60 days ago **and** it has not been transferred or traded (i.e. change of holder) during the last 60 days

[[cpnav:web-domains-console]]

:::info
If you are the domain name's **holder** but you are currently not able to manage it in the OVHcloud Console, neither by using your own access nor via your domain name's administrative contact, please consult [this guide](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/managing-contacts#special-case-of-a-domain-holder) first before proceeding.

:::

## Instructions

:::warning
The following instructions describe the most common way to transfer a domain name, valid for most Top Level Domains (TLD). However, the specific rules for processes regarding TLDs are solely defined by the appropriate allocation authority i.e. the **registry**. Registrars such as OVHcloud must adhere to these rules and have no influence on registry decisions.

The exact procedure for domain name transfers may therefore vary, especially in case of some country-code TLDs (ccTLD, such as .lu, .uk, .hk, .ro) and a few special purpose TLDs (.am, .fm, etc.). Transfers might also be prohibited for various reasons, e.g. outstanding payment, abuse case or registry lock.

We recommend to consult the following resources in case of any doubt:

- The website of the respective TLD registry
- The [list of TLDs available at OVHcloud](/links/web/domains-tld)
- [ICANN's explanation of EPP Status Codes](https://www.icann.org/resources/pages/epp-status-codes-2014-06-16-en) (to find out which status codes currently apply to your domain name, carry out a *Whois* search, preferably using the respective TLD registry's website)
- Your new registrar's website and management interface, especially for questions about a pending transfer process

Depending on the new registrar you choose, transferring a domain name may be a paid operation. Please verify this before you continue.

:::

### 1 - Remove the transfer protection for the domain name

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">Domains & DNS</ManagerLink> page, then choose the domain name concerned. The domain name's <code className="action">Overview</code> tab is displayed.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Domains & DNS page and list of the account's domain names" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/listing.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    In the **Transfer and security** section, find the **Transfer protection** line. By default, this protection is active: the domain name is **Locked**.

    :::warning
    If your domain name extension does not use a transfer code, the **Outgoing transfer tag** line replaces the **Auth Code** line: unlock the domain name, then click <code className="action">Enter transfer tag</code>.

    :::

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Transfer and security section with transfer protection locked" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/general-information/transfer-protection-locked.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    Click <code className="action">Prepare outgoing transfer</code>. In the **Unlock domain name** window that appears, click <code className="action">Unlock</code> to remove this protection. Wait a few minutes for the status to change from **Unlocking in progress** to **Unlocked**.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Unlock domain name window" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/general-information/unlock-domain-window.png" loading="lazy" />

    :::info
    If the <code className="action">Prepare outgoing transfer</code> link is greyed out and a tooltip states "You don't have the required permissions to perform this action", this means that you do not have sufficient rights to unlock the domain name.

    In addition, if the **Authorization code** window displays the message "**The AUTH/INFO code is not managed by OVHcloud. Contact the registry for your extension directly to obtain it.**", this means that your domain name's transfer code cannot be retrieved via the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>.

    In both cases, check that your account is the **administrator** contact of the domain name using our guide on [contact management](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/managing-contacts) then check that the domain name extension can be unlocked from the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>.

    Some *transfer codes* are directly managed by the *registry* of your domain name extension. A *registry* is an organisation that manages all domain names for a given extension. For example, the **AFNIC** manages all domain names with the extension *.fr*. If this is the case, you must contact the *registry* managing your domain name extension directly to retrieve the *transfer code*.

    :::
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

:::info
Once the protection is lifted, the domain name will remain unlocked for seven days. After this period, the protection will be automatically reactivated. If you do not request a domain name transfer to your new registrar during this time, it will be necessary to remove the protection again.

:::

### 2 - Retrieve the transfer code

:::warning
Note that it is still possible to unlock and retrieve the transfer code of your domain name after its expiration. According to registry rules, a domain name in the [redemption period](https://www.icann.org/resources/pages/epp-status-codes-2014-06-16-en) may need to be restored so that it can be transferred. Contact your new registrar for transfer details.

:::

Once you have removed the transfer protection, you can retrieve your domain name's transfer code.

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">Domains & DNS</ManagerLink> page, then choose the domain name concerned. The domain name's <code className="action">Overview</code> tab is displayed.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Domains & DNS page and list of the account's domain names" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/listing.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    In the **Transfer and security** section, under the **Auth Code** line, click <code className="action">Show authorisation code</code>. This link only appears once the domain name has been unlocked: refresh the page if necessary.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Transfer and security section with the domain name unlocked and the link to display the code" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/general-information/transfer-protection-unlocked.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    The **Authorization code** window appears, displaying your AUTH/INFO code (also known as transfer code, domain password, AUTH-CODE or EPP-Code).

    The code will be requested by your new registrar to complete the transfer process. You can verify the details with your provider.

    We recommend copying the code using the <code className="action">Copy</code> button rather than typing it by hand, since some characters are easily confused.

    :::warning
    As this window points out, transferring your domain name to another registrar deletes the OVHcloud DNS zone and its content. Your website and email service may no longer work.

    :::
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Once the transfer code has been retrieved, **do not lock your domain name again unless you no longer wish to transfer it**.

### 3 - Launch the transfer at your new registrar

Once you have completed the previous steps, start the transfer process, usually by placing an order with your new registrar. The transfer may take up to 10 days.

Depending on the extension, you may receive an email from OVHcloud about the outgoing transfer. Confirming it speeds up the process and declining it cancels the transfer; if you take no action, the transfer completes automatically, usually after 5 days. See [Confirming a change of holder or an outgoing transfer](/guides/web-cloud/domains/confirm-holder-change-or-transfer).

You can also ask our [support team](/links/support-contact) to speed up the outgoing transfer.

For more information, contact the new registrar you have chosen.

:::warning
If your new registrar requests a new transfer code, lock your domain name again (<code className="action">Prepare outgoing transfer</code>, then <code className="action">Lock</code>), then unlock it a few minutes later. You can then retrieve the new transfer code.

:::

#### Cancelling an outgoing transfer

An outgoing transfer cannot be cancelled from the OVHcloud Console, and OVHcloud cannot interrupt a request that is being validated with the registry. To cancel it, contact your new registrar, who handles the request, to find out their cancellation procedure, especially if the validation period (5 days by default) has not yet elapsed.
