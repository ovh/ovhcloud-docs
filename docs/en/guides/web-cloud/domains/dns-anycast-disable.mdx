## Objective

The [Anycast DNS](/links/web/domains-options-anycast) option speeds up your domain name's DNS resolution across the globe. Do you no longer need this acceleration, or no longer wish to renew it when it expires?

**Find out how to cancel the Anycast DNS option for your domain name from the OVHcloud Console.**

## Requirements

- An OVHcloud [domain name](/links/web/domains).
- The Anycast DNS option must be active on the domain name concerned. To check this, see our guide "[How to enable Anycast DNS for your domain name](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-anycast-enable)".

[[cpnav:web-domains]]

## Instructions

:::info
The cancellation does not take effect immediately: the Anycast DNS option remains active and operational until its expiry date. It is not renewed and is removed on that date.
:::

Click the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">Domains & DNS</ManagerLink> page, then choose the domain name concerned. The domain name's <code className="action">Overview</code> tab is displayed.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="List of domain names in the OVHcloud Console" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/listing.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    In the **DNS configuration** section of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, the **Anycast DNS** row shows the status of the option, **Active**. Click <code className="action">Terminate</code>, just below.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="DNS configuration card with the Anycast DNS option active and the Terminate button" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/general-information/anycast-active.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    The **Terminate service** window is displayed. It offers the **Terminate at expiration** option, which shows the date on which your service will be deleted, and reminds you that the termination will be scheduled for the expiration date.

    Click <code className="action">Confirm termination</code> to confirm the termination of the Anycast DNS service.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Once the termination has been recorded, check that it has been taken into account in the **DNS configuration** section of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab: the Anycast DNS option shows **Termination scheduled**. The service remains active until its expiration date, shown in the termination window. As long as the termination has not taken effect, you can cancel it: click <code className="action">Manage termination</code>, then <code className="action">Confirm cancellation</code> in the window that appears.

:::info
After the cancellation's effective date, your domain name no longer uses the Anycast DNS servers. Bear in mind the natural DNS propagation time, which can take up to 48 hours.
:::
