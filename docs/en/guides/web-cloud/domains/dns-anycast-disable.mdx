## Objective

The [Anycast DNS](/links/web/domains-options-anycast) option speeds up your domain name's DNS resolution across the globe. Do you no longer need this acceleration, or no longer wish to renew it when it expires?

**Find out how to cancel the Anycast DNS option for your domain name from your OVHcloud Control Panel.**

## Requirements

- An OVHcloud [domain name](/links/web/domains).
- The Anycast DNS option must be active on the domain name concerned. To check this, see our guide "[How to enable Anycast DNS for your domain name](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-anycast-enable)".
- Access to your OVHcloud Control Panel.

## Instructions

:::info
The cancellation does not take effect immediately: the Anycast DNS option remains active and operational until its expiry date. It is not renewed and is removed on that date.
:::

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **4** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/#/web-domains/domain">Domain names</ManagerLink> page, then select the domain name concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="List of domain names in the OVHcloud Control Panel" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-names.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    Once on the domain name concerned, select the <code className="action">DNS servers</code> tab.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="DNS servers tab of a domain name" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-servers.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    In the right-hand column of the page that appears, click the <code className="action">Cancel Anycast DNS</code> button.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Cancel Anycast DNS button in the DNS servers tab" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-servers/terminate-anycast-dns.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 4">
    The page that appears sets out the purpose of the option, along with its automatic renewal date. Click the <code className="action">Cancel Anycast DNS</code> button.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Cancellation page of the Anycast DNS option" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-servers/terminate-anycast-dns-page.png" loading="lazy" />

    In the confirmation window that appears, click <code className="action">Confirm</code> to cancel the Anycast DNS service.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Confirmation window for the cancellation of the Anycast DNS service" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-servers/terminate-anycast-dns-confirm.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Once the cancellation has been recorded, check it from your domain name's <code className="action">General information</code> tab: the **Configuration** section shows the status of the Anycast DNS option, along with its effective date.

:::info
After the cancellation's effective date, your domain name no longer uses the Anycast DNS servers. Bear in mind the natural DNS propagation time, which can take up to 48 hours.
:::
