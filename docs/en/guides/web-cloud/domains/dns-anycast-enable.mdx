## Objective

Do you need your services (e.g. your website) to be accessible in record time across the globe? Do you want your domain name's DNS resolution to be optimized?  
OVHcloud offers the [Anycast DNS](/links/web/domains-options-anycast) option.

**Find out how to speed up access to your web services with the DNS resolution speed offered by your domain name's Anycast DNS option.**

## Requirements

- A domain name with an extension compatible with the Anycast DNS option.

[[cpnav:web-domains]]

## Instructions

Click the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">Domains & DNS</ManagerLink> page, then choose the domain name concerned. The domain name's <code className="action">Overview</code> tab is displayed.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Domains & DNS page and list of the account's domain names" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/listing.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    In the **Associated Services** card of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, **Security & SSL** part, locate the **Anycast DNS** box, which presents the benefits and the price of the option. Click the <code className="action">Order</code> button.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Associated Services card of the Overview tab, Security & SSL part, and Anycast DNS order box" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/general-information/anycast-offer.png" loading="lazy" />

    :::info
    The <code className="action">Order</code> button is greyed out if your domain name uses external DNS servers: first configure your domain name on the OVHcloud DNS servers. If your domain name does not have an OVHcloud DNS zone, the box offers the <code className="action">Enable my DNS zone</code> button instead.

    :::
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    The **Order Anycast DNS** page is displayed. There is no duration to choose: as the **Commitment** section states, the Anycast DNS option expires on the same date as your DNS zone, and its price is calculated on a pro rata basis for the remaining period.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Anycast DNS option order page and order summary" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/general-information/anycast-order.png" loading="lazy" />

    In the summary, click <code className="action">Continue order</code>. The order page opens, in a new tab by default: continue until you validate the order with a payment method to complete its processing.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

It may take several minutes for the Anycast DNS option to be enabled.
