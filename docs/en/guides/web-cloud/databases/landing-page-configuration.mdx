## Configuring your Web Cloud Databases service

Configure and administer your OVHcloud Web Cloud Databases service: access, databases, users and rights, connection, PostgreSQL extensions, and logs.

To find your way around based on your needs:

- **Do you want to open access and connect to your databases?** See the *Access and connection* section.
- **Are you managing your databases, users, and their rights?** See the *Databases and users* section.
- **Are you administering the server (configuration, logs)?** See the *Server and operation* section.
