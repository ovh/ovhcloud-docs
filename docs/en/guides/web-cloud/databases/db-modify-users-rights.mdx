## Objective

The [Web Cloud Databases](/links/web/databases) solution can contain several databases. You can define one or more users for managing and using your databases. These users may have higher or lower rights depending on their roles in your databases.
While using the product, you may need to modify a user's rights on your [Web Cloud Databases](/links/web/databases).

**Find out how to modify a user's rights on your Web Cloud Databases solution.**

## Requirements

- A [Web Cloud Databases](/links/web/databases) solution and one or more users.

[[cpnav:web-cloud-databases]]

## Instructions

:::info
To create a new user on your Web Cloud Databases solution, refer to the **Creating a user** section of our guide "[Creating databases and users on your database server](/guides/web-cloud/databases/db-create-databases-users)".

:::

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **4** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/databases">Web Cloud DB</ManagerLink> page, then select the instance concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Cloud DB page and list of the account's instances" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-cloud-databases.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    On the page that appears, click on the <code className="action">Users & grants</code> tab.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Users & grants tab of a Web Cloud DB instance" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-cloud-databases/users-and-rights/wcdb-tab.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    Each user is shown in a card bearing their name. In the card of the user concerned, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the **More actions** label, then click <code className="action">Manage grants</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="More actions menu of a user and Manage grants entry" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-cloud-databases/users-and-rights/wcdb-change-password.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 4">
    The panel titled **Grants for** followed by the user's name opens on the right. It lists all the databases on your Web Cloud Databases solution and shows, for each of them, the level of rights your user has, in a drop-down menu to its right.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Rights panel of a user, one level per database" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-cloud-databases/users-and-rights/manage-grants.png" loading="lazy" />

    This is where you can modify your user's rights for each of your databases. To do this, for each database concerned, simply select, in the database's drop-down menu, the level of rights you want to assign to your user. The change takes effect immediately: the message **Grant updated** is displayed.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
