## Objective

[Web Cloud Databases](/links/web/databases) can be activated free of charge from our [web hosting](/links/web/hosting) Performance, Agency, Agency Plus and Agency Max plans. In this case, they are associated with the web hosting plan from which they have been activated. As you use your services, you may find that you need to unlink your Web Cloud Databases solution from the web hosting plan it is linked to.

**This guide explains how to unlink your Web Cloud Databases solution from a web hosting plan.**

## Requirements

- You have a [Web Cloud Databases instance](/links/web/databases) associated with a [Web Hosting](/links/web/hosting) Performance, Agency, Agency Plus or Agency Max plan.
- You are the [Administrator](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/managing-contacts) contact of the OVHcloud services concerned.

[[cpnav:web-hosting]]

## Instructions

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web hosting</ManagerLink> page, then select the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web hosting page and list of the account's hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    In the **Databases** section, you will find the **Web Cloud DB** entry. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to its right, then click <code className="action">Detach my PrivateSQL database</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Databases section and the menu of the Web Cloud DB row" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/general-information/wcdb-detach.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    On the new page that opens, choose the **Commitment** period you want for your Web Cloud Databases solution. The **Summary** reminds you that your database will now be billed at the standard rate and that this action is irreversible. Click <code className="action">Next</code>, then follow the steps indicated until you pay.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Detach my PrivateSQL database page, with the offer, commitment and summary" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/general-information/detach-web-cloud-db.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Once you have completed these 3 steps and paid the purchase order, your Web Cloud Databases solution will be detached within a few hours at most.
