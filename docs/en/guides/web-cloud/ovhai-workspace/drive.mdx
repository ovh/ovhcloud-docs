## Objective

Drive is the file storage application of OVHai Workspace. It lists your files and folders, and lets you create office documents directly in the browser.

**Find out how to store, create and organise your files in OVHai Workspace.**

## Requirements

- An OVHai Workspace account.
- Being signed in to your workspace.

## Instructions <a name="instructions"></a>

### Opening the application

From the home page of your workspace, click <code className="action">Get started</code> on the **Drive** card, or click <code className="action">Drive</code> in the application bar on the left of the screen.

### Finding your way around

The left-hand panel separates what belongs to you (**My drive**) from what others have shared with you (**Shared with me**), while **Home** shows both together.

Sort the list by clicking the **Name**, **Modified** or **Size** headers. The icons above the list create or import a folder or a file.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="The Drive application on My drive, with a folder, a document and a text file" src="/images/web-cloud/ovhai-workspace/drive/drive-home-en.png" loading="lazy" style={{ display: 'block', marginInline: 'auto' }} />

:::warning
The **Trash** is not available yet: consider any deletion as permanent.

:::

### Creating a folder

1. Click <code className="action">New</code> at the top of the left-hand panel, then <code className="action">New folder</code>.
2. Enter the **Folder name**, then click <code className="action">Create</code>.

The folder appears in the list, with its creation date in the **Modified** column.

### Creating a document

<CardGrid columns={2}>
<div>

1. Click <code className="action">New</code>, then point to <code className="action">New file</code>.
2. Choose the format you need:
    - <code className="action">New document</code> for a text document;
    - <code className="action">New spreadsheet</code> for a spreadsheet;
    - <code className="action">New presentation</code> for a presentation.
3. Enter the **File name**, then click <code className="action">Create</code>. The extension (`.docx`, `.xlsx` or `.pptx`) is added if you leave it out.

### Uploading a file

1. Click <code className="action">New</code>, then <code className="action">Upload a file</code>.
2. Select the file on your computer.

The icons above the list also let you import a whole folder.

### Opening a file

Double-click a file to open it in the application that matches its format. The bar at the top offers <code className="action">Back</code>, to return to the list, and <code className="action">Download</code>.

| Files | Open in | Editable |
|---|---|---|
| Documents, spreadsheets and presentations: `.docx`, `.xlsx`, `.pptx`, `.odt`, `.ods`, `.odp` | OnlyOffice | Yes |
| Markdown notes: `.md` | The Markdown editor | Yes |
| PDF | The PDF viewer | No |
| Images, sounds and videos | The image viewer or the media player | No |
| Text and data: `.txt`, `.csv`, `.json`, `.yaml`, `.xml`, `.log` | The text viewer | No |

### Managing a file or a folder

Sharing, moving and copying appear in the action bar, but are not available yet.

### Searching for a file

Type a word in the **Search my files** field above the list. The **Search my workspace** field, at the top of the screen, searches all the applications at once instead.

### What the assistant can do with your files

The assistant searches your workspace and reads the content of files, not just their names:
