## Objective

Your OVHai Workspace account gives you access to Email, Calendar, Drive, Visio and Contacts with a single identifier: your email address.

**Find out how to create your account, sign in, manage and secure it, personalise the interface and recover your access if you have lost it.**

## Requirements

- An invitation to create your account (see [Getting an account](#getting-an-account)).
- A French mobile phone number, to receive a code by text message.
- An email address hosted outside OVHai Workspace, to use as your recovery address.
- Being at least 18 years old.

## Instructions <a name="instructions"></a>

### Getting an account <a name="getting-an-account"></a>

During the test phase (alpha), OVHai Workspace is free and open by invitation. You create your account yourself.

1. On the sign-in screen, click <code className="action">Join the waiting list</code>. Enter your email address, then click <code className="action">Join the list</code>.
2. As soon as a place becomes available, you receive a link at this address to create your space. Remember to check your spam folder.
3. Open the link. In **OVHai email address**, choose your address: it will be your identifier in every application. Click <code className="action">Start</code>.
4. Choose a **Password** of at least 8 characters and confirm it in **Confirm the password**, then click <code className="action">Continue</code>.
5. Enter your French mobile number in **Mobile number**, then click <code className="action">Continue</code>. Enter the code you received by text message in **Verification code**, then click <code className="action">Verify the code</code>.
6. Enter your **Display name** (your first and last name) and your **Recovery email address**, which must differ from your OVHai address.
7. Tick the box confirming that you are at least 18 years old and accept the terms of use and the privacy policy, then click <code className="action">Create my account</code>.

### Monitoring your storage space

The **Storage** section of the <code className="action">Account</code> tab shows the space used and the total space of your account; the same gauge appears at the bottom of the left-hand panel of Email, Calendar, Drive and Visio.

Everything counts: your files and their versions, your emails, your attachments, your meeting recordings and the Email trash. Space taken by items deleted from this trash is given back after about an hour.

### Securing your account

Open the <code className="action">Security</code> tab of the settings. Every change asks for your current password.

2. Enter the new one in **New password**, then a second time in **Confirm new password**.
3. Click <code className="action">Change password</code>.

The button stays greyed out while the fields are empty.

#### Setting a recovery address <a name="recovery-address"></a>

The recovery address receives security notices and the password reset link; without it, you cannot [recover access to your account](#recovering-access) if you forget your password. Choose an address you check regularly, hosted outside OVHai Workspace.

1. In the **Recovery address** section, click <code className="action">Set</code>.
2. Enter the address in **Recovery email**, then your password in **Current password**.
3. Click <code className="action">Save</code>.

The section then shows the address you chose, with the <code className="action">Change</code> and <code className="action">Remove</code> buttons.

:::warning
No confirmation email is sent to this address: check it carefully before saving it.

:::

#### Adding a phone number <a name="phone-number"></a>

The phone number receives a code by text message when you reset your password. The **Phone number** section shows the verified number.

1. In the **Phone number** section, click <code className="action">Add</code>, or <code className="action">Change</code> to change the number.
2. Enter the number in **Mobile number**, in international format (+33 for France), then your password in **Current password**.
3. Click <code className="action">Send a code</code>.
4. Enter the code you received by text message in **Verification code**, then click <code className="action">Verify</code>.

#### Turning on two-factor authentication <a name="two-factor-authentication"></a>

Two-factor authentication adds, to your password, a 6-digit code generated by an authenticator app installed on your phone.

1. In the **Two-factor authentication** section, click <code className="action">Turn on</code>.
2. Enter your password in **Current password**, then click <code className="action">Continue</code>.
3. Scan the QR code shown with your authenticator app. If you cannot scan it, enter the key shown under **Can't scan it?** in the app.
4. Enter the 6-digit code shown by the app, then click <code className="action">Confirm</code>.
5. 10 recovery codes are shown. Keep them somewhere safe, for example with <code className="action">Copy codes</code>, then click <code className="action">I have saved these codes</code>.

:::warning
The recovery codes are shown only once. Each one signs you in once if you lose your authenticator app.

:::

The section then shows the date two-factor authentication was turned on and the number of recovery codes left. To replace your codes, click <code className="action">Generate new recovery codes</code>, then enter your password and a code from your app (or, with <code className="action">Use a recovery code instead</code>, one of your codes). The old codes then stop working.

### Personalising the interface

<CardGrid columns={2}>
<div>

The <code className="action">Preferences</code> tab of the settings sets the appearance of your whole workspace:

- **Language**: English or Français. The page reloads.
- **Theme**: Light or Dark.
- **Sidebar placement**: the side of the screen where the AI assistant is shown, left or right.
- **App order**: the arrows move an application up or down in the application bar.
- **Sounds**: turns notification sounds on or off, and sets their volume.

</div>

### Signing out

Click your account menu, then <code className="action">Logout</code>. On a shared computer, always sign out.

### Recovering access to your account <a name="recovering-access"></a>

<CardGrid columns={2}>
<div>

If you have forgotten your password:

1. On the sign-in screen, click <code className="action">Forgot your password?</code>.
2. Enter the email address of your OVHai Workspace account, then click <code className="action">Continue</code>.
3. Open the link sent to your [recovery address](#recovery-address). If it has expired, click <code className="action">Ask for a new link</code>.
4. Confirm your identity: enter the code sent by text message to your [phone number](#phone-number), or the code from your authenticator app.
5. Enter your new password, confirm it in **Confirm the password**, then click <code className="action">Save and continue</code>.

Without a recovery address, your password cannot be reset: set it while you still have access to your account.

</div>
