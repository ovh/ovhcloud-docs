## Objective

Calendar is the scheduling application of OVHai Workspace. It shows a mini calendar and the list of your calendars on the left, and the selected period in the middle.

**Find out how to create events, organise your calendars and navigate your schedule in OVHai Workspace.**

## Requirements

- An OVHai Workspace account.
- Being signed in to your workspace.

## Instructions <a name="instructions"></a>

### Opening the application

From the home page of your workspace, click <code className="action">Get started</code> on the **Calendar** card, or click <code className="action">Calendar</code> in the application bar on the left of the screen.

### Finding your way around

The left-hand panel holds the mini calendar and the **My calendars** list. The selected period is shown in the middle, with the arrows, the <code className="action">Today</code> button and the choice of view above it. The AI assistant sits in the side panel, on the right by default.

### Creating an event

<CardGrid columns={2}>
<div>

1. Click <code className="action">New event</code> at the top of the left-hand panel.
2. Enter the title of the event.
3. Enter the start date and time on the **Start** line, then the end date and time on the **End** line. Turn on **All-day** for an all-day event.
4. For a recurring event, replace **Does not repeat** with **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, **Yearly** or **Custom…**.
5. In **Add attendees**, enter the people to invite.
6. Add a **Description** if needed, and turn on **Add a video link** to attach a Visio meeting to the event.
7. Click <code className="action">Create</code>.

The button at the top right of the form shows the calendar of the event, **Personal** (the default calendar) until you choose another one: click it to change it.

</div>

### Changing the view

Four views are available at the top right of the calendar: <code className="action">Day</code>, <code className="action">Week</code>, <code className="action">Month</code> and <code className="action">Year</code>. The week view opens by default; you can change this in the settings (see [Configuring Calendar](#settings)).

To move through time, use the arrows next to the period shown, or click <code className="action">Today</code> to go back to the current date. The mini calendar in the left-hand panel offers the same navigation: click a date to show it, and use its own arrows to change month.

### Organising your calendars

<CardGrid columns={2}>
<div>

The **My calendars** section, under the mini calendar, lists your calendars. Each event belongs to one of them, so you can, for example, separate personal and project appointments. The star marks the default calendar.

To create a calendar, click the plus sign next to **My calendars**, enter the **Calendar name**, choose a colour, then click <code className="action">Create</code>.

Right-click a calendar to show its actions:

- <code className="action">New event here</code> creates an event directly in this calendar;
- <code className="action">Show only this</code> hides the others;
- <code className="action">Set as default</code> makes it the calendar offered when you create an event;
- <code className="action">Rename…</code> and <code className="action">Change colour…</code> change the name and colour of the calendar;
- <code className="action">Sharing…</code> (see [Sharing a calendar](#sharing-a-calendar));
- <code className="action">Delete calendar…</code>.

</div>

#### Sharing a calendar <a name="sharing-a-calendar"></a>

<CardGrid columns={2}>
<div>

1. Right-click the calendar, then click <code className="action">Sharing…</code>.
2. In **Add people**, enter the people to share it with.
3. Choose their access: **Can view** or **Can edit**.
4. Click <code className="action">Share</code>, then <code className="action">Done</code>.

### Searching for an event

The **Search events** field, at the top of the calendar, looks through your events: the results are listed with their calendar, date and time. The **Search my workspace** field, at the top of the screen, searches all the applications at once instead.

### Configuring Calendar <a name="settings"></a>

Open your account menu, then <code className="action">Settings</code>, and choose the <code className="action">Calendar</code> tab:

- **Default view**: the view shown when the calendar opens (Day, Week, Month or Year).
- **Week starts on**, **Working days**, and **Hide non-working days in week view**.
- **Working hours start** and **Working hours end**: the hours outside this range are shaded in the day and week views.
- **Time zone**: it takes your browser's time zone on first start. Check it if you organise meetings with people in another time zone.
- **Notification sound**: the sound played when an invitation, an update or a reply arrives, or **None**.
- **Declining a video meeting removes my access**: if this option is on, declining an invitation that includes a video meeting removes your access to that meeting.

### What the assistant can do with your calendar

Describe the event in one sentence, naming the calendar if you have several:

The assistant shows the operations it runs (looking for free slots, reading your calendars, creating the event). It asks for your approval before creating or changing an event: <code className="action">Approve</code>, <code className="action">Decline</code> or <code className="action">Always accept</code> (see [Staying in control of what it does](/guides/web-cloud/ovhai-workspace/landing-page-ovhai-workspace#staying-in-control)). It then shows the event it created, with its calendar: check it in your schedule.
