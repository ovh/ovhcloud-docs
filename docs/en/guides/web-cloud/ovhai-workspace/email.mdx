## Objective

Email is the messaging application of OVHai Workspace. It shows your mailbox in three panels: your folders, the list of emails, and the email you are reading.

**Find out how to read, write, organise and search your emails in OVHai Workspace.**

## Requirements

- An OVHai Workspace account.
- Being signed in to your workspace.

## Instructions <a name="instructions"></a>

### Opening the application

From the home page of your workspace, click <code className="action">Get started</code> on the **Email** card, or click <code className="action">Email</code> in the application bar on the left of the screen.

### Finding your way around

Your folders are on the left of the screen. The AI assistant sits in the side panel, on the right by default.

The folders are: **Inbox**, **Starred**, **Sent**, **Drafts**, **Archive**, **Spam** and **Trash**. An **Outbox** folder appears while an email is waiting to be sent. The number next to a folder counts unread emails or drafts.

Click <code className="action">Collapse menu</code>, at the bottom of the folder panel, to give the email list more room. The gauge above it shows the storage space used by your account.

### Writing an email

<CardGrid columns={2}>
<div>

1. Click <code className="action">Compose</code> at the top of the folder panel.
2. Enter the address of your recipients in **To**, then press `Enter`. The <code className="action">Cc</code> and <code className="action">Bcc</code> links add recipients in copy and in blind copy.
3. Enter a subject in **Subject**.
4. Write your text in the body of the email.
5. Click <code className="action">Send</code>.

If you close the window without sending, the email is saved in **Drafts**.

</div>
<div>

<img className="thumbnail" alt="The New message window, with the recipient, the subject, the body and the toolbar" src="/images/web-cloud/ovhai-workspace/email/email-compose-en.png" loading="lazy" style={{ display: 'block', marginInline: 'auto' }} />

</div>
</CardGrid>

The toolbar at the bottom of the window formats the text (<code className="action">Rich text</code>), inserts an emoji, attaches a file and discards the draft. The sparkle button asks the AI assistant to write the email (see [What the assistant can do with your messages](#assistant)).

#### Cancelling a send

The email does not leave straight away: it waits 10 seconds by default in the **Outbox** folder. During this delay, the "Sending…" notification offers <code className="action">Undo</code>: the email then goes back to **Drafts**.

You can adjust this delay in the settings (see [Configuring Email](#settings)).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="The Sending… notification with the Undo button, and the Outbox folder in the folder list" src="/images/web-cloud/ovhai-workspace/email/email-undo-en.png" loading="lazy" style={{ display: 'block', marginInline: 'auto' }} />

#### Attaching a file

<CardGrid columns={2}>
<div>

Click the paperclip icon (<code className="action">Attach</code>), then choose:

- <code className="action">From your computer</code> to send a file from your device;
- <code className="action">From another app</code> to pick a file from your Drive.

Each attachment is shown above the toolbar, with its size. Click the bin next to its name to remove it.

</div>
<div>

<img className="thumbnail" alt="The Attach menu open, with From your computer and From another app" src="/images/web-cloud/ovhai-workspace/email/email-attach-en.png" loading="lazy" style={{ display: 'block', marginInline: 'auto' }} />

</div>
</CardGrid>

#### Scheduling an email

<CardGrid columns={2}>
<div>

1. Click the arrow next to the <code className="action">Send</code> button (**Send options**), then <code className="action">Schedule send…</code>.
2. Pick the date and time of sending, then click <code className="action">Set time</code>.
3. The send button becomes "Send in …": click it to confirm.

The "Scheduled for …" notification confirms it and offers <code className="action">Undo</code>. The email waits in **Outbox** until the time you chose.

</div>
<div>

</div>
</CardGrid>

### Reading and sorting your emails

<CardGrid columns={2}>
<div>

Click an email in the list to open it. Replies to the same email are grouped into a thread: the number under the sender shows how many emails it holds, and the arrow next to it expands the thread.

The bar above the open email offers, from left to right:

- **Reply** and **Forward**: a reply adds "Re:" to the subject; a forward adds "Fwd:" and keeps the attachments;
- **Add star**: marks the email with a star and places it in **Starred**; click again (**Remove star**) to take it out;
- **Mark as unread**;
- **Archive**: moves the email to **Archive**;
- **Mark as spam**;
- **Move to Trash**.

An attachment you receive is shown under the email. The button next to its name sends it to your Drive.

</div>
<div>

<img className="thumbnail" alt="A received email with its attachment, the button that sends it to Drive, and the Reply and Forward buttons" src="/images/web-cloud/ovhai-workspace/email/email-attachment-en.png" loading="lazy" style={{ display: 'block', marginInline: 'auto' }} />

</div>
</CardGrid>

Above the list, the <code className="action">All</code>, <code className="action">Unread</code> and <code className="action">With attachment</code> filters narrow it down without changing folder. The <code className="action">…</code> button adds the <code className="action">With attachment</code> filter, which is then shown as a tag; click its cross to remove it. The refresh icon fetches new emails immediately.

### Searching your emails

Enter a word in the **Search emails** field above the list. The search covers all your folders, drafts included.

The **Search my workspace** field, at the top of the screen, searches all the applications at once instead.

### Configuring Email <a name="settings"></a>

Open your account menu, then <code className="action">Settings</code>, and choose the <code className="action">Email</code> tab:

- **Prefer plain text**: shows emails as plain text, even when an HTML version exists.
- **Block remote images**: removes images loaded from the Internet, which are often used to find out whether you opened an email. A banner above the email then lets you show them for that email only.
- **Always show images from**: the list of senders whose images are shown without the banner.
- **Undo Send delay**: the number of seconds during which you can cancel a send (10 by default). The value `0` sends emails immediately.
- **Sound on new mail**, **Sound when sending** and **Sound when cancelling a send**: the sound played, or **None**.

### What the assistant can do with your messages <a name="assistant"></a>

The assistant has access to the mailbox you are viewing: you can hand it reading as well as writing.

<CardGrid columns={2}>
<div>

In the writing window, the sparkle button asks it to write the email in progress. The assistant reads the open email, then asks for your approval before changing the draft, unless you have always accepted the action. <code className="action">Approve</code> runs the action once, <code className="action">Decline</code> prevents it and <code className="action">Always accept</code> lets it repeat the action without asking you (see [Staying in control of what it does](/guides/web-cloud/ovhai-workspace/landing-page-ovhai-workspace#staying-in-control)). It asks you for the recipient if it does not know it.

</div>
<div>

<img className="thumbnail" alt="The assistant's approval request, with the proposed change and the Decline, Always accept and Approve buttons" src="/images/web-cloud/ovhai-workspace/email/email-ai-approve-en.png" loading="lazy" style={{ display: 'block', marginInline: 'auto' }} />

</div>
