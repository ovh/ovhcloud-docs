## Objective

Contacts is the address book of OVHai Workspace. It keeps the people you work with in one place, and makes them available to Email and to the AI assistant.

**Find out how to create, find and organise your contacts in OVHai Workspace.**

## Requirements

- An OVHai Workspace account.
- Being signed in to your workspace.

## Instructions <a name="instructions"></a>

### Opening the application

From the home page of your workspace, click <code className="action">Get started</code> on the **Contacts** card, or click <code className="action">Contacts</code> in the application bar on the left of the screen.

### Finding your way around

The left-hand panel offers:

- **All contacts**, with their number;
- **Collected**, the contacts gathered by the application;
- **My address books**, the list of your address books (the **Contacts** address book exists by default).

In the middle, the list of contacts and the **Search contacts** field; on the right, the card of the selected contact.

### Creating a contact

<CardGrid columns={2}>
<div>

1. Click <code className="action">New contact</code> at the top of the left-hand panel.
2. Fill in the **Name**, then the **Email** and the **Phone**. The <code className="action">Add</code> button under each of these fields adds another one, for a second address or a second number.
3. If needed, fill in the **Organization**, the **Job title** and a **Note**.
4. Click <code className="action">Save</code>.

### Viewing and editing a contact

Click a contact to show their card: job title and organisation, email addresses, phone numbers and note.

- Click an email address to open a new email to this person in Email.
- Click a phone number to call it with the phone application of your device.
- <code className="action">Edit</code> reopens the contact form; <code className="action">Delete</code> deletes the contact.

### Organising your contacts in address books

To create an address book, click the folder icon next to **My address books**, enter its name, then click <code className="action">Create</code>.

To move a contact to another address book, open their card and choose the address book in the **Address book** list, under **Organize**.

### Searching for a contact

Enter a word in the **Search contacts** field: the search also covers the organisation. The **Search my workspace** field, at the top of the screen, searches all the applications at once instead.

### What the assistant can do with your contacts

The assistant reads your contacts to answer questions or complete requests about a person:
