## Objective

Visio is the video conferencing application of OVHai Workspace. It shows your meetings of the day, lets you start or schedule new ones, and keeps the summaries of those that have taken place.

**Find out how to start, schedule, prepare and join a video meeting in OVHai Workspace.**

## Requirements

- An OVHai Workspace account.
- Being signed in to your workspace.
- A microphone and a camera to take part in a meeting.

## Instructions <a name="instructions"></a>

### Opening the application

From the home page of your workspace, click <code className="action">Get started</code> on the **Visio** card, or click <code className="action">Visio</code> in the application bar on the left of the screen.

### Finding your way around

Under **My space**, **Meetings** shows the schedule of the day: the time, the <code className="action">Schedule a meeting</code> and <code className="action">Join with a link</code> buttons, then your **Scheduled** meetings. The arrows next to the date move to the previous or next day. **History** keeps the summaries of your past meetings.

Each scheduled meeting offers <code className="action">Join</code>, the sparkle button (**Prepare the meeting**, see [What the assistant can do with your meetings](#assistant)) and an icon that copies the meeting link.

### Starting a meeting now

<CardGrid columns={2}>
<div>

1. Click <code className="action">New meeting</code> at the top of the left-hand panel.
2. Enter the **Meeting name**.
3. Under **Invite people**, add the email addresses of the participants.
4. Click <code className="action">Start</code>.

Only the people you invite can join.

</div>

### Scheduling a meeting

Click <code className="action">Schedule a meeting</code>. The event form of [Calendar](/guides/web-cloud/ovhai-workspace/calendar) opens, with the video link already turned on ("Video meeting link will be created"): fill in the title, the date, the time and the attendees, then click <code className="action">Create</code>.

Any event created in Calendar with the **Add a video link** option shows up in Visio the same way.

### Joining a meeting

Click <code className="action">Join</code> on the meeting card.

Your account can only be present once in the same meeting: joining from a second device replaces the first connection.

<CardGrid columns={2}>
<div>

A preparation screen appears before you join. It shows your preview, with the microphone and camera buttons, and lets you choose the microphone, the audio output and the camera. It also shows who organises the meeting and who has already joined.

This is where the **Note-taking** switch ("Enable notes and transcription") is, **turned off by default**. Then click <code className="action">Start the meeting</code>.

</div>
<div>

<img className="thumbnail" alt="The preparation screen: camera preview, device choice, meeting name, Note-taking switch and Start the meeting button" src="/images/web-cloud/ovhai-workspace/visio/visio-prejoin-en.png" loading="lazy" style={{ display: 'block', marginInline: 'auto' }} />

</div>
</CardGrid>

:::warning
Check the state of your microphone and camera when you enter the meeting: they may turn on depending on your settings (see [Configuring Visio](#settings)) and on the permissions given to your browser.

:::

### During the meeting

The bottom bar contains:

- full screen;
- the meeting duration;
- the microphone, the camera and the speaker (the arrow next to each of the three lets you choose the device);
- screen sharing;
- the <code className="action">CC</code> subtitles;
- reactions;
- raising your hand;
- the red button that leaves the meeting.

Two panels open on the right of the meeting:

- **Participants**, with <code className="action">Add people</code> to invite someone during the meeting;
- **Meeting options**, with three switches: <code className="action">Enable transcript</code>, <code className="action">Enable recording</code> and <code className="action">Enable AI Summary</code>. The AI notes, the transcript and the recording are then available in the meeting summary.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="A meeting in progress: the participant tile, the Meeting options panel and its three switches, and the control bar at the bottom" src="/images/web-cloud/ovhai-workspace/visio/visio-meeting-en.png" loading="lazy" style={{ display: 'block', marginInline: 'auto' }} />

### Leaving a meeting

Click the red button. If you are alone in the meeting, Visio asks you to choose:

- <code className="action">Keep meeting open</code>: others can still join later;
- <code className="action">End meeting</code>: the meeting is deleted.

The **Search my workspace** field, at the top of the screen, searches all the applications at once instead.

### What the assistant can do with your meetings <a name="assistant"></a>

The sparkle button of a scheduled meeting (**Prepare the meeting**) asks the assistant to prepare the meeting. The assistant reads the meeting details and the matching event in your calendar, then looks through your emails and your Drive for anything related. It gives you a recap and flags points to watch, for example a meeting with no invitee.

You can also ask the assistant questions during the meeting. When you join a meeting that has already started, a notification may offer to summarise what has been said so far (see [Configuring Visio](#settings)).

### Configuring Visio <a name="settings"></a>

Open your account menu, then <code className="action">Settings</code>, and choose the <code className="action">Visio</code> tab:

- **Record meetings by default** and **Enable AI Summary by default**: start the recording or the summary automatically in the meetings you organise.
- **Default background effect** (None, Blur or Office background), **Blur intensity** and **Mirror my camera**.
- **Enable microphone on join** and **Enable camera on join**.
- **Enable subtitles automatically on join**, and **Subtitle translation language**: English, French, Spanish, German, Italian or Portuguese.
- **Offer an AI summary on join after this many captions**, and **Catch-up toast frequency**: the notification that offers to summarise a meeting already under way.
