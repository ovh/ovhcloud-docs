## Objective

The OVHcloud Console allows you to retrieve your SMPP credentials, change your password, manage access to the service, and transfer SMS credits.

**Find out how to manage the settings of an SMS SMPP account from the OVHcloud Console.**

:::info
We recommend that you read the [technical specifications of the OVHcloud SMPP solution](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/smpp-specification).

:::

## Requirements

- an [OVHcloud SMS SMPP account](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/sms/api-sms/)

[[cpnav:telecom-sms]]

## Instructions

Select your SMPP account. Its name sets it apart from other OVHcloud SMS accounts: it starts with `smpp-` instead of `sms-` for standard SMS accounts. Its account format is **SMPP Marketing**.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="List of SMS accounts in the OVHcloud Console, including an SMPP account" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/smpp-control-panel/console/smpp-account-list.png" loading="lazy" />

### Credentials

Click the <code className="action">Options</code> tab. In the **General information** section, you can find the credentials required to use your service: **Status**, **System ID**, secure and non-secure access points, **Windowing** and **Throughput**. Use the button to the right of the system ID and of each access point to copy its contents.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="General information section of the Options tab of an SMPP account" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/smpp-control-panel/console/smpp-credentials.png" loading="lazy" />

If you have forgotten your SMPP password, click <code className="action">Generate a new password</code>.

Click <code className="action">Send</code> to confirm this action. An email with the steps to change your password is sent to the contact email address of your OVHcloud account.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Confirmation window for generating a new SMPP password" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/smpp-control-panel/console/smpp-generate-password.png" loading="lazy" />

### Access management

Click the <code className="action">Options</code> tab.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Allowed IPs section of the Options tab of an SMPP account" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/smpp-control-panel/console/smpp-allowed-ips.png" loading="lazy" />

The **Allowed IPs** section lists the IP addresses of your SMPP clients that are allowed to access the SMPP server. Each address can be removed from the list.

Click the <code className="action">Add an IP</code> button to add IP addresses to this list. You can allow up to 10 IP addresses. In the **Add an allowed IP** window, enter an IPv4 address in the **IP address** field, then click <code className="action">Send</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Add an allowed IP window of an SMPP account" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/smpp-control-panel/console/smpp-add-ip.png" loading="lazy" />
