## About the OVHcloud SMS service

[OVHcloud SMS](/links/telecom/sms) is a messaging service that lets you send SMS messages to your customers and contacts, individually or as bulk campaigns, from the OVHcloud Control Panel or directly from your applications via the API. Find all the guides here to set up, send and manage your SMS messages.

With this service, you can:

- Send SMS messages from the Control Panel, by email, via a URL (http2sms) or through the OVHcloud API
- Launch and track your marketing and transactional SMS campaigns
- Manage your senders, users and recipient lists
- Receive replies from your recipients and hold two-way conversations with them using Time2Chat
- Keep track of your history and top up your SMS credits automatically
