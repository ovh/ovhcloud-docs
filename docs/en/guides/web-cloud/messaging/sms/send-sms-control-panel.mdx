OVHcloud SMS offers are only available in the following countries: France, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Spain, Italy and Poland.
:::

## Objective

You can send SMS messages directly from the OVHcloud Console. This guide gives step-by-step instructions on how to send your first SMS messages.

## Requirements

- an [OVHcloud SMS account](/links/telecom/sms) with SMS credits

[[cpnav:telecom-sms]]

<img className="thumbnail" alt="List of SMS accounts in the OVHcloud Console" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/sms-account-list.png" loading="lazy" />

## Instructions

From the <code className="action">SMS & Campaigns</code> tab, click the <code className="action">Send an SMS or a campaign</code> button, located to the left of the <code className="action">Actions</code> menu.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Send an SMS or a campaign button in the SMS & Campaigns tab" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/send-sms-control-panel/console/send-sms-or-campaign-button.png" loading="lazy" />

### Step 1: Configure the sender and recipient. <a name="configure-sender-recipient"></a>

:::info
For more information on creating and using a sender, refer to our guide "[Everything you need to know about SMS senders](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-senders)".

:::

Once you are on the **Send an SMS or a campaign** page, you can adjust several settings to suit your needs. The first ones are in the **Configure sender and recipient** section.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Configure sender and recipient section of the Send an SMS or a campaign page" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/send-sms-control-panel/console/send-form-sender-recipients.png" loading="lazy" />

For the SMS sender, in the **Sender** field, you can select either a short number that allows recipients to send back replies (**Reply-enabled number (France only)**), or one of the alphanumeric senders of your account.

:::info
For more information on SMS messages that allow replies, refer to our guide "[Sending SMS messages that allow replies](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-with-reply)".

:::

Next, enter the recipient phone number in the **Recipients** field, in international format (+44xxxxxxxxxx), then click <code className="action">Add recipient</code>.

You can also send SMS messages to multiple recipients. Next to the **Recipients** field, the **Pick from a list** menu offers two sources:

- A list of recipients in .csv format, created beforehand from the <code className="action">Recipients</code> tab.
For further information on this, you can refer to our [guide on SMS recipient lists](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/liste-de-destinataire-sms).

- The contacts of an address book. You can create one directly from the OVHcloud Console, or import one as a .csv or .txt file, from the <code className="action">Actions</code> menu of the <code className="action">Contacts</code> tab (**Create an address book** or **Import**).
Feel free to refer to our [guide on managing SMS address books](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-address-books) for further information.

### Step 2: Compose your SMS message. <a name="compose-sms"></a>

:::info
For legal reasons, commercial SMS messages will only be sent between **08:00 and 20:00, from Monday to Saturday**.

:::

Once you have selected a sender and entered your recipients, you can now start composing your message, in the **Compose a message** section.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Compose a message section of the Send an SMS or a campaign page" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/send-sms-control-panel/console/send-form-compose.png" loading="lazy" />

Type your message in the **Message** field. You will see a counter showing the number of remaining characters, and the number of corresponding SMS messages.

:::info
We recommend not exceeding 8 SMS per message. Beyond this limit, operators can no longer guarantee message delivery.

:::

The two tables below list the characters that are authorised in 7bit encoding. The characters in the "**Extensions**" table count as double. 

The maximum size of an SMS message is 160 characters in 7bit encoding (GSM 03.38 standard).

If you use characters that do not appear in these tables, the encoding will switch to Unicode, reducing the maximum size of an SMS message to 70 characters.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="List of authorised SMS characters" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/send-sms-control-panel/smsauthorizedcharacters.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Commercial SMS size <a name="commercial-sms-size"></a>

A commercial SMS message must include the STOP clause. This contains 11 characters and is automatically deducted from the 160 base characters of the first SMS.
The table below therefore indicates the maximum number of characters allowed for commercial SMS messages.

Example: in 7bit encoding, if your message is longer than 149 characters, it will be sent as 2 SMS messages and will therefore cost 2 credits.

| Encoding | First SMS | Second SMS and following |
|---|---|---|
| 7bit (GSM 03.38 standard) | 149 characters | 153 characters |
| Unicode | 59 characters | 70 characters |

#### Advanced options

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Advanced options section with deferred sending selected" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/send-sms-control-panel/console/send-form-advanced-options.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Advanced options** section, you can either send messages immediately (**Send immediately**) or set up deferred sending (**Deferred sending**). For deferred sending, enter the sending date and time in the **Date** and **Time** fields.
