## Objective

Spain requires prior declaration of any alphanumeric sender (Sender ID) with its regulator (CNMC). This guide explains what information to provide and how to open the request.

## Requirements

- An [OVHcloud SMS account](/links/telecom/sms).
- You want to create or have validated an [alphanumeric sender](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-senders#alphanumeric-sender) to send SMS messages to Spain.

## Instructions

### Information to provide

When creating a new Sender ID for Spain, the following information must be provided:

| Information | Description |
|---|---|
| Alias (Sender ID) | The alphanumeric sender to be declared |
| Sender ID holder | Legal entity (Legal) or natural person (Natural) |
| Tax Identification Number (NIF) | NIF of the holder (VAT number for a legal entity, or identity document for a natural person) |
| Company name or first and last name(s) | Of the holder |
| Sender ID representative | NIF, first and last name(s), address and email of the representative (legal entity or natural person). In the case of a legal entity, the details of the representative of that legal entity must also be provided here |
| Third party involved (TERCERO) | Information about any third party involved (other than OVHcloud) in the sending, or confirmation that it is a direct send. For example, a brand holding the alias MyBrand entrusts its sends to its marketing agency, which then triggers campaigns via OVHcloud. The agency is the Tercero: it uses the alias on behalf of the holder without itself being the holder |

### Opening the request

This declaration is made through OVHcloud support:

1. Go to your [OVHcloud Control Panel](/links/support-contact).
2. Open a support ticket specifying that it concerns a **declaration of an SMS Sender ID for Spain**.
3. Provide the information listed above.
