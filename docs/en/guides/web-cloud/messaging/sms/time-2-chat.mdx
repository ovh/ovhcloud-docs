## Objective

Time2Chat is a conversational SMS messaging solution. It allows a business to send and receive messages from a single number starting with "09", available throughout France.

This guide presents the principle of Time2Chat, its benefits, the access conditions and the first steps to use it.

## Requirements

- An [OVHcloud SMS account](/links/telecom/sms).

## Instructions

### General information about Time2Chat

Time2Chat is a two-way conversational messaging solution (brand ↔ customer) that allows a business and its customers to exchange SMS messages in France.

Unlike a standard SMS send, the service is based on a dedicated number starting with "09", assigned specifically to each business. You can use this number to send and receive SMS messages.

A conversation "session" begins as soon as a first message is sent — whether it comes from the brand or the customer. From that moment, the session remains active for 24 hours. During this period, both parties can exchange an unlimited number of SMS messages through the same channel, subject to credits being available on the brand's side. Once the period has elapsed, any new message from the brand or the customer automatically starts a new 24-hour session.

#### Usage examples

- Booking or confirming an appointment.
- Order tracking or delivery notification.
- After-sales service and customer support.
- Satisfaction surveys or quick polls.
- Direct interaction with no app to install.

#### Benefits for your business

- **Universal channel**: SMS remains accessible to anyone with a mobile phone plan.
- **Single, identifiable number** (starting with "09"), reinforcing brand recognition.
- **Smooth experience**: natural and continuous exchanges, with no format constraints.
- **Compliance**: adherence to French regulations on professional messaging.

#### Differences with the legacy SMS offer

The Time2Chat service is based on a conversational model, different from standard SMS sends:

| Feature | Standard SMS | Time2Chat |
|----------------|------------------------|-----------|
| Senders | Shared short number allowing replies, which may change.<br/> Alphanumeric sender. | Dedicated number starting with 09. |
| Type of use | One-way send (notification, alert). | Two-way conversation. |
| Reply handling | One-off replies on the short number, with no conversation thread.<br/> No reply possible for an alphanumeric sender. | 24-hour conversation thread between the brand and the customer. |
| Billing | 1 SMS sent = 1 credit. | 1 SMS sent = 2 credits. |

:::info
The customer agrees to use the Time2Chat service in a balanced way, aiming for an **exchange ratio close to 1:1** between messages sent and received.

:::

#### Access conditions

The launch phase of Time2Chat is reserved for customers who already hold an SMS sender of the virtual number (VLN) type starting with "06" or "07".

We will soon offer this service to all customers who have an [OVHcloud SMS account](/links/telecom/sms).

:::info
Existing SMS credits can be used on Time2Chat.

You can also buy a pack of SMS credits from the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/sms">SMS section of the OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>. Click your SMS account, then, in the **General information** section of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, click <code className="action">Order credits</code>.

:::

### Ordering a Time2Chat number

In the <code className="action">Senders</code> tab of your SMS account, click the <code className="action">Order a Time2Chat number</code> button.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Order a Time2Chat number button in the Senders tab of the OVHcloud Console" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/time-2-chat/console/senders-order-time2chat.png" loading="lazy" />

In the form that appears, fill in all the fields, then click <code className="action">Send</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Time2Chat number order form in the OVHcloud Console" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/time-2-chat/console/time2chat-order-form.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
The information entered can be modified later from the OVHcloud Console.

:::

Follow all the steps before proceeding to payment.

### Configuring a Time2Chat sender

Once your Time2Chat number has been ordered, click the <code className="action">Senders</code> tab of your SMS account. The Time2Chat number automatically appears in the list of senders, with the origin "Virtual" in the **Origin** column. To configure it, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the number, then select <code className="action">Configure</code>. The **Configure the Time2Chat number** page appears.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Configure the Time2Chat number page in the OVHcloud Console" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/time-2-chat/console/time2chat-configure-number.png" loading="lazy" />

This page has three sections, each of which you save with its own <code className="action">Update</code> button:

- **Contact information**: this information is provided in response to incoming SMS messages. Enter the contact name, the phone number and the website. If a customer replies with the keyword "contact", they automatically receive a message containing this information.
- **Automatic response**: enter the message sent automatically in reply to incoming SMS messages (160 characters maximum). If you do not reply to a customer message within 24 hours, an automatic reply (for example: "Your message has been received") is sent to indicate the end of the conversation.
- **KYC** (**K**now **Y**our **C**ustomer): enter the company name, the brand using the service, the main use case, as well as the contact details of the operational contact (name and email).

These elements allow you to personalise your Time2Chat number and to ensure the smooth operation of two-way exchanges between your brand and your customers.

As soon as the customer replies, the **24-hour session** starts. All exchanges made during this period are deducted from your **available SMS credits**, whether they come from your initial pack or from additional purchases.

### Configuring notifications on message reception

You can configure notifications on the reception of SMS messages on your Time2Chat virtual number, so you are alerted by email and/or by SMS whenever a customer replies.

In the tab bar of your SMS account, click <code className="action">Options</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Notification on reply section in the Options tab of the OVHcloud Console" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-with-reply/console/options-reply-cards.png" loading="lazy" />

:::warning
**Information on data confidentiality**

This notification service must be reserved for your own use.

Indeed, the notifications contain information relating to the recipient of your SMS as well as data from your OVHcloud account (name of the SMS account containing your OVHcloud identifier).

:::

In the **Notification on reply** section, click <code className="action">Add a notification</code>. In the **Add a notification** panel, configure a notification by email or by SMS:

- Email notification
    - Sender: you must enter a valid email address belonging to you.
    - Email address: enter the recipient email address of the notification.
- SMS notification
    - Sender: choose a sender from those already validated in your SMS account.
    - Number: enter the recipient number of the notification in **international format**.

Once your notification is configured, click the <code className="action">Save</code> button. You can add several notifications, edit them or delete them.

### Using Time2Chat with the OVHcloud API

If you are new to the OVHcloud API, first read the [First Steps with the OVHcloud APIs](/guides/manage-and-operate/api/first-steps) guide.

The OVHcloud API lets you generate a dedicated web chat interface for your number, on which you exchange messages with your customers.

In the methods below, `serviceName` corresponds to your SMS account (for example `sms-XXXXXX-1`) and `number` to your Time2Chat number in international format (for example `00339XXXXXXXX`).

To generate the web access to the chat and obtain the URL of the interface associated with your Time2Chat number, call the following method:

<Api version="v1" section="/sms" method="POST" route={"/sms/\{serviceName\}/virtualNumbers/\{number\}/chatAccess"} />

The response contains a `url` field: open this link in a browser to access the chat interface and exchange with your customers. You can also retrieve the URL of an existing access with the `GET` method, or delete an access with the `DELETE` method.

You can also manage messages without the chat interface, by integrating Time2Chat directly into your own applications.

The API methods useful for this are:

| Need | Method |
|------|--------|
| Send an SMS | <Api version="v1" section="/sms" method="POST" route={"/sms/\{serviceName\}/virtualNumbers/\{number\}/jobs"} /> |
| List the IDs of received messages | <Api version="v1" section="/sms" method="GET" route={"/sms/\{serviceName\}/virtualNumbers/\{number\}/incoming"} /> |
| Read a received message (sender, content) | <Api version="v1" section="/sms" method="GET" route={"/sms/\{serviceName\}/virtualNumbers/\{number\}/incoming/\{id\}"} /> |
| List the IDs of sent messages | <Api version="v1" section="/sms" method="GET" route={"/sms/\{serviceName\}/virtualNumbers/\{number\}/outgoing"} /> |
| Read a sent message (recipient, content, delivery status) | <Api version="v1" section="/sms" method="GET" route={"/sms/\{serviceName\}/virtualNumbers/\{number\}/outgoing/\{id\}"} /> |

:::warning
OVHcloud will not be able to assist you with the development of your applications. We recommend contacting a [specialist provider](/links/partner).
:::
