SMS response is available in France only.
:::

## Objective

The [SMS response](https://www.ovhcloud.com/fr/sms/marketing/sms-response/) service lets you send an SMS message that the recipient can reply to. You can thus create a reply exchange (booking a medical appointment, games, etc.) with your recipient.

The sender of the SMS response will be a random short number. Its use is included in your SMS offer and the reply is not charged at a premium rate for the recipient.

:::info
**Important**

- The SMS response service is only available for France.
- When your recipient sends a reply, one SMS credit (minimum) is deducted.
- When you reply again to your recipient, one SMS credit (minimum) is deducted.

:::

## Requirements

- An [OVHcloud SMS account](/links/telecom/sms) with credits.

## Instructions

### How it works and limitations

Replies are possible from French mobile operators (Bouygues Telecom, Free, Orange, SFR and their associated virtual operators (MVNOs)).

This means that only numbers geolocated in France and associated with a subscription with one of these operators use compatible SMS messages and will have their replies processed.

Recipients can only reply to your SMS when you use the [virtual number](/links/telecom/sms-vln) or the short number when sending. The latter, with 5 digits, is randomly assigned and is specific to the conversation. It therefore cannot be kept.

The SMS reply service works very simply:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="how SMS with reply works" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-with-reply/SMSreponse.png" loading="lazy" />

The service lets the recipient reply to the SMS received within 48 hours. After this time, the reply will not be taken into account.

The reply (if you enable it) can then be:

- **Automatic** and **unique**: you define a reply (in the OVHcloud Console) that will be sent systematically.
- **Dynamic**: you call a script that handles the reply.

### Managing the reply options

Before sending your SMS response, we recommend that you configure the reply reception options.

In the tab bar, click <code className="action">Options</code>. The reply settings are located in two sections of this tab.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Options tab, Automatic reply to incoming SMS and Notification on reply sections" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-with-reply/console/options-reply-cards.png" loading="lazy" />

You can configure an **automatic reply to incoming SMS** (to reply to your recipient or forward their reply to a script) and/or a **notification on reply** (to keep yourself informed of your recipients' replies).

#### Action on reception

In the **Automatic reply to incoming SMS** section, click <code className="action">Modify</code>. In the **Modify the automatic reply** panel, from the **Response type** drop-down menu, select one of the options offered:

| Option | Effect | Additional action |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed text | An automatic reply with a fixed text is sent. | In the **Reply message** field, enter the text that will be sent by SMS to the recipient<br/>(the corresponding number of credits will be debited from your SMS account on each automatic reply) |
| CGI URL | The reply is forwarded to an external CGI URL. | Enter the URL of your CGI script |
| No reply | No automatic reply is sent. | None |

Once your option is configured, click the <code className="action">Save</code> button.

:::warning
This change takes effect immediately.
:::

:::info
If you choose to reply by SMS, the short number sending the message may be different from the short number that sent your initial SMS.

:::

#### Notification on reception

:::warning
**Information on data confidentiality**

This notification service must be reserved for your own use.

Indeed, the notifications contain information relating to the recipient of your SMS as well as data from your OVHcloud account (name of the SMS account containing your OVHcloud identifier).

:::

In the **Notification on reply** section, click <code className="action">Add a notification</code> to open the **Add a notification** panel.

Then configure a notification by email or by SMS by selecting **Email** or **Text**.

- Email notification (sent to an email address, from an address you choose)
    - **Sender**: you must enter a valid email address belonging to you. This is the address the notification is sent from.
    - **Email address**: enter the recipient email address of the notification.

- SMS notification (sent to a number, from one of your senders)
    - **Sender**: choose a sender from those already validated in your SMS account.
    - **Number**: enter the recipient number of the notification in **international format**.

Once your notification is configured, click the <code className="action">Save</code> button. You can add several notifications: they are listed in the **Notification on reply** section, where the <code className="action">⋮</code> button of each one lets you edit or delete it.

### Sending an SMS response

In the OVHcloud Console, click the <code className="action">SMS & Campaigns</code> tab of your SMS account. Then click <code className="action">Send an SMS or a campaign</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Send an SMS or a campaign button in the SMS & Campaigns tab" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/send-sms-control-panel/console/send-sms-or-campaign-button.png" loading="lazy" />

Make sure that the <code className="action">Reply-enabled number (France only)</code> option is selected in the **Sender** drop-down menu.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Sending form, Sender drop-down menu set to Reply-enabled number (France only)" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/send-sms-control-panel/console/send-form-sender-recipients.png" loading="lazy" />

Then fill in the fields for the recipient(s) and your message. For more details, refer to [our guide on sending SMS messages via the OVHcloud Console](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/send-sms-control-panel).

### Viewing replies

In the OVHcloud Console, click the <code className="action">SMS & Campaigns</code> tab of your SMS account and check that the **SMS** view is selected. Then click <code className="action">Filters</code> and, under **Type**, keep only **Received**. If needed, adjust the **Period** displayed.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="SMS & Campaigns tab, Type filter set to Received and list of received SMS messages" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-with-reply/console/received-sms-filter.png" loading="lazy" />

The list then displays all the SMS messages received in reply to your sends.
