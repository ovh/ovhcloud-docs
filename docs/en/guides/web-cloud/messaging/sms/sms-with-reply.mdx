## Objective

The [SMS response](https://www.ovhcloud.com/fr/sms/marketing/sms-response/) service lets you send an SMS message that the recipient can reply to. You can thus create a reply exchange (booking a medical appointment, games, etc.) with your recipient.

The sender of the SMS response will be a random short number. Its use is included in your SMS offer and the reply is not charged at a premium rate for the recipient.

:::info
**Important**

- The SMS response service is only available for France.
- When your recipient sends a reply, one SMS credit (minimum) is deducted.
- When you reply again to your recipient, one SMS credit (minimum) is deducted.

:::

## Requirements

- An [OVHcloud SMS account](/links/telecom/sms) with credits.

## Instructions

### How it works and limitations

Replies are possible from French mobile operators (Bouygues Telecom, Free, Orange, SFR and their associated virtual operators (MVNOs)).

This means that only numbers geolocated in France and associated with a subscription with one of these operators use compatible SMS messages and will have their replies processed.

Recipients can only reply to your SMS when you use the [virtual number](/links/telecom/sms-vln) or the short number when sending. The latter, with 5 digits, is randomly assigned and is specific to the conversation. It therefore cannot be kept.

The SMS reply service works very simply:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="how SMS with reply works" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-with-reply/SMSreponse.png" loading="lazy" />

The service lets the recipient reply to the SMS received within 48 hours. After this time, the reply will not be taken into account.

The reply (if you enable it) can then be:

- **Automatic** and **unique**: you define a reply (in the Control Panel) that will be sent systematically.
- **Dynamic**: you call a script that handles the reply.

### Managing the reply options

Before sending your SMS response, we recommend that you configure the reply reception options.

In the tab bar, click <code className="action">Options</code> then <code className="action">Reply options</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="SMS response options" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-with-reply/SMSreponse-options.png" loading="lazy" />

You can configure an **action on reception** (to reply to your recipient or trigger a script) and/or a **notification on reception** (to keep yourself informed of your recipients' replies).

#### Action on reception

From the drop-down menu, choose one of the options offered:

| Option | Additional action |
|---|---|
| Reply with a predefined text | Enter the text that will be sent by SMS to the recipient<br/>(the corresponding number of credits will be debited from your SMS account on each automatic reply) |
| Call a CGI | Enter the URL of your CGI script |
| None | None |

Once your option is configured, click the <code className="action">Confirm</code> button.

:::info
If you choose to reply by SMS, the short number sending the message may be different from the short number that sent your initial SMS.

:::

#### Notification on reception

:::warning
**Information on data confidentiality**

This notification service must be reserved for your own use.

Indeed, the notifications contain information relating to the recipient of your SMS as well as data from your OVHcloud account (name of the SMS account containing your OVHcloud identifier).

:::

Click <code className="action">Add a notification</code> to access the following menu.

Then configure a notification by email or by SMS.

- Email notification
    - Sender: you must enter a valid email address belonging to you.
    - Email address: enter the recipient email address of the notification.

- SMS notification
    - Sender: choose a sender from those already validated in your SMS account.
    - Number: enter the recipient number of the notification in **international format**.

Once your notification is configured, click the <code className="action">Confirm</code> button. You can add several notifications, edit them or delete them.

### Sending an SMS response

In the Control Panel, select the <code className="action">Home</code> tab of your SMS account. Then click <code className="action">Send an SMS</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="sending an SMS" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-with-reply/SMSreponse-envoi.png" loading="lazy" />

Make sure that your sender is the `Number enabling replies (France only)`.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="sending an SMS" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-with-reply/SMSreponse-expediteur.png" loading="lazy" />

Then fill in the fields for the recipient(s) and your message. For more details, refer to [our guide on sending SMS messages from the Control Panel](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/send-sms-control-panel).

### Viewing replies

In the Control Panel, click the <code className="action">Message and campaign</code> tab of your SMS account and select <code className="action">SMS management</code>. Then click <code className="action">SMS received</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="SMS received" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-with-reply/SMSreponse-recus.png" loading="lazy" />

A table lists all the SMS messages received in reply to your sends. You can sort these SMS messages by clicking the column headers of the table.
