:::info
OVHcloud SMS offers are only available in the following countries: France, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Spain, Italy and Poland.
:::

## Objective

OVHcloud provides tools in the OVHcloud Console for sending SMS messages. This guide helps you get familiar with these tools and send your first SMS campaign.

## Requirements

- an [OVHcloud SMS account](/links/telecom/sms) with SMS credits

[[cpnav:telecom-sms]]

<img className="thumbnail" alt="List of SMS accounts in the Web Cloud section of the OVHcloud Console" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/sms-account-list.png" loading="lazy" />

## Instructions

The tabs of your SMS account give you access to the main features for sending your SMS campaign. The **Overview** tab also offers the <code className="action">Order credits</code> and <code className="action">Transfer credits</code> links.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Tabs of an SMS account in the OVHcloud Console" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/ma-premiere-campagne-sms/console/account-tabs.png" loading="lazy" />

### Step 1: Add contacts

#### Add a contact list

A contact list can be imported via a .csv or .txt file.

To add a recipient list, click the <code className="action">Recipients</code> tab, then click <code className="action">Add a list</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Recipients tab and Add a list button in the OVHcloud Console" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/liste-de-destinataire-sms/console/recipients-add-list.png" loading="lazy" />

With the following guide, you can find out how to [create a list of SMS recipients](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/liste-de-destinataire-sms).

#### Add an address book

Unlike contact lists, address books contain contacts that you can name and more easily identify for targeted campaigns.

To add an address book, click the <code className="action">Contacts</code> tab, then <code className="action">Actions</code> and <code className="action">Create an address book</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Actions menu of the Contacts tab with the Create an address book option in the OVHcloud Console" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/ma-premiere-campagne-sms/console/contacts-actions-menu.png" loading="lazy" />

Refer to our guide on [Managing SMS address books](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-address-books) to find out more.

### Step 2: Create a sender

By default, SMS messages from an OVHcloud account in France are sent from a short number, which enables recipients to reply. You may want to request an alphanumeric sender via the <code className="action">Senders</code> tab and the <code className="action">Add a sender</code> button, so that your SMS messages are sent with your company or organisation's name.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Senders tab and Add a sender button in the OVHcloud Console" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/ma-premiere-campagne-sms/console/senders-add-sender.png" loading="lazy" />

See the [section on choosing an SMS sender in our guide "Sending SMS messages via the OVHcloud Console"](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/send-sms-control-panel#configure-sender-recipient).

### Step 3: Sending an SMS campaign

In the <code className="action">SMS & Campaigns</code> tab, you can access the sending options, the log of SMS messages sent and received, and the SMS messages scheduled for deferred sending.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="SMS & Campaigns tab of an SMS account in the OVHcloud Console" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/ma-premiere-campagne-sms/console/sms-campaigns-tab.png" loading="lazy" />

To send a single SMS message via the OVHcloud Console, see our guide on [sending SMS messages via the OVHcloud Console](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/send-sms-control-panel).

To send an SMS campaign, click <code className="action">Send an SMS or a campaign</code>. The same form is used to send a single SMS message and a campaign. To display your campaigns rather than your SMS messages in the list, select **Campaigns** at the top right of the list.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Send an SMS or a campaign button and Campaigns selector in the OVHcloud Console" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/ma-premiere-campagne-sms/console/campaigns-send-button.png" loading="lazy" />

First, enter a name for your campaign in the **Campaign name** field.

Then choose the sender from those available in the **Sender** list.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Sender and recipient configuration section of the sending form in the OVHcloud Console" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/send-sms-control-panel/console/send-form-sender-recipients.png" loading="lazy" />

Then compose your message in the **Compose a message** section and, in the **Advanced options** section, choose between **Send immediately** and **Deferred sending**. For deferred sending, set the date and time of sending in the **Date** and **Time** fields.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Advanced options section of the sending form with deferred sending selected in the OVHcloud Console" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/send-sms-control-panel/console/send-form-advanced-options.png" loading="lazy" />

For more information on the size of commercial SMS messages and on message formats, refer to our guide on [sending messages via the OVHcloud Console](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/send-sms-control-panel#commercial-sms-size).

You just need to click the <code className="action">Send</code> button to send or schedule your campaign.
