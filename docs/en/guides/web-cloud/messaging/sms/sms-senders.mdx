OVHcloud SMS offers are only available in the following countries: France, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Spain, Italy and Poland.

Spain also requires a regulatory declaration of the alphanumeric sender (Sender ID) with its regulator before it can be used. See the "[Country-specific requirements](#country-specific-requirements)" section.

## Objective

This guide explains how to create and use senders for sending your SMS messages.

## Requirements

- An [OVHcloud SMS account](/links/telecom/sms).

## Instructions

### Available sender types

OVHcloud allows you to choose a sender type to send your SMS messages.

The "Short number allowing replies" sender is available by default on our SMS solutions. However, you can also use an alphanumeric sender or a virtual mobile number.

#### Short number allowing replies

**Only for OVHcloud accounts in France excluding French overseas departments and territories.**

It allows you to receive a reply via <code className="action">Message and campaign</code> > <code className="action">SMS management</code> > <code className="action">SMS received</code>.

:::warning
**Sending SMS containing a URL**

It is not possible to send an SMS containing a URL via a short number allowing replies. Any such message will be blocked.

If you need to send SMS messages containing URLs, you must use an alphanumeric sender.

:::

#### Alphanumeric sender

You can customise your sender. However, you will no longer be able to receive a reply from the recipient of your SMS. To access the management of SMS senders, select the <code className="action">Senders</code> tab (1) when you are on the relevant SMS account.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="sms send control panel" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-senders/sms-send-control-panel04E.png" loading="lazy" />

If you want to add an additional SMS sender, click on the <code className="action">Actions</code> button in the centre, then on <code className="action">Add</code> (2).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="sms send control panel" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-senders/sms-send-control-panel05E.png" loading="lazy" />

On the addition page, you have several options to configure a new SMS sender (3):

- **Add senders manually**: You must enter the desired sender, a description, and a justification for using this sender (4). A supporting document is also required.

**Example**: if you want to send your SMS with the name of your company as the sender, a company document will be requested.

The validation of an alphanumeric sender is usually processed within 72 hours after its creation.

:::info
We ask for a supporting document as part of our security policy. By default, this is a letterhead from the company or brand, including authorisation from a responsible person with a signature and company stamp, an identity document, or a Kbis extract if it is not a registered brand.

:::

- **Add senders from personal data**: You can request a sender based on the contact details of your OVHcloud account. A drop-down list of available senders will then appear.

- **Add senders from your OVHcloud domain names**: You can use a domain name available in your OVHcloud account as a sender. A drop-down list of available senders will then appear.

##### Country-specific requirements <a name="country-specific-requirements"></a>

<details>
<summary>Spain</summary>

Spain requires prior declaration of any alphanumeric sender with its regulator, the CNMC (Comisión Nacional de los Mercados y la Competencia). This requirement applies when you want to create an alphanumeric sender or have one validated to send SMS messages to Spain.

**Information to provide**

When creating a new alphanumeric sender for Spain, provide the following information:

| Information | Description |
|---|---|
| Alias | The alphanumeric sender to be declared |
| Alphanumeric sender holder | Legal entity (Legal) or natural person (Natural) |
| Tax Identification Number (NIF) | NIF of the holder (VAT number for a legal entity, or a copy of both sides of the identity document for a natural person) |
| Company name or first and last name(s) | Of the holder |
| Alphanumeric sender representative | NIF, first and last name(s), address and email of the representative (legal entity or natural person). If the representative is a legal entity, also provide the details of the natural person who represents it. |
| Third party involved (Tercero) | Information about any third party involved (other than OVHcloud) in the sending, or confirmation that the messages are sent directly. For example, a brand holding the alias MyBrand entrusts the sending of its SMS messages to its marketing agency, which then triggers campaigns via OVHcloud. The agency is the Tercero: it uses the alias on behalf of the holder without itself being the holder. |

All the information in the table is mandatory: an incomplete request is likely to be refused.

**Opening the request**

First request your alphanumeric sender in the OVHcloud Control Panel, then submit this declaration to OVHcloud support:

1. Go to the [OVHcloud Help Centre](/links/support-contact).
2. Open a support ticket specifying that it concerns a **declaration of an SMS Sender ID for Spain**.
3. Provide the information listed above.

Until the CNMC has validated the declaration, your recipients in Spain see a phone number instead of your alphanumeric sender. The declaration is needed only once per sender: once it has been validated, you can use the sender as often as you need.

</details>

#### Virtual mobile number

**Only for OVHcloud accounts in France.**

If you have an SMS offer with a virtual mobile number, you can use this number as a sender. For more information, see our [page on virtual mobile numbers](/links/telecom/sms-vln).

:::info
If you already have an SMS account, it is not possible to create a virtual mobile number for an existing account. You will need to order a new SMS account via the [virtual mobile number](/links/telecom/sms-vln) offer page.

:::
