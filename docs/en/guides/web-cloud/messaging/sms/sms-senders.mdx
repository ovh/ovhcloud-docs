## Objective

This guide explains how to create and use senders for sending your SMS messages.

## Requirements

- An [OVHcloud SMS account](/links/telecom/sms).

## Instructions

### Available sender types

OVHcloud allows you to choose a sender type to send your SMS messages.

The "Short number allowing replies" sender is available by default on our SMS solutions. However, you can also use an alphanumeric sender or a virtual mobile number.

#### Short number allowing replies

**Only for OVHcloud accounts in France excluding French overseas departments and territories.**

It allows you to receive replies, which you can find in the <code className="action">SMS & Campaigns</code> tab: display the **SMS** view, click <code className="action">Filters</code>, then select the **Received** type.

:::warning
**Sending SMS containing a URL**

It is not possible to send an SMS containing a URL via a short number allowing replies. Any such message will be blocked.

If you need to send SMS messages containing URLs, you must use an alphanumeric sender.

:::

#### Alphanumeric sender

:::info
To send SMS messages to Spain or Austria, you must first declare your alphanumeric sender (Sender ID) to the national regulator. See the "[Country-specific requirements](#country-specific-requirements)" section.

:::

You can customise your sender. However, you will no longer be able to receive a reply from the recipient of your SMS. To access the management of SMS senders, select the <code className="action">Senders</code> tab when you are on the relevant SMS account.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Senders tab of an SMS account in the OVHcloud Console" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/ma-premiere-campagne-sms/console/senders-add-sender.png" loading="lazy" />

If you want to add an additional SMS sender, click the <code className="action">Add a sender</code> button.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Add a sender page in the OVHcloud Console, Add mode section" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-senders/console/add-sender-page.png" loading="lazy" />

On the **Add a sender** page, the **Add mode** section offers several options to configure a new SMS sender. Select the one that suits you:

- **Manually add senders**: enter the desired sender and a justification for using this sender. The sender has a maximum of 11 characters, letters and digits only, including at least one letter. A phone number cannot be used as a sender, and some countries also require a request to OVHcloud support. A description and supporting documents (up to 10 files of 10 MB maximum each) can also be added.

**Example**: if you want to send your SMS with the name of your company as the sender, a company document will be requested.

The validation of an alphanumeric sender is usually processed within 72 hours after its creation.

:::info

We ask for a supporting document as part of our security policy. By default, this is a letterhead from the company or brand, including authorisation from a responsible person with a signature and company stamp, an identity document, or a Kbis extract if it is not a registered brand.

:::

- **Add senders from personal data**: you can request a sender based on the contact details of your OVHcloud account. Then select the sender from the list of available senders.

- **Add senders from your OVHcloud domain names**: you can use a domain name available in your OVHcloud account as a sender. Then select the sender from the list of available senders.

##### Country-specific requirements <a name="country-specific-requirements"></a>

<details>
<summary>Spain</summary>

Spain requires prior declaration of any alphanumeric sender with its regulator, the CNMC (Comisión Nacional de los Mercados y la Competencia). This requirement applies when you want to create an alphanumeric sender or have one validated to send SMS messages to Spain.

**Information to provide**

When creating a new alphanumeric sender for Spain, provide the following information:

| Information | Description |
|---|---|
| Alias | The alphanumeric sender to be declared |
| Alphanumeric sender holder | Legal entity (Legal) or natural person (Natural) |
| Tax Identification Number (NIF) | NIF of the holder (VAT number for a legal entity, or a copy of both sides of the identity document for a natural person) |
| Company name or first and last name(s) | Of the holder |
| Alphanumeric sender representative | NIF, first and last name(s), address and email of the representative (legal entity or natural person). If the representative is a legal entity, also provide the details of the natural person who represents it. |
| Third party involved (*Tercero*) | Information about any third party involved (other than OVHcloud) in the sending, or confirmation that the messages are sent directly. For example, a brand holding the alias "MyBrand" entrusts the sending of its SMS messages to its marketing agency, which then triggers campaigns via OVHcloud. The agency is the *Tercero*: it uses the alias on behalf of the holder without itself being the holder. |

:::warning
All the information in the table is mandatory: an incomplete request is likely to be refused.

:::

**Opening the request**

First request your alphanumeric sender in the OVHcloud Console, then submit this declaration to [OVHcloud support](/links/support-contact) by opening a support ticket.

In your ticket, specify that it concerns a **declaration of an SMS Sender ID for Spain** and provide the information listed above.

The declaration is needed only once per sender: once it has been validated, you can use the sender as often as you need.

:::info
Until the CNMC has validated the declaration, your recipients in Spain see a phone number instead of your alphanumeric sender.

:::

</details>

<details>
<summary>Austria</summary>

Austria requires prior declaration of any alphanumeric sender with its regulator, the RTR-GmbH (Rundfunk und Telekom Regulierungs-GmbH). This requirement applies when you want to create an alphanumeric sender or have one validated to send SMS messages to Austria, as well as to the senders you already use for this purpose.

**Information to provide**

For each alphanumeric sender concerned, complete and sign the <a href="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-senders/sms-sender-id-austria-form.pdf" download>Sender ID declaration form (PDF)</a>. It asks for the following information:

| Information | Form field | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Alias (Sender ID) | *Sender ID(s)* | The alphanumeric sender to be declared |
| Sender ID holder | *Inhaber (owner)* | Company name (legal entity) |
| Company address | *Adresse (address)* | Full address of the company |
| Contact email | *e-mail* | Email address at which the RTR-GmbH can contact you |
| Reason | *Begründung (reason)* | For example Information, TAN, notification, with a short description |
| Authorised signatory | *Name; Job Title; Company Signature* | Name, job title and signature of the authorised signatory |

:::warning
All the information is mandatory: an incomplete request is likely to be refused.

:::

**Opening the request**

First request your alphanumeric sender in the OVHcloud Console, then submit this declaration to [OVHcloud support](/links/support-contact) by opening a support ticket.

In your ticket, specify that it concerns a **declaration of an SMS Sender ID for Austria** and attach the completed and signed form.

The declaration is needed only once per sender: once it has been validated, you can use the sender as often as you need.

:::warning
Until the RTR-GmbH has validated the declaration, SMS messages sent to Austria with this alphanumeric sender may not be delivered, or may be received with a phone number instead of your sender. In the meantime, we recommend that you do not use this sender for messages to Austria.

:::

</details>

#### Virtual mobile number

**Only for OVHcloud accounts in France.**

If you have an SMS offer with a virtual mobile number, you can use this number as a sender. For more information, see our [page on virtual mobile numbers](/links/telecom/sms-vln).

:::info
If you already have an SMS account, it is not possible to create a virtual mobile number for an existing account. You will need to order a new SMS account via the [virtual mobile number](/links/telecom/sms-vln) offer page.

:::
