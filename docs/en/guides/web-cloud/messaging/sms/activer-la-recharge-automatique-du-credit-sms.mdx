## Objective

This guide explains what SMS credits are, how to set up automatic re-crediting, and how to transfer credits between your SMS accounts.

## Requirements

- an [OVHcloud SMS account](/links/telecom/sms)

## Instructions

### SMS credits

1 SMS credit is the cost for sending 1 SMS message in the United Kingdom. The price is degressive depending on the number of SMS credits you buy at once. 

You can view the list of SMS packs on the [SMS packs page](/links/telecom/sms).

**E.g. to purchase a pack of 100 SMS credits, each credit costs £0.06 ex. VAT.**

Sending 1 SMS message in the United Kingdom costs 1 credit. With this pack, you can send 100 SMS messages in the United Kingdom.

Sending 1 SMS message in India costs 0.4 credit. With this pack, you can send 250 SMS messages in India.

On the [SMS pricing page](/links/telecom/sms-prices), you can view the price in credits for sending SMS messages, depending on their destination.

:::info
An SMS message can only contain a limited number of characters, depending on its encoding. The details on encoding and valid characters are available in the following guide:

[Sending SMS messages via the OVHcloud Console](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/send-sms-control-panel#compose-sms)

:::

### Automatic re-credit

To ensure that you are never short of credit on your account, you can enable automatic re-crediting. Once a minimum threshold of remaining credit has been reached, a new number of credits is automatically added to your SMS account.

:::warning
You can only enable automatic re-crediting if the following conditions are met:

- a SEPA direct debit payment method is present and validated in your OVHcloud account.
- your SMS service must be at least 2 months old.

:::

Go to the <code className="action">Options</code> tab. In the **Auto top-up** section, click <code className="action">Manage</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Options tab of an SMS account, Auto top-up section with the Manage button" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/activer-la-recharge-automatique-du-credit-sms/console/options-auto-top-up.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Manage automatic recharge** panel, fill in the required fields:

- **Minimum threshold**: when this threshold is reached, automatic re-crediting is triggered.
- **Credit quantity**: defines the number of credits to re-credit onto your SMS account. The possible choices are: **Disabled**, 100, 200, 250, 500, 1000, 5000 and 10000. To disable automatic re-crediting, select **Disabled**.

Click the <code className="action">Save</code> button to apply the settings.

:::info

The selected number of credits is ordered and billed automatically.

:::

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Manage automatic recharge panel with the Minimum threshold and Credit quantity fields" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/activer-la-recharge-automatique-du-credit-sms/console/auto-top-up-panel.png" loading="lazy" />

### Transferring credits

:::info
SMS credits can only be transferred between SMS accounts within the same OVHcloud account. Credits cannot be transferred between two different OVHcloud accounts.

:::

In the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab of your SMS account, in the **General information** section, click <code className="action">Transfer credits</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Overview tab of an SMS account, General information section with the Transfer credits link" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/activer-la-recharge-automatique-du-credit-sms/console/overview-transfer-credits.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Transfer credits** panel, choose:

- **Source account**: the SMS account that will transfer the credits.
- **Target account**: the SMS account that will receive the credits.
- **Number of credits**: the number of credits to be transferred.

Click <code className="action">Send</code> to execute the transfer. The transfer is immediate.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Transfer credits panel with the Source account, Target account and Number of credits fields" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/activer-la-recharge-automatique-du-credit-sms/console/transfer-credits-panel.png" loading="lazy" />
