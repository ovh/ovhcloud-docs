HLR Lookup is available in France only.
:::

## Objective

Before sending an SMS message, a [Home Location Register (HLR) Lookup](https://www.ovhcloud.com/fr/sms/home-location-register/) request lets you check whether the recipient's mobile number is still valid and active. The result also indicates the operator the number is attached to.

By checking your numbers with HLR Lookup, you improve the deliverability of your SMS campaigns and avoid sending failures.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Diagram of the HLR flow: sorting valid and inactive numbers before sending" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-hlr/img_4065.png" loading="lazy" />

HLR Lookup also lets you clean your number lists by identifying inactive or unassigned numbers, to keep your customer file up to date.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Diagram of cleaning a number list via HLR" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-hlr/img_4067.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
HLR Lookup is an action that is independent of sending SMS messages. It is up to you to carry it out before each campaign or when cleaning your contact lists.

:::

**This guide explains how to use HLR Lookup from the OVHcloud Console.**

## Requirements

- An [OVHcloud SMS account](/links/telecom/sms) with SMS credits.

[[cpnav:telecom-sms]]

## Instructions

<img className="thumbnail" alt="List of SMS accounts in the OVHcloud Console" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/sms-account-list.png" loading="lazy" />

Select the <code className="action">SMS & Campaigns</code> tab, then click the <code className="action">Actions</code> button.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Actions menu of the SMS & Campaigns tab, with the HLR option" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-hlr/console/actions-hlr.png" loading="lazy" />

In the menu that appears, click <code className="action">HLR</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="HLR page in the OVHcloud Console" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-hlr/console/hlr-page.png" loading="lazy" />

### New HLR request

Click <code className="action">Send an HLR request</code>. In the **New HLR request** window that opens, enter the number you want to test in the **Phone number** field, tick the **I accept the terms & conditions of use for the service.** box, then click <code className="action">Send</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="New HLR request window with the Phone number field" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-hlr/console/hlr-new-request.png" loading="lazy" />

Each request is charged 0.1 SMS credit. See the [SMS pricing](/links/telecom/sms-prices) for more details.

### Viewing past requests

The results of your requests appear in the table at the bottom of the page.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Table of HLR request results" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-hlr/console/hlr-results-table.png" loading="lazy" />

### The different statuses

HLR reports indicate, in the table columns:

- **Date**: the date of the HLR request;
- **Cost**: the cost of the HLR request (for example "0.1 credit");
- **Status**: the status of the HLR request (**Pending**, **In progress**, **Done** or **Error**);
- **Number**: the recipient's number;
- **Carrier**: the recipient's operator;
- **Valid**: the validity of the recipient's number (**Yes** or **No**);
- **Reachable**: the recipient's reachability (**Yes** or **No**);
- **Ported**: the recipient's ported status (**Yes** or **No**);
- **Roaming**: the recipient's roaming status (**Yes** or **No**).

### Importing the recipients file

Click the <code className="action">Recipients</code> tab, then <code className="action">Add a list</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Recipients tab and Add a list button" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/liste-de-destinataire-sms/console/recipients-add-list.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Add a recipient list** panel that opens, add your file in `.csv` or `.txt` format in the **CSV file (.csv, .txt)** area.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Add a recipient list panel" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/liste-de-destinataire-sms/console/add-recipient-list-panel.png" loading="lazy" />

Refer to the "[SMS recipient list](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/liste-de-destinataire-sms)" guide for more details.

### Cleaning the file

Once your list is uploaded to the OVHcloud Console, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button on its row in the <code className="action">Recipients</code> tab, then <code className="action">Clean</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Clean option in the menu of a recipient list" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-hlr/console/recipient-list-clean.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Clean the list** window, 2 options are available:

- **Freemium**: deduplication and syntax check (free).
- **Premium**: deduplication, then a check with the operator that each number exists, via an HLR request (this action is charged 0.1 SMS credit per contact).

Select the option you want, then click <code className="action">Clean</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Clean the list window with the Freemium and Premium options" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-hlr/console/clean-list-options.png" loading="lazy" />

Cleaning the database deduplicates your contacts and removes those that are invalid.
The cleaned list replaces the old one, which is saved and remains accessible. You will receive a report by email at the end of the operation.
