:::

## Objective

Before sending an SMS message, a [Home Location Register (HLR) Lookup](https://www.ovhcloud.com/fr/sms/home-location-register/) request lets you check whether the recipient's mobile number is still valid and active. The result also indicates the operator the number is attached to.

By checking your numbers with HLR Lookup, you improve the deliverability of your SMS campaigns and avoid sending failures.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Diagram of the HLR flow: sorting valid and inactive numbers before sending" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-hlr/img_4065.png" loading="lazy" />

HLR Lookup also lets you clean your number lists by identifying inactive or unassigned numbers, to keep your customer file up to date.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Diagram of cleaning a number list via HLR" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-hlr/img_4067.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
HLR Lookup is an action that is independent of sending SMS messages. It is up to you to carry it out before each campaign or when cleaning your contact lists.

:::

**This guide explains how to use HLR Lookup from your OVHcloud Control Panel.**

## Requirements

- An [OVHcloud SMS account](/links/telecom/sms) with SMS credits.

## Instructions

### Accessing the OVHcloud Control Panel

- **Direct link:** <ManagerLink to="/#/telecom/sms">SMS</ManagerLink>

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Telecom SMS Control Panel" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/telecom/tpl-telecom-03-fr-sms.png" loading="lazy" />

Select the <code className="action">Message and campaign</code> > <code className="action">SMS management</code> tab.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="SMS management" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-hlr/gestion-des-sms.png" loading="lazy" />

Click <code className="action">HLR</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="HLR section in the Control Panel" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-hlr/img_4792.png" loading="lazy" />

### New HLR request

Enter the number you want to test, then click <code className="action">Send lookup</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="New HLR request form" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-hlr/img_4793.png" loading="lazy" />

Each request is charged 0.1 SMS credit. See the [SMS pricing](/links/telecom/sms-prices) for more details.

### Viewing past requests

The results of your requests appear in the table at the bottom of the page.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Table of HLR request results" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-hlr/tableau-historique.png" loading="lazy" />

### The different statuses

HLR reports indicate:

- the date of the HLR request;
- the cost of the HLR request;
- the status of the HLR request;
- the recipient's number;
- the recipient's operator;
- the recipient's active/inactive status;
- the recipient's reachability;
- the recipient's ported status;
- the recipient's roaming status.

### Importing the recipients file

Go to <code className="action">Contacts</code> > <code className="action">Create a contact list</code> and add your file in `.csv` or `.txt` format.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Create a contact list" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-hlr/creer-liste-contacts.png" loading="lazy" />

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Add contacts file" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-hlr/ajouter-fichier-contacts.png" loading="lazy" />

Refer to the "[SMS recipient list](/guides/web-cloud/messaging/sms/liste-de-destinataire-sms)" guide for more details.

### Cleaning the file

Once your address book is uploaded to the Control Panel, select it and proceed with the cleaning.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Selecting the contact address book to clean" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-hlr/nettoyer-liste.png" loading="lazy" />

Two options are available:

- Freemium: deduplication and syntax checks (free).
- Premium: deduplication and verification of contact validity via an HLR request (this action is charged 0.1 SMS credit per contact).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Freemium and Premium cleaning options" src="/images/web-cloud/messaging/sms/sms-hlr/options-de-nettoyage.png" loading="lazy" />

Cleaning the database deduplicates your contacts and removes those that are invalid.
The cleaned file replaces the old one, which is saved and remains accessible. You will receive a report by email at the end of the operation.
