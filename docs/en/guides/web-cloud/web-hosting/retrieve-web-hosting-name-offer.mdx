## Objective

You have forgotten the name of your [web hosting offer](/links/web/hosting)?
You want to check its details or compare it to another offer?

During the use of your services, you may need the name of your web hosting offer.

**Find out how to identify the type of web hosting offer you have from the OVHcloud Console.**

## Requirements

- Have a [web hosting offer](/links/web/hosting).

[[cpnav:web-hosting-console]]

## Instructions

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **2** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web Hosting</ManagerLink> page, then choose the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting page and list of the account's hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    In the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, locate the **Subscription** section. The name of your web hosting plan appears under **Plan** (for example: Perso, Pro, Performance).

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Subscription section and the plan name under Plan" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/general-information/subscription-card.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
