## Objective

Access to your website's logs and statistics is included in your web hosting plan, and is accessible via the OVHcloud Console.

**Find out how to view your website's statistics and logs with your web hosting plan.**

## Requirements

- An [OVHcloud Web Hosting plan](/links/web/hosting).

[[cpnav:web-hosting-console]]

## Instructions

To access the different statistical data and logs on your web hosting plan, click on the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web Hosting</ManagerLink> page, then choose the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting page and list of the account's hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    On the page that pops up, click on the <code className="action">Statistics and logs</code> tab.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Statistics and logs tab of a web hosting plan" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/statistics-and-logs/tab.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    The screen that opens has 5 sections:

    - [Visit statistics](#website-stats): the <code className="action">View statistics</code> link opens your web hosting plan's visit statistics tool.
    - [Logs](#website-logs): the <code className="action">View logs</code> link displays raw logs for your web hosting plan.
    - [Access](#admin-user): the <code className="action">Manage</code> link displays the users who are allowed to access statistics and logs.
    - [Infrastructure statistics](#infra-stats): presents graphical statistics (HTTP requests, FTP commands, CPU usage, outgoing connections, etc.).
    - **Database statistics**: presents the number of SQL requests and their execution time for the selected database.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Top of the Statistics and logs tab with its sections" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/statistics-and-logs/overview.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Visit statistics <a name="website-stats"></a>

To better monitor and manage traffic for your websites, you can use **OVHcloud Web Statistics**, a tool for statistics on visits to and audience measurement of your websites hosted on your web hosting plan.

<video className="thumbnail" autoPlay loop muted playsInline aria-label="ows dashboard" src="/images/assets/screens/other/web-tools/logs/ows-presentation.mp4" />

The **OVHcloud Web Statistics** dashboard has 7 sections:

- **Dashboard**: Shows traffic on your web hosting plan's websites.
- **Browsers**: Ranking of the most frequently used web browsers for browsing your websites.
- **Geolocation**: Proportion of visitors depending on their location.
- **Requests**: Ranking of the most visited pages on your websites.
- **Robots**: Lists the robots that pass through your sites.
- **Status**: Failure and success statistics based on HTTP codes returned.
- **FAQ**: Section dedicated to the most common questions, also explains technical terms that you may encounter in the tool.

The `Period selection` field in the top right-hand corner allows you to select a specific period.

### Website logs <a name="website-logs"></a>

:::info
We will not be able to assist you in interpreting your web hosting plan's logs, as this is strictly a web development issue not related to web hosting plans.

Do not hesitate to contact a [specialist provider](/links/partner) if you experience any difficulties.
:::

You can view your website's raw logs with a delay of around 5 minutes.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="osl statistiques dashboard" src="/images/assets/screens/other/web-tools/logs/osl-statistics-board.png" loading="lazy" />

Different types of logs are available:

- **Web**: Contain the different logs for visits to your website, as well as the different actions taken on your website. For example, you can use it to detect attempts at malicious actions.
- **FTP**: The different connections/commands in FTP will be recorded and stored in these logs.
- **Error**: Find the errors generated by your website here.
- **CGI**: The various calls to cgi.bin scripts that have been made are recorded in these logs.
- **out**: Contain the history of various external requests (TCP outgoing connections) made from your web hosting plan to remote hosts.
- **SSH**: These logs show the different connections/commands made with the SSH protocol.
- **CRON**: Find here the results of the execution of your scheduled tasks [(CRON)](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/cron-tasks) on your web hosting plan.

:::tip
To view CDN statistics and/or logs, please refer to our dedicated guide: "[Web Hosting - View CDN statistics and logs](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/cdn-statistics-and-logs)".
:::

### Infrastructure statistics <a name="infra-stats"></a>

In this section, you can view the activity of your Web Hosting plan's infrastructure, in order to view resource usage.
To do this, click on the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web Hosting</ManagerLink> page, then choose the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting page and list of the account's hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    On the page that appears, click on the <code className="action">Statistics and logs</code> tab, then go to the **Infrastructure statistics** section.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    Different types of graphs are available from the list at the top left of the section. Next to it, choose the period displayed (**Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly** or **Yearly**). The <code className="action">Export CSV</code> button downloads the data of the displayed graph.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Infrastructure statistics section of the Statistics and logs tab" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/statistics-and-logs/infrastructure-statistics.png" loading="lazy" />

    - **FTP commands**: main commands (upload, download, login, delete) via FTP.
    - **HTTP Requests**: number and return code of HTTP requests (2xx, 3xx, 4xx, 5xx).
    - **Mean Response Time**: mean response time of HTTP requests.
    - **Outbound TCP Connections**: requests from your website to an external website.
    - **CPU Usage**: CPU usage on your hosting instance.
    - **Worker Overload**: moments when your hosting exceeds its resources.

    The number of SQL requests is shown in the **Database statistics** section, below, for the database you choose.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### User administration <a name="admin-user"></a>

Creating a user will allow someone to access your web hosting plan's statistics without having access to your OVHcloud account.

To create a new user, click on the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web Hosting</ManagerLink> page, then choose the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting page and list of the account's hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    On the page that appears, click on the <code className="action">Statistics and logs</code> tab, then, in the **Access** section, on <code className="action">Manage</code>. The **Statistics and logs access** panel opens on the right.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    Click <code className="action">Add a user</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Statistics and logs access panel with the Add a user button" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/statistics-and-logs/users-panel.png" loading="lazy" />

    Enter a **Login**, a **Description** if you wish, then a **Password** that you confirm in the **Confirm password** field. The password must contain between 9 and 30 characters, including one lowercase letter, one uppercase letter and one digit, and only letters and digits. Then click <code className="action">Create</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Form for creating a user for statistics and logs" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/statistics-and-logs/add-user-form.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

To access your website's statistics with a user you have created, you must enter the following address by replacing `000` with the cluster number of your web hosting plan and `domain.tld` with the domain name of your website (without `www`):

```bash
https://logs.cluster000.hosting.ovh.net/domain.tld/
```

You can also retrieve the statistics/logs access link directly from the OVHcloud Console. To do this, click on the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web Hosting</ManagerLink> page, then choose the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting page and list of the account's hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    On the page that appears, click on the <code className="action">Statistics and logs</code> tab, then go to the **Website visit statistics** section.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    Click the <code className="action">View statistics</code> link. The statistics open in a new tab of your browser.

    :::info
    The page that opens asks for the login and password of a user allowed to access statistics and logs. If you do not have one yet, create it by following the "[User administration](#admin-user)" part of this guide.

    :::

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="View statistics link in the Visit statistics section" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/statistics-and-logs/view-statistics.png" loading="lazy" />

    On the new page that appears, retrieve the URL in the address bar of your browser.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

:::warning
If you have enabled separate logs on one of your [websites](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/multisites-configure-multisite), users created here cannot access statistics for that specific website.
:::
