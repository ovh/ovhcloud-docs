## Objective

You may see the error page "**Site not installed**" displayed on your web browser, particularly when your website is first installed.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="website not installed" src="/images/assets/screens/other/browsers/errors/site-not-installed.png" loading="lazy" />

**Find out how to identify and resolve the "Site not installed" error page.**

[[fragment:support-scope]]

## Requirements

- An OVHcloud [shared hosting plan](/links/web/hosting).
- Access to the management of the [DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit) your domain name is attached to.

[[cpnav:web-hosting]]

## Instructions

The "**Site not installed**" page appears for two reasons:

- 1: [Your domain name or subdomain is not declared correctly on one of the websites hosted on your web hosting plan](#check-my-websites).
- 2: [Your domain name does not point to the IP address of your web hosting plan](#check-dns-domain).

The following two parts will allow you to correct the `Site not installed` error in both cases.

### 1 - Verify the declaration of your domain name or subdomain on your website hosted on your web hosting plan <a name="check-my-websites"></a>

Click the tabs below to display each of the **3** steps in turn.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/#/web/hosting">Hosting plans</ManagerLink> page, then select the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    On the page that appears, click the <code className="action">My sites</code> tab.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="My sites" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/my-websites.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    In the table that appears, click the <code className="action">></code> button to the left of the name of the website concerned to display the domain names and subdomains linked to it.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Website" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/my-websites/tab.png" loading="lazy" />

    |Scenarios|Actions to take|
    |---|---|
    |The domain name or subdomain linked to your website **appears** in the table.|If you have just added your domain name or subdomain to your website hosted on your web hosting plan, please wait around **twenty minutes**, then refresh your web browser cache. If the message "Site not installed" still appears, go to [part 2](#check-dns-domain).|
    |The domain name or subdomain linked to your website **does not appear** in the table.|Add your domain name or subdomain by following our guide "[How to associate a domain name with an existing website?](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/my-websites-add-domain-existing-website)".|
    |The domain name or subdomain **has been removed** from the table without any action on your part.|Your domain name or its DNS zone may be managed from another account. Add your domain name or subdomain by following our guide "[How to associate a domain name with an existing website? - Add an external domain name](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/my-websites-add-domain-existing-website)".|
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### 2 - Check the IP pointing in your domain name's active DNS zone <a name="check-dns-domain"></a>

This step involves checking that your domain or subdomain points to your Web Hosting plan's IP address from its active DNS zone.

:::info
To find out more about DNS, visit the following pages:

- [Editing an OVHcloud DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit);
- [Creating an OVHcloud DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-create);
- [Modify an OVHcloud domain name's DNS servers](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-server-edit).

:::

#### 2\.1 Identify the IP address of your OVHcloud web hosting plan

Click the tabs below to display each of the **2** steps in turn.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/#/web/hosting">Hosting plans</ManagerLink> page, then select the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    In the **General information** box, you will see the address under **IPv4**.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="find-ipv4-and-ipv6" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/general-information/find-ipv4-and-ipv6.png" loading="lazy" />

Copy the IPv4 address, then continue reading the guide.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

You can also find the IP address associated with your web hosting plan in our guide "[List of IP addresses associated with OVHcloud web hosting plans](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/clusters-and-shared-hosting-ip)".

#### 2\.2 Check the IP address recorded in your domain name's active DNS zone

You must now check that your web hosting plan's IP address appears in your domain name's active DNS zone.

:::info
Before going any further, please note that whenever a change is made in a domain name's active **DNS zone**, a propagation delay of **4 to 24 hours** may be required for the information to be updated across the DNS network.

If you modify the **DNS servers** associated with your domain name directly, this delay can be up to **48 hours**.

:::

To do this, click the tabs below to display each of the **3** steps in turn.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/#/web/hosting">Hosting plans</ManagerLink> page, then select the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    The table that appears will display a DNS record for each row, linked to your domain name at OVHcloud. You can filter the table content by record type or by domain name.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="dns zone" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/tab.png" loading="lazy" />

    :::info
    If the <code className="action">DNS zone</code> tab of your domain name appears as follows:<br/><br/><img className="thumbnail" alt="DNS zone tab of a domain name that is not managed from the OVHcloud Control Panel" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/zone-without-domain-top-of-the-page.png" loading="lazy" /><br/>

    This means that your domain name is not managed from your OVHcloud Control Panel.<br/> Determine its registrar and the DNS servers it is associated with using our [WHOIS](/links/web/domains-whois) tool.<br/> Identify and modify the relevant DNS zone accordingly by following the dedicated section of the guide "[How to associate a domain name with an existing website? - Add an external domain name](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/my-websites-add-domain-existing-website)".

    :::

    Go to step 3 to view the various possible scenarios and the actions to be taken.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    |Possible scenarios|Action to take|
    |---|---|
    |In the active DNS zone, your domain or subdomain points to your Web Hosting plan's IP address with an A record (for an IPv4 address) or an AAAA record (for an IPv6 address).<br/><br/><img className="thumbnail" alt="DNS zone filtered on A records" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/nx-filter-a.png" loading="lazy" />|This indicates that your domain name is correctly configured.<br/>Wait for the DNS propagation time to complete if the change is recent.<br/><br/>Restart your devices (PC, smartphone, router, etc.) and clear your web browser cache. Your domain name's previous configuration may still be cached, which could delay your update from being displayed.|
    |The active DNS zone does not have A or AAAA records that link your domain name or subdomain to the IP address of your Web Hosting plan.|Add the new A or AAAA DNS record or correct the existing record by following [this guide](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit).|
    |The existing A or AAAA DNS record in the DNS zone for your domain name or subdomain points to an IP address other than your Web Hosting plan's.|Add the new A or AAAA DNS record or correct the existing record by following [this guide](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit).|
    |This warning is displayed in the <code className="action">DNS zone</code> tab:<br/><br/><img className="thumbnail" alt="Warning shown in the DNS zone when the domain name uses other OVHcloud DNS servers" src="/images/assets/screens/control-panel/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/message-other-ovh-dns-servers.png" loading="lazy" />|Modify your domain name's DNS servers accordingly by following our guide on "[How to modify the DNS servers of an OVHcloud domain name](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-server-edit)".|
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
