## Objective

You may see the error page "**Site not installed**" displayed on your web browser, particularly when your website is first installed.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="website not installed" src="/images/assets/screens/other/browsers/errors/site-not-installed.png" loading="lazy" />

**Find out how to identify and resolve the "Site not installed" error page.**

[[fragment:support-scope]]

## Requirements

- An OVHcloud [shared hosting plan](/links/web/hosting).
- Access to the management of the [DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit) your domain name is attached to.

[[cpnav:web-hosting]]

## Instructions

The "**Site not installed**" page appears for two reasons:

- 1: [Your domain name or subdomain is not declared correctly on one of the websites hosted on your web hosting plan](#check-my-websites).
- 2: [Your domain name does not point to the IP address of your web hosting plan](#check-dns-domain).

The following two parts will allow you to correct the `Site not installed` error in both cases.

### 1 - Verify the declaration of your domain name or subdomain on your website hosted on your web hosting plan <a name="check-my-websites"></a>

Click the tabs below to display each of the **3** steps in turn.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web Hosting</ManagerLink> page, then choose the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting page and list of the account's hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    Click the <code className="action">My sites</code> tab. In the table, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, to the right of the website concerned, then, in the **Domain names** group, <code className="action">Manage my domains</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Actions menu of a website" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/my-websites/tab.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    The **Manage my domains** panel opens on the right and lists the domain names and subdomains associated with this website.

    |Scenarios|Actions to take|
    |---|---|
    |The domain name or subdomain linked to your website **appears** in the panel.|If you have just added your domain name or subdomain to your website hosted on your web hosting plan, please wait around **twenty minutes**, then refresh your web browser cache. If the message "Site not installed" still appears, go to [part 2](#check-dns-domain).|
    |The domain name or subdomain linked to your website **does not appear** in the panel.|Add your domain name or subdomain by following our guide "[How to associate a domain name with an existing website?](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/my-websites-add-domain-existing-website)".|
    |The domain name or subdomain **has been removed** from the panel without any action on your part.|Your domain name or its DNS zone may be managed from another account. Add your domain name or subdomain by following our guide "[How to associate a domain name with an existing website?](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/my-websites-add-domain-existing-website)".|
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### 2 - Check the IP pointing in your domain name's active DNS zone <a name="check-dns-domain"></a>

This step involves checking that your domain or subdomain points to your Web Hosting plan's IP address from its active DNS zone.

:::info
To find out more about DNS, visit the following pages:

- [Editing an OVHcloud DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit);
- [Creating an OVHcloud DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-create);
- [Modify an OVHcloud domain name's DNS servers](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-server-edit).

:::

#### 2\.1 Identify the IP address of your OVHcloud web hosting plan

Click the tabs below to display each of the **2** steps in turn.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web Hosting</ManagerLink> page, then choose the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting page and list of the account's hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    The hosting plan opens on the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab. In the **General information** section, find the **IPv4** entry.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="General information section, IPv4 and IPv6 addresses" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/general-information/ipv4-ipv6.png" loading="lazy" />

    Copy the IPv4 address using the copy button to its right, then continue reading the guide.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

You can also find the IP address associated with your web hosting plan in our guide "[List of IP addresses associated with OVHcloud web hosting plans](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/clusters-and-shared-hosting-ip)".

#### 2\.2 Check the IP address recorded in your domain name's active DNS zone

You must now check that your web hosting plan's IP address appears in your domain name's active DNS zone.

:::info
Before going any further, please note that whenever a change is made in a domain name's active **DNS zone**, a propagation delay of up to **24 hours** may be required for the information to be updated across the DNS network.

If you modify the **DNS servers** associated with your domain name directly, this delay can be up to **48 hours**.

:::

The DNS zone is viewed from the domain name, not from the web hosting plan. Click on the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">Domains & DNS</ManagerLink> page, then choose the domain name concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Domains & DNS page and list of the account's domain names" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/listing.png" loading="lazy" />

    Under each name, the **Domain + Zone** or **Zone only** label indicates whether the domain name is registered with OVHcloud or whether only its DNS zone is managed there.

    Then click on the <code className="action">DNS Zone</code> tab.

    :::info
    You can also open the zone from the domain name's <code className="action">Overview</code> tab: its **DNS zone** section shows the number of records and has a <code className="action">Manage DNS zone</code> button.
    :::
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    The table that appears displays one DNS record per row, in four columns: **Subdomain**, **Type**, **Target** and **TTL (in seconds)**.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="DNS Zone tab and records table" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/tab.png" loading="lazy" />

    To keep only the records that matter here, click <code className="action">Filters</code> and select <code className="action">A</code> in the **Record type** list, then repeat with <code className="action">AAAA</code> if the list offers it (it only contains the types present in your zone). A search field also lets you find a specific subdomain.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Filters button and Record type list of the DNS zone" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/filter-type.png" loading="lazy" />

    :::info
    If your domain name's tab bar is limited to **Overview**, **DNS Zone**, **DynHost** and **Redirections**, without the **Hosts** tab, only the DNS zone is managed in your OVHcloud account: the domain name itself is registered elsewhere.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Reduced tab bar of a DNS zone with no associated domain name" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/standalone-zone.png" loading="lazy" />

    Find out its "registrar" and the DNS servers it is associated with using our [WHOIS](/links/web/domains-whois) tool. Find and modify the DNS zone concerned accordingly by following the dedicated section of the guide "[How to associate a domain name with an existing website?](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/my-websites-add-domain-existing-website)".
    :::

    Go to step 3 to view the various possible scenarios and the actions to be taken.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    |Possible scenarios|Action to take|
    |---|---|
    |An **A** (IPv4 address) or **AAAA** (IPv6 address) record has, in the **Target** column, the IP address of your web hosting plan noted in the previous step.|This indicates that your domain name is correctly configured.<br/>Wait for the DNS propagation time to complete if the change is recent.<br/><br/>Restart your devices (PC, smartphone, router, etc.) and clear your web browser cache. Your domain name's previous configuration may still be cached, which could delay your update from being displayed.|
    |The table does not contain any **A** or **AAAA** record for your domain name or subdomain.|Add the new A or AAAA DNS record by following [this guide](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit).|
    |The **A** or **AAAA** record exists, but its **Target** is an IP address other than your web hosting plan's.|Correct the existing record by following [this guide](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit).|
    |A banner states that this DNS zone is not authoritative for your domain name.|The changes made here are not taken into account: your domain name uses other DNS servers. Modify them by following our guide "[Modifying an OVHcloud domain name's DNS servers](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-server-edit)".|
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
