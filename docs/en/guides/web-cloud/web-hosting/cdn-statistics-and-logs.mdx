## Objective

This guide will show you how to view the statistics and logs generated by the [CDN option](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/cdn-how-to-use-cdn) available with your [web hosting](/links/web/hosting) plan.

**Find out how to view your website's statistics and CDN logs with your web hosting plan.**

## Requirements

- A [web hosting plan](/links/web/hosting) with the CDN option enabled on at least one domain name.

[[cpnav:web-hosting-console]]

## Instructions

### Viewing CDN statistics

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **4** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web Hosting</ManagerLink> page, then choose the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting page and list of the account's hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    On the page that pops up, click on the <code className="action">Statistics and logs</code> tab.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Statistics and logs tab of a web hosting plan" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/statistics-and-logs/tab.png" loading="lazy" />

    In the **Visit statistics** section, click the <code className="action">View statistics</code> link. The page opens in a new tab of your browser.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="View statistics link in the Visit statistics section" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/statistics-and-logs/view-statistics.png" loading="lazy" />

    :::info
    The page that opens asks for the login and password of a user allowed to access statistics and logs. If you do not have one yet, create it from the **Access** section of the <code className="action">Statistics and logs</code> tab, by following the "[User administration](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/logs-and-statistics#admin-user)" part of our guide on statistics and logs.

    :::

  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    Once logged in, you can access the **OVHcloud Web Statistics** tool. In this tool, click on the form labelled <code className="action">Select domain</code> located at the top of the page, then choose a domain name for which the CDN is active.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 4">
    {/* v8-review: TO DOCUMENT — external tool OVHcloud Web Statistics: no « Cache » entry in the left menu and no CDN-enabled domain in « Sélection du domaine » on two Performance hostings with CDN active (checked 2026-10-08). Confirm with the OWStats team whether CDN statistics still exist there. */}

    In the left column, click on the <code className="action">Cache</code> tab.

    You will now see the CDN statistics for your domain name.

    If you need further information, please refer to our guide on "[Web hosting - View website statistics and logs](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/logs-and-statistics)".
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### View the CDN logs

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **4** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web Hosting</ManagerLink> page, then choose the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting page and list of the account's hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    On the page that pops up, click on the <code className="action">Statistics and logs</code> tab.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Statistics and logs tab of a web hosting plan" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/statistics-and-logs/tab.png" loading="lazy" />

    In the **Logs** section, click the <code className="action">View logs</code> link. The page opens in a new tab of your browser.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="View logs link in the Logs section" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/statistics-and-logs/view-logs.png" loading="lazy" />

    :::info
    The page that opens asks for the login and password of a user allowed to access statistics and logs. If you do not have one yet, create it from the **Access** section of the <code className="action">Statistics and logs</code> tab, by following the "[User administration](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/logs-and-statistics#admin-user)" part of our guide on statistics and logs.

    :::

  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    Once logged in, on the page that appears, click the <code className="action">CDN</code> button in the banner at the top of the page.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="OSL CDN" src="/images/assets/screens/other/web-tools/logs/osl-cdn.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 4">
    On the new page that appears:

    - Select the domain or subdomain concerned via the <code className="action">Subdomain</code> dropdown menu.
    - Enter the date for which you want to view the CDN logs in the <code className="action">Date</code> field.
    - Then click the <code className="action">Generate</code> button.

    :::info
    CDN logs are unavailable the same day they are generated, so you will need to wait for the next day to view them.

    :::

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="OSL CDN - Logs generated" src="/images/assets/screens/other/web-tools/logs/osl-cdn-subdomain-date-selection.png" loading="lazy" />

In the table that pops up, click the <code className="action">Download</code> button to the right of the domain or subdomain concerned to retrieve the CDN logs.

    If you need further information, please refer to our guide on "[Web hosting - View website statistics and logs](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/logs-and-statistics)".
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
