## Getting started with your Web Hosting plan

Just subscribed to an OVHcloud Web Hosting plan? This page gathers the guides to get you started: activate your plan, create and publish your first website, enable the included email addresses, and find the key information about your plan. To configure your plan in more detail, see the Configuration page.

To find your way around based on your needs:

- **Just starting out and want to create and publish your website?** See the *Get started* and *Publish and manage your websites* sections.
- **Want to enable the included emails or manage your websites from the Control Panel?** See the *Publish and manage your websites* section.
- **Looking for the technical information about your plan (offer, cluster)?** See the *Know your hosting plan* section.
