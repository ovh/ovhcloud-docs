## Managing a CMS with one click on your Web Hosting plan

A CMS (Content Management System) is software that lets you create and manage a website without coding, from an administration interface. A 1-click module automatically installs and preconfigures a CMS (WordPress, PrestaShop, Joomla, etc.) on your OVHcloud Web Hosting plan, directly from your Control Panel and without manual installation.

This page brings together the guides to install and manage a 1-click module, change the administrator password and secure access to your CMS.

To find the right guide for your needs:

- **Do you want to install or manage a CMS with one click?** See the *1-click module* section.
- **Do you need to administer or secure your CMS (password, admin area access)?** See the *Administer and secure a CMS* section.

## Installing a CMS manually

1-click installation does not cover every CMS. You can also manually install the CMS of your choice (WordPress, Joomla, PrestaShop, Drupal and more) on your Web Hosting plan.

See the [Installing a CMS](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/landing-page-cms-tutorials) page.
