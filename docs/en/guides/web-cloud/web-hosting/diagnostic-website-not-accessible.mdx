## Requirements

- Administrative access to your domain name in order to change the DNS servers and edit the [DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit)
- No outstanding [payments](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/invoice-management#pay-bills) and [renewals](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/automatic-renewal) of related services (domain name and web hosting plan)

## Instructions

### 1 - Check the validity of your domain name subscription

:::warning
You are solely responsible for renewing your web services.<br/>
As a hosting provider, OVHcloud is required to permanently delete any web services (domain names, hosting plans, email accounts, etc.) that have not been renewed in time, as well as all of the data they store.

As a result, we strongly recommend that you enable [automatic renewal](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/automatic-renewal#instructions) for all of your OVHcloud subscriptions.
:::

To check that your domain name subscription is valid, click on the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/manage-services">My offers and services</ManagerLink> page, then find your domain name in the table.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="My offers and services page filtered to domain names" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/account/manage-services-domains.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    Renew your domain if necessary via the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of its row, then <code className="action">Renew service</code>. The renewal takes place in a new window.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Actions menu of a domain name on manual renewal with Renew service" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/account/manage-services-domain-actions.png" loading="lazy" />

    The <code className="action">Renew service</code> action only appears for a domain on manual renewal. If your domain is on automatic renewal, the menu offers <code className="action">Bring forward payment</code> instead.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    After this renewal, your website will be accessible again within 48 hours maximum.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### 2 - Check the DNS servers

To check the validity of your [DNS servers](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-server-edit), go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">Domains & DNS</ManagerLink> page, then select the domain name concerned.

**Click on the scenario that matches your situation to view the content.**

<details>
<summary>Scenario 1 - No anomalies appear on the DNS servers</summary>

To check the DNS servers declared, click on the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">Domains & DNS</ManagerLink> page, then choose the domain name concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Domains & DNS page and list of the account's domain names" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/listing.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    The domain name opens on the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab. Check the servers shown in the **DNS servers hosting the zone** list of the **DNS configuration** section:

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="DNS configuration card with the configuration and the list of DNS servers hosting the zone" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/general-information/dns-servers-ovhcloud.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    If they are identical to the targets of the `NS` type entries in the <code className="action">DNS Zone</code> tab, go to [part 3](#step3). To display only these entries, click <code className="action">Filters</code> above the table, then select <code className="action">NS</code> in the **Record type** list:

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="DNS zone filtered on the NS records of a domain name" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/filter-ns.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

</details>

<details>
<summary>Scenario 2 - A warning appears above the DNS zone</summary>

Above the DNS zone in your OVHcloud Console, a warning states "**This DNS zone is not authoritative for your domain name.**": the DNS servers used by your domain are not the ones indicated in your zone. To find out which ones are used, see the **DNS servers hosting the zone** list in the **DNS configuration** section of the domain name's <code className="action">Overview</code> tab.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="DNS configuration card with the configuration and the list of DNS servers hosting the zone" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/general-information/dns-servers-ovhcloud.png" loading="lazy" />

Two scenarios are possible:

- The servers listed are of the type "ns **?** .ovh.net" and "dns **?** .ovh.net" (replace "**?**" with any number):

Modify the DNS servers as described in our guide "[How to modify the DNS servers of an OVHcloud domain name](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-server-edit)", so that they are identical to the targets of the `NS` type records in your **DNS zone**.

Your website will then be available within a maximum of 48 hours.

- The servers listed are not of the type "ns **?** .ovh.net" and "dns **?** .ovh.net" (the **Configuration** line of the section then shows **Custom**):

:::warning
In this situation, please contact your DNS Zone provider, your webmaster or a [OVHcloud partner](/links/partner) before making any changes.

The DNS servers used by your domain name may be functional and the problem accessing your website be linked to a missing or incorrect entry in the active [DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-general-information). Changing the DNS servers in this situation might make your email addresses or other online applications related to your domain name unavailable.
:::

It does not list the DNS servers in use, hence the pointer to the Overview « DNS servers » section. */}

</details>

<details>
<summary>Scenario 3 - No NS-type entries appear in the DNS zone</summary>

Your domain's **DNS zone** does not contain any `NS` record: the **Record type** list of the <code className="action">Filters</code> button, which only contains the types present in the zone, does not offer the `NS` type.

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **4** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">Domains & DNS</ManagerLink> page, select the domain name concerned, then open the <code className="action">DNS Zone</code> tab.

  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    Back up the current zone: open the <code className="action">More actions</code> menu of the **DNS zone** card and click <code className="action">Modify zone</code>.

    Copy and paste the content of your **DNS zone** into a text document. Save this document locally. This backup is essential: the reset in step 3 recreates the missing `NS` records, but it replaces all the records in the zone. It will allow you to then recreate, identically, the records you still need (subdomains, `TXT`, `CNAME` records, etc.). The same menu also offers <code className="action">Export zone</code>, which saves the zone directly to a file.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="More actions menu of the DNS zone card with Modify zone and Reset zone" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/zone-actions.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    Open the <code className="action">More actions</code> menu of the **DNS zone** card and click <code className="action">Reset zone</code>. In the **Reset DNS zone** panel, select **Custom values**.

    Enter your hosting (**Where should your domain name point to?**) and email (**Which server should your emails be routed to?**) servers, then click <code className="action">Reset zone</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Reset DNS zone panel with custom values" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/reset-zone.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 4">
    Your website will be accessible again within 24 hours maximum.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

</details>

### 3 - Check the DNS zone <a name="step3"></a>

In this step, you will find your hosting plan's IP address, then add it to your **DNS zone**.

If your website is not hosted on the OVHcloud infrastructure or is managed by another provider, please contact the concerned support service.

If your website is hosted on one of our [Web Hosting offers](/links/web/hosting), click on the tabs below to view each of the **2** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web Hosting</ManagerLink> page, then choose the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting page and list of the account's hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    The hosting plan opens on the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab. In the **General information** section, you will see the **IPv4** and **IPv6** entries.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="General information section, IPv4 and IPv6 addresses" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/general-information/ipv4-ipv6.png" loading="lazy" />

    Copy the IPv4 and/or IPv6 address using the copy button to the right of each address.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Then refer to it in your domain's [DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit), by modifying or creating one or more `A` entries. To display the existing entries, click <code className="action">Filters</code> in the <code className="action">DNS Zone</code> tab, then select <code className="action">A</code> in the **Record type** list.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Filters button and Record type list of the DNS zone" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/filter-type.png" loading="lazy" />

Your website will then be available within a maximum of 24 hours.
