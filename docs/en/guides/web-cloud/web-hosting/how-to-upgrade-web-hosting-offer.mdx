## Objective

In your <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>, you can increase the capacity of your [web hosting plans](/links/web/hosting). A subscription upgrade provides the following advantages:

- More powerful hosting
- More FTP storage space
- Additional databases
- Additional email accounts
- Additional features such as [mailing lists](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/feature-mailing-list) (from [the Professional offer](/links/web/hosting)) or the [Web Cloud Databases service](/links/web/databases) (included in [the Performance offers](/links/web/hosting))

**This guide explains how to upgrade your OVHcloud hosting plan without any service interruptions.**

- An [OVHcloud web hosting plan](/links/web/hosting)

### Modifying your web hosting plan <a name="modify"></a>

To modify your subscription, click on the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web Hosting</ManagerLink> page, then choose the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting page and list of the account's hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    In the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, find the **Subscription** section. Your current plan is shown next to **Plan**. Click <code className="action">Change plan</code> just below.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Subscription section and its Change plan link" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/general-information/subscription-card.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    On the page that opens, choose the range you want in the **Choose your hosting range** section (*Eco*, *Business* or *Agency*), then your new plan in the **Choose your hosting plan** section. Your current plan is labelled `Your current plan`. Plans that cannot be selected are greyed out.

    The **Duration** section requires no choice: the expiry date of your new plan is aligned with that of your current subscription, and a prorated amount is applied.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Change plan page, choice of range, hosting plan and duration" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/general-information/change-offer-page.png" loading="lazy" />

    Click <code className="action">Continue order</code>: the order is created immediately. The purchase order opens in a new tab: the plan change takes effect once it has been paid.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Checking that your web hosting plan is compatible with a lower range solution <a name="checks"></a>

:::warning
You can only change your subscription to a lower range plan if it is the **next lower plan**.
For example, you cannot switch from a *Agency* plan to a *Professional* plan in a single operation.
You will **first** need to downgrade your web hosting plan from the *Agency* plan to the *Performance* plan, and **then** to the *Professional* plan.
:::

**Before changing to a lower range**, please check the following 6 points:

#### 1 - Start SQL databases

Make sure there are enough [databases](/links/web/hosting-options-startsql) in the new plan. Also check that they are of sufficient size.

Otherwise, delete unused databases and reduce the amount of data in them, if necessary. This quantity must not exceed the maximum database size for the new solution. If you need assistance with how to proceed, please contact [OVHcloud partners](/links/partner).

Once you have deleted data from your databases, recalculate the quota used. To do this, click on the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web Hosting</ManagerLink> page, then choose the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting page and list of the account's hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    On the page that pops up, click on the <code className="action">Databases</code> tab.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Databases tab of a web hosting plan" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/databases.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    In the table, click the <code className="action">⁝</code> button in the **Actions** column, to the right of the database concerned, then <code className="action">Recalculate quota</code>. The recalculation starts immediately, with no confirmation required.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Menu of a row in the databases table" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/databases/row-actions-menu.png" loading="lazy" />

    Once launched, the operation may take several minutes. When it ends, the **Read-only** status of your database disappears.
    Your database is now fully operational again.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

:::info
Recalculating your database quota may take up to **15 minutes**. Refresh the page in your web browser if the recalculated quota does not appear after that time.
:::

#### 2 - Web Cloud Databases

If you are using the [Web Cloud Databases](/guides/web-cloud/databases/db-getting-started) solution included with your web hosting plan [Performance](/links/web/hosting), and you want to change your web hosting plan to a [Professional](/links/web/hosting) solution, you will need to detach the Web Cloud Databases solution from your web hosting plan.

To do this, click on the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web Hosting</ManagerLink> page, then choose the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting page and list of the account's hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    In the **Databases** section, you will see **Web Cloud DB**. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to its right, then <code className="action">Detach my PrivateSQL database</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Databases section and menu of the Web Cloud DB row" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/general-information/wcdb-detach.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    On the new page that opens, choose the **Commitment** period you want for your Web Cloud Databases solution. The **Summary** reminds you that your database will now be billed at the standard price and that this action is irreversible. Click <code className="action">Next</code>, then follow the steps until payment.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Detach my PrivateSQL database page, offer, commitment and summary" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/general-information/detach-web-cloud-db.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

With this action, you can order a Web Cloud Databases solution that is independent of your *Performance* subscription. Your server data will be stored.

If you do not want to keep this data, you can also delete your Web Cloud Databases solution before switching to the *Professional* solution:

1. Back up your data by following the instructions in this [guide](/guides/web-cloud/databases/db-backup-export-database-server).<br/>
2. Terminate your Web Cloud Databases server from the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>. To do this, click on the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/databases">Web Cloud DB</ManagerLink> page, then select the instance concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Cloud DB page and list of instances" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-cloud-databases.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    In the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, find the **Subscription** section, then click <code className="action">Terminate service</code>. If your instance is renewed manually, this link is in the section's <code className="action">Additional actions</code> menu.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    In the **Terminate service** window, choose <code className="action">Terminate at expiration</code> or <code className="action">Terminate immediately</code>, then click <code className="action">Confirm termination</code> (or <code className="action">Terminate now</code> if you chose <code className="action">Terminate immediately</code>). With <code className="action">Terminate immediately</code>, your server and all its data are deleted as soon as you confirm.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Terminate service window of a Web Cloud DB instance" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-cloud-databases/terminate-service.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

#### Billing when you change plans <a name="billing"></a>

**Case 1**: When you upgrade your plan to a higher plan, a *pro rata* calculation will be applied until the next renewal date for this initial subscription.
This calculation is the price difference between your original plan and your new plan.

> **Example:**
>
> You subscribed to a [Personal](/links/web/hosting) subscription on 1 January 2024.
>
> On October 31, 2024, you upgrade this **Personal** plan to the [Professional](/links/web/hosting) plan.
>
> Therefore, the amount corresponding to the remaining duration on the **Personal** subscription (2 months, from 01 November 2024 to 01 January 2025) is automatically deducted from the cost of the new **Professional** subscription, until 01 January 2025. You will only pay the difference.  
> From 01 January 2025, the **Professional** subscription will then be billed at its current price.

Follow [these instructions](#modify) to change your solution.

<Region zones={['eu']}>

**Case 2**: When you downgrade your original plan, the remaining subscription time for the original plan is permanently lost. No refund will be made for this remaining time, even if the subscription has several months left. As a result, you will need to pay the full price for your new plan at the time of the downgrade.

> **Example:**
>
> You subscribed to a [Professional](/links/web/hosting) subscription on 1 January 2024.
>
> On October 31, 2024, you downgrade this **Professional** plan to the [Personal](/links/web/hosting) plan.
>
> As a result, the amount corresponding to the remaining duration on the **Professional** subscription (2 months, from 01 November 2024 to 01 January 2025) is lost.
> From 01 November 2024, the **Personal** subscription is billed at the current price (even if you had paid for the remaining 2 months of the **Professional** offer).

Follow [these instructions](#modify) to change your solution.

</Region>
