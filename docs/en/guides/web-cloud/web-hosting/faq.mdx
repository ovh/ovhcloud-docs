<summary>How do I get my website online?</summary>

To put your website online, you will need the following information:

- A [domain name](/links/web/domains) that will correspond to the web address from which your website will be accessible via a web browser (example: *domain.tld*). This domain name must also be associated with your web hosting plan to view the website.
- A [web hosting plan](/links/web/hosting) on which to install your website.

Here are the main steps to follow:

1. Define your project (turn-key website (CMS) installed manually or with OVHcloud 1-click modules/website created by you or a provider, etc.).
2. Put the website files online in your web hosting plan's FTP storage space.
3. Link the website to a database (if the website uses one).
4. Go to your website.

:::tip
For more details on putting a website online on a web hosting plan, please read our detailed guide on [Publishing a website on your Web Hosting plan](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/hosting-how-to-get-my-website-online).

If you choose to install a CMS (WordPress, Joomla!, PrestaShop, Drupal) with our 1-click modules solution, please read our detailed guide "[Setting up your website with a 1-click module (CMS)](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/cms-install-1-click-modules)".

If your website already exists with another provider, and you would like to migrate it to OVHcloud, please read our detailed guide on "[Migrating your website and associated services to OVHcloud](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/hosting-migrating-to-ovh)".

To help you configure your web hosting plan, you can refer to the full set of [guides for our web hosting solutions](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/landing-page-web-hosting).

:::

<summary>How do I transfer my website, database, domain name and emails to OVHcloud without any service interruptions?</summary>

Here are the main steps to follow:

1. Order hosting and email accounts from OVHcloud.
2. Create and pre-configure a DNS zone for your domain name at OVHcloud.
3. Retrieve a full backup of your website.
4. Import your website backup onto your OVHcloud hosting plan.
5. Recreate your email addresses identically at OVHcloud.
6. Declare OVHcloud email servers in your domain name's active DNS zone.
7. Transfer the content of your old email accounts to your new ones at OVHcloud.
8. Reconfigure your email software.
9. Replace your domain name's active DNS servers with OVHcloud DNS servers.
10. Transfer your domain name to OVHcloud.

:::tip
Find all the details in our guide "[Migrating your website and associated services to OVHcloud](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/hosting-migrating-to-ovh)".

:::

<summary>How do I display my website with a URL in HTTPS?</summary>

In order for your website to be accessible with a URL in "HTTPS" (e.g.: `https://domain.tld`), you will need two prerequisites:

- You must have an active SSL certificate for your domain name (or installed on your Web Hosting plan).
- In your website's source code, it must force URLs to be rewritten to HTTPS.

OVHcloud offers [several SSL certificates](/links/web/hosting-options) on web hosting plans.

To activate an SSL certificate on your Web Hosting plan for your website, click on the tabs below to view each of the **4** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web Hosting</ManagerLink> page, then choose the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting page and list of the account's hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    On the page that appears, click the <code className="action">SSL</code> tab.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="SSL tab of a web hosting plan" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/ssl-certificates.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    Above the certificates table, click <code className="action">Add a certificate</code>. In the panel that opens, choose the certificate you want under **Certificate type** from the [available certificates](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ssl-on-webhosting).

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Add a certificate panel and choice of the certificate type" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/ssl-certificates/enable-ssl-lets-encrypt.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 4">
    Continue until you have finished installing the SSL certificate (by confirming the purchase order beforehand, if you choose one of the Sectigo SSL certificates).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

:::tip
Find all the details in the following guides:

- [Web Hosting - Manage an SSL certificate](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ssl-on-webhosting)
- [Web Hosting - How to activate a free Let's Encrypt SSL certificate](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ssl-letsencrypt)
- [Web Hosting - How to activate a Sectigo DV SSL certificate](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ssl-dv)
- [Web Hosting - How to activate a Sectigo EV SSL certificate](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ssl-ev)
- [Web Hosting - How to install a custom SSL certificate](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ssl-custom)

:::

Once you have installed and set up an SSL certificate of your choice on the OVHcloud side, check that the source code of your website rewrites the URLs for accessing your website in HTTPS. If you experience any difficulties with this, please contact your webmaster or one of our [partners](/links/partner).

<summary>What do I do if the OVHcloud "Website under construction" page remains displayed once my website is online?</summary>

<img className="thumbnail" alt="site-in-construction" src="/images/assets/screens/other/browsers/errors/site-en-construction.png" loading="lazy" />

When your web hosting plan is set up, OVHcloud sets up this holding page in the form of a **index.html** file, which is contained in the `www` folder in your web hosting plan's FTP storage space.

There are two possible scenarios:

- If you have installed a [1-click module](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/cms-install-1-click-modules): This file is automatically deleted by our installation robots.
- If you have set up your website manually, then follow the steps below:
- [Log in to your web hosting plan's FTP storage space](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ftp-connection).
- Once you have logged in to the FTP storage space, go down to the `www` directory.
- Rename the file **index.html** to **index.html.old**. This will disable the page after a few minutes.

:::tip
Find more details in the following guides:

- [Logging in to your web hosting plan's FTP storage space](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ftp-connection)
- [Web Hosting - Changing an FTP user password](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ftp-change-password)
- [Tutorial - Using FileZilla with your OVHcloud hosting](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ftp-filezilla-user-guide)
- [Tutorial - Using Cyberduck with a web hosting plan](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ftp-cyberduck-user-guide-on-mac)

<summary>My website redirects to the OVHcloud webmail interface. What should I do?</summary>

<img className="thumbnail" alt="webmail-login-interface" src="/images/assets/screens/website/webmail/webmail-login-interface.png" loading="lazy" />

This situation indicates an incorrect configuration in the [DNS servers](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-server-edit) or [DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit) associated with your domain name.

The most common case is that you have ordered your domain name and web hosting plan separately, so they are not automatically linked to each other via your domain name's DNS zone.

To correct this, click on the tabs below to view each of the **4** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="DNS zone filtered on the NS records of a domain name" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/filter-ns.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

There are three possible scenarios. Click on the tabs below to view the solution to each case.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Case 1">
    The `Targets` (DNS servers) of the `NS` type entries declared in the domain name's DNS zone **are identical** to those noted in the **DNS configuration** section.

    In this case, this means that your domain name (or its *www* sub-domain) points to the IP address of our redirection server (213.186.33.5).

    1. Log in to the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>, then go to the <code className="action">Web Cloud</code> universe.
    2. Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">Domains & DNS</ManagerLink> page, then select the domain name concerned.
    3. On the page that opens, open the <code className="action">DNS zone</code> tab.
    4. In the table that appears (representing your domain name's DNS zone), identify the entry of type `A` whose `Target` is set to the IP address `213.186.33.5`.
    5. On the row concerned, open the <code className="action">⋮</code> actions menu and click <code className="action">Modify</code>.
    6. In the edit form, replace the IP address `213.186.33.5` in the `Target` field with the IP address of the web hosting plan where your website is located, then confirm your changes.

    :::tip
    If necessary, also refer to the following detailed guides:

    - [Editing an OVHcloud DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit)
    - [Web Hosting - List of IP addresses by cluster](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/clusters-and-shared-hosting-ip)

    :::

  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Case 2">
    The `Targets` (DNS servers) of the `NS` type entries declared in the domain name's DNS zone **are not identical** to those noted in the **DNS configuration** section. However, the `Targets` (DNS servers) have one of the following forms:

    - `nsXX.ovh.net` and `dnsXX.ovh.net` **or** `nsXXX.ovh.net` and `dnsXXX.ovh.net` (where each `X` designates a number between **0** and **9**)
    - `nsXX.ovh.ca` and `dnsXX.ovh.ca` **or** `nsXXX.ovh.ca` and `dnsXXX.ovh.ca` (where each `X` denotes a number between **0** and **9**)
    - `ns200.anycast.me` and `dns200.anycast.me` (if you have subscribed to the [DNS anycast](/links/web/domains-options) option)

    :::info
    If one of your DNS servers shown in the **DNS configuration** section has the following form: `sdnsX.ovh.net`, `sdnsX.ovh.ca`, `vpsXXXXXX.ovh.net` or `vpsXXXXXX.ovh.ca` (where each `X` is a digit between **0** and **9**), go directly to **Case 3**.
    These are the names of DNS servers provided by OVHcloud, which only allow our customers to host their DNS configuration directly on their own servers (Dedicated Servers, VPS, etc.).

    :::

    In this case, this means that your domain name is not using the correct OVHcloud DNS servers to apply the DNS zone configuration listed in the <code className="action">DNS zone</code> tab.

    To correct this, follow these steps:

    1. Log in to the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>, then go to the <code className="action">Web Cloud</code> universe.
    2. Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">Domains & DNS</ManagerLink> page, then choose the domain name concerned.
    3. The domain name opens on the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab. In the **DNS configuration** section, click <code className="action">Edit DNS servers</code>.
    4. In the **Modify DNS servers** panel, select the <code className="action">Use OVHcloud default DNS</code> option, then click the <code className="action">Apply configuration</code> button.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Modify DNS servers panel of a domain name" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/general-information/modify-dns-servers.png" loading="lazy" />

    It can take up to **48** hours to propagate the DNS server update applied to a domain name.

    :::tip
    Find all the details in our guide "[How to modify the DNS servers of an OVHcloud domain name](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-server-edit)".

    :::

  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Case 3">
    The `Targets` (DNS servers) of the `NS` type entries declared in the domain name's DNS zone **are not identical** to those noted in the **DNS configuration** section. In addition, the names of the DNS servers noted in the **DNS configuration** section have none of the forms described in **Case 2**, except for the following forms: `sdnsX.ovh.net`, `sdnsX.ovh.ca`, `vpsXXXXXX.ovh.net` or `vpsXXXXXX.ovh.ca` (where each `X` is a digit between **0** and **9**).

    In this case, the active DNS zone applied to your domain name is not managed directly by OVHcloud. Contact your Webmaster, domain name provider, DNS provider or one of our [partners](/links/partner).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<summary>I ordered a domain name with accents and it is displayed strangely in the OVHcloud Console. What should I do?</summary>

You do not need to take any action in this situation. Even if your domain is displayed in [internationalised notation (IDN)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internationalized_domain_name) in the OVHcloud Console, it will work and display completely normally elsewhere. Your website's address will be displayed as you requested it. Your email addresses will also be displayed as you wish to your correspondents.

:::danger
We do not recommend using an email address with an Internationalized Domain Name (IDN) from an email client (Outlook, macOS Mail, etc.). Some email clients do not interpret domain names with accented characters, which blocks the transmission of emails. A sender sending you an email will receive an automatic message stating that your email address does not exist.

**It is recommended that you reserve the same domain name without accents, in addition to your domain name with accented characters, to avoid any incompatibility in email exchanges.**
