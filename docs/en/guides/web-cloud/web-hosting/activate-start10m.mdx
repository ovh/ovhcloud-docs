## Objective

With [free hosting](/links/web/domains-free-hosting), OVHcloud offers you a 100 MB web hosting plan, and an email account with 5 GB of storage.  
This guide will show you how to activate a free hosting on your [domain name](/links/web/domains).

:::warning
This 100 MB free hosting plan is suitable for a simple webpage presentation, it **does not include a database**.  
It is also suitable if you do not need several MX Plan email accounts.  
If you would like to set up a website with several pages and require a database, such as a CMS (WordPress, Joomla!, PrestaShop, Drupal, etc.), please order one of [our web hosting plans](/links/web/hosting) directly from our website or the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>.

:::

**Find out how to activate your free hosting plan.**

## Requirements

- A [domain name](/links/web/domains) in your <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud account</ManagerLink>, detached from any web hosting plan, and with no associated [MX Plan](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-generalities).

:::info
The free hosting is **only** available to our customers in Europe.

:::

## Instructions

To activate your free hosting plan, click on the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">Domains & DNS</ManagerLink> page, then choose the domain name concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Domains & DNS page and list of the account's domain names" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/listing.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    On the domain name's dashboard, scroll down to the **Associated Services** section. In the **Services** column, under the **Website & Hosting** category, click <code className="action">New free hosting 100 MB</code>.

    This entry only appears if the domain name meets the conditions described in the requirements: no web hosting plan or MX Plan must be associated with it.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="New free hosting 100 MB entry in the Associated Services section of a domain name" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/general-information/free-hosting-entry.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    The **Activate free hosting** page is displayed. The **Your offer** section reminds you of the chosen offer, **Free 100M hosting**, included with your domain name.

    In the **DNS configuration** section, choose the changes to make to your DNS zone by ticking one box, both or none:

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Activate free hosting page with the Your offer, DNS configuration and Commitment sections" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/general-information/free-hosting-order.png" loading="lazy" />

    | Choice                                       	| Description                                                                                                               								|
    |--------------------------------------------	|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
    | DNS A record                         	| The domain name will point to the IP address of the free hosting plan.                                               								|
    | MX DNS record 	| The email servers (`mx1.mail.ovh.net`, `mx2.mail.ovh.net`, `mx3.mail.ovh.net`, etc.) of OVHcloud will be applied to the domain name. 	|

    If you do not tick any box, your domain name keeps pointing to its current records, even once the free hosting is set up. You will then have to update your DNS zone yourself when your new website is ready. This choice is suitable if you do not want to interrupt a website already in production.

    :::warning
    If you tick one or both of the `DNS A record` and `DNS MX record` boxes, this will overwrite the configuration originally set up in your domain's DNS zone.  
    Tick both boxes to automatically configure your domain name with the free hosting and email service included.

    If your DNS zone is not managed in your OVHcloud Console, you will need to make the changes manually in your external DNS zone.

    For more details, please refer to our guide on [Editing an OVHcloud DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit).

    :::

    The **Commitment** section indicates that the free hosting is renewed automatically and free of charge every year, until your domain name expires. The summary displayed on the right shows the domain name, the offer and the order total.

    Click <code className="action">Continue</code> to open the order form, read the contracts, then validate your order to complete the activation request for your free hosting.

    {/* v8-review: TO DOCUMENT — the « Continue » / « Continue order » buttons open the OVHcloud order form (order.eu.ovhcloud.com, express/review) in a new tab; it signed the test session out on 2026-10-08, so the contracts step that v6 showed as section 4 was not seen in the Console flow. Confirm the contracts are presented there. */}
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

:::info
If your project quickly needs a hosting plan with a database, more storage space or more email addresses, you can switch directly and only from the free hosting to a **Perso** hosting plan from the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>.

To switch to the **Professional** or **Performance** plan, you will need to switch from the free hosting to the **Personal** plan beforehand.

You can also choose to delete the free offer after backing up your hosting data and the contents of your email account.

For more details, see our [hosting offers](/links/web/hosting).

:::

Once your order has been confirmed, you will be sent an email with the information for the [FTP connection](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ftp-connection) to your free hosting plan.

Please refer to our guide on [Creating an MX Plan email account](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-creation) to take advantage of the email account included with your free hosting plan.
