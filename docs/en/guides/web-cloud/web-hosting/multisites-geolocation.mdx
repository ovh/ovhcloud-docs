## Objective

Search engines (Google, Bing, Yahoo, etc.) use indexing and referencing robots across all websites. They give priority to websites geolocated in the country you are searching from.

**Example**: If you run a search via a search engine while you are located in England, websites geolocated in England will appear higher in the search results than other websites.

This geolocation is based on the IP address of the hosting plan your website is on.

The geolocation option on your hosting plan can be useful for search engine optimisation (SEO) if your website is mainly intended to be viewed in a country other than the one your shared hosting plan is located in.

**Find out how to geolocate your website using our geolocated IP addresses.**

:::info
This procedure applies to <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/">the OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>, currently available in beta. To follow it, switch to this interface from your usual Control Panel.

:::

## Requirements

- An [OVHcloud shared hosting plan](/links/web/hosting)
- A [domain name](/links/web/domains)

## Instructions

For websites that are mainly viewed abroad and hosted on our OVHcloud shared hosting infrastructure, we offer an IP address geolocation option. It allows websites to be better referenced in the country where the IP address chosen with the option is located.

To use the IP geolocation option, click the tabs below to display each of the **5** steps in turn.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web hosting</ManagerLink> page, then choose the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web hosting entry in the menu and list of hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-sites.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    On the page that appears, click the <code className="action">My sites</code> tab.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="My sites tab of a web hosting plan" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/my-sites.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    In the table that appears, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the <code className="action">Actions</code> column, to the right of the website concerned. In the **Domain names** part of the menu, click <code className="action">Manage</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Actions menu of a website in the Console" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/my-websites/tab.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 4">
    The <code className="action">Manage my domains</code> panel opens and lists the domain names and subdomains linked to this site. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the domain name or subdomain concerned, then click <code className="action">Manage my options</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Manage my domains panel and its linked domain names" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/my-websites/associated-domains-options.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 5">
    In the <code className="action">Manage options</code> panel, select <code className="action">Country IP</code> to display the <code className="action">Choose a country</code> drop-down list.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Country IP option and drop-down list of available countries" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/multisite/country-ip-selection.png" loading="lazy" />

    Choose the IP address of the country you want to geolocate your site in, from the 13 countries offered: *Belgium, Czechia, Germany, Spain, Finland, France, Ireland, Italy, Lithuania, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, United Kingdom*.

    Finally, click <code className="action">Confirm</code>.

    :::warning
    The <code className="action">Country IP</code> option and the <code className="action">Enable CDN</code> option belong to the same **Performance and delivery** choice: they are mutually exclusive. Selecting <code className="action">Country IP</code> disables the CDN on this domain name.
    :::
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

:::info
Once you have completed the steps above, if the active DNS zone of your domain name is fully managed in your OVHcloud Control Panel, the A record in your domain name's DNS zone will change automatically. Check that the IP address has been updated using our guide on [editing an OVHcloud DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit).

Otherwise, you will need to make the change manually with the provider managing the active DNS zone of your domain name. You can find [here](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/clusters-and-shared-hosting-ip) the documentation listing all the IP addresses of our OVHcloud shared hosting infrastructure.

In both cases, a propagation delay of **4 to 24 hours** after the change will be required for it to be fully effective and visible on the internet.

:::
