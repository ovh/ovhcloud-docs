## Objective

Search engines (Google, Bing, Yahoo, etc.) use robots in order to index and rank websites. They prioritise geolocated sites in the country you are searching from.

**Example**: If you search through a search engine and are located in England, geolocated websites in England will show up higher in search results than other websites.

This geolocation is based on the IP address of the hosting plan where your website is located.

The geolocation option on your hosting can be useful for SEO if your website is mainly visited in a country different from where your shared hosting is located.

**This guide explains how to geolocate your website using our geolocated IP addresses.**

:::info
This procedure applies to <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/">the OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>, currently available in beta. To follow it, switch to this interface from your usual Control Panel.

:::

## Requirements

- An [OVHcloud Web Hosting plan](/links/web/hosting)
- One or more [domain names](/links/web/domains)

[[cpnav:web-hosting]]

## Instructions

For websites hosted on the OVHcloud shared hosting infrastructure which are mainly accessed by international visitors, we offer a geolocation by IP address option. It allows for a better search ranking in the country where the IP address you have selected is located.

To use the IP geolocation option, click on the tabs below to view each of the **5** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web Hosting</ManagerLink> page, then choose the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting page and list of the account's hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    On the page that opens, click on the <code className="action">My sites</code> tab.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="My sites tab of a web hosting plan" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/my-sites.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    In the table that appears, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, to the right of the website concerned. In the **Domain names** part of the menu, click <code className="action">Manage my domains</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Actions menu of a website in the Console" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/my-websites/tab.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 4">
    The <code className="action">Manage my domains</code> panel opens and lists the domain names and subdomains associated with this site. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the domain name or subdomain concerned, then <code className="action">Manage my options</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Manage my domains panel and its associated domain names" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/my-websites/associated-domains-options.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 5">
    In the <code className="action">Manage options</code> panel, select <code className="action">Country IP</code> to display the <code className="action">Choose a country</code> drop-down menu.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Country IP option and drop-down menu of available countries" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/my-websites/country-ip-selection.png" loading="lazy" />

    Choose the IP address of the country for which you want to geolocate your website, from the 13 countries offered: *Belgium, Czech Republic, Finland, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Lithuania, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Spain, United Kingdom*.

    Finally, click <code className="action">Confirm</code>.

    :::warning
    The <code className="action">Country IP</code> option and the <code className="action">Enable CDN</code> option belong to the same **Performance and delivery** choice: they are mutually exclusive. Selecting <code className="action">Country IP</code> disables the CDN on this domain name.
    :::
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

:::info
Once you have carried out the steps above, and if your domain name's active DNS zone is fully managed in your <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud account</ManagerLink>, the A record in your domain name's DNS zone will be automatically changed. You can check that the IP address has been updated using our guide on [Editing an OVHcloud DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit).

Otherwise, you will need to make the change manually with the provider that manages your domain name's active DNS zone. Find [here](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/clusters-and-shared-hosting-ip) the documentation that lists all the IP addresses in our OVHcloud shared hosting infrastructure.

In both cases, you will need to wait between **4 and 24 hours** for the changes to propagate fully, and be visible on the internet.

:::
