## Objective

Want to create a website for your business or a personal blog? Need an e-commerce store to sell your products online? This guide to getting started with an OVHcloud web hosting plan will show you the key steps for setting it up. You will also find instructions for creating professional email addresses associated with your domain name. It will enable you to put your project online quickly and easily, so that you can communicate effectively with your audience.

:::info
- Want to migrate an existing website from another hosting provider? Read directly our dedicated guide: [Migrating your website and associated services to OVHcloud](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/hosting-migrating-to-ovh).
<Region zones={['eu']}>
- You only want a simple homepage for your professional activity? Read our dedicated guide: [Web Hosting - Activate free hosting](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/activate-start10m).
</Region>

:::

## Requirements

<Region zones={['eu']}>
- A [OVHcloud Web Hosting](/links/web/hosting) plan with at least one database available (apart from the free hosting offer).
</Region>
<Region zones={['ca','apac']}>
- A [OVHcloud Web Hosting](/links/web/hosting) plan with at least one database available.
</Region>
- An email confirming that your web hosting plan has been set up.
- A [domain name](/links/web/domains) and an associated DNS zone at OVHcloud.
- Access to all these services (Web Hosting, Domain name, DNS zone) from a single OVHcloud account.

## Instructions

### 1 - Link your domain name to a website on your web hosting plan <a name="part-1"></a>

:::tip
If you have subscribed to your domain name and web hosting plan in the same order, these two services are already linked. Skip to [Part 2](#part-2) of this guide.

:::

Click the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web Hosting</ManagerLink> page, then choose the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting page and list of the account's hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    Click the <code className="action">My sites</code> tab. In the table, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, to the right of the website created automatically when your web hosting plan was created, then, in the **Domain names** group, <code className="action">Add a domain name</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Actions menu of a website and Add a domain name entry" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/my-websites/tab.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    The **Add a domain** page opens. Fill it in from top to bottom, then confirm.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### 2 - Install a 1-click module for your website <a name="part-2"></a>

On our web hosting plans, OVHcloud offers free WordPress, Joomla!, PrestaShop and Drupal CMS installations with the 1-click module option.

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **4** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web Hosting</ManagerLink> page, then choose the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting page and list of the account's hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    Click the <code className="action">Modules</code> tab. The three sections at the top show the number of **Installed** modules, your web hosting plan's PHP version with its compatibility (**PHP** section), and the number of **available** modules.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Modules tab and its three summary sections" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/modules/tab.png" loading="lazy" />

    :::info
    A module can only be installed if your web hosting plan's PHP version is compatible. When it is not, the section displays **Incompatible** and no module is offered. See the "[PHP versions](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/configure-your-web-hosting#php-versions)" section of our guide on configuring your web hosting plan to upgrade it.
    :::
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    Click the <code className="action">Add a module</code> button.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 4">
    A panel opens on the right. Leave the <code className="action">Simple</code> mode selected, choose the CMS to install under **Choose the module**, then the domain name under **Domain** — **without the "www"** in front (example: `domain.tld`, not `www.domain.tld`). Finally, click <code className="action">Install</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Install a module panel, choice of the CMS and the domain" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/modules/install-module-drawer.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### 3 - Activate the email addresses included with your web hosting plan <a name="part-3"></a>

:::tip
If you have subscribed to your domain name and web hosting plan in the same order, the email addresses included with the web hosting plan are already associated with your domain name. Skip to [Part 4](#part-4) of this guide.

:::

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **3** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web Hosting</ManagerLink> page, then choose the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting page and list of the account's hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    In the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, locate the **Complimentary services** section. The **Email addresses** entry shows the number of addresses included in your plan and the status of the service. As long as it is **Inactive**, click <code className="action">Activate my email plan</code> just below.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Complimentary services section and Activate my email plan link" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/general-information/complimentary-services.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    The activation process has two steps. Under **Email addresses**, select the domain name concerned. Under **Activation**, accept the contracts then confirm: your OVHcloud order form opens, as this option is included free of charge in your web hosting plan.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<details>

<summary>Click here for more information.</summary>

Read our detailed guide "[Web Hosting - Activating email addresses included](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/activate-email-hosting)".

</details>

### 4 - Create a custom email address with your domain name <a name="part-4"></a>

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **4** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/email">Emails</ManagerLink> page. The table lists all your email services; locate the row of your MX Plan offer, then click it. The **Offer type** column shows the name of the offer as returned by the system, which does not always mention `MX Plan`.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Emails page and Offer type column of the email services" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/emails-listing.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    Click the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab. The **Email accounts quota** section shows the number of addresses already configured out of the total included in your plan.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Email account tab of an MX Plan service and its accounts" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-account-tab.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    Click the <code className="action">Add account</code> button.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 4">
    A panel opens on the right. Enter the **Account name**, which precedes the `@` of your domain name, then its **Password**. The **Description** is optional and the **Account size** is the one included in your plan. Finally, click <code className="action">Add</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Add account panel and its creation fields" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/add-account-drawer.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Repeat this step for each email address you would like to create (within the limits of your web hosting plan).

<details>

<summary>Click here for more information.</summary>

Read our detailed guide "[Creating an email address](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-creation)".

</details>

### 5 - Additional options available with your web hosting plan <a name="part-5"></a>

Your web hosting plan is not limited to installing a 1-click module. You can host websites created by yourself or a web developer (blog, CMS, online store, etc.). If you would like to find out more about the possibilities of your web hosting plan, please refer to our more detailed guide "[How to create a website - Carrying out your project in 5 steps](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/website-project)".
