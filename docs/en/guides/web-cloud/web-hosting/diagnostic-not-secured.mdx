## Objective <a name="objective"></a>

Several error messages may appear if your website is inaccessible. The examples below indicate that your Web Hosting plan does not contain any [SSL certificate](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ssl-on-webhosting) (if your website does not display one of the anomalies described in this guide, please refer to the "[Go further](#go-further)" section):

|Firefox:<br/>"Warning: Potential Security Risk Ahead"|<img className="thumbnail" alt="notsecured_firefox" src="/images/assets/screens/other/browsers/errors/notsecured-firefox.png" loading="lazy" />|
|Edge:<br/>"Your connection isn't private"|<img className="thumbnail" alt="notsecured_edge" src="/images/assets/screens/other/browsers/errors/notsecured-edge.png" loading="lazy" />|

## Requirements

- Administrative rights to manage your domain name's [DNS servers](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-server-general-information) and [DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-general-information)

## Instructions

To solve this issue, you will need to:

1. Determine the hosting plan to which your domain name is linked, in order to intervene on the correct server.
2. Create, activate or renew an [SSL certificate](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ssl-on-webhosting) for your domain name on the concerned hosting plan.

### 1 - Check the hosting plan attached to your domain name

#### Check the hosting IP address

The error messages mentioned [above](#objective) do not necessarily mean that your website is hosted on one of our [Web Cloud offers](/links/web/hosting). For this reason, you will need to check the IP address of the server your [domain name](/links/web/domains) is attached to.

To find the IP address of your [OVHcloud hosting plan](/links/web/hosting), click on the tabs below to view each of the **2** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web Hosting</ManagerLink> page, then choose the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting page and list of the account's hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    The hosting plan opens on the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab. In the **General information** section, you will see the **IPv4** and **IPv6** entries.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="General information section, IPv4 and IPv6 addresses" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/general-information/ipv4-ipv6.png" loading="lazy" />

    Note the IPv4 and/or IPv6 address, using the copy button to the right of each address, then continue reading the guide.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

#### Check the IP address in the DNS zone

You now need to check that the IP address listed in the [DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit) corresponds to the one of your [OVHcloud Web Hosting plan](/links/web/hosting).

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **2** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">Domains & DNS</ManagerLink> page, select the domain name concerned, then open the <code className="action">DNS Zone</code> tab.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Domains & DNS page and list of the account's domain names" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/listing.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    The table displays one DNS record per row, with the **Subdomain**, **Type**, **Target** and **TTL (in seconds)** columns. To display only the `A` entries, click <code className="action">Filters</code>, then select <code className="action">A</code> in the **Record type** list.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Filters button and Record type list of the DNS zone" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/dns-zone/filter-type.png" loading="lazy" />

    Note the **Target** of the `A` record for your domain name.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

#### Perform the necessary actions

**Click on the scenario that matches your situation to view the content.**

<details>
<summary>The IP address corresponds to that of your shared hosting plan</summary>

The IP address listed in the [DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit) corresponds to your Web Hosting plan's IP address. Proceed to [part 2](#step2).

</details>

<details>
<summary>The IP address does not concern any of the hosting plans within your account but appears in the Web Cloud server list</summary>

The IP address listed in the zone does not concern any of the Web Hosting plans within your <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud account</ManagerLink>, but appears in the [list of our Web Cloud servers](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/clusters-and-shared-hosting-ip).

Check that you do not have a hosting plan with this IP address within one of your other <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud customer accounts</ManagerLink>, if you have several of them. If necessary, contact your webmaster or the [OVHcloud partners](/links/partner) for further information.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The IP address is not your hosting plan's one, nor does it appear in the Web Cloud server list</summary>

The IP address entered in the zone is not your hosting plan's one, nor does it appear on the [list of our Web Cloud servers](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/clusters-and-shared-hosting-ip).

Contact your webmaster or the [OVHcloud partners](/links/partner) for further information.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Your domain uses other OVHcloud DNS servers (ns?.ovh.net / dns?.ovh.net)</summary>

Above the DNS zone in your OVHcloud Console, a warning states "**This DNS zone is not authoritative for your domain name.**": your domain uses other [DNS](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit) servers, and the changes made in this zone are not taken into account.

To find out which DNS servers your domain uses, open the domain name's <code className="action">Overview</code> tab and look at the **DNS configuration** section: the **DNS servers hosting the zone** list shows them. If they appear as "ns **?** .ovh.net" or "dns **?** .ovh.net" (the "**?**" being the relevant DNS server number), these are other OVHcloud DNS servers.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="DNS configuration card with the configuration and the list of DNS servers hosting the zone" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/domain-dns/general-information/dns-servers-ovhcloud.png" loading="lazy" />

You must modify your domain's DNS servers to match the `NS` records of the DNS zone. To perform this operation, follow the instructions of [this guide](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-server-edit).

{/* v8-review: TO DOCUMENT — the « This DNS zone is not authoritative for your domain name. » banner was not seen live (no domain on the test account uses servers other than its zone's); its wording comes from ZoneBanners.tsx and zone.json. Unlike v6, it does not list the DNS servers in use, hence the pointer to the Overview « DNS servers » section. */}

</details>

<details>
<summary>Your domain uses external (non-OVHcloud) DNS servers</summary>

Above the DNS zone in your OVHcloud Console, the same "**This DNS zone is not authoritative for your domain name.**" warning is displayed. In the **DNS configuration** section of the domain name's <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, the **Configuration** line shows **Custom** and the DNS servers listed do not appear as "ns **?** .ovh.net" or "dns **?** .ovh.net".

Contact your webmaster or the [OVHcloud partners](/links/partner) for further information.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Your domain name does not appear in your OVHcloud Console</summary>

Your domain name does not appear on the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/domains/listing">Domains & DNS</ManagerLink> page of your OVHcloud Console.

This means that your domain name is not managed from your OVHcloud Console.

Check if it is managed from one of your other <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud customer accounts</ManagerLink>, if you have created more than one of them.

You can also check the registrar of your domain name and its actual DNS servers with our [WHOIS tool](/links/web/domains-whois).

If necessary, contact your webmaster or the [OVHcloud partners](/links/partner) about this.

</details>

### 2 - Check your Web Hosting plan's SSL certificate <a name="step2"></a>

Click on the tabs below to view each of the **2** steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/hosting">Web Hosting</ManagerLink> page, then choose the web hosting plan concerned.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Web Hosting page and list of the account's hosting plans" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/hosting-plans.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    Click on the <code className="action">SSL</code> tab.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="SSL tab of a web hosting plan" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/ssl-certificates.png" loading="lazy" />

    Find your domain name in the **Main domain** column, then check its **Status** column. If your domain name does not appear in the table, your hosting plan does not contain an SSL certificate for this domain.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="SSL certificates table and its Status column" src="/images/assets/screens/console/product-selection/web-cloud/web-hosting/ssl-certificates/tab.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

#### Scenario 1: Your Web Hosting plan does not contain any SSL certificate

Activate an [SSL certificate](/links/web/hosting-options-ssl) on your Web Hosting plan by following the instructions in this [guide](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ssl-on-webhosting).

#### Scenario 2: The SSL certificate on your Web Hosting plan does not work

If you have generated a **Let's Encrypt SSL certificate**, enable the SSL option on your hosting by following the instructions in [this guide](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/ssl-on-webhosting).

If you have **imported an SSL certificate** from another provider and it does not work, contact the appropriate support team.

If you have **ordered an SSL certificate** from our partner [SECTIGO](https://sectigo.com/), check if you have received an email offering to renew it.
<br/>If necessary, contact the [SECTIGO support team](https://sectigo.com/support) for more information.

:::info
To check all the emails sent by our services, go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/customer/communications">Communications</ManagerLink> page.
:::
