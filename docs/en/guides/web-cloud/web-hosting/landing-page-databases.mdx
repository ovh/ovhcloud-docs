## Managing your Web Hosting plan's databases

Create, manage and back up the databases of your OVHcloud Web Hosting plan. This page brings together the guides to manage your databases, back up, import and restore their data, and resolve the most common errors.

To find the right guide for your needs:

- **Need to create or configure a database?** See the *Manage a database* section (create, password, duplicate).
- **Need to back up or restore your data?** See the *Backup and restoration* section (retrieve, import, restore).
- **Running into a database error?** See the *Troubleshooting* section (common errors, full database).
