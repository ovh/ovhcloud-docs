## Objective

**Managed Hosting for WordPress** is a new OVHcloud offer that allows you to host and manage your WordPress websites with ease. Designed for professionals, agencies, and freelancers, this service frees you from technical tasks related to infrastructure management and updates, so you can focus on your content and business. This guide presents the product concept, its main benefits, and the available features.

## Requirements

- An active [Managed Hosting for WordPress](/links/web/managed-hosting-wordpress) offer.

## Instructions

### Product overview

#### 100% WordPress management by OVHcloud

With Managed Hosting for WordPress, **OVHcloud takes care of everything**:

- Hosting, infrastructure, cache, and security.
- Installation and maintenance of WordPress.
- Automatic backups and updates.
- Performance and availability monitoring.
- Simplified management of domains, plugins, and staging environments.

No server knowledge is required: everything is **accessible from the OVHcloud Console**. You can thus **create, import, and manage your WordPress sites in just a few clicks**, without ever having to use FTP, a database, or the command line.

#### A complete solution

Each website benefits from an optimized, ready-to-use environment:

- **Automatic WordPress installation** as soon as you place your order.
- **Managed infrastructure**: maintenance, patches, software updates, and security handled by OVHcloud.
- **Guaranteed performance**: integrated WordPress cache, optimised infrastructure hosting and continuous monitoring.
- **Built-in scalability**: monitor usage in real time across key metrics (storage, traffic, site count), and scale effortlessly as your needs evolve.
- **Full compatibility**: access via the OVHcloud Console, allowing integration into your existing workflows.

### Features

#### Create a website

To create a website, click the <code className="action">Create a site</code> button above the table in the <code className="action">My sites</code> tab. The **Create a site** panel opens on the right of the screen:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Create a site panel open to the right of the My sites tab" src="/images/web-cloud/managed-hosting/01-managed-wordpress-getting-started/console/managed_wordpress_create_site.png" loading="lazy" />

Fill in the form fields to define the credentials for your WordPress website:

- **Email**: enter the WordPress site administrator's email address. This field is pre-filled with the email address of your OVHcloud account.
- **Password**: set the password associated with this administrator account. It must contain at least 8 characters, including uppercase letters, numbers and special characters.
- **Confirm password**: enter this password again.
- **PHP version**: choose the desired PHP version for your WordPress environment. Check the available PHP versions on [this page](/guides/web-cloud/web-hosting/web-hosting-main-info).
- **Language**: select the default language for the administration interface.

The **Email**, **Password** and **Confirm password** fields are mandatory: the <code className="action">Create site</code> button remains inactive until they are filled in correctly. Then click <code className="action">Create site</code> to validate the information.

You stay on the <code className="action">My sites</code> tab and a message confirms the creation of your website.

In the table, a new line appears for your new website:

- **Site name** column: displays the main domain name of your website or, as long as no domain name is associated with it, its technical URL. This technical URL serves as a staging (pre-production) URL: it allows you to access your website before associating it with your own domain name.
- **Status** column: indicates the progress of the website creation. The status first appears as **Creating...**, while the infrastructure is deployed and WordPress is installed. Once your website is operational, the status changes to **Active**, indicating that your WordPress site is now available online via its technical URL.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="My sites tab displaying the table of WordPress sites and their status" src="/images/guides/web-cloud/managed-hosting/wordpress-domain-link/console/managed-hosting-for-wordpress-my-sites-tab.png" loading="lazy" />

You can also track the progress of your website creation by clicking on the <code className="action">Operations</code> tab. The table in this tab shows the status and progress of each ongoing or completed operation.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Operations tab displaying the table of operations of a Managed WordPress resource" src="/images/web-cloud/managed-hosting/01-managed-wordpress-getting-started/console/managed_wordpress_task_new_line.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Import a website

You can import an existing WordPress website into Managed Hosting for WordPress. The import can automatically transfer:

- WordPress content (plugins, themes, media, articles, pages, users).
- Files.
- Database.

To import a WordPress website, click the <code className="action">Import a site</code> button above the table in the <code className="action">My sites</code> tab.

A window titled **Disable your anti-bot protection** appears first. To avoid any connection failure, it asks you to temporarily disable the anti-bot protections of your source website: security plugins, captchas, network firewalls or locks such as Cloudflare. You can re-enable them once the import is complete. After disabling these protections, click <code className="action">Validate</code>. The **Import a site** panel opens on the right of the screen:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Import a site panel open to the right of the My sites tab" src="/images/web-cloud/managed-hosting/01-managed-wordpress-getting-started/console/managed_wordpress_import_site.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
An information message at the top of the panel tells you that the operation may take up to 48 hours depending on the complexity of your website. If necessary, our technical teams will step in to complete the import.

:::

Fill in the form fields, all of which are mandatory:

- **Admin URL**: enter the address used to log in to the administration interface of your current WordPress website, for example `https://www.domain.tld/wp-admin`.
- **Administrator login**: enter the username or email address of a WordPress administrator account on your current website.
- **Administrator password**: enter the password of this WordPress administrator account.

These details allow the system to access your existing website to automatically extract the files, database, and necessary configuration.

The <code className="action">Continue</code> button remains inactive until the mandatory fields are filled in. Once they are filled in, click <code className="action">Continue</code> to start the import process.

You can then select the items to import, including plugins, themes, media, and databases.

:::warning
To ensure perfect data consistency, make sure not to make any changes to your site until the import is complete.

Please take the necessary steps to avoid any data discrepancies during the import.

:::

Once all items are selected, click on <code className="action">Start import</code>.

#### Delete a website

:::danger
Deleting a website is permanent, and will delete all of the website's content.
Please ensure you back up your data before deleting anything.

:::

##### Delete a single website

To delete a website, go to the <code className="action">My sites</code> tab and identify the row in the table corresponding to your website. In the **Actions** column, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of this site, then <code className="action">Delete website</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Menu of the ⋮ button of a site, with the Delete website entry in last position" src="/images/guides/web-cloud/managed-hosting/wordpress-domain-link/console/adding-domain.png" loading="lazy" />

A window titled **Delete site** appears. It reminds you that this action is irreversible: all the site's data will be permanently deleted. Click <code className="action">Delete</code> to confirm the deletion of your website.

You stay on the <code className="action">My sites</code> tab and a message confirms the deletion of your website.

When your website is permanently deleted, its corresponding row in the table disappears.

##### Delete multiple websites at once

To delete several websites at the same time, go to the <code className="action">My sites</code> tab. Identify the rows in the table corresponding to the websites you want to delete and tick the boxes on the left. The <code className="action">Actions</code> button above the table then shows the number of selected sites. Click this button, then <code className="action">Delete</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="My sites tab with two sites ticked and the Actions menu open on the Delete entry" src="/images/web-cloud/managed-hosting/01-managed-wordpress-getting-started/console/managed_wordpress_delete_several_sites.png" loading="lazy" />

A window titled **Delete X sites** appears, X being the number of selected sites. It reminds you that this action is irreversible and that these sites will be permanently deleted. Click <code className="action">Delete X sites</code> to confirm the deletion of your websites.

You stay on the <code className="action">My sites</code> tab and a message confirms the deletion of your websites. If the deletion of one or more sites fails, this message shows the number of sites deleted and the number of failures.

When your websites are permanently deleted, their corresponding rows in the table disappear.

#### Manage your websites

To manage all your websites, go to the <code className="action">My sites</code> tab, click the <code className="action">Administer</code> button above the table, then <code className="action">Manage my sites</code>. This entry opens, in a new tab of your browser, the fleet manager integrated into Managed Hosting for WordPress. This space allows you to manage multiple WordPress websites simultaneously, from a unified interface.

The <code className="action">Manage my sites</code> entry also appears at the top of the <code className="action">Actions</code> button menu.

##### Dashboard

The fleet manager is a centralized monitoring and management tool automatically provided with your offer. It allows you to manage your managed WordPress websites without having to log in to each website individually.

From this dashboard, you can:

- View the overall status of all your websites: available updates, alerts, performance, security.
- Manage WordPress updates (plugins, themes, etc.) centrally.
- Quickly access each website to check its status or take action.
- Monitor recent activities (changes, updates, synchronizations).
- Use the integrated security, monitoring, backup, and analysis features (in future phases).

#### Smart Automatic Updates

Automatic updates can introduce compatibility issues that affect site stability, performance, and user experience. When managing multiple WordPress sites, manually handling updates increases operational overhead and the risk of service disruption for end users.

Managed WordPress Hosting mitigates these risks through a controlled update process:

- **Pre-deployment testing**
    All updates—including WordPress core, plugins, and themes—are first applied to a cloned environment of your site.
- **Validation checks**
    The cloned site is evaluated for performance, functionality, and user experience to detect regressions or errors before deployment.
- **Automated decision process**
    - If no issues are detected, the update is automatically deployed to the live site.
    - If issues are identified, the live site remains unchanged. You are notified via the dashboard and receive an email outlining the detected problems along with recommended remediation steps.

This approach ensures updates are applied safely while preserving site reliability and minimizing impact on end users.
