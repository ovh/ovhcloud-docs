## Objective

The [OVHcloud Video Center](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/video-center/) is a video hosting and streaming solution hosted in Europe, with no advertising or third-party tracking. It allows you to import your videos, embed them on any web page via an iframe code, and manage them from a dedicated interface.

**This guide explains how to access the Video Center from your OVHcloud Control Panel and manage your videos: importing, embedding via iframe, sharing, downloading and deleting.**

## Requirements

- An active [OVHcloud Video Center](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/video-center/).

## Instructions

### Accessing the Video Center from the Control Panel

Go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/video-center">Video Center</ManagerLink> page in your OVHcloud Control Panel.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Video Center page listing the videos hosted on the account" src="/images/guides/web-cloud/video-center/manage-videos/video-center-selection-plan.png" loading="lazy" />

Select your offer from the list.

The page displays your Video Center subscription:

- **Your current solution**: name of your subscribed plan.
- **Number of videos**: number of videos already hosted against your total quota.
- **Number of minutes**: cumulative duration of your videos relative to the plan quota.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Video Center page in the OVHcloud Control Panel showing video and minute counters" src="/images/guides/web-cloud/video-center/manage-videos/access-video-center.png" loading="lazy" />

To access the video management interface, click <code className="action">Access Video Center</code>. The interface opens in a new tab.

### Importing a video

From the Video Center interface, the import area is located at the top of the page, below the navigation bar.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Main Video Center interface showing the import area and video list" src="/images/guides/web-cloud/video-center/manage-videos/en-vc-dashboard.png" loading="lazy" />

2 methods are available:

- **Drag and drop**: drag a video file from your file explorer directly into the dotted area.
- **Browse files**: click <code className="action">Browse files</code>, navigate to the file to import from your local machine, then select it.

:::info
The maximum size per video is **500 MB**. Common video formats (MP4, MOV, AVI, etc.) are accepted.
:::


Once the file is selected, the import starts automatically. The video appears in the **My videos** section once processing is complete.

### Embedding a video in a web page

The Video Center allows you to embed videos on web pages via an iframe code.

In the **My videos** section, locate the video to embed and click <code className="action">Integrate</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Iframe integration window showing the code to copy" src="/images/guides/web-cloud/video-center/manage-videos/en-vc-integrate-iframe.png" loading="lazy" />

The **Embed video** window displays the **iframe embed code**. Click the copy icon to copy it to your clipboard, then paste it into the HTML source code of your web page at the desired location.

Click <code className="action">Close</code> to close the window.

To discover the different embedding methods (HTML, CMS editor, WordPress) and customise the video player, see the [Embed a video on your website](/guides/web-cloud/video-center/website-integration) guide.

### Sharing a video

The Video Center allows you to share a direct link to the video player.

In the **My videos** section, click <code className="action">Share</code> next to the relevant video.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Sharing window showing the video sharing URL" src="/images/guides/web-cloud/video-center/manage-videos/en-vc-share.png" loading="lazy" />

The **Share video** window displays the **sharing URL**. Click the copy icon to copy it, then send it to your recipients.

Click <code className="action">Close</code> to close the window.

### Downloading or deleting a video

To access additional actions on a video, click the <code className="action">...</code> button to the right of the relevant video.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Action menu showing the Edit, Download and Delete options" src="/images/guides/web-cloud/video-center/manage-videos/en-vc-actions-menu.png" loading="lazy" />

The menu offers 3 options:

| Action | Description |
|---|---|
| <code className="action">Edit</code> | Edits the video information (title, tags). |
| <code className="action">Download</code> | Downloads the original video file to your local machine. |
| <code className="action">Delete</code> | Permanently deletes the video from your Video Center. |

:::warning
Deleting a video is irreversible. Any iframe code or sharing link associated with this video will immediately stop working.
:::
