## Objective

With OVHcloud email solutions, you can send and receive your emails from a device and client of your choice. OVHcloud provides an online email service called Outlook Web App (OWA) that lets you access an account from anywhere via a web browser.

## Instructions

This guide will help you better understand the usual tasks available in an email account using OWA. However, since this interface was not originally created by OVHcloud, we are unable to provide specific instructions for any settings not covered in this guide.

For Exchange-specific features, you can find some additional guides in the [Go further](#go-further) section at the bottom of this guide.

:::info
After logging in and getting familiar with the interface, you do not need to follow the instructions in the order given.
:::


### Logging in to OWA

To log in to OWA with your email address, open the [webmail login page](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/mail/). Enter your full email address and password. Then click <code className="action">Sign in</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="useowa" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/email-owa/use-owa-step1.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
After entering your email address and password on the webmail login page, you may be redirected to the **OWA** login window, where you will need to enter these same credentials again.
:::

<img className="thumbnail" alt="OWA login window" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/email-owa/owa-login-window.png" loading="lazy" />

:::warning
If you are redirected to a **Roundcube** interface, this means you are using the legacy version of the MX Plan offer. For more information about your MX Plan offer, see our [Getting started with the MX Plan offer](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-generalities) page.

To familiarise yourself with the **Roundcube** interface, see our guide [Using your email address from the Roundcube webmail](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-roundcube).
:::


If this is your first time logging in to OWA with this email address, you will be prompted to set the interface language and time zone. Then click <code className="action">Save</code> to continue.

:::info
Time zones are listed according to [the UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_Universal_Time#/media/File:World_Time_Zones_Map.png), not in alphabetical order of cities.

### Understanding the OWA display

The OWA interface contains several sections. Refer to the table and image below to familiarise yourself with it.

|Parts|Description|  
|---|---|  
|Top section (1)|Contains two tab bars: the first one provides access to general settings (such as the [Options section](#accessing-the-options-section)). The second bar can be used for specific actions with your address (such as sending or replying to emails).|  
|Left-hand side (2)|Displays the list of folders for your email address. These folders appear as a tree-view that you can expand or collapse.|
|Central segment (3)|Displays the list of messages (read and unread) from the folder selected in the left-hand menu. This section can also display search results.|
|Right-hand side (4)|Displays the reading pane when an email has been selected.|

<img className="thumbnail" alt="useowa" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/email-owa/use-owa-step4.png" loading="lazy" />

Note that you can change the size of the vertical sections by clicking and dragging their border lines.

### Viewing emails

To view your emails, select a folder on the left-hand side. Incoming emails that are not processed by inbox rules will appear in the "Inbox" folder. To check whether you have received any new emails, see if a number appears next to the corresponding folder.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="useowa" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/email-owa/use-owa-step5.png" loading="lazy" />

To read an email, select its folder if necessary. Then click on the email to display its content in the reading pane. Unread messages appear in bold to distinguish them from read messages.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="useowa" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/email-owa/use-owa-step6.png" loading="lazy" />

### Sorting and filtering emails

At the top right of the message list, the <code className="action">Filter</code> button opens a menu that gathers all display options for the selected folder.

- **Filter by category**: select an entry to display only a selection of emails among <code className="action">All</code>, <code className="action">Unread</code>, <code className="action">To me</code> (emails addressed directly to your address), <code className="action">Flagged</code> (emails marked for follow-up) or <code className="action">Mentions</code> (emails in which your address is mentioned).

- **Sort by**: hover over the <code className="action">Sort by</code> entry to choose the sort criterion for emails: **Date**, **From**, **To**, **Subject**, **Attachments**, **Importance** or **Size**. The arrow to the left of the criterion indicates the current order; click the same criterion again to reverse it.

- **Show as**: hover over the <code className="action">Show as</code> entry to switch between the **Messages** view (one email per line) and the **Conversations** view (emails grouped by discussion thread).

### Sending and replying

To **send a new message**, click the <code className="action">New</code> icon at the top of the OWA interface. The editing pane will appear on the right-hand side. Fill in the fields of your email (recipients, subject, message body, attachments). Click <code className="action">Send</code> once your email is ready.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="useowa" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/email-owa/use-owa-step7.png" loading="lazy" />

To **reply to a message**, first click on it to display it. Then click <code className="action">Reply all</code> to reply to all recipients. Use the down-arrow button if you only want to reply to the sender of the email (excluding any recipient in copy), then click <code className="action">Reply</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="useowa" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/email-owa/use-owa-step8.png" loading="lazy" />

When you choose to reply, the quick-reply editor will appear above the email. Type your reply there, and once you are ready to send your message, click <code className="action">Send</code>. Note that for each reply option (such as adding a signature), you must first expand it to the full editing pane by clicking the double-arrow symbol.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="useowa" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/email-owa/use-owa-step9.png" loading="lazy" />

### Organising your inbox

OWA offers several ways to organise your inbox. You can:

- [create folders and subfolders](#creating-a-folder),
- [move emails](#moving-emails),
- [set rules](#creating-inbox-rules) to automatically perform actions when a new email is received,
- [block a sender](#blocking-a-sender) to stop receiving their messages.

#### Creating a folder

To create a new folder, right-click the name of your email address in the folder tree, then choose <code className="action">Create new folder</code>. You can create a subfolder in existing folders in the same way by clicking <code className="action">Create new subfolder</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="useowa" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/email-owa/use-owa-step10.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Moving emails

To **move an email**, you can simply drag-and-drop it into the target folder, or right-click it and select <code className="action">Move</code>.
To **move multiple emails** at once, select them all using their tick boxes. Then click <code className="action">Move</code> (on the right-hand side) or <code className="action">Move to</code> (in the top section). Then choose the destination folder.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="useowa" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/email-owa/use-owa-step11.png" loading="lazy" />

### Managing your contacts

To manage your contacts, first click the blue app launcher button at the top left of the page (which also gives access to calendar, tasks and other modules), then click <code className="action">People</code>.

On the new page, you can add a new contact, create a contact list and remove existing contacts.

#### Adding a contact

Click <code className="action">New</code>, then enter the details of the contact you want to add. Once done, click <code className="action">Save</code>.

Click the down arrow next to <code className="action">New</code>, then click <code className="action">Contact list</code>. Give it a name, add contacts to it, then click <code className="action">Save</code>.

### Adding an automatic reply

In OWA, you can create an automatic reply on your inbox so that emails are not left unanswered while you are away. To do so, click the gear icon at the top, then click <code className="action">Automatic replies</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="useowa" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/email-owa/use-owa-step20.png" loading="lazy" />

In the window that opens, select the "Send automatic replies" option. You can then configure the auto-responder to match several criteria, such as:

- send automatic reply emails for a fixed time interval, or continuously until manually disabled
- define which senders will receive automatic reply emails (internal senders only, or include external senders)

Fill in the requested information depending on the task you want to perform with this rule. Once done, click <code className="action">OK</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="useowa" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/email-owa/use-owa-step21.png" loading="lazy" />

For more detailed instructions on creating automatic replies, please refer to our guide: [Creating an automatic reply in OWA](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/owa-automatic-replies).

### Adding a signature

To add an email signature, click the gear icon at the top, then click <code className="action">Options</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="useowa" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/email-owa/use-owa-step12.png" loading="lazy" />

On the left-hand side of the new page, click <code className="action">Email signature</code>. In the tree options, this item is located under "Mail" and "Layout". From here, you can enable, disable and edit the signature.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="useowa" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/email-owa/use-owa-step22.png" loading="lazy" />

Compose your email signature in the editor box. You can specify whether you want to include the default signature in new emails only, or also in replies and forwarded emails. Once you are done, click <code className="action">Save</code> to confirm.

For instructions on creating automatic signatures using domain-wide templates, please refer to our guide: [Creating automatic signatures](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-footers).

To access all your settings, click the gear icon at the top, then click <code className="action">Options</code>.

### Cookie management

The webmail used for our email offers is based on Microsoft Outlook Web App software. It is therefore likely to exchange metadata with Microsoft servers, in the form of cookies named `appsforoffice.microsoft.com`.

If you want to disable these exchanges, you can use a content blocking extension on your browser (such as uBlock Origin or Ghostery).
However, disabling these cookies may affect the stability of your webmail.
