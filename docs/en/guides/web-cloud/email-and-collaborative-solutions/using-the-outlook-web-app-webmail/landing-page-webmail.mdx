## What is a webmail?

A **webmail** is an interface that lets you read, send and organise your emails directly from a web browser, without installing or configuring any email software. Your messages stay hosted on OVHcloud servers: you can access them from any computer connected to the Internet, simply by logging in.

The simplest entry point is the OVHcloud **webmail login page**: enter your full email address and password to open your mailbox.
