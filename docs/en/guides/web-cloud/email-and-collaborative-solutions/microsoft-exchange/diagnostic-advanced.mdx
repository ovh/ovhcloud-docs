## Objective

Since there is a multitude of reasons why errors might occur on Exchange email accounts, an automatic check of the account's functionalities helps to narrow down possible causes. The test results are therefore useful for a support request regarding issues on your Exchange service.

**This guide explains how to launch an Exchange diagnostic check and interpret the results.**

## Requirements

- an [OVHcloud Exchange solution](/links/web/emails-exchange) already set up
- credentials for the Exchange account to be checked
- being up to date in the [payments](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/invoice-management#pay-bills) for this service and the related domain name.

## Instructions

### Running the diagnostic

Click the <code className="action">Diagnostics</code> tab and select the Exchange account concerned from the **Email account to diagnose** list. Enter the account password in the **Password** field, then click <code className="action">Run diagnostic</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Diagnostics tab, Email account to diagnose list and Password field" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/diagnostic-advanced/console/exchange-diagnostic-01.png" loading="lazy" />

The diagnostic takes between 3 and 10 minutes.

The results page offers the following actions to continue:

- <code className="action">New diagnostic</code>: lets you run another diagnostic.

- <code className="action">Create a support request</code>: lets you create a ticket with our technical support if the diagnostic has found an error.

### Error explanations

Refer to the following summary of possible errors to find the quickest resolution.

### The account has been blocked for sending spam <a name="blocked"></a>

A blocked account still receives emails but sending has been disabled by the automatic spam protection system.

{/* v8-gap: CG-029 (oversight) — v6 tab « Comptes e-mail » of the Exchange service: a mailbox blocked for spam showed the mention `SPAM` in the « statut » column of the accounts table, which let the reader check the block. The Console's « Statut » column (tab « Gestion des comptes », sub-tab « Compte email ») maps the API state only and shows « Actif » for a blocked mailbox, so the check is left out until the blocked state is restored */}

Please follow the instructions in [this guide](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/troubleshooting/locked-for-spam) to enable our security teams to re-enable the account.

### The account subscription is suspended <a name="expired"></a>

As your subscription is no longer active, sending and receiving have been disabled.

To reactivate your subscription, reconfigure its [billing frequency](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/manage-billing-exchange#periodicity) in the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>. In the **Subscription** section of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab of your Exchange service, click <code className="action">Manage my service accounts</code>. On the **Service management** page, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then <code className="action">Change renewal period</code>.

### Account locked due to the security policy

If there is a security policy activated in the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>, the account could be temporarily locked. 

You can for example decide that the account will be locked after multiple unsuccessful login attempts, for a period of time determined by you. 

In this case, you can either wait until the account becomes available again or you can contact our Exchange teams by creating a support request.

You can find more information about this topic in the [security policy guide](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/security-policy).

### Webmail authentication has failed <a name="password"></a>

This can be caused by entering an incorrect account password. First verify via a [webmail login](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/email-owa) that the password is correct, then restart the diagnostics.

If necessary, you can change the password of the account concerned in the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>. In the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab of your Exchange service, <code className="action">Email account</code> sub-tab, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account, then <code className="action">Edit</code>. On the **Edit an Exchange account** page, enter the new password in the **Password** and **Confirmation** fields, then click <code className="action">Save</code>. If the issue persists, create a support request.

### The domain MX record is not valid

This error indicates that emails cannot be received. It comes with the error "**WARNING: The test email was not received**".

### The domain's SRV record is not valid

The SRV record is essential to the automatic configuration of your Exchange account with a compatible email software such as Microsoft Outlook.

You can verify these settings in your [domain's DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit).

Here are the mandatory values for an Exchange service:

<Region zones={['eu']}>

Field        | Value
------------ | -------------
Sub-domain   | _autodiscover._tcp
Priority     | 0
Weight       | 0
Port         | 443
Target       | [Your hostname](#hostname) (ex<b>?</b>.mail.ovh.net)

</Region>

### The test email could not be sent from this account 

This error indicates a general email sending failure which may have several causes:

- [Your account has been suspended](#expired)
- [The password that was entered is incorrect](#password)
- [Your account has been blocked for sending spam](#blocked)
- [An incident has occurred on the infrastructure](https://web-cloud.status-ovhcloud.com/)
