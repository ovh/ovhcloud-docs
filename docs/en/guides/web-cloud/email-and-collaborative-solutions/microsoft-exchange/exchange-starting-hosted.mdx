## Objective

With the Hosted Exchange service, you can get professional email accounts to use for collaborative work, with features such as calendar and contact syncing.

**Find out how to get started with your Hosted Exchange service.**

## Requirements

- You need to have a subscription to a [Hosted Exchange](/links/web/emails-hosted-exchange) solution.
- You need to have received an email confirming that your Hosted Exchange solution has been set up.
- You must have a domain name.

## Instructions

### Access your service management

Once your Hosted Exchange service has been created and is available, select it in the OVHcloud Console.

If none of your Exchange services has been configured yet, the OVHcloud Console displays the **Create your first email address** page instead of the list of services: click <code className="action">Create an email address</code> (if several services need to be configured, first choose yours in the **Service to set up** field).

:::info

The name of a Hosted Exchange service in the OVHcloud Console begins with **hosted-**, contains part of your NIC handle and ends with a number (1 for the first Hosted Exchange service installed, 2 for the second, etc.).

:::

### Configure your service for the first time

Since you have not used your service yet, you will need to configure it for the first time. Once you have done so, you can then use your new Exchange email addresses.

When you open your Hosted Exchange service for the first time, the **Add a domain** page is displayed directly. On the right of the page, the **Your setup** section summarises the two items to configure: the **Domain** and the **Accounts**.

Depending on your situation, this page lets you do the following.

#### Choosing a domain name

On the **Add a domain** page, select one of the following two options:

- **Select a domain from the list**: Choose one of your domain names from the **Domain name** drop-down menu.
- **Enter a domain name not managed by your OVH account**: Enter, in the **Domain name** field, a domain name that is not managed in your OVHcloud customer account **but that you can configure**.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Add a domain page of a Hosted Exchange service, choosing the domain name" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-starting-hosted/console/exchange-wizard01.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Choosing the configuration type

Once you have specified the domain name, the **Configuration** section appears just below it. The question "**How do you want to configure your domain?**" determines the type of configuration for your domain name.

- If you use an Exchange solution alone or with other **OVHcloud email solutions**, you can configure your domain automatically with the **Recommended configuration**, or manually with the **Custom configuration**, using only OVHcloud email servers. With the **Recommended configuration**, all emails sent to your domain name are routed to this service as soon as the configuration is saved.
- If you use your Exchange solution alongside an email service **external to the OVHcloud email solutions**, select **Custom configuration**: in the **Target email server URL** field, enter the URL of your external email service's incoming server, in place of the OVHcloud server suggested.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Configuration section of the Add a domain page, choice between the recommended and custom configurations" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-starting-hosted/console/exchange-wizard02.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Configuring the DNS zone

- **Recommended configuration**: The domain name entered is automatically configured in its <Tooltip term="dnsZone">DNS zone</Tooltip> (MX, SRV, SPF and DKIM records), if it is managed in the same OVHcloud customer account as your Exchange service.
- **Custom configuration**: The domain name is not managed in the same OVHcloud customer account as your platform, it is managed by another domain name provider, or you want to configure your domain yourself. The 4 boxes for automatically configuring the MX, SRV, SPF and DKIM records are ticked by default: untick those of the records you want to configure yourself. You can then find the values to enter in the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab of your platform: click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the domain name, then <code className="action">Diagnostics</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Custom configuration with the Target email server URL field and the DNS boxes" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-starting-hosted/console/exchange-wizard03.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Creating the first accounts

Once the domain name has been saved with the <code className="action">Save the configuration</code> button, the **Create the first accounts** section appears. Your service comes with ready-to-use accounts: choose the name of your Exchange email addresses (**Email address**) and their **Password**, add a **First name** and a **Last name** if you wish, then click <code className="action">Create the first accounts</code> (<code className="action">Create the first account</code> if your service only has one account). The accounts you do not name now can be named later: to skip this step, click <code className="action">Create my accounts later</code>.

#### Special case

- If you configure your Exchange platform with a domain name that is not managed in the same OVHcloud customer account as this platform, or that is managed by another domain name provider, the **DNS configuration required** page appears after the configuration is saved.

This page prompts you to add a **<Tooltip term="cname">CNAME</Tooltip> record** to the domain name's DNS zone: the record to create is shown under **CNAME record to create**, and its value under **Target**. The purpose of this entry is to check that you actually manage this domain name. Once the CNAME record has been validated, the **Create the first accounts** section appears.

:::warning

Without this validation by CNAME record, you cannot use the platform with this domain name.

:::

- If your domain name is not managed in the same OVHcloud customer account as this platform, if it is managed by another provider, or if you have chosen the **Custom configuration**, then once the domain name has been validated, find the values to enter in your DNS zone from the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab of your service: click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the domain name, then <code className="action">Diagnostics</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Diagnostics page of a domain, MX tab with the priorities and targets" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-starting-hosted/console/exchange-wizard06.png" loading="lazy" />

The page that opens has one tab per record (`MX`, `SRV`, `SPF` and `DKIM`), with its status and the values to enter in your DNS zone. The **MX records** define where your emails are received. The **SRV record** enables the automatic configuration of your email addresses.

You can find the DNS zone configuration details for your email service on our page "[Configure an MX record for email management](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-mx)".

### Add additional domain names (optional)

Once you have finished the first configuration of your domain, you can also configure additional domain names, if you wish and if you have not already done so.

:::warning
All of the addresses created on your Exchange service will be visible in the directory of addresses for that service, including those with a different domain name. To stop different domain names from being displayed this way, you will need to order a new Hosted Exchange solution for the domain name(s) concerned.

:::

To add a new domain name, select the Hosted Exchange service concerned in the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink> and click the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab. The table shows the domain names currently configured, or about to be configured, on your service. To add new ones, click the <code className="action">Add a domain</code> button above the table, then follow the steps in the **Add a domain** panel that opens.

To find out more, please refer to our guide "[How to add a domain name to your Exchange service](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-adding-domain)".

:::info
If a domain name requires a specific action for its configuration, a red badge appears in the **Diagnostic** column of the table. To display the changes to make, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the domain name, then <code className="action">Diagnostics</code>. If this domain name does not use the OVHcloud configuration (its DNS servers), you will need to make the changes in the interface you use to manage your domain's configuration.

:::

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Associated domains tab of a Hosted Exchange service, Add a domain button above the table" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-starting-hosted/console/first-steps-hosted-exchange-add-domain.png" loading="lazy" />

### Configure additional Exchange accounts (optional)

You can configure additional accounts, if you wish and if you did not already do so during the first configuration.

To do this, click the Hosted Exchange service concerned in the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>, then the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab. The table in the **Email account** sub-tab, open by default, shows the accounts currently configured, or waiting to be configured, on your service.

Accounts waiting to be configured appear in the table with the status **Active not configured** and an address in the form "*@configureme.me*". To configure them, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account, then <code className="action">Configure</code>. Then follow the steps.

:::info

Repeat this step as many times as necessary, depending on the number of accounts you have. You can order new ones with the <code className="action">Order accounts</code> button above the table.

:::

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Account management tab of a Hosted Exchange service, Order accounts button" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-starting-hosted/console/first-steps-hosted-exchange-add-account.png" loading="lazy" />

### Use your email addresses

Once you have configured your accounts, you can start using them. To do this, OVHcloud offers the **Outlook Web App** (OWA) webmail. You can access it at the following address: [webmail](/links/web/email). To log in, enter your email address credentials. If you require any assistance using OWA, please refer to [our documentation](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/landing-page-microsoft-exchange).

If this is your first time logging in to OWA with this email address, you will be prompted to set the interface language and time zone. Next, click <code className="action">Save</code> to continue.

:::info
Time zones are listed according to [the UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_Universal_Time#/media/File:World_Time_Zones_Map.png), not in alphabetical order of cities.

**Example**: For Western Europe, it is UTC +1 (Brussels, Copenhagen, Madrid, Paris).

:::

If you would like to configure your email address on an email client or mobile device (e.g. a smartphone or tablet), please refer to [our documentation](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/landing-page-microsoft-exchange). To optimise the way you use your Exchange email address on an email client, ensure that it is compatible with the service.

OVHcloud offers optional Outlook licences with your Exchange email account in the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>.

To subscribe, go to our page "[How to obtain an Outlook licence for Exchange](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/office-outlook-license)".

<Region zones={['eu']}>

You can also get [Microsoft 365 licences](/links/web/ms365). We recommend using one of these solutions if you would like to use the Outlook email client, or other software from the Microsoft Office suite.

</Region>

:::info
With Exchange, you can sync all of your settings (filters, email signatures, folders, etc.), regardless of whether you are using a web application or a compatible email client.
This way, if you use Exchange on three devices via different login interfaces (webmail, compatible email software or client), all of your information will be available at the same time.

:::

### Set up collaborative features (optional)

Now that your Hosted Exchange service is configured and functional, you can set up the service's collaborative features in the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>. You can use these features to create resources (meeting rooms, equipment, etc.), groups, and more.

To enable these features, select the Hosted Exchange service concerned in the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>. Depending on the feature, the action to perform is in the sub-tabs of the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab (shared accounts, groups and resources) or in the sections of the <code className="action">Settings</code> tab (footers and external contacts).

For assistance with these features, please refer to [our documentation](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/landing-page-microsoft-exchange).
