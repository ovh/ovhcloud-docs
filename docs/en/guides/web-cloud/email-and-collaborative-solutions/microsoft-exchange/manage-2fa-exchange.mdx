## Objective

To increase the security of your Exchange account, you can enable two-factor authentication (2FA). Each time you log in to OWA (Outlook Web App) webmail, you then enter a random code in addition to your password. This code is generated by a one-time password (OTP) app installed on your smartphone or tablet.

:::warning
Two-factor authentication only protects access to OWA webmail. It does not apply to email clients (Outlook, Mail, Thunderbird, etc.) or to mobile devices: these connections (ActiveSync, EWS, IMAP, POP, etc.) remain protected by the account password alone. We therefore recommend choosing a strong password.

:::

**This guide explains how to enable two-factor authentication on your Exchange account.**

## Requirements

- an [OVHcloud Exchange solution](/links/web/emails-exchange) already set up
- an OTP mobile application installed on your Android or iOS smartphone or tablet

:::info
**OTP mobile applications**

You can choose from a number of OTP applications. Here are two examples that are free to use.

- For Android: Free OTP
- For iOS: OTP Auth

:::

## Instructions

### First configuration

#### Step 1: Enable two-factor authentication on the platform

For the initial configuration, you need to enable two-factor authentication on the platform before enabling it on an account.

Select the <code className="action">Settings</code> tab of your platform.

1. In the **Account security** section, click the <code className="action">Edit account lockout</code> link.
1. In the **Account lockout** panel that opens, turn on the **Two-factor authentication** switch.
1. To finish, click <code className="action">Save</code> at the bottom of the panel.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Account lockout panel opened from the Settings tab, with the Two-factor authentication switch" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/manage-2fa-exchange/console/2fa-exchange-00.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Step 2: Enable two-factor authentication on an account

After enabling two-factor authentication on your platform, you can enable it on one of your accounts.

Still from your Exchange platform, click the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab (**Email account** sub-tab, open by default). Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then <code className="action">Enable two-factor authentication</code>. In the window that opens, click <code className="action">Confirm</code>.

The next time they log in to webmail, the account user will need to set up their OTP application.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="⋮ menu of an account in the Account management tab, Enable two-factor authentication entry" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/manage-2fa-exchange/console/2fa-exchange-01.png" loading="lazy" />

To link the Exchange account to your OTP app, log in to your [webmail](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/mail/).

At the first attempt to log in, a QR code will appear. Use your OTP app to scan it, then enter the code that the app generates to log in.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="2fa-exchange" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/manage-2fa-exchange/2fa-exchange-02.png" loading="lazy" />

When you next log in, webmail will only ask for the code generated by the app.

### Disabling two-factor authentication

You can disable two-factor authentication on your account in three ways.

In the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab of your Exchange platform (**Email account** sub-tab, open by default), click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account on which two-factor authentication is active.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="⋮ menu of an account with two-factor authentication active, Disable, Reset and Remove entries" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/manage-2fa-exchange/console/2fa-exchange-04.png" loading="lazy" />

Select the option that you need according to the table below:

| Purpose | Description |
|------------------|------------------|
| <code className="action">Disable two-factor authentication</code> | Suspends two-factor authentication for 1 to 8760 hours, to be entered in the **Disabling period** field of the confirmation window. Once this period has passed, two-factor authentication is reactivated automatically. <br/> *For example: a user has forgotten their phone and cannot authenticate themselves with the OTP app.* |
| <code className="action">Reset two-factor authentication</code> | Resets the QR code requested when first logging in to webmail.<br/> *For example: a user has changed phones and needs to reconfigure their OTP application.* |
| <code className="action">Remove two-factor authentication</code> | Fully removes two-factor authentication from the account. |

In the window that opens, click <code className="action">Confirm</code>.
