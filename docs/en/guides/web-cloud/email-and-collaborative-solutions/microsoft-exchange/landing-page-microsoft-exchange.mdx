## What is Microsoft Exchange?

[OVHcloud Microsoft Exchange](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/emails/hosted-exchange/) is the most comprehensive collaborative email solution (Hosted, Private and Dedicated): email addresses, calendars, contacts and tasks synchronised across all your devices, resources, shared accounts and advanced administration. Find all the guides here to order, configure and manage your accounts.

With Microsoft Exchange, you can:

- Order and get started with a Hosted, Private or Dedicated Exchange platform
- Associate your domain name with your email platform and validate its DNS configuration
- Access your email, calendars and contacts from the Outlook Web App webmail
- Configure your accounts in an email client on your computer or mobile
- Manage advanced features (groups, delegation, resources, shared accounts, two-factor authentication)

## Accessing your email

Access your email, calendars and contacts online via the [Outlook Web App (OWA)](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/email-owa) webmail — no installation required.

## Finding your server name

Most email clients configure your account automatically via **Autodiscover**: simply enter your email address and password.

<Region zones={['eu']}>
For a manual configuration (ActiveSync or EWS), the server name takes the form `ex?.mail.ovh.net`.

To find your server number, log in to your <ManagerLink to="/#/web/exchange">OVHcloud Control Panel</ManagerLink> and select your Microsoft Exchange platform. The full server name is shown in the **General information** tab (for example: `ex3.mail.ovh.net`).

</Region>
