## Objective

With an Outlook licence you can use the Outlook email client to view and manage the emails in your Exchange account.

OVHcloud offers the Outlook licence for a monthly cost of €2 ex VAT. It will be linked to an OVHcloud Exchange account that you manage.

After subscribing, you can download the Outlook client as one of the following 3 versions:

- Outlook for Windows 32 bit
- Outlook for Windows 64 bit
- Outlook for MAC 64 bit

**This guide explains how to order an Outlook licence from your OVHcloud Exchange platform and how to activate it.**

## Instructions

### Ordering an Outlook licence

#### For a Hosted Exchange account

In the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then <code className="action">Order an Outlook license</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="⋮ menu of a Hosted Exchange account, Order an Outlook license entry" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/office-outlook-license/console/order-outlook01.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Order Outlook licenses** window, choose the renewal period for your licence (**1 month** or **1 year**), then tick the box to accept the terms. Then click <code className="action">Next</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Order Outlook licenses window, choice of the period and acceptance of the terms" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/office-outlook-license/console/order-outlook02.png" loading="lazy" />

The purchase order is then generated: click <code className="action">Order</code> to open the payment page in a new tab, then pay for your order using one of the payment methods available to you.

Your Outlook licence is available in the OVHcloud Console about 2 hours after your payment has been accepted: the account's **Outlook** column then shows **Active**.

<Region zones={['eu']}>

#### For a Private Exchange account

In the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then <code className="action">Activate the Outlook licence</code>. This option only appears on an account that has already been configured.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="⋮ menu of a configured Private Exchange account, Activate the Outlook licence entry" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/office-outlook-license/console/order-outlook03.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Activate the Outlook licence?** window, click <code className="action">Confirm</code> to activate the licence for the account concerned. The licence is then billed for as long as it stays active.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Activate the Outlook licence confirmation window, with the Confirm button" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/office-outlook-license/console/order-outlook04.png" loading="lazy" />

Your Outlook licence is available in the OVHcloud Console after a few moments.

:::info
Your Outlook licence will be billed on your Private Exchange platform every month.

:::

</Region>


### Downloading and installing Outlook

You now need to download the appropriate Outlook installation file for your device.

In the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab of your Exchange platform, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then <code className="action">Access the Outlook licence</code>.

In the **Outlook licence** window, if no link has been generated for this account yet, choose the version from the **Outlook version** drop-down menu based on your operating system, then the language from the **Language** drop-down menu. Then click <code className="action">Generate the link</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Outlook licence window with the Outlook version and Language drop-down menus" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/office-outlook-license/console/order-outlook05.png" loading="lazy" />

After a few moments, the window shows the **Download link** and the **Licence key** to use when installing Outlook on your device. This link is only valid for a limited time: its expiry date is shown just below.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Outlook licence window showing the licence key and the download link" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/office-outlook-license/console/order-outlook06.png" loading="lazy" />

The downloaded file is in the .ISO format, i.e. a disk image. Start the installation and enter the licence key when prompted.

### Cancelling the Outlook licence on your account

In the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the Hosted Exchange account concerned, then <code className="action">Cancel the Outlook licence</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="⋮ menu of a Hosted Exchange account with a licence, Cancel the Outlook licence entry" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/office-outlook-license/console/order-outlook07.png" loading="lazy" />

Once cancelled, the Outlook licence remains usable until its expiry date, and is then not renewed. In the **Cancel this Outlook licence?** window, click <code className="action">Cancel the licence</code> to confirm.
