## Objective

Exchange accounts can be configured on various compatible email clients. This allows you to use your email address from your preferred device. Thunderbird is a free and open-source email client.

**This guide explains how to configure your Exchange email address on Thunderbird for Windows.**

## Requirements

- A [Hosted Exchange](/links/web/emails-hosted-exchange) or [Private Exchange](/links/web/emails-private-exchange) email account
- The Thunderbird software installed on your Windows device
- The credentials for the email account you want to configure

### Add the account

- **On the first launch of the application**: a configuration wizard appears and prompts you to enter your email address.

- **If an account is already configured on the application**:

    1. Click on the <code className="action">☰</code> menu in the top horizontal bar.
    2. Click on <code className="action">New Account</code>.
    3. Click on <code className="action">Email Address</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Thunderbird new account menu on Windows" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/how-to-configure-thunderbird/configuration-thunderbird-win-01.png" loading="lazy" />

Follow the configuration steps by clicking successively on the **5** tabs below:

  <Tab label="Step 1">

     - The email address to configure.
    
    Click on <code className="action">Continue</code> to complete the settings.

  <Tab label="Step 3">

### Use the email address

Once your email address is configured, you can start sending and receiving emails.

OVHcloud also offers a web application to access your email address from a browser. To open the OVHcloud webmail, click on [this link](/links/web/email). You can log in using the credentials of your email address.

### Retrieve a backup of your email account

If you need to perform an operation that could result in the loss of your email account data, we recommend making a backup of the affected email account beforehand. To do this, refer to the "**Export**" section in the "**Thunderbird**" part of our guide "[Manually migrate your email address](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/manual-email-migration)".

### Modify existing settings

If your email account is already configured and you need to access the account settings to modify them:

1. Click on the <code className="action">☰</code> menu in the top horizontal bar.
2. Click on <code className="action">Account Settings</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Thunderbird Account Settings menu on Windows" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/how-to-configure-thunderbird/configuration-thunderbird-win-07.png" loading="lazy" />

- To modify settings related to the **reception** of your emails, click on <code className="action">Server Settings</code> in the left column under your email address.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Thunderbird incoming server settings for editing email reception" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/how-to-configure-thunderbird/configuration-thunderbird-exchange-win-08.png" loading="lazy" />

- To modify settings related to the **sending** of your emails, click on <code className="action">Outgoing Server (SMTP)</code> at the bottom of the left column.
- Click on the relevant email address in the list, then click on <code className="action">Edit</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Thunderbird outgoing SMTP server settings for editing email sending" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/how-to-configure-thunderbird/configuration-thunderbird-exchange-win-09.png" loading="lazy" />
