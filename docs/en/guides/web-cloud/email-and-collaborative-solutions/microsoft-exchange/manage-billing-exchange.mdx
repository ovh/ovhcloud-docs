## Instructions

### Hosted Exchange

#### Ordering additional accounts

In the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab (**Email account** sub-tab, open by default), click the <code className="action">Order accounts</code> button, above the table. In the **Order Exchange accounts** panel that opens, fill in the requested information (account type, payment type and number of accounts), accept the terms by ticking the box provided, then click <code className="action">Order</code>. The purchase order is then generated and opens in a new tab, where you can pay for your order.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Account management tab of a Hosted Exchange service, Order accounts button" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/manage-billing-exchange/console/billing-exchange-00.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Changing the renewal frequency for your accounts <a name="periodicity"></a>

In the **Subscription** section of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab of your Exchange platform, click <code className="action">Manage my service accounts</code>, under **Service management**.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Manage my service accounts link in the Subscription section of the Overview tab" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/manage-billing-exchange/console/billing-exchange-01.png" loading="lazy" />

The **Service management** page opens in your Exchange platform and lists its accounts. You can also reach it from the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab: click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of an account, then <code className="action">Manage my service</code>. The page is then filtered on that account.

On this page, you can view and modify the billing for each of your accounts. The **Automatic renewal** column shows the renewal period of each account (**Monthly** or **Yearly**). Change it account by account: click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then <code className="action">Change renewal period</code>. Each modification on an account will be applied on the date listed in the **Renewal date** column.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Service management page, an account's ⋮ menu with the Change renewal period entry" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/manage-billing-exchange/console/billing-exchange-02.png" loading="lazy" />

In the example below, you will see the actions you need to take in order to set an account to monthly renewal:

> In the **Change renewal period** window that opens, select **Monthly** under **Renewal period**, then click <code className="action">Edit</code> to apply the change.
>
> <img className="thumbnail" alt="Change renewal period window, Monthly or Yearly choice for the renewal period" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/manage-billing-exchange/console/billing-exchange-03.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Deleting accounts

To delete an account from your Hosted Exchange platform, first terminate it from the **Service management** page. To reach this page, refer to the section "[Changing the renewal frequency for your accounts](#periodicity)". Then click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account concerned and select <code className="action">Terminate at expiration</code>. In the **Termination at expiration** window, click <code className="action">Confirm termination</code>.

Once you have terminated it, the account will be suspended on its expiry date. If you would like to delete the email address hosted by the account before its expiry date, reset the account.

In the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab of your platform, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then <code className="action">Reset this account</code>. In the **Reset an account** window, click <code className="action">Confirm</code>. Once you have reset the account, it will remain empty until its expiry date.

### Private Exchange

:::info
On a Private Exchange platform, only monthly renewal is available for accounts. You cannot configure any other renewal frequencies.

:::


#### Ordering additional accounts

In the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab (**Email account** sub-tab, open by default), click the <code className="action">Add an account</code> button, above the table. The account will be billed for the current month.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Account management tab of a Private Exchange service, Add an account button" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/manage-billing-exchange/console/billing-exchange-06.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Deleting accounts

:::warning
To delete an email account on a Private Exchange platform, **it is necessary that it has been created** (attached to a domain name on your Exchange platform). An unconfigured account cannot be deleted.

:::


In the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then <code className="action">Delete this account</code>. In the **Delete an account** window, click <code className="action">Delete</code>. The account is immediately removed from the platform, and you do not need to terminate it.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="⋮ menu of a Private Exchange account, with the Delete this account entry" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/manage-billing-exchange/console/billing-exchange-07.png" loading="lazy" />

</Region>

### Cancel the platform

Once you have deleted the email accounts, you need to delete the domain name(s) associated with your Exchange platform before terminating it. In the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the domain name you want to delete, then <code className="action">Delete this domain</code>. In the **Delete domain** window, click <code className="action">Delete</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="⋮ menu of a domain name in the Associated domains tab, Delete this domain entry" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/manage-billing-exchange/console/billing-exchange-del-dom.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Subscription** section of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab of your Exchange platform, click <code className="action">Terminate service</code>, under **Service management**. In the **Terminate service** window, select **At expiration** to terminate the platform on its expiry date, then click <code className="action">Terminate</code>. To delete it as soon as you confirm, select **Terminate immediately**, then click <code className="action">Terminate now</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Terminate service link in the Subscription section of the Overview tab" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/manage-billing-exchange/console/billing-exchange-08.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
With **At expiration**, this action suspends and then deletes all of the accounts on the Exchange platform on their expiry date.

:::
