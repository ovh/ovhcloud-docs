## Objective

With the Private Exchange service, you get a dedicated Exchange server for your professional email accounts, with collaborative features such as calendar and contact syncing.

Once your order has been delivered, the server must be configured for the first time before you can create email accounts. This configuration takes place entirely in the OVHcloud Control Panel, in three stages: choosing your email server address ([step 2](#step-2-choose-your-email-server-address)), validating the domain name that carries it ([step 3](#step-3-validate-the-ownership-of-your-domain-name)), then adding the domain name(s) for your email addresses ([step 5](#step-5-add-your-domain-name-and-create-your-accounts)).

**This guide explains the first steps to take once your Private Exchange server has been delivered.**

## Requirements

- an [OVHcloud Private Exchange solution](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/emails/private-exchange/)
- the email confirming that your Private Exchange service has been delivered
- a domain name whose DNS zone you can edit (managed at OVHcloud or outside OVHcloud)

## Instructions

### Step 1: Receive the delivery email

Once your order has been processed, an email is sent to the contact email address listed in your OVHcloud Control Panel. It confirms that your Private Exchange service is available and invites you to configure it from the OVHcloud Control Panel.

To find this email again from the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Control Panel</ManagerLink>, click on your account name in the top right-hand corner, then click <code className="action">Service emails</code>. Look for the email with a subject similar to:

> **\[ab12345-ovh] Your Private Exchange SE service is ready!**

:::info
The name of a Private Exchange service in your OVHcloud Control Panel begins with **private-**, contains part of your NIC handle and ends with a figure (1 for the first Private Exchange service installed, 2 for the second, etc.).

:::

At this stage, the server exists but nothing can be used yet: its address has not been chosen, and no domain name is associated with it. Move on to [step 2](#step-2-choose-your-email-server-address).

### Step 2: Choose your email server address

Select your Private Exchange service in the OVHcloud Control Panel. As the service has never been configured, a configuration assistant opens instead of the usual management page: **Welcome to your Private Exchange area**.

In the **Choose your email server address** section, two fields are required:

- **Your sub-domain**: the host part of the address, for example `mail`.
- **Your domain name**: the domain name that will carry your server address, for example `mydomain.ovh`.

Together, they form your **email server address**, for example `mail.mydomain.ovh`. This address is used as:

- the webmail access link (Outlook Web App, OWA),
- the server name to enter when configuring an email client such as Outlook,
- the name covered by the SSL certificate issued for your server.

The message displayed under the fields depends on where your domain name is managed.

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Same OVHcloud account">

If the domain name is managed in the same OVHcloud account as your Private Exchange service, an information message confirms that the service will be configured automatically. This configuration takes several minutes.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Another account or outside OVHcloud">

If the domain name is managed outside OVHcloud, or in another OVHcloud account, a warning reminds you that you must be able to edit its DNS zone. You will have to add a DNS record yourself in [step 3](#step-3-validate-the-ownership-of-your-domain-name).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Configuration assistant with a domain name managed outside OVHcloud" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-starting-private/private-exchange-welcome-external.png" loading="lazy" />

:::warning
**The email server address cannot be changed once confirmed.** Check the sub-domain and the domain name carefully before continuing. If you need a different address later, the only option is to order a new Private Exchange service.

:::

Click <code className="action">Next</code>. A confirmation window displays the address that will be assigned to your server and reminds you that this information cannot be changed later. Click <code className="action">Confirm</code> to continue.

### Step 3: Validate the ownership of your domain name

This step only concerns domain names that are **not managed in the same OVHcloud account** as your Private Exchange service (domain name managed outside OVHcloud, or in another OVHcloud account).

:::info
**Domain name managed in the same OVHcloud account**: the configuration is automatic and you do not need to validate anything. The page described below may nevertheless be displayed for a few moments while the automatic configuration is in progress. **Do not add the record yourself: wait.** The page disappears on its own once the record has been created and detected. You can then move on to [step 4](#step-4-check-that-your-server-is-ready).

:::

For a domain name managed outside OVHcloud or in another account, the assistant displays the **Configuring your Private Exchange product** page, with the section **Confirm your domain ownership**. To prove that you control the domain name chosen in [step 2](#step-2-choose-your-email-server-address), a **CNAME** record must be added to its DNS zone. The record to create is displayed on the page, with a button to copy it.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Domain ownership validation page with the CNAME record to add" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-starting-private/private-exchange-cname.png" loading="lazy" />

Copy the CNAME record **before leaving the page**, then create it in the DNS zone of your domain name, from the interface where you manage its DNS zone.

- Once the record has been added, it may take up to 1 hour to be detected. You can leave the page: the process continues in the background.
- **You have 48 hours to add the CNAME record.** After this period, the configuration is cancelled and you must restart it from the beginning.

Once the CNAME record has been detected, the server is finalised automatically: the SSL certificate is generated for your email server address and the webmail is activated. No further email is sent at this stage: check the status of your server directly in the OVHcloud Control Panel, as described in [step 4](#step-4-check-that-your-server-is-ready).

### Step 4: Check that your server is ready

Select your Private Exchange service again in the OVHcloud Control Panel. The assistant no longer appears: you land on the <code className="action">General information</code> tab of the service.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="General information tab of a Private Exchange service after its first configuration" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-starting-private/private-exchange-general-information.png" loading="lazy" />

Check the following elements:

- **Server diagnostic** (Summary): the `A`, `AAAA`, `PTR` and `PTR v6` badges indicate whether the DNS records of your email server address are correct. When a badge is not green, click on it to display the details and the values to configure.
- **SSL Certificate** (Summary): must be **Active**, with its expiry date.
- **Webmail** (Connection): the link to your Outlook Web App, in the form `https://mail.mydomain.ovh`.
- **Associated domains** (Statistics): displays **0** at this stage. This is normal: the domain name for your email addresses is added in [step 5](#step-5-add-your-domain-name-and-create-your-accounts).

If your domain name is managed outside OVHcloud or in another account, the `A` and `AAAA` badges will stay orange until you have created the corresponding records yourself. Click on each badge: the details show the record type, the sub-domain and the target value (the IPv4 or IPv6 address of your server) to enter in the DNS zone of your domain name, from the interface where you manage its DNS zone.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Details of the A record displayed from the server diagnostic" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-starting-private/private-exchange-diagnostic-a-record.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
The `PTR` and `PTR v6` records (reverse DNS) are managed by OVHcloud. If one of these badges reports a problem, you cannot fix it yourself: contact our support teams.

:::

### Step 5: Add your domain name and create your accounts

Your server is ready, but you cannot create email accounts until a domain name has been associated with it. This domain name is the one that will appear in your email addresses (for example `john.smith@mydomain.ovh`). It may be the same as the one used for the server address, or a different one.

Click on the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab, then on the <code className="action">Add a domain</code> button, and follow the steps described in our guide on [Adding a domain name to an Exchange service](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-adding-domain).

:::warning
In the last step of adding a domain, the **Recommended configuration** option redirects **all emails sent to your domain name** to your Private Exchange service, **immediately**. If this domain name already receives emails on another service (another OVHcloud email solution, a third-party email service or an in-house server), choose **Custom configuration**: it lets you keep the other email solution running alongside your Private Exchange service and decide when to switch.

:::

Once the domain name has been added, keep an eye on the **Diagnostic** column of the table rather than the **Status** column: the status can be **OK** while the `MX`, `SRV`, `SPF` or `DKIM` badges are still red. A red badge means that a DNS record is missing or incorrect. Click on it to display the value to configure. As long as the `MX` record is not correct, your domain name will not receive any emails on your Private Exchange service.

- **Domain name managed at OVHcloud, with the recommended configuration**: the records are created for you in the DNS zone. The badges may stay red for a while, until the changes have propagated (up to 24 hours): you do not need to do anything.
- **Domain name not managed by your OVHcloud account**: the domain name first appears with the mode **Validating domain** and the status **Confirmation**, with a red `CNAME` badge in the **Diagnostic** column. You must prove that you own it: click on the `CNAME` badge to display the record to create in the DNS zone of your domain name (in the form `xxxxx-check.mydomain.ovh` pointing to `ovh.com`). **You have 48 hours to do this.** Once the domain name has been validated, create the email records yourself from the values displayed in the **Diagnostic** column. Please refer to our guide on [Creating a CNAME record to validate your domain name on your email solution](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-dns-cname).

You can then create your first email accounts from the <code className="action">Email accounts</code> tab.

### Renewing the SSL certificate

The SSL certificate issued for your email server address has a limited validity period. Its expiry date is displayed under **SSL Certificate**, in the **Summary** of the <code className="action">General information</code> tab. When the expiry date is near, or once it has passed, a message invites you to renew the certificate. Do not wait for it to expire: with an expired certificate, the webmail and email clients display security warnings.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Expired SSL certificate with the renewal option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-starting-private/private-exchange-ssl-expired.png" loading="lazy" />

1. Click the <code className="action">...</code> button next to the message, then <code className="action">Renew the SSL certificate</code>.
2. In the **SSL certificate renewal** window, select the email address that will receive the renewal confirmation. Only the administrative addresses of your domain names are offered, such as `admin@`, `administrator@` or `hostmaster@`, followed by your domain name or your email server address. **The address you select must exist**: if none of them does, create the corresponding email account (or an alias to an existing account) before continuing.
3. Click <code className="action">Next</code>. Check the information displayed (server model, server address and confirmation email), then click <code className="action">Confirm</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="SSL certificate renewal window, choice of the confirmation email address" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-starting-private/private-exchange-ssl-renewal.png" loading="lazy" />

:::warning
You must be able to read the emails received at the selected address: once the renewal has been processed, an email detailing the next steps is sent to it. Without this email, the renewal cannot be completed.

:::

Once confirmed, a message at the top of the page confirms that the renewal has been processed and reminds you of the email address the next steps have been sent to. In the meantime, the **SSL Certificate** section indicates that the certificate is being installed and cannot be renewed again. Follow the instructions in the email you have received: the new expiry date is displayed once the certificate has been installed.
