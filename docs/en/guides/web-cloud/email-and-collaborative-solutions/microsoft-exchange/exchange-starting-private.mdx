## Objective

With the Private Exchange service, you get a dedicated Exchange server for your professional email accounts, with collaborative features such as calendar and contact synchronisation.

Hosted Exchange hosts your accounts on one of several shared servers, each with an IP address shared by all the customers of that server. Private Exchange gives you your own Exchange server: its resources are reserved for you, it comes with a **dedicated IP address**, and the SSL certificate is issued for your own server address. As the only sender on that IP address, you alone control the sending reputation of your email domain: no other customer's traffic can affect the deliverability of your emails, and vice versa.

**This guide explains the first steps to take once your Private Exchange server has been delivered.**

## Requirements

- an [OVHcloud Private Exchange solution](/links/web/emails-private-exchange)
- the email confirming that your Private Exchange service has been delivered
- a domain name whose DNS zone you can edit, if it is not managed in the same OVHcloud customer account as your Private Exchange service

## Instructions

Once your order has been delivered, you must configure the server for the first time before you can create email accounts. This configuration takes place entirely in the OVHcloud Console, and three of the five steps require an action from you: choosing your email server address ([step 2](#step-2-choose-your-email-server-address)), validating the domain name that carries it if it is not managed in the same OVHcloud customer account ([step 3](#step-3-validate-the-ownership-of-your-domain-name)), then adding the domain name(s) for your email addresses and creating your accounts ([step 5](#step-5-add-your-domain-name-and-create-your-accounts)).

### Step 1: Receive the delivery email

Once your order has been processed, an email is sent to the contact email address listed in your OVHcloud customer account. It confirms that your Private Exchange service is available and invites you to configure it from the OVHcloud Console.

To find this email in the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>, click the button showing your initials in the top right-hand corner, then click <code className="action">My messages</code>. Look for the email with a subject similar to:

> **\[ab12345-ovh] Your Private Exchange SE service is ready!**

:::info
The name of a Private Exchange service in the OVHcloud Console begins with **private-**, contains part of your NIC handle and ends with a number (1 for the first Private Exchange service installed, 2 for the second, etc.). **SE** is the Exchange version installed on your server; it is displayed under **Exchange version**, in the **General information** section of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab.

:::

At this stage, the server exists but is not yet usable: its address has not been chosen, and no domain name is associated with it. Move on to [step 2](#step-2-choose-your-email-server-address).

### Step 2: Choose your email server address

Select your Private Exchange service in the OVHcloud Console. As the service has never been configured, a configuration assistant opens instead of the usual management page. It opens on the **Choose your mail server address** page.

On this page, 2 fields are required:

- **Your subdomain**: the host part of the address, for example `mail`.
- **Your domain name**: the domain name that will carry your server address, for example `mydomain.ovh`.

Together, they form your **email server address**, for example `mail.mydomain.ovh`. This address is used as:

- the Outlook Web App (OWA) webmail access link
- the server name to enter when configuring an email client such as Outlook
- the name covered by the SSL certificate issued for your server

What happens next depends on where your domain name is managed.

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Same OVHcloud customer account">

If the domain name is managed in the same OVHcloud customer account as your Private Exchange service, the service is configured automatically. This configuration takes several minutes.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Another customer account or outside OVHcloud">

If the domain name is managed outside OVHcloud, or in another OVHcloud customer account, you must be able to edit its DNS zone: you will add a record to it yourself in [step 3](#step-3-validate-the-ownership-of-your-domain-name).

</Tab>
</Tabs>

:::warning
**The email server address cannot be changed once confirmed.** Check the sub-domain and the domain name carefully before continuing. If you need a different address later, the only option is to order a new Private Exchange service.

:::

Click <code className="action">Save the configuration</code>. In the **Are you sure you want to continue?** window, click <code className="action">Confirm</code> to assign this address to your server permanently.

### Step 3: Validate the ownership of your domain name

This step only concerns domain names that are **not managed in the same OVHcloud customer account** as your Private Exchange service (domain name managed outside OVHcloud, or in another OVHcloud customer account).

:::info
**Domain name managed in the same OVHcloud customer account**: the configuration is automatic and you do not need to validate anything. The section described below may still appear for a few moments while the automatic configuration is in progress. **Do not add the CNAME record yourself: wait.** The page disappears on its own once the record has been created and detected. You can then move on to [step 4](#step-4-check-that-your-server-is-ready).

:::

For a domain name managed outside OVHcloud or in another customer account, the assistant displays, below the chosen address, the **Configuring your Private Exchange product** section, subtitled **Confirm ownership of your domain**. To prove that you control the domain name chosen in [step 2](#step-2-choose-your-email-server-address), a **<Tooltip term="cname">CNAME</Tooltip>** record must be added to its DNS zone. The record to create is displayed on the page, with a button to copy it.

Copy the CNAME record **before leaving the page**, then create it from the interface where you manage your domain name's DNS zone.

- Once the record has been added, it may take up to 1 hour to be detected. You can leave the page: the process continues in the background.
- **You have 48 hours to add the CNAME record.** After this period, the configuration is cancelled and you must restart it from the beginning.

Once the CNAME record has been detected, the server configuration is completed automatically: the SSL certificate is generated for your email server address and the webmail is activated. Check the status of your server directly in the OVHcloud Console, as described in [step 4](#step-4-check-that-your-server-is-ready).

### Step 4: Check that your server is ready

Select your Private Exchange service again in the OVHcloud Console. The assistant no longer appears: the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab of the service is displayed.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="General information section of the Overview tab of a Private Exchange service" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-starting-private/console/private-exchange-general-information.png" loading="lazy" />

Check the following elements:

- **Server diagnostics** (General information): the `A`, `AAAA`, `PTR` and `PTR v6` badges indicate whether the DNS records of your email server address are correct. When a badge is not green, click <code className="action">View diagnostics</code> to display the details and the values to configure.
- **SSL Certificate** (General information): shows, after **Expiration:**, the expiry date of the certificate issued for your server.
- **Webmail** (General information): the <code className="action">OWA</code> link opens your Outlook Web App, at an address in the form `https://mail.mydomain.ovh`. Your email server address itself is displayed under **Server**.
- **Associated domains** (Statistics): displays **0** at this stage. This is normal: the domain name for your email addresses is added in [step 5](#step-5-add-your-domain-name-and-create-your-accounts).

If your domain name is managed outside OVHcloud or in another customer account, the `A` and `AAAA` badges stay red until you have created the corresponding records yourself. Click <code className="action">View diagnostics</code>. The **Server diagnostics** panel has one tab per record: in the `A` and `AAAA` tabs, the **Configuration error** status is followed by the **Record type**, the **Sub-domain** and the **Target** (the IPv4 or IPv6 address of your server). Enter these values from the interface where you manage your domain name's DNS zone.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Server diagnostics panel open on the A record with a configuration error" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-starting-private/console/private-exchange-diagnostic-a-record.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
The `PTR` and `PTR v6` records (reverse DNS) are managed by OVHcloud. If one of these badges reports a problem, you cannot fix it yourself: contact our [support team](/links/support).

:::

### Step 5: Add your domain name and create your accounts

#### Add your domain name

Your server is ready, but you cannot create email accounts until a domain name has been associated with it. This domain name will appear in your email addresses (for example `john.smith@mydomain.ovh`). It may be the same as the one used for the server address, or a different one.

Click on the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab, then on the <code className="action">Add a domain</code> button, and follow the steps described in our guide [How to add a domain name to your Exchange service](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-adding-domain).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Associated domains tab of a Private Exchange service with the Add a domain button" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-starting-private/console/private-exchange-associated-domains.png" loading="lazy" />

:::warning
When you add a domain, the **Recommended configuration** option routes **all emails sent to your domain name** to your Private Exchange service, **immediately**. If this domain name already receives emails through another service (another OVHcloud email solution, a third-party email service or an in-house server), choose **Custom configuration**: it lets you keep the other email solution running alongside your Private Exchange service and decide when to switch.

:::

Once the domain name has been added, check the **Diagnostic** column of the table rather than the **Status** column: the status can be **Active** while the `MX`, `SRV`, `SPF` or `DKIM` badges are not yet green. A badge that is not green means that a DNS record is missing or incorrect. To display the value to configure, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the domain name's row, then <code className="action">Diagnostics</code>. As long as the `MX` record is not correct, your domain name will not receive any emails on your Private Exchange service. The `SRV` record is needed to configure an Exchange account automatically in a compatible email client such as Outlook.

- **Domain name managed at OVHcloud, with the Recommended configuration option**: the records are created for you in the DNS zone. The badges only turn green once the changes have propagated, which can take up to 24 hours: you do not need to do anything.
- **Domain name not managed by your OVHcloud customer account**: the domain name first appears with the mode **Domain validation in progress** and the status **CNAME Validation**, with an orange `CNAME` badge in the **Diagnostic** column. You must prove that you own it: hover over the `CNAME` badge to display, in the **Owner verification** box, the record to create in the DNS zone of your domain name (in the form `xxxxx-check.mydomain.ovh` pointing to `ovh.com`). **You have 48 hours to do this.** Once the domain name has been validated, create the email records yourself from the values displayed in its diagnostics (the <code className="action">⋮</code> button of its row, then <code className="action">Diagnostics</code>). Please refer to our guide on [Creating a CNAME record to validate your domain name on your email solution](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-dns-cname).

:::tip
**SPF and DKIM protect your sending reputation.** With Private Exchange, that reputation is yours alone, so these two records matter even more: the SPF record tells receiving servers that your server is allowed to send emails for your domain name, and the DKIM signature proves that your emails have not been altered in transit. Without them, your emails are far more likely to be treated as spam, whatever the quality of your dedicated IP address.

The **Recommended configuration** creates both records for a domain name managed at OVHcloud (check the `SPF` and `DKIM` badges); for a domain name managed elsewhere, create them yourself. Please refer to our guides on [the SPF record](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-spf) and [the DKIM record](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-dkim), and complement them with a [DMARC record](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-dmarc).

:::

#### Create your email accounts

Once your domain name is associated, open the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab. In the **Email account** sub-tab, open by default, click <code className="action">Add an account</code> to create your email accounts for this domain name (for example `john.smith@mydomain.ovh`). Your users can then sign in to the webmail at your email server address, or set up their accounts in an email client: see our [Microsoft Exchange guides](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/landing-page-microsoft-exchange).

### Renewing the SSL certificate

The SSL certificate issued for your email server address has a limited validity period. Its expiry date is displayed under **SSL Certificate**, in the **General information** section of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab. Once this date has passed, the **SSL certificate expired** alert invites you to renew the certificate. Renew it without delay: with an expired certificate, the webmail and email clients display security warnings.

1. In the **SSL certificate expired** alert, click <code className="action">Renew</code>.
2. In the **SSL Certificate Renewal** window, select the email address that will receive the renewal confirmation. Only the administrative addresses of your domain names are offered, such as `admin@`, `administrator@` or `hostmaster@`, followed by your domain name or your email server address. **The address you select must exist**: if none of them does, create the corresponding email account (or an alias pointing to an existing account) before continuing.
3. Click <code className="action">Next</code>. Check the information displayed (**Server reference**, **Server address** and **Confirmation email**), then click <code className="action">Confirm</code>.

:::warning
You must be able to read the emails received at the selected address: once the renewal has been processed, an email detailing the next steps is sent to it. Without this email, the renewal cannot be completed.

:::

Once you confirm, the renewal request is sent and the next steps are emailed to you at the selected address. While the new certificate is being generated, no other renewal is possible. Follow the instructions in the email you have received: the new expiry date is displayed under **SSL Certificate** once the certificate has been installed.
