## Objective

A **shared account** is a mailbox shared between several Exchange accounts and can only be accessed through them. A shared account does not have a password. It is therefore necessary to delegate its access to one or more Exchange accounts of the same service.
<br/>Shared accounts can be accessed via delegation from OWA (Exchange webmail) or via Outlook email software.

**This guide explains how to create and manage a shared account on your Exchange platform.**

## Requirements

- An [OVHcloud Exchange solution](/links/web/emails-exchange)

## Instructions

### Adding a shared account

In the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab of your Exchange platform, open the <code className="action">Shared accounts</code> sub-tab, then click the <code className="action">Add a shared account</code> button, above the table.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Shared accounts sub-tab of an Exchange service, with the Add a shared account button above the table" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-shared-account/console/exchange-shared_accounts01.png" loading="lazy" />

The **Add shared account** window opens. Fill in the requested fields:

|Field|Description|
|---|---|
|Email account|Choose the name of your shared account, then select its domain name from the drop-down list after the `@`. **This must not be an existing email address.**|
|Quota|Enter the desired storage quota for your shared account, in MiB or GiB, [depending on available space](#size). It must be between 100 MiB and 10 GiB.|
|First name|Optional: the first name associated with your shared account.|
|Last name|Optional: the last name associated with your shared account.|
|Display name|Optional: the display name you want to appear when sending from your shared account.|
|Hide address in directory|Tick this box to hide the shared account's address in the address list of your Exchange platform.|

<a name="size"></a>

:::info
The space available for creating a shared account depends on the number of accounts subscribed on the Exchange service. Each account adds 5 GB of space for shared accounts.

**Example:**

You have subscribed to 4 accounts on your Exchange service, so you have **4 x 5 GB** i.e. **20 GB** of allocated space for shared accounts on this Exchange service.

:::

Then click <code className="action">Add</code>, at the bottom of the window, to create the shared account.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Add shared account window with the Email account, Quota and Display name fields" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-shared-account/console/exchange-shared_accounts02.png" loading="lazy" />

### Managing the delegation of a shared account

Once you have created your shared account, you will need to grant access permissions to one or more accounts of the Exchange service.

A shared account cannot be accessed directly because it does not have a password. It cannot be configured directly in an Outlook client, or accessed via webmail.

You will need to set up a delegation between an Exchange account and the shared account.

In the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab of your Exchange platform, open the <code className="action">Shared accounts</code> sub-tab. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the shared account's row, then <code className="action">Configure delegations</code>. On the **Delegation for shared account …** page, you can choose which accounts from your list can access the shared account.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="⋮ menu of a shared account open on the Configure delegations entry" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-shared-account/console/exchange-shared_accounts03.png" loading="lazy" />

For each account, tick the boxes of the permissions to grant it on the shared account:

|Field|Description|
|---|---|
|Access|Allows the account to view and manage the shared account from OWA (webmail) or Outlook.|
|Send As|Allows the account to send emails "as" the shared email address.|
|Send on Behalf|Allows the account to send emails "on behalf of" the shared email address.|

Then click <code className="action">Save</code>, below the table, to apply the changes.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Delegation for shared account page, with the Access, Send As and Send on Behalf columns" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-shared-account/console/exchange-shared-account-delegation.png" loading="lazy" />

For example, to allow the accounts **guide-exchange@** and **test@** to access the shared account **shared_test@**, tick their **Access** box. For **guide-exchange@** to also be able to send emails "as" **shared_test@**, tick its **Send As** box. For **test@** to be able to send emails "on behalf of" **shared_test@**, tick its **Send on Behalf** box.

### Using the account shared from OWA (webmail)

Log in to the Exchange webmail (OWA) at [webmail](/links/web/email) with an email account that has the **Access** permission on the shared account.
<br/>In our example, we connect with the **guide-exchange@** account.

Once you have logged in, right-click on the main tree for your email address in the left-hand column. Select <code className="action">Add shared folder...</code> from the context menu.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="emails" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-shared-account/exchange-shared_accounts05.png" loading="lazy" />

Enter the name of your shared account and click <code className="action">Add</code> when it is found in the Exchange directory.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="emails" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-shared-account/exchange-shared_accounts06.png" loading="lazy" />

In our example, the shared account is now accessible from the **guide-exchange@** account.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="emails" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-shared-account/exchange-shared_accounts07.png" loading="lazy" />

### Using the shared account from Outlook

In the Outlook application, you will see your shared account in the left-hand column in the same way as in OWA.
