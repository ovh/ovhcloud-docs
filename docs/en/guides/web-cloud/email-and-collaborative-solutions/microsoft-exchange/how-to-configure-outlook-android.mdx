## Objective

You can configure your Exchange email addresses in the Outlook for Android app, available for free on the Google Play Store. When configured as an **Exchange** account, your address syncs via the ActiveSync protocol, so your emails, calendar and contacts are available on your device.

**Find out how to configure an Exchange account via ActiveSync in the Outlook for Android app.**

[[fragment:support-scope]]

:::warning
We recommend that you contact a [specialist service provider](/links/transversal/marketplace-support-collaboration) and/or the software publisher if you experience any difficulties.
:::

## Requirements

- You have an [Exchange](/links/web/emails-exchange) solution.
- You have the [Outlook app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.microsoft.office.outlook&hl=en) installed on your Android device.
- You have the login credentials for the email address you want to configure.

:::info
This guide was written using a device running Android 17, with version 5.2645.1 of the Outlook app.
:::

## Instructions

### Add the account <a name="add-account"></a>

Adding an account works differently **if no account is set up** or **if an account is already set up** in the Outlook app. Select the tab that matches your situation:

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="First setup">
    <CardGrid columns={2}>
    <div>

    When you open the Outlook app for the first time, tap <code className="action">Add account</code>.

    </div>
    <div>

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Outlook for Android, welcome screen with the Add account button" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/how-to-configure-outlook-android/outlook-android-01-welcome.png" loading="lazy" />

    </div>
    </CardGrid>
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Existing setup">
    <CardGrid columns={2}>
    <div>

    1. Tap your account icon, in the upper left of the screen.
    2. In the panel that opens, tap the <code className="action">+</code> button.
    3. Tap <code className="action">Add an account</code>.

    </div>
    <div>

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Outlook for Android, navigation panel and Add an account option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/how-to-configure-outlook-android/outlook-android-02-add-account.png" loading="lazy" />

    </div>
    </CardGrid>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Follow the configuration steps by going through the tabs below:

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    <CardGrid columns={2}>
    <div>

    Enter your full email address, then tap <code className="action">Continue</code>.

    Do not use the <code className="action">ADD GOOGLE ACCOUNT</code> button: it is only for Google accounts.

    </div>
    <div>

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Outlook for Android, entering the email address" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/how-to-configure-outlook-android/outlook-android-03-email.png" loading="lazy" />

    </div>
    </CardGrid>
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    <CardGrid columns={2}>
    <div>

    Outlook automatically looks up your account settings. Wait a few seconds.

    If the "**Connect Exchange**" window opens, go straight to step 3.

    </div>
    <div>

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Outlook for Android, Connect Exchange window opened by the automatic detection" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/how-to-configure-outlook-android/outlook-android-04-exchange-detected.png" loading="lazy" />

    </div>
    </CardGrid>

    <CardGrid columns={2}>
    <div>

    If Outlook only shows a window offering **IMAP** and **POP3**, select the Exchange account type manually:

    1. Tap <code className="action">IMAP</code>. This choice only opens the next menu: your account will not be configured as IMAP.
    2. In the "**Connect IMAP**" window, tap the <code className="action">?</code> icon in the upper right of the screen.
    3. Tap <code className="action">Change account provider</code>.
    4. In the list of account types, tap <code className="action">Exchange</code>.

    </div>
    <div>

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Outlook for Android, window offering IMAP and POP3" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/how-to-configure-outlook-android/outlook-android-05-imap-pop.png" loading="lazy" />

    </div>
    </CardGrid>

    <CardGrid columns={2}>
    <div>

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Outlook for Android, Change account provider menu" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/how-to-configure-outlook-android/outlook-android-06-change-provider.png" loading="lazy" />

    </div>
    <div>

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Outlook for Android, list of account types with Exchange" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/how-to-configure-outlook-android/outlook-android-07-account-type.png" loading="lazy" />

    </div>
    </CardGrid>
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    <CardGrid columns={2}>
    <div>

    In the "**Connect Exchange**" window, turn on <code className="action">ADVANCED SETTINGS</code> if the advanced fields are not shown, then fill in the following information:

    - **Email Address**: your full email address, already filled in.
    - **Password**: the password for your email address.
    - **Description**: a name to identify this account among your other accounts in Outlook (optional).
    - **Server**: the name of your Exchange server, if it is not already filled in. To find it, see the "[Finding your server name](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/landing-page-microsoft-exchange#finding-your-server-name)" section of the "Microsoft Exchange - OVHcloud email hub" page.
    - **Domain**: leave this field empty.
    - **Username**: your full email address. This field is required: the connection cannot start while it is empty.

    Tap <code className="action">✓</code>, in the upper right of the screen, to log in.

    </div>
    <div>

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Outlook for Android, Exchange account settings with the server ex?.mail.ovh.net" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/how-to-configure-outlook-android/outlook-android-08-exchange-settings.png" loading="lazy" />

    </div>
    </CardGrid>
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 4">
    Once you are connected, Outlook asks whether you want to add another account. Tap <code className="action">MAYBE LATER</code>, then choose whether to turn on notifications.

    Your inbox appears.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

:::warning
Do not configure your Exchange address as **IMAP** or **POP3**: only the **Exchange** account type syncs your calendar and contacts.
:::

:::tip
If the "**Unable to log in**" message appears after step 3, check your password. After a failed attempt, Outlook clears the **Server** and **Username** fields: fill them in again before you retry.
:::

:::info
With an Exchange account, the Outlook for Android app does not connect directly to your server: a Microsoft cloud service syncs your emails, calendar and contacts between the Exchange server and the app. For more information, see the [Microsoft documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/exchange/clients/outlook-for-ios-and-android/use-basic-auth).
:::

### Use the email address

Once your email address is configured, you can send and receive messages, and view your calendar and contacts in the Outlook app.

:::tip
OVHcloud offers a web application that allows you to access your email address from a web browser: the [webmail](/links/web/email). You can log in using your email credentials.
:::

### Change the account settings <a name="modify-settings"></a>

You cannot change the server settings of an account already configured in the Outlook app. To change them, remove the account from the app, then add it again:

<CardGrid columns={2}>
<div>

1. Tap your account icon, in the upper left of the screen.
2. Tap the settings icon (<code className="action">⛭</code>), at the bottom of the panel that opens.
3. In the "**General**" section, tap <code className="action">Accounts</code>, then the account concerned.
4. Tap <code className="action">Remove account</code>, then confirm the removal.

Removing the account deletes it from the app, along with the data stored on your device. Your emails stay on the Exchange server.

If you have sync issues, first tap <code className="action">Reset account</code> on the same page.

</div>
<div>

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Outlook for Android, Exchange account settings with Remove account" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/how-to-configure-outlook-android/outlook-android-09-delete-account.png" loading="lazy" />

</div>
</CardGrid>

:::tip
Once your account is removed, follow the steps in the "[Add the account](#add-account)" section of this guide.
:::
