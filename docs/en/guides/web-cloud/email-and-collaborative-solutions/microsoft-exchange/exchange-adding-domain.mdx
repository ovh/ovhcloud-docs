## Instructions

### Add a domain name

1. Click on the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab of your Exchange or Email Pro service.
1. The table will show you the domain names currently associated with your service.
1. Click the <code className="action">Add a domain</code> button above the table.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Associated domains tab of an Exchange service, with the Add a domain button above the table" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-adding-domain/console/add_domain_exchange_step1.png" loading="lazy" />

:::warning
By default, all email accounts on a service are interconnected. All of the addresses created on your email service will be able to view all of the service’s addresses in the directory, including those with a different domain name.

<Region zones={['eu']}>

To unlink the display of domains, you need to order another [Exchange or Email Pro](/links/web/emails) service for the domain name(s) concerned.

</Region>

<Region zones={['ca']}>

To unlink the display of domains, you need to order another [Exchange](/links/web/emails-exchange) service for the domain name(s) concerned.

</Region>

:::


The **Add a domain** panel opens on the right of the page. You have two options:

- **Select a domain from the list**: Choose your domain name from the **Domain name** drop-down menu. It lists the domain names that you fully manage (or at least whose DNS zone you manage) in the OVHcloud Console.

- **Enter a domain name not managed by your OVH account**: Enter your domain name in the **Domain name** field. You must be able to modify this domain name's DNS zone so that the service can be configured.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Add a domain panel open on the right of the Associated domains tab" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-adding-domain/console/add_domain_exchange_step2.png" loading="lazy" />

Once you have specified the domain name, the **Configuration** section appears just below it, in the same panel: choose the domain's configuration mode there.

- **If you have selected from the list a domain name managed by your OVHcloud account**: You can choose between 2 modes.
    - **Recommended configuration**: Your DNS zone is configured automatically. This is suitable if you do not have a specific configuration in your DNS zone for the MX, SPF, DKIM and SRV records. All emails sent to your domain name are then routed to this service as soon as the domain is added.
    - **Custom configuration**: This is suitable if you have already configured an email solution on your domain name. You can choose the elements that interest you: the 4 automatic configuration boxes are ticked by default. Untick those of the records you want to configure yourself.
        - If you would like to use a private email service or one external to OVHcloud in addition to this email service, enter the host name of the email server in the **Target email server URL** field.
        - *Automatically configure the MX record (reception)*: Automatically adds the OVHcloud receiving servers (applies to all OVHcloud email solutions).
        - *Automatically configure the SRV record (autodiscover)*: Enables email software to automatically configure Exchange accounts on your domain name.
        - *Automatically configure the SPF record*: Automatically adds the SPF record to authorise OVHcloud email sending servers to transmit your emails. This record is valid for all OVHcloud email solutions.
        - *Automatically configure and enable the DKIM record*: Automatically adds the records required to authenticate your email sending.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Custom configuration with the Target email server URL field and the DNS boxes" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-adding-domain/console/add_domain_exchange_step2-1b.png" loading="lazy" />

- **If you have entered a domain name that is not managed by your OVHcloud account**: This means that the domain name, and more specifically its DNS zone, is not managed from the OVHcloud Console. It can also be registered with another registrar. You must then configure it directly from its management interface, whatever mode you choose next.
    - **Recommended configuration**: This is suitable if you only use OVHcloud email solutions.

    - **Custom configuration**: The **Target email server URL** field and the 4 boxes are displayed as for a domain name from the list. If you would like to use a private email service or one external to OVHcloud in addition to this email service, enter the host name of the email server in this field.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Add a domain panel with a domain name that is not managed entered" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-adding-domain/console/add_domain_exchange_step2-2.png" loading="lazy" />

Once you have made your choices, click the <code className="action">Add</code> button at the bottom of the panel to confirm the domain addition.

### Configure the domain name (DNS zone)

Once you have added the domain name, check its configuration in the table. In the **Diagnostic** column, a green badge indicates that the domain name is correctly configured.

If a badge is red or orange:

- **If you chose automatic configuration when you added your domain**: It may take a few moments for the display in the OVHcloud Console to refresh.

- **If you have entered a domain name that is not managed by your OVHcloud account**:
    - If the `CNAME` badge is orange (domain name being validated), refer to our guide "[Creating a CNAME record to validate your domain name on your email solution](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-dns-cname)".
    - Once the domain name has been validated, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the domain name's row, then <code className="action">Diagnostics</code> to display the changes you need to make. The page that opens has one tab per record (`MX`, `SRV`, `SPF` and `DKIM`), with its status and the records to create in the DNS zone. If this domain name does not use the OVHcloud configuration (its DNS servers), you will need to make the changes from your domain name's management interface.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Table of the Associated domains tab with the badges in the Diagnostic column" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-adding-domain/console/add_domain_exchange_step4.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
Modifying a domain name's configuration can take between 4 and 24 hours to propagate fully.

:::


To check a domain name's configuration, go back to the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab of your service. If the badge is now green, the domain name is correctly configured. Otherwise, the propagation may not be complete yet.

### Modify the mode of an associated domain

You can change the mode of an associated domain on your service. First, it is necessary to understand the difference in operation between authoritative and non-authoritative modes.

:::info
**Authoritative / non-authoritative**

- Choosing the **authoritative** mode on your email service (*Server A*) involves hosting all of your domain name’s email addresses on this service.<br/>For example, if you send an email to the address *mary.johnson@mydomain.ovh*, the *Server A* will return a failure message to the sender because this address does not exist on the *Server A*.<br/><br/>

- The **non-authoritative** mode on your email service (*Server A*) allows distribution of your domain name’s email addresses between your primary email service (*Server A*) and another email service (*Server B*).<br/>For example, if you send an email to the address *mary.johnson@mydomain.ovh*, *Server A* will send the email to *Server B* so that it can deliver it.<br/>

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Authoritative" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-adding-domain/add_domain_exchange_authoritative.png" loading="lazy" />

:::


1. Click on the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab.
1. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the domain name concerned.
1. Click <code className="action">Configuration</code>.
1. In the **Domain configuration** window, select the mode you want from the **Mode** drop-down menu: **Authoritative** or **Non-authoritative**. In **Non-authoritative** mode, also fill in the **Target email server** field.
1. Click <code className="action">Next</code>, check the summary, then click <code className="action">Configure</code>.

:::warning
If a domain name is already declared in **authoritative** mode on another email platform, you cannot add it with the **Recommended configuration**. For the two platforms to coexist, the domain name must be in **non-authoritative** mode on each of them: choose the **Custom configuration** when you add it, then switch it to **non-authoritative** mode on the other platform.

:::


### Configure and use accounts

<Region zones={['eu']}>

Now that you have added the domain names to your service, you can configure your email accounts with them. You can do this in the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab, in the **Email account** sub-tab opened by default (the <code className="action">Accounts</code> tab on Email Pro). If required, you can order additional accounts with the <code className="action">Order accounts</code> button (Hosted Exchange and Email Pro) or create them with the <code className="action">Add an account</code> button (Private Exchange).

</Region>

<Region zones={['ca']}>

Now that you have added the domain names to your service, you can configure your email accounts with them. You can do this in the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab, in the **Email account** sub-tab opened by default (the <code className="action">Accounts</code> tab on Email Pro). If required, you can order additional accounts with the <code className="action">Order accounts</code> button (Hosted Exchange and Email Pro).

</Region>

As a reminder, all of the addresses created on your service will be able to view all of the addresses for that service in the directory, including those with a different domain name.

Once the accounts are fully configured, you can start using them. To do this, OVHcloud offers **webmail**, accessible [here](/links/web/email). To optimise the use of your email accounts on a software application, ensure that it is compatible with the service.

</Region>

### Delete a domain name from a service

<Region zones={['eu']}>

If you would like to remove a domain name from your Exchange or Email Pro service, you will need to ensure that it is not linked to any email accounts, aliases, resources, shared accounts (only on Exchange), groups, external contacts or footers that are still configured. In this case, you will need to **attach these accounts to another domain name** on your service, or **delete them**.

</Region>

<Region zones={['ca']}>

If you would like to remove a domain name from your Exchange service, you will need to ensure that it is not linked to any email accounts, aliases, resources, shared accounts (only on Exchange), groups, external contacts or footers that are still configured. In this case, you will need to **attach these accounts to another domain name** on your service, or **delete them**.

</Region>

:::warning
Before deleting email accounts, make sure they are not being used. You may need to back up these accounts. If necessary, please refer to our guide "[Manually migrate your email address](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/manual-email-migration)", which describes how to export account data from the OVHcloud Console or an email client.

:::


Go to the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab of your service. In the table, the **Accounts** column shows the number of accounts associated with each domain name.

You cannot delete a domain name as long as email accounts are attached to it. In this case, you have 2 options:

**Attach accounts to another domain name**:

1. Go to the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab (<code className="action">Accounts</code> on Email Pro).
1. To the right of the account you want to modify, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button.
1. Click <code className="action">Edit</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Account management tab, ⋮ menu of an Exchange account open on the Edit entry" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-adding-domain/console/add_domain_exchange_step8.png" loading="lazy" />

1. On the **Edit an Exchange account** page (**Edit an Email Pro account** panel on Email Pro), select the account's new domain name from the drop-down menu that follows the `@` in the **Email account** field.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Edit an Exchange account page, list of domain names in the Email account field" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-adding-domain/console/add_domain_exchange_step9.png" loading="lazy" />

**Delete accounts from your service**:

1. Go to the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab (<code className="action">Accounts</code> on Email Pro).
1. To the right of the account you want to delete, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button.
1. Click <code className="action">Reset this account</code> (Hosted Exchange) or <code className="action">Reset</code> (Email Pro).

<Region zones={['eu']}>

On a Private Exchange service, click <code className="action">Delete this account</code> in step 3.

</Region>

<img className="thumbnail" alt="⋮ menu of an account in the Account management tab, Reset this account entry" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-adding-domain/console/add_domain_exchange_step7.png" loading="lazy" />

Once the accounts have been attached to another domain name, reset or deleted, you can delete the domain name.

In the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab of your service, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the domain name concerned, then <code className="action">Delete this domain</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="⋮ menu of a domain name in the Associated domains tab, with the Delete this domain action" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-adding-domain/console/add_domain_exchange_step10.png" loading="lazy" />
