## Objective

Exchange groups enable multiple participants to communicate by sending emails to a unique group address. With this collaborative feature you can create and manage mailing lists that include Exchange users as well as external contacts.

**This guide explains how to use Exchange groups via the OVHcloud Console and Outlook Web App (OWA).**

## Requirements

- A subscription to an [OVHcloud Exchange solution](/links/web/emails-exchange)

## Instructions

### Creating a new group

Click the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab, then the <code className="action">Groups</code> sub-tab.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Groups sub-tab of an Exchange service, with the Create a contact group button above the table" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-groups/console/exchange-groups-create01.png" loading="lazy" />

Click the <code className="action">Create a contact group</code> button, above the table, to open the **Create a contact group** panel and define the group's settings:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Create a contact group panel open to the right of the Groups sub-tab" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-groups/console/exchange-groups-create02.png" loading="lazy" />

- **Group email address**: Set a new address that will send messages to the mailing list. Enter the part before the `@`, then select the domain from the drop-down list that follows it. Make sure the address does not already exist.
- **Display name**: Enter the group name that will appear in the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink> and in your [OVHcloud webmail](/links/web/email) (OWA).
- **Company**: This field is optional.
- **Maximum receive size (MB)** and **Maximum send size (MB)**: Enter the maximum size of incoming and outgoing emails. The **Maximum value (100)** box, ticked by default under each field, applies the maximum size allowed (100 MB): untick it to enter a lower value.
- **Subscription management** and **Unsubscription management**: These two mandatory settings define whether users can join or leave the group themselves (**Open**, the default setting) or not (**Closed**). The **Subscription management** list also offers **Moderated**: each subscription request must then be approved.
- **Hide address in directory**: If this box is ticked, the group address will not appear in the Exchange service's directory.
- **Sender authentication required**: If this box is ticked, only users on the same platform will be able to send a message to the group address.

Click the <code className="action">Create</code> button, at the bottom of the panel, to create the group.

The contacts and administrators of the group are designated once the group has been created, using the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the group in the table, then the <code className="action">Configure users</code> option (see the section "[Managing users in a group](#managing-users-in-a-group)"). The choices will only include email addresses and external contacts that are already created on the service.

- **Administrator**: Email account authorised to send an email to all group contacts.
- **Contact**: Email account or external contact that will receive emails sent to the group by administrators.

:::info
Please note that administrators must be configured as **Contact** in order to receive group emails.

:::

### Managing groups

To adjust a group's settings after it has been created, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the group in the table, then <code className="action">Configure group</code>. The **Configure group** panel opens, filled in with the current values. Once you have made your changes, click <code className="action">Configure</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="⋮ menu of a group in the Groups sub-tab, with Configure group as the first entry" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-groups/console/exchange-groups-options01.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Managing users in a group

To add contacts to your group or define its administrators, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the group in the table, then <code className="action">Configure users</code>.

The **Users of group …** page first lists the accounts of the first domain of your service. To display another domain, or all of them, select the domain you want or **All domains** in the **Filter by domain** drop-down list. For each address in the **Email address** column, tick the **Administrator** box, the **Contact** box or both, depending on the role to give to the account. External contacts cannot be group administrators.

Once you have made your choices, click the <code className="action">Save</code> button.

:::info
A group can contain a maximum number of 10,000 contacts.

:::


<img className="thumbnail" alt="Users of group page, with the Administrator and Contact columns to tick" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-groups/console/exchange-group-options-users01.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Managing group delegations

To delegate permissions on the group to other accounts, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the group in the table, then <code className="action">Configure delegations</code>. The **Delegation for group …** page works like the one for an Exchange account, with two permissions: **Send As** and **Send on Behalf**. For more details, see the guide "[Delegating permissions on an email account](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/permissions-delegation)".

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Delegation for group page, with the Send As and Send on Behalf checkboxes" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-groups/console/exchange-groups-options-delegation01.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
Please note that any changes to this service may take a few minutes to be applied. You can check the status of most operations by clicking the <code className="action">Operations</code> tab of your service.

:::


### Messaging a group in OWA

You can now test your mailing list via [OVHcloud webmail](/links/web/email) (OWA): simply send an email to the group address.
