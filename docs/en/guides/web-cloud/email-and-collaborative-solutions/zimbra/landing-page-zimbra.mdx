## What is Zimbra?

[OVHcloud Zimbra](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/emails/) is an open-source collaborative email solution that brings together your emails, shared calendars and contacts in a single interface, with a modern webmail and mobile device synchronisation. Find all the guides here to configure and manage your Zimbra email solution.

With Zimbra, you can:

- Access your email from the Zimbra webmail, wherever you are
- Configure your mailboxes in an email client on your computer or mobile device
- Synchronise your emails, calendars and contacts on your mobile devices
- Migrate an MX Plan email address to a Zimbra account
- Share folders and synchronise your calendars via CalDAV and WebDAV

**Settings for manual configuration** (username = your full email address):

| Server | Host | Security | Port |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Incoming — IMAP | `imap.mail.ovh.net` or `ssl0.ovh.net` | SSL/TLS | 993 |
| Incoming — POP | `pop.mail.ovh.net` or `ssl0.ovh.net` | SSL/TLS | 995 |
| Outgoing — SMTP | `smtp.mail.ovh.net` or `ssl0.ovh.net` | SSL/TLS | 465 |
