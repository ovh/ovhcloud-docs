### Configure your Zimbra service <a name="zimbra-conf"></a>

Before you start configuring your Zimbra email accounts, take note of the three elements that structure your Zimbra service hierarchically:

- [**Organisation**](#organizations): It enables domain names to be grouped together in order to associate them.
- [**Domain name**](#domains): It is essential to create an email account. You must add at least one domain name to your Zimbra service.
- [**Email accounts**](#emails): By using the domain names added to your Zimbra service, you can create an email address.

:::info
An *Organisation* is used to represent an entity (company, association, personal project, etc.). It enables email accounts to be partitioned, specific security policies to be applied (upcoming feature) and rights to be delegated to an organisation (upcoming feature). By using organisations, you can make it easier to browse and manage your Zimbra platform.

:::


The diagram below summarizes the hierarchical link between the above-mentioned elements.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="zimbra" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/getting-started-zimbra/zimbra_organization.png" loading="lazy" />

### Organisations <a name="organizations"></a>

If you are adding a large number of domain names to your Zimbra service, it may be useful to group them together by associating them with an "organisation". The **Zimbra** page of the OVHcloud Console lists your organisations: for each one, the table shows its domain names and its number of email accounts.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Zimbra page of the OVHcloud Console, table of organisations" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/getting-started-zimbra/console/zimbra_organization_tab.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Create an organisation <a name="organizations-create"></a>

To create an organisation, click <code className="action">Create a new organisation</code>, above the table of organisations. In the **Add an organisation** window, enter the **Name** of the organisation and its **Label** (2 to 12 characters), a short description that allows you to identify it when you filter the display of domain names and email accounts of your Zimbra service. Then click <code className="action">Add</code>.

#### Filter by organisation <a name="organizations-filters"></a>

To display only the items linked to an organisation, click its label in the table on the **Zimbra** page. Your Zimbra service then opens filtered on that organisation: the <code className="action">Email account</code> and <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tabs only display its email accounts and its domain names.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Table of organisations on the Zimbra page, with the label of an organisation highlighted" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/getting-started-zimbra/console/zimbra_organization_filter.png" loading="lazy" />

The filter remains applied when you switch from one tab to another. To remove the filter, click the name of your Zimbra service in the breadcrumb trail at the top of the page.

### Domains <a name="domains"></a>

:::warning
For optimal operation when you use the same domain name between OVHcloud solutions [Exchange](/links/web/emails-hosted-exchange), [Email Pro](/links/web/email-pro) and Zimbra, it is necessary to configure the domain name in **Non-authoritative** mode. To find out how to configure a domain name in non-authoritative mode on an Exchange or Email Pro platform, please read our guide "[How to add a domain name to your Exchange service](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-adding-domain)".

:::


The <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab lists the domain names added to your Zimbra service.

Its table gives the following information, among others:

- **Organisation**: the label of the organisation chosen when the domain name was added.
- **Number of accounts**: the number of email accounts created under this domain name.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Associated domains tab of a Zimbra service, table of domain names" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/getting-started-zimbra/console/zimbra_domain_tab.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Add a domain name <a name="domains-add"></a>

:::warning
You need to [create an organisation](#organizations) to add a domain name to your Zimbra service.

:::


To add a domain name to your Zimbra service, click on the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab, then click <code className="action">Add a domain</code>.

In the **Add a domain** panel, select an organisation from the **Organisation** drop-down menu, then select one of the following two options:

- **Select a domain from the list** (internal domain): The list shows the domain names that you manage in the OVHcloud Console.
- **Enter a domain name that is not managed by your OVHcloud account** (external domain): Enter a domain name that is not managed in the OVHcloud Console, or that is registered with a different registrar and managed by you.

Select the tab that corresponds to your choice:

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Internal domain">
    Select a domain name managed in the OVHcloud Console from the **Domain name** list.
    
    To configure your <Tooltip term="dnsZone">DNS zone</Tooltip>, select one of the two options in the **Configuration** section:
    
    - **Recommended configuration**: Your DNS zone will be configured automatically. This option is suitable if you have not configured an email solution on your domain name.
    - **Custom configuration**: If you have already configured an email solution on your domain name, you can choose the elements that interest you.
       - *Configure MX record automatically (receive emails)*: This allows you to enter the OVHcloud incoming servers automatically (applies to all OVHcloud email solutions).
       - *Configure the SPF record automatically*: This allows you to enter the record automatically, authorising the OVHcloud sending email servers to send your emails. This record is valid for all OVHcloud email solutions.
       - *Configure the DKIM record automatically*: This allows you to automatically enter the records required to authenticate your email sending.
       - *Configure the SRV record automatically (autodiscover)*: This allows the automatic configuration of the parameters of an email account when you add it to an email software (Outlook, Mail for Mac, Thunderbird, etc.).
    
    Click <code className="action">Add</code> to finish adding your domain and start the configuration process.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="External domain">
    Enter in the **Domain name** field a domain name that is not managed in the OVHcloud Console. Make sure that you can modify the DNS zone of the domain name concerned.
    
    Then click <code className="action">Add</code>.
    
    The domain name appears in the table of the **Associated domains** tab, with the **CNAME** badge in its **Status** column. Hover over this badge to display the **Domain verification** box, which shows the <Tooltip term="cname">CNAME</Tooltip> record to create: you need to enter this record in the DNS zone of the domain name so that it can be validated on your Zimbra platform. Click the <code className="action">CNAME</code> badge to open the **Verify** page, which details the record.
    
    :::warning
    If the CNAME record is not visible in the DNS zone after 48 hours, the operation is cancelled. You will then need to retry the operation.

    :::

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

#### Modify a domain name <a name="domains-modify"></a>

You can modify your domain name to change its organisation or to check its associated DNS records.

From the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab of your Zimbra service, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the domain name concerned to display the options.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Associated domains tab of a Zimbra service, menu of a domain name open" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/getting-started-zimbra/console/zimbra_domain_modify01.png" loading="lazy" />

- Click <code className="action">Edit</code> to modify the organisation associated with your domain name: in the **Edit the domain** window, select the organisation in the **Organisation** list, then click <code className="action">Edit</code>.
- Click <code className="action">Diagnostic</code> to display the diagnostic page for the DNS records of the domain name. Check that none of the **MX**, **SRV**, **SPF** and **DKIM** tabs displays an alert, and follow the instructions in each tab with an alert to configure the DNS record concerned:
    - **MX**: essential for receiving your emails.
    - **SPF**: security record that is required by the majority of recipient email servers to legitimise OVHcloud email sending servers with your domain name.
    - **DKIM**: provides a signature system for each email sent by your Zimbra service. The signature is verified by the recipient using the public key visible in your DNS zone.
    - **SRV**: facilitates the configuration of your Zimbra account when you add it to an email software (Outlook, Mail for Mac, Thunderbird, etc.).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Diagnostic page of a domain name, SRV tab showing an alert" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/getting-started-zimbra/console/zimbra_domain_modify02.png" loading="lazy" />

### Email accounts <a name="emails"></a>

You can manage your Zimbra service email addresses from the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab. Its table lists the email accounts on your service and gives, among other things, the following for each one:

- **Organisation**: if your email account domain name is linked to an organisation, you will automatically find its label in this column.
- **Service plan**: this column shows the solution associated with the account, since your Zimbra service can host several.
- **Used quota**: this column shows the total capacity of your email account and the space it occupies.

At the top of this page, in the **Sign in to Webmail** section, you will also find a link to [webmail](/links/web/email), so that you can log in directly to the content of your email account via your web browser.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Email account tab of a Zimbra service, table of email accounts" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/getting-started-zimbra/console/zimbra_emailaccounts_tab.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Create an email account <a name="emails-create"></a>

To create an email account on your Zimbra service, click on the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab, then <code className="action">New account</code> and finally <code className="action">Configure an account</code>.

On the **Configure an email account** page, fill in the following fields:

- **Email address**: enter the *account name* that your email address will contain (your "first name.surname", for example), and *select a domain name* in the dropdown menu.

:::warning
The name of your email address must meet the following conditions:

- Minimum 2 characters.
- Maximum 32 characters.
- No accents.
- No special characters, except for the following characters: `.`, `+`, `-` and `_`. Two of these characters cannot follow each other.
- The part before the "@" must end with a letter or a digit.

:::


- **Name**: enter a name.
- **First name**: enter a first name.
- **Display name**: enter the name that will appear as a sender when emails are sent from this address.
- **Password**: set a strong password of at least 10 characters, including an upper-case letter, a digit and a special character, or generate one with the key icon to the right of the field. For security reasons, do not use the same password twice, choose one that has no relation to your personal information (for example, avoid your surname, first name and date of birth) and change it regularly. The **Force change on next connection** option, ticked by default, asks the account user to change this password at their next login.

:::warning
The password must meet the following requirements:

- Minimum 10 characters.
- Maximum 64 characters.
- Minimum 1 upper case.
- Minimum 1 digit.
- Minimum 1 special character.
- No accents.

:::


- **Service plan**: select the account's plan from the drop-down menu (Zimbra Starter or Zimbra Pro), or **Included with a domain name** to use a service included with one of your domain names (see [Choose the service of a new account](#services-choice)). The number of accounts still available is shown for each choice.

Click <code className="action">Create</code> to start creating the account.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Configure an email account page of a Zimbra service" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/getting-started-zimbra/console/zimbra_emailaccounts_add.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Change plan <a name="emails-offer"></a>

It is possible to upgrade or downgrade any Zimbra account.

1. Click on the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab.
1. To the right of the email account concerned, click on <code className="action">⋮</code>.
1. Click on <code className="action">Change plan</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Email account tab of a Zimbra service, menu of an email account open" src="/images/assets/screens/email-clients/zimbra/console/zimbra-change-offer.png" loading="lazy" />

1. On the **Select your new plan** page, choose the plan you want, then click <code className="action">Continue my order</code>.

:::warning
Before switching to a lower plan, make sure the following points:

- No file is stored on your storage volume "Briefcase" if you are switching to the Starter plan.
- The content of your email account must be less than 15 GB if you are switching to the Starter plan.

:::


### Services <a name="services"></a>

Each email account uses a service, that is, a slot of the Starter or Pro plan you have ordered. To display your services, click <code className="action">Manage my services</code> in the **Subscription** section of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab of your Zimbra service. The **Services** page shows how many accounts are configured for each plan (for example **Starter** "3 / 3 accounts configured") and lists your services with, for each one:

- **Email account**: the account that uses this service;
- **Cost/mailbox**: "Free" for a service linked to a domain name;
- **Linked service**: the domain name the service is attached to.

The table also shows the service ID, the plan, the renewal and the renewal date of each service. The search on this page applies to the address of the email accounts, the service IDs and the linked domain names.

#### Choose the service of a new account <a name="services-choice"></a>

In the form for creating an email account, the **Service plan** drop-down menu determines the service on which the account is placed:

- **Zimbra Starter** or **Zimbra Pro**: the account is placed on the first free service of that plan that is not linked to any domain name;
- **Included with a domain name**: then choose the service in the **Linked domain name** drop-down menu, where each service is shown with its domain name and its plan.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Configure an email account form, Service plan drop-down menu open with the choices Zimbra Starter, Zimbra Pro and Included with a domain name" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/getting-started-zimbra/console/zimbra-account-service-plan-list.png" loading="lazy" />

If the domain name of the address has an included service that is still free, the OVHcloud Console selects it automatically.

You can also start from the [Services](#services) page: click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button of a free service, then <code className="action">Configure account</code>. The creation form opens on that service.

You can also place the account on a specific service with the OVHcloud API.

First, retrieve the service ID (`id`) and the domain name it is linked to (`currentState.domainPromotionLink`) with the following API call:

<Api version="v2" section="/zimbra" method="GET" route={"/zimbra/platform/\{platformId\}/slot"} />

Then create the account, entering this ID in the `targetSpec.slotId` field:

<Api version="v2" section="/zimbra" method="POST" route={"/zimbra/platform/\{platformId\}/account"} />

:::info
The service of an account cannot be changed after the account is created.
:::

### Redirections <a name="redirections"></a>

To create a redirection for a Zimbra email address, log in to [webmail](/links/web/email).  
You can create a redirection using inbox rules, called filters in webmail. These rules, which are applied when an email is received, can be used to forward or redirect an email.

To redirect emails from your Zimbra account to another email address, we will apply a transfer rule. Follow the tabs below to set up your redirection.

:::info
In our example below, we have chosen to redirect all incoming emails to another email address. To understand the example in the screenshots, we are logged on to the account **zimbra@mydomain.ovh** and we would like to redirect emails from this address to **address@example.com**.

:::


<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Step 1">
    Click the &#9881; button in the top right-hand corner of your webmail window, then click <code className="action">Settings</code>.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="zimbra" src="/images/assets/screens/email-clients/zimbra/zimbra_settings01.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 2">
    Click the <code className="action">Filters</code> section in the settings window, then click the <code className="action">Add a filter</code> button.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="zimbra" src="/images/assets/screens/email-clients/zimbra/zimbra_redirection02.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Step 3">
    - First click <u>Advanced Mode</u> in the top right-hand corner to set up this rule.
    - Enter a name for your filter in the `Filter name` box.
    - Leave the dropdown menu on `all` in the sentence "If an incoming message meets ... of these conditions".
    - In the first dropdown menu of the rules, choose `To`, leave `contains`, then enter the source email address in the box to the right.
    - Under "Then", select `Forward to` from the drop-down menu and enter the destination email address.
    - Click <code className="action">+ Add an action</code> below, then select `Keep in Inbox`.
    - Click <code className="action">Save</code> from your filter window and also from the settings window.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Automatic replies <a name="autoreply"></a>

When you need to leave the office and cannot process your emails, you can set up an absence message. Follow the steps below:

- Click the &#9881; button in the top right-hand corner of your webmail window, then click <code className="action">Settings</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="zimbra" src="/images/assets/screens/email-clients/zimbra/zimbra_settings01.png" loading="lazy" />

- Click on the `Out of Office` section in the settings window.
- Tick the box "Enable automatic reply during these dates (included)".
- Enter the absence start date before the "From" comment.
- Untick the "No end date" box if you want to determine an absence end date and determine it.
- In the box, enter your absence message.
- Click <code className="action">Save</code> to finish setting up your absence message.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="zimbra" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/getting-started-zimbra/zimbra_autoreply01.png" loading="lazy" />

:::warning
Emails that a filter forwards with the <code className="action">Forward to</code> action alone do not stay in your inbox, and their senders do not receive your automatic reply. Add the <code className="action">Keep in Inbox</code> action to this filter (see the "[Redirections](#redirections)" section).
:::
