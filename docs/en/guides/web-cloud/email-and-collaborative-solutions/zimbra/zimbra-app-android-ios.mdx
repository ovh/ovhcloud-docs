## Objective

The official Zimbra app lets you access your Zimbra Pro email account and keep all its collaborative data — emails, folders, calendars and contacts — in sync in a single, free application. The Zimbra app is available on Android and iOS.

**Find out how to configure your Zimbra Pro email account on the official Zimbra app.**

[[fragment:support-scope]]

## Requirements

- A [Zimbra Pro](/links/web/zimbra) account
- The Zimbra app installed on your device (see the download links below)
- Login details for the email account you would like to configure

## Instructions

### Configuring your email account

Follow the steps below to configure your email account:

1. When you first launch the application, enter your **Zimbra Pro email address** and the associated **password**, then press <code className="action">Login</code>.

   <img className="thumbnail" alt="Zimbra app login screen" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-app-android-ios/zimbra-app-login.png" loading="lazy" style={{ maxWidth: '280px' }} />

2. You now have access to the content of your email account. By default, your inbox appears.

   <img className="thumbnail" alt="Zimbra app inbox" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-app-android-ios/zimbra-app-inbox.png" loading="lazy" style={{ maxWidth: '280px' }} />

:::tip
The Zimbra app interface is similar to the webmail interface. For more details on the features and settings, please read our [How to use Zimbra webmail](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-zimbra) guide.
:::

### Synchronising your emails, folders, calendars and contacts

Because the Zimbra app is the native client for your Zimbra Pro account, it synchronises all your collaborative data at once — no extra configuration is required. Once logged in, the following data is kept up to date on your device:

- **Emails and folders**: your inbox and your full folder tree.
- **Calendars**: your events and appointments.
- **Contacts**: your address book.

Use the menu <code className="action">☰</code> in the upper left corner of the screen to switch between your emails, calendars and contacts.

### View another Zimbra email account <a name="modify-settings"></a>

The Zimbra app only allows you to configure one email account at a time. To view another Zimbra email account, log out of the one you are currently using.

Select the profile icon at the top right of your screen, then press <code className="action">Logout</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Zimbra app profile menu showing the Logout option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/zimbra-app-android-ios/zimbra-app-profile-logout.png" loading="lazy" style={{ maxWidth: '280px' }} />

### What if a blank screen appears when I launch the application?

This is a loading error caused by network connectivity. We recommend waiting a few moments for the application to finish attempting to connect.

If nothing happens, check that your mobile network connectivity is correct, then close the application completely and restart it. Repeat as necessary.

If the problem persists, reinstall the application. This will reset the settings and eliminate potential corruption in the application configuration file.
