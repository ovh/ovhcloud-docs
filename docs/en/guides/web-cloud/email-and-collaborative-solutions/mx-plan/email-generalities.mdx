## Objective

If you have just purchased an MX Plan solution, this means you have email addresses that you can use to send and receive messages from a device of your choice.

**Find out how to get started with an MX Plan solution.**

<Region zones={['eu']}>

:::warning
On MX Plan, it is not possible to switch from one email technology to another (for example, between Roundcube, OWA and Zimbra). The email technology assigned to your service is fixed and cannot be changed.
:::

</Region>

### Create an email address <a name="create-email"></a>

To find out how to create an email address, click on the tab corresponding to the email technology used by your MX Plan service:

<ZoneTabs>
  <Tab label="Roundcube" availableIn={['eu']}>
    To create an email address, click the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab. Its table lists the accounts already created. To add a new email account, click the <code className="action">Add account</code> button, above the table.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Email account tab of an MX Plan service, with the Add account button above the table" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-creation/console/mxplan-creation-legacy-step2.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Add account** panel that opens, enter the following information:
    
    - **Account name**: Enter the part of the address before "@" (for example, first name.surname); the domain name is already shown after the field. The name must contain between 2 and 32 characters, with no spaces or accents; only the characters ".", "-" and "_" are allowed.
    - **Description**: Optional information about the email address, visible only in the **Description** column of the table in the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab.
    - **Account size**: Select the size to assign to the email account.
    - **Password**: [Enter a password](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/manage-ovh-password) or generate one using the key icon to the right of the field. The password must contain between 9 and 30 characters, with no accented characters. For security reasons, we recommend not using the same password twice, choosing one that does not contain any personal information (e.g. your surname, first name and date of birth), and renewing it regularly.
    
    Once you have filled in the fields, click <code className="action">Add</code>. Repeat this step as necessary, depending on the number of accounts you have.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Add account panel open on the Email account tab of an MX Plan service" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-creation/console/mxplan-creation-legacy-step3.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Zimbra and OWA" availableIn={['eu']}>
    To create an email address, click the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab. Its table lists the accounts already created. To add a new email account, click the <code className="action">Add account</code> button.
    
In the **Add account** panel that opens, enter the following information:
    
    - **Email address**: Enter your new email address (e.g. your first name.surname). The domain name is already shown after the field.
    - **First name**: Enter a first name.
    - **Last name**: Enter a last name.
    - **Display name**: Enter the name that will be displayed as a sender when emails are sent from this address.
    - **Account size**: Select the size to assign to the email account.
    - **Password**: [Enter a password](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/manage-ovh-password). The password must contain at least 10 characters, including 1 digit, 1 special character (`$`, `!`, `&`, …) and 1 uppercase letter. For security reasons, we recommend not using the same password twice, choosing one that does not contain any personal information (e.g. your surname, first name and date of birth), and renewing it regularly.
    
    Once you have filled in the fields, click <code className="action">Add</code>. Repeat this step as necessary, depending on the number of accounts you have.
  </Tab>
</ZoneTabs>

### Redirections and Aliases <a name="redirection-alias"></a>

Do you want to redirect your emails to another recipient, create an alias or systematically copy another email address?

To do this, click on the tab corresponding to your email technology:

<ZoneTabs>
  <Tab label="Roundcube" availableIn={['eu']}>
    To add a redirection or an alias, click the <code className="action">Redirections</code> tab of your MX Plan service.
    The table of redirections that are already active appears. Above it, click the <code className="action">Create a redirection</code> button.
    
    - **From address**: Enter the email address you would like to redirect.
    - **To address**: Enter the destination address of your redirection. This can be one of your OVHcloud email addresses, or an external email address.
    - **Keep a local copy**: Tick this box if you would like to keep a copy of the email received at the original address (**From address**). Otherwise, the email is only forwarded to the destination address (**To address**).
    
    Once you have filled in the fields, click <code className="action">Create</code>.
    
    To understand how to use redirections and aliases on your MX Plan service, please read our full guide: "[Using email aliases and redirections](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/feature-redirections)".
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="OWA and Zimbra" availableIn={['eu']}>
    When you are using **OWA** or **Zimbra**, you can do this in two ways:
    
    1. **Create your redirection via webmail**: Use the inbox rules or filters of the webmail, which forward or redirect an email as soon as it is received. Please follow our guide "[Creating inbox rules in OWA (MX Plan)](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/creating-inbox-rules-in-owa-mx-plan)" or read the "[Filters](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-zimbra#filters)" section of our guide "[How to use Zimbra webmail](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-zimbra)".
    
    2. **Create your redirection or alias via the OVHcloud Console**: To add a redirection or an alias, click the <code className="action">Redirections</code> tab. The table of redirections that are already active appears. Above it, click the <code className="action">Create a redirection</code> button.
    
        - **From address**: Enter the email address you would like to redirect.
        - **To address**: Enter the destination address of your redirection. This can be one of your OVHcloud email addresses, or an external email address.
        - **Keep a local copy**: Tick this box if you would like to keep a copy of the email received at the original address (**From address**). Otherwise, the email is only forwarded to the destination address (**To address**).
    
        Once you have filled in the fields, click <code className="action">Create</code>.
    
    To understand how to use redirections and aliases on your MX Plan service, please read our full guide: "[Using email aliases and redirections](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/feature-redirections)".
  </Tab>
</ZoneTabs>


### Automatic reply <a name="autoreply"></a>

It is important to be able to set up an automatic response in case you cannot view or process your emails.

Select the tab corresponding to the email technology of your MX Plan solution:

<ZoneTabs>
  <Tab label="Roundcube" availableIn={['eu']}>
    To add an automatic reply to one of your email addresses, click the <code className="action">Responders</code> tab of your MX Plan service.
    The table of responders that have already been created appears. Above it, click the <code className="action">Add a responder</code> button.
    
    For more details on setting up a responder in the OVHcloud Console, please read our guide "[MX Plan - How to create an automatic reply on an email address](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/feature-auto-responses)".
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Zimbra" availableIn={['eu']}>
    Set up the automatic reply directly in the webmail by logging in to the email address. For more details, please read the "[Automatic Replies](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-zimbra#auto-reply)" section of our guide "[How to use Zimbra webmail](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-zimbra)".
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="OWA">
    Set up the automatic reply directly in the webmail by logging in to the email address. For more details, please read the "[Adding an automatic reply](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/email-owa#adding-an-automatic-reply)" section of our guide "[Using your email address from the Outlook Web App (OWA) webmail](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/email-owa)".
  </Tab>
</ZoneTabs>
