### OWA and Zimbra

This section documents MX Plan offers using the **OWA** and **Zimbra** webmail technologies.

#### Create an email account

To set up a new email account, go to the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab. The table lists the email accounts already configured, and the **Email accounts quota** section shows how many accounts are configured out of the total available to you. Next, click the <code className="action">Add account</code> button, above the table.

In the **Add account** panel, enter the following information:

- **Email address**: Enter the name you would like for your email address (firstname.lastname, for example). The domain name is shown to the right of the field.

:::warning
The name of your email address must meet the following conditions:

- The part before the "@" must end with a letter or a number.
- No accents
- No special characters, except for the following characters: `.`, `+`, `-` and `_`

:::


- **First name**: Enter a first name.
- **Last name**: Enter a last name.
- **Display name**: Enter the name you want to be displayed as the sender of emails sent from this address. It is prefilled with the first and last names.
- **Account size**: Select the account size you want from the drop-down menu. This is the available space for the account to store messages.
- **Password**: Type in a strong password, or generate one using the key icon to the right of the field. For security reasons, we recommend not using the same password twice and choosing one that does not contain any personal information (e.g. your surname, first name and date of birth). We also recommend renewing it regularly.

:::warning
The password must meet the following requirements:

- Minimum 10 characters
- At least 1 digit and 1 special character (`$`, `!`, `&`, …)
- At least 1 uppercase letter

:::


Once you have filled in all of the required fields, click <code className="action">Add</code>. The account you have added will appear in the table; you will need to wait a few minutes for it to become available.

Repeat this step as necessary according to the number of accounts to create.

#### Delete an email account

On MX Plan services using the Zimbra or OWA webmail, deleting an account is referred to as *resetting an account*.

:::warning
Before deleting email accounts, make sure they are not used. You may need to back up these accounts. If required, please refer to our guide on [Manually migrate your email address](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/manual-email-migration), which explains how to export account data from the OVHcloud Console or email software.

:::


In the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account you want to delete, then click <code className="action">Reset</code>.

In the **Reset account** window that opens, click <code className="action">Reset</code> to confirm. Resetting deletes the account's data and name; the account can then be configured again.

### MX Plan Roundcube

This section is dedicated to MX Plan offers that use the **Roundcube** webmail technology.

#### Create an email account

To create a new email address, go to the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab. The table lists all of the email accounts of your MX Plan solution. Then click the <code className="action">Add account</code> button, above the table.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Email account tab of an MX Plan service, with the Add account button above the table" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-creation/console/mxplan-creation-legacy-step2.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Add account** panel that opens, enter the following information:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Add account panel: Account name, Description, Account size and Password fields" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-creation/console/mxplan-creation-legacy-step3.png" loading="lazy" />

- **Account name**: Enter the name you want for your email address (firstname.lastname, for example). The domain name is shown to the right of the field. The account name must contain 2 to 32 characters, with no spaces or accents; only ".", "-" and "_" are allowed.
- **Description**: Optional field. Enter a short description that will distinguish this account from the other accounts in the OVHcloud Console.
- **Account size**: Select the size of account you want from the drop-down menu. This is the available space for the account to store messages.
- **Password**: Type in a password, or generate one using the key icon to the right of the field. The password must contain 9 to 30 characters, with no accented characters. For security reasons, we recommend not using the same password twice, choosing one that does not contain any personal information (e.g. your surname, first name and date of birth) and renewing it regularly.

Once you have filled in all of the required fields, click <code className="action">Add</code> to create the email address, then wait a few minutes for it to become available.

Repeat this step as necessary according to the number of accounts to create.

#### View emails

On the [Webmail login page](/links/web/email), enter your email address and password. Then click the <code className="action">Login</code> button.

Your inbox will then appear. You can find more information in our guide on [Using your email account via the RoundCube webmail interface](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-roundcube).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="email" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-creation/mxplan-creation-legacy-step4.png" loading="lazy" />

To view your emails using an email client, please refer to the section ["View an email account using a device"](#configdevices).

#### Delete an email account

:::warning
Before deleting email accounts, make sure they are not used. You may need to back up these accounts. If required, please refer to our guide on [Manually migrate your email address](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/manual-email-migration), which explains how to export account data from the OVHcloud Console or email software.

:::


In the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account you want to delete, then click <code className="action">Delete</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Email account tab, with an account's ⋮ menu open on the Delete entry" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-creation/console/mxplan-legacy-reset.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Delete account** window that opens, click <code className="action">Delete</code> to confirm. This action is irreversible and results in the loss of all of the account's data.
