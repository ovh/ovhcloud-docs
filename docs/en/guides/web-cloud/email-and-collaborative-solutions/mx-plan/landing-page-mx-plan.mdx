## What is MX Plan?

MX Plan is OVHcloud's shared email solution, easy to set up, which links custom email addresses to your domain name (`firstname@yourdomain.com`). Find all the guides here to create, configure and manage your email accounts.

With an MX Plan offer, you can:

- Create and manage custom email addresses on your domain name
- Access your email from webmail, wherever you are
- Configure your mailboxes in an email client on your computer or mobile device
- Enable advanced features (auto-replies, filters, redirections, delegation, mailing lists)
