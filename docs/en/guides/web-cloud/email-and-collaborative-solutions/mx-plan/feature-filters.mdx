## Objective

With a filter you can configure conditions for your account's incoming emails and set actions based on them.

For example: you can automatically delete emails tagged as spam by our spam protection.

- Condition: email subject contains *[SPAM]*
- Action: delete email

**This guide explains how to create and configure a filter on your email address.**

## Instructions <a name="oldmxplan"></a>

The <code className="action">Email account</code> tab of your MX Plan service lists your email addresses. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then <code className="action">Manage filters</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="⋮ menu of an account in the Email account tab, Manage filters option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/feature-filters/console/mxplan-filters-01.png" loading="lazy" />

The **Filters for …** page lists the filters configured for this email address.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Filters page of an email address, Create a filter button" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/feature-filters/console/mxplan-filters-02.png" loading="lazy" />

To add a filter to your email address, click the <code className="action">Create a filter</code> button. In the **Information** section of the panel that opens, define the following:

- **Filter name:** choose a name for your filter.

- **Priority:** sets the order in which your filters run on an incoming message, from 1 to 99. A priority 1 filter runs before a priority 2 filter.

- **Enable filter:** untick this option if you want to apply the filter later.

### Understanding the email filter settings

The **Create a filter** panel, which is displayed when you add a filter, has 3 sections: **Information**, **Rules** and **Action**.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Create a filter panel with its Information, Rules and Action sections" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/feature-filters/console/mxplan-filters-03.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Rules

The **Rules** section lets you define the messages the filter applies to.

First choice (header):

- **Sender (From):** corresponds to the sender's email address, for example: "If the sender ...".
- **Recipient (To):** corresponds to the recipient's email address, for example: "If the recipient ...".
- **Subject:** corresponds to the subject of the message, for example: "If the subject of the message ...".
- **Other:** the OVHcloud Console does not let you specify which email header element to use.

Second choice (rule):

- **Contains:** example: "... contains ...".
- **Does not contain:** example: "... does not contain ...".
- **SPF check:** enter a value of the [SPF record](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-spf) to take into account, for example: "... has no SPF record ...".

:::info
SPF (Sender Policy Framework) allows the server that receives an email to ensure that it has been sent from a trusted server.
It helps prevent identity fraud from email addresses that use your domain name (spoofing). For more information on the SPF record, please refer to our guide on [How to improve email security with an SPF record](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-spf).

:::


Third choice (value):

- Example: [SPAM]

Fourth choice (<code className="action">New rule</code>):

- This button allows you to add one or more conditions for the same action.

#### Action

The **Action** section lets you define the action to apply.

In the **Action** drop-down menu, you can choose between:

- **Accept:** emails that meet the conditions are received normally.
- **Delete:** emails that meet the conditions are deleted.
- **Redirect to:** redirects emails that meet the conditions to the email address of your choice, to be entered in the **Parameter (email)** field.
- **Forward to:** forwards emails that meet the conditions to one of the email addresses of your domain, to be entered in the **Parameter (email)** field.

Then click <code className="action">Create</code> to save the filter.

### Examples

#### Deleting spam emails

:::warning
In our example, we will mention [SPAM] as it appears in the subject line of an email. It is added automatically when the receiving server’s anti-spam solution delivers emails that it considers unwanted directly to the inbox. This is the case with MX Plan services using the Roundcube webmail.

:::


||Header|Rule|Value|Action|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Filter settings|Subject|Contains|[SPAM]|Delete|
|What the filter will do|If the subject of the message|contains|[SPAM]|then delete the message.|

#### Redirecting emails from a certain sender

||Header|Rule|Value|Action|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Filter settings|Sender (From)|Contains|contact@example.com|Redirect to: user@example.net|
|What the filter will do|If the sender|is|contact@example.com|then redirect the email to user@example.net|

#### Redirecting emails sent to a mailing list

||Header|Rule|Value|Action|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Filter settings|Recipient (To)|Contains|ml@example.com|Forward to: user@example.org|
|What the filter will do|If the message was sent to the mailing list|called|ml@example.com|then redirect the message to my other address: user@example.org|

#### Deleting emails containing an unwanted phrase, excluding one sender

You only need to add one filter, with two rules. After filling in the first one, click <code className="action">New rule</code> to add the second:

||Header|Rule|Value|
|---|---|---|---|
|Settings of rule 1|Subject|Contains|money|
|Settings of rule 2|Sender (From)|Does not contain|contact@example.net|

Filter action: **Delete**.

If the subject of the message contains the word `money` **and** the sender of the message is not `contact@example.net`, then the message will be deleted.
