## Objective

This OVHcloud feature allows you to set up an automatic email responder (auto-reply) that sends a message to anyone trying to contact you by email in your absence.

**This guide explains how to configure automatic replies for your email addresses.**

## Requirements

<Region zones={['eu']}>

- An OVHcloud MX Plan, available as part of our [web hosting plans](/links/web/hosting), the [free hosting](/links/web/domains-free-hosting) included with a domain name (activated in advance), or ordered separately as a standalone solution

</Region>

### Creating an auto-reply

Click the <code className="action">Responders</code> tab of your MX Plan service. Its table lists the automatic replies in place.

Above the table, click the <code className="action">Add a responder</code> button.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Responders tab of an MX Plan service, Add a responder button above the table" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/feature-auto-responses/console/email_responder01.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Add a responder** panel, fill in the following fields:

- **Email account**: the email address or alias concerned by the automatic reply. Select an existing email address of your email solution, or enter a free name in the case of an alias: the responder is then not linked to an existing address. The domain name is displayed to the right of the field.
- **Responder duration**:
    - **Temporary**: define a start (**From**) and end (**Until**) date and time for the automatic reply to run (useful if you are going on holiday, for example).
    - **Permanent**: the automatic reply stays active until you delete it or switch it to **Temporary**.
- **Send a copy** (free name) or **Keep messages on the server** (existing email address): this box allows you to forward the messages received during your absence to the address of your choice, or to keep them in the mailbox.

:::warning
If you untick this box, messages received during your absence will be automatically deleted.

:::


- **Copy address** (only for a free name, when the **Send a copy** box is ticked): select from the list the email address of your solution that will receive the emails sent to the alias.
- **Message**: this is the message your correspondents will receive when they send you an email. Write it using Latin characters only: other characters do not work on the responder.

Then click <code className="action">Add</code>. Your responder will be available within 5 to 10 minutes.

:::tip
If you would like to delegate the management of an automatic reply for an email address, please refer to our guide "[MX Plan - Delegating the management of your email accounts to another person](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/feature-delegation)".

:::


### Modifying or deleting an automatic reply

Once your automatic reply has been created, it appears in the table of the <code className="action">Responders</code> tab of your MX Plan service. To modify or delete it, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of it, then <code className="action">Edit</code> or <code className="action">Delete</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="⋮ menu of a responder in the Responders tab, with the Edit and Delete entries" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/feature-auto-responses/console/email_responder02.png" loading="lazy" />

The **Edit responder** panel lets you change the duration, the dates and the message of the automatic reply, but not its email account. Click <code className="action">Edit</code> to save your changes.

To delete it, click <code className="action">Delete</code> in the **Delete responder** window: this action cannot be undone.
