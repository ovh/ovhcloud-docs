## Objective

You have just purchased an Email Pro solution, which gives you affordable professional email addresses to support or start up your business.

**This guide explains the steps to correctly configure your Email Pro solution.**

## Requirements

- An [Email Pro](/links/web/email-pro) solution
- An email confirming that your Email Pro solution has been set up
- A domain name

## Instructions

### Step 1: Add your domain name

:::info
The name of an Email Pro service in the OVHcloud Console begins with *emailpro-*, contains part of your NIC handle, and ends with a number (1 for the first Email Pro service installed, 2 for the second, etc.).

:::


After ordering your Email Pro service, go to the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/web-cloud/email">Emails</ManagerLink> page of the OVHcloud Console. In the left-hand menu, expand <code className="action">Emails</code> and click <code className="action">Exchange</code>: your Email Pro services are listed there, with "Email Pro" in the **Offer type** column. Click the name of your service.

As long as no domain is associated with your service, it opens directly on the **Add a domain** page. To add a domain to a service that already has one, go to the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab, then click the <code className="action">Add a domain</code> button above the table.

You have two options:

- **Select a domain from the list**: Then choose your domain from the **Domain name** drop-down menu. Only the domain names that you manage in the OVHcloud Console are displayed. If the domain name is registered with OVHcloud but does not appear in the list, add it with the **Enter a domain name not managed by your OVH account** option.
- **Enter a domain name not managed by your OVH account**: Choose this option if the domain name is registered with OVHcloud but managed from another OVHcloud customer account, **or** if your domain name is registered with another registrar. You must be able to modify its DNS zone for the Email Pro service to work.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Add a domain page of an Email Pro service, with the Select a domain from the list option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/first-config/console/emailpro-01.png" loading="lazy" />

Once you have specified the domain name, the **Configuration** section appears just below it.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Configuration section with the Recommended configuration and Custom configuration options" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/first-config/console/emailpro-02.png" loading="lazy" />

Whatever the domain name, choose one of the two configurations, which correspond to the domain's two operating modes (the **Recommended configuration** is selected by default):

- **Recommended configuration** (**authoritative** mode): Choose this if Email Pro is the only email solution used with your domain name. All emails sent to this domain are then routed to this service as soon as the configuration is saved.
- **Custom configuration** (**non-authoritative** mode): Choose this if you use the Email Pro solution **and another email solution** with your domain name.

The mode applied to each domain is then displayed in the **Mode** column of the table in the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab.

> **Understanding Authoritative and Non-Authoritative Modes**
>
> - When an email is sent to your Email Pro platform (*Inbound mail server Email Pro*) in **authoritative** mode, this means that all of your domain name's email addresses are hosted only on this platform.
>
>   For example, if an email is sent to the address "*mary.johnson@mydomain.ovh*", the Email Pro server "*Inbound mail server Email Pro*" returns a failure message to the sender, because this address does not exist on the Email Pro server "*Inbound mail server Email Pro*".
> - When an email is sent to your Email Pro platform (*Inbound mail server Email Pro*) in **non-authoritative** mode, this means that your domain name's email addresses are divided between your main email platform (*Inbound mail server Email Pro*) and another email service (*Inbound mail server MX Plan*).
>
>   For example, if an email is sent to the address "*mary.johnson@mydomain.ovh*", the Email Pro server "*Inbound mail server Email Pro*" forwards the email to the MX Plan server "*Inbound mail server MX Plan*" so that it can deliver it.
>
> <img className="thumbnail" alt="Diagram of email routing in authoritative and non-authoritative mode" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/first-config/authoritative-mode.png" loading="lazy" />
>

:::warning
If this domain name is already declared in **authoritative** mode on another email platform, you cannot add it with the **Recommended configuration**. For the two platforms to coexist, the domain name must be in **non-authoritative** mode on each of them: choose the **Custom configuration** here, then switch the domain name to **non-authoritative** mode on the other platform.

:::


If you choose the **Custom configuration**, fill in the **Target email server URL** field according to the other email service you use:

- **OVHcloud email (Exchange or MX Plan)**: Keep the default value, "*mx1.mail.ovh.net*" (works the same way with *mx0.mail.ovh.net*, *mx2.mail.ovh.net*, *mx3.mail.ovh.net*, *mx4.mail.ovh.net*).
- **Email external to OVHcloud (email solution from another provider, private email server)**: Enter the host name of this external service's incoming server, and make sure it authorises email requests from your Email Pro service.

You can change this mode later: in the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the domain, then <code className="action">Configure</code>.

**If you have selected a domain name managed by OVHcloud**, its DNS zone can be configured automatically. The **Recommended configuration** sets up the 4 records below. With the **Custom configuration**, 4 boxes, ticked by default, appear below the **Target email server URL** field: untick any record you do not want configured automatically.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Custom configuration, with the Target email server URL field and the automatic DNS configuration boxes" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/first-config/console/emailpro-03.png" loading="lazy" />

- **Automatically configure the MX record (reception)**: The DNS record of the email servers, required to receive emails on the domain name concerned.
- **Automatically configure the SRV record (autodiscover)**: A DNS record that automatically configures your email software when you add your email address to it.
- **Automatically configure the SPF record**: A DNS record that lists the servers authorised to send emails for your domain name. Refer to our guide "[How to improve email security with an SPF record](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-spf)" for more information.
- **Automatically configure and enable the DKIM record**: Sets up an encrypted digital signature to secure email exchanges. Refer to our guide "[How to improve email security with a DKIM record](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-dkim)" for more information.

**For a domain name not managed by OVHcloud**, configure its DNS zone manually by following Step 2 of this guide.

Once you have made your choices, click the <code className="action">Save the configuration</code> button (<code className="action">Add</code> if you went through the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab) to start adding the domain.

If you are adding the first domain of your service, the **Create the first accounts** section then appears: name your accounts there, or click <code className="action">Create my accounts later</code> to configure them in Step 3.

### Step 2: Configure your domain name

Once the domain name has been added as an associated domain, check its configuration in the table of the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab.

The **Diagnostic** column lets you check the domain name's DNS configuration. A red badge appears if this configuration needs to be changed. There are two possibilities:

- **Automatic configuration when you add an OVHcloud domain name**: If you have just made the change, it may take a few hours before it is displayed in <ManagerLink to="/">the OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>.

- **Manual configuration for a domain name not managed by OVHcloud**: As long as the domain name has not been validated, the CNAME record to create is displayed in the **Diagnostic** column (**Owner verification**). Refer to our guide "[Creating a CNAME record to validate your domain name on your email solution](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-dns-cname)". Once the domain has been validated, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the domain's row, then <code className="action">Diagnostics</code> to display the changes you need to make. The page that opens has one tab per record (MX, SRV, SPF and DKIM), showing its status and the record expected in your DNS zone.
    - *For an MX record*, refer to our guide "[Configure an MX record for email management](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-mx)".
    - *For an SRV record*, complete your DNS zone with the information in the <code className="action">SRV</code> tab of this page. You can use our guide "[Editing an OVHcloud DNS zone](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-edit)" to add this record.
    - *For an SPF record*, complete your DNS zone with the information in the <code className="action">SPF</code> tab. You can use our guide "[How to improve email security with an SPF record](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-spf)" to add this record.
    - *For a DKIM record*, complete your DNS zone with the information in the <code className="action">DKIM</code> tab. You can use our guide "[How to improve email security with a DKIM record](/guides/web-cloud/domains/dns-zone-dkim)" to add this record.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Associated domains tab, with a domain's ⋮ menu open on the Diagnostics entry" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/first-config/console/emailpro-04.png" loading="lazy" />

### Step 3: Configure the Email Pro accounts

To configure your email addresses, open the <code className="action">Accounts</code> tab. The table displays the accounts you have ordered in the form "*@configureme.me*".

To configure them, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account's row, then <code className="action">Configure</code> (<code className="action">Edit</code> for an account that is already configured).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Accounts tab, with an email account's ⋮ menu open on the Edit entry" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/first-config/console/emailpro-05.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Add an Email Pro account** panel (**Edit an Email Pro account** for an account that is already configured), fill in the following information:

- **Email account**: Enter the name that your email address will bear (for example, firstname.lastname), and select the domain concerned from the list.

:::warning
The name of your email address must meet the following conditions:

- Minimum 2 characters
- Maximum 32 characters
- No accents
- No special characters, except for the following characters: `.`, `,`, `-` and `_`

:::


- **First name**: Enter a first name.
- **Last name**: Enter a last name.
- **Display name**: Enter the name that will be displayed as the sender when emails are sent from this address.
- **Password** and **Confirmation**: Set a strong password of at least 10 characters, including a number, an upper-case letter and a special character (for example `$`, `!` or `&`). For security reasons, do not use the same password twice. Choose one that has no connection with your personal information (for example, avoid your last name, first name and date of birth). Change it regularly.

:::warning
The password must meet the following requirements:

- Minimum 10 characters
- Maximum 30 characters
- No accents

:::


Once you have filled in the information, click <code className="action">Add</code> in the panel (<code className="action">Edit</code> for an account that is already configured) to start configuring the account.

:::info
Repeat this step for each account you have. You can order additional accounts with the <code className="action">Order accounts</code> button.

:::


### Step 4: Use your email addresses

Once you have configured your accounts, you can start using them straight away. To do this, OVHcloud offers a web application, available at [OVHcloud webmail](/links/web/email). You will need to enter your email address and password to log in.

If this is your first time logging in to OWA with this email address, you will be prompted to set the interface language and time zone. Next, click <code className="action">Save</code> to continue.

:::info
Time zones are listed according to [the UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_Universal_Time#/media/File:World_Time_Zones_Map.png), not in alphabetical order of cities.

**Example**: For Western Europe, it is UTC +1 (Brussels, Copenhagen, Madrid, Paris).

:::


If you would like to configure your email address on an email client or device (e.g. a smartphone or tablet), you can refer to our [configuration guides](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/landing-page-email-pro). If you simply need the information required to configure your Email Pro account, the settings to use are listed below:

|Server type|Server name|Security type|Port|
|---|---|---|---|
|Incoming|pro?.mail.ovh.net|SSL/TLS|993|
|Outgoing|pro?.mail.ovh.net|STARTTLS|587|

:::info
In our example, we use the server name: pro?.mail.ovh.net. Replace the "?" with the server number of your Email Pro service.

In the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, which opens by default, the server name is shown on the **Server** line of the **General information** section.

:::
