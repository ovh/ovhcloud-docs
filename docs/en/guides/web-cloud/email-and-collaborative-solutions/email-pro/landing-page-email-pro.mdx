## What is Email Pro?

[OVHcloud Email Pro](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/emails/email-pro/) is a professional collaborative email solution based on Exchange technology: email accounts on your domain name, shared calendars, contacts and folders, and multi-device synchronisation. Find all the guides here to configure and manage your accounts.

With Email Pro, you can:

- Configure and manage professional email accounts on your domain name
- Access your email from the Outlook Web App (OWA) webmail
- Configure your accounts in an email client on your computer or mobile
- Share calendars, contacts and folders with members of your organisation
- Delegate rights, manage security and customise the footers of your accounts

## Accessing your email

Access your email, calendars and contacts online, without installation, via the [Outlook Web App (OWA)](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/using-the-outlook-web-app-webmail/email-owa) webmail.

## Finding your server name

Your Email Pro server name is required to manually configure an email client (IMAP, POP or SMTP). It takes the form `pro?.mail.ovh.net`, where `?` corresponds to your server number.

To find it, log in to your <ManagerLink to="/#/web/email_pro">OVHcloud Control Panel</ManagerLink> and select your Email Pro service. In the **General information** tab, under **Connection**, the full server name is shown (for example: `pro3.mail.ovh.net`).
