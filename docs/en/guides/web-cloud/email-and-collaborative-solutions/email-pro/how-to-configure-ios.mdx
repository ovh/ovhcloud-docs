## Objective

You can configure Email Pro accounts on email clients, if they are compatible. By doing so, you can use your email address through your preferred email application.

**This guide explains how to configure an Email Pro account on iPhone and iPad, via the Mail app.**

[[fragment:support-scope]]

## Requirements

- an [OVHcloud Email Pro account](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/emails/email-pro/)
- the Mail app installed on your iOS device
- login credentials for the email account to be configured

## Instructions

### Adding an account <a name="addaccount"></a>

:::info
In this guide, we will use as the server name: pro?.mail.ovh.net. You will need to replace the "?" with the actual number indicating the appropriate server for your Email Pro service.

Click <ManagerLink to="/#/web/email_pro">this link</ManagerLink> to access the <code className="action">Email Pro</code> section. The server name is visible in the **Connection** section of the <code className="action">General information</code> tab.

:::


On your device’s home screen, go to <code className="action">Settings</code> (cogwheel icon). There are several ways you can add an account, depending on your iOS version:

- **For iOS 7, 8, 9 and 10**: Go to <code className="action">Mail, Contacts, Calendar</code>, then <code className="action">Add account</code>. Choose <code className="action">Other</code>, then <code className="action">Add Mail Account</code>. Then proceed to step 5 of the table below.

- **For iOS 11, 12 and 13**: Go to <code className="action">Accounts and passwords</code>, then <code className="action">Add account</code>. Choose <code className="action">Other</code>, then <code className="action">Add Mail Account</code>. Then proceed to step 5 of the table below.

- **For iOS versions 14 and above**: Follow the instructions in the table below.

At the end of the configuration process, ensure that you leave <code className="action">Mail</code> activated, so that the application can use this account, then click <code className="action">Save</code>.

To check that the account has been correctly configured, you can send a test email.

If you need to enter any settings manually in your account preferences, the technical settings you will need to use with our Email Pro solution are listed below:

|Server type|Server name|SSL|Port|
|---|---|---|---|
|Incoming|pro?.mail.ovh.net|Yes|993|
|Outgoing|pro?.mail.ovh.net|Yes|587|

### Using your email address

Once you have configured your email address, you can start using it! You can now send and receive emails.

OVHcloud also offers a web application that includes [collaborative features](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/emails/), accessible via [Webmail](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/mail/). You can log in using your email credentials.

:::info
If you experience any difficulties receiving or sending emails, please read our [FAQ about OVHcloud email services](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/faq-emails).

:::
