## Objective

The Email Pro service offers flexible management for account billing. This guide details how to configure it.

**Find out how to manage subscriptions for your Email Pro accounts.**

## Requirements

- An [Email Pro account](/links/web/email-pro)

## Instructions

### Ordering additional accounts

In the <code className="action">Accounts</code> tab, click the <code className="action">Order accounts</code> button, on the left above the table.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Order accounts button in the Accounts tab of an Email Pro platform" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/manage-billing-emailpro/console/billing-emailpro-01.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Order Email Pro accounts** window, choose the payment type (**monthly** or **yearly**) and the number of accounts, tick the box to accept the terms, click <code className="action">Next</code>, then <code className="action">Order</code>. The payment page opens in a new tab.

### Modifying the renewal frequency for your accounts <a name="periodicity"></a>

In the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab of your Email Pro platform, click <code className="action">Manage my service accounts</code> in the **Subscription** section.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Manage my service accounts link in the Subscription section of the Overview tab" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/manage-billing-emailpro/console/billing-emailpro-02.png" loading="lazy" />

The **Service management** page lists the accounts on your platform and their renewal date. The renewal period is managed account by account: click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then <code className="action">Change renewal period</code>. In the **Change renewal period** window, select the renewal period you want:

- **Monthly**: You are billed for the account each month on the date listed in the **Renewal date** column.
- **Yearly**: You are billed for 12 months on the date listed in the **Renewal date** column.

:::info
For simplified management, the billing for each Email Pro account is automatically aligned at the beginning of the month. To make this alignment, and move the subscription date if necessary, you will receive a prorated invoice for your Email Pro account.

**Example**: An order placed on 8 January with a monthly subscription will be billed again on 8 February. The following invoice will cover the period between 8 February and 1 March.

:::

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Change renewal period window opened from an account's menu on the Service management page" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/manage-billing-emailpro/console/billing-emailpro-03.png" loading="lazy" />

Finally, click <code className="action">Edit</code> to confirm your choice.

To stop renewing an account, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of that account, then <code className="action">Terminate at expiration</code>. Confirm in the **Termination at expiration** window by clicking <code className="action">Confirm termination</code>. The account will be suspended, then deleted, on the date listed in the **Renewal date** column.

### Deleting accounts <a name="deletion"></a>

To delete an account from your Email Pro platform, first terminate it with the <code className="action">Terminate at expiration</code> action on the **Service management** page (see the section "[Modifying the renewal frequency for your accounts](#periodicity)").

Once it has been terminated, the account will be suspended on its expiry date. To delete the email address hosted by the account before its expiry date, reset the account.

In the <code className="action">Accounts</code> tab of your platform, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then <code className="action">Reset</code>. Confirm in the **Reset an account** window by clicking <code className="action">Reset</code>. Once it has been reset, the account will remain empty until it expires.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Reset an account window opened from an account's menu in the Accounts tab" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/manage-billing-emailpro/console/billing-emailpro-04.png" loading="lazy" />

### Cancelling the service

{/* v8-gap: CG-090 (open) — v6 « Résilier » on the dashboard terminated the whole platform (every account deleted at expiry). The Console offers no platform termination: the subscription card withholds it and « Mes services » sends an Email Pro row to the per-account page. Rewrite this section when CG-090 is restored */}

The Email Pro platform is billed per account. To stop being billed, terminate each of its accounts with the <code className="action">Terminate at expiration</code> action on the **Service management** page (see the section "[Modifying the renewal frequency for your accounts](#periodicity)").

:::warning
You will lose all the data associated with these accounts.

:::

Once the email accounts have been deleted, you can remove the domain name(s) associated with your Email Pro platform. A domain name can only be deleted once it is no longer associated with any email account. In the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the domain name you want to delete, then <code className="action">Delete this domain</code>. Confirm in the **Delete domain** window by clicking <code className="action">Delete</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Domain name menu in the Associated domains tab, with the Delete this domain action" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/manage-billing-emailpro/console/billing-emailpro-del-dom.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
Each terminated account is suspended, then deleted on its renewal date, shown in the **Renewal date** column of the **Service management** page.

:::
