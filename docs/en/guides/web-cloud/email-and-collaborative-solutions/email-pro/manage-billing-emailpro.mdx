## Objective

The Email Pro service offers flexible management for account billing. This guide details how to configure it.

**Find out how to manage subscriptions for your Email Pro accounts.**

## Requirements

- An [Email Pro](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/emails/email-pro/) account

## Instructions

### Ordering additional accounts

In the <code className="action">Accounts</code> tab, click the <code className="action">Order accounts</code> button, on the left above the accounts table.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Order accounts button in the Accounts tab of an Email Pro platform" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/manage-billing-emailpro/console/billing-emailpro-01.png" loading="lazy" />

### Modifying the renewal frequency for your accounts <a name="periodicity"></a>

In the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab of your Email Pro platform, click <code className="action">Manage my service accounts</code> in the **Subscription** section.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Manage my service accounts link in the Subscription section of the Overview tab" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/manage-billing-emailpro/console/billing-emailpro-02.png" loading="lazy" />

The **Service management** page lists the accounts on your platform and their renewal date. You change the renewal period for each account separately: click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account, then <code className="action">Change renewal period</code>. In the window that opens, select the renewal period you want:

- **Monthly**: You are billed for the account each month on the date listed in the **Renewal date** column.
- **Yearly**: You are billed for 12 months on the date listed in the **Renewal date** column.

:::info
For simplified management, the billing for each Email Pro account is automatically aligned at the beginning of the month. To make this alignment, and move the subscription date if necessary, you will receive an invoice from your Email Pro account on a pro rata basis.

**Example**: An order placed on 08 January with a monthly subscription will be billed again on 08 February. The following invoice will cover the period between February 08 and March 1st.

:::

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Change renewal period window opened from an account's menu on the Service management page" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/manage-billing-emailpro/console/billing-emailpro-03.png" loading="lazy" />

Finally, click <code className="action">Edit</code> to confirm your choice.

To stop renewing an account, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of that account, then <code className="action">Terminate at expiration</code>. Confirm in the **Termination at expiration** window by clicking <code className="action">Confirm termination</code>. On the date listed in the **Renewal date** column, the account is suspended, then deleted.

### Deleting accounts <a name="deletion"></a>

To delete an account from your Email Pro platform, first cancel it with the <code className="action">Terminate at expiration</code> action on the **Service management** page, as described in "[Modifying the renewal frequency for your accounts](#periodicity)".

Once you have cancelled it, the account will be suspended on its expiry date. If you would like to delete the email address hosted by the account before its expiry date, you can reset it.

In the <code className="action">Accounts</code> tab of your platform, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then click <code className="action">Reset</code>. Confirm in the **Reset an account** window by clicking <code className="action">Reset</code>. Once you have reset the account, it will remain empty until its expiry date.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Reset an account window opened from an account's menu in the Accounts tab" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/manage-billing-emailpro/console/billing-emailpro-04.png" loading="lazy" />

### Cancelling the service

Once you have deleted your email accounts, you will need to delete the domain name(s) associated with your Email Pro platform before cancelling it. To do this, go to the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the domain name you want to delete, then <code className="action">Delete this domain</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Domain name menu in the Associated domains tab, with the Delete this domain action" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/manage-billing-emailpro/console/billing-emailpro-del-dom.png" loading="lazy" />

In the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab of your Email Pro platform, click <code className="action">Terminate service</code> in the **Subscription** section. The **Terminate service** window opens with the **Terminate immediately** option selected.

:::warning
Immediate termination cannot be undone: your Email Pro platform and all of its data are permanently deleted as soon as you confirm, and no refund is issued.
:::

Click <code className="action">Terminate now</code> to confirm the termination.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Terminate service window opened from the Subscription section of the Overview tab" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/manage-billing-emailpro/console/billing-emailpro-05.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
If the window offers a scheduled termination (**At expiration** or **At the commitment end date**), all of the accounts on the Email Pro platform are suspended, then deleted on that date.

:::
