## Objective

There are several advantages to subscribing to the OVHcloud offers **Microsoft 365 Apps for Business** or **Microsoft 365 Apps for Enterprise**. Billing is monthly and you can use one licence for 5 PCs/Macs, 5 tablets and 5 smartphones.

Your Microsoft 365 licences are collected into a group, also called `Service`. An Apps for Business licence group can contain up to 300 licences, an Apps for Enterprise licence group is unlimited.

Here is the list of software included in the suite:

- Apps for Business licences: Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Outlook, OneNote, Publisher.
- Apps for Enterprise licences: Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Outlook, OneNote, Publisher, Access.

**This guide explains how to subscribe to a Microsoft 365 licence and manage it in the OVHcloud Console.**

## Instructions

### Order a license

1. Go to the [Microsoft 365 sales page](/links/web/ms365). You can also open it from the Microsoft 365 section of the OVHcloud Console: click <code className="action">Order</code> (<code className="action">Order a Microsoft 365 service</code> if you have no Microsoft 365 service yet), then click <code className="action">Order</code> in the **Microsoft 365** section of the **Order a Microsoft 365 service** page. The sales page opens in a new tab.
1. Choose from our available solutions, namely "Apps for Business" and "Apps for Enterprise".
1. Set the renewal frequency for your licence group.
1. Define the number of licences you want, then finalise your order.

### Activate your license

To activate the licence ordered:

1. Select the Microsoft 365 service concerned.
1. Click the <code className="action">Licenses</code> tab.
1. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the licence to activate, then click <code className="action">Edit</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Menu ⋮ of an unconfigured licence open, Edit option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-office/office-csp1/console/Outlook-cps1-01.png" loading="lazy" />

1. Enter the licence user information (**First name**, **Last name** and **Login**), then click <code className="action">Save</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Edit user window with the First name, Last name and Login fields" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-office/office-csp1/console/Outlook-cps1-02.png" loading="lazy" />

### Install the Microsoft 365 suite on your device <a name="install365"></a>

Once the licence has been activated, you will receive an email at the contact email address of your OVHcloud account. You can also find this email in the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>: click your initials in the top right-hand corner, then click <code className="action">My messages</code>. In the **Messages** tab, displayed by default, look for the email whose subject contains "A new Microsoft Office user has been created".

This email contains the information required to download and install your Microsoft 365 suite, namely **the activation email address** and the **password**.

Go to [https://portal.office.com/](https://portal.office.com/) and log in with **the activation email address** and the **password** previously configured. You will be taken to a window where you can download the Microsoft 365 suite to your desktop, along with the installation instructions.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Office portal, Install Office menu open, Premium Office apps option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-office/office-csp1/Outlook-cps1-04.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Install Suite 365 on multiple devices

With a license, the user can install Microsoft 365 on **5 Windows and macOS devices**, on **5 tablets** and **5 smartphones**. This represents a total of **15 devices** for a license. All of these devices must be owned by the licensee.

To install your license on several devices, simply reproduce [the previous step](#install365) *on each device*, ensuring that each is managed *by the same user*.

### Change a license password

If you would like to set your own licence password:

1. Select the Microsoft 365 service concerned.
1. Click the <code className="action">Licenses</code> tab.
1. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the licence concerned, then click <code className="action">Change password</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Menu ⋮ of a licence with the Active status open, Change password option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-office/office-csp1/console/Outlook-cps1-03.png" loading="lazy" />

1. On the **Change password** page, choose **Automatically generate a password** and enter the **Notification email**, or choose **Manually set a password** and enter the **Password**. Then click <code className="action">Save</code>.

### Add a license to an existing group

To add licences to an existing group:

1. Select the Microsoft 365 service concerned, then click the <code className="action">Licenses</code> tab.
1. Click the <code className="action">Order licenses</code> button, above the table.
1. In the **Order new licenses** window, select the **License type**, enter the **Number** of licences, then click <code className="action">Order</code>. You are redirected to the order form: once you have paid, go back to the <code className="action">Licenses</code> tab to configure your new users.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Order new licenses window with the License type and Number fields" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-office/office-csp1/console/Outlook-cps1-05.png" loading="lazy" />

For a licence group of the **Pay as you go** type, the <code className="action">Order licenses</code> button is replaced by <code className="action">Add a user</code>. It opens a page of the same name: enter the **First name**, **Last name**, **Login** and **License type**, then click <code className="action">Save</code>.

### Manage your subscriptions <a name="managesubscriptions"></a>

#### Delete a license in a license group

In the <code className="action">Licenses</code> tab of your licence group, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the licence you want to delete, then click <code className="action">Delete</code>. In the **Delete user** window, click <code className="action">Delete</code> again to confirm.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Menu ⋮ of a licence with the Active status open, Delete option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-office/office-csp1/console/Outlook-cps1-06.png" loading="lazy" />

:::warning
The user is deleted immediately after you confirm.

:::

:::info
Usage for the current month will be billed at the end of the month.

:::


#### Cancel a license group

To terminate your Microsoft 365 licence group (CSP1):

1. Click your initials in the top right-hand corner of the OVHcloud Console.
1. Click <code className="action">My offers and services</code>.
1. Identify your licence group in the table, for example by entering its name in the **Search** field.
1. In the **Actions** column, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button on the row of the licence group you want to terminate, then click <code className="action">Cancel my subscription</code>.
1. In the **Terminate service** window, specify the reason for the termination, then click <code className="action">Terminate</code>.

:::warning
You will lose all the data associated with this service.

:::

:::info
Usage for the current month will be billed at the end of the month.

:::
