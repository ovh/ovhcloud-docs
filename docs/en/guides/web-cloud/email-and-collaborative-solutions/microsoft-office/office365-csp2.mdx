## Objective

Office 365 for Resellers (CSP2) is a service that allows you to purchase different types of Microsoft 365 licences at discounted prices so you can resell them to your customers.

**This guide explains how to subscribe to and manage an Office 365 Reseller (CSP2) service from the OVHcloud Console.**

## Requirements

- An <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud customer account</ManagerLink>
- An [MPN ID](https://learn.microsoft.com/partner-center/mpn-create-a-partner-center-account) (Microsoft Partner Network IDentifier)
- Registration with the Microsoft Cloud Solution Provider (CSP) program as an "indirect reseller" in the region where you operate (for example: "EU" for Europe)

:::warning
Since 01/07/2022, all Office 365 reseller services (CSP2) must have an MPN ID registered in the "CSP indirect reseller" program. They will be disabled by Microsoft otherwise.

An MPN ID is now mandatory for all new subscriptions.

:::


If you do not already have an MPN ID, you can create one (if you are eligible according to the Microsoft terms and conditions) by following the official Microsoft documentation: "[What is the Microsoft Cloud Partner Program?](https://docs.microsoft.com/partner-center/mpn-create-a-partner-center-account)".

To register as an indirect reseller, please refer to the Microsoft documentation ["Enroll as an indirect reseller"](https://docs.microsoft.com/partner-center/enrolling-in-the-csp-program#enroll-as-an-indirect-reseller).

:::tip
With the MPN ID, you can receive a cash-back rebate for the subscriptions you order via the OVHcloud Console. This cash-back process is subject to rules defined by Microsoft, depending on the volume of subscriptions you generate.

:::

## Instructions

### Order an Office 365 Reseller service

From the Microsoft 365 section of the OVHcloud Console, click <code className="action">Order</code> (<code className="action">Order a Microsoft 365 service</code> if you have no Microsoft 365 service yet), then, on the **Order a Microsoft 365 service** page, click <code className="action">Order</code> in the **Microsoft 365 Reseller** section. The order takes place in 3 steps:

1. In the **Reseller information** section, enter the MPN ID you previously created with Microsoft in the **Microsoft partner ID** field, then click <code className="action">Next</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Microsoft 365 Reseller order page, Microsoft partner ID field" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-office/office-csp2/console/csp2-01.png" loading="lazy" />

1. In the **Platform configuration and end customer information** section, choose **New platform** or, if your platform already exists at Microsoft, **Delegation of a platform previously created at Microsoft**. For a delegation, first accept the partnership request using the <code className="action">Consent form</code> link, then fill in the **Existing Office 365 tenant (Delegation only)**. In both cases, fill in the contact information for the end customer (country, postcode, first name, last name and email address): this information is required to define the manager of the licence group (*Tenant*).

    - *Optional*: for a new platform, you can define a custom **subdomain** (subject to the available names). Enable the **I want a custom subdomain** option, then enter it in the **Enter your subdomain** field. Otherwise, a generic subdomain is created in the form `bizofficeXXX.onmicrosoft.com`.

    Then click <code className="action">Next</code>.

1. In the **Select your licenses** section, tick the licences you want to include in your group and specify their number, then click <code className="action">Continue my order</code>: the order is completed in a new tab.

:::warning
Please ensure that the email address entered when you created your licence group is valid, as it will be used to receive credentials for the Microsoft platform.

:::

:::info
Once the order is placed, go to the Microsoft 365 administration portal to validate the contract with Microsoft (Microsoft Customer Agreement, MCA). Your licences are delivered after this validation.

:::

:::warning
For products that are under licence, it is not possible to transfer an Office 365 reseller *Tenant* from one OVHcloud NIC handle to another. It is necessary to cancel the subscription on the original OVHcloud customer account and subscribe to the same type of licence on the new OVHcloud customer account.

:::


### Manage your Office 365 Reseller service

Once the service is available, select it in the Microsoft 365 section of the OVHcloud Console (type **Reseller**).

The **Overview** tab, displayed when you open the service, shows the following information:

- **Service display name**: it is used as the page title. To customise it, click the pencil icon to the right of the title, enter the new name, then confirm.
- **Service ID** (**General information** section): the internal name of your service, visible only in the OVHcloud Console. It also corresponds to the *Tenant* at Microsoft.
- **MPN ID** (**General information** section): your Microsoft partner identifier. To change it, click the pencil icon to its right, enter the new identifier (6 or 7 digits), then confirm.
- **Creation date** (**My offer** section): indicates when the service was created.

The <code className="action">Licenses</code> tab also shows:

- **Microsoft administration portal**: link to the Office portal that allows you to manage your subscriptions.
- You can manage your Microsoft *Tenant*'s admin password directly in the Microsoft administration interface. Please refer to [the Microsoft documentation](https://support.microsoft.com/account-billing/reset-a-forgotten-microsoft-account-password-eff4f067-5042-c1a3-fe72-b04d60556c37).
- You can also manage additional domain names from the Microsoft administration interface. See [the Microsoft documentation](https://support.microsoft.com/office/connect-your-domain-to-office-365-cd74b4fa-6d34-4669-9937-ed178ac84515).

The <code className="action">Tasks</code> tab lists the operations of your service and their status.

:::info
If you have not yet accepted the Microsoft Customer Agreement (MCA), accept it from the Microsoft 365 administration portal, then click <code className="action">I have approved the MCA</code> in the **Overview** tab.

:::

### Manage your subscriptions

You can change the number of licences in your subscriptions or cancel them. The <code className="action">Licenses</code> tab of your service displays them in a table:

- **ID**: each type of licence ordered has a unique identifier (ID).
- **Status**: the status of your licence.
- **License name**: indicates the type of licence subscribed.
- **Number of licenses**: indicates the number of available licences.
- **Creation date**: indicates when the subscription to the selected licence type was created.
- **Last update**: indicates when the subscription was last updated (such as adding a licence).

To change the total number of licences in a subscription, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the subscription, then click <code className="action">Change quantity</code>. In the **Edit subscription** window, enter the **New number of licenses**, then click <code className="action">Save</code>. The additional licences are billed as soon as you save.

To cancel the subscription and all of its licences, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the subscription, then click <code className="action">Delete subscription</code>. In the **Delete subscription — …** window, click <code className="action">Delete subscription</code> again to confirm.

To subscribe to a licence type you do not have yet, click <code className="action">New subscription</code>, above the table. In the **Add a new subscription** window, select the **License type**, enter the **Number of licenses**, then click <code className="action">Save</code>.

:::info
Microsoft has special conditions of use for academic licences that must be complied with. You can find these official documents according to your language and region in [the Microsoft licensing documentation](https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/docs).

:::

### Manage users

Now that you have your licences available, you need to manage the users who will use them. You can do this directly via the [Microsoft administrative portal](https://portal.office.com/Admin/Default.aspx).

To log in, enter the username and the password from the email OVHcloud sent you confirming the installation of your licence group. This information is sent to the address provided when the licence group was created.
