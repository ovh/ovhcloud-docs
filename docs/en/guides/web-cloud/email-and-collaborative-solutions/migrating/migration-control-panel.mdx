## Requirements

- An MX Plan email account (as MX Plan standalone or included in an OVHcloud [Web Hosting plan](/links/web/hosting)).

<Region zones={['eu']}>
- An [Exchange](/links/web/emails-hosted-exchange), [Email Pro](/links/web/email-pro) or [Zimbra](/links/web/emails-zimbra) service with at least one unconfigured account (which will appear as "@configureme.me").
</Region>

<Region zones={['ca']}>
- An [Exchange](/links/web/emails-hosted-exchange) service with at least one unconfigured account (which will appear as "@configureme.me").
</Region>

- **No redirections set on the MX Plan email account you want to migrate**.

[[cpnav:web-email-pro+web-mx-plan+web-exchange]]

:::tip
Whatever migration method you use, we strongly recommend backing up your mailbox beforehand, as a precaution against any data loss. Our guide [Manually migrate your email address](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/manual-email-migration) explains step by step how to back up a mailbox (for example by exporting it to a PST file): make this backup before you start the migration.
:::

## Instructions

### Step 1: Defining your project

Email Pro and Exchange share a common set of features. However, there are differences in use cases. By choosing an Exchange account, you get all the collaborative features, such as calendars and contact synchronisation. The Email Pro solution offers a few advanced features as well, but they are limited to webmail use only.

Choose the solution you want to migrate your MX Plan email accounts to before starting. To help you decide, the [OVHcloud professional email solutions page](/links/web/emails) offers a detailed comparison of the available services. You can also manage a mixed solution and use one or more Email Pro and Exchange accounts for the same domain name. Furthermore, if you need to migrate multiple accounts, we recommend that you set up a migration plan.

### Step 2: Ordering your Email Pro or Exchange accounts

This step is optional if you already have an Exchange or Email Pro service to which you are migrating.

Order the Email Pro or Exchange service you want. Follow the steps, then wait for the service to be installed. You will receive a confirmation email once the installation is complete.

:::info
Once an account has been delivered, it is essential to keep it in the "@configureme.me" state. It will be renamed during the migration.

:::


### Step 3: Carrying out the migration

Before starting your migration, identify the version of the MX Plan you are migrating from.

:::info
The email technology of your MX Plan offer may vary depending on the date of activation of your offer or if a migration has recently taken place. You can recognise it by its webmail interface. To identify the technology of your email service, open its <code className="action">Overview</code> tab in the OVHcloud Console: it is shown on the **Webmail** line of the **General information** section.

### 3.1 Manual migration of an MX Plan offer to Exchange, Email Pro or Zimbra

:::warning
<Region zones={['eu']}>
This section concerns MX Plan services using the Zimbra or OWA webmail technology. It does not apply to an MX Plan service using the Roundcube webmail, because its email accounts cannot be renamed.

To migrate an MX Plan service using the Roundcube webmail:

- to an Email Pro or Exchange platform, follow the section "[Automatic migration of an MX Plan Roundcube offer to Exchange or Email Pro](#roundcube-mxplan)" of this guide;
- to a Zimbra platform, follow our guide on [Zimbra - Migrate an MX Plan email account](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/migrate-mxplan-to-zimbra).

</Region>

<Region zones={['ca']}>
This section concerns MX Plan services using the OWA webmail technology.
</Region>

:::


:::warning
If you have just ordered your new email solution, first add the domain name to your email platform, then start your migration. <br/> - *For example, to migrate the "myemail@mydomain.ovh" account, you need to add the domain name "mydomain.ovh" to your platform.*

Open the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab of your platform, then click <code className="action">Add a domain</code>. In the **Add a domain** panel, follow the steps, then click <code className="action">Add</code>. Once the domain name is added, make sure that **Active** is present in the **Status** column.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Associated domains tab of an Exchange service, Add a domain button and Status column" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-platform/console/account_migration_adddomain.png" loading="lazy" />

<Region zones={['eu']}>
To find out more about adding a domain name, follow the [Email Pro guide](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/first-config), [Exchange guide](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-adding-domain) or [the Zimbra guide](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/getting-started-zimbra).
</Region>

<Region zones={['ca']}>
To find out more about adding a domain name, follow the [Exchange guide](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-adding-domain).
</Region>

:::


Your MX Plan migration will be done in 3 main steps: **Renaming**, **Creating** and **Migrating**.

<video className="thumbnail" autoPlay loop muted playsInline aria-label="Exchange" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-control-panel/mxplan-migration-configure-account.mp4" />

1\. **Rename** the MX Plan account to be migrated with a temporary name (example: to migrate the MX Plan account *john.smith@mydomain.ovh*, rename it to *john.smith01@mydomain.ovh*).

In the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab of your MX Plan service, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account, then <code className="action">Edit</code>. In the **Edit account** panel, change the part of the address before the "@", then click <code className="action">Edit</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Opened ⋮ menu of an MX Plan account, Edit option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-control-panel/console/mxplan-migration-configure-account01.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
Account modification is not immediate, please wait until the operation is complete before proceeding to the next step.

:::


2\. **Create** your email address on the new account on your Email Pro or Exchange platform (using the previous example, you will create *john.smith@mydomain.ovh* on your new platform).

In the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab (Exchange) or <code className="action">Accounts</code> tab (Email Pro) of your new platform, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of an unconfigured account, then <code className="action">Configure</code>. Fill in the form, then click <code className="action">Save</code> (Exchange) or <code className="action">Add</code> (Email Pro).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Opened ⋮ menu of an unconfigured Exchange account, Configure option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-platform/console/migration_platform05.png" loading="lazy" />

3\. **Migrate** the MX Plan account to your new platform account using our [OMM](/links/web/omm) (OVHcloud Mail Migrator) tool.

For more information on OMM, please read our guide on [Migrating email accounts via the OVHcloud Mail Migrator](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/omm-migrate-email-account-to-ovhcloud).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Create a migration form of OVHcloud Mail Migrator, with source and destination accounts" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-control-panel/mxplan-migration-configure-account03.png" loading="lazy" />

The migration time depends on the amount of content to migrate to your new account. This may vary from a few minutes to several hours.

:::warning
Check that you can find your items after the migration by logging into the [OVHcloud webmail](/links/web/email). **Do not delete or reset your original account until you have confirmed that everything has been migrated correctly** — once the original account is deleted, any items that were not migrated will be permanently lost.
:::

You can keep or delete the original account with the temporary name after this migration.

If you want to delete it, open the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab of your MX Plan, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account, then <code className="action">Reset</code>. In the **Reset account** window, click <code className="action">Reset</code>. Resetting deletes the data and name associated with the account, which can then be configured again.

### 3.2 Automatic migration of an MX Plan Roundcube offer to Exchange or Email Pro

:::warning
This section concerns only MX Plan services using the Roundcube webmail technology. There is no automatic migration for an MX Plan service using the Zimbra or OWA webmail: follow the section "[3.1 Manual migration of an MX Plan offer to Exchange, Email Pro or Zimbra](#all-mxplan)".

:::


:::info
Your OVHcloud account must be the administrative **and** technical contact for the MX Plan service to be migrated, **as well as** for the Email Pro or Exchange service you are migrating to. Otherwise, this destination service is not offered during the migration.

For more information on editing contacts, please refer to our guide on [managing contacts for your services](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/managing-contacts).

:::

If you cannot bring these contacts together on one OVHcloud account, do not start the automatic migration described below: migrate your address manually. As an MX Plan Roundcube account cannot be renamed, you must delete the address from the MX Plan before recreating it on your new service:

1. Export the content of your address from your email client, following our guide "[Manually migrate your email address](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/manual-email-migration)". Export your [Roundcube contacts](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-roundcube#export-contacts) as well.
2. Check that your export files contain all your items, then [delete the email account](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/delete-email-account) from your MX Plan.
3. Add your domain name to your Email Pro or Exchange service if it is not there yet (see section "[3.1 Manual migration of an MX Plan offer to Exchange, Email Pro or Zimbra](#all-mxplan)"), then create the same address on it.
4. Import your export file and your contacts into the new account.

:::warning
Deleting the MX Plan account is permanent: any item missing from your export files is lost. Before deleting the account, write down the configuration of your filters, auto-replies and delegations.
:::


You can migrate from two interfaces:<br/>

- **The configuration wizard for Hosted Exchange**, only if you have just ordered a Hosted Exchange service and have not yet set up anything on it.
- **The MX Plan section**, as soon as you have an Email Pro or Exchange service (already configured or not) and an MX Plan account that you want to migrate.

> As a reminder, before starting the migration, check in the <code className="action">Redirections</code> and <code className="action">Responders</code> tabs of your service that no **redirection** or **auto-reply** is configured on your MX Plan.
>
> <img className="thumbnail" alt="Redirections and Responders tabs of an MX Plan Roundcube service" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-control-panel/console/mxplan-legacy-redirect.png" loading="lazy" />

Once you are ready, follow the steps below, depending on the interface you have selected. The migration time depends on how much content is migrated to your new account. This may vary from a few minutes to several hours.

:::warning
Once the migration is confirmed, you will no longer be able to access your original MX Plan email account, or cancel the migration process. We strongly advise you to carry out this operation at a favourable time.

Even if you can no longer access your current email account, existing and newly arriving messages will not be lost. All will be immediately accessible from your new account.

:::

:::warning
Before starting the migration, note the following limits:

- You can only migrate one email account at a time.
- MX Plan filters, auto-replies, delegations and mailing lists are not migrated.
- When migrating to Email Pro, your Roundcube contacts are not migrated. Whatever the destination, [export them from Roundcube](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-roundcube#export-contacts) beforehand so that you can import them into your new account.
- Your original MX Plan mailbox is deleted within 15 days of the migration.

:::


#### **Migration with the Exchange configuration assistant**

A wizard will guide you through configuring your new Exchange service. During this process, you can select the MX Plan email accounts to migrate.

If the configuration wizard does not appear, the general information for the Exchange service will appear instead. In this case, you will need to migrate your accounts via the MX Plan interface.

#### **Migration from the MX Plan interface**

To migrate from this interface, go to the <code className="action">Web Cloud</code> section of the OVHcloud Console, expand <code className="action">Emails</code> in the left-hand menu, click <code className="action">MX Plan</code> and select the relevant domain. In the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the email account to migrate (also called the source account), then <code className="action">Migrate account</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Opened ⋮ menu of an MX Plan Roundcube account, Migrate account option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-control-panel/console/access_the_migration_tool.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Migrate an email account** panel, at the **Destination service** step, select the service you want to migrate the address to, then click <code className="action">Next</code>. If the panel displays "No destination service available", check that you have an Email Pro or Exchange service and that the domain name has been added to it, as described in the section "[3.1 Manual migration of an MX Plan offer to Exchange, Email Pro or Zimbra](#all-mxplan)".

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Migrate an email account panel, Destination service step" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-control-panel/console/account_migration_steps.png" loading="lazy" />

If the selected service has at least one "free" account (i.e. one that has not yet been set up), the migration is carried out to one of these accounts.

At the **Information** step, read the information displayed, tick the confirmation box, then click <code className="action">Next</code> to continue.

If you do not have a "free" account, the panel offers you the choice of another destination service or of ordering new accounts. Follow the steps, then wait until the accounts are installed to carry out the operation again.

At the **Confirmation** step, enter the password of the source email address (the one you want to migrate), then click <code className="action">Migrate</code>. Repeat the operation for each other account to migrate.

</Region>

### Step 4: Verifying your domain configuration

At this stage, your email addresses should already be migrated and functional. For security reasons, check in the OVHcloud Console that the configuration of your domain is correct.

<Region zones={['eu']}>
To do this, select the relevant Email Pro, Exchange or Zimbra service, then open the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab of your platform. Check the **Diagnostic** column: a record (MX, SRV, SPF, DKIM) displayed in red needs to be corrected.
</Region>

<Region zones={['ca']}>
To do this, select the relevant Exchange service, then open the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab of your platform. Check the **Diagnostic** column: a record (MX, SRV, SPF, DKIM) displayed in red needs to be corrected.
</Region>

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Diagnostic column of the Associated domains tab, MX, SRV, SPF and DKIM records" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-platform/console/check_the_dns_records_associated_domains.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
If you have just migrated or modified a DNS record for your domain, it may take a few hours to be updated in the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>.

:::


To modify the configuration, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the domain, then <code className="action">Diagnostics</code> (<code className="action">Diagnostic</code> for Zimbra), and carry out the requested operation. This operation can take up to 24 hours to propagate fully.

### Step 5: Using your migrated email accounts

Now, you can start using your migrated email accounts. To do this, OVHcloud offers an online interface (*web app*), available here: [webmail](/links/web/email). You will need to enter your email credentials.

If you have configured one of the migrated accounts on an email client (such as Outlook), you must set it up again. The login details for the OVHcloud server have changed following the migration. To help you make changes, please read the respective guides in the [Email Pro](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/landing-page-email-pro) and [Hosted Exchange](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/landing-page-mx-plan) guide sections. Even if you are unable to reconfigure the account immediately, access via the online application is still possible.

### Organise the contents of your email accounts following a migration <a name="content-after-migration"></a>

When you first log in to your new email account, the migrated content may be partially hidden. To view all items in the webmail, click on the arrow symbol next to the `Inbox` to reveal the subfolders. The migrated content of your old email account should appear.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Viewing the migrated folders and emails in the OVHcloud webmail" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-control-panel/owa_migrate_content.png" loading="lazy" />

The default folders, such as "sent items" or "trash" appear in English ("Sent items" and "Trash"), except for the folders you have created.

After a migration, check every folder and subfolder in your account to make sure nothing is missing.

### Migrating manually

You can also manually migrate your email addresses to your new OVHcloud email solution using only your email software. Please refer to our guide on [Migrating your email address manually](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/manual-email-migration). However, we recommend that you use this method only when the main methods are not possible.
