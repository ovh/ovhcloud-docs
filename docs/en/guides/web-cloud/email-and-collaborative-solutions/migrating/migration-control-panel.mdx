## Objective

The OVHcloud Console lets you automatically migrate an <Tooltip term="mxPlan">MX Plan</Tooltip> email address that uses the Roundcube webmail to an Email Pro or Exchange account: the address, its messages and its folders move to the new account in a single operation.

**Find out how to migrate an MX Plan Roundcube email address automatically to an Email Pro or Exchange account.**

:::info
This automatic migration only applies to MX Plan services using the Roundcube webmail, to Email Pro or Exchange. For any other case (MX Plan using the <Tooltip term="zimbra">Zimbra</Tooltip> or <Tooltip term="owa">OWA</Tooltip> webmail, migration to Zimbra, change of offer from Email Pro, Exchange or Zimbra), see our guide "[Migrate your email accounts to another OVHcloud email offer](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-platform)".
:::

## Requirements

- An MX Plan email address using the Roundcube webmail (as MX Plan standalone or included in an OVHcloud [Web Hosting plan](/links/web/hosting)).
- An [Email Pro](/links/web/email-pro) or [Exchange](/links/web/emails-hosted-exchange) service with at least one unconfigured account, which appears as "@configureme.me".
- Your OVHcloud account must be the administrative **and** technical contact for the MX Plan service to migrate, **as well as** for the destination Email Pro or Exchange service. Otherwise, this destination service is not offered during the migration. To change the contacts, see our guide "[Managing contacts for your services](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/managing-contacts)".
- **No redirection and no auto-reply set on the MX Plan email address to migrate**.

[[cpnav:web-mx-plan-console]]

:::tip
Whatever migration method you use, we strongly recommend backing up your mailbox beforehand, as a precaution against any data loss. Our guide [Manually migrate your email address](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/manual-email-migration) explains step by step how to back up a mailbox (for example by exporting it to a PST file): make this backup before you start the migration.
:::

## Instructions

### Step 1: Check the technology of your MX Plan

:::info
The email technology of your MX Plan offer may vary depending on the date of activation of your offer or if a migration has recently taken place. You can recognise it by its webmail interface. To identify the technology of your email service, open its <code className="action">Overview</code> tab in the OVHcloud Console: it is shown on the **Webmail** line of the **General information** section.

<WebmailLookup />

:::

If your MX Plan does not use the Roundcube webmail, the automatic migration is not available: follow our guide "[Migrate your email accounts to another OVHcloud email offer](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-platform)".

### Step 2: Prepare the destination service

If you do not have an Email Pro or Exchange service yet, order it, then wait until it is installed: you will receive an email once the installation is complete. To choose between these offers, see the [OVHcloud professional email solutions page](/links/web/emails), which offers a detailed comparison.

:::info
Once the account is ordered, leave it in the "@configureme.me" form: the migration configures it with your MX Plan address.

:::

If you cannot bring the administrative and technical contacts of both services together on one OVHcloud account, do not start the automatic migration: migrate your address manually. As an MX Plan Roundcube account cannot be renamed, you must delete the address from the MX Plan before recreating it on your new service: <a name="manual-migration"></a>

1. Export the content of your address from your email client, following our guide "[Manually migrate your email address](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/manual-email-migration)". Export your [Roundcube contacts](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-roundcube#export-contacts) as well.
2. Check that your export files contain all your items, then [delete the email account](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/delete-email-account) from your MX Plan.
3. Add your domain name to your Email Pro or Exchange service if it is not there yet (see "[step 1](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-platform#destination)" of our migration guide), then create the same address on it.
4. Import your export file and your contacts into the new account.

:::warning
Deleting the MX Plan account is permanent: any item missing from your export files is lost. Before deleting the account, write down the configuration of your filters, auto-replies and delegations.
:::

### Step 3: Start the migration <a name="roundcube-mxplan"></a>

:::warning
Before starting the migration, take into account the following limits:

- You can only migrate one email account at a time.
- MX Plan filters, auto-replies, delegations and mailing lists are not migrated.
- When migrating to Email Pro, your Roundcube contacts are not migrated. Whatever the destination, [export them from Roundcube](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/email-roundcube#export-contacts) beforehand so that you can import them into your new account.
- Your original MX Plan mailbox is deleted within 15 days of the migration.
- Once the migration is confirmed, you will no longer be able to access your original MX Plan email address or cancel the process. Existing and newly arriving messages are not lost: they are accessible from your new account. Choose a convenient time for this operation.

:::

{/* v8-gap: CG-086 (open) — the Hosted Exchange configuration assistant no longer offers to migrate MX Plan accounts; only the MX Plan interface is described. */}

> Before starting the migration, check in the <code className="action">Redirections</code> and <code className="action">Responders</code> tabs of your MX Plan service that no **redirection** or **auto-reply** is configured on the address to migrate.
>
> <img className="thumbnail" alt="Redirections and Responders tabs of an MX Plan Roundcube service" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-control-panel/console/mxplan-legacy-redirect.png" loading="lazy" />

Go to the <code className="action">Web Cloud</code> section of the OVHcloud Console, expand <code className="action">Emails</code> in the left-hand menu, click <code className="action">MX Plan</code> and select the relevant domain. In the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the email account to migrate (also called the source account), then <code className="action">Migrate account</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Opened ⋮ menu of an MX Plan Roundcube account, Migrate account option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-control-panel/console/access_the_migration_tool.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Migrate an email account** panel, at the **Destination service** step, select the service you want to migrate the address to, then click <code className="action">Next</code>. If the panel displays "No destination service available", check that you have an Email Pro or Exchange service, that your OVHcloud account is the administrative and technical contact of both services, as described in the [requirements](#requirements), and that the domain name has been added to the service.

{/* v8-defect: CD-132 — with several services of a type (Hosted or Private Exchange), choosing one in the tile's list does not enable "Next". */}

:::info
If you have several Hosted Exchange or Private Exchange services, the <code className="action">Next</code> button may stay greyed out after you choose a service from the list. In this case, migrate your address manually, as described in [step 2](#manual-migration).
:::

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Migrate an email account panel, Destination service step" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-control-panel/console/account_migration_steps.png" loading="lazy" />

If the selected service has at least one "free" account (i.e. one that has not yet been set up), the migration uses one of them.

At the **Information** step, read the information displayed, tick the confirmation box, then click <code className="action">Next</code> to continue.

If you do not have a "free" account, the panel offers you the choice of another destination service or of ordering new accounts. Follow the steps, then wait until the accounts are installed to carry out the operation again.

At the **Confirmation** step, enter the password of the source email address (the one you want to migrate), then click <code className="action">Migrate</code>. Repeat the operation for each other account to migrate.

The migration takes from a few minutes to several hours, depending on how much content is migrated to your new account.

### Step 4: Check and use your migrated address

Once the migration is complete:

- check the configuration of your domain name, as described in "[step 4](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-platform#check-domain)" of our migration guide;
- log in to the [webmail](/links/web/email) with the credentials of your email address and find the migrated content, as described in "[step 5](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-platform#content-after-migration)" of the same guide;
- if your address was configured on an email client, set it up again: the server login details have changed. See the [Email Pro](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/landing-page-email-pro) and [Hosted Exchange](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/landing-page-microsoft-exchange) guides.
