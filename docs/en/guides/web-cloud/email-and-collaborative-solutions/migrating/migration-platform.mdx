## Requirements

<Region zones={['eu']}>
- A **"source"** platform with configured [Exchange](/links/web/emails-hosted-exchange) or [Email Pro](/links/web/email-pro) accounts or [Zimbra](/links/web/emails-zimbra).
- A "**destination**" platform with [Exchange](/links/web/emails-hosted-exchange), [Email Pro](/links/web/email-pro), [Zimbra](/links/web/emails-zimbra) or MX Plan accounts (via the MX Plan solution or included in [OVHcloud Web Hosting plans](/links/web/hosting)). This platform must have unconfigured accounts or be available to host the email accounts that need to be migrated.
</Region>

<Region zones={['ca']}>
- A **"source"** platform with configured [Exchange](/links/web/emails-hosted-exchange) accounts.
- A "**destination**" platform with [Exchange](/links/web/emails-hosted-exchange) or MX Plan accounts (via the MX Plan solution or included in [OVHcloud Web Hosting plans](/links/web/hosting)). This platform must have unconfigured accounts or be available to host the email accounts that need to be migrated.
</Region>

:::tip
Whatever migration method you use, we strongly recommend backing up your mailbox beforehand, as a precaution against any data loss. Our guide [Manually migrate your email address](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/manual-email-migration) explains step by step how to back up a mailbox (for example by exporting it to a PST file): make this backup before you start the migration.
:::

## Instructions

### Configuring the destination platform

:::warning
If you have just ordered your new email offer, add the domain name to your email platform before starting your migration. If you are migrating to an MX Plan platform, the attached domain name being "fixed", you can directly proceed to the [next step](#accountsmigration).

Open the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab of your platform, then click <code className="action">Add a domain</code>. In the **Add a domain** panel, follow the steps, then click <code className="action">Add</code>. Once the domain name is added, make sure **Active** is shown in the **Status** column.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Associated domains tab of an Exchange service, Add a domain button and Status column" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-platform/console/account_migration_adddomain.png" loading="lazy" />

For more details on adding a domain name, follow [the Email Pro guide](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/first-config), [the Exchange guide](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-adding-domain) or [the Zimbra guide](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/getting-started-zimbra).

:::


### Migrate email accounts <a name="accountsmigration"></a>

Your email accounts will be migrated in 3 main steps: **Rename** the original email account, **create** the new email account and **migrate** from the original platform to the new one.

<video className="thumbnail" autoPlay loop muted playsInline aria-label="email-migration" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-platform/migration_platform03.mp4" />

:::warning
Special cases:

- If you need to migrate an **Exchange or Zimbra PRO** account to an **Email Pro** or **Zimbra STARTER** account, you must ensure that your email accounts do not exceed 15 GB, the capacity shared by both Email Pro and Zimbra STARTER. Collaborative features, calendar and contact synchronization are not available on Email Pro or Zimbra STARTER and cannot be migrated.
- If you need to migrate an **Exchange, Email Pro or Zimbra** account to an **MX Plan** account, you must ensure that your email account does not exceed 5 GB. Collaborative features, calendar and contact synchronization are not available on MX Plan and cannot be migrated.

:::


#### Rename

Rename the email account to be migrated with a provisional name (example: to migrate the email account *john.smith@mydomain.ovh*, rename it to *john.smith01@mydomain.ovh*).

Open the accounts tab of your email platform: <code className="action">Account management</code> for Exchange, <code className="action">Accounts</code> for Email Pro, <code className="action">Email account</code> for Zimbra. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account, then <code className="action">Edit</code> (<code className="action">Settings</code> for Zimbra). Change the part of the address before the "@", then click <code className="action">Save</code> (<code className="action">Edit</code> for Email Pro).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Opened ⋮ menu of an Exchange account, Edit option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-platform/console/migration_platform04.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Create

Re-create your email address on the new account for your Email Pro, Exchange or MX Plan platform. (Using the previous example, you will create *john.smith@mydomain.ovh* on your new platform.)

On an Exchange or Email Pro platform, open the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab (Exchange) or <code className="action">Accounts</code> tab (Email Pro). Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of an unconfigured account (**Active not configured** status), then <code className="action">Configure</code>. Fill in the form, then click <code className="action">Save</code> (Exchange) or <code className="action">Add</code> (Email Pro). On an MX Plan platform, open the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab, click <code className="action">Add account</code>, fill in the **Create email account** panel, then click <code className="action">Add</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Opened ⋮ menu of an unconfigured Exchange account, Configure option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-platform/console/migration_platform05.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Migrate

:::warning
Only the data of your email accounts will be migrated (emails, contacts, calendars, inbox rules, etc.). The features linked to your platform will need to be recreated on the new platform:

- [Alias](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/feature-redirections)
- [Delegated rights](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/permissions-delegation)
- [Groups](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-groups)
- External contacts
- [Footer](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-footers)

:::


Migrate the source email account to your new platform account using our [OMM](/links/web/omm) tool (OVHcloud Mail Migrator).

For more information on OMM, please read our guide on [Migrating email accounts via the OVHcloud Mail Migrator](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/omm-migrate-email-account-to-ovhcloud).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Migrating the email account with the OVHcloud Mail Migrator" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-platform/migration_platform06.png" loading="lazy" />

The migration time depends on the amount of data to migrate to your new account. This may vary from a few minutes to several hours.

:::warning
After the migration, verify that all of your elements are present by logging into webmail at [webmail](/links/web/email). **Do not delete or reset your original account until you have confirmed that everything has been migrated correctly** — once the original account is deleted, any items that were not migrated will be permanently lost.
:::

Once the migration is complete, you can keep or delete the original account with the temporary name.

If you would like to delete it, open the accounts tab of your original email platform, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account, then <code className="action">Reset this account</code> (Exchange) or <code className="action">Reset</code> (Email Pro), and confirm in the **Reset an account** window with <code className="action">Confirm</code> (Exchange) or <code className="action">Reset</code> (Email Pro). Resetting deletes the account's data and name, and the account remains billed. On Zimbra, click <code className="action">Delete</code>, then <code className="action">Delete</code> in the **Delete account** window: the deletion is permanent.

### Check or modify your domain configuration

At this stage, your email addresses should already be migrated and functional. For security reasons, check in the OVHcloud Console that your domain is correctly configured.

To do this, select the relevant Email Pro, Exchange or Zimbra service, then open the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab of your platform. Check the **Diagnostic** column: a record (MX, SRV, SPF, DKIM) displayed in red needs to be corrected.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Diagnostic column of the Associated domains tab, MX, SRV, SPF and DKIM records" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-platform/console/check_the_dns_records_associated_domains.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
If you have just migrated or modified a DNS record for your domain, it may take a few hours to be updated when you go to the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>.

:::


To modify the configuration, click the badge of the relevant record (`MX`, `SRV`, `SPF` or `DKIM`) in the **Diagnostic** column, and complete the operation shown in the panel that opens. DNS changes can take up to 24 hours to propagate fully.

### Use your migrated email addresses

Now, you can start using your migrated email addresses. To do this, OVHcloud offers a web client (_web app_), available here [webmail](/links/web/email). You will need to enter your email credentials.

If you have configured one of the migrated accounts on a local email client (e.g. Outlook, Thunderbird), you will need to configure it again. The login details for the OVHcloud server have changed following the migration.

:::info
You can also manually migrate external email addresses to OVHcloud by using our [OVHcloud Mail Migrator](/links/web/omm) tool (OMM). To do this, you must have the login details (user, password, servers) of the source email and the destination email.

:::
