## Objective

<Region zones={['eu']}>
Your needs are changing and you want to move from one OVHcloud email offer to another (<Tooltip term="mxPlan">MX Plan</Tooltip>, Email Pro, Exchange or <Tooltip term="zimbra">Zimbra</Tooltip>) while keeping your email addresses? This guide helps you choose the method that suits your case, then describes the migration step by step.
</Region>

**Find out how to migrate your email accounts to another OVHcloud email offer.**

## Requirements

<Region zones={['eu']}>

- A **"source"** email offer: MX Plan (via the MX Plan solution or included in an [OVHcloud web hosting plan](/links/web/hosting)), [Email Pro](/links/web/email-pro), [Exchange](/links/web/emails-hosted-exchange) or [Zimbra](/links/web/emails-zimbra).
- A **"destination"** email offer: MX Plan, [Email Pro](/links/web/email-pro), [Exchange](/links/web/emails-hosted-exchange) or [Zimbra](/links/web/emails-zimbra), with one available account for each address to migrate. On Email Pro and Exchange, this is an unconfigured account, which appears as "@configureme.me".

</Region>

<Region zones={['ca']}>

- A **"source"** email offer: MX Plan (via the MX Plan solution or included in an [OVHcloud web hosting plan](/links/web/hosting)) or [Exchange](/links/web/emails-hosted-exchange).
- A **"destination"** email offer: MX Plan or [Exchange](/links/web/emails-hosted-exchange), with one available account for each address to migrate. On Exchange, this is an unconfigured account, which appears as "@configureme.me".

</Region>

- For an MX Plan address, **no redirection configured on the email address to migrate**.

:::tip
Whatever migration method you use, we strongly recommend backing up your mailbox beforehand, as a precaution against any data loss. Our guide "[Manually migrate your email address](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/manual-email-migration)" explains step by step how to back up a mailbox (for example by exporting it to a PST file): make this backup before you start the migration.
:::

## Instructions

### Choose the migration method <a name="choose-method"></a>

Find your situation in the table below, then follow the method shown.

<Region zones={['eu']}>

| Your situation | Method | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|
| You are changing OVHcloud email offer and keeping the same address (from MX Plan, Email Pro, Exchange or Zimbra to another of these offers) (except the MX Plan Roundcube cases below) | Rename, create, then migrate with OVHcloud Mail Migrator | This guide, from [step 1](#destination) |
| Your address is on an MX Plan using the Roundcube webmail and you are migrating to Email Pro or Exchange | Automatic migration from the OVHcloud Console | Our guide "[MX Plan - Migrate automatically to Email Pro or Exchange](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-control-panel)" |
| Your address is with another provider (Gmail, Yahoo or any <Tooltip term="imap">IMAP</Tooltip> or <Tooltip term="pop">POP</Tooltip> service) and you are migrating to OVHcloud, or the other way round | OVHcloud Mail Migrator | Our guide "[Migrate email accounts via OVHcloud Mail Migrator](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/omm-migrate-email-account-to-ovhcloud)" |
| Your emails are only stored on your computer (POP configuration or local archiving), or you have an export file (PST, ICS) | File import with OVHcloud Mail Migrator, or from your email client | Our guides "[Migrate email accounts via OVHcloud Mail Migrator](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/omm-migrate-email-account-to-ovhcloud)" and "[Manually migrate your email address](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/manual-email-migration)" |
| None of the methods above is possible | Export then import from your email client | Our guide "[Manually migrate your email address](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/manual-email-migration)" |

</Region>

- **MX Plan Roundcube to Email Pro or Exchange**: use the [automatic migration](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-control-panel).
- **MX Plan Roundcube to Zimbra**: an MX Plan Roundcube account cannot be renamed. Follow the steps in this guide, applying the [variant for a source account that cannot be renamed](#no-rename).

### Step 1: Prepare the destination offer <a name="destination"></a>

Before you continue, choose the offer you want to migrate your email addresses to. To help you choose, see the [OVHcloud professional email solutions](/links/web/emails) page, which offers a detailed comparison of the offers. If you need to migrate several accounts, we recommend setting up a migration plan.

<Region zones={['eu']}>
If you do not have the destination offer yet, order it and wait until it is installed: you will receive an email once the installation is complete. On Email Pro and Exchange, leave the ordered accounts in the "@configureme.me" form: they will be configured during the migration.
</Region>

<Region zones={['ca']}>
If you do not have the destination offer yet, order it and wait until it is installed: you will receive an email once the installation is complete. On Exchange, leave the ordered accounts in the "@configureme.me" form: they will be configured during the migration.
</Region>

:::warning
If you have just ordered your new email offer, first add the domain name of your addresses to this offer. For example, to migrate the address *john.smith@mydomain.ovh*, add the domain name *mydomain.ovh*. If you are migrating to an MX Plan offer, the attached domain name is fixed: go directly to the [next step](#accountsmigration).

Open the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab of your offer, then click <code className="action">Add a domain</code>. In the **Add a domain** panel, follow the steps, then click <code className="action">Add</code>. Once the domain name is added, make sure **Active** is shown in the **Status** column.

<Region zones={['eu']}>
For more details on adding a domain name, follow [the Email Pro guide](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/first-config), [the Exchange guide](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-adding-domain) or [the Zimbra guide](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/getting-started-zimbra).
</Region>

For more details on adding a domain name, follow [the Exchange guide](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/exchange-adding-domain).

### Step 2: Migrate the email accounts <a name="accountsmigration"></a>

Each email account is migrated in 3 stages: **rename** the original account, **create** the address on the destination offer, then **migrate** the content with OVHcloud Mail Migrator.

<video className="thumbnail" autoPlay loop muted playsInline aria-label="Email migration" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-platform/migration_platform03.mp4" />

:::warning
Before you start, take into account the limits of the destination offer:

<Region zones={['eu']}>

- To **Email Pro** or **Zimbra STARTER**, from an **Exchange** or **Zimbra PRO** account: your email accounts must not exceed 15 GB, the capacity shared by Email Pro and Zimbra STARTER. Collaborative features, calendar and contact synchronisation are not available on Email Pro or Zimbra STARTER and cannot be migrated.
- To **MX Plan**: your email account must not exceed 5 GB. Collaborative features, calendar and contact synchronisation are not available on MX Plan and cannot be migrated.

</Region>

<Region zones={['ca']}>

- To **MX Plan**: your email account must not exceed 5 GB. Collaborative features, calendar and contact synchronisation are not available on MX Plan and cannot be migrated.

</Region>

:::

#### Rename the original account

Rename the email account to migrate with a temporary name. For example, to migrate the email account *john.smith@mydomain.ovh*, rename it to *john.smith01@mydomain.ovh*.

<Region zones={['eu']}>
Open the accounts tab of your original email offer: <code className="action">Email account</code> for MX Plan and Zimbra, <code className="action">Account management</code> for Exchange, <code className="action">Accounts</code> for Email Pro. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account, then <code className="action">Edit</code> (<code className="action">Settings</code> for Zimbra). Change the part of the address before the "@", then click <code className="action">Save</code> (<code className="action">Edit</code> for MX Plan and Email Pro).
</Region>

<Region zones={['ca']}>
Open the accounts tab of your original email offer: <code className="action">Email account</code> for MX Plan, <code className="action">Account management</code> for Exchange. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account, then <code className="action">Edit</code>. Change the part of the address before the "@", then click <code className="action">Save</code> (<code className="action">Edit</code> for MX Plan).
</Region>

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Opened ⋮ menu of an Exchange account, Edit option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-platform/console/migration_platform04.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
The change to the account is not immediate: wait until the operation is complete before you go to the next stage.
:::

#### Create the address on the destination offer

Create your email address on the destination offer. Using the previous example, create *john.smith@mydomain.ovh*.

<Region zones={['eu']}>

- **Email Pro or Exchange**: open the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab (Exchange) or <code className="action">Accounts</code> tab (Email Pro). Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of an unconfigured account (**Active not configured** status), then <code className="action">Configure</code>. Fill in the form, then click <code className="action">Save</code> (Exchange) or <code className="action">Add</code> (Email Pro).
- **MX Plan**: open the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab, click <code className="action">Add account</code>, fill in the **Add account** panel, then click <code className="action">Add</code>.
- **Zimbra**: follow the "[Create an email account](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/zimbra/getting-started-zimbra#emails-create)" section of our guide "Getting started with the Zimbra solution".

</Region>

<Region zones={['ca']}>

- **Exchange**: open the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of an unconfigured account (**Active not configured** status), then <code className="action">Configure</code>. Fill in the form, then click <code className="action">Save</code>.
- **MX Plan**: open the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab, click <code className="action">Add account</code>, fill in the **Add account** panel, then click <code className="action">Add</code>.

</Region>

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Opened ⋮ menu of an unconfigured Exchange account, Configure option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-platform/console/migration_platform05.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Migrate the content with OVHcloud Mail Migrator

Migrate the original email account (*john.smith01@mydomain.ovh* in our example) to the account created on the destination offer (*john.smith@mydomain.ovh*) using our [OVHcloud Mail Migrator](/links/web/omm) (OMM) tool. Choose the type of each account in the `OVHcloud` category, or `Autodetect`.

For the detailed steps, see our guide "[Migrate email accounts via OVHcloud Mail Migrator](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/omm-migrate-email-account-to-ovhcloud)".

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Create a migration form of OVHcloud Mail Migrator, with source and destination accounts" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-platform/migration_platform06.png" loading="lazy" />

OMM migrates your emails and, when the destination offer supports them, your contacts, calendars and tasks. The items specific to your original offer are not migrated and must be recreated on the destination offer:

- [aliases](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/feature-redirections);
- [delegated rights](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/permissions-delegation);
- [groups](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-groups);
- external contacts;
- [footers](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/feature-footers).

The migration takes from a few minutes to several hours, depending on the amount of data to migrate.

<Region zones={['eu']}>

#### Variant: the original account cannot be renamed <a name="no-rename"></a>

An MX Plan account using the Roundcube webmail cannot be renamed. To migrate it to Zimbra, reverse the order: the temporary address is carried by the destination account, which is renamed at the end.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Migration with a temporary destination account: contact@mydomain.ovh migrated to contact2@mydomain.ovh, then the original address deleted and contact2 renamed to contact" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-platform/migration-temporary-destination.png" loading="lazy" />

1. Create an account with a temporary name on Zimbra, for example *contact2@mydomain.ovh* to migrate *contact@mydomain.ovh*.
2. Migrate the content of the MX Plan account to this temporary account with OVHcloud Mail Migrator, as described above.
3. Check that all your items have been migrated, then [delete the original MX Plan email account](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/delete-email-account).
4. Once the deletion is effective, rename the temporary Zimbra account with the original address.

In the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab of your Zimbra service, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the temporary account, then <code className="action">Settings</code>. On the **Account settings** page, change the part before the "@" in the **Email address** field (the domain name cannot be changed), then click <code className="action">Save</code>.

In this case, Step 3 does not apply: go directly to [Step 4](#check-domain).

</Region>

### Step 3: Delete the original account

:::warning
After the migration, check that you can find all your items by logging in to the [webmail](/links/web/email). **Do not delete your original address until you have confirmed that everything has been migrated**: once the address is deleted, any items that were not migrated will be permanently lost.
:::

Once the migration is checked, you can keep or delete the original account, which carries the temporary name.

To delete it, open the accounts tab of your original email offer, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account, then:

<Region zones={['eu']}>

- **Exchange or Email Pro**: click <code className="action">Reset this account</code> (Exchange) or <code className="action">Reset</code> (Email Pro), then confirm in the **Reset an account** window with <code className="action">Confirm</code> (Exchange) or <code className="action">Reset</code> (Email Pro). Resetting deletes the account's data and name; the account remains billed and can be configured again.
- **MX Plan**: click <code className="action">Reset</code>, then <code className="action">Reset</code> in the **Reset account** window. Resetting deletes the account's data and name; the account can then be configured again.
- **Zimbra**: click <code className="action">Delete</code>, then <code className="action">Delete</code> in the **Delete account** window. The deletion is permanent.

</Region>

<Region zones={['ca']}>

- **Exchange**: click <code className="action">Reset this account</code>, then confirm in the **Reset an account** window with <code className="action">Confirm</code>. Resetting deletes the account's data and name; the account remains billed and can be configured again.
- **MX Plan**: click <code className="action">Reset</code>, then <code className="action">Reset</code> in the **Reset account** window. Resetting deletes the account's data and name; the account can then be configured again.

</Region>

### Step 4: Check your domain configuration <a name="check-domain"></a>

At this stage, your email addresses should already be migrated and working. For security reasons, check in the OVHcloud Console that your domain is correctly configured.

<Region zones={['eu']}>
To do this, select the relevant Email Pro, Exchange or Zimbra service, then open the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab of your offer. Check the **Diagnostic** column: a record (MX, SRV, <Tooltip term="spf">SPF</Tooltip>, <Tooltip term="dkim">DKIM</Tooltip>) displayed in red needs to be corrected.
</Region>

<Region zones={['ca']}>
To do this, select the relevant Exchange service, then open the <code className="action">Associated domains</code> tab of your offer. Check the **Diagnostic** column: a record (MX, SRV, SPF, DKIM) displayed in red needs to be corrected.
</Region>

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Diagnostic column of the Associated domains tab, MX, SRV, SPF and DKIM records" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/migrating/migration-platform/console/check_the_dns_records_associated_domains.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
If you have just migrated or modified a DNS record for your domain, it may take a few hours to be updated in the OVHcloud Console.

:::

To modify the configuration, click the badge of the relevant record (`MX`, `SRV`, `SPF` or `DKIM`) in the **Diagnostic** column, and carry out the operation shown in the panel that opens. <Tooltip term="dnsPropagation">Propagation</Tooltip> can take up to 24 hours.

### Step 5: Use your migrated email addresses <a name="content-after-migration"></a>

You can now use your migrated email addresses from the [webmail](/links/web/email), with the credentials of your email address.

If you have configured one of the migrated addresses on an email client (e.g. Outlook, Thunderbird), set it up again: the login details for the OVHcloud server have changed with the migration. For help, see the guides for your new offer.

When you first log in to your new account, the migrated content may be partially hidden. In the webmail, click the arrow next to **Inbox** to reveal the subfolders: the migrated content of your old email account appears there. Default folders such as "Sent Items" or "Trash" may keep their English names, whatever the language of your webmail; the folders you created are not affected.
