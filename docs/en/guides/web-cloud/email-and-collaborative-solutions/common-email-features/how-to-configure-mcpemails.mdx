## Objective

OVHcloud email accounts can be used with compatible third-party email clients and online services. MCPEmails is a hosted MCP (Model Context Protocol) server, published by Albretsen AS (Norway) under the AGPL-3.0 licence, that gives AI assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor access to a mailbox over IMAP and SMTP. The assistant can then read, search, send, reply to and organise emails in that mailbox.

**This guide explains how to connect your OVHcloud email address to an AI assistant through MCPEmails.**

:::info
**Third-party service**

MCPEmails is not an OVHcloud product and is outside the scope of OVHcloud support. This guide is maintained by its publisher, Albretsen AS (Norway), who can be reached at hello@mcpemails.com. The service publishes its [terms of use](https://mcpemails.com/terms) and [privacy policy](https://mcpemails.com/privacy), and its source code is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/Albretsen/MCPEmails).
:::

## Instructions

### Step 1: Identify your email solution and your server names

The server names depend on the OVHcloud email solution the address belongs to. All three solutions serve email at your own domain name, so the address itself does not tell you which one you have. Check the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Control Panel</ManagerLink>, in the <code className="action">Web Cloud</code> section: MX Plan addresses are listed under <code className="action">Emails</code>, while Email Pro and Exchange each have their own section.

Use the settings for your solution. The username is always your **full** email address, and the password is the password of the email account itself (not your OVHcloud customer account password).

<Region zones={['eu']}>

| Solution | Incoming server (IMAP) | Outgoing server (SMTP) |
|:---|:---|:---|
| MX Plan | imap.mail.ovh.net **or** ssl0.ovh.net, port 993, SSL/TLS | smtp.mail.ovh.net **or** ssl0.ovh.net, port 465, SSL/TLS (port 587 with STARTTLS also works) |
| Email Pro | pro?.mail.ovh.net, port 993, SSL/TLS | pro?.mail.ovh.net, port 587, STARTTLS |
| Exchange | ex?.mail.ovh.net, port 993, SSL/TLS | ex?.mail.ovh.net, port 587, STARTTLS |

</Region>

:::info
<Region zones={['eu']}>
For Email Pro and Exchange, replace the "?" with the number designating the server of your service. It cannot be guessed from your domain name. Go to the <ManagerLink to="/#/web/email_pro">Email Pro</ManagerLink> or <ManagerLink to="/#/web/exchange">Exchange</ManagerLink> section of your OVHcloud Control Panel: the server name is displayed in the **Connection** section of the <code className="action">General information</code> tab.
</Region>

<Region zones={['ca', 'apac']}>
These are the server names given in the OVHcloud guides for your solution. The port and security settings are the same as for any other email client.
</Region>

:::

These are the same settings as in the OVHcloud guides for Thunderbird, Outlook and the other email clients listed in the sidebar for your solution.

### Step 2: Connect the email address in MCPEmails

1. Log in to the [MCPEmails dashboard](https://mcpemails.com/) and click <code className="action">Connect inbox</code>.
2. Enter your OVHcloud email address. MCPEmails looks up the mail servers for the domain. If it cannot find them, or if the servers it proposes are not the ones from Step 1, choose <code className="action">Other (IMAP)</code>.
3. Enter the password of the email account in the <code className="action">Password</code> field.
4. Open <code className="action">Advanced</code> and check the <code className="action">Incoming server (IMAP)</code>, <code className="action">Outgoing server (SMTP)</code> and <code className="action">Port</code> fields against the table in Step 1. For Email Pro and Exchange, enter the numbered server name from your OVHcloud Control Panel, since it cannot be prefilled.
5. Click <code className="action">Connect</code>. MCPEmails connects to the OVHcloud servers, signs in and reads the mailbox folder list. If the sign-in is refused, check that you used the email account password and not your OVHcloud customer account password, and that the server names match your solution.

:::info
If you change the password of the email account later in the OVHcloud Control Panel, update it in the MCPEmails dashboard as well, otherwise the connection stops working.
:::

### Step 3: Add MCPEmails to your AI assistant

MCPEmails exposes one MCP server URL for every client: `https://mcpemails.com/api/mcp`

- **Claude** (claude.ai or Claude Desktop): add a custom connector in the connectors settings and paste the URL above. Claude opens the MCPEmails authorisation page; sign in with your MCPEmails account and approve the access.
- **ChatGPT**: custom MCP connectors are added from the connector settings on chatgpt.com, on the ChatGPT plans for which OpenAI allows them. Paste the URL above and approve the access on the MCPEmails authorisation page.
- **Cursor** and other MCP clients with OAuth support: add an MCP server with the URL above and complete the authorisation. Clients without OAuth support use an API key created in the MCPEmails dashboard instead.

The step-by-step instructions for each client are kept up to date in the [MCPEmails documentation](https://mcpemails.com/docs).

### Step 4: Use the email address with your assistant

Once the mailbox is connected and the assistant is authorised, you can ask the assistant to list your unread messages, search the mailbox, draft or send a reply, or move messages between folders. Messages are fetched live from the OVHcloud servers over IMAP for each request, and sending goes through the OVHcloud SMTP server, so the emails sent by the assistant appear in your mailbox like any other.

You can keep using your email address as before, in your usual email client or through the [OVHcloud webmail](/links/web/email).

### Things to know

- **Folder names**: MX Plan matches folders by name. If your mailbox folders are in French (for example `Elements envoyes` or `Corbeille`), tell the assistant the folder name when it looks for Sent or Trash.
- **Moving messages on MX Plan**: the MX Plan IMAP server does not advertise the MOVE extension, so a move is performed as a copy followed by a deletion. If the connection drops in between, a message can briefly exist in both folders.
- **Disconnecting**: you can remove the mailbox from MCPEmails at any time from its dashboard. Changing the email account password in the OVHcloud Control Panel also ends the access.
