## Objective

In this guide, you can find information and help on configuring your **redirections** and **alias** emails, such as sending emails received from address A to address B.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="emails" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/feature-redirections/schema-redirect00.png" loading="lazy" />

**This guide explains how to manage aliases and email redirections.**

<details>

<summary>What is an email redirection?</summary>


You can use redirections to change the routing of an email sent to an email address, forwarding it to one or more other email addresses.

For example, you may want emails sent to **contact@mydomain.ovh** forwarded to **john.smith@otherdomain.ovh**. This way, you can automatically send emails received by **contact@mydomain.ovh** to **john.smith@otherdomain.ovh**.

</details>


<details>

<summary>What is an email alias?</summary>


Unlike redirection, an alias address is not an actual email account, but functions as a secondary address for the email account associated with it.

By creating an alias for your email account, you can give your contacts a "masking" address instead of revealing your personal email address to them. An email account can have multiple aliases.

For example, your email address is **john.smith@mydomain.ovh** and your alias is **information@mydomain.ovh**. You can then send your contacts the address **information@mydomain.ovh** and receive emails on **john.smith@mydomain.ovh**, without the sender knowing the address **john.smith@mydomain.ovh**.

</details>


<details>

##### MX Plan / redirect <a name="redirect-manager-mxplan"></a>

From your MX Plan service, select the domain concerned.

The steps below create a **simple redirection** or a **redirection with a local copy** (see [diagrams 1 and 2](#diagram) at the beginning of this guide). They are the same for the Redirect solution and for any MX Plan email technology:

1. Click the <code className="action">Redirections</code> tab. The table of redirections that are already active appears.
1. Click the <code className="action">Create a redirection</code> button, above the table.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Redirections tab of an MX Plan service, Create a redirection button" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/feature-redirections/console/mxplan-legacy-1.png" loading="lazy" />

1. In the **Create a redirection** window, enter the following information:
    - **From address**: enter the part of the email address to redirect that comes before "@". The subdomain field is optional: leave it empty to use the main domain.
    - **To address**: enter the destination address, whether it is one of your OVHcloud email addresses or an external email address.
    - **Keep a local copy**: tick this box to receive the email on your main address as well as on the redirection address (see [diagram 2](#diagram) at the beginning of this guide). Leave it unticked to send the email only to the redirection address (see [diagram 1](#diagram) at the beginning of this guide).

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Create a redirection window with the From address and To address fields" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/feature-redirections/console/mxplan-legacy-3.png" loading="lazy" />

1. Click <code className="action">Create</code>.

:::info
To modify the destination address or delete a redirection, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the redirection concerned, then <code className="action">Edit</code> or <code className="action">Delete</code>.

:::

:::info
When you tick the **Keep a local copy** box, a redirection from the email address to itself is created automatically in the redirections list: it materialises this local copy.

:::


:::warning
A redirection also forwards the spam the email address receives, which can get it blocked for spam. To redirect emails without the spam, replace the redirection with a filter that excludes emails whose subject contains `[SPAM]`. The OVHcloud anti-spam system adds this tag to the subject of the unwanted emails it delivers to the inbox, if your domain uses the OVHcloud MX servers.

<Region zones={['eu']}>
- **MX Plan with the Roundcube webmail**: create a filter in the OVHcloud Console, with the rule <code className="action">Subject</code>, <code className="action">Does not contain</code>, `[SPAM]` and the action <code className="action">Redirect to</code>. See our guide [Creating filters for your email addresses](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/mx-plan/feature-filters).
</Region>
- **MX Plan with the OWA webmail**: create the redirection in the webmail and add an exception to it (see the "[Outlook Web App (OWA)](#redirect-webmail-owa)" section).

:::

<Region zones={['eu', 'ca']}>

##### Exchange <a name="redirect-manager-exchange"></a>

The redirection of an Exchange account is set in the account settings:

1. In your Exchange platform, click the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab, then the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account, then <code className="action">Edit</code>.
2. On the **Edit an Exchange account** page, enter the address to which emails should be redirected in the **Forwarding email** field.
3. Tick the **Keep a copy** box to keep a copy of the emails in the account (**redirection with local copy**, [diagram 2](#diagram)). If the box is not ticked, it is a **simple redirection** ([diagram 1](#diagram)).
4. Click <code className="action">Save</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Edit an Exchange account page, Forwarding email field and Keep a copy box" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/feature-redirections/console/exchange-redirect.png" loading="lazy" />

:::warning
The redirection also forwards the spam the account receives, which can get it blocked for spam. If this happens, remove the redirection and see our guide [What to do if your email address is blocked for spam](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/troubleshooting/locked-for-spam).
:::

</Region>

### Delete an alias <a name="alias-delete"></a>

#### Exchange / Email Pro / MX Plan <a name="alias-delete-exchange-emp-mxplan"></a>

1. From your email service (Exchange, Email Pro or MX Plan), open the list of your email accounts, as for [creating an alias](#alias-exchange-emp-mxplan).
1. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the email address concerned, then <code className="action">Configure aliases</code> (Exchange) or <code className="action">Manage aliases</code> (Email Pro and MX Plan).
1. On the **Aliases for …** page, click the recycle bin icon to the right of the alias concerned, then, in the confirmation window, <code className="action">Delete</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Aliases page of an Exchange email account, alias delete icons" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/feature-redirections/console/email-alias04.png" loading="lazy" />

<Region zones={['eu']}>
#### MX Plan Roundcube <a name="alias-delete-mxplan-roundcube"></a>

To delete an alias on an MX Plan Roundcube email account, you must do it the same way as a redirection. You will therefore need to go to the redirection management of your MX Plan service.

1. Click the <code className="action">Redirections</code> tab.
1. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the redirection concerned, then <code className="action">Delete</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Opened ⋮ menu of an MX Plan redirection, Delete option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/feature-redirections/console/mxplan-redirect-delete01.png" loading="lazy" />

1. In the **Delete the redirection** window, click <code className="action">Delete</code>.

:::warning
It is not possible to modify the source address of a redirection or an alias: you must delete the redirection, then recreate it. Only the destination address can be modified, using the <code className="action">Edit</code> action.

:::

</Region>
