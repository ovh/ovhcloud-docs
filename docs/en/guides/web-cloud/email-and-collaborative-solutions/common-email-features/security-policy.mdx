## Objective

With OVHcloud email services, you can create professional email accounts. In order to secure this environment, you can manage the global security settings of your email accounts.

**Find out how to manage the security policy of your email service.**

## Requirements

- An [OVHcloud email solution](/links/web/emails)

[[cpnav:web-email-pro-console+web-mx-plan-console+web-exchange-console]]

## Instructions

The security policy management of your email service has four aspects:

- enhancing email account security when users try to log in
- adding complexity to the security of your email account passwords
- strengthening the verification of incoming messages on our servers and to your email addresses (for [Exchange](/links/web/emails-hosted-exchange) accounts only)
- configuring how so-called "unwanted" messages will be displayed in your inbox (for [Exchange](/links/web/emails-hosted-exchange) accounts only)

To access the security policy of your email service, follow the instructions below.

Continue to the aspect(s) you want to modify.

- [Enhance connection security](#enhanced-security): Define whether accounts should lock after a number of unsuccessful log-in attempts.
- [Add complexity to the security of passwords](#password-complexity): Define a complexity requirement, as well as rules for changing passwords.
- [Strengthen the verification of incoming messages](#incoming-messages-verification): Specify whether our servers should check if the messages received come from a legitimate source (i.e. DKIM and/or SPF checks).
- [Define the display of unwanted messages](#unwanted-messages-management): Specify whether unwanted messages should have a tag to identify them, or be automatically moved to the trash.

### Enhance connection security <a name="enhanced-security"></a>

This allows you to set whether email accounts should lock after a number of unsuccessful log-in attempts.

In the **Account security** section, click <code className="action">Edit account lockout</code>. In the **Account lockout** panel, fill in the following fields:

- **Lockout threshold**: turn on the toggle, then set the number of unsuccessful log-in attempts before the account locks. Turn it off to not apply a lockout threshold.
- **Reset delay**: if a lockout threshold is set, specify, in minutes, the time after which the counter of unsuccessful log-in attempts returns to zero.
- **Lockout duration**: if a lockout threshold is set, specify, in minutes, how long the account remains locked once the threshold is reached.

Once this information has been entered, click <code className="action">Save</code>.

### Add complexity to the security of passwords <a name="password-complexity"></a>

This allows you to set a complexity requirement, as well as rules for changing passwords.

In the **Account security** section, click <code className="action">Edit password policy</code>. In the **Password policy** panel, turn on the toggle of each setting you want to apply and fill in the following fields:

- **Complexity requirements**: enforces rules on password complexity. A password must:
    - not contain all or part of the user's account name;
    - be at least 6 characters long;
    - contain characters from at least 3 of the following 4 categories: uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and special characters (`!` or `$` for example).
- **Prevent change**: enforces a minimum lifetime for the passwords of your email accounts. Users must wait a certain number of days before they can change their password.
- **Maximum lifetime**: enforces a maximum lifetime for the passwords of your email accounts. Users must change their password once this time limit is reached.
- **Keep history** (Exchange only): if a maximum lifetime is set, specify the number of previous passwords that cannot be reused.
- **Minimum length**: enforces a minimum password length, applied when a user changes their password.

On MX Plan, these settings are called **Require complexity**, **Limit minimum age**, **Limit maximum age** and **Require a minimum length**.

Once this information has been entered, click <code className="action">Save</code>.

### Strengthen the verification of incoming messages <a name="incoming-messages-verification"></a> (Exchange only)

This allows you to specify whether our servers should check if the messages received in your email accounts come from a legitimate source (DKIM and/or SPF checks).

In the <code className="action">Settings</code> tab of your Exchange service, click the <code className="action">Message security</code> section to expand it, then click <code className="action">Edit incoming messages</code>. In the **Incoming messages** panel, tick the appropriate boxes:

- **Enable DKIM signature verification**: define whether our servers should check the DKIM signature of the messages you receive on your Exchange accounts. This action guarantees the authenticity of the sending domain and the integrity of the message, making it possible to identify non-legitimate mailings, which are then marked as spam.
- **Enable SPF protection verification**: define whether our servers must verify that the source of the messages you receive is present in the SPF record of the sender domain. This verification can identify illegitimate mailings, which are then marked as spam.

Once you have made your choice, click <code className="action">Save</code>.

### Define the display of unwanted messages <a name="unwanted-messages-management"></a>	 (Exchange only)

This allows you to define whether unwanted messages you receive on your email accounts should be tagged to identify them, or automatically moved to the trash.

If necessary, expand the <code className="action">Message security</code> section, then click <code className="action">Edit spam messages</code>. In the **Spam messages** panel, tick the appropriate boxes:

- **Identify spam messages.**: specify whether our servers should tag received messages considered "unwanted" as spam.
- **Move spam messages to trash.**: define whether our servers should automatically move any "unwanted" messages to the trash.

Once you have made your choice, click <code className="action">Save</code>.
