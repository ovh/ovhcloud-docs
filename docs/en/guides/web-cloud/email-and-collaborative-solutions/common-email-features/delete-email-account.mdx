## Objective

You want to:

- Delete an email account you no longer use.
- Reset an email account to use it with a new email address.
- Reset an email account to cancel it.

**This guide explains how to delete or reset an email account of your email solution.**

## Instructions <a name="instructions"></a>

OVHcloud offers 4 email solutions, and the concept of account deletion differs depending on which email solution you choose.

<Region zones={['eu']}>

- **MX Plan email**: This offer is sold as a bundle of email accounts. When you delete or reset an account, you free up an account "slot" of the email service, which you can then reuse for a new email address. The price of your offer remains unchanged: to reduce the number of slots, you need to change your offer or [cancel your service](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/how-to-cancel-services).
- **Email Pro**, **Hosted Exchange** and **Zimbra**: These offers are on-demand. You order an individual subscription per email account. If you want to delete an email account, you will need to **reset** it. Once you have reset your email account, you can use this account again to create a new email address. You can also [cancel the subscription](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/manage-billing-exchange#deleting-accounts) for this account if you wish to permanently delete it.

</Region>

<Region zones={['ca']}>

- **MX Plan email**: This offer is sold as a bundle of email accounts. When you delete or reset an account, you free up an account "slot" of the email service, which you can then reuse for a new email address. The price of your offer remains unchanged: to reduce the number of slots, you need to change your offer or [cancel your service](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/how-to-cancel-services).
- **Hosted Exchange**: This offer is on-demand. You order an individual subscription per email account. If you want to delete an email account, you will need to **reset** it. Once you have reset your email account, you can use this account again to create a new email address. You can also [cancel the subscription](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/manage-billing-exchange#deleting-accounts) for this account if you wish to permanently delete it.

</Region>

<Region zones={['apac']}>

- **MX Plan email**: This offer is sold as a bundle of email accounts. When you delete or reset an account, you free up an account "slot" of the email service, which you can then reuse for a new email address. The price of your offer remains unchanged: to reduce the number of slots, you need to change your offer or [cancel your service](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/how-to-cancel-services).

</Region>

### Delete or reset an email account

:::info
Can't find a <code className="action">Delete</code> action in the OVHcloud Control Panel? Depending on your email solution, this action is named <code className="action">Reset this account</code>. Resetting an account deletes its data and its email address: the slot that is freed up can then be configured for a new email address.
:::

Select the tab corresponding to your email solution:

<ZoneTabs>
  <Tab label="MX Plan (Zimbra / OWA)">
    <Region zones={['eu']}>

    To identify the email technology associated with your MX Plan service, please refer to the "[Identifying the email technology of your MX Plan solution](#whichmxplan)" section of this guide.

    </Region>
    
    1. Go to the <code className="action">Email accounts</code> tab. The tab lists your existing email accounts.
    1. Click the <code className="action">...</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then click <code className="action">Reset this account</code>. This technology does not offer a <code className="action">Delete</code> action: resetting the account deletes its data and its email address, then frees up its slot.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Reset an MX Plan email account in the OVHcloud Control Panel" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/email-reset-account/email-mxplan-new-reset.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="MX Plan (Roundcube)" availableIn={['eu']}>
    To identify the email technology associated with your MX Plan service, please refer to the "[Identifying the email technology of your MX Plan solution](#whichmxplan)" section of this guide.
    
    1. Go to the <code className="action">Emails</code> tab. The tab lists your existing email accounts.
    1. Click the <code className="action">...</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then click <code className="action">Delete account</code>.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Delete an MX Plan Roundcube email account in the OVHcloud Control Panel" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/email-reset-account/email-mxplan-legacy-reset.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Email Pro" availableIn={['eu']}>
    1. Go to the <code className="action">Email accounts</code> tab. The tab lists your existing email accounts.
    1. Click the <code className="action">...</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then click <code className="action">Reset this account</code>.
    
    After resetting your account, if you want to permanently delete it, you must cancel it. To do this, see our guide "[Managing the billing for your Email Pro accounts](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/manage-billing-emailpro)".
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Reset an Email Pro account in the OVHcloud Control Panel" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/email-reset-account/emailpro-reset.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Exchange" availableIn={['eu', 'ca']}>
    1. Go to the <code className="action">Email accounts</code> tab.
    1. Click the <code className="action">...</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then click <code className="action">Reset this account</code>.
    
    After resetting your account, if you want to permanently delete it, you must cancel it. To do this, see our guide "[Managing the billing for your Exchange accounts](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/manage-billing-exchange)".
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Reset an Exchange account in the OVHcloud Control Panel" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/email-reset-account/exchange-reset.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Zimbra STARTER/PRO" availableIn={['eu']}>
    1. Go to the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab. The tab lists your existing email accounts.
    1. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then click <code className="action">Delete</code>.
    
    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Delete a Zimbra email account in the OVHcloud Control Panel" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/email-reset-account/email-zimbra-reset.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
