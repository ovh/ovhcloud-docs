## Objective

You want to:

- Delete an email account you no longer use.
- Reset an email account to use it with a new email address.
- Reset an email account to cancel it.

**This guide explains how to delete or reset an email account of your email solution.**

- A preconfigured OVHcloud email solution:

## Instructions <a name="instructions"></a>

OVHcloud offers 4 email solutions, and the concept of account deletion differs depending on which email solution you choose.

<Region zones={['eu']}>

- **MX Plan email**: This offer is sold as a bundle of email accounts. When you delete or reset an account, you free up an account "slot" of the email service, which you can then reuse for a new email address. The price of your offer remains unchanged: to reduce the number of slots, you need to change your offer or [cancel your service](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/how-to-cancel-services).
- **Email Pro**, **Hosted Exchange** and **Zimbra**: These offers are on-demand. You order an individual subscription per email account. If you want to delete an email account, you will need to **reset** it. Once you have reset your email account, you can use this account again to create a new email address. You can also [cancel the subscription](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/manage-billing-exchange#deleting-accounts) for this account if you wish to permanently delete it.

</Region>

<Region zones={['ca']}>

- **MX Plan email**: This offer is sold as a bundle of email accounts. When you delete or reset an account, you free up an account "slot" of the email service, which you can then reuse for a new email address. The price of your offer remains unchanged: to reduce the number of slots, you need to change your offer or [cancel your service](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/how-to-cancel-services).
- **Hosted Exchange**: This offer is on-demand. You order an individual subscription per email account. If you want to delete an email account, you will need to **reset** it. Once you have reset your email account, you can use this account again to create a new email address. You can also [cancel the subscription](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/manage-billing-exchange#deleting-accounts) for this account if you wish to permanently delete it.

</Region>

<Region zones={['apac']}>

- **MX Plan email**: This offer is sold as a bundle of email accounts. When you delete or reset an account, you free up an account "slot" of the email service, which you can then reuse for a new email address. The price of your offer remains unchanged: to reduce the number of slots, you need to change your offer or [cancel your service](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/how-to-cancel-services).

</Region>

### Delete or reset an email account

:::info
Can't find a <code className="action">Delete</code> action in the OVHcloud Console? Depending on your email solution, this action is named <code className="action">Reset</code> or <code className="action">Reset this account</code>. Resetting an account deletes its data and its email address: the slot that is freed up can then be configured for a new email address.
:::

Select the tab corresponding to your email solution:

<ZoneTabs>
  <Tab label="MX Plan (Zimbra / OWA)">
    <Region zones={['eu']}>

    To identify the email technology associated with your MX Plan service, please refer to the "[Identifying the email technology of your MX Plan solution](#whichmxplan)" section of this guide.

    </Region>

    1. Click the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab to display the list of your email accounts.
    1. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then click <code className="action">Reset</code>. These services do not offer a <code className="action">Delete</code> action: resetting the account deletes its data and its email address, then frees up its slot.
    1. In the **Reset account** window, click <code className="action">Reset</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Opened ⋮ menu of an MX Plan email account, Reset option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/email-reset-account/console/email-mxplan-new-reset.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="MX Plan (Roundcube)" availableIn={['eu']}>
    To identify the email technology associated with your MX Plan service, please refer to the "[Identifying the email technology of your MX Plan solution](#whichmxplan)" section of this guide.

    1. Click the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab to display the list of your email accounts.
    1. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then click <code className="action">Delete</code>.
    1. In the **Delete account** window, click <code className="action">Delete</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Opened ⋮ menu of an MX Plan Roundcube email account, Delete option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/email-reset-account/console/email-mxplan-legacy-reset.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Email Pro" availableIn={['eu']}>
    1. Click the <code className="action">Accounts</code> tab to display the list of your email accounts.
    1. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then click <code className="action">Reset</code>.
    1. In the **Reset an account** window, click <code className="action">Reset</code>.

    After resetting your account, if you want to permanently delete it, you must cancel it. To do this, see our guide "[Managing the billing for your Email Pro accounts](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/email-pro/manage-billing-emailpro)".

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Opened ⋮ menu of an Email Pro account, Reset option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/email-reset-account/console/emailpro-reset.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Exchange" availableIn={['eu', 'ca']}>
    1. Click the <code className="action">Account management</code> tab, then the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab to display the list of your email accounts.
    1. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then click <code className="action">Reset this account</code>.
    1. In the **Reset an account** window, click <code className="action">Confirm</code>.

    After resetting your account, if you want to permanently delete it, you must cancel it. To do this, see our guide "[Managing the billing for Exchange accounts](/guides/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/microsoft-exchange/manage-billing-exchange)".

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Opened ⋮ menu of an Exchange account, Reset this account option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/email-reset-account/console/exchange-reset.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Zimbra STARTER/PRO" availableIn={['eu']}>
    1. Click the <code className="action">Email account</code> tab to display the list of your email accounts.
    1. Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button to the right of the account concerned, then click <code className="action">Delete</code>.
    1. In the **Delete account** window, click <code className="action">Delete</code>.

    <img className="thumbnail" alt="Opened ⋮ menu of a Zimbra email account, Delete option" src="/images/web-cloud/email-and-collaborative-solutions/common-email-features/email-reset-account/console/email-zimbra-reset.png" loading="lazy" />
  </Tab>
</ZoneTabs>
