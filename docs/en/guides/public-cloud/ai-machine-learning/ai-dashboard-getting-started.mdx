## Objective

OVHcloud provides a set of managed AI tools designed for building your machine learning projects.

This guide explains how to find your way around the OVHcloud AI tools in the OVHcloud Console, and where the items of the former AI Dashboard are now located.

## Requirements

- A [Public Cloud project](/links/public-cloud/public-cloud) in your OVHcloud account

[[cpnav:publiccloud-projects]]

## Instructions

In the OVHcloud Console, the AI tools are grouped under **AI** in the left-hand menu of your project: <code className="action">AI Notebooks</code>, <code className="action">AI Training</code>, <code className="action">AI Deploy</code>, <code className="action">AI Endpoints</code> and <code className="action">AI Agents</code>.

:::info
The former **AI Dashboard** page does not exist in the OVHcloud Console. Its transversal items (users, tokens, registries and data stores) are now gathered on the **Configuration** page, which is shared by AI Notebooks, AI Training and AI Deploy: the same project-wide settings are displayed whichever product you open it from.
:::

### Product list pages

Each product page lists your notebooks, jobs or apps with their status (for example **Running** or **Stopped**), and gives access to their creation (<code className="action">Create a notebook</code>, <code className="action">Create a job</code>, <code className="action">Create an app</code>). From these pages, you can easily access [AI Notebooks](/guides/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-notebooks-definition), [AI Training](/guides/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-training-submit-job) and [AI Deploy](/guides/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-deploy-getting-started).

You can find more details on the prices of the AI tools [here](/links/public-cloud/prices-ai).

### Configuration page

Click <code className="action">Configuration</code> on the list page of AI Notebooks, AI Training or AI Deploy. The page contains the following tabs:

- <code className="action">Users</code>: create a user with a dedicated AI role, or open the users page of your project with <code className="action">Manage users</code>. To know how to manage your users, please read the following guide: [Manage AI users and roles](/guides/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-users).
- <code className="action">Token</code>: create, regenerate and delete the application tokens that you can then use with your notebooks, jobs or apps.
- <code className="action">Docker registries</code>: link your private Docker registries, and find the commands to push images to the shared registry of the project (see [Manage registries](/guides/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-manage-registries)).
- <code className="action">Private Git repositories</code> and <code className="action">S3 volumes</code>: link the private Git repositories and S3-compatible volumes your AI workloads can use.

### Command Line Interface

The creation pages and the detail pages of notebooks, jobs and apps show the **CLI equivalent** of your configuration, as `ovhai` commands. To install and authenticate the CLI, see the [CLI installation guide](/guides/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-cli-install-client).
