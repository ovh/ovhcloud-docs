## Objective

The **users** of the **OVHcloud AI Solutions** are simply the users of your [Public Cloud project](/links/public-cloud/public-cloud), which can be created and managed in the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>.

The objective of this guide is to demonstrate how to create, configure, and delete AI users and their roles.

## Requirements

- A [Public Cloud project](/links/public-cloud/public-cloud) in your OVHcloud account

[[cpnav:publiccloud-users-roles]]

## Creating and Editing AI users

To grant access to **OVHcloud AI Solutions** to the users of your Public Cloud Project, you can assign them either the **AI Operator** (`ai_training_operator`) or **AI Reader** (`ai_training_read`) role.

- The **AI Operator** role provides complete access to **AI Notebooks, AI Training, and AI Deploy** solutions. With this role, users can launch, stop, and delete AI Solutions, as well as authenticate to existing AI Notebooks, Jobs, and Apps, and the [ovhai CLI](/guides/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-cli-install-client) using their credentials.

- If you don't need to launch, stop, or delete **AI Solutions**, but are only interested in accessing already existing deployed ones, the **AI Reader** role is sufficient. This role allows users to access existing AI Solutions, but not to launch, stop, or delete them.

In addition to the AI role, we strongly recommend adding the **ObjectStore operator** role to your AI users. This role provides read/write access to the **OVHcloud Object Storage**, which is a persistent storage solution to store your [data](/guides/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-data) that is incorporated into our AI Solutions. This way, you can access your data within the **AI Solutions**.

**1\. Create a new user**

You can create the user from the users page of your project, or from the **Configuration** page of an AI product.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="From the project users page">
    In the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, click <code className="action">Project Management</code>, then open the <code className="action">Users</code> tab.

    Click <code className="action">Create a user</code>, enter a **User description**, and **tick the required roles** to use the AI Solutions with the Object Storage (**AI Operator** or **AI Reader**, depending on the level of access you want to grant, and **ObjectStore operator**). Then click <code className="action">Create</code>.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="From an AI product">
    In the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, click <code className="action">AI Notebooks</code>, <code className="action">AI Training</code> or <code className="action">AI Deploy</code> under **AI**, then click <code className="action">Configuration</code>. The <code className="action">Users</code> tab opens.

    Click <code className="action">Add a user</code>, enter a **Description**, select the AI **Role** (`ai_training_operator` or `ai_training_read`), then click <code className="action">Create</code>.

    This dialog assigns a single AI role. To add the **ObjectStore operator** role, edit the roles of the user from the project users page (the <code className="action">Manage users</code> button opens it).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

A password is generated: it allows you to authenticate to your existing AI Notebooks, AI Training jobs, AI Deploy apps and to the `ovhai` CLI to launch new ones. It is displayed only once: copy it before closing the dialog.

:::info
- If you ever lose the password of a user, you can generate a new one at any time from the <code className="action">Users</code> tab of <code className="action">Project Management</code>: click the <code className="action">...</code> button next to your user, then <code className="action">Generate a password</code>, and confirm with <code className="action">Generate</code>.
- Access to **AI Solutions** can be revoked anytime by deleting the user or removing its **AI Operator / AI Reader** role.

:::


**2\. Edit an existing user roles**

To edit an existing user, go to the <code className="action">Users</code> tab of <code className="action">Project Management</code>, click the <code className="action">...</code> button next to the user, and select <code className="action">Edit roles</code> to modify its existing roles.
