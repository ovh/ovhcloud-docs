## Objective

The SQL queries of a Lakehouse, from the data console or from your tools, run on a Compute Pool. By default, the shared Compute Pool of the region runs them. A dedicated Compute Pool reserves compute for your Lakehouse.

**This guide explains how to add a dedicated Compute Pool, size it and suspend it.**

## Requirements

- A Lakehouse in your Public Cloud project. See [Lakehouse - Getting started](/guides/public-cloud/data-analytics/lakehouse/lakehouse-getting-started).

[[cpnav:publiccloud-analytics-console|en]]

## Instructions

Click **Lakehouse**, then click the name of your Lakehouse and open the **Compute Pools** tab.

The **Shared Compute Pool** card shows the JDBC URL and the Trino command line of the Lakehouse. This SQL address never changes, whether you add, resize or delete a dedicated Compute Pool.

### Adding a dedicated Compute Pool

Click **Add a dedicated Compute Pool** and fill in the form:

- **Name**: 3 to 63 lowercase letters, digits or underscores, starting with a letter. It cannot be changed afterwards.
- **Size**: the profile of each worker, from XS (1 vCPU, 8 GiB) to L (8 vCPU, 64 GiB). The form shows the memory available to each query.
- **Minimum workers** and **Maximum workers**: between 1 and 50; the Compute Pool adjusts its number of workers within this range.
- **Idle suspend (minutes)**: after this number of minutes without a query, the Compute Pool suspends itself. `0` keeps it running; the maximum is 1440.

The form shows how many compute units the Compute Pool uses (the coordinator counts as one node) and the limit of the region. A Lakehouse can have only one dedicated Compute Pool.

Click **Add**. The Compute Pool starts in about 2 to 3 minutes. Once it is running, every SQL query of the Lakehouse runs on it.

:::warning
SQL queries are refused while the Compute Pool starts. Wait until it is running before sending queries, or make your tools retry.

:::

### Suspending and resuming

A dedicated Compute Pool suspends itself after its idle delay. You can also suspend it from its card.

The next SQL query resumes it. Resuming takes 1 to 2 minutes, during which queries fail with an error such as `502 Bad Gateway` or `CATALOG_NOT_FOUND`: retry them once the Compute Pool is running again.

### Resizing the dedicated Compute Pool

Click **Resize** on the card of the dedicated Compute Pool, change its size, its workers or its idle suspend, then click **Save**. Changing the size or the workers restarts the Compute Pool: running queries are lost, and it serves again in a minute or two at the same address. Changing only the idle suspend does not restart it.

### Deleting the dedicated Compute Pool

To delete the dedicated Compute Pool, click **Delete** on its card and confirm. The SQL queries of the Lakehouse then run again on the shared Compute Pool, at the same address.
