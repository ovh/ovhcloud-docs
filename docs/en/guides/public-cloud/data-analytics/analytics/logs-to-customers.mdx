## Objective

Analytics services allow you to send logs of your service to your own Logs Data Platform (LDP) data stream.

**This guide explains how to forward service logs to your own LDP stream from the OVHcloud Console or with the OVHcloud API.**

## Requirements

- An [Analytics service](/links/public-cloud/analytics) up and running
- To use the API: access to the <ApiLink>OVHcloud API</ApiLink>
- A Logs Data Platform account within this OVHcloud account with at least one destination stream configured
    - If you are not familiar with all the LDP *Stream* configuration possibilities, simply create a new one with the default options (indexing & websocket enabled, long-term storage disabled) for the purpose of this guide.
- A running Analytics service

## Instructions

### Create your subscription

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="OVHcloud Console">
    {/* CP-STEPS-START:create-log-subscription */}
    Go to your Analytics service and open the <code className="action">Observability</code> tab. In the **Logs** section, click <code className="action">Manage streaming</code>. If your service exposes several log kinds, first select the kind to forward in the list of the **Logs** section: the panel manages one log kind at a time.

    In the **Manage log streaming** panel, under **Subscribe to a stream**, select your **LDP account** and the **Stream** of destination, then click <code className="action">Subscribe</code>.

    The subscription is listed under **Current subscriptions** and the logs start to be forwarded to your LDP stream. If your service exposes several log kinds, repeat the operation for each kind you want to forward.
    {/* CP-STEPS-END:create-log-subscription */}
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="OVHcloud API">
    First retrieve the required information.

    **Retrieve your LDP destination `streamId`**:

    {/* CP-STEPS-START:retrieve-ldp-stream-id */}
    - In the OVHcloud Console, open <code className="action">Observability</code> > <code className="action">Logs Management</code> in the left-hand menu, select your Logs Data Platform account, then open the <code className="action">Data streams</code> tab.
    - Click the name of your target stream. The stream ID is the identifier that follows `/streams/` in the address of the page that opens (`.../logs-management/<ldp-account>/streams/<streamId>/dashboard`): copy it.
    {/* CP-STEPS-END:retrieve-ldp-stream-id */}

    **Retrieve your LDP destination `serviceName`**:

    - It refers to your Public Cloud project ID. To copy it, click the <code className="action">Project</code> selector at the top of the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, then click <code className="action">Copy project id</code> on the row of your project.

    **Retrieve your `clusterId`**:

    {/* CP-STEPS-START:retrieve-cluster-id */}
    - Go to your Analytics service and copy the **Service ID** from the **General information** card of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab.
    {/* CP-STEPS-END:retrieve-cluster-id */}

    Then use the following API call:

    <Api version="v1" section="/cloud" method="POST" route={"/cloud/project/\{serviceName\}/database/kafka/\{clusterId\}/log/subscription"} />

    Replace `kafka` in the route with your service's engine (for example `opensearch` or `grafana`).

    ```console
    body : {
        streamId: <LDP destination stream ID>
    }
    ```
    The logs will start to be forwarded to your LDP stream.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Find logs in Graylog

To find logs of your service in your Graylogs stream you can use the following Graylog queries:

Query: `clusterID: "<Engine>-<HostID>"`

{/* CP-STEPS-START:find-host-id-in-cp */}
You can find this `HostID` in the OVHcloud Console:

- Find the **Service ID** (in the **General information** card of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab), formatted as a UUID (AAAAAAAA-BBBB-CCCC-DDDDDDDDDDDD)
- `HostID` is the first part of the UUID (AAAAAAAA)
{/* CP-STEPS-END:find-host-id-in-cp */}

### Delete subscription

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="OVHcloud Console">
    {/* CP-STEPS-START:delete-log-subscription */}
    In the <code className="action">Observability</code> tab of your service, click <code className="action">Manage streaming</code> in the **Logs** section (if the service exposes several log kinds, select the kind first). Under **Current subscriptions**, click <code className="action">Unsubscribe</code> on the row of the subscription, then <code className="action">Confirm</code>.
    {/* CP-STEPS-END:delete-log-subscription */}
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="OVHcloud API">
    Delete the subscription using the `subscriptionId` concerned and this API call:

    <Api version="v1" section="/cloud" method="DELETE" route={"/cloud/project/\{serviceName\}/database/kafka/\{clusterId\}/log/subscription/\{subscriptionId\}"} />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

If you delete your Analytics service, all subscriptions of this service are deleted automatically.
