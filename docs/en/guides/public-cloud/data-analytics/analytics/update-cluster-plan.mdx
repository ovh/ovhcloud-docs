## Objective

Learn how to upgrade the service plan of your cluster according to your needs.

:::warning
Once you have upgraded your solution, you will not be able to downgrade to a lower solution.

:::


## Requirements

- Access to the <ApiLink>OVHcloud API</ApiLink>
- A [Public Cloud project](/links/public-cloud/public-cloud) in your OVHcloud account

[[cpnav:publiccloud-analytics]]

## Instructions

### Using the OVHcloud Console

{/* CP-STEPS-START:update-cluster-plan-cp */}
In the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, click your product under **Analytics**, then click the name of your service.

In the **General information** card of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, click <code className="action">Upgrade</code>. In the panel that opens, select the new **Plan** of your cluster, then click <code className="action">Save</code>.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:update-cluster-plan-cp */}

### Using the OVHcloud API

:::tip
If you are not familiar with using the OVHcloud API, please refer to our guide on [Getting started with the OVHcloud API](/guides/manage-and-operate/api/first-steps).

:::


To upgrade or downgrade the service plan of your cluster, use the following API call:

<Api version="v1" section="/cloud" method="PUT" route={"/cloud/project/\{serviceName\}/database/kafka/\{clusterId\}"} />

Replace `kafka` in the route with your service's engine (for example `opensearch` or `grafana`).

```console
body : {
    plan: <essential|business|enterprise>
}
```

If you downgrade to an Essential plan, your current flavor and/or number of nodes will be downgraded automatically if they exceed the limits of the Essential plan.
