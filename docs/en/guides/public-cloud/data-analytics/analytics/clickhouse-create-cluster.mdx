## Objective

ClickHouse is an open-source, columnar analytical database system designed for real-time processing of massive data volumes, providing high performance, scalability, and low latency.

**This guide explains how to create a ClickHouse cluster via the OVHcloud Console.**

## Requirements

- A [Public Cloud project](/links/public-cloud/public-cloud) in your OVHcloud account.

[[cpnav:publiccloud-analytics]]

## Instructions

### Subscribing to the service

{/* CP-STEPS-START:subscribing-to-the-service */}
In the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, click <code className="action">ClickHouse</code> under **Analytics**, then click <code className="action">Create a service</code>. The **Create a managed database** page opens.

#### Selecting your Analytics service

Under **Database engine**, select ClickHouse and its **Version**.

A random name is generated for your service in the **Name** field. You can change it at this step or later.

#### Selecting a region

Under **Select a region**, choose the geographical region of the datacentre where your service will be hosted, and the deployment mode (1-AZ or 3-AZ).

#### Selecting a plan

:::info
Currently, only the **Production** plan is available. The **Discovery** plan will be coming soon.
:::

Under **Plan**, choose an appropriate service plan. If needed, you can upgrade or downgrade the plan after creation.

Visit the capabilities page of your selected Analytics service for detailed information on each plan's properties.

#### Selecting the node type

Under **Node type**, choose the instance type for the nodes of your service. You can change it afterward. The number of nodes (**Nodes**) depends on the plan previously chosen.

#### Selecting the storage

Under **Storage**, select the additional storage. Storage can be scaled up to 3 times the base storage.

#### Configuring the network and IP restrictions

Under **Network**, choose **Public network** or **Private network (vRack)**. Under **IP restrictions**, add the IP addresses that will access your service (<code className="action">Add an IP</code>, <code className="action">Add my current IP</code>).

#### Reviewing and confirming

The **Summary** helps you review your service configuration. The components of the price are also summarized with a monthly estimation.

Click the <code className="action">API and Terraform equivalent</code> button to display the equivalent API call and Terraform configuration. Use this information to automate service creation with the [OVHcloud API](/guides/manage-and-operate/api/first-steps) or the OVHcloud Terraform Provider.

When you are ready, click the <code className="action">Create the database</code> button to create your service.

In a matter of minutes, your new ClickHouse service will be deployed. Its status changes to **Active** when it is ready to use.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:subscribing-to-the-service */}
