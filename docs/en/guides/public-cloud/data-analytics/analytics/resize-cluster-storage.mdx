## Objective

Learn how to adjust the storage space of your cluster according to your needs.

:::info
This feature is only available for engines that use storage space.

:::


## Requirements

- Access to the OVHcloud <ApiLink>API</ApiLink> (create your credentials by consulting [this guide](/guides/manage-and-operate/api/first-steps))
- A [Public Cloud project](/links/public-cloud/public-cloud) in your OVHcloud account

[[cpnav:publiccloud-analytics]]

## Instructions

You can increase or decrease the storage of your service at any time using either the OVHcloud <ApiLink>API</ApiLink> or the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>.

:::info
When changing the storage space of your service, the system checks that at least 20% of you total storage space will remain free after the resize operation. As a consequence, you might not be able to perform a storage space decrease if the new size you request is too close to your current disk space usage.

:::

### Using the OVHcloud Console

{/* CP-STEPS-START:adjust-storage-cp */}

In the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, click your product under **Analytics**, then click the name of your service.

In the **General information** card of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, click <code className="action">Resize storage</code> on the **Storage** line, adjust the storage space of your cluster, then click <code className="action">Save</code>. You can also change the storage with the <code className="action">Upgrade</code> action of the same card.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:adjust-storage-cp */}

### Using the OVHcloud API

As an example, for OpenSearch services, you can perform a storage space increase or decrease using this endpoint:

<Api version="v1" section="/cloud" method="PUT" route={"/cloud/project/\{serviceName\}/database/opensearch/\{clusterId\}"} />

Other engines using a storage space provide similar endpoints, e.g.:

<Api version="v1" section="/cloud" method="PUT" route={"/cloud/project/\{serviceName\}/database/kafka/\{clusterId\}"} />
