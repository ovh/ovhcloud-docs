## Objective

The Analytics service allows you to focus on building and deploying cloud applications while OVHcloud takes care of the Analytics infrastructure and maintenance.

**This guide explains how to restore a backup of an Analytics service solution in the OVHcloud Console.**

We continuously improve our offers. You can follow and submit ideas to add to our roadmap at [https://github.com/orgs/ovh/projects/16/views/5](https://github.com/orgs/ovh/projects/16/views/5).

## Requirements

- An [Analytics service](/links/public-cloud/analytics) up and running

[[cpnav:publiccloud-analytics]]

## Instructions

### Step 1: Select the Analytics service you want to restore data from

{/* CP-STEPS-START:step-1-select-the-analytics-service-you-want-to-restore-data-from */}
In the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, click your product under **Analytics**, then click the name of the service you want to restore the backup from.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:step-1-select-the-analytics-service-you-want-to-restore-data-from */}

### Step 2: Go to the backup tab

{/* CP-STEPS-START:step-2-go-to-the-backup-tab */}
In the tab list, click on <code className="action">Backups</code>.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:step-2-go-to-the-backup-tab */}

### Step 3: Select the backup to restore from

{/* CP-STEPS-START:step-3-select-the-backup-to-restore-from */}
Select the backup from which you want to restore. To help you choose, observe the dates at which the backups have been performed.

Click on the <code className="action">...</code> button corresponding to the chosen backup, then click on <code className="action">Fork</code> to open the **Fork a cluster** panel. You can also click the <code className="action">Fork</code> button above the list.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:step-3-select-the-backup-to-restore-from */}

### Step 4: Configure the target service

{/* CP-STEPS-START:step-4-configure-the-target-service */}
When restoring a backup, you create a new separate service on which the backup data will be imported. You are able to configure this new service as you wish in the **Fork a cluster** panel.

#### Name

Enter a **New cluster name**.

#### Restore point

In **Fork source**, choose the restore point from which the service will be duplicated:

- **From a backup**: the most common option, restore from one of the listed backups (**Backup** field).
- **Point in time**: if your service supports point in time recovery (see [Automated Backup guide](/guides/public-cloud/data-analytics/analytics/backups) for more details), restore from a specific date and time.
- **Latest state**: restore from the most recent data.

#### New cluster configuration

You cannot change the engine. In the **New cluster configuration** section, you can choose the **Version**, a different **Region**, another service **Plan**, a bigger **Node type**, the **Number of nodes** and additional **Storage** for your new service. The network of the new service cannot be changed in this panel.

:::info
The targeted node type and storage must be large enough to restore the backup.
:::

The estimated price of the new service is displayed at the bottom of the panel. Now click on <code className="action">Create fork</code> and the new service will be created. Please note that depending on the backup size, it can take some time before the service is available.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:step-4-configure-the-target-service */}

### Step 5: Wait for service creation

{/* CP-STEPS-START:step-5-wait-for-service-creation */}
Now all you have to do is wait for your service to be ready.
This new service is now completely independent from the one you forked the backup from. You can safely delete the old service without impacting the new one.

:::warning
The IP restrictions of the old service are applied to the new one, but the users created on the old service are not duplicated: recreate them before using your new service. Check the IP restrictions in the <code className="action">Configuration</code> tab of the new service.

:::

{/* CP-STEPS-END:step-5-wait-for-service-creation */}
