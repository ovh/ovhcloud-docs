## Objective

You can change the contacts for project administration and billing of a Public Cloud project in the OVHcloud Console.<br/>
Modifying these contacts enables you to separate the technical management of services within a project from the project's billing.

**This guide explains how to change the contacts of a Public Cloud project in the OVHcloud Console.**

## Requirements

- A [Public Cloud project](/links/public-cloud/public-cloud) in your OVHcloud account
- Admin and billing contacts in the same OVHcloud subsidiary

[[cpnav:publiccloud-contacts-rights]]

## Instructions

{/* CP-STEPS-START:instructions */}
In the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, click <code className="action">Project Management</code>, then the <code className="action">Contacts & Rights</code> tab.

On this page you can see the administrative and billing contacts associated with your project.

Click on one of the <code className="action">Edit</code> links to modify the current contacts. This will open the **Contact management** page of the OVHcloud Console in a new tab. The <code className="action">Edit</code> links are only available for accounts in the EU zone, and not for a project in discovery mode.

The contacts of your Public Cloud project are displayed in the <code className="action">My services</code> tab. Click on the <code className="action">...</code> button and then on <code className="action">Edit contacts</code>.

In the **Edit contacts** window, enter the desired NIC handle for each contact you wish to change (**Admin contact**, **Technical contact**, **Billing contact**). Bear in mind that the OVHcloud accounts for `Admin` and `Billing` contacts have to be registered in the same OVHcloud subsidiary.

Once you have clicked <code className="action">Confirm</code>, both OVHcloud accounts affected by the change will receive a confirmation email. This email contains a token with which the contact change can be accepted in the <code className="action">My requests</code> tab of the **Contact management** page: click <code className="action">...</code> in the row of the request, then <code className="action">Accept request</code>, and enter the token in the **Validation code** field.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:instructions */}

For a more detailed explanation of this process, please consult our guide [Managing contacts for your services](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/managing-contacts).
