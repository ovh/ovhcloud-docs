## Objective

If your instance lacks resources due to increased activity or new needs, you can increase its resources in just a few clicks with the Public Cloud.

**This guide explains how to resize your instance from the OVHcloud Console.**

:::warning
Only upscaling is possible for classic models.
In addition, this manipulation causes the instance to be shut down for the time of the operation.

:::


:::tip
Flex instances allow resizing to higher or lower models due to a locked single disk size.

:::


## Requirements

- A [Public Cloud instance](/links/public-cloud/public-cloud) in your OVHcloud account

[[cpnav:publiccloud-instances]]

## Instructions

{/* CP-STEPS-START:resize-instance */}
In the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, click <code className="action">Instances</code> under **Compute**, then click on the name of the instance.

In the **General information** card of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, click <code className="action">Modify model</code> on the **Model** row. The **Change model** page opens.

Select the model of your choice in the **Instance model** list and in the table of models. Enable **Include unavailable** to also display the models that cannot be selected (out of stock, quota reached or disk too small).

:::info
For classic models, you can switch to any flavor that has a similar or bigger disk. You can't switch to a model with a smaller disk: these models are marked **Disk too small**.<br/>

Only **Flexible instances** can be upgraded and downgraded while maintaining a fixed disk size of 50GB.

:::


If your disk is equal to or smaller than 50GB, you can switch to a flexible model, if it is offered in the list.

:::warning
If you are editing a flex instance, you cannot revert to a classic instance via the OVHcloud Console. For more information, consult the guide on [Revert a flex instance](/guides/public-cloud/compute/revert-a-flex-instance).

:::


Once the selection has been made, click on <code className="action">Confirm</code> to confirm your choice. Changing the model may cause downtime: we recommend creating a snapshot of your instance first.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:resize-instance */}

### Resizing a disk in Windows

When performing a resize for a Windows Instance, the partition size is not automatically updated. You must extend it using the **disk manager**:

- Right-click on the <code className="action">Start</code> menu and launch the disk manager by clicking on <code className="action">Disk Management</code>:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Windows Start menu context menu with Disk Management option" src="/images/public-cloud/compute/resize-instance-manager/2980.png" loading="lazy" />

- Right-click on the main partition, then click on <code className="action">Extend Volume</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Right-click on main partition to extend volume" src="/images/public-cloud/compute/resize-instance-manager/2981a.png" loading="lazy" />

- In the `Extend Volume Wizard` menu, click on <code className="action">Next</code> to proceed. In the next tab, choose the disk resources to extend and click on <code className="action">Next</code>. 

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Extend Volume Wizard showing disk resources selection" src="/images/public-cloud/compute/resize-instance-manager/2978a.png" loading="lazy" />

Once done, click on <code className="action">Finish</code> to confirm your choice.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Extend Volume Wizard completion step" src="/images/public-cloud/compute/resize-instance-manager/wizard2021.png" loading="lazy" />

- The new disk size will then be displayed in the disk manager.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Disk manager displaying the new disk size" src="/images/public-cloud/compute/resize-instance-manager/2979.png" loading="lazy" />
