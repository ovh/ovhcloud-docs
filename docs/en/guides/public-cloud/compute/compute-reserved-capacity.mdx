## Objective

Reserved Capacity lets you commit to a quantity of compute capacity in a given region, for a fixed period, in exchange for a discount on the base price. You secure access to high-demand instance models and lower your monthly costs.

**In this guide, you will learn how to:**

- Create a reservation: choose the region, the model, the commitment period and the number of instances
- Validate your reservation by paying the down payment
- Follow the status of a reservation
- Manage the instances attached to a reservation

## Requirements

- A [Public Cloud project](/guides/public-cloud/cross-functional/create-a-public-cloud-project) in your OVHcloud account
- A valid payment method, to pay the reservation down payment
- For the API steps: access to the [OVHcloud API](/guides/manage-and-operate/api/first-steps)

## Introduction

A reservation commits you to a quantity of a specific instance model, in the region you choose, for 12 or 36 months. In exchange, you get a discount on the base monthly price of the model. Once your reservation is active, the reserved capacity is guaranteed for the whole commitment period.

:::info
At the moment, only the **b300-1990** model is eligible for Reserved Capacity.
:::

### How Reserved Capacity works <a name="how-it-works"></a>

Committing for 1 year or 3 years gives you a discount on the base monthly price. The commitment period you choose determines the discount and the down payment you pay when you order:

| Commitment period | Down payment |
|---|---|
| 12 months | 1 month |
| 36 months | 3 months |

Your monthly payments start with month 1. The down payment is held until the end of the commitment, and pays for the last month(s) of the commitment once all previous months are paid. If the reservation ends before the end of its commitment period, the down payment is forfeited.

:::info
OVHcloud books the reserved capacity only once your down payment has been paid. Until then, the resource is not reserved.
:::

### Creating a reservation <a name="create"></a>

You can create a reservation from the OVHcloud Console or with the OVHcloud API.

<Tabs>
<Tab label="OVHcloud Console">

On the **Reserved Capacity** page, click <code className="action">Reserve capacity</code> to open the **Create a reservation** page.

#### Step 1 — Name the reservation

In the **Reservation name** field, keep the suggested name (for example `Reservation-GPU-20261113`) or enter your own. The name can contain up to 60 characters: letters, numbers, underscores (`_`) and hyphens (`-`).

#### Step 2 — Select a region

In the **Select a region** section, pick one region.

The stock is confirmed when you order.

{/* DRAFT: To verify — whether the reservation start date can be chosen (the page says "starting now or on a chosen date", but no date field is shown in the sandbox). */}

#### Step 3 — Select a model

In the **Select a model** section, pick the instance model to reserve. A reservation holds a single model. For each model, the table shows its GPUs, total vRAM, memory, vCores, storage and price excluding VAT.

{/* DRAFT: To verify — the PRD lists stock statuses (Available / Quantity limited / Currently unavailable), which are not displayed in the sandbox. */}

#### Step 4 — Select the commitment period

In the **Select the commitment period** section, choose <code className="action">12 months</code> or <code className="action">36 months</code>. Each option shows its discount, the monthly total excluding VAT, and the down payment paid at order.

#### Step 5 — Set the number of instances and reserve

The **Instance reservation** summary panel, on the right of the page, is updated as you go. Set the **Number of instances to reserve** with the <code className="action">-</code> and <code className="action">+</code> buttons, then check the summary:

- the name of the reservation and the reserved model and region;
- the commitment period and its discount;
- the start and end dates of the reservation;
- the monthly price, excluding VAT, compared with the price without commitment;
- **Due today**: the down payment.

Click <code className="action">Reserve</code>. To leave the page without reserving, click <code className="action">Cancel</code>.

Your reservation is created with the `Pending` status, and stays in it until you make the down payment.

</Tab>
<Tab label="OVHcloud API v2">

First, retrieve the ID of the model to reserve from the list of instance flavors available in your project:

<Api version="v2" section="/publicCloud" method="GET" route={"/publicCloud/project/\{projectId\}/reference/instance/flavor"} />

Then create the reservation:

<Api version="v2" section="/publicCloud" method="POST" route={"/publicCloud/project/\{projectId\}/compute/reservation"} />

| Parameter | Required | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| `projectId` (path) | Yes | ID of your Public Cloud project. | `5f3b8a9c0d1e2f3a4b5c6d7e8f9a0b1c` |
| `targetSpec.name` | Yes | Name of the reservation. | `b300-training-eu` |
| `targetSpec.flavor.id` | Yes | ID of the model to reserve, retrieved in the previous step. | `0a1b2c3d-4e5f-6a7b-8c9d-0e1f2a3b4c5d` |
| `targetSpec.location.region` | Yes | Region in which the capacity is reserved. | `EU-WEST-PAR` |
| `targetSpec.location.availabilityZone` | No | Availability zone, in a 3-AZ region only. If you leave it empty, the zone with the most stock for the model is chosen. It is ignored in other regions. | `eu-west-par-a` |
| `targetSpec.quantity` | Yes | Number of instances to reserve. | `4` |
| `targetSpec.duration` | Yes | Commitment period, as an ISO 8601 duration. | `P1Y` (12 months) or `P3Y` (36 months) |

Example request body:

```json
{
  "targetSpec": {
    "name": "b300-training-eu",
    "flavor": { "id": "<flavorId>" },
    "location": { "region": "<region>" },
    "quantity": 4,
    "duration": "P1Y"
  }
}
```

The response returns the reservation with its `id`. Keep it to follow and manage the reservation.

### Paying the down payment <a name="down-payment"></a>

After you click <code className="action">Reserve</code>, your reservation stays in the `Pending` status until you make the down payment: 1 month for a 12-month commitment, 3 months for a 36-month commitment.

The down payment is billed through a purchase order. To pay it, go to **Compute** > **Reserved Capacity**, open your reservation, then click <code className="action">View the payment</code> in the **Payment pending** banner. The **Down payment** field of the reservation shows `Payment not received` until the payment is received, then `Complete`.

{/* DRAFT: To verify — where "View the payment" leads (purchase order page, payment page) and which payment methods are accepted. */}

:::warning
You have 2 business days to pay the down payment. If it is not paid within this time, OVHcloud reserves the right to cancel your reservation.
:::

OVHcloud books the capacity only once the down payment has been paid, and checks the stock again at that moment:

- If the full quantity is still available, your reservation becomes `Active`.
- If the full quantity is no longer available, your reservation is cancelled and your down payment is fully refunded. Reservations are never partially delivered: for example, if you reserved 4 instances and only 3 are available, none is reserved.

### Reservation statuses <a name="statuses"></a>

Each reservation has one of the following statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Pending` | The reservation is waiting for its down payment to be paid. |
| `Creating` | The down payment has been received and the reserved capacity is being set up. |
| `Active` | The reservation is in force, whether or not you use its instances. |
| `Ended` | The commitment period is over, but some instances are still running. These instances are automatically billed at the public hourly rate from then on. |
| `Error` | The reservation is in error, for example after a payment issue. |
| `Cancelled` | The reservation has been cancelled, for example because its down payment was not paid in time. |
| `Deleting` | The reservation is being deleted. |

### Managing an existing reservation <a name="manage"></a>

You can follow your reservations and deploy reserved instances from the OVHcloud Console or with the OVHcloud API.

<Tabs>
<Tab label="OVHcloud Console">

Go to **Compute** > **Reserved Capacity** in your Public Cloud project. The list shows each reservation with its name and ID, region, model (**Flavor**), the number of instances launched out of the number reserved (**Launched / reserved**), its duration, start and end dates, and status. Click the name of a reservation to open its page.

#### Reservation overview

The top of the page shows the name and status of the reservation, then:

- **Down payment**: `Payment not received` or `Complete`;
- **Slot**: the reserved model and region, for example `B300-1990 · WAW1`;
- **Reserved instances**: the number of instances reserved;
- **Commitment**, **Start date** and **End date**.

This information cannot be edited.

#### Reserved instances

The **Instances** section lists the instances that use the reservation, with their name and ID, region, model (**Flavor**), image, public and private IP addresses, volumes and status.

You can create instances once the reservation is `Active`. To deploy a new instance on the reserved capacity, click <code className="action">Add instance</code>. The button is unavailable once all the reserved instances have been created.

On the **Create an instance on your reserved capacity** page, the region and the model are locked by the reservation. Configure the rest of the instance as usual: name, image, SSH key, <Tooltip term="snapshot">snapshot</Tooltip> settings, additional storage, network, public connectivity and post-installation script. See [How to create a Public Cloud instance and connect to it](/guides/public-cloud/compute/getting-started) for details on each setting.

The summary panel shows how many reserved slots remain. Set the **Number of instances to be created**, then click <code className="action">Create</code>. The monthly price of these instances is covered by the reservation, so nothing is due today. Options such as storage, IP addresses and gateways are billed as usual.

To run an action on a reserved instance, click the <code className="action">...</code> button at the end of its row:

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| <code className="action">Details</code> | Opens the instance page. |
| <code className="action">Create a snapshot</code> | Creates a snapshot of the instance. See [Backing up an instance](/guides/public-cloud/compute/save-an-instance). |
| <code className="action">Create an automatic snapshot</code> | Schedules automatic snapshots of the instance. |
| <code className="action">Hard stop</code> | Stops the instance without deleting it. |
| <code className="action">Shelve</code> | Shelves the instance. See [Shelve or pause an instance](/guides/public-cloud/compute/suspend-or-pause-an-instance). |
| <code className="action">Reboot in rescue mode</code> | Reboots the instance into a rescue environment, to diagnose or repair it. See [How to activate rescue mode on a Public Cloud instance](/guides/public-cloud/compute/put-an-instance-in-rescue-mode). |
| <code className="action">Hot reboot (soft)</code> | Reboots the operating system, without cutting the power. |
| <code className="action">Cold reboot (hard)</code> | Fully reboots the instance: power off, then power on. |
| <code className="action">Reinstall</code> | Reinstalls the operating system of the instance. |
| <code className="action">Delete</code> | Permanently deletes the instance. |

:::warning
The <code className="action">Create a snapshot</code>, <code className="action">Create an automatic snapshot</code> and <code className="action">Shelve</code> actions only save the system disk of the instance: its ephemeral disk is not saved. When you restore the snapshot or unshelve the instance, only the system disk is restored, with an empty ephemeral disk.
:::

The available actions depend on the state of the instance. For example, a stopped instance does not offer the stop and reboot actions.

#### Listing and viewing your reservations

To list the reservations of your project:

<Api version="v2" section="/publicCloud" method="GET" route={"/publicCloud/project/\{projectId\}/compute/reservation"} />

To view the details of a reservation:

<Api version="v2" section="/publicCloud" method="GET" route={"/publicCloud/project/\{projectId\}/compute/reservation/\{reservationId\}"} />

The response contains:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `targetSpec` | What you requested: name, model, location, quantity and commitment period. |
| `currentState` | What is actually reserved, including the start date (`startTime`) and end date (`endTime`) of the commitment, and the reserved flavor (`flavor`). It is empty while the reservation is being created. |
| `resourceStatus` | Technical status of the reservation, for example `CREATING`, `READY` or `ERROR`. |
| `currentTasks` | Operations in progress on the reservation, with any errors. |

{/* DRAFT: To verify — mapping between the API resourceStatus values and the Pending / Creating / Active / Ended / Error / Cancelled / Deleting statuses shown in the Console. */}

#### Deploying a reserved instance

Once the reservation is ready, it exposes a dedicated reserved flavor in `currentState.flavor`. To consume the reserved capacity, create your instance with the ID of this flavor (`currentState.flavor.id`), in the same region and availability zone as the reservation (`currentState.location`):

<Api version="v2" section="/publicCloud" method="POST" route={"/publicCloud/project/\{projectId\}/compute/instance"} />

| Parameter | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `targetSpec.flavor.id` | Yes | ID of the reserved flavor, read from `currentState.flavor.id`. |
| `targetSpec.location.region` | Yes | Region of the reservation. |
| `targetSpec.location.availabilityZone` | No | Availability zone of the reservation, in a 3-AZ region. |

Fill in the other parameters (name, image, networks, SSH key) as for any instance.

To run actions on a reserved instance (reboot, rescue mode, etc.), use the same calls as for any other instance:

<Api version="v2" section="/publicCloud" method="POST" route={"/publicCloud/project/\{projectId\}/compute/instance/\{instanceId\}/action"} />

### Cancelling or stopping a reservation <a name="delete"></a>

You can cancel a reservation yourself only while it is `Pending`, before you make the down payment.

<Tabs>
<Tab label="OVHcloud Console">

To cancel a pending reservation, open its page and click <code className="action">Delete reservation</code>, then confirm. This button is only shown while the reservation is `Pending`.

</Tab>
<Tab label="OVHcloud API v2">

With the API, you can delete a reservation whose status is `Pending`:

<Api version="v2" section="/publicCloud" method="DELETE" route={"/publicCloud/project/\{projectId\}/compute/reservation/\{reservationId\}"} />

</Tab>
</Tabs>

:::warning
Once a reservation is active, it can no longer be cancelled from the OVHcloud Console or the API. To stop or delete an active reservation, contact our [Support](/links/support) team.
:::

### Limitations <a name="limitations"></a>

| Limitation | Details |
|---|---|
| Renewal | A reservation is not renewed automatically. When the commitment period ends, create a new reservation to commit for another 12 or 36 months. |
| Partial delivery | A reservation is delivered in full or not at all. If the full quantity is no longer available when the down payment is paid, the reservation is cancelled and the down payment refunded. |
| Cancellation | A reservation can only be cancelled from the OVHcloud Console while it is `Pending`. Active reservations can only be stopped by our Support team. |
