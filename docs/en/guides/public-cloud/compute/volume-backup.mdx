## Objective

If you value the data stored in your Block Storage volumes, it is worth organising the backup of this data to prevent the potential impact of any issue on this data, whether it is a human error or a cluster incident.

A **volume snapshot** is a recovery point stored within the same storage cluster as the original volume. Operations will be fast, but in case of an incident on the cluster, both volume and volume snapshot might be unavailable. Volume snapshot creation does not require the volume to be detached from the instance.

A **volume backup** is an image created out of your volume, which is stored in the Object Storage cluster where the original volume is located.
The level of resiliency is ideal and will allow you to quickly react to any incident on your volume, creating another volume from the backup. Volume backup creation requires the volume to be detached from the instance.

Both volume snapshot and volume backup allow you to:

- Create backups of your volume with a few clicks and keep them for as long as you need.
- Use the backups to restore your volume state.
- Use the backups as a template to create identical volumes.

**This guide explains how to create a backup of your Block Storage volume in the OVHcloud Console.**

## Requirements

- A detached [Block Storage volume](/guides/public-cloud/compute/create-and-configure-an-additional-disk-on-an-instance) created in your [Public Cloud project](/links/public-cloud/public-cloud)

## Limitations

Backups of encrypted LUKS volume types can only be restored to the same encrypted LUKS volume type.

## Instructions

In the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, click <code className="action">Block Storage</code> under **Storage**. In the row of the volume concerned, click the <code className="action">...</code> button, then select <code className="action">Create a backup</code>.

:::info
**To create a backup, the volume must first be detached from its instance.**

- For a Linux environment, refer to the **Using Linux** section of the guide "[Create and configure an additional disk on an instance](/guides/public-cloud/compute/create-and-configure-an-additional-disk-on-an-instance)".
- For a Windows environment, refer to the **Using Windows** section of the guide "[Create and configure an additional disk on an instance](/guides/public-cloud/compute/create-and-configure-an-additional-disk-on-an-instance)".

:::


Enter a name for the backup, take note of the pricing information, then click <code className="action">Create</code>.

You can also create a backup from the **Volume Backup** page: in the left-hand menu, click <code className="action">Volume Backup</code> under **Storage**, then <code className="action">Create a volume backup</code>. Select the **Volume to back up**, enter the backup name and click <code className="action">Create</code>. If the volume is attached to an instance, you are first asked to detach it (<code className="action">Detach your volume</code>).

To create a volume snapshot instead, follow the guide [Creating a volume snapshot](/guides/public-cloud/compute/storage-create-volume-snapshot).

The backup creation, be it a volume snapshot or a volume backup, may take several hours, depending on the amount of data present on the volume, as well as the instance resource utilisation for the volume snapshot and other host-specific factors.

We therefore recommend that you perform your snapshots outside of your production hours. 

Other best practices include:

- Avoid creating snapshots and backups during peak hours (04:00 AM - 10:00 PM Paris time).
- Regularly validate that you are able to get your data back from your volume snapshot or volume backup.
- Install the qemu-guest agent if it is not installed, or try to disable it if necessary.
- Even if not mandatory, prefer to detach your volume when creating a volume snapshot.

Since a volume snapshot / volume backup is a clone of the entire disk, it will have the maximum size of the original volume, regardless of the actual disk space allocation.

You will find the list of your volume snapshots on the <code className="action">Volume Snapshot</code> page, under **Storage** in the left-hand menu. After the snapshot is created, it will be added to this table.

You will find the list of your volume backups on the <code className="action">Volume Backup</code> page, under **Storage** in the left-hand menu. As soon as the volume backup creation is requested, it will be added to this table.

Click the <code className="action">...</code> button to <code className="action">Delete</code> or to <code className="action">Create a volume</code> based on the respective volume snapshot or volume backup. For a volume backup, you can also select <code className="action">Restore a volume</code>.
You can find further information in [this guide](/guides/public-cloud/compute/storage-create-volume-from-backup).
