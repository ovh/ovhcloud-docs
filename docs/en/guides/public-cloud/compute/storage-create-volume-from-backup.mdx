## Objective

You can attach additional disks to your Public Cloud instances based on previously created volume snapshots.

This may be useful in the following cases:

- If you want to restore additional disk data.
- If you want to have a highly available, high-performance storage space with your data.
- If you want to move your data to another instance.

**This guide explains how to create and configure an additional disk on one of your instances from a volume snapshot.**

## Requirements

- A [Public Cloud instance](/links/public-cloud/public-cloud) in your OVHcloud account
- A volume snapshot in the same OpenStack region
- Administrative access (sudo) to your instance via SSH or RDP

## Instructions

### Creating the disk from a volume snapshot

In the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, click <code className="action">Volume Snapshot</code> under **Storage**.

Click the <code className="action">...</code> button on the row of the snapshot you want to create a volume from, then <code className="action">Create a volume</code>.

In the **Create a volume from a snapshot** window, enter the **Volume name** and the **Volume capacity** of the new disk, check the estimated amount, then click <code className="action">Create volume</code>.

To create a volume from a volume backup instead, click <code className="action">Volume Backup</code> under **Storage**, then follow the same steps from the <code className="action">...</code> button of the backup.

It may take a few minutes to create the disk, depending on its size.

### Attaching the disk to an instance

Once the disk has been created, you can choose to attach it to an instance. To do this, click <code className="action">Block Storage</code> under **Storage** in the left-hand menu.

Click the <code className="action">...</code> button on the row of the volume of your choice, then <code className="action">Attach to instance</code>.

In the **Attach Volume to an instance** panel, select the **Instance** and click <code className="action">Confirm</code> to attach the disk.

The process of attaching the disk to your instance will then begin, and this may take a few minutes.

Once the attachment is complete, you can follow these steps on how to configure the additional disk [using Linux](/guides/public-cloud/compute/create-and-configure-an-additional-disk-on-an-instance#using-linux) or [using Windows](/guides/public-cloud/compute/create-and-configure-an-additional-disk-on-an-instance#using-windows).
