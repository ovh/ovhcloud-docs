:::info
You must have **project permission** `delete` in order to delete projects. These permissions are set at user level in the [Organization IAM](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/iam-organization#resources-managed-by-the-organization-iam).
:::

Deleting a Project **completely and permanently erases its data**. Use only when necessary!

## Choose the Project to delete

You can delete a Project via the [Project Card menu](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/manage-navigation#your-Projects-list) in the [Homepage](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/manage-navigation).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="delete Project" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/project/picts/project_menu_delete.png" loading="lazy" />

## Confirm decision by typing Project name

After you click on *Delete Project* you will be prompted to confirm your decision by typong your Project name.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="confirm delete" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/project/picts/confirm_delete.png" loading="lazy" />

## Click on link on email received

You will receive an email with a link to confirm the deletion of your Project. Just click on the confirmation link to delete the Project.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="email" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/project/picts/confirm_delete_email.png" loading="lazy" />
