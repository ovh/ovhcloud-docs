## Objective

The Logs Explorer is a comprehensive tool to **query and export logs from job executions and live deployments** on the Data Platform.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Control Center logs explorer" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/cc/logs/picts/cc-logs-explorer.png" loading="lazy" />

Like all subcomponents in the Control Center, the Logs Explorer can be opened at the level of the organization. However, logs exploration will only be possible inside a specific Project. If the Logs Explorer is opened at the level of one Project, then this field cannot be edited.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Control Center right dataplant" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/cc/logs/picts/cc-right-dataplant.png" loading="lazy" />

## Get logs

### Get logs of past job executions

The Logs Explorer allows you to check the logs of:

- [Data Processing Engine actions](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/landing-page-dpe-actions) job executions
- [Data Processing Engine workflows](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/dpe-workflows) job executions
- [Connectors metadata extract](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/connectors-analyzer-extract-metadata) jobs
- [Lakehouse Manager physical build](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/lakehouse-manager-manage-tables) jobs

Choose *Jobs* as the **Resource** on the left column.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Get logs of past job executions — Control Center right jobs" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/cc/logs/picts/cc-right-jobs.png" loading="lazy" />

Choose the **type of jobs** to explore.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Get logs of past job executions — Control Center jobs type" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/cc/logs/picts/cc-jobs-type.png" loading="lazy" />

By default, the Logs Explorer will display the logs for all jobs matching this type. You can drill-down on a specific object in the bottom left list - ranked by number of executions.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Get logs of past job executions — Control Center jobs object" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/cc/logs/picts/cc-jobs-object.png" loading="lazy" />

You can drill-down further by filtering on a specific **execution ID** or a specific time-range above the display.

:::warning
Make sure that the execution that you want to display is contained in the time-range that you are filtered on.
:::

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Get logs of past job executions — Control Center jobs execution timeframe" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/cc/logs/picts/cc-jobs-execution-timeframe.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
Note that logs will be queried by successive batches of at most 250 logs. Scroll down in the console to load more logs.
:::

### Get logs of deployments

The Logs Explorer also allows you to check the logs of:

- [APIs](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/landing-page-app-services-apis)
- [Applications](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/landing-page-app-services-apps) 
- [Notebooks](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/dpe-notebooks)
- [Always-up deployments in the Data Processing Engine](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/dpe-jobs-preferences#always-up)
- The *App Service* component: an infrastructure service to control APIs and apps

Choose *Deployments* as the **Resource** on the left column.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Get logs of deployments — Control Center right deployments" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/cc/logs/picts/cc-right-deployments.png" loading="lazy" />

Choose the **type of deployment** to explore.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Get logs of deployments — Control Center deployments type" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/cc/logs/picts/cc-deployments-type.png" loading="lazy" />

By default, the Logs Explorer will display the logs for all deployments matching this type. You can drill-down on a specific object in the bottom left list - ranked by number of active workers. By default, the logs of all workers for that deployed object are aggregated altogether in the display.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Get logs of deployments — Control Center deployments object" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/cc/logs/picts/cc-deployments-object.png" loading="lazy" />

You can drill-down further by filtering on a specific **worker ID** or a specific time-range above the display.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Get logs of deployments — Control Center deployment worker timeframe" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/cc/logs/picts/cc-deployment-worker-timeframe.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
Note that logs will be queried by successive batches of at most 250 logs. Scroll down in the console to load more logs.
:::

## Filter logs

### Search in logs

To filter down your log result, it is possible to either search for an exact substring or to match to a [regular expression (Regex)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_expression) using the search bar above the console. Searching something in the searchbar will **run a new log query on the whole timeframe** and return the logs matching the search filters.

By default, simply typing text in the searchbar will search for all logs containing this substring, ignoring lowercase/capital letters.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Search in logs — Control Center left searchlog" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/cc/logs/picts/cc-left-searchlog.png" loading="lazy" />

Alternatively, it is possible to start your search with any of the following operators:

- `=` followed by text: search for all logs containing this substring (case sensitive)
- `!=` followed by text: search for all logs not containing this substring (case sensitive)
- `~` followed by a regex: search for all logs matching the regular expression
- `!~` followed by a regex: search for all logs that don't match the regular expression

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Search in logs — Control Center left searchlog2" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/cc/logs/picts/cc-left-searchlog2.png" loading="lazy" />

### Severity

Logs have different level of severity - ranged from *INFO* to *CRITICAL* - to filter on.

:::info
If you don't see all the levels that you should be seeing, make sure your jobs are [configured to export the correct level](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/dpe-jobs-preferences#logs-level) of logs.
:::

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Severity — Control Center left severity" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/cc/logs/picts/cc-left-severity.png" loading="lazy" />

### Timestamp

As mentioned above, logs are all associated to a specific timestamp, so they can be filtered on a time-range. For jobs execution logs, it is also possible to filter on one specific execution.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Timestamp — Control Center jobs execution timeframe" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/cc/logs/picts/cc-jobs-execution-timeframe.png" loading="lazy" />

## Share logs

Logs can be shared in one of two ways: by sharing the link to the Logs Explorer or by exporting a text file containing the logs.

### Share an Explorer link

Click on the **link** icon to get a link for the page with the current filter configuration.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Share an Explorer link — Control Center left link" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/cc/logs/picts/cc-left-link.png" loading="lazy" />

### Export a log file

Click on the **export** icon to download a .log file containing the logs currently displayed in the UI.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Export a log file — Control Center left export" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/cc/logs/picts/cc-left-export.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
Share those log files to the Data Platform Support team whenever you need help debugging a Project on Data Platform.
:::
