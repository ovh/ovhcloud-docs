## Objective

Subscribers are the people who receive the email an [alert](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/landing-page-control-center-alerting) sends when its trigger conditions are met. Subscribers are set per alert, and the same list applies whether the alert watches a [job execution](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/control-center-alerting-job-execution) or a [health check](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/control-center-alerting-health-checks).

Subscribers are the users who receive the email notification sent when the trigger conditions (and triggering options) of the alert are met.

In the Subscribers tab of the alert, find the panel *Users*. This table lists all the users in your organization that are currently subscribed to the alert, meaning they will be notified of the alert on their [account](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/manage-organizations-profile)'s email address.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="alerts" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/cc/alerting/picts/cc-subscribers-users.png" loading="lazy" />

To link a new user, click on **Add users** in the top right.

You can either add existing users from your organization, or whole new users. If you add a new user, they will automatically be added in your organization as simple users with no permission. Don't forget to [grant them access to the Projects](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/iam-users#manage-organization-iam-users) if you want them to access the alert's page on the Platform after receiving alert emails.

:::warning
New subscribed users won't be able to receive notifications until they verify their email address.
:::
