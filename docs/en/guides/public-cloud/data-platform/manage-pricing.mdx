## Objective

Data Platform is billed pay-as-you-go. There are no up-front costs, no per-user licence and no data transfer charges: you pay for the compute you run, by the minute, and for the data you store and query, by volume.

This page explains what generates a cost, the units those costs are counted in, and where to find the current prices. It does not list prices: those live on the OVHcloud public cloud price list, which is the single source of truth for them.

[See the Data Platform prices](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-ie/public-cloud/prices/#data-platform)

## What you pay for

The total cost of a Data Platform project is the sum of a small number of components.

**Storage.** The data held in the [Lakehouse Manager](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/landing-page-lakehouse-manager), tables and [buckets](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/lakehouse-manager-buckets) alike, charged on the volume stored over the month.

**Data processing.** The compute consumed by [Data Processing Engine jobs](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/landing-page-dpe-jobs): loads, aggregations, SQL, custom Python and PySpark. Charged on compute allocated for the duration of each run.

**Analytics queries.** The queries run from the [Analytics Manager](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/landing-page-analytics-manager) and through external [consumers](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/landing-page-connectors-consumers), charged on the volume of data scanned.

**Application Services.** The compute behind deployed [APIs and apps](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/landing-page-app-services), charged for the time each deployment is up rather than per request.

**Support.** OVHcloud support levels above the standard one are charged as a percentage of the total OVHcloud bill. See the [OVHcloud support levels](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-ie/support-levels/) for what each includes.

## What is not billed

Data Platform does not charge:

- data transfer, in either direction, for moving data into or out of your account
- data ingest fees
- service API calls

## Billing units

Compute is expressed in **Data Platform Units**.

:::info
**1 DPU is 1 vCPU and 4 GB of RAM.** Compute cost is therefore DPU multiplied by the time those DPU were allocated, usually expressed in DPU hours.
:::

The other units follow the resource they measure: storage in GB or TB held over the month, and analytics queries in TB scanned.

Knowing which components hold DPU, and for how long, is most of cost control:

- A [job](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/landing-page-dpe-jobs) in serverless mode holds its DPU only while it runs.
- A job in always-up mode holds its DPU continuously, until the execution mode is set back to serverless.
- A [notebook](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/dpe-notebooks) holds all its assigned DPU for as long as it is turned on, whether or not code is running.
- A deployed API or app holds its DPU while deployed.

## Worked cost examples

Three examples of what a project costs in practice, at small, mid-market and enterprise scale. They show which components each workload consumes and in what volumes, so the shape of a bill is visible before you build one.

:::warning
**Figures are as of 1 September 2026** and are illustrative, not a quote. Rates change: the [price list](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-ie/public-cloud/prices/#data-platform) is the only current source.
:::

:::details See three worked cost examples

The three following examples provide insight into the total cost in operating OVHcloud Data Platform to load, process, query data, and expose dashboards.

#### Cost example: Smaller Company

This hypothetical customer is a small retail business leveraging Data Platform data warehouse capabilities for BI. They ingest 2 GB of data every working day, totaling 40 GB (80M lines) monthly. The process requires 2 DPU for 2 hours daily to load data, plus an additional 2 DPU for 2 hours daily to aggregate tables. Consequently, their total monthly DPU usage reaches 160 hours (2 DPU x 4 hours/day x 20 working days). Their total storage currently stands at 500 GB, and their analytics team scans an average of 2 TB of stored data monthly.

| Services            | Volume / month                          | Cost / month |
| :------------------ | :-------------------------------------- | :----------- |
| Storage             | 500 GB                                  | 10.50 €      |
| Data Processing     | 160 h of DPU (2 DPU x 4h x 20 work days) | 12.96 €      |
| Lakehouse/Analytics Queries | 2 TB                                    | 10.96 €      |
| **Total cost excl. VAT** |                                         | **34.42 €**  |

#### Cost example: Mid-Market

This hypothetical customer is a medium-sized Tech company using Data Platform to centralize and aggregate data analytics from their main Information System tools. They ingest a continuous stream of 720 GB of data per month (1.4 billion lines). Their Data Engineering team performs advanced data transformation using PySpark. Overall, data loading and transformation consume an average of 20 DPUs for 6 hours per working day each month, resulting in 2,400 hours of DPU usage for data processing (20 DPU x 6 hours x 20 working days). Their total lakehouse storage is 25 TB, and their data analysts run queries scanning a total of 50 TB of stored data. Additionally, they use 4 DPUs 24/7 to run deployed Application Services, totaling 2,920 hours of additional DPU usage (4 DPU x 730 hours).

This client decided to subscribe to [OVHcloud Business Support](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-ie/support-levels/business/) to access our technical expert team 24/7 with less than 30 min response time.

#### Cost example: Enterprise

This hypothetical customer is a health insurance company operating in multiple countries, using the solution to cover the analytics needs of its subsidiaries, aggregated at a group level. They utilize an average of 40 DPUs for approximately 10 hours each, 6 days a week, for advanced data exploration and processing. They store 100 TB of data in their Data Lakehouse. Their data analyst team scans about 500 TB of data for their queries, both locally in the Data Lakehouse and on their remote on-prem storage via direct queries. Additionally, they use 4 DPUs to host APIs and application services deployed 5 days a week (400 hours a month), totaling 1,600 hours of additional DPU usage (4 DPU x 400 hours).

## Your usage and your invoices

OVHcloud produces a monthly usage statement for every customer with at least one active service. From a project or an organization, open the configuration wheel at the top right of the screen, then **Billing information**.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Screenshot of Billing Information in Control Center" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/billing/picts/billing-information.png" loading="lazy" />

The statement itemizes the month's usage in credits consumed and currency spent, and carries an estimate of the next OVHcloud invoice alongside the history of previous ones, split per service.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Screenshot of Billing Information in Control Center" src="/images/public-cloud/data-platform/product/billing/picts/billing-information-2.PNG" loading="lazy" />

:::info
For each Data Platform service the statement shows its region, its type, its volume of usage and the related cost.
:::

Invoices themselves, for Data Platform and every other OVHcloud service, are accessed and managed from the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Control Panel</ManagerLink>. See the [OVHcloud article on managing bills](/guides/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/invoice-management) for what can be done there.

Live resource consumption, as opposed to its cost, is visible in the [Control Center](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/landing-page-control-center): CPU, memory, storage and network use per component, at project or organization level.

## Subscriptions and billing accounts

Subscription plans, committed resources and the ownership of a billing account are managed at OVHcloud account level rather than inside a project, from the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Control Panel</ManagerLink>.

## Keeping costs down

- [Build a dashboard on your billing data](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/tutorials-billing-analysis) so consumption is visible before it appears on an invoice. The [FOCUS and Superset tutorial](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/tutorials-finops-focus-superset) takes the same data further for FinOps reporting.
- [Set quotas](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/manage-organizations-settings#quotas) at organization level as a safeguard against unintended consumption.
- Prefer a shared [environment](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/dpe-environments) in always-up mode over always-up on individual actions, so the same resources serve several workloads.
- Archive cold data to [OVHcloud Object Storage](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-ie/public-cloud/object-storage/), in the Standard, Infrequent Access or Cold Archive class as the access pattern requires.
- For a structural review, OVHcloud [Professional Services](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-ie/professional-services/) and the data consultancies in the [OVHcloud Partner Program](https://partner.ovhcloud.com/en-ie/directory/) assess data architecture and cost together.
