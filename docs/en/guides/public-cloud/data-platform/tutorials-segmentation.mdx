## Objective

[Segmentation](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/dpe-jobs-segmentation) will allow you to unleash the Data Processing Engine's power of scalability. When executed, your [actions](/guides/public-cloud/data-platform/landing-page-dpe-actions) will be **split into multiple tasks**, each task will manage a smaller dataset.

:::info
With great power comes with great responsibility: segmentation options should be configured carefully. Depending on your segmentation choices, your action may be much faster... or much slower.
:::
