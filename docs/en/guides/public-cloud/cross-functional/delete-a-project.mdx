## Objective

When you no longer need your [Public Cloud](/links/public-cloud/public-cloud) project, you can delete it directly from the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>.

When you delete your Public Cloud project, a final invoice is issued for the outstanding amount.

:::warning
Please note that deleting a project is not the same as deactivating your Public Cloud instances. When you delete a project, the resources contained in it will be permanently lost. This action can not be undone.
:::

**This guide explains how to delete a Public Cloud project.**

## Requirements

- A [Public Cloud project](/links/public-cloud/public-cloud) in your OVHcloud account

[[cpnav:publiccloud-project-settings]]

## Instructions

{/* CP-STEPS-START:delete-project */}
1. In the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, click <code className="action">Project Management</code>.
2. Click the <code className="action">Settings</code> tab.
3. In the **Delete project** section, click <code className="action">Delete project</code>.
4. The **Cancel project** window informs you that all of your services, resources, settings and data will be destroyed, with the exception of failover IPs, which will be parked. Click <code className="action">Yes, cancel</code> to proceed.

:::info
A project with a Savings Plan cannot be deleted: the **Delete project** section displays a warning and the window offers <code className="action">Contact Customer Support</code> instead. Contact our support team to delete this project.
:::

An email is then sent to your contact email address, asking you to either confirm or cancel the project deletion. After clicking the confirmation link, you will be redirected to a web page where you will need to enter your account password.<br/>After entering and confirming your password, your project will be submitted for deletion.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:delete-project */}

:::warning
Please note that when a project is marked for deletion, it remains in suspend status for up to 7 days. The project is not immediately deleted. In situations where this may affect the creation of new projects on accounts with limited project quota, please contact our support team for further assistance.

:::
