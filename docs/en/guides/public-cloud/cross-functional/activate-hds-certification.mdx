## Objective

To make your Public Cloud infrastructure and platforms compliant with [healthcare data hosting](/links/hosted-private-cloud/certification-hds) in France, they must necessarily be certified as a healthcare data hosting provider (HDS).

OVHcloud Public Cloud services are HDS certified for certain activities listed in the [certification reference](https://esante.gouv.fr/labels-certifications/hds/certification-des-hebergeurs-de-donnees-de-sante) of the French Digital Health Agency.

The list of certified OVHcloud products is available on the page “[OVHcloud products HDS certification](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/hds-certification)”.

**Find out how to enable the option for hosting healthcare data on your Public Cloud project**

## Requirements

- You must have signed up to the [Business or Enterprise level of support](/links/support) for your OVHcloud account.

[[cpnav:publiccloud-project-settings|en]]

## Instructions

### Enabling the HDS option when creating a new Public Cloud project

{/* CP-STEPS-START:enable-hds-new-project */}
[Create a new Public Cloud project](/guides/public-cloud/cross-functional/create-a-public-cloud-project): on the **Create a Public Cloud project** page, the **Compliance for healthcare data hosting and HDS certification** section is displayed if you have subscribed to the Business or Enterprise support level.

Tick the **Enable HDS certification for this project** box. Check the information, then tick the box in the **Contracts** section to accept the contracts. Click <code className="action">Create my project</code> to finish creating your project. The services in this project will be HDS certified, in accordance with the contractual conditions.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:enable-hds-new-project */}

### Enabling the HDS option on an existing Public Cloud project

{/* CP-STEPS-START:enable-hds-existing-project */}
Select the Public Cloud project for which you would like the services to be HDS certified.

In the left-hand menu, click <code className="action">Project Management</code>, then the <code className="action">Settings</code> tab. If you have subscribed to the Business or Enterprise support level, the **Compliance for healthcare data hosting and HDS certification** section is displayed: tick the **Healthcare data hosting and HDS certification for this project.** box.

You will then have access to the special conditions for hosting healthcare data.

Review the information and check the box for accepting the contracts. Finally, click <code className="action">Update</code> to finalize the HDS certification process for your project, in accordance with the contractual conditions.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:enable-hds-existing-project */}
