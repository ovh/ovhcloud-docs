## Objective

Depending on your use case, you may need to grant other users access to your project, without giving them full access to your services. 
To this end, you can delegate read-only or read/write permissions for your projects to other OVHcloud customer accounts.

**This guide explains how to delegate access rights for a Public Cloud project in the OVHcloud Console.**

### Requirements

- A [Public Cloud project](/links/public-cloud/public-cloud) in your OVHcloud account

[[cpnav:publiccloud-contacts-rights]]

## Instructions

{/* CP-STEPS-START:contacts-rights */}
In the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, click <code className="action">Project Management</code>, then the <code className="action">Contacts & Rights</code> tab.

This tab shows the admin contact and the billing contact of your project. To change them, click <code className="action">Edit</code> next to the contact: the page for managing your contacts opens in a new tab. Follow the instructions in our guide [Changing project contacts](/guides/public-cloud/compute/change-project-contacts) to complete this process.

:::info
The <code className="action">Edit</code> links are only available for accounts in the EU zone, and not for a project in discovery mode.
:::
{/* CP-STEPS-END:contacts-rights */}

### Adding contacts and permissions

{/* CP-STEPS-START:add-contacts-permissions */}
Only the admin contact of the project can add contacts. In a project in discovery mode, contacts cannot be added.

1. Click <code className="action">Create a contact</code>.
2. In the **Create a contact** window, enter the NIC handle of the user (in the form xx00000-ovh) in the **Customer ID (NIC handle)** field.
3. In the **Permissions** list, select `Read-only` or `Read/Write`.
4. Click <code className="action">Add</code> to confirm the access delegation.

:::info
Note that `Read/Write` permissions will allow changes to the project that might affect future billing.
:::

A confirmation email will be sent to you and the new user who will now be able to open the project in the Public Cloud universe of their OVHcloud Console.

The added OVHcloud customer accounts and their respective permissions are listed in the table, in the **Customer ID (NIC handle)** and **Permissions** columns.

To revoke an access, click the <code className="action">Remove contact</code> icon in the **Actions** column of the account's row, then click <code className="action">Delete</code> in the **Remove this contact?** window.
{/* CP-STEPS-END:add-contacts-permissions */}
