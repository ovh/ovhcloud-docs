## Objective

Public Cloud Databases for MongoDB back up your service automatically. You may also want your own copy of your data, for example to keep it outside OVHcloud, or to move it to another service.

**This guide explains how to export your data with `mongodump`, restore it with `mongorestore`, and what to do with a Discovery service before the end of life of this plan.**

## Requirements

- A [Public Cloud project](/links/public-cloud/public-cloud) in your OVHcloud account
- A Public Cloud Databases for MongoDB service
- The IP address of your machine [authorised on the service](/guides/public-cloud/databases/mongodb-manage-control-panel)
- A MongoDB user with the `backup@admin` role to export, and the `readWriteAnyDatabase@admin` and `restore@admin` roles to restore

## Instructions

### End of life of the Discovery plan

The free Discovery plan (`db2-free` node type) is no longer sold since 31 August 2026:

- **31 October 2026**: Discovery services are suspended. They can be reactivated at no extra cost, but only to switch them to another plan or to retrieve their data.
- **30 November 2026**: Discovery services are deleted, with all their data.

Before these dates, choose one of the following options.

#### Switch your service to the Production plan

Your service and its data are kept. Billing for the Production plan starts as soon as you switch.

1. In the OVHcloud Control Panel, open your MongoDB service, then the <code className="action">Configuration</code> tab.
2. On the `Plan` row, click <code className="action">Edit</code>.
3. In the `Modify plan` window, select the **Production** plan, then click <code className="action">Edit</code>.

Your service is updated to a Production service with `db2-2` nodes.

#### Export your data

Export your data free of charge with `mongodump`, following the [next section](#export-your-data-with-mongodump). You can then restore it on another MongoDB service with `mongorestore`.

### Install the MongoDB Database Tools

Install the [MongoDB Database Tools](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/database-tools/installation/) on your machine. They include the `mongodump` and `mongorestore` binaries.

### Build the connection string

Copy the `mongodb+srv://` URI from the `Connection information` section of the <code className="action">Dashboard</code> tab. For `mongodump` and `mongorestore`, replace the `/admin` path with an `authSource=admin` parameter:

```bash
mongodb+srv://<username>:<password>@<srv-hostname>/?authSource=admin&replicaSet=replicaset
```

:::warning
If you keep `/admin` in the path, `mongodump` exports only the `admin` database, and `mongorestore` restores only into it. Your other databases are skipped, without any error.
:::

### Export your data with mongodump

Export all your databases to a compressed archive:

```bash
mongodump --gzip --archive=backup.gz --readPreference=secondaryPreferred \
  --uri="mongodb+srv://<username>:<password>@<srv-hostname>/?authSource=admin&replicaSet=replicaset"
```

The output lists each collection exported, with its number of documents. The export reads from a secondary node, to limit the load on the primary.

Depending on the amount of data and your network connection, this operation can take from a few seconds to several hours. Once it completes, the `backup.gz` file holds your data.

### Restore your data with mongorestore

:::warning
`mongorestore` merges the archive with the data already in the service: collections that are not in the archive are left untouched, and documents that already exist are not overwritten.
:::

Restore the archive, excluding the `admin` database:

```bash
mongorestore --gzip --archive=backup.gz --nsExclude 'admin.*' \
  --uri="mongodb+srv://<username>:<password>@<srv-hostname>/?authSource=admin&replicaSet=replicaset"
```

Users are not restored: on Public Cloud Databases for MongoDB, users are managed only from the OVHcloud Control Panel or the OVHcloud API.

The last line of the output shows the number of documents restored and the number of documents that failed. Documents that already exist in the service are counted as failed. To replace the collections of the archive instead, add the `--drop` option:

```bash
mongorestore --gzip --archive=backup.gz --nsExclude 'admin.*' --drop \
  --uri="mongodb+srv://<username>:<password>@<srv-hostname>/?authSource=admin&replicaSet=replicaset"
```
