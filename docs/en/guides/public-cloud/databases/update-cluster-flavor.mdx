## Objective

Learn how to adjust the flavor of your cluster according to your needs.

## How to upgrade your flavor

This option is available through the OVHcloud <ApiLink>API</ApiLink> and <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>.

### Requirements

- A [Public Cloud project](/links/public-cloud/public-cloud) in your OVHcloud account
- Access to the <ApiLink>OVHcloud API</ApiLink> (optional)

[[cpnav:publiccloud-databases|en]]

### Instructions

{/* CP-STEPS-START:upgrade-instructions */}
In the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, click your engine under **Databases**, then click the name of your service.

In the **General information** card of the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, click <code className="action">Upgrade</code>. In the panel that opens, select the new **Node flavor** of your cluster, then click <code className="action">Save</code>.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:upgrade-instructions */}

## How to downgrade your flavor

This option is currently available via the OVHcloud <ApiLink>API</ApiLink>.

### Requirements

- Access to the OVHcloud <ApiLink>API</ApiLink> (create your credentials by consulting [this guide](/guides/manage-and-operate/api/first-steps))
- A [Public Cloud project](/links/public-cloud/public-cloud) in your OVHcloud account

### Instructions

In order to be able to downgrade your flavor, you must have a threshold of 20% between your current disk space used (base storage + additional storage) and the maximum storage space allowed for the flavor.

For instance, if you have a PostgreSQL cluster with db1-15 as flavor and less than 384 GB (80% of 480GB) of disk space used, you can decrease the flavor to db1-7 on that endpoint:

<Api version="v1" section="/cloud" method="PUT" route={"/cloud/project/\{serviceName\}/database/mongodb/\{clusterId\}"} />
