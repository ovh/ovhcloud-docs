## Objective

**This guide takes you through the first steps with Public Cloud Databases for MongoDB: creating a service, authorising your IP address, creating a user and connecting.**

:::warning
The free **Discovery** plan (`db2-free` node type) is no longer sold. Existing Discovery services are suspended on 31 October 2026, then deleted with their data on 30 November 2026. Find out how to [switch your service to the Production plan or export your data](/guides/public-cloud/databases/mongodb-howto-backup-restore).
:::

## Requirements

- A [Public Cloud project](/links/public-cloud/public-cloud) in your OVHcloud account

[[cpnav:publiccloud-databases]]

## Instructions

### Create the service

Click <code className="action">Create your managed database</code>, or <code className="action">Create a service</code> if your project already contains databases. In the `Create a database service` page:

1. In the `Engine` field, select **MongoDB**, then its `Version`: **7.0** or **8.0**.
2. In the `Service plan` field, select **Production** (3 nodes) or **Advanced**. The [capabilities page](/guides/public-cloud/databases/mongodb-concept-capabilities) details the differences between plans.
3. Select the `Datacentre`, the `Instance` (node type) and, if needed, additional `Storage`.
4. Select the `Network` type. You can already add the public IP address of your machine in `Authorised IPs`. Then enter a `Name` for your service.
5. Check `Your order`, then click <code className="action">Order</code>.

The service appears in the list of your services. It can be used once its status is `READY`, after several minutes.

### Authorise your IP address

Your service rejects all connections until you authorise an IP address or a CIDR block. If you did not add your IP address when creating the service, open your service, then the <code className="action">Configuration</code> tab. In the `IPs` section, add the public IP address of the machine that connects to the service, then click <code className="action">Save changes</code>. The [dedicated guide](/guides/public-cloud/databases/mongodb-manage-control-panel) gives more details.

### Create your first user

No user is created with the service. Open the <code className="action">Users</code> tab, then click <code className="action">Add user</code>:

1. Enter a username.
2. Add one or more roles, for example `readWrite` on the database used by your application, or `readWriteAnyDatabase` on the `admin` database.
3. Click <code className="action">Create User</code>.

Note the password displayed: it cannot be retrieved later. You can generate a new one with the <code className="action">Reset password</code> action of the user.

:::info
Users are managed only from the OVHcloud Control Panel or the OVHcloud API. A user created with the `createUser` command is deleted automatically a few minutes later.
:::

### Connect to the service

In the <code className="action">Dashboard</code> tab, the `Connection information` section displays the URI of your service. Replace `<username>` and `<password>` with the credentials of your user.

Then follow one of these guides to connect:

- [Connect with mongosh](/guides/public-cloud/databases/mongodb-connect-cli)
- [Connect with MongoDB Compass](/guides/public-cloud/databases/mongodb-connect-compass)
- [Connect with Python](/guides/public-cloud/databases/mongodb-connect-python)
- [Connect with PHP](/guides/public-cloud/databases/mongodb-connect-php)

Other integration examples are available in the following repository: [https://github.com/ovh/public-cloud-databases-examples/tree/main/databases/mongodb](https://github.com/ovh/public-cloud-databases-examples/tree/main/databases/mongodb).
