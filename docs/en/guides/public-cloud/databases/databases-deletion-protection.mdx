## Objective

Public Cloud databases allow you to protect your service against accidental deletion.

**This guide explains how to configure deletion protection for your Public Cloud databases service so that the service cannot be deleted until the deletion protection is disabled.**

## Requirements

- A [Public Cloud databases service](/links/public-cloud/databases) up and running
- Access to the <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink> or to the <ApiLink>OVHcloud API</ApiLink>

## Instructions

### Enable the deletion protection for your service

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Via the OVHcloud Console">
    In the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, click your engine under **Databases**, then click the name of your service.
    
    Open the <code className="action">Configuration</code> tab. In the **Configuration** section, turn on the **Deletion protection** switch. The change is applied immediately.
    
    As long as the protection is enabled, the service cannot be deleted: the **Danger zone** section of the <code className="action">Configuration</code> tab indicates that deletion protection is enabled.
    
    To disable the protection, turn off the **Deletion protection** switch, then confirm with <code className="action">Disable</code>. The service can then be deleted.
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Via the OVHcloud API">
    You can enable deletion protection for your service using the API endpoint:
    
    <Api version="v1" section="/cloud" method="PUT" route={"/cloud/project/\{serviceName\}/database/mysql/\{clusterId\}"} />
    
    Replace `mysql` in the route with your service's engine (for example `postgresql` or `mongodb`).
    
    The boolean property `deletionProtection` should be set to `true` to activate the protection.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
