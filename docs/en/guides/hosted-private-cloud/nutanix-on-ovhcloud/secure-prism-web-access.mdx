## Objective

After delivery, Prism Central is accessible on the public Internet. Access restriction to one or more IP addresses can be achieved through the [OVHcloud Load Balancer](/links/network/load-balancer).

**This guide explains how to secure access to Prism Central.**

:::warning
OVHcloud is providing you with services for which you are responsible, with regard to their configuration and management. You are therefore responsible for ensuring they function correctly.

This guide is designed to assist you in common tasks as much as possible. Nevertheless, we recommend that you contact the [OVHcloud Professional Services team](/links/professional-services) or a [specialist service provider](/links/partner) if you have difficulties or doubts concerning the administration, usage or implementation of services on a server.

:::

## Requirements

- A Nutanix cluster in your OVHcloud account

[[cpnav:privatecloud-nutanix-console|en]]

## Instructions

### Step 1: Find the relevant Load Balancer

{/* CP-STEPS-START:find-load-balancer */}

Select your cluster. On its <code className="action">Overview</code> tab, the **Network** section shows the Load Balancer of your cluster in the **Load balancer** row.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:find-load-balancer */}

### Step 2: Access your Load Balancer front-ends

{/* CP-STEPS-START:access-load-balancer-front-ends */}

Click the Load Balancer name in the **Load balancer** row to open its page.

Open the <code className="action">Frontends</code> tab.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:access-load-balancer-front-ends */}

### Step 3: Edit the Prism Central front-end

{/* CP-STEPS-START:edit-prism-central-front-end */}

In the table, click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column of the Prism Central frontend, then <code className="action">Edit</code>. The **Edit Frontend** page opens.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:edit-prism-central-front-end */}

### Step 4: Add the authorised IPs

{/* CP-STEPS-START:add-authorised-ips */}

Click <code className="action">Advanced settings</code> to expand this section. In the **Whitelisted IPs** field, enter your public ISP IP address or any IP addresses from which access should be granted to Prism Central Web, in CIDR notation, then click <code className="action">Add</code> for each of them. If the list is empty, access is allowed from any address.

Click <code className="action">Save changes</code>. Then, to apply the configuration, go to the <code className="action">Overview</code> tab and, in the **General information** section, under **Deployment**, click <code className="action">Apply N changes</code>, where N is the number of pending changes. A single click applies the configuration to every zone of your Load Balancer.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:add-authorised-ips */}
