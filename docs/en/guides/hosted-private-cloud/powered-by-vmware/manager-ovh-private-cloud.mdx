## Objective

The OVHcloud Console enables you to easily manage your Hosted Private Cloud infrastructure.

**This guide explains the management options available.**

## Requirements

- A [Hosted Private Cloud infrastructure](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/enterprise/products/hosted-private-cloud/)

[[cpnav:privatecloud-vmware-vsphere-console]]

## Instructions

### Hosted Private Cloud View

{/* CP-STEPS-START:hosted-private-cloud-view */}

Using an administrator account, go to **Private Cloud** › **Managed VMware vSphere** in the OVHcloud Console and select your service in the list by clicking its name. The service page opens on its **Dashboard** tab; the other tabs are **Datacenter**, **Users**, **Access control**, **Encryption**, **Operations** and **Logs**.

The page title shows the description of your infrastructure. You can change it by clicking the pencil icon next to the title, entering the new description and confirming with the ✓ icon. The service name itself cannot be changed.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:hosted-private-cloud-view */}

#### Dashboard

{/* CP-STEPS-START:general-information */}

The **Dashboard** tab gives you an overview of your service, in four sections.

The **General information** section shows:

- The **Description**, editable through the pencil icon next to it
- The **Software solution**, which shows the vCSA version installed
- The **Private Cloud zone** and the **Region**: the location of your Hosted Private Cloud
- The **Number of datacenters** in your infrastructure
- Links to your management interfaces: <code className="action">Open vScope</code> and <code className="action">Open web interface</code> (the vSphere client)

The **Options** section shows your enabled and disabled options (Zerto, vRealize Operations (vROps), NSX-T, Logs, Windows licence (SPLA)), each with direct access to its modification through the action on its row (for example <code className="action">Enable</code>, <code className="action">Modify options</code> or <code className="action">Disable</code>). It also shows the **Number of IP blocks** you own: to order more, open the ⋮ menu next to it and click <code className="action">Order IP blocks</code>.

The **Compliance** section shows the sector-specific compliance of your service. Click <code className="action">Enable sector-specific compliance</code> to enable a certification (HDS or PCI DSS).

The **Subscription** section allows you to sign up to the OVHcloud Hosted Private Cloud mailing list (**Mailing list**, <code className="action">Sign up to the mailing list</code>). It shows the creation date and the **Automatic renewal** date of your service, and allows for service cancellation through the <code className="action">Cancel the service</code> link.

The access policy of your infrastructure (`Open` or `Restricted`) is shown on the **Access control** tab, under **vCenter access policy** (see below).

{/* CP-STEPS-END:general-information */}

#### Datacenters

{/* CP-STEPS-START:datacenters */}

The **Datacenter** tab shows your current virtual datacenters, with their range, resources and number of virtual machines, and offers the <code className="action">Order a datacenter</code> button to add more as you need. The ⋮ menu (**Actions** column) of a datacenter also lets you order a host or a datastore for it.<br/>
Click a datacenter's name to open the full Datacenter view (see below), which will give you more details and options.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:datacenters */}

#### Users

{/* CP-STEPS-START:users */}

The **Users** tab lists all the accounts which can log in to vSphere and allows the creation of new ones with the <code className="action">Create User</code> button.

You have several options when you open the ⋮ menu (**Actions** column) of a user:

- <code className="action">Edit</code>: edit the user's details and permissions shown in the summary table
- <code className="action">View/Edit the rights for each DC</code>: see/change the user's rights for each datacenter
- <code className="action">Change password</code>: change the user's password
- <code className="action">Delete this user</code>: delete the user

Clicking <code className="action">View/Edit the rights for each DC</code> will take you to the rights management page, with one row per datacenter.

Open the ⋮ menu (**Actions** column) of a datacenter and click <code className="action">Modify rights</code> to edit them in the **Editing rights** window.

- **vSphere access**: to set the global rights for a user in vSphere

|Right|Description|
|---|---|
|Operator|reserved for OVHcloud administrators|
|None|no access|
|Read-only|read-only access|
|Read/Write|write access|

- **Access to the VM Network**: concerns management rights over the public network section (a.k.a "VM Network" in the vSphere interface)

|Right|Description|
|---|---|
|Operator|allows VMs to be configured on a public network|
|None|no access|
|Read-only|read access only|

- **Access to V(x)Lans / GENEVE**: concerns management rights over the private network section (VXLAN for Dedicated Cloud, VLAN for SDDC and Hosted Private Cloud Premier packs)

|Right|Description|
|---|---|
|Operator|allows VMs to be configured on a private network|
|Administrator|allows port groups to be managed on the virtual switch (creation, modification, deletion), for SDDC and Hosted Private Cloud Premier packs only|
|None|no access|
|Read-only|read access only|

- **Host and storage management**: gives the right to add or delete hosts and storage through the OVHcloud plugin in the vSphere client

{/* CP-STEPS-END:users */}

#### Access control and encryption

{/* CP-STEPS-START:security */}

The vCenter access policy and the other security settings are spread over two tabs, **Access control** and **Encryption**.

The **Access control** tab shows, in its **Session settings** section, the **vCenter access policy** of your infrastructure (`Open` or `Restricted`). The policy is displayed for information only: to change it, contact OVHcloud support. You can configure the other session settings (**Session timeout**, **Number of concurrent connections** and **Logout policy**) with the <code className="action">Edit</code> button on their row. Below, the list of IPs and blocks authorised to connect lets you add one with the <code className="action">Associate an IP block</code> button.

:::warning
 If the access policy is set to "Restricted" and no IP address is authorised, no user will be able to connect to the vSphere client. However, virtual machines will remain accessible.

:::

On the **Encryption** tab, you can also add (<code className="action">Associate a KMS server</code>) or see your Key Management Servers.<br/>
Check out [Enabling Virtual Machine Encryption](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vm-encrypt) for more details.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:security */}

#### Operations

{/* CP-STEPS-START:operations */}

The **Operations** tab keeps a log of all tasks executed on your infrastructure.<br/>
By default, the list shows the tasks in progress: you can filter it by state (all states, in progress, done or canceled) and show more columns with the <code className="action">Columns</code> button. If a task's processing date can be changed, its ⋮ menu (**Actions** column) offers <code className="action">Modify the processing date</code>.

:::info
If your access to the vSphere client is denied, a scheduled maintenance may be in progress. Check this tab first before taking further measures.

:::

{/* CP-STEPS-END:operations */}


#### Windows Licence

{/* CP-STEPS-START:windows-licence */}

Your Windows SPLA licence status is shown on the **Windows licence (SPLA)** row of the **Options** section, on the **Dashboard** tab. If the option is disabled, you can order it with the <code className="action">Activate the SPLA</code> button. Licences are then activated per virtual machine, from the **Virtual machines** tab of a datacenter (⋮ menu of the VM, <code className="action">Activate licence</code>). The **Datacenter** tab shows, for each datacenter, the number of Windows VMs and of licensed Windows VMs.<br/>
More information on the [pricing page](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/enterprise/products/hosted-private-cloud/images-licenses/).

{/* CP-STEPS-END:windows-licence */}

### Datacenter View

{/* CP-STEPS-START:datacenter-view */}

A Hosted Private Cloud may include multiple virtual datacenters.<br/>
They are listed on the **Datacenter** tab of your service. Click on a datacenter's name to open its management page.<br/>
The datacenter name can be customized by clicking the pencil icon next to it at the top of the page; its description can be edited from the **General information** section of its **Dashboard** tab (see below).

{/* CP-STEPS-END:datacenter-view */}

#### Datacenter dashboard

{/* CP-STEPS-START:datacenter-general-information */}

The datacenter's **Dashboard** tab gives you an overview of it. Its **General information** section shows:

- The **Name** and **Description**, editable via the pencil icon next to each
- The **Commercial range**, which shows the VMware services subscribed
- The **Hosts** and **Datastores** fields, which give you the quantity of each in the datacenter, with the <code className="action">Order a host</code> and <code className="action">Order a datastore</code> buttons
- Links to your management interfaces: <code className="action">Go to vScope</code> and <code className="action">Web interface</code>

The **Characteristics** section details the datacenter's resources (CPU, RAM, disk space) and its number of virtual machines.

The backup status of your virtual machines is shown in the **Backup** column of the **Virtual machines** tab, and the status of your Disaster Recovery Plan solution on the **Zerto** row of the **Options** section.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:datacenter-general-information */}

#### Hosts

{/* CP-STEPS-START:hosts */}

The **Hosts** tab gives a summary of your hosts in the selected datacenter.<br/>
*If a host is billed hourly, you can switch it to monthly billing: open its ⋮ menu (**Actions** column) and click <code className="action">Convert to monthly billing</code>.*<br/>
You can also order one host via the <code className="action">Order a host</code> button.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:hosts */}

#### Datastores

{/* CP-STEPS-START:datastores */}

The **Datastores** tab gives a summary of your datastores in the selected datacenter; the **Scope** column shows whether each one is global.<br/>
*The datastore mode is switchable to global via its ⋮ menu (**Actions** column) › <code className="action">Convert to global</code>. The same menu offers <code className="action">Convert to monthly billing</code>.*<br/>
You can also order a datastore via the <code className="action">Order a datastore</code> button.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:datastores */}

#### Backup

{/* CP-STEPS-START:backup */}

The **Backup** tab gives access to the backup solution of the datacenter. On current services, it presents Managed Backup for VMware, a backup solution based on Veeam and operated by OVHcloud, with links to protect your infrastructure and to the related tutorials (including the migration from Veeam Managed Backup to Managed Backup for VMware).

On a service that still uses Veeam Managed Backup, the tab allows you to activate and manage that solution instead. Check out the [Activating and using Veeam Managed Backup](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/veeam-backup-as-a-service) guide for more details.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:backup */}

#### Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)

{/* CP-STEPS-START:disaster-recovery-plan */}

The **DRP** tab allows you to build a DRP plan with Zerto: choose the type of plan, **Between two OVH Private Clouds** or **Between your infrastructure and an OVH Private Cloud**, then click <code className="action">Activate Zerto DRP</code>.<br/>
Check out [Setting up Zerto Virtual Replication for your DRP](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/zerto-virtual-replication-vmware-vsphere-drp) for more details.

:::warning
 You need to own a secondary Hosted Private Cloud solution to enable the **Between two OVH Private Clouds** Zerto option.

:::

{/* CP-STEPS-END:disaster-recovery-plan */}
