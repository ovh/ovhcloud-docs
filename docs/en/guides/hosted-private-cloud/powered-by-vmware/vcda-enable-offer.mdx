## Objective

OVHcloud provides a migration option based on **VMware Cloud Director Availability (VCDA)** to move your virtual machines to a Public VCF as-a-Service organisation.

**This guide explains how to enable this option from the OVHcloud Console, then where to manage allowed IP addresses after activation.**

## Requirements

- An active [Public VCF as-a-Service](/links/hosted-private-cloud/vmware-vcd) organisation.
- The public IP address (CIDR `/32`) of the server hosting your on-premises VCDA appliance.

:::info
The IP entered during activation is used to allow your source environment to communicate with the OVHcloud VCDA service.
:::

[[cpnav:privatecloud-vmware-vcf-console|en]]

## Instructions

### Step 1: Open the Public VCF as-a-Service service

{/* CP-STEPS-START:open-public-vcf-service */}

The landing page shows your organisations list. Select the organisation where you want to enable migration.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:open-public-vcf-service */}

### Step 2: Start activation

{/* CP-STEPS-START:start-activation */}

Once inside the organisation, the **General information** tab is displayed. In its **VCDA migration** section, click <code className="action">Order</code>.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:start-activation */}

### Step 3: Fill in the first activation form

{/* CP-STEPS-START:first-activation-form */}

On the **Managed VCDA for Public VCF** page, under **Enter your infrastructure information**, enter the public IP address of your source server in CIDR `/32` format in the **Your Public IP** field, then click <code className="action">Order</code> in the **Summary**.

In the **Confirm your order** step, accept the contracts, then click <code className="action">Confirm and pay</code>. This operation enables the VCDA migration option on the organisation and adds this IP to the allow list.

:::warning
Make sure you enter the actual public egress IP used by your on-premises VCDA appliance. An incorrect IP will prevent pairing with the cloud service.
:::

After validation, a confirmation email is sent to you, with all the information needed to connect your VCDA to ours.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:first-activation-form */}

### Step 4: Use the Migration tab after activation

{/* CP-STEPS-START:migration-tab */}

Once the option is enabled, a <code className="action">Migration</code> tab appears in the organisation interface.

In this tab, the **VCDA IP for Public VCFaaS** section shows the VCDA service IP deployed on your organisation (useful if you need to allow flows on your source side).

{/* CP-STEPS-END:migration-tab */}
