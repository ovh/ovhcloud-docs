## Objective

This guide describes the functionality of the **VRA** view of the **DRP** tab for Zerto on Managed VMware vSphere.

This view allows you to:

- Display all Virtual Replication Appliances (VRAs) present on the installation.
- Select one or more VRAs using checkboxes.
- Apply a target resource configuration via the **Target resources** list.

## Prerequisites

- Have an active Managed VMware vSphere service with Zerto enabled.
- Have access to the **DRP** tab of your datacenter.
- Have the necessary rights to launch a resizing operation.
- Verify that your PCC has sufficient resources (CPU, RAM, storage) to apply configuration changes.

{/* CP-STEPS-START:cp-access */}

:::info
The VRA view is accessible from the data centre's **DRP** tab by clicking <code className="action">VRA</code>.
:::

:::info
The profiles offered in the **Target resources** list form a fixed list: they do not depend on the VRAs selected.
:::

[[cpnav:privatecloud-vmware-vsphere-console|en]]

{/* CP-STEPS-END:cp-access */}

## In practice

### Step 1: View the VRA list

{/* CP-STEPS-START:view-vra-list */}

The VRA view displays a table containing the VRAs present on your installation, with the following columns:

- **Name**: the VRA name.
- **VMId**: the VM identifier.
- **Resources**: the associated resources (for example, 2 vCPU · 6 GiB RAM).

Each row has a checkbox for selection.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:view-vra-list */}

### Step 2: Select the VRAs to resize

{/* CP-STEPS-START:select-vras */}

You can select one or more VRAs at the same time.

As long as no VRA is selected:

- The **Target resources** list is disabled.
- The <code className="action">Start the resizing</code> button is disabled.

When at least one VRA is selected:

- The **Target resources** list becomes available.

The <code className="action">Start the resizing</code> button becomes active only after:

- Selection of at least one VRA.
- Choice of a profile in **Target resources**.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:select-vras */}

### Step 3: Choose the Target resources configuration

{/* CP-STEPS-START:choose-target-resources */}

In the **Target resources** list, choose the destination profile: XS (1 vCPU, 2 GiB RAM), S (2 vCPU, 6 GiB RAM), L (4 vCPU, 12 GiB RAM) or XL (4 vCPU, 16 GiB RAM).

{/* CP-STEPS-END:choose-target-resources */}

### Step 4: Confirm the resize operation

{/* CP-STEPS-START:confirm-resize */}

Click <code className="action">Start the resizing</code> after selecting the VRAs and the target profile.

A confirmation window opens with the title **Confirm the VRA reconfiguration**. This window displays:

- The target profile chosen.
- The list of VRAs that will be reconfigured.
- A warning message indicating that the resizing will affect the replications configured in your VPGs for a few minutes.

Available actions:

- <code className="action">Confirm</code>: starts the reconfiguration of the selected VRAs.
- <code className="action">Cancel</code>: closes the window without applying any changes.

:::warning
Before confirming, verify that your PCC has sufficient resources (CPU, RAM, and storage) to apply the new configuration.
:::

{/* CP-STEPS-END:confirm-resize */}
