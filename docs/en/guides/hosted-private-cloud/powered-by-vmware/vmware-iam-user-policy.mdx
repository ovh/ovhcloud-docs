## Objective

**This guide details how to associate an OVHcloud identity (a user) with a global IAM policy**.

## Requirements

- An [OVHcloud account](/guides/account-and-service-management/account-information/ovhcloud-account-creation).
- One or more Hosted Private Cloud products - VMware on OVHcloud linked to this account (Hosted Private Cloud powered by VMware, VMware Service Pack).
- IAM enabled for your Hosted Private Cloud service - VMware on OVHcloud. Follow the steps in the guide [IAM for VMware on OVHcloud - How to enable IAM](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vmware-iam-activation).

[[cpnav:iam-policies]]

## Instructions

### How do I associate a user with a global IAM policy?

{/* CP-STEPS-START:associate-user-policy */}

<img className="thumbnail" alt="My policies page with the list of policies and the Actions column" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vmware-iam-user-policy/console/iam_policies_resize.png" loading="lazy" />

You will find here the policies you have already created.

Click the <code className="action">⋮</code> button in the **Actions** column, to the right of your policy, then click <code className="action">Edit policy</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Edit policy page with the Identities section and the Summary section on the right" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vmware-iam-user-policy/console/iam_policies_modify.png" loading="lazy" />

Then select the identities you want in the **Identities** section, where each identity type has its own tab: **Users**, **Groups**, **Service accounts** and **Other OVHcloud accounts**. Your local users are listed on the **Users** tab; for user groups, click the <code className="action">Groups</code> tab. Tick the checkbox of each identity you want to add to the policy.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Identities section with the identity type tabs and the list of users with checkboxes" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vmware-iam-user-policy/console/iam_add_user.png" loading="lazy" />

Only IAM Groups (not vSphere IAM Groups) are listed on the **Groups** tab. Make sure you pick the right group from the list: to narrow it down, use the **Search** field above it.

Then, to finish your policy, click <code className="action">Save</code> at the bottom of the form, or <code className="action">Edit policy</code> in the **Summary** section on the right.

:::info
If your identity is not present, you will need to add it first in the OVHcloud Console, on the <ManagerLink to="/beta/#/iam/identities/users">Identities</ManagerLink> page.

:::
