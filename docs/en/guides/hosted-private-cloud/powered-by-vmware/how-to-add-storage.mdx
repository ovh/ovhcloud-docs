## Objective

Within your OVHcloud environment, you can add a new dedicated datastore in just a few minutes.

**Learn how to add storage to your infrastructure.**

## Requirements

- Being an administrative contact of your [Hosted Private Cloud infrastructure](/links/hosted-private-cloud/enterprise) to receive login credentials
- A user account with access to vSphere (created in the <ManagerLink console to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>)

## Instructions

:::warning
Adding resources through vSphere adds them as hourly resources for billing purposes. If you wish to switch them to monthly resources, you can do so in the <ManagerLink console to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>: on the <code className="action">Datastores</code> tab of your datacentre, open the ⋮ menu (**Actions** column) of the resource and click <code className="action">Convert to monthly billing</code>.
For more information, see our guide [Managing your Hosted Private Cloud in the OVHcloud Console](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/manager-ovh-private-cloud).

:::


In the vSphere interface menu, go to the <code className="action">Hosts and Clusters</code> dashboard.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Menu" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/how-to-add-storage/en01dash.png" loading="lazy" />

The <code className="action">Configure</code> tab gives you access to the OVHcloud plugin.<br/>
in the <code className="action">Add Storage</code> menu, choose the type of storage you would like to add and click <code className="action">Next</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Choose" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/how-to-add-storage/en02choose.png" loading="lazy" />

Verify your order and confirm by clicking <code className="action">Next</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Confirm" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/how-to-add-storage/en03validate.png" loading="lazy" />

Your order is confirmed. The progression of the delivery is showing with an estimated time of availability.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Delivery" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/how-to-add-storage/en04deliver.png" loading="lazy" />

Once done, a new datastore is showing in your datacenter and is ready for use.
