## Objective

This guide explains how to add a new ESXi server storage to an existing vSAN cluster.

## Requirements

- Being an administrative contact of your [Hosted Private Cloud infrastructure](/links/hosted-private-cloud/enterprise) to receive login credentials
- A user account with access to vSphere as well as the specific rights for NSX (created in the <ManagerLink console to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>)
- A deployed [vSan Datastore](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vmware-vsan)

[[cpnav:privatecloud-vmware-vsphere-console|en]]

## Instructions

### Order a new vSan host
{/* CP-STEPS-START:order-vsan-host */}

With your administrative credentials, go to the <code className="action">Datacenter</code> tab of your service, click the name of your datacenter and go to the <code className="action">Hosts</code> tab.

Click on the <code className="action">Order a host</code> button.

In the **Order a host** window, select the vSAN host that fits your needs and click <code className="action">Confirm order</code>. The order continues in a new browser tab.

Verify the recap of your order and click <code className="action">Next</code>.

Check the boxes to accept the terms and conditions and go on to the payment. You will get an order confirmation.

The new host will be automatically integrated to your infrastructure and you will receive a confirmation email when it is ready for use in vSphere.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:order-vsan-host */}
### Add new vSan host storage to your datastore

In the vSphere interface menu, go to the <code className="action">Hosts and Clusters</code> dashboard.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Menu" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vmware-vsan-add-host/en07hosts.png" loading="lazy" />

You should see your new host in the cluster.

On the left side, select your cluster, go in the <code className="action">Configure</code> tab then <code className="action">vSAN</code> / <code className="action">Disk Management</code>.

Click on <code className="action">Claim Unused Disks</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="vSAN" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vmware-vsan-add-host/en08cluster.png" loading="lazy" />

Available disks are assigned to cache or capacity (see [Official VMware VSAN page](https://docs.vmware.com/en/VMware-vSphere/6.7/com.vmware.vsphere.vsan-planning.doc/GUID-18F531E9-FF08-49F5-9879-8E46583D4C70.html) for explanation) depending on types and sizes. You can customize it to your need.

Click on <code className="action">Create</code> when ready.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="vSAN" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vmware-vsan-add-host/en09claim.png" loading="lazy" />

The new disk group will take some time to be created and added to the vSAN datastore.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="vSAN" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vmware-vsan-add-host/en10progress.png" loading="lazy" />

Once done, the datastore is ready for use with the added storage.
