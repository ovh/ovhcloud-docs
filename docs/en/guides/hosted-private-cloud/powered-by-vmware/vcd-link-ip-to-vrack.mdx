## Objective

When you order a new Public VCF as-a-Service organization, a vRack and a block of public IP addresses are delivered to you.

This IP block is not directly linked to your vRack. You will need to link it manually.

**Find out how to link the block of public IP addresses delivered with your Public VCF as-a-Service organization and its vRack.**

## Requirements

- A [Public VCF as-a-Service](/links/hosted-private-cloud/vmware-vcd) on OVHcloud solution.
- Being the technical administrator of the managed [VMware vSphere on OVHcloud](/links/hosted-private-cloud/vmware) infrastructure.

[[cpnav:privatecloud-vmware-vcf-console+network-vrack-console]]

## Instructions

{/* CP-STEPS-START:link-ip-block-vrack */}

1. Select your organization, then open the <code className="action">Virtual Datacenters</code> tab.

2. The **vRack** column shows the vRack attached to each Virtual Datacenter of your organization, with an ID in the form `pn-xxxxxxx`. It also appears in the <code className="action">General information</code> tab of the Virtual Datacenter.

3. Expand the <code className="action">Network</code> menu in the left-hand column, select <code className="action">vRack</code>, then your vRack `pn-xxxxxxx`.

4. Open the <code className="action">Attached services</code> tab and click <code className="action">Attach a service</code>. On the **Eligible services** page, set the **Service type** filter to **Additional IPv4 blocks**, then click <code className="action">Attach</code> on the row of the IP block to link to your vRack/organization.

5. The public IP block is now linked to your vRack.

    To make these addresses usable inside your Public VCF as-a-Service environment, you also need to declare the public gateway in VMware Cloud Director (VCD).

    Follow the guide [Public VCF as-a-Service - Declare the public IP gateway in VCD](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcd-declare-public-gateway) to complete this step.
