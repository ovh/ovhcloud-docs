## Objective

OVHcloud offers a VM migration feature to Public VCF as-a-Service based on **VMware Cloud Director Availability (VCDA)**. This feature allows you to migrate your VMs from a vSphere or VCD on-premises environment to your Public VCF as-a-Service organisation.

**This guide walks you through your first migration: data replication, monitoring, and the final cutover (migration) to your Public VCF as-a-Service organisation.**

## Requirements

- An active [Public VCF as-a-Service](/links/hosted-private-cloud/vmware-vcd) organisation.
- The migration option enabled on your organisation from your <ManagerLink to="/">OVHcloud Control Panel</ManagerLink>. If needed, refer to [Enable the VCDA migration option on your Public VCF as-a-Service organisation](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcda-enable-offer).
- A **VCDA on-premises** appliance deployed, configured and paired with your Public VCF as-a-Service organisation. If this is not yet the case, refer to the [Public VCF as-a-Service Migration with VCDA - Getting Started](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcda-getting-started) guide.
- A user account on your Public VCF as-a-Service organisation with enough rights to manage replications.

## Instructions

### Workflow overview

The migration workflow has 3 main steps:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="VCDA migration workflow: start the replication, wait for it to reach the Healthy state, then trigger the final migration (cutover)" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcda-trigger-migration/workflow-en.png" loading="lazy" />

You can drive the operations from two interfaces:

- **Your on-premises VCDA appliance UI**, for source-side operations (recommended for this guide);
- **The VMware Cloud Director UI** of your Public VCF as-a-Service organisation, through the **Availability plugin**.

#### Accessing the Availability plugin in VMware Cloud Director

From your Public VCF as-a-Service organisation, open the VCD portal and click <code className="action">More</code> > <code className="action">Availability</code> to access the VCDA plugin:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Availability plugin in VMware Cloud Director" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcda-trigger-migration/01-vcd-availability-plugin.png" loading="lazy" />

The plugin shows the status of inbound and outbound replications for your organisation.

### Step 1: Start the replication

In this example, the migration is triggered **from** the on-premises VCDA appliance **to** the Public VCF as-a-Service organisation.

Log in to your on-premises VCDA appliance interface (`https://<appliance-IP>`) with the `root` account.

In the left menu, click <code className="action">Outgoing Replications</code>, then the <code className="action">vApp</code> tab.

Click the <code className="action">New migration</code> button (cloud icon with an upward arrow) to start the wizard.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="New migration button" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcda-trigger-migration/02-new-migration-button.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Authentication to the target organisation

The wizard prompts you to authenticate against the Public VCF as-a-Service organisation that will be the migration target.

Enter:

- **Username**: your username on the Public VCF as-a-Service organisation (in the `user@organization` format);
- **Password**: the associated password.

Click <code className="action">LOGIN</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Authentication in the migration wizard" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcda-trigger-migration/03-wizard-auth.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Step 1.1: Selecting source VMs

Select the source vCenter from the <code className="action">Select VMs to replicate from</code> dropdown, then tick the virtual machine(s) you want to migrate.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Source VM selection" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcda-trigger-migration/04-source-vm-selection.png" loading="lazy" />

Click <code className="action">Next</code>.

#### Step 1.2: Destination vDC and storage policy

Select the target **virtual datacenter (vDC)** in your Public VCF as-a-Service organisation and the **storage policy** to use for the migrated VMs.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="vDC and storage policy selection" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcda-trigger-migration/05-destination-vdc.png" loading="lazy" />

Click <code className="action">Next</code>.

#### Step 1.3: Replication settings

This step allows you to adjust the replication settings:

- **Compress replication traffic**: enable it only on limited bandwidth links (increased CPU usage);
- **Delay start synchronization**: to postpone the start of the initial synchronisation;
- **VDC policy settings**: placement and sizing policies to apply on the target (leave <code className="action">None</code> to keep the original configuration).

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Replication settings" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcda-trigger-migration/06-settings.png" loading="lazy" />

Click <code className="action">Next</code>.

#### Step 1.4: Summary

The wizard displays a summary of the upcoming migration. Check:

- the selected VMs;
- the source and destination sites;
- the vDC and storage policy;
- the sizing and placement policies.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Migration summary" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcda-trigger-migration/07-ready-to-complete.png" loading="lazy" />

Click <code className="action">Finish</code> to start the replication.

### Step 2: Monitor the replication progress

Once the replication is started, it appears in the <code className="action">Outgoing Replications</code> list with the **Synchronizing** state.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Replication in progress" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcda-trigger-migration/08-replication-in-progress.png" loading="lazy" />

The replication details show:

- the checksum progress (block verification);
- the transferred bytes;
- the status of the source-side and target-side components.

:::warning
Wait until the replication reaches the **Healthy** state before triggering the final migration. The initial replication time depends on disk sizes and available bandwidth.
:::

When the replication is ready to be cut over, the state becomes **Healthy** and the **Recovery state** shows **Not started**:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Replication ready" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcda-trigger-migration/09-replication-ready.png" loading="lazy" />

### Step 3: Trigger the migration

Once the replication is in the **Healthy** state, you can trigger the cutover.

Select the replication in the list, then click <code className="action">ALL ACTIONS</code> > <code className="action">Migrate</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Migrate menu" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcda-trigger-migration/10-migrate-menu.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Step 3.1: Migration settings

Configure the cutover parameters:

- **Power settings**: tick <code className="action">Power on recovered vApps</code> to automatically start the VMs on the target side after the cutover;
- **Network Settings**:
    - <code className="action">Apply preconfigured network settings on migrate</code>: applies the network settings defined in the recovery profile;
    - <code className="action">Connect all VMs to network</code>: simply reconnects the VMs to the chosen network;
- **VDC policy settings**: placement, sizing and storage policies to use on the target.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Migration settings" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcda-trigger-migration/11-migrate-settings.png" loading="lazy" />

Click <code className="action">Next</code>.

#### Step 3.2: Confirmation

Check the summary:

- the vApps to migrate;
- the recovery site;
- the network settings;
- the storage policy.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Migration confirmation" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcda-trigger-migration/12-migrate-confirm.png" loading="lazy" />

:::info
The migration workflow automatically performs the following steps:

1. Final synchronisation (if the source VM is powered on);
2. Shutdown of the source VM;
3. Final synchronisation (once the source VM is off);
4. Import of the VM into the target site.

:::

Click <code className="action">Finish</code> to start the migration.

#### Monitoring the migration

The cutover appears in the replication list with the **Migrate** state and a progress percentage:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Migration in progress" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcda-trigger-migration/13-migration-in-progress.png" loading="lazy" />

#### Migration completed

Once the migration is finalised, the VM appears in your Public VCF as-a-Service organisation and the replication state changes to **Failed-Over** in the **Incoming Replications** view of the VCD portal:

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Migration completed" src="/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/vcda-trigger-migration/14-migration-completed.png" loading="lazy" />

You can now find your migrated VMs directly in your Public VCF as-a-Service vDC, ready to use.
