## Objective

This documentation aims to explain the configuration options for NSX Edges and the steps to manage them within a VMware on OVHcloud environment. It also outlines technical limitations and provides guidance on how to adapt these configurations to specific needs.

## Requirements

- Use of **VMware NSX 4.1.1** to access customization options.
- Get a basic understanding of VMware concepts and NSX Edge features.

## General Overview

### Default Configuration

When creating a VMware environment, OVHcloud automatically provides:

- **2 Medium NSX Edges**:
    - 4 vCPU.
    - 8 GB RAM.

This default configuration meets most standard requirements for connectivity and network security.

### Customization

You can adapt your infrastructure to meet your specific needs:

1. **NSX Edge Sizes**:
    - Medium: 4 vCPU, 8 GB RAM.
    - Large: 8 vCPU, 32 GB RAM.
    - XL: 16 vCPU, 64 GB RAM.
2. **Number of NSX Edges**:
    - Minimum: 2 (default configuration).
    - Maximum: Up to **10 NSX Edges per cluster**.

### Limitations

- **1 NSX Edge cluster per vDC**.
- **10 NSX Edges maximum per cluster** (Broadcom/VMware limitation).
- NSX version 4.0.1 does not support:
    - Ordering new vDCs under NSX 4.1.1.
    - Modifying or adding NSX Edges.

## Instructions

### Steps to customize NSX Edges
{/* CP-STEPS-START:customise-nsx-edges */}

From the <code className="action">Datacenter</code> tab of your service, click the name of your datacenter, then click the <code className="action">NSX Edge Nodes</code> tab. The Edges and the actions below are displayed once the NSX Edge option is enabled on your datacenter; to enable it, contact the OVHcloud support team.

1. **Order new NSX Edges**:

    Click <code className="action">Add an NSX Edge</code>.

    In the **Add NSX Edge nodes** window, select the size and click <code className="action">Order</code>.

    :::info
    All Edges must be the same size: when adding a new Edge, it will automatically match the size of existing Edges. The window therefore only offers the current size of your Edges.

    :::


2. **Modify the size of existing NSX Edges**:

    Click <code className="action">Resize</code>.

    The new size applies to all the NSX Edges of the datacenter. In the **Resize NSX Edge nodes** window, choose a new size (Medium, Large or Extra large; your current size is marked **Current level**) and apply the changes by clicking <code className="action">Confirm</code>.

3. **Remove unused NSX Edges**:

    Deleting an NSX Edge is not yet available in the OVHcloud Console. To remove an unused Edge, contact the OVHcloud support team.

    :::info
    The Edge must be in resilience mode before deletion to ensure no ongoing traffic is affected.

    :::

{/* CP-STEPS-END:customise-nsx-edges */}
