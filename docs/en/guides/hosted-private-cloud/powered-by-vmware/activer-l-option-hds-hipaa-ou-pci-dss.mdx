## Objective

To configure your Hosted Private Cloud for [healthcare data hosting](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/enterprise/products/hosted-private-cloud/safety-compliance/hds/) (HDS certification in France as well as regulatory compliance for healthcare data hosting in Italy, United Kingdom, Germany and Poland) or [financial data hosting](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-gb/enterprise/solutions/certified-cloud-solutions/financial-data-hosting-pci-dss/) with PCI DSS certification, you have to follow the technical activation process below.

**Find out how to host healthcare or payment card data on your Hosted Private Cloud.**

## Requirements

:::info
These options can be activated as part of the hosting of healthcare data or financial data. Currently, a Private Cloud cannot be both PCI DSS and HDS at the same time.

:::


- a Private Cloud infrastructure in version 6.0 or higher

[[cpnav:privatecloud-vmware-vsphere-console]]

## Instructions

### Ensure that the security option is enabled

{/* CP-STEPS-START:enable-security-option */}

To adapt your Private Cloud for this certification, one of the corresponding security options must be enabled. To verify this, go to **Private Cloud** › **Managed VMware vSphere** in the OVHcloud Console and select the service concerned in the list.

The service opens on its <code className="action">Dashboard</code> tab. Check the activation status of the security options in the **Compliance** section, on the **Sector-specific compliance** row.

If the desired security option is not enabled, click <code className="action">Enable sector-specific compliance</code> on this row to open the activation wizard. Several requirements are essential.

- **[NSX](/links/hosted-private-cloud/vmware-nsx) and [vROps](/links/hosted-private-cloud/vmware-vrops) options must be installed**: on the <code className="action">Dashboard</code> tab, in the **Options** section, you can check the activation status of these options on the **Network and security virtualisation (NSX-T)** and **vRealize Operations (vROps)** rows. If vROps is not enabled, click <code className="action">Enable options</code> on its row.

- **The vCenter access policy must be restricted**: from the <code className="action">Access control</code> tab, you can check the status of the access policy in the **Session settings** section, on the **vCenter access policy** row. The policy is displayed there but cannot be changed from the OVHcloud Console: if it is not restricted, contact our support teams to have it changed. Our documentation "[Managing your Hosted Private Cloud in the OVHcloud Console](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/manager-ovh-private-cloud)" can assist you in this process.

- **You must have at least one IP address authorised to log in to vCenter**: from the <code className="action">Access control</code> tab, make sure you have at least one authorised IP address in the list of IPs and blocks authorised to connect. If necessary, use the <code className="action">Associate an IP block</code> button. Our documentation "[Managing your Hosted Private Cloud in the OVHcloud Console](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/manager-ovh-private-cloud)" can assist you in this process.

To make sure you can always log in, we recommend that you have at least two authorised IP addresses. For reasons of accessibility, the latter must be fixed and not dynamic.

- **The "admin" user information is complete and they have the necessary permission**: from the <code className="action">Users</code> tab, make sure for the "admin" user that the telephone number and email address are correctly filled in and that they have the "**token validator**" permission (**Token validator** column). If necessary, open the ⋮ menu (**Actions** column) of the user, then click <code className="action">Edit</code> to edit the user: the **Edit** window shows the **Email** and **Telephone number** fields and the **Token validator** permission. Our documentation "[Managing your Hosted Private Cloud in the OVHcloud Console](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/manager-ovh-private-cloud)" can assist you in this process.

To make sure you can always log in to vCenter, we recommend that you have at least two users with the necessary information and permissions (with different email addresses and telephone number).

The activation wizard has four steps:

1. The first step lets you choose the certification to enable on your service (for example **HDS (Healthcare Data) Certification** or **Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) Certification**). Select it, then click <code className="action">Next</code>.
2. The second step ensures that the vCenter access policy is restricted and that you have at least one IP that can connect to the vCenter. It displays the access policy and the number of authorised IPs and blocks.
3. The next step ensures that the necessary information of (at least) the admin user is filled in: at least one of your admin users must have a mobile number, an email address, and be registered as a "token validator". If needed, the step links to the <code className="action">Users</code> tab so that you can configure your users.
4. The last step summarises your order. Click <code className="action">Place order</code> to confirm it.

Once the activation steps have been completed, you will need to:

- validate the token sent by SMS to users with the "**token validator**" permission. This confirms that you will be able to receive these tokens, which are essential for validating operations
- complete the documents you will receive by email to finalise the contractual part

{/* CP-STEPS-END:enable-security-option */}

In the meantime, we advise you to take your first steps with the secure interface by continuing reading this documentation. 

:::info
Consider that the vSphere interface will not be accessible during activation of the security option.

:::


### Start with the secure interface

After the security option activation, you will receive the token validation process by email. It details, among other things, how they work and what you need to do to be able to use them. 

As a security measure following the security option activation:

- all existing users on your Private Cloud are disabled
- you must change your users' passwords to reactivate them
- changing your users' passwords must now be done from the secure interface. You will no longer be able to perform this manipulation from the OVHcloud Console. 

As a reminder, access to the interface will only be possible once the security option has been activated.

Then log in to the secure interface via the link provided in the email you received. This should look like `https://pcc-xxx-xxx-xxx-xxx.ovh.com/secure/password-lost`. Once logged in, you will be able to change the password of the "admin" user and then the password of additional users.

For further details, please refer to our guide on [Using the secure interface](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/interface-secure).
