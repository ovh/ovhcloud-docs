## Objective

**This page answers the most common questions about Encryption at Rest on OVHcloud dedicated servers.**

:::info
The encryption keys are generated, stored and rotated by OVHcloud. Using your own keys is not available. For more information, read [What is Encryption at Rest on a dedicated server?](./encryption-at-rest-overview)
:::

## General questions

### Who holds the encryption keys?

OVHcloud. The keys are generated, stored and rotated by OVHcloud, and they are never sent to you. Using your own keys (Key Management Service, Secret Manager or BYOK) is not available.

### Will OVHcloud give me the passphrase or the PSID of my disks?

No. OVHcloud never asks for these secrets and never gives them out. Never include them in a support request.

### Do I need to do anything when the server reboots?

No. The disks are unlocked automatically at each boot, before the operating system starts.

### Are my backups still compatible?

Yes. Your backup tools (Veeam, Restic, etc.) read your data as the operating system sees it, once the disks are unlocked.

### Can I encrypt only some of the disks?

No. Encryption at Rest applies to all the disks of the server, or to none.

### Can I enable Encryption at Rest again after disabling it?

Yes. As with the first activation, enabling it erases all the data on the server and requires an OS reinstall.

### Does a disk replacement take longer?

No. A disk replacement takes the same time as on a server without Encryption at Rest.

## Troubleshooting

### My server reboots in a loop after enabling Encryption at Rest, or after a reboot.

If unlocking the disks fails, the boot sequence starts again on its own. **Stop rebooting the server:** repeated attempts can lock the disks out. Then contact support and send the information listed below.

### What should I send to support?

If you have access to the KVM or IPMI console (see [How to use the IPMI console with a dedicated server](./ipmi)), send a capture of the boot console showing:

- The banner displayed by the unlocking agent.
- Every `Result: FAILED (...)` line.
- The `Workflow Complete` block (result and number of disks).
- The line with the workflow ID.

Also say since when the problem has occurred and after which event (reboot, disk replacement, enabling request).

### Can I use rescue mode?

Yes, the Linux rescue mode. The disks are unlocked automatically, as at a normal boot. If unlocking fails, the error is shown in the login banner of the rescue session: report it to support. The Windows rescue mode is not compatible with Encryption at Rest.

### The Control Panel shows the status "Error".

The last operation did not succeed and the server keeps its previous state. Try again. If the error persists, contact support.
