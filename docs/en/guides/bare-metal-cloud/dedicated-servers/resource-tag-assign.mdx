## Objective

Tags are labels that can be attributed to your resources, allowing you to organize and manage them more efficiently.

Each tag consists of two parts:

- **Key**: Represents an attribute or category.
- **Value**: Corresponds to the information associated with that key.

For example, you can categorize your resources by site, service, or even security level. Using tags can make it easier to search, organize your resources, manage associated costs, and apply policies with the desired granularity.

**This guide explains how to create, assign and delete tags for each dedicated server from the OVHcloud Console.**

## Requirements

- A [dedicated server](/links/bare-metal/bare-metal) in your OVHcloud account.

[[cpnav:baremetal-dedicated-servers|en]]

## Instructions

### Assign a tag to a dedicated server via the OVHcloud Console



{/* CP-STEPS-START:assign-tag */}

On the **Overview** tab of your server, the **Tags** section lists the tags already assigned. Click <code className="action">Manage my tags</code>.

You are automatically pointed to the **Tags** section of the <code className="action">Server</code> tab. You can also open the <code className="action">Server</code> tab directly and go to its **Tags** section.

Click the <code className="action">Assign a tag</code> button.

In the **Assign a tag** panel that opens, click the **Select or type a key** field to open the drop-down list, then select the key you want.

Next, click the **Select or type a value** field and select the appropriate value from the drop-down list.

:::warning
**If you want to use a key or value that does not yet exist**, you can create it by entering it in the field, then clicking <code className="action">Add your-value</code>, where "your-value" corresponds to the text you have entered.

:::

To assign several tags at once, click <code className="action">Add another key/value</code> and fill in the new pair.

Finally, click the <code className="action">Assign</code> button at the bottom of the panel.

You will receive a confirmation message, then see the list of tags applied to the chosen server in the **Tags** section.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:assign-tag */}

### Delete a tag on a dedicated server

{/* CP-STEPS-START:delete-tag */}

To find the list of tags assigned to your server:

1. Click <code className="action">Dedicated servers</code> and select your server from the list.
1. Go to the <code className="action">Server</code> tab, then to its **Tags** section.

Tick the checkbox of the tag you want to remove from your server. Only tags whose **Tag type** is **Custom tag** can be selected.
Then click the <code className="action">Actions</code> button above the table and select <code className="action">Unassign</code>.

The **Unassign tags** window will appear. Click the <code className="action">Unassign</code> button to unassign the tag.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:delete-tag */}
