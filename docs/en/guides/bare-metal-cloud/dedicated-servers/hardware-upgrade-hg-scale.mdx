## Objective

Our High Grade and Scale servers offer a scalable option that allows you increase your disk space and RAM.

**The purpose of this guide is to walk you through the process of requesting a hardware upgrade on OVHcloud High Grade and SCALE dedicated servers.**

:::info
The steps in this guide only apply to our new ranges (Advance, High Grade and Scale). For older ranges, the request must be made via a support ticket.

:::


## Requirements

- An [Advance](/links/bare-metal/advance) (excluding models delivered before May 2025), [High Grade](/links/bare-metal/hg) or [SCALE](/links/bare-metal/scale) server

[[cpnav:baremetal-dedicated-servers|en]]

## Instructions

### Upgrade your RAM

{/* CP-STEPS-START:upgrade-ram */}

On your server's page, click the <code className="action">Server</code> tab. In the **Hardware** section, click <code className="action">Increase RAM</code> on the **Memory** row.

In the **Upgrade your RAM** window, select the desired RAM option under **Select a RAM option** and click <code className="action">Order</code>. The order opens in a new browser tab, where you can complete the payment.

Once the order is paid, a confirmation email will be sent to your account with a link to schedule the intervention for the RAM upgrade.

Follow the link provided in the email, or go back to the **Server** tab of your server. In the **Hardware** section, click <code className="action">Schedule your intervention</code>.

In the **Intervention details** window, select the available date and time slot. Under **Have you backed up your data?**, tick the box <code className="action">Yes, I confirm that I have made a full backup of my data, or I know that I do not want to back up my data.</code> Please note that our data center technicians require a preparation period, so the first date of availability is set after a minimum period of 10 days.

Next, click <code className="action">Confirm</code>.

Once done, you will receive an email with the date and time of the intervention.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:upgrade-ram */}

### Upgrade Storage

{/* CP-STEPS-START:upgrade-storage */}

On your server's page, click the <code className="action">Server</code> tab. In the **Hardware** section, click <code className="action">Increase storage</code> on the **Data disks** row.

In the **Upgrade your storage** window, select the desired storage option under **Select a storage option** and click <code className="action">Order</code>. The order opens in a new browser tab, where you can complete the payment.

Once the order is paid, a confirmation email will be sent to your account with a link to schedule the intervention for the hardware upgrade.

Follow the link provided in the email, or go back to the **Server** tab of your server. In the **Hardware** section, click <code className="action">Schedule your intervention</code>.

In the **Intervention details** window, select the available date and time slot. Under **Have you backed up your data?**, tick the box <code className="action">Yes, I confirm that I have made a full backup of my data, or I know that I do not want to back up my data.</code> Please note that our data center technicians require a preparation period, so the first date of availability is set after a minimum period of 10 days.

Next, click <code className="action">Confirm</code>.

Once done, you will receive an email with the date and time of the intervention.

{/* CP-STEPS-END:upgrade-storage */}

If you would like to schedule an upgrade in RAM and storage during the same intervention, you will need to order and pay for each option one after the other within 1 hour.
