## Objective

Our VPS services offer flexibility, reliability, and performance for a variety of hosting needs. You can proceed with upgrading your RAM, vCPU, or storage in the <ManagerLink console to="/">OVHcloud Console</ManagerLink>.

**Learn how to add vCores, memory, and storage to your VPS service.** 

## Requirements

- A [Virtual Private Server](/links/bare-metal/vps) in your OVHcloud account

[[cpnav:baremetal-vps-console]]

## Instructions

{/* CP-STEPS-START:cp-access */}

:::info
The upgrade options available in the OVHcloud Console depend on the range and model of the selected VPS: only ranges and configurations higher than the current one are offered.

:::


On the **Overview** tab, the **Your configuration** section shows the model of your VPS and its resources: processor, memory and primary disk. These resources are not upgraded one by one. vCores, memory and storage change together when you move your VPS to a higher range, using the <code className="action">Upgrade</code> link under the model.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Your configuration section of the VPS Overview tab, with the Upgrade link under the model" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/virtual-private-servers/upgrade-resources/console/vps-overview-your-configuration.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:cp-access */}

### 1. To add **vCores**

{/* CP-STEPS-START:add-vcores */}

On the **Overview** tab, in the **Your configuration** section, click <code className="action">Upgrade</code> under the model of your VPS. The **Upgrade** page opens: the choice of range, the choice of configuration and the order summary are all on this one page.

In the **Model choice** section, select a higher range. Your current range is marked **Current model**.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Model choice section of the VPS Upgrade page, listing the higher ranges" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/virtual-private-servers/upgrade-resources/console/vps-upgrade-model-choice.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Configuration choice** section, select the configuration you want within that range. Each configuration sets the vCores, memory and storage together.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Configuration choice section of the VPS Upgrade page" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/virtual-private-servers/upgrade-resources/console/vps-upgrade-configuration-choice.png" loading="lazy" />

In the **Upgrading** section, tick the **Acceptance of the terms and conditions of use** box.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Upgrading section of the VPS Upgrade page with the terms and conditions box ticked" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/virtual-private-servers/upgrade-resources/console/vps-upgrade-terms.png" loading="lazy" />

:::warning
The VPS reboots once during the migration, which causes around ten minutes of downtime. A model change cannot be undone.

:::

Review your changes in the **Upgrading** section: the new range and its resources, the date the upgrade applies from, and the amount **Due today**, billed pro rata for the remainder of the current month. Then click <code className="action">Confirm the upgrade</code>.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Upgrading summary of the VPS Upgrade page with the Confirm the upgrade button" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/virtual-private-servers/upgrade-resources/console/vps-upgrade-confirm.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:add-vcores */}

### 2. To upgrade **Memory**

{/* CP-STEPS-START:upgrade-memory */}

In the OVHcloud Console, memory is not upgraded on its own: it changes together with the vCores and storage when you move your VPS to a higher range and configuration. The options available depend on the VPS range you currently have, as only higher ranges and configurations are offered.

To get more memory, on the **Overview** tab, in the **Your configuration** section, click <code className="action">Upgrade</code>, then choose a higher range and a configuration with the memory you need, as described above for vCores.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Configuration choice section of the VPS Upgrade page, with the Memory column highlighted" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/virtual-private-servers/upgrade-resources/console/vps-upgrade-memory-column.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:upgrade-memory */}

### 3. To upgrade **Storage**

{/* CP-STEPS-START:upgrade-storage */}

In the OVHcloud Console, storage is not upgraded on its own: it changes together with the vCores and memory when you move your VPS to a higher range and configuration. The options available depend on the VPS range you currently have, as only higher ranges and configurations are offered.

To get more storage, on the **Overview** tab, in the **Your configuration** section, click <code className="action">Upgrade</code>, then choose a higher range and a configuration with the storage you need, as described above for vCores.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Configuration choice section of the VPS Upgrade page, with the Storage column highlighted" src="/images/bare-metal-cloud/virtual-private-servers/upgrade-resources/console/vps-upgrade-storage-column.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:upgrade-storage */}

## FAQ

<details>

<summary>Will I have the same IP address?</summary>


Yes, you will keep the same IP address after upgrading your VPS.

</details>


<details>

<summary>Do I keep my data on the server?</summary>


Yes, after an upgrade you will still have your data. When upgrading your drive you may have to expand the partitions.

</details>


<details>

<summary>What happens to the backup/snapshot? Can I use it on the new VPS?</summary>


Yes. After upgrades you will still have access to your backups and snapshots.

</details>


<details>

<summary>What happens to the software license on the old VPS? Can they be automatically moved over to the new VPS?</summary>


If you have an active license, it will remain attached to the VPS. The pricing may change based on the license agreement or requirements with the provider.  
If there are any changes to the license, they will be explained before upgrading the VPS.

</details>


<details>

<summary>Would the bandwidth change?</summary>


In some cases the bandwidth may change, specifically when moving from a lower‑tier VPS to the next tier.

</details>


<details>

<summary>Would this upgrade be immediate, or would I have time to use both at the same time (configure, transfer data, etc.)?</summary>


The upgrade keeps all of your data. The VPS reboots once during the migration, which causes around ten minutes of downtime. Upgrading will allocate more resources to your existing VPS.

</details>
