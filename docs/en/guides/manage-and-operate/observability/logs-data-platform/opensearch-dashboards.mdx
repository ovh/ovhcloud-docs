## Objective

This guide will help you make full use of [OpenSearch Dashboards](https://opensearch.org/docs/latest/dashboards/index/) and build dashboards from your logs.

## Requirements

This is what you need to know to get you started:

- You are already sending logs on a stream you own: [see the quick start tutorial](/guides/manage-and-operate/observability/logs-data-platform/getting-started-quick-start)
- There is nothing else

After some training you will be able to do this kind of Dashboard:

## Instructions

### Create your Stream Alias

To access your logs from OpenSearch Dashboards, you will need to setup an OpenSearch Alias and link them to your Graylog streams, so here we go:

{/* CP-STEPS-START:create-your-stream-alias */}

1. In the OVHcloud Console, open the **Observability** section, go to the **Logs Management** page, choose your Logs Data Platform service in the **Service** list, then click the <code className="action">OpenSearch aliases</code> tab and the <code className="action">Add an alias</code> button
2. Enter a suffix for your alias (the platform adds a prefix to it) and define a description
3. Save the entry by clicking the <code className="action">Add</code> button
4. Once the alias has been created, use the `⋮` button to the right of the alias and select the <code className="action">Attach content</code> option.
5. In the **data streams** tab, click each Graylog stream you want to associate to your alias, then click <code className="action">Confirm</code>. An alias groups either data streams or OpenSearch indexes, never both.
6. The alias is now ready to use.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Attach content window with the data streams and OpenSearch indexes tabs and the Available and Attached lists" src="/images/manage-and-operate/observability/logs-data-platform/visualization-opensearch-dashboards/console/os-attach-content.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:create-your-stream-alias */}

So now Logs Data Platform knows which stream you want to browse. Now let's configure OpenSearch Dashboards and see if it works!

### Access your OpenSearch Dashboards instance

{/* CP-STEPS-START:create-and-access-osd-instance */}

To create your OpenSearch Dashboards instance, open the **Dashboards** page of the **Observability** section, click the <code className="action">OpenSearch Dashboards instances</code> tab, then click <code className="action">Add an OpenSearch Dashboard</code>. Enter a description and click <code className="action">Order</code> to launch the creation of your instance. The panel shows the monthly price of an instance. Your instance will be created and delivered in approximately 20 minutes.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="Add an OpenSearch Dashboard panel with the Description field, the monthly price and the Order button" src="/images/manage-and-operate/observability/logs-data-platform/visualization-opensearch-dashboards/console/osd-add-instance.png" loading="lazy" />

Use the `⋮` button to the right of your instance and select the <code className="action">Access your OpenSearch Dashboards instance</code> option to be redirected.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="OpenSearch Dashboards instances tab with the actions menu of an instance" src="/images/manage-and-operate/observability/logs-data-platform/visualization-opensearch-dashboards/console/osd-instances-menu.png" loading="lazy" />

{/* CP-STEPS-END:create-and-access-osd-instance */}

Once there, use the right Identity provider to sign in and set on the first page. Click on the <code className="action">Add data</code> button to add sample data you can play with. However if you want to use your own data, you must configure **Index Patterns** in the stack management page. This page is accessible by clicking on the <code className="action">Manage</code> button next to the "Add data" button. You can also use the "Stack Management" option in the left side menu, displayable by using the top left the sandwich button.

In this configuration page, you can configure as the Index name, the full name of your alias **ldp-XXXXX-a-XXXXX**, but you can also use wildcards. Choose **timestamp** for the time field name then click on Discover tab to read your log entries.

<img className="thumbnail" alt="osd setup" src="/images/manage-and-operate/observability/logs-data-platform/visualization-opensearch-dashboards/osd_setup.png" loading="lazy" />

You can also explore any [OpenSearch index](/guides/manage-and-operate/observability/logs-data-platform/index-as-a-service) you created on the platform. One OpenSearch Dashboards instance allows you to explore all the data you delivered on Logs Data Platform.

To know what you can do with OpenSearch Dashboards, read the [OpenSearch Dashboards documentation](https://opensearch.org/docs/latest/dashboards/index/)
