## Objective

The RACI below details shared responsibilities between OVHcloud and the customer for the Cloud HSM service.

Cloud HSM is a managed key custody service. Your keys are stored in partitions dedicated to you on HSM hardware shared between several customers. Each Cloud HSM cluster is made up of exactly three partitions, located on three different physical HSMs according to a layout predefined by OVHcloud for the region you choose. You access the service through public endpoints, with mutual TLS (mTLS) authentication and OVHcloud IAM authorisation, using the OVHcloud PKCS#11 driver.

OVHcloud operates the HSM hardware, maintains the service and replicates your keys between the partitions of your cluster. You manage your keys, the access rights to your clusters and the integration of your applications, including switching to another endpoint if one becomes unavailable.

:::info
Keys created in Cloud HSM never leave the HSM in plaintext. Key wrapping and key export are not available: you cannot back up, export or recover your keys outside the service. You can, however, import existing keys using a wrapped key import.
:::

### 1. Before subscription

#### 1.1. Specify service as needed

| **Activity** | **Customer** | **OVHcloud** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Provide the personal data required to subscribe to the service | RA | I |
| Choose the primary region of the cluster among the regions equipped with HSM hardware | RA | I |
| Choose the number of clusters | RA | I |
| Define the partition layout for each region (three availability zones, or one availability zone and a remote location) | I | RA |
| Publish the service documentation and compliance evidence (custody model, tenancy model, vendor certifications) | I | RA |
