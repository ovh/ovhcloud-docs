## Objective

The RACI below details shared responsibilities between OVHcloud and the customer for the Dedicated HSM service.

Dedicated HSM provides one or more whole physical HSM appliances (Thales Luna or ProvenRun ProvenHSM, as you prefer), dedicated to you and hosted in OVHcloud datacentres. OVHcloud owns the appliance and is responsible for its physical hosting, power and network connectivity. After handover, you have exclusive administrative custody of the appliance: you administer it with the vendor tools and procedures, exactly as you would an on-premises HSM. OVHcloud never accesses your HSM administration credentials, security configuration or key material.

:::info
The OVHcloud SLA for Dedicated HSM covers the physical hosting, network and power provided by OVHcloud. It does not cover the cryptographic availability of the HSM, the vendor firmware, or the configuration and administration performed by the customer.

OVHcloud does not back up the keys or content stored on the appliance, and Dedicated HSM is not integrated with OVHcloud IAM, Logs Data Platform or OVHcloud KMS.
:::

| Choose the region of the service among the regions equipped with HSM hardware | RA | I |
