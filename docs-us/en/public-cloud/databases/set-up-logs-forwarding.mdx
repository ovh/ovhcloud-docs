# Databases & Analytics - How to set up logs forwarding

Cloud Databases and Analytics allow you to send logs of your service to your own Logs Data Platform (LDP) data stream.

 

## Requirements

- Access to the [OVHcloud Console](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/) and [OVHcloud API](https://api.us.ovhcloud.com/)

- A [Cloud Database service](https://us.ovhcloud.com/public-cloud/databases/) up and running

  :::info

  We will use Valkey as the example in this guide.

  :::

- A [Logs Data Platform account](/manage-and-operate/logs-data-platform/getting-started) within this OVHcloud account with at least one destination stream configured

  :::warning

  **NOTE:** If you are not familiar with all the LDP *Stream* configuration possibilities, create a new one with the default options (indexing and websocket enabled, long-term storage disabled) for this guide.

  :::

 

## Instructions

### Create your subscription

<Tabs>

<Tab label="Via OVHcloud Console">

#### Logs direct access

Log in to the [OVHcloud Console](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/#/public-cloud/project) and select your Public Cloud project. In the left-hand menu, click your engine under `Databases`, then click the name of the service you want to manage.

On your database service page, open the `Observability` tab, then the **Logs** view.

{/* cp-shot: ![Manager database tab](/images/public-cloud/databases/set-up-logs-forwarding/manager-database-tab.png) */}

 

#### Logs access through LDP

From the **Logs** view, click `Manage streaming`. If your service exposes several log kinds, first select the kind to forward in the list of the **Logs** view: the panel manages one log kind at a time.

In the **Manage log streaming** panel, under **Subscribe to a stream**, select your **LDP account** and the **Stream**, then click `Subscribe`.

The subscription is listed under **Current subscriptions**. If your service exposes several log kinds, repeat the operation for each kind you want to forward.

Once the subscription is enabled, all the logs will be pushed to [Logs Data Platform](https://us.ovhcloud.com/identity-security-operations/logs-data-platform/) to archive generated logs and perform advanced searches, and create alerts or visualizations.

For more information, please refer to our [Quick Start for Logs Data Platform](/manage-and-operate/logs-data-platform/getting-started) guide.

{/* cp-shot: ![Manager database tab subscribe](/images/public-cloud/databases/set-up-logs-forwarding/manager-database-tab-subscribe.png) */}

</Tab>

<Tab label="Via OVHcloud API">

<details>
<summary>Retrieve the required information</summary>

Retrieve your LDP destination `streamId`:

- In the [OVHcloud Console](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/), open `Observability` > `Logs Management` in the left-hand menu, select your Logs Data Platform account, then open the `Data streams` tab.
- Click the name of your target stream. The stream ID is the identifier that follows `/streams/` in the address of the page that opens (`.../logs-management/<ldp-account>/streams/<streamId>/dashboard`): copy it.

{/* cp-shot: ![Manager datastream tab more options](/images/public-cloud/databases/set-up-logs-forwarding/manager-datastream-tab-more-options.png) */}

Retrieve your LDP destination `serviceName`:

- This is your Public Cloud project ID. To copy it, click the `Project` selector at the top of the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, then click the copy icon on the row of your project.

Retrieve your `clusterId`:

- In the [OVHcloud Console](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/#/public-cloud/project), select your Public Cloud project. In the left-hand menu, click your engine under `Databases`, then click the name of the service you want to manage.
- In the **General information** card of the `Overview` tab, you can find the **Service ID** field, which corresponds to the cluster ID.

Retrieve the available `kind` values for your service:

- <Api version="v1" section="/cloud" method="GET" route={"/cloud/project/\{serviceName\}/database/valkey/\{clusterId\}/log/kind"} regions={["us"]} />

 

</details>

Use the following API call:

<Api version="v1" section="/cloud" method="POST" route={"/cloud/project/\{serviceName\}/database/\{engine\}/\{clusterId\}/log/subscription"} regions={["us"]} />

**Request Body:**

```console
{
  kind: "string",
  streamId: <LDP destination stream ID>
}
```

</Tab>

</Tabs>

 

### Find logs in Graylog

To find logs of your service in your Graylog stream, you can use the following Graylog queries:

#### MongoDB[®](#go-further)

Query: `cluster: "<HostID>"`

You can find this `HostID` in the OVHcloud Console:

- Open the `Overview` tab of your MongoDB service
- In the **Connection details** card, the **Host** field has the format `<HostID>.database.cloud.ovh.us`

 

#### Other Engines

Query: `clusterID: "<Engine>-<HostID>"`

You can find this `HostID` in the OVHcloud Console:

- Find the **Service ID** (in the **General information** card of the `Overview` tab), formatted as a UUID (AAAAAAAA-BBBB-CCCC-DDDDDDDDDDDD)
- `HostID` is the first part of the UUID (AAAAAAAA)

 

### Delete your subscription

<Tabs>

<Tab label="Via OVHcloud Console">

#### Method 1: Unsubscribe

In the [OVHcloud Console](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/#/public-cloud/project), select your Public Cloud project. In the left-hand menu, click your engine under `Databases`, then click the name of the service.

In the **Manage log streaming** panel of the `Observability` tab (**Logs** view > `Manage streaming`; if the service exposes several log kinds, select the kind first), click `Unsubscribe` on the subscription in **Current subscriptions**, then `Confirm`.

{/* cp-shot: ![Manager database tab unsubscribe](/images/public-cloud/databases/set-up-logs-forwarding/manager-database-tab-unsubscribe.png) */}

 

#### Method 2: Cancel the database service

If you delete your database service, all subscriptions of this service are deleted automatically.

For instructions to cancel a Database or Analytics service, see our [Databases & Analytics - FAQ](/public-cloud/databases/faq#service-plans-and-instances) guide.

</Tab>

<Tab label="Via OVHcloud API">

#### Method 1: Unsubscribe

You can delete subscriptions using the `subscriptionId` concerned in this API call:

<Api version="v1" section="/cloud" method="DELETE" route={"/cloud/project/\{serviceName\}/database/\{engine\}/\{clusterId\}/log/subscription/\{subscriptionId\}"} regions={["us"]} />

 

#### Method 2: Cancel the database service

If you delete your database service, all subscriptions of this service are deleted automatically.

<Api version="v1" section="/cloud" method="DELETE" route={"/cloud/project/\{serviceName\}/database/\{engine\}/\{clusterId\}"} regions={["us"]} />

</Tab>
