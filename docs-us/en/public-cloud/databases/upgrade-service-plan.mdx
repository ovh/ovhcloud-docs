# Databases & Analytics - Upgrade your service plan

**Learn how to upgrade the service plan of your Cloud Databases or Analytics service according to your needs.**

:::warning

**NOTE:** Once you have upgraded your solution, you will not be able to downgrade to a lower solution.

:::

 

## Requirements

- Access to the [OVHcloud Console](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/) or the [OVHcloud API](https://api.us.ovhcloud.com/)
- A [Public Cloud project](https://us.ovhcloud.com/public-cloud/) in your OVHcloud account

 

## Instructions

Log in to the [OVHcloud Console](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/#/public-cloud/project) and select your Public Cloud project. In the left-hand menu of your project, click your engine under **Databases** (or under **Analytics**, depending on your engine), then click the name of your service.

In the **General information** card of the `Overview` tab, click `Upgrade`.

{/* cp-shot: ![Manager database configuration tab](/images/public-cloud/databases/upgrade-service-plan/manager-database-configuration-tab.png) */}

In the panel that opens, select the new **Plan** of your cluster. Depending on your existing configuration, you may be required to upgrade your node model.

Click `Save` to submit your changes.

{/* cp-shot: ![Manager database edit](/images/public-cloud/databases/upgrade-service-plan/manager-database-edit.png) */}

To downgrade the service plan of your cluster, use the OVHcloud API.

 

#### Using the OVHcloud API

:::info

If you are not familiar with using the OVHcloud API, please refer to our [First Steps with the OVHcloud API](/manage-and-operate/api/first-steps) guide.

:::

To upgrade or downgrade the service plan of your cluster, use the API call like the one below, but for your engine:

<Api version="v1" section="/cloud" method="PUT" route={"/cloud/project/\{serviceName\}/database/postgresql/\{clusterId\}"} regions={["us"]} />

**Request Body:**

```
{
"plan": "<essential|business|production|enterprise>"
}
```

If you downgrade to an Essential plan, your current flavor and/or number of nodes will be downgraded automatically if they exceed the limits of the Essential plan.
