# AI Training - Getting started

## Requirements

- A **Public cloud** project
- Optionally, container objects to attach data to the **job** at step 8; see our [create data container](/storage-and-backup/object-storage/pcs-create-container) guide.

---

### OVHcloud Console access

- **Direct link:** [Public Cloud Projects](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/#/public-cloud/project)
- **Navigation path:** `Public Cloud` > `Projects` > Select your project > `AI` > `AI Training`

---

 

## Instructions

### Step 1 - Going to the AI Training menu

Click [this link](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/#/public-cloud/project) and select your Public Cloud project. In the left-hand menu of your project, click `AI Training` under **AI**.

{/* cp-shot: ![Manager AI onboarding](/images/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-training-submit-job/manager-ai-onboarding.png) */}

 

### Step 2 - Starting a job submission

If the project has not been authorized for AI services yet, authorize it from the banner displayed on the page. Upon authorizing the project, you grant OVHcloud access to your Object Storage containers. This access is only used to synchronize your data within **AI Training** with your containers.

From the list of **jobs**, start the job submission by clicking the `Create a job` button. The page that opens displays a summary of your configuration and its estimated price per hour on the right-hand side.

{/* cp-shot: ![Manager AI service list](/images/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-training-submit-job/manager-ai-service-list.png) */}

 

### Step 3 - Naming your job and selecting a region

Enter a name for your job. This will make it easier to manage when you have multiple jobs running.

Each **job** is executed in an OVHcloud region. Each region has its own **AI Training** cluster with potentially varying capabilities. For more information, see the [capabilities](/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-training-capabilities).

Select the desired region.

 

### Step 4 - Specifying the amount of resources

Select the CPU or GPU flavor you need for your training workload, then adjust the quantity.

The max amount of GPUs or CPUs you can select for your **job** is region-dependent. If you choose a GPU, a fixed ratio of CPUs is applied based on the number of GPUs. Similarly, there is a fixed ratio of Memory based on the number of CPUs. For more information, see the [capabilities](/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-training-capabilities).

Once the amount of resources is set, you can see a preview of the billing rate in the summary.

 

### Step 5 - Providing a Docker image

A job is basically a Docker container that is run within the OVHcloud infrastructure. You need to provide a Docker image to be executed in the Docker image section. There are several options you can choose from:

 

#### Preset Images

With the ready-to-use image catalog option, OVHcloud provides a set of images from which you can choose to ease the submission of your first **jobs**. The provided images are essentially a JupyterLab environment bundled with some Deep Learning technology such as TensorFlow or MXNet. These images are planned to be removed, as the product is more focused on using custom images.

 

#### Custom Images

Preset images cannot cover all your needs, so you can specify your own image if necessary with `Custom image`. You can use any image that is accessible from **AI Training**.

This includes public images (e.g., Docker Hub), images within the shared registry, or images in your added private registry. For more information, see how to [add a private registry](/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/manage-registries).

 

### Step 6 - Privacy Settings

Next, select your privacy settings: `Private` (access requires a token) or `Public` (access without a token).

:::warning

**NOTE:** *Public access* will expose your data and code to anyone getting the AI Training job link. Be careful and don't use it with sensitive data. On the other hand, *Private access* will require a username and password combination or an AI token to access the job content, ensuring a secure environment.

:::

 

### Step 7 - Job lifecycle

:::warning

**NOTE:** Actually, our main motivation is to keep the platform up-to-date, in terms of security patches and new feature alignment, so we need to update and restart hosts from time to time, which requires getting them free from any customer workload.

This also allows us to address host-located incidents, e.g., when a single GPU fails due to faulty hardware (only 1 customer impacted, but we still need to stop the host to bring it to PCI maintenance, so every remaining workload must be evicted or rescheduled elsewhere).

:::

By default, your job will automatically shut down after 7 consecutive days of being in `Running` status. Rest assured that all your settings and data will be preserved. You have the option to turn on `Automatically restart after timeout`, which will automatically restart your job back on every 7 days, ensuring minimal disruption to your workflow.

 

### Step 8 - Advanced configuration

*This step is optional.*

In the advanced configuration section, turn on the switch of each option you need.

First, the Docker image you provided in Step 5 includes an entrypoint for your container. You can override this entrypoint by specifying your own command (`Commands`).

Then, by default, your AI Training job comes with **ephemeral storage** (local storage). But in this step, you can also link Object Storage containers (`Volumes`) and Git repositories (`Git Public`) to your job, either as input for your training workload or as output for your results (e.g., model weights).

If you want to learn more about configuring containers and Git repositories in the job, you can refer to this [model training example](/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-training-train-first-ml-model). For now, we will launch a classic job without any external volumes added to it.

You can also add `Labels` to your job.

Finally, `SSH key` allows you to access your job remotely.

 

### Step 9 - Submitting your job

Before submission, check the overview of the **job** you configured in the summary. Click `CLI equivalent` to get the equivalent command to use with the `ovhai`**&#x20;CLI**.

Click `Create the job` to confirm and launch the creation of your **job** on the cluster.

When your job is created, it will appear in the list of your AI Training jobs.

 

### Step 10 - Consulting your job

From this list, you can access your job details by clicking on its name. The `Overview` tab includes several components, like the job resources, status, lifecycle, and access URL (connection details). This URL is of the form `https://<JOB-ID>.job.<REGION>.ai.cloud.ovh.us/`. Moreover, you can access any available port by adding it to the job URL this way: `https://<JOB-ID>-<PORT>.job.<REGION>.ai.cloud.ovh.us/`. You can check the list of available ports in the [capabilities](/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-training-capabilities).

You can also check your job logs in the `Observability` tab.

{/* cp-shot: ![Manager AI dashboard - step 3](/images/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-training-submit-job/manager-ai-dashboard-3.png) */}

 

### Step 11 - Stopping your job

If you are done using your job, if your model converged prematurely or if you just wish to interrupt your job you can do so from the **jobs** list and the job page.

From the list of **jobs**, click the `⋮` button at the far right of the entry and interrupt the job by clicking `Stop`. Alternatively, from the `Overview` tab of the **job**, click `Stop` in the general information area. Confirm with `Stop`.

{/* cp-shot: ![AI training stop job](/images/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-training-submit-job/ai-training-stop-job.png) */}

After that, if you no longer need your job, you can delete it. To do so, click the `⋮` button on the row of the job, then select `Delete` (or click `Delete job` in the general information area) and confirm.

{/* cp-shot: ![Manager AI service list more options](/images/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-training-submit-job/manager-ai-service-list-more-options.png) */}

 

### Step 12 - Cloning your job

You can also click `Clone job` (from the `⋮` button of the job list or the general information area) to clone the job with the same configuration. This will launch a new job identical to the original (e.g., `example-job`), allowing you to rerun your workflow without re‑configuring the job.

{/* cp-shot: ![AI training stop job](/images/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-training-submit-job/ai-training-stop-job.png) */}
