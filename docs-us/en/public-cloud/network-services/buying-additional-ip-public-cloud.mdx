# Buying an Additional IP

**Learn how to obtain an Additional IP for an OVHcloud Public Cloud project.**

|                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Since October 6th, 2022 our service "Failover IP" is named [Additional IP](https://us.ovhcloud.com/network/additional-ip/). This renaming does not affect its technical features. |

You may need to configure an Additional IP address on your instances for different reasons. For example, you may be:

- Hosting several websites on your instance.
- Hosting international projects.

To meet these needs, you can order an Additional IP address for your Public Cloud instances.

These Additional IP addresses can only be migrated to instances in the same project.

 

## Requirements

- access to the [OVHcloud Console](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/)
- a [Public Cloud instance](/public-cloud/compute/getting-started) in your Public Cloud project

 

## Instructions

From the [OVHcloud Console](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/#/public-cloud/project), select your Public Cloud project.

1. In the left-hand menu, open `Public IP` under **Network**.
2. Open the `Additional IP` tab.
3. Click `Order an Additional IP`.

{/* cp-shot: ![Network IP buy - step 1](/images/public-cloud/network-services/buying-additional-ip-public-cloud/network-ip-buy-01.png) */}

 

There are three steps to the ordering process:

1. Select Additional IP and click Next.
2. Select United States of America and click Next.
3. Select your instance from the drop-down menu, then click Generate purchase order.

{/* cp-shot: ![Network IP buy - step 2](/images/public-cloud/network-services/buying-additional-ip-public-cloud/network-ip-buy-02.png) */}

 

The purchase order will open automatically for you to complete your purchase.

See our guide on [managing OVHcloud orders](/account/billing/manage-orders) for more details.

You can also find the purchase order in the My orders section of your customer account in the OVHcloud Console.

The next step will be the IP configuration in your OS; please refer to our [guide](/public-cloud/network-services/configuring-additional-ip-public-cloud).
