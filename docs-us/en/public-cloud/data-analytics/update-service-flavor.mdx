# Analytics - Update your service flavor

## Objective

Learn how to adjust the flavor of your cluster according to your needs.

## Requirements

- Access to the OVHcloud [API](https://api.us.ovhcloud.com/) (create your credentials by consulting [this guide](/manage-and-operate/api/first-steps))
- A [Public Cloud project](https://us.ovhcloud.com/public-cloud/) in your OVHcloud account

#### OVHcloud Console access

- **Direct link:** [Public Cloud projects](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/#/public-cloud/project)
- **Navigation path:** `Public Cloud` > Select your project > **Analytics** group

## Instructions

### Upgrading your flavor

This option is available through the OVHcloud [API](https://api.us.ovhcloud.com/) and the [OVHcloud Console](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/).

In the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, click your product under **Analytics**, then click the name of your service.

In the **General information** card of the `Overview` tab, click `Upgrade`. In the panel that opens, select the new **Node flavor** of your cluster, then click `Save`. You can also view the hourly and monthly costs.

{/* cp-shot: ![Manager database configuration tab](/images/public-cloud/databases/update-service-flavor/manager-database-configuration-tab.png) */}

{/* cp-shot: ![Manager database edit increase](/images/public-cloud/databases/update-service-flavor/manager-database-edit-increase.png) */}

### Downgrading your flavor

This option is currently available via the OVHcloud [API](https://api.us.ovhcloud.com/).

In order to be able to downgrade your flavor, you must have a threshold of 20% between your current disk space used (base storage + additional storage) and the maximum storage space allowed for the flavor.

{/* cp-shot: ![Manager database edit decrease](/images/public-cloud/databases/update-service-flavor/manager-database-edit-decrease.png) */}

For instance, if you have an OpenSearch cluster with db1-15 as flavor and less than 384 GB (80% of 480 GB) of disk space used, you can decrease the flavor to db1-7 on that endpoint:
