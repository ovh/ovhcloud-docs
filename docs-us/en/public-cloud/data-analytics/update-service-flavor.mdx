# Databases & Analytics - Update your service flavor

**Learn how to adjust the flavor of your Cloud Database or Analytics service according to your needs.**

 

## How to upgrade your flavor

This option is available through the OVHcloud [API](https://api.us.ovhcloud.com/) and the [OVHcloud Console](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/).

 

### Requirements

- Access to the [OVHcloud Console](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/) or the [API](https://api.us.ovhcloud.com/)
- A [Public Cloud project](https://us.ovhcloud.com/public-cloud/) in your OVHcloud account

 

### Instructions

To adjust the flavor of your cluster, log in to the [OVHcloud Console](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/#/public-cloud/project) and select your Public Cloud project.

In the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, click your product under **Analytics**, then click the name of your service.

In the general information card of the `Overview` tab, click `Upgrade`. In the panel that opens, select the new flavor of your cluster, then click `Save`. You can also view the hourly and monthly costs.

## How to downgrade your flavor

This option is currently available via the OVHcloud [API](https://api.us.ovhcloud.com/).

 

### Requirements

- Access to the OVHcloud [API](https://api.us.ovhcloud.com/) (create your credentials by consulting [this guide](/manage-and-operate/api/first-steps))
- A [Public Cloud project](https://us.ovhcloud.com/public-cloud/) in your OVHcloud account

 

### Instructions

To be able to downgrade your flavor, you must have a threshold of 20% between your current disk space used (base storage + additional storage) and the maximum storage space allowed for the flavor.

{/* cp-shot: ![Manager database edit decrease](/images/public-cloud/databases/update-service-flavor/manager-database-edit-decrease.png) */}

For instance, if you have a PostgreSQL® cluster with db1-15 as the flavor and less than 384 GB (80% of 480GB) of disk space used, you can decrease the flavor to db1-7 on that endpoint:
