# Analytics - Capabilities and limitations

## Analytics[\*](#go-further)

With our Analytics services, we manage the infrastructure and administration, including set-up, backup, scalability, and security. Choose from a large portfolio of popular engines.

We continuously improve our offers. You can follow and submit ideas to add to our roadmap at [https://github.com/orgs/ovh/projects/16/views/5](https://github.com/orgs/ovh/projects/16/views/5).

## Analytics services

We currently offer these data analytics engines:

### Dashboards

Dashboards is a monitoring and analysis solution commonly used to visualize and analyze data from various sources.<br/>
See our [Dashboards Capabilities and Limitations](/public-cloud/data-analytics/dashboards-capabilities-limitations) guide.

### Kafka®

Kafka® is a high-performance distributed streaming system designed for durability, scalability, and real-time data streaming across multiple applications and platforms.<br/>
See our [Kafka® Capabilities and Limitations](/public-cloud/data-analytics/kafka-capabilities-limitations) guide.

### Kafka® Connect

Kafka® Connect is a Kafka® extension to facilitate connection to your external data systems.<br/>
See our [Kafka® Connect Capabilities and Limitations](/public-cloud/data-analytics/kafka-connect-capabilities-limitations) guide.

### Kafka® MirrorMaker

Kafka® MirrorMaker is a data replication system between two Kafka® clusters.<br/>
See our [Kafka® MirrorMaker Capabilities and Limitations](/public-cloud/data-analytics/kafka-mirrormaker-capabilities-limitations) guide.

### OpenSearch™

OpenSearch™ is an open-source fully managed search, analytics, and observability engine. It is ideal for logs management and offers search and analytical aggregations.<br/>
OpenSearch™ is an open-source fork derived from Elasticsearch.<br/>
See our [OpenSearch™ Capabilities and Limitations](/public-cloud/data-analytics/opensearch-capabilities) guide.

## Specific feature Plans

### Essential

The Essential plan typically offers the basic features and functionalities required for managing an engine. It is designed for test and development environments or non-critical projects.

### Business/Production

The Business/Production plan is more comprehensive and suitable for organizations with higher engine demands. This is the first production-ready offering.

### Enterprise

The Enterprise plan is the most advanced and robust option available for managed engines. It is dedicated to organizations with complex and high-performance engine requirements.

### Plan capabilities overview

Here is an overview of the various plans' capabilities:

| Feature           | Essential | Business/Production | Enterprise |
| ----------------- | ------------------- | ------------------- | ---------- |
| SLA               | None                | 99.9%               | 99.95%     |
| High availability |                     | ✓                   | ✓          |

Your choice of plan affects the number of nodes your cluster can run.
