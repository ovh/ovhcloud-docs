# Analytics - How to set up logs forwarding

## Objective

Analytics services allow you to send logs of your service to your own Logs Data Platform (LDP) data stream.

**This guide explains how to forward service logs to your own LDP stream from the OVHcloud Console or with the OVHcloud API.**

## Requirements

- An [Analytics service](https://us.ovhcloud.com/public-cloud/cloud-analytics/) up and running
- To use the API: access to the [OVHcloud API](https://api.us.ovhcloud.com/)
- A [Logs Data Platform account](/manage-and-operate/logs-data-platform/getting-started) within this OVHcloud account with at least one destination stream configured
    - If you are not familiar with all the LDP *Stream* configuration possibilities, create a new one with the default options (indexing and websocket enabled, long-term storage disabled) for this guide.

#### OVHcloud Console access

- **Direct link:** [Public Cloud projects](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/#/public-cloud/project)
- **Navigation path:** `Public Cloud` > Select your project > **Analytics** group

## Instructions

### Create your subscription

<Tabs>

<Tab label="Via OVHcloud Console">

Go to your Analytics service and open the `Observability` tab.

{/* cp-shot: ![Manager database tab](/images/public-cloud/databases/set-up-logs-forwarding/manager-database-tab.png) */}

In the **Logs** section, click `Manage streaming`. If your service exposes several log kinds, first select the kind to forward in the list of the **Logs** section: the panel manages one log kind at a time.

In the **Manage log streaming** panel, under **Subscribe to a stream**, select your LDP account and the destination stream, then click `Subscribe`.

The subscription is listed under **Current subscriptions** and the logs start to be forwarded to your LDP stream. If your service exposes several log kinds, repeat the operation for each kind you want to forward.

Once the subscription is enabled, all the logs will be pushed to [Logs Data Platform](https://us.ovhcloud.com/identity-security-operations/logs-data-platform/) to archive generated logs and perform advanced searches, and create alerts or visualizations.

For more information, please refer to our [Quick Start for Logs Data Platform](/manage-and-operate/logs-data-platform/getting-started) guide.

{/* cp-shot: ![Manager database tab subscribe](/images/public-cloud/databases/set-up-logs-forwarding/manager-database-tab-subscribe.png) */}

</Tab>

<Tab label="Via OVHcloud API">

First retrieve the required information.

**Retrieve your LDP destination `streamId`**:

- In the OVHcloud Console, open `Observability` > `Logs Management` in the left-hand menu, select your Logs Data Platform account, then open the `Data streams` tab.
- Click the name of your target stream. The stream ID is the identifier that follows `/streams/` in the address of the page that opens (`.../logs-management/<ldp-account>/streams/<streamId>/dashboard`): copy it.

{/* cp-shot: ![Manager datastream tab more options](/images/public-cloud/databases/set-up-logs-forwarding/manager-datastream-tab-more-options.png) */}

**Retrieve your LDP destination `serviceName`**:

- It refers to your Public Cloud project ID. To copy it, click the `Project` selector at the top of the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, then click the copy icon on the row of your project.

**Retrieve your `clusterId`**:

- Go to your Analytics service and copy the **Service ID** from the **General information** card of the `Overview` tab.

**Retrieve the available `kind` values for your service**:

<Api version="v1" section="/cloud" method="GET" route={"/cloud/project/\{serviceName\}/database/kafka/\{clusterId\}/log/kind"} regions={["us"]} />

Then use the following API call:

<Api version="v1" section="/cloud" method="POST" route={"/cloud/project/\{serviceName\}/database/\{engine\}/\{clusterId\}/log/subscription"} regions={["us"]} />

Replace `kafka` in the route with your service's engine (for example `opensearch` or `grafana`).

**Request Body:**

```console
body : {
    kind: "string",
    streamId: <LDP destination stream ID>
}
```

The logs will start to be forwarded to your LDP stream.

</Tab>

</Tabs>

### Find logs in Graylog

To find logs of your service in your Graylog stream, you can use the following Graylog queries:

Query: `clusterID: "<Engine>-<HostID>"`

You can find this `HostID` in the OVHcloud Console:

- Find the **Service ID** (in the **General information** card of the `Overview` tab), formatted as a UUID (AAAAAAAA-BBBB-CCCC-DDDDDDDDDDDD)
- `HostID` is the first part of the UUID (AAAAAAAA)

### Delete your subscription

<Tabs>

<Tab label="Via OVHcloud Console">

In the `Observability` tab of your service, click `Manage streaming` in the **Logs** section (if the service exposes several log kinds, select the kind first).

Under **Current subscriptions**, click `Unsubscribe` on the row of the subscription, then `Confirm`.

{/* cp-shot: ![Manager database tab unsubscribe](/images/public-cloud/databases/set-up-logs-forwarding/manager-database-tab-unsubscribe.png) */}

</Tab>

<Tab label="Via OVHcloud API">

Delete the subscription using the `subscriptionId` concerned and this API call:

<Api version="v1" section="/cloud" method="DELETE" route={"/cloud/project/\{serviceName\}/database/\{engine\}/\{clusterId\}/log/subscription/\{subscriptionId\}"} regions={["us"]} />

</Tab>

</Tabs>

If you delete your Analytics service, all subscriptions of this service are deleted automatically.
