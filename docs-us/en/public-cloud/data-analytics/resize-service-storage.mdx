# Analytics - Resize your service storage

## Objective

Learn how to adjust the storage space of your cluster according to your needs.

:::info

This feature is only available for engines that use storage space.

:::

## Requirements

- Access to the [OVHcloud API](https://api.us.ovhcloud.com/) (create your credentials by consulting [this guide](/manage-and-operate/api/first-steps))
- A [Public Cloud project](https://us.ovhcloud.com/public-cloud/) in your OVHcloud account

#### OVHcloud Console access

- **Direct link:** [Public Cloud projects](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/#/public-cloud/project)
- **Navigation path:** `Public Cloud` > Select your project > **Analytics** group

## Instructions

You can increase or decrease the storage of your service at any time using either the [OVHcloud API](https://api.us.ovhcloud.com/) or the [OVHcloud Console](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/).

:::info

When changing the storage space of your service, the system checks that at least 20% of your total storage space will remain free after the resize operation. As a consequence, you might not be able to perform a storage space decrease if the new size you requested is too close to your current disk space usage.

:::

### Using the OVHcloud Console

In the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, click your product under **Analytics**, then click the name of your service.

In the **General information** card of the `Overview` tab, click `Resize storage` on the **Storage** line, adjust the storage space of your cluster, then click `Save`. You can also change the storage with the `Upgrade` action of the same card.

### Using the OVHcloud API

As an example, for OpenSearch services, you can perform a storage space increase or decrease using this endpoint:

<Api version="v1" section="/cloud/project" method="PUT" route={"/cloud/project/\{serviceName\}/database/opensearch/\{clusterId\}"} regions={["us"]} />

Other engines using a storage space provide similar endpoints, e.g.:

<Api version="v1" section="/cloud/project" method="PUT" route={"/cloud/project/\{serviceName\}/database/kafka/\{clusterId\}"} regions={["us"]} />

:::warning

**NOTE:** Use the "storage" field if available. If not, then the "disk" field will work.

:::
