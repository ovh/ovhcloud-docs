# Analytics - Upgrade your service plan

**Learn how to upgrade the service plan of your Analytics service according to your needs.**

:::warning
**NOTE:** Once you have upgraded your solution, you will not be able to downgrade to a lower solution.

:::

## Requirements

- Access to the [OVHcloud API](https://api.us.ovhcloud.com/)
- A [Public Cloud project](https://us.ovhcloud.com/public-cloud/) in your OVHcloud account

#### OVHcloud Console access

- **Direct link:** [Public Cloud projects](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/#/public-cloud/project)
- **Navigation path:** `Public Cloud` > Select your project > **Analytics** group

## Instructions

### Using the OVHcloud Console

In the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, click your product under **Analytics**, then click the name of your service.

In the **General information** card of the `Overview` tab, click `Upgrade`. In the panel that opens, select the new **Plan** of your service, then click `Save`. Depending on your existing configuration, you may be required to upgrade your node model.

{/* cp-shot: ![Manager database configuration tab](/images/public-cloud/databases/upgrade-service-plan/manager-database-configuration-tab.png) */}

{/* cp-shot: ![Manager database edit](/images/public-cloud/databases/upgrade-service-plan/manager-database-edit.png) */}

Currently, the OVHcloud Console does not allow downgrading the service plan for some engines, but you can do it using the API.

### Using the OVHcloud API

:::info
If you are not familiar with using the OVHcloud API, please refer to our guide on [First steps with the OVHcloud API](/manage-and-operate/api/first-steps).

:::

To upgrade or downgrade the service plan of your cluster, use the following API call:

<Api version="v1" section="/cloud" method="PUT" route={"/cloud/project/\{serviceName\}/database/kafka/\{clusterId\}"} regions={["us"]} />

Replace `kafka` in the route with your service's engine (for example `opensearch` or `grafana`).

```console
body : {
    plan: <essential|business|production|enterprise>
}
```

If you downgrade to an Essential plan, your current flavor and/or number of nodes will be downgraded automatically if they exceed the limits of the Essential plan.
