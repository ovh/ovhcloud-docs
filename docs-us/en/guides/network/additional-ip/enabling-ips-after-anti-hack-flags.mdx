# Enabling IPs After Anti-Hack Flags

**Learn how to check alerts on your IPs and unblock them via OVHcloud Control Panel and OVHcloud API.**

For every IP available with OVHcloud products and services, as an Internet Service Provider, we will register and reserve it with organizations such as [RIPE](https://www.ripe.net/) or [ARIN](https://www.arin.net/). This means that we appear as the IP abuse contact for litigation in the WHOIS database.

If an IP is reported to organizations such as Spamhaus and SpamCop, which work to combat SPAM, malicious websites, and phishing, then the reputation of the entire OVHcloud network is at stake.

It is therefore important that OVHcloud takes care of the reputation, quality, and security of the network, which also forms an important part of your service.

 

### How will I know if my IP has been flagged?

Once an IP receives an anti-hack flag, an email will be sent to your account containing information like the example below:

> Dear Customer,\
> \
> The IP address 135.xxx.xxx.xxx had to be blocked by our services due to the various alerts received.\
> Please don't hesitate to contact our technical support team so that this situation does not become critical.\
> \
> OVH Customer Support

 

### What to do after receiving the email alert?

Identify the issue, resolve it, and then unblock your IP.

 

## Instructions

### Identify and resolve the issue

Before unblocking an IP:

- Stop sending emails (e.g. stop all email software such as qmail, Postfix, Sendmail, etc.).
- Check the email queue (e.g. qmHandle for qmail, postqueue -p for Postfix) and clear it.
- Analyze your logs using the **Message-ID** found in the block alert.
- If you are indeed sending SPAM or illegitimate emails, we strongly recommend you resolve the issue **before** unblocking the IP.

Once the issue has been resolved, you can unblock your IP by performing the following steps.

**WARNING:** Do not unblock the IP under any circumstances without having suspended the sending of emails from your server, otherwise you will immediately get blocked for a second time (and a longer duration).

 

### Via the OVHcloud Control Panel

Log in to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/#/hub) and select `Bare Metal Cloud` from the top navigation bar.

In the left-hand menu, select `Network`, then `IP`.

![antihack\_enable\_ip01.png](/images/network/additional-ip/enabling-ips-after-anti-hack-flags/22233819207955.png)

 

Toward the top of the next screen, you will see a yellow alert message. Click `View all alerts`.

![antihack\_enable\_ip02.png](/images/network/additional-ip/enabling-ips-after-anti-hack-flags/22233819214995.png)

 

If you have an alert on any of your IP(s), the information will be displayed right underneath:

![antihack\_enable\_ip03.png](/images/network/additional-ip/enabling-ips-after-anti-hack-flags/22233848978835.png)

 

In the **My public IP addresses and associated services** section, click on the *more options* `...` button next to the corresponding IP/service and select `Anti-Hack`.

![antihack\_enable\_ip04.png](/images/network/additional-ip/enabling-ips-after-anti-hack-flags/22233819229459.png)

 

In the pop-up window, select \[code]Unblock IP.

![antihack\_enable\_ip05.png](/images/network/additional-ip/enabling-ips-after-anti-hack-flags/22233819234963.png)

 

You will see a green alert notifying you of success.

![antihack\_enable\_ip06.png](/images/network/additional-ip/enabling-ips-after-anti-hack-flags/22233819238675.png)

 

### Via the OVHcloud API

Log in to the [OVHcloud API interface](https://api.us.ovhcloud.com/) according to the [relevant guide](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018130839) and follow the steps below.

Use the following API call to retrieve details about the blocked IP, such as the length of time it has been blocked:

<Api version="v1" section="/ip" method="GET" route={"/ip/\{ip\}/antihack/\{ipBlocked\}"} regions={["us"]} />

![antihack\_enable\_ip07.png](/images/network/additional-ip/enabling-ips-after-anti-hack-flags/22234346667411.png)

 

Use the next API call to unblock your IP address. A "null" result means it was successful.

<Api version="v1" section="/ip" method="POST" route={"/ip/\{ip\}/antihack/\{ipBlocked\}/unblock"} regions={["us"]} />

![antihack\_enable\_ip08.png](/images/network/additional-ip/enabling-ips-after-anti-hack-flags/22234346685843.png)

 

## Conclusion

Having read this guide, you should be able to check alerts on your IPs and unblock them using OVHcloud Control Panel and the OVHcloud API.
