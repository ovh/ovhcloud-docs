## OVHcloud Connect

Use the OVHcloud Connect solution to establish a secure, high-performance connection between your company network and the OVHcloud vRack. Enjoy dedicated bandwidth ranging from 200 Mbps to 10 Gbps, offering improved latency compared to the public network (Internet).
