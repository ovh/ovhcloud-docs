# How to Install OVHcloud Connect Direct from the OVHcloud Control Panel

## Objective

With OVHcloud Connect, you can link your company network to your private OVHcloud vRack network, without creating a VPN tunnel through the internet. This will give you a quicker, more stable connection with guaranteed bandwidth.

**This guide will show you how to set up the OVHcloud Connect Direct solution via the OVHcloud Control Panel.**

## Requirements

:::warning

To ensure correct operation of this service, you must be aware of the [technical capabilities and limitations of the OVHcloud Connect solution](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/7048308555667) and configure your network devices accordingly.

:::

- An [OVHcloud Connect Direct solution](https://us.ovhcloud.com/network/ovhcloud-connect/)
- An OVHcloud vRack
- Access to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/)

## Instructions

Log in to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/), go to the `Bare Metal Cloud` section and click on `Network`. Next, open `OVHcloud Connect` and click on your solution.

![occdirect1.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect1-png.png)

### Step 1: Associating a vRack

You will need to link a vRack to your solution first. Click the `Attach a vRack` button and select an existing vRack from the drop-down menu.

![occdirect2.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect2-png.png)

A message will confirm the vRack association.

### Step 2: Adding a PoP configuration

:::warning

Changing the PoP configuration from L2 to L3, or vice versa, would require deleting the entire configuration. We advise you to think carefully about your configuration choice before going any further.

:::

:::info

For more details on the differences between levels L2 and L3, see our [FAQ](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/6980557660819).

:::

#### Configuration

Once you have linked your vRack, two `PoP configuration` menus will appear. Click the `Add a PoP configuration` button in the first menu, select L2 from the drop-down menu, then confirm.

![occdirect3.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect3-png.png)

The L2 configuration will then be enabled in the two `PoP configuration` menus.

![occdirect4.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect4-png.png)

#### Configuration L3

Once you have connected your vRack, click `Add a PoP configuration` and select the L3 configuration from the drop-down menu.

![occdirect5.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect5-png.png)

You will then need to enter the following:

![occdirect6.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect6-png.png)

![occdirect7.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect7-png.png)

The `PoP configuration` menu will then appear.

![occdirect8.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect8-png.png)

You can also add a second PoP L3 configuration via the second `PoP configuration` menu.

### Step 3: Adding a data center configuration

#### Configuration

When your PoP configuration is active, click `Add a configuration` under the `DC configuration` menu. Select a data center from the drop-down menu and confirm.

![occdirect9.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect9-png.png)

![occdirect10.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect10-png.png)

The DC configuration will then begin.

![occdirect11.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect11-png.png)

#### Configuration L3

When your PoP configuration has been set, click `Add a configuration` under the `DC configuration` menu.

![occdirect12.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect12-png.png)

Select a data center from the drop-down menu, then enter the information required.

![occdirect13.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect13-png.png)

![occdirect14.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect14-png.png)

You can add additional data center configurations by clicking on the `...` button, then `Add a configuration`.

![occdirect15.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect15-png.png)

You must also add a routing configuration.

##### **Adding a routing configuration**

Click on the `(...)` button on the desired data center then on `Add routing configuration` .

![occdirect16.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect16-png.png)

Then choose the routing type between Static and BGP.

![occdirect17.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect17-png.png)

If you choose the type BGP, then enter the required information.

![occdirect18.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect18-png.png)

![occdirect19.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect19-png.png)

If you choose the Static type, enter the required information:

![occdirect20.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect20-png.png)

![occdirect21.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect21-png.png)

You can add multiple routing configurations within the same data center. The configuration type (BGP or Static) chosen for your first routing configuration will then apply to the next configuration on the same data center.

![occdirect22.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect22-png.png)

### Deletion of vCenter resources

Each resource (PoP or DC) can be deleted individually, but deleting a parent resource such as DC or PoP will automatically delete all the subresources.

Recursive removal is slower than sequential removal of each resource.

:::info

If a DC configuration is shared between two or more OVHcloud Connect services, removing the PoP configuration from a single OVHcloud Connect service will not affect the DC resource.

:::

#### Deleting a routing Configuration

To delete a routing configuration, click the `...` button on the routing configuration to delete, then click `Delete`.

![occdirect23.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect23-png.png)

#### Deleting a DC configuration

To delete a DC configuration, click the `...` button on the DC configuration to delete, then click `Delete`.

![occdirect24.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect24-png.png)

:::info

Deleting a DC configuration will delete the related routing configurations.

:::

#### Deleting a PoP configuration

To delete a PoP configuration, click the `...` button on the PoP configuration to delete, then click `Delete configuration`.

![occdirect25.png](/images/network/ovhcloud-connect/how-to-install-ovhcloud-connect-direct-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/occdirect25-png.png)

:::info

Deleting a PoP configuration will delete the related DC and routing configurations.

:::
