# Generating OVHcloud account logs with Logs Data Platform

Please refer to [this documentation](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/27980753174035) to learn more about Logs Data Platform before continuing with this guide.

 

## Requirements

- An [OVHcloud customer account](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001286870)
- A Logs Data Platform account within this OVHcloud account with at least one active stream configured ([this guide](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/28017122298771) will walk you through all the necessary steps)

 

## Glossary

**Logs Data Platform (LDP)**: Fully managed and secured log management platform proposed by OVHcloud. Find more information on the [Logs Data Platform service page](https://us.ovhcloud.com/logs-data-platform/).

**Data stream**: A logical partition of logs that you create in an LDP account that you will use when ingesting, visualizing, or querying your logs. Multiple sources can be stored in the same data stream, and it is the unit for defining a logs pipeline (retention policy, archiving, live streaming, etc.), access rights, and alert policies.

**Logs forwarding**: Feature integrated into an OVHcloud product to ingest logs from its services to a data stream of an LDP account in the same OVHcloud account. The feature has to be enabled by the customer per service.

**Logs forwarding subscription**: When enabling the logs forwarding for a given OVHcloud service to a given LDP data stream, a subscription is created and attached to the data stream for further management by the customer.

 

## Instructions

### OVHcloud Account logs types

OVHcloud account offers three levels of logs:

- **Audit Logs**: Provide a security-relevant, chronological set of records documenting the sequence of actions in your OVHcloud account (i.e. logins, password change, etc.).
- **Activity Logs**: Provide all records of actions in your OVHcloud account from API calls and actions done in the Control Panel.
- **Access policy logs**: Provide all records of access evaluation in your OVHcloud account, including actions from third-party integration (i.e. actions authorized or unauthorized through IAM policies).

 

### Enable logs forwarding

You can enable the forwarding of the OVHcloud account logs via API. You will have to target a stream of one of your LDP accounts. The logs will be forwarded to that stream. Enabling the forwarding will create a subscription for this stream ID.

Note that enabling the forwarding is free of charge, but you will be charged for the usage of your Logs Data Platform service as per the standard price plan. For LDP pricing refer to the [Logs Data Platform product page](https://us.ovhcloud.com/logs-data-platform/).

#### Logs direct access

From the OVHcloud Control Panel, navigate to the `Account Logs` section via the `Identity, Security & Operations` menu.

Once there, select the tab for the type of logs you want to subscribe to: `Audit logs`, `Activity logs`, or `Access policy logs`.

![manager\_accountLogs\_auditTab.png](/images/manage-and-operate/api/generating-ovhcloud-account-logs-with-logs-data-platform/53119940818451.png)

 

#### Logs access through LDP

From the `Logs` tab, you can `Subscribe` to an LDP data stream. Once the subscription is enabled, all the logs will be pushed to [Logs Data Platform](https://us.ovhcloud.com/identity-security-operations/logs-data-platform/) to archive generated logs and perform advanced searches, and create alerts or visualizations.

For more information, please refer to our [Quick Start for Logs Data Platform](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/28017122298771) guide.

![manager\_accountLogs\_auditTab\_subscribe.png](/images/manage-and-operate/api/generating-ovhcloud-account-logs-with-logs-data-platform/53119940819347.png)

<Tab label="Via OVHcloud API">

To enable forwarding, you can use the following API call to forward API and Control Panel logs:

- Audit logs:
  <Api version="v1" section="/me" method="POST" route={"/me/logs/audit/log/subscription"} regions={["us"]} />
- Activity logs:
  <Api version="v1" section="/me" method="POST" route={"/me/api/log/subscription"} regions={["us"]} />
- Access policy logs:
  <Api version="v2" section="/iam" method="POST" route={"/iam/log/subscription"} regions={["us"]} />

For example, for activity logs:

```json
POST /me/api/log/subscription
{
    "kind": "access"
    "streamId": "ab51887e-0b98-4752-a514-f2513523a5cd"
}
```

The API requires:

- A `streamId`, which is the target data stream of your LDP account where your OVHcloud account logs will be forwarded to.
- A `kind`, which is the category of logs you want to forward into this data stream.

You will get in response an `operationId`, so you can use it to retrieve the `subscriptionId` for further management purposes using the Logs Data Platform read operation endpoint:

<Api version="v1" section="/dbaas/logs" method="GET" route={"/dbaas/logs/\{serviceName\}/operation"} regions={["us"]} />

:::info

You can find your `streamId` in the `Logs Data Platform` section of the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/).

Go to the `Data streams` tab of your Logs Data Platform account. In the table, click the *more options*`...` button to the right of the target data stream, then click `Copy stream ID`.

![manager\_ldp\_datastreamTab\_moreOptions.png](/images/manage-and-operate/api/generating-ovhcloud-account-logs-with-logs-data-platform/53119953220243.png)

:::

Alternatively, you can retrieve your streams using the Logs Data Platform API:

<Api version="v1" section="/dbaas/logs" method="GET" route={"/dbaas/logs/\{serviceName\}/output/graylog/stream"} regions={["us"]} />

<Api version="v1" section="/dbaas/logs" method="GET" route={"/dbaas/logs/\{serviceName\}/output/graylog/stream/\{streamId\}"} regions={["us"]} />

You can find the available kind using the following APIs:

- Audit logs kinds:
  <Api version="v1" section="/me" method="GET" route={"/me/logs/audit/log/kind"} regions={["us"]} />
- Activity logs kinds:
  <Api version="v1" section="/me" method="GET" route={"/me/api/log/kind"} regions={["us"]} />
- Access policy logs kinds:
  <Api version="v2" section="/iam" method="GET" route={"/iam/log/kind"} regions={["us"]} />

</Tab>

### Access to OVHcloud account logs

Now that your OVHcloud account logs are ingested and stored in your Logs Data Platform data stream, you can query your logs and build dashboards to have a graphical representation using the web-based UI of Graylog.

- Retrieve the admin user (the Logs Data Platform service name) and the password in your Logs Data Platform account `Home` tab.

  ![operate\_LDP\_ingest\_accountLogs02\*\*.png](/images/manage-and-operate/api/generating-ovhcloud-account-logs-with-logs-data-platform/53119940820755.png)

* Open the Graylog `web-ui`. You can retrieve the link from your account home page, or use your access point depending on your account region (for example, the Beauharnois region is [https://bhs2.logs.ovh.com/](https://bhs2.logs.ovh.com/)).

* Log in to Graylog using your **Logs Data Platform service name** and **password**.

  ![operate\_LDP\_ingest\_accountLogs03\*.png](/images/manage-and-operate/api/generating-ovhcloud-account-logs-with-logs-data-platform/53119953225363.png)

- Search through your logs across the data stream of your Logs Data Platform account. You can refer to the [Graylog writing search queries documentation](https://go2docs.graylog.org/4-0/making_sense_of_your_log_data/writing_search_queries.html?tocpath=Searching%20Your%20Log%20Data%7C_____1) for details on the search syntax.

  ![operate\_LDP\_ingest\_accountLogs04.png](/images/manage-and-operate/api/generating-ovhcloud-account-logs-with-logs-data-platform/53119953227283.png)

For more details about how to use your logs with Logs Data Platform, refer to the documentation [Logs Data Platform - Visualizing, querying, and exploiting your logs](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/sections/27230995950611-Visualizing-Querying-and-Exploiting-Your-Logs). This includes:

- How to set up alerts.
- How to view the logs in real time through a WebSocket.
- How to build visualization with OpenSearch Dashboards.
- How to integrate with OpenSearch API.
- How to to connect with Grafana.

 

### Details of generated logs

#### Audit logs

For every action related to the security of the OVHcloud account, an entry is generated. The Logs generated are:

**For login:**

![graylogs\_auditLog\_ovhcloudLogin.png](/images/manage-and-operate/api/generating-ovhcloud-account-logs-with-logs-data-platform/53119940825875.png)

The fields displayed depend on the type of log; you may not see all fields for every log you generate.

:::

| **Field**                      | **Value**                 | **Description**                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| account                        | String                    | OVHcloud account concerned by the action                                                                      |
| authDetails\_userDetails\_type | ACCOUNT, PROVIDER or USER | Indicate if the user is a root account (ACCOUNT), a local user (USER) or a user coming from an SSO (PROVIDER) |
| authDetails\_userDetails\_user | String                    | Name of the user                                                                                              |
| client\_ip                     | String                    | IP of the user who performed the action                                                                       |
| client\_ip\_geolocation        | String                    | Geolocalisation of the user who performed the action                                                          |
| client\_ip\_city\_name         | String                    | City name of the user who performed the action (if available)                                                 |
| client\_ip\_country\_code      | String                    | Country code of the user who performed the action                                                             |
| loginSuccessDetails\_mfaType   | String                    | Indicate the type of MFA use: BACKUP\_COD, MAIL, NONE, SMS, TOTP, U2F, UNKNOWN                                |
| loginSuccessDetails\_userAgent | String                    | User agent of the user                                                                                        |
| source                         | String                    | iam.ovhcloud                                                                                                  |
| type                           | String                    | LOGIN\_SUCCESS                                                                                                |

### Manage subscriptions

At any point, you can retrieve subscriptions attached to your Logs Data Platform data stream and choose to disable the forwarding by cancelling your subscription on your stream. So that your Logs Data Platform stream doesn't receive your audit logs anymore.

Note that this doesn't delete the logs that have been stored prior to the subscription cancellation, as data stored in a logs stream is immutable unless you delete the entire stream.

<Tabs>

<Tab label="Via OVHcloud Control Panel">

You can unsubscribe from your service logs in two ways:

1. From the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/), navigate to the `Account Logs` section via the `Identity, Security & Operations` menu.

   Once there, select the tab for the type of logs you want to unsubscribe from: `Audit logs`, `Activity logs`, or `Access policy logs`.

   Click `Unsubscribe`.

   ![manager\_accountLogs\_auditTab\_activeSubscription.png](/images/manage-and-operate/api/generating-ovhcloud-account-logs-with-logs-data-platform/53119940830611.png)

2) From the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/), navigate to the `Logs Data Platform` section via the `Identity, Security & Operations` menu.

   Once there, select your service and the `Data stream` tab.

   Click the *more options* `...` button to the right of your stream and choose `Manage subscriptions`.

   Click the *trash* `🗑️` icon to the right of any subscription you wish to delete.

   ![manager\_LDP\_datastreamTab\_manageSubscriptions.png](/images/manage-and-operate/api/generating-ovhcloud-account-logs-with-logs-data-platform/53119953234195.png)

</Tab>

<Tab label="Via OVHcloud API">

The three following Logs Data Platform API routes respectively allow you to:

- Retrieve a list of `subscriptionId`s associated with a specific logs stream based on its `streamId`.
  <Api version="v1" section="/dbaas/logs" method="GET" route={"/dbaas/logs/\{serviceName\}/output/graylog/stream/\{streamId\}/subscription"} regions={["us"]} />

* Retrieve the information (such as the *resource type*, in this case **account-api**, **account-iam** and **account-audit**, and *resource name* – the name of the OVHcloud account) of the service associated with the subscription based on its `subscriptionId`.
  <Api version="v1" section="/dbaas/logs" method="GET" route={"/dbaas/logs/\{serviceName\}/output/graylog/stream/\{streamId\}/subscription/\{subscriptionId\}"} regions={["us"]} />

- Delete a subscription based on its `subscriptionId`.
  <Api version="v1" section="/dbaas/logs" method="DELETE" route={"/dbaas/logs/\{serviceName\}/output/graylog/stream/\{streamId\}/subscription/\{subscriptionId\}"} regions={["us"]} />
