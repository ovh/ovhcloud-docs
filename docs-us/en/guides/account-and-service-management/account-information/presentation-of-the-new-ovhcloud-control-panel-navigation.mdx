# Presentation of the new OVHcloud Control Panel navigation

## Requirements

- Access to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/)

 

## Instructions

### How do I access the Beta version of OVHcloud Control Panel?

To access this version of the OVHcloud Control Panel, toggle to the `Beta version` option at the top of your dashboard.

![controlPanel\_turnOnBeta\*.png](/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/presentation-of-the-new-ovhcloud-control-panel-navigation/40735771065619.png)

 

### How do I access my account?

Access your account management at any time by clicking on your `name` in the top right-hand corner of the OVHcloud Control Panel.

![controlPanel\_accountOptions\*.png](/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/presentation-of-the-new-ovhcloud-control-panel-navigation/40735771072403.png)

 

### How do I manage my account and my information?

Click your name to update your profile or payment methods.

The same menu gives you quick access to your invoices, orders, and support tickets.

![controlPanel\_accountMenu\*.png](/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/presentation-of-the-new-ovhcloud-control-panel-navigation/40735771072915.png)

### How do I access my services?

The menu for accessing OVHcloud services is now displayed on the left-hand side of the OVHcloud Control Panel. All OVHcloud services are accessible here, so you can easily supplement your solution with additional, adapted services.

The services you have are indicated by a blue chip1.

![controlPanel\_accessServices.png](/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/presentation-of-the-new-ovhcloud-control-panel-navigation/40735771073939.png)

 

### Additional shortcuts

In the bottom left-hand corner of the OVHcloud Control Panel, some shortcuts enable you to find out more about operating your services and stay informed about the status of your services in real time.

![controlPanel\_shortcuts\*.png](/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/presentation-of-the-new-ovhcloud-control-panel-navigation/40735771075347.png)
