# Securing my OVHcloud account and managing my personal information

**Learn about best practices for managing your account and personal data.**

Your data and services are mainly managed via the OVHcloud Control Panel. It is therefore essential to adopt best practices for managing your account to secure and maintain access to it.

You can also use the OVHcloud Control Panel to manage your personal data and delegate access to your services to other users.

 

## Requirements

- an [OVHcloud account](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001286870)
- access to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/)

:::info

If you experience any difficulties logging in to your account, please read the “[What do I do if I can't log in?](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/27647266084883#login-failure)” section of our guide on [Logging in to the OVHcloud Control Panel](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/27647266084883).

:::

## Instructions

### OVHcloud account management best practices

The best practices listed below should be applied as soon as you create your OVHcloud account, to optimize security for your services and data.

 

#### Creating a strong, unique password

The security of your services and data depends on the efficiency of your password. It must be **complex, unique, and regularly renewed**.

You can find all of our password recommendations in our guide on [Setting and managing an account password](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/19376441290131).

 

#### Saving your password

If you have not already done so, save your password in a [password manager](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/19376441290131#use-a-password-manager).

:::info

The **KeePass** and **Bitwarden** software are free and open-source password managers.

:::

 

#### Adding a backup email address

If you experience any issues accessing your primary email address, a backup email address will help you retain access to your account, and manage your services as a result.\
This second address **must be different** from the first, in particular, to facilitate your procedures in case of password recovery.

To enter this backup email address, log in to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/), `click your name` in the top right-hand corner, then `click on your initials`.

Then, in the **My profile** box, click the `Edit my profile` button.

![MyAccount\_OCP\_GS\_loggingIn02\*.png](/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/securing-my-ovhcloud-account-and-managing-my-personal-information/27653446952851.png)

 

:::warning

**NOTE:** As a reminder, the backup email address must be **different** than the account’s main email address.\
Avoid using email addresses linked to a domain name that you manage from your Control Panel. If your domain is blocked, you will no longer receive our notifications.

:::

 

#### Enabling two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication increases security for your OVHcloud account.

You can find all the details to enable it in our guide on [Securing your OVHcloud account with two-factor authentication](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/27649264348947).

:::info

Enabling two-factor authentication on your OVHcloud account is a mandatory requirement for compliance with the [PCI-DSS certification](https://us.ovhcloud.com/enterprise/certification-conformity/pci-dss/), but is **strongly recommended for any business**.

:::

 

### How to manage your personal information

#### Changing your personal details

To enter this backup email address, log in to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/), `click your name` in the top right-hand corner, then `click on your initials`.

Then, in the **My profile** box, click the `Edit my profile` button.

![MyAccount\_OCP\_GS\_loggingIn02\*.png](/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/securing-my-ovhcloud-account-and-managing-my-personal-information/27653446952851.png)

 

Edit your details, then click `Confirm`.

:::warning

**NOTE:** It is imperative to keep your details **up-to-date** and **accurate**. You will be asked to provide your details when you get in touch with the OVHcloud support team. There may be instances where you are required to provide proof that your details are accurate (such as proof that your address is identical to the address detailed on the account. This may be requested when you follow the email address change procedure).

:::

 

#### Changing your primary email address

If you would like to change your primary contact email address, you will need to validate it using the **link and the code** sent to your current primary contact email address. Once you have received the code, click the `I have received my validation code` button.

![MyAccount\_OCP\_GS\_loggingIn03\*\*.png](/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/securing-my-ovhcloud-account-and-managing-my-personal-information/27655619004307.png)

 

Next, enter the code into the `Validation code` section, and click `Confirm`.

![MyAccount\_OCP\_GS\_loggingIn04\*\*.png](/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/securing-my-ovhcloud-account-and-managing-my-personal-information/27655626166675.png)

 

#### Changing the account type

Click on the `Type of account` button when you edit your profile. A drop-down menu allows you to define the type of account corresponding to your situation.

![identifiant client](/images/account-and-service-management/account-information/securing-my-ovhcloud-account-and-managing-my-personal-information/account-and-service-management-account-information-all-about-username-images-nic.png)

Once you have entered this information, click the `Save changes` button at the bottom of the screen.

 

#### Exercising your personal data rights

For information regarding your personal data and information, please view our [Exercising Your Privacy Rights](https://us.ovhcloud.com/legal/privacy-rights/) page.

 

#### Deleting your account

You can request for your account and all of the data associated with it to be deleted by [contacting our customer support team](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001819764).

To delete your account, you will need to meet the following conditions:

- Your account must no longer contain any services.

- Your account must not have any outstanding bills or unpaid orders.

 

### Managing access to your account

We advise you to never share your account details.

However, you may find that you need to delegate third-party access to your OVHcloud services. The third parties could be members of your company (for example a webmaster or a proxy), or independent entities (for example an accounting firm or IT developers).

In this scenario, OVHcloud enables you to grant access to your account while protecting your credentials (password and two-factor authentication).

 

#### Creating users

A user can have read or write access to your account, or all of your services. You can create, manage, and delete these users via the OVHcloud Control Panel. To find out more, please refer to our [guide on user management](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/19071154516115).

 

## What to do in the event of identity theft

If you receive an email notifying you of an unknown connection to your OVHcloud account, we advise [changing your account password immediately](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/19376441290131#change-your-password) and [securing it with two-factor authentication](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001825710).

If you observe any suspicious activity on your account, please inform our support team by creating a support request in your [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/).
