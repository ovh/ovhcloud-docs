# Best practices for managing your OVHcloud services and account

This guide contains the essential elements for managing your account and the billing of your OVHcloud services.

 

## Requirements

- Access to your [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/)
- Active OVHcloud services in your OVHcloud account

 

## Instructions

### Account settings

#### Account security

[Create a strong password](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/19376441290131) that will ensure your OVHcloud account's privacy.

[Enable two-factor authentication](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001825710) to prevent any fraudulent access.

For the security measures to be efficient, make sure your email addresses (main and backup) and your mobile number are **up-to-date and accessible**.

 

#### Personal information

Your personal information (postal address, email address, etc.) must be up-to-date in your customer account to guarantee the security and proper functioning of your services.

You can check your personal information in the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/) by clicking on your `name` in the top right-hand corner and then your `Access my account`.

![controlPanel\_accountMenu\*.png](/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/best-practices-for-managing-your-ovhcloud-services-and-account/42619169451027.png)

In the **My profile** panel, select `Edit my profile`.

![manager\_myAccout\_gneneralInformationTab.png](/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/best-practices-for-managing-your-ovhcloud-services-and-account/42619169452691.png)

For more information, read our [Securing my OVHcloud account and managing my personal information](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/27649264348947) guide.

Ensure your email address is always **up to date, accessible, and secured**. Email messages from our services will be sent to this address, including the ones meant to reset your Control Panel's password.

:::warning

**NOTE:** Add a **backup email** to your personal information to prevent any loss of access to your [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/).

:::

 

#### Automatic renewal

:::info

To prevent any service interruption and for the good functioning of the auto-renewal, register a payment method in your [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/).

Check regularly that your payment method is **valid**.

:::

For more information, please follow the instructions of these guides:

- [Managing commitments in the OVHcloud Control Panel](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406907355155)
- [Managing payment methods](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001824230)

If you decide not to activate the auto-renewal on your services, you will receive notifications prompting you to make a **manual payment** to either renew your service in advance or pay your pending bill.

 

### Order follow-up

Did you know that you can [track your orders](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408994807827) in your [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/)?

Just click the OVHcloud logo at the top-left corner of your screen, then click on `View my orders →`.

You will find out the steps of delivery and you will know if actions are required from your side.

For more information on the ordering process, see [this guide](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/29332308749587).

 

### Service cancellation

If you want to stop using one of your services, you can either cancel it or delete it immediately through your [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/).

For instructions to cancel services, consult the [How to cancel your OVHcloud services](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/39669113388563) guide.

 

### Account closure

If you want to close your OVHcloud personal account, first check that it does not contain any active services, pending invoices, or ongoing orders.\
Then, [submit a request](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001819764) to our support teams to ask for the permanent deletion of your account.
