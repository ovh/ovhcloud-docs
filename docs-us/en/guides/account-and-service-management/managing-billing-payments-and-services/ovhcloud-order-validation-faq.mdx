# OVHcloud order validation FAQ

**Learn about the OVHcloud order validation process and what to do if your order requires further action.**

The order validation process is meant to ensure authorized access, prevent fraudulent activity, and keep our Cloud safe.

:::warning

**NOTE:** Any further information we request is intended to **confirm the information we have already collected from you** in some form. This process helps ensure that we can maximize the resources available to our customers. See section 2(b) of our [Terms of Service](https://us.ovhcloud.com/legal/terms-of-service/) for more detailed information.

:::

 

## Requirements

- an [OVHcloud customer account](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001286870)
- at least one order placed

 

## Instructions

### Viewing your order's status

Once you place an order, you can view the status from the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/) by:

1. Clicking your `name` in the upper-right corner and then your `initials` on the right-hand pop-out menu.
2. Selecting `My orders`.
3. Looking at the **Status** column.

If your order seems stuck in the **Validation** status, it means that your order is being reviewed and we will contact you directly if further information is required.

To learn about other order statuses, see [this guide](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408994807827).

![account\_billing\_validation01\*.png](/images/account-and-service-management/managing-billing-payments-and-services/ovhcloud-order-validation-faq/29332329332243.png)

 

### Handling validation requests

If your order cannot be verified, it will change from a **Confirmation** status to an **Awaiting documents** status and an email will be sent to you with details as to why your order cannot be delivered. The subject line of this email will be:

`ACTION REQUIRED - Order Pending: [Order xxxxxxxx]`

**WARNING:** Please check the spam folder of the email address used for your OVHcloud Control Panel account.

Below is an *example* of the email you will receive. 

> Hello,
>
> Thank you for your recent order (xxxxxxx) with OVHcloud.
>
> To process your order, we need some additional information. This information is used solely for this purpose and is deleted after its use, per our Privacy Policy ([https://us.ovhcloud.com/legal/privacy-policy#use](https://us.ovhcloud.com/legal/privacy-policy#use "Follow link")).
>
> Please provide full-color photos of the following using the OVHcloudShare app (see instructions below):
>
> - A government-issued photo ID
> - A picture of the credit card used in the transaction including:\
>   1\. The name matching the listed name in the Manager account, AND\
>   2\. The last 4 digits of the card number
> - A photo of yourself holding the government-issued Photo ID provided above.
>
> Please note that this order can expire if the requested documentation is not received within 48 hours of this request. You may upload the files to the following address: [https://files.us.ovhcloud.com](https://files.us.ovhcloud.com/ "Follow link").
>
> Please respond to this email to confirm your successful upload of the documents. For additional instruction in using OVHcloudShare, please review our guide: [https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500003372301-How-to-Use-OVHcloudShare](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500003372301-How-to-Use-OVHcloudShare "Follow link"). After we review the information you provide, we will validate the transaction and approve delivery/provisioning.
>
> We thank you for your understanding, your cooperation in this matter, and for choosing OVHcloud.

 

From here, as the email states, you will have to [upload your supporting documentation](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500003372301) and reply to the validation email.

:::info

Replying to the email is the best way to receive updates throughout the validation process and resolve any issues specific to your case.

:::

 

### How we handle your personal information

All personal identifying information we collect from you is immediately destroyed after validation is complete. To read more about your personal data, see our [Privacy Policy](https://us.ovhcloud.com/legal/privacy-policy/).

 

### FAQ

<details>
<summary>How long do I have to submit the requested documents?</summary>

You have approximately 48 hours to submit the requested documentation before the order will be canceled.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Will I be charged before validation is complete?</summary>

When you submit an order, the amount will be preauthorized on the payment method you provided. If the order validation fails, the preauthorization will be reversed.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is this required for all of my future orders?</summary>

Possibly. We will contact you directly if validation is needed again. There are circumstances other than orders for which validation may be requested.

</details>

<details>
<summary>If I don't want to submit these items, will I receive a refund?</summary>

Yes, the preauthorization will be reversed.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What should I do if the card name doesn't match?</summary>

We will review your order and contact you if more information is required.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What should I do if I use PayPal or a digital credit card?</summary>

We will review your order and contact you if more information is required.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How can I obtain updates on this process?</summary>

If you have replied to the initial email sent to you, that email thread is the best way to receive any updates. You can also [monitor the status of your order](#h_01HHCW0YQHV5PJT4AGXGX2R4CY) in the OVHcloud Control Panel.

Either way, you will be notified of the result of the validation process

</details>

<details>
<summary>I've uploaded all the documents. Now what?</summary>

Confirm that you have uploaded the documents by replying to the initial email.

</details>
