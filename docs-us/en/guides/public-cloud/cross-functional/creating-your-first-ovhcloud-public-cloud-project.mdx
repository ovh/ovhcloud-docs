# Creating your first OVHcloud Public Cloud project

|                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Free trial:** Create your first Public Cloud project and get **$200** free credit\[1]. [Get started now!](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/#/public-cloud/) |

 

## Requirements

- Access to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://manager.us.ovhcloud.com/)

 

## Instructions

### Initiate project creation

Log in to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://manager.us.ovhcloud.com/) and go to the `Public Cloud` section.

On the Public Cloud home page, click `Create your first OVH Public Cloud project`.

![orderpubliccloud01.png](/images/public-cloud/cross-functional/creating-your-first-ovhcloud-public-cloud-project/18271880277907.png)

Fill in the **Project name** field and click the `Create my project` button to proceed.

![orderpubliccloud02.png](/images/public-cloud/cross-functional/creating-your-first-ovhcloud-public-cloud-project/18271900891283.png)

 

### Complete your order

You will be redirected to the OVHcloud website to complete your order.

Click `Continue` to proceed.

:::warning

**NOTE:** As part of our anti-fraud check, the "Order summary" page lists a $0.99 charge for the first project added to an account. The charge will be a credit that gets applied to your first bill. Any additional projects added to an account will not incur this initial charge and will simply be charged according to resource consumption.

:::

On the next page:

1. Confirm your billing address and contact information.
2. Accept the Terms and Service and Privacy Policy.
3. Add or confirm your payment method.
4. Click `Pay`.

By default, the available resources for a project are 20 instances (servers), 20 vCores (CPU), and 40 GB of RAM. An increase in the resource limit may be requested by [submitting a ticket](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/dedicated/index.html#/ticket) to our Customer Support team.

:::tip

**Congratulations!** You have created your first public cloud project!

:::
