# Databases & Analytics - Getting started

**Learn how to order a database or analytics solution in the OVHcloud Control Panel.**

Cloud Databases and Analytics[\*](#go-further) services allow you to focus on building and deploying cloud applications while OVHcloud takes care of the service infrastructure and maintenance.

 

## Requirements

- Access to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/#/hub)
- A [Public Cloud project](https://us.ovhcloud.com/public-cloud/) in your OVHcloud account

 

## Instructions

From the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/#/hub), navigate to the `Databases` section by clicking the `Public Cloud` menu and selecting your Public Cloud project.

:::info

- Selecting `Data Streaming` will give you access to the [Kafka®](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/21760884547091), [Kafka® Connect](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/21883484919059), and [Kafka® MirrorMaker](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/21884355100819) services.
- Selecting `Data Analysis` will give you access to [Dashboards](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/21949690066323) and [OpenSearch™](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/21890623420947) services.1

:::

Click on the button `Create a managed database` (click `Create a service` if your project already contains services.)

![manager\_database\_onboardingPage.png](/images/public-cloud/databases/databases-analytics-getting-started/41875092541203.png)

 

### Step 1: Select your engine type

Click on the type of engine you want to use and then (if available) select the version to install from the respective drop-down menu.

![manager\_database\_order\_databaseTypes.png](/images/public-cloud/databases/databases-analytics-getting-started/41875072765459.png)

 

### Step 2: Select a service plan

Choose an appropriate service plan. You will be able to upgrade the plan after creation.

Please visit the [capabilities page](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/sections/20348597107603-Public-Cloud-Databases) of your selected engine type for detailed information on each plan's properties.

![manager\_database\_order\_servicePlans.png](/images/public-cloud/databases/databases-analytics-getting-started/41875092542227.png)

 

### Step 3: Select a region

Choose the geographical region of the data center in which your service will be hosted.

![manager\_database\_order\_regions.png](/images/public-cloud/databases/databases-analytics-getting-started/41875092543251.png)

 

### Step 4: Node type

You can choose the node template in this step.

Please visit the [capabilities page](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/sections/20348597107603-Public-Cloud-Databases) of your selected engine type for detailed information on the hardware resources and other properties of the engine installation.

Take note of the pricing information.

![manager\_database\_order\_nodeType.png](/images/public-cloud/databases/databases-analytics-getting-started/41875092544019.png)

 

### Step 5: Cluster sizing

Additional storage can be ordered and, depending on the engine, the number of nodes in your cluster can be adjusted:

![manager\_database\_order\_clusterSizing.png](/images/public-cloud/databases/databases-analytics-getting-started/41875072773267.png)

 

### Step 6: Configure your options

Choose your network configuration:

![manager\_database\_order\_publicNetwork.png](/images/public-cloud/databases/databases-analytics-getting-started/41875092548115.png)

 

#### Connecting a private network (optional)

If you already have a private subnet available, check the box **Private** and select it from the drop-down menu. Note that this option might not be available for the selected service type.

You can be forwarded to create a private network or subnet by clicking on the respective links. You will have to start the ordering process over in that case.

Please follow [this guide](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002093130) for detailed instructions.

![manager\_database\_order\_privateNetwork.png](/images/public-cloud/databases/databases-analytics-getting-started/41875072776723.png)

 

### Step 7: Summary and confirmation

The panel on the right side of the page will display a summary of your order.

![manager\_database\_order\_summary.png](/images/public-cloud/databases/databases-analytics-getting-started/41875072778387.png)

Within a few minutes, your new service will be deployed. Messages in the OVHcloud Control Panel will inform you when the service is ready to use.

Continue with the *Configure your instance to accept incoming connections&#x20;*&#x67;uide of your selected engine type available [here](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/sections/20348597107603-Public-Cloud-Databases) to configure your service after installation.

Note that the configuration options might be different, depending on the engine type. You will find an example in this repository: [https://github.com/ovh/public-cloud-databases-examples](https://github.com/ovh/public-cloud-databases-examples).
