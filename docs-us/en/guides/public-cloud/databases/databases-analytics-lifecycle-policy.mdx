# Databases & Analytics - Lifecycle policy

**Learn about the OVHcloud policy for major versions of engines provided in Cloud Databases and Analytics.**

Cloud Databases and Analytics[\*](#go-further) allow you to focus on building and deploying cloud applications while OVHcloud takes care of the database infrastructure and maintenance. Each service is powered by third-party database management systems (DBMS), maintained by third-party companies or communities such as PostgreSQL® community, Oracle, MongoDB®, Apache Foundation, Apache Kafka®, Dashboard, OpenSearch™, and others.

 

## Lifecycle policy

This policy is being provided to help customers understand the lifecycle of OVHcloud Cloud Databases and Analytics, to anticipate and prepare transitions to newer versions.

**End of Life (EOL)** refers to the deadline after which affected Cloud Databases and Analytics services are deprecated and will no longer be supported or maintained.

OVHcloud aims to follow the EOL schedule set by the original authors and maintainers of the databases and analytics engines, also known as upstream projects. Once the upstream project retires a specific version, it no longer receives security updates or critical bug fixes.

Continued use of outdated services means that they no longer offer our customers the level of protection their business needs. Therefore, by following the upstream project's EOL schedule, we ensure that OVHcloud services are always running on supported versions of the DBMS software.

 

### Service coverage

This lifecycle policy applies to:

- All Cloud Databases and Analytics services;
- Including all the service plans (Essential, Business, Enterprise, Discovery, Production);
- And all proposed database and analytics engines, such as MySQL®, PostgreSQL®, MongoDB®, Kafka®, Dashboards, etc.;
- Whatever state; if they are **up and running** or in a **sleeping state** (powered off, waiting for payment).

 

### Providing new major versions

Once a new major version is considered stable to use, OVHcloud will do its best to provide it to our customers as soon as we can. Since many factors can come into play, we cannot provide an exact timeline or commitment.

 

### Prior EOL

When OVHcloud defines the EOL date for a service major version:

- Customers will receive an EOL email announcement via their main contact point (account admin);
- The OVHcloud Control Panel will also show an EOL notification for affected services;
- Email reminders may also be sent to customers.

 

### Upon EOL

Once the EOL date is reached:

- Affected **running services** will be **automatically upgraded to the latest available version**. For example, if the latest version is PostgreSQL 13, then upon PostgreSQL 9.6 EOL, it will be upgraded to the latest PostgreSQL 13 instead of PostgreSQL 10, 11, or 12.
- Affected **sleeping state** (powered-off) services **will no longer be accessible and their backups will be deleted**.

 

### Potential impacts

New versions can introduce minor changes but also breaking changes, leading to malfunctions in your code and applications. A specific feature might not work properly, or your entire website or application can become non-functional and unavailable. **These impacts cannot be predicted by OVHcloud** and customers are responsible for their own code. We recommend browsing the official documentation of the engine to review the newly introduced features and/or deprecated ones.

 

## Recommendations for upgrades

We highly recommend performing the version upgrade well before EOL so that you can test compatibility for any breaking changes, plan for unforeseen issues, and migrate to the newer version at your own schedule.

Cloud Databases and Analytics offer database forking (a copy) as an efficient tool to verify the version upgrade so that you can safely test compatibility without committing your production services to a one-way upgrade.

To perform a fork, navigate to the "Overview" page of your service, and scroll down until you see a "New database fork" (or "New service fork") button. This will allow you to make a separate new database service that is cloned from the current one's backups.

### Dashboard

### Kafka®

Cloud Databases for Kafka® *major.minor* version will reach EOL approximately one year after it's made available on Cloud Databases. For example:

Kafka® versions, features, and bug fixes can be viewed on their [official downloads page](https://kafka.apache.org/downloads).

 

### MongoDB®

MongoDB® EOL will coincide with the [official MongoDB® Lifecycle schedule](https://www.mongodb.com/legal/support-policy/lifecycles).

 

### MySQL®

### OpenSearch™

OpenSearch™ EOL will coincide with the [official OpenSearch™ versioning](https://opensearch.org/docs/latest/version-history/).

 

### PostgreSQL®

PostgreSQL® EOL will coincide with the [official PostgreSQL® versioning policy](https://www.postgresql.org/support/versioning/).

 

### Valkey

Valkey EOL will coincide with the [official Valkey Community Releases policy](https://valkey.io/topics/releases).
