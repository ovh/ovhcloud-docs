# Dashboards - Configure your Dashboards instance to accept incoming connections

**Learn how to configure your Dashboards instance to accept incoming connections.**

Cloud Analytics allow you to focus on building and deploying cloud applications while OVHcloud takes care of the engine infrastructure and maintenance in operational conditions.

 

## Requirements

- A [Public Cloud project](https://us.ovhcloud.com/public-cloud/) in your OVHcloud account
- Access to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/)
- A Dashboards service running on your OVHcloud Cloud Analytics ([this guide](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/20611621210515) can help you to meet this requirement)

 

## Instructions

### Configure your Dashboards instance to accept incoming connections

Before making a connection, we need to verify that our Dashboards instance is correctly configured.

From the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/), navigate to your Dashboards instance via the `Public Cloud` and `Data Analysis` menus.

 

#### Step 1: Verify your user roles and password

Select the `Users` tab. Click *more options* `...` to the right of your user and select `Reset the password`.

![dashboards\_usersTab\_moreOptions.png](/images/public-cloud/databases/dashboards-configure-your-dashboards-instance-to-accept-incoming-connections/43105960110995.png)

 

#### Step 2: Authorize incoming connections to your Dashboards instance

:::warning

**NOTE:** For security reasons, the default network configuration doesn't allow any incoming connections. It is thus critical that you authorize suitable IP addresses to successfully access your service.

:::

In this step, select the `Configuration` tab. IP addresses must be authorized here before they can connect to your service.

In the **IPs** panel, enter the information for the IP you wish to authorize, then click the *add* `+` button and `Save changes`.

![dashabords\_configurationTab.png](/images/public-cloud/databases/dashboards-configure-your-dashboards-instance-to-accept-incoming-connections/43105975745811.png)

You can remove access via the *delete* `⛔️` button.

:::info

If you want to allow connections from the outside, you can enter the IP 0.0.0.0/0. Please use it carefully.

:::

 

### Connect to your Dashboards instance

From the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/), navigate to your Dashboards instance via the `Public Cloud` and `Data Analysis` menus.

In the `Dashboard` tab, retrieve the URI for your service from the **Connection information** panel.

![dashboards\_dashboardTab.png](/images/public-cloud/databases/dashboards-configure-your-dashboards-instance-to-accept-incoming-connections/43105960123795.png)

Then connect to your Dashboards instance:

![DBaaS\_grafana\_acceptconnections03.png](/images/public-cloud/databases/dashboards-configure-your-dashboards-instance-to-accept-incoming-connections/21949917056147.png)

:::tip

**Congratulations!** You are now connected to your Grafana[®](#go-further) instance.

:::

![DBaaS\_grafana\_acceptconnections04.png](/images/public-cloud/databases/dashboards-configure-your-dashboards-instance-to-accept-incoming-connections/21949853906067.png)

Learn more about Grafana® in the following tutorial: [Grafana® fundamentals](https://grafana.com/tutorials/grafana-fundamentals/?utm_source=grafana_gettingstarted).
