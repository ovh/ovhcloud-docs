# Databases & Analytics - Resize your service storage

**Learn how to adjust the storage space of your Cloud Database or Analytics service according to your needs.**

:::info

This feature is only available for engines that use storage space.

:::

 

## How to increase or decrease storage

This option is available through the OVHcloud [API](https://api.us.ovhcloud.com/) and the [Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/).

:::info

When changing the storage space of your service, the system checks that at least 20% of your total storage space will remain free after the resize operation. As a consequence, you might not be able to perform a storage space decrease if the new size you requested is too close to your current disk space usage.

:::

 

### How to adjust the storage space using the Control Panel

#### Requirements

- Access to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/)
- A [Public Cloud project](https://us.ovhcloud.com/public-cloud/) in your OVHcloud account

 

#### Instructions

To adjust the storage space of your cluster, log in to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/), navigate to the `Databases` (or `Data Streaming` or `Data Analysis`, depending on your service) area via the `Public Cloud` menu.

Then select your engine.

Select the `Configuration` tab, and look at the **Service** panel. Next to **Storage**, click `Edit`.

![Manager database configuration tab](/images/public-cloud/databases/resize-service-storage/manager-database-configuration-tab.png)

Use the `slider` to adjust the storage space of your cluster, then click `Edit`.

![Manager database edit](/images/public-cloud/databases/resize-service-storage/manager-database-edit.png)

 

### How to adjust the storage space using the API

#### Requirements

- Access to the OVHcloud [API](https://api.us.ovhcloud.com/) (create your credentials by consulting [this guide](/guides/manage-and-operate/api/first-steps))
- A [Public Cloud project](https://us.ovhcloud.com/public-cloud/) in your OVHcloud account

 

#### Instructions

As an example, for PostgreSQL[®](#go-further) services, you can perform a storage space increase or decrease using this endpoint:

<Api version="v1" section="/cloud/project" method="PUT" route={"/cloud/project/\{serviceName\}/database/postgresql/\{clusterId\}"} regions={["us"]} />

Other engines using a storage space provide similar endpoints, e.g.:

<Api version="v1" section="/cloud/project" method="PUT" route={"/cloud/project/\{serviceName\}/database/kafka/\{clusterId\}"} regions={["us"]} />

:::warning

**NOTE:** Use the "storage" field if available. If not, then the "disk" field will work.

:::
