### Overall

<details>
<summary>What are OVHcloud Cloud Databases and Analytics?</summary>

Cloud Databases and Analytics is a managed service allowing you to deploy and use database or analytics instances easily.

We provide cost-efficient services and take care of the infrastructure. We provide, by design, various features such as automatic backups, software updates, public or private networks, observability tools such as logs and metrics, and end-to-end security mechanisms.

Our Database and Analytics services can be reached via Public network (Internet) or Private network (vRack), allowing you multiple use cases - connect with OVHcloud Public Cloud instances or Kubernetes clusters, OVHcloud Bare Metal, Hosted Private Cloud, OVHcloud VPS, etc. - but also allowing for Hybrid Cloud as long as you can use the public network (Internet).

</details>

<details>
<summary>What are the supported Databases and Analytics services?</summary>

We are adding more and more Databases and Analytics over time. Please find the list and proposed versions in our [Databases & Analytics - Capabilities and Limitations](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/20364077114643) guide.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I connect Databases and Analytics services to other OVHcloud products?</summary>

Yes. Databases and Analytics services can be connected to a public network (Internet) or a private network (vRack).

You can then connect them to any product compliant with these network topologies:

- Public network: any OVHcloud Cloud product.
- Private network (vRack): Bare Metal Cloud, Hosted Private Cloud, Public Cloud Instances, Public Cloud managed Kubernetes clusters.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I connect Databases and Analytics services to third-party services?</summary>

Yes. You can reach your Databases and Analytics services with a public network (Internet). You can then connect a third-party service, for example, another Cloud provider or on-premises infrastructure.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where can I find the product roadmap?</summary>

We expose our roadmap publicly on [GitHub](https://github.com/orgs/ovh/projects/16/views/5?filterQuery=main-product%3A%22Managed+Databases%22%2C%22Data+Platform%22%2C%22Data+Processing%22%2C%22Data+Integration%22). You can filter by a label such as "Databases".

</details>

 

### Service plans and instances

<details>
<summary>What are the major differences between Essential/Discovery, Business/Production, and Enterprise plans?</summary>

We built service plans based on business use cases. They have major differentiators related to each DBMS.

Overall, we designed three service plans with these usages in mind:

- Essential/Discovery: A perfect fit for testing, proof-of-concept, and development. Not for production purposes, mainly due to the lack of SLA, High Resiliency via clustering, or Private Network.
- Business/Production: First level for development or production infrastructure. High resiliency is provided with SLA.
- Enterprise: The highest level of services, with all the features and a better SLA.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is managed by OVHcloud on my behalf?</summary>

OVHcloud manages various tasks on your behalf:

- Setup: We are providing you with an engine instance, with state-of-the-art installation, meaning that we take care of the provisioning, initial setup of the Operating System, and engine software.
- Once your engine is running: Based on your service plan, we perform administrative tasks such as daily backups, updating the software, and providing security tools for authentication.

Still, since we provide engine access, as a customer, you are responsible for managing the services:

- Configuring the settings based on your use cases, including network, security, and backups.
- Defining your relational or NoSQL data schemas that will fit your needs.
- Performance tuning

</details>

<details>
<summary>How many DB instances can I run?</summary>

OVHcloud Public Cloud allows you to create projects, and then you will have quotas and billing per project. Quotas are visible in your OVHcloud Control Panel; they depend on your history. The default quota is limited to 20 instances per project.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I get started?</summary>

Cloud Databases and Analytics require a Public Cloud project. Once a project is created, you can start engine instances via the OVHcloud Control Panel in just a few minutes.

Please read our [Getting Started with Cloud Databases and Analytics](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/20611621210515) guide.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I import data into an engine instance?</summary>

We use official and un-modified (vanilla) versions of engines, allowing you to rely on official documentation for each engine to import data to Databases and Analytics. For example, for PostgreSQL®, you can use pg\_dump and pg\_restore, and for MySQL®, you can use mysqldump and mysqlimport.

Please refer to the official website for each engine's documentation:

- [MongoDB®](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/manual/)
- [MySQL®](https://dev.mysql.com/doc/)
- [PostgreSQL®](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/)
- [Valkey](https://valkey.io/docs/)

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I cancel my database or analytics instance?</summary>

From the OVHcloud Control Panel, navigate to the `Databases` (or `Data Streaming` or `Data Analytics`) section via the `Public Cloud` menu. Select the *more options* `...` button to the right of the instance you wish to cancel, and choose `Delete`.

![manager\_database\_serviceList\_moreOptions.png](/images/public-cloud/databases/databases-analytics-faq/42093680625427.png)

</details>

 

### Software and versions

<details>
<summary>What are the proposed engine versions?</summary>

We are adding more and more Databases and Analytics engines over time. Please find the list and proposed versions in our [Databases & Analytics - Capabilities and Limitations](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/20364077114643) guide.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is a minor and a major version?</summary>

Each software provides newer versions of its code, usually including bug fixes, security enhancements, and improvements.

In general, a minor version upgrade includes only changes that are backward-compatible with existing applications. In contrast, a major version can bring incompatibilities.

Each engine acts differently, and its official documentation is the source of truth.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How can I select the engine version during the engine instance creation?</summary>

You can create a Database or Analytics instance via API or the OVHcloud Control Panel. In both cases, you can select various parameters such as engine type and versions.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I manually update my engine version?</summary>

Yes, when all the required conditions are met:

- when your service is up and running,
- when another engine version is available, and
- when the engine allows it.

If all the conditions are validated, you can perform the update directly via API or the OVHcloud Control Panel.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What are the risks and my responsibilities for Databases and Analytics updates?</summary>

An update of an engine must always be tested, and a customer should follow the engine's official best practices. Usually, new major versions introduce new features and deprecate a few others. Based on your usage, it could lead to an outage of your application code.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I test a new version before upgrading?</summary>

Yes. Once you have a Database or Analytics instance up and running, you can restore a backup as a new instance (Fork).

During this process, you can select various options, such as another version of your engine. Once you have verified the correct usage of your new services, you can either keep them or delete them.

Since we provide hourly billing, this operation can be repeated as many times as you need (and without a strong billing increase).

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens when I use a deprecated version of an engine on my Databases and Analytics instance?</summary>

You will be warned to upgrade your version.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What are the supported extensions for each engine?</summary>

Each engine has its own specificities for extensions. As a general rule, we only provide officially supported extensions.

Please find the list and proposed versions in our [Databases & Analytics - Capabilities and Limitations](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/20364077114643) guide.

</details>

 

### Billing

<details>
<summary>How are the services billed?</summary>

Cloud Databases and Analytics are billed with a "pay-as-you-go" model. The entire Public Cloud ecosystem allows you to create projects, attach a payment method to the project (credit card, PayPal, etc.), and finally launch various products and services inside this project.

You don't pay before using the service. At the end of the month, we summarize your spending in one bill per project. Cloud Databases and Analytics services are billed per hour, for the service range and flavor selected during instance creation.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is included in the pricing?</summary>

Cloud Databases and Analytics pricing includes:

- Setup and management of the services.
- Selected number of nodes and internal characteristics such as vCPU, RAM, and Storage.
- Ingress/Egress network traffic, based on the limits of our regional flavors.
- Backups (retention varies; see our capabilities).
- Logs and Metrics (retention varies; see our capabilities).
- When required, software licenses.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is not included in the pricing?</summary>

Our pricing does not include:

- Additional backup retention costs.
- Additional logs and metric retention costs.

</details>

<details>
<summary>When does the billing begin and end?</summary>

Each instance's billing:

- starts when the service is up and running. For example, if the service takes 5 minutes to start, you don't pay for this setup time.
- stops when you delete your service.

</details>

 

### Flavors, resiliency, and deployments

<details>
<summary>How should I select the required amount of compute, RAM, and storage?</summary>

To select the right amount of compute, RAM, and storage, you will need to assess your needs.

If you already have Databases or Analytics instances, you can check what is consumed today. For a new service, you will have to forecast your needs. Keep in mind that you can scale up or down your DB.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Once my instance is up, can I scale my service up or down?</summary>

For each instance, you can:

- Select a higher service plan if required (Essential/Discovery to Business/Production or Business/Production to Enterprise).
- Select a higher or lower range of compute and storage, provided that the new storage is enough to contain your data.

For each DB instance, you cannot:

- Select a lower service plan.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Will my DB instance remain available during scaling?</summary>

In most cases, and if your plan supports high availability, your instance will remain available during the scaling. However, the status of your service will switch to "updating", preventing you from modifying your service (e.g., adding a user).

</details>

<details>
<summary>What are the performances for each flavor?</summary>

We do not provide benchmark results so far for Cloud Databases and Analytics. Each business use case is different, and benchmarks may strongly differ compared to your real-life use cases. Just keep in mind that our performance gaps are linear.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where are my instances deployed?</summary>

During the Databases or Analytics instance creation, you can select the region and data center to deploy your service. Please find the exact list in our [Databases & Analytics - Capabilities and Limitations](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/20364077114643) guide.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How is high availability performed?</summary>

To provide high availability, we use clustering principles. Your service and your data are replicated across multiple nodes, preventing the overall system from a node failure. Your service is resilient mainly because we duplicated the data and configuration in multiple nodes.

The exact implementation may differ for each engine. Indeed, each engine has its own mechanisms and best practices.

For example:

- MongoDB® high-availability starts with 3 nodes.
- PostgreSQL® or MySQL® start with 2 nodes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why are nodes called "primary" and "replicas"?</summary>

For relational DBMS, such as MySQL® and PostgreSQL®, each node has a role:

- A primary node can accept READ and WRITE operations. It acts like the source of truth and is replicated to replica nodes.
- A replica node is a copy of the primary node from the data perspective and can accept READ operations only. Replica nodes are very helpful to achieve high availability, but also improve READ performance.

In case of a Primary Node failure, a Replica can be elected as Primary.

</details>

<details>
<summary>In case of a node failure, is failover automatic?</summary>

Yes, in case of a node failure, the failover mechanism is automatic, regardless of the DBMS. OVHcloud is taking care of the failover process; new nodes are elected and promoted if necessary.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What are the consequences of a failover for my cluster?</summary>

A failover is automatic, but the whole process can take from a few seconds up to 1 minute.

In case of a failover, your cluster will be in degraded mode:

- READ operations can be performed as usual.
- WRITE operations will be discarded until the failover process is over.

</details>

 

### Backups and maintenance

<details>
<summary>How do you perform backups?</summary>

Backups are performed differently according to the engine. See our [Databases & Analytics - Automated Backups](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/20735484294547) guide for more details.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I perform manual backups?</summary>

Yes, you can perform manual backups.

We use official and unmodified (vanilla) versions of engines, allowing you to rely on official documentation for each engine to perform backups for Cloud Databases and Analytics. For example, for PostgreSQL®, you can use pg\_dump and pg\_restore. For MySQL®, you can use mysqldump and mysqlimport.

Please refer to the official website for each engine's documentation:

- [MongoDB®](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/manual/)
- [MySQL®](https://dev.mysql.com/doc/)
- [PostgreSQL®](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/)
- [Valkey](https://valkey.io/docs/)

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I directly download backups made by OVHcloud?</summary>

No, you cannot directly download backups made by OVHcloud. But you can restore them to another service and then use official command lines to dump the data.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where are my backups stored, and for how long?</summary>

When possible, we store your encrypted backups outside the region of your service for sustainability reasons. The location of the backup is available, along with all the other information about the backup, in the API.

The period during which the backup is stored depends on the plan you choose. You can find it in our [Databases & Analytics - Automated Backups](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/20735484294547) guide.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What are my responsibilities for backups?</summary>

As a customer, you are solely responsible for making the appropriate backups for your services.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is a backup window? Will my Database or Analytics instance be available during backup events?</summary>

The backup window is the period of the day during which the backup operations are executed. Your instance will remain available.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I modify the backup window?</summary>

Yes, you can modify the backup window via the OVHcloud control panel, Terraform, or the API.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I modify the backup location?</summary>

Yes, you can. Please refer to our [Databases & Analytics - Automated Backups](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/20735484294547) guide for more details.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is a maintenance window? Will my Database or Analytics instance be available during maintenance events?</summary>

The maintenance window is the period of the day during which the maintenance operation, such as security patches, can be executed. Depending on the maintenance operation, your Database or Analytics instance will or will not be available during this event, but in most cases, your instance will be available.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I modify the maintenance window?</summary>

Yes, you can modify the maintenance window via the OVHcloud Control Panel, Terraform, or the API. See our [Databases & Analytics - Maintenance Operations](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/32150505697043#how-can-i-monitor-the-planned-maintenance) guide for more information.

</details>

 

### Security

<details>
<summary>What are the security mechanisms put in place for my Database or Analytics instance?</summary>

Each Database or Analytics instance is strongly secured through multiple actions:

- **Infrastructure**: We rely on OpenStack open-source software stack for our whole Public Cloud. We are audited by official organizations and certified with ISO27001 norms, for example.
- **Public or Private Network**: Based on your needs and your internal policies about security, you can opt for public network access (Internet) or Private Network (vRack) access.
- **Network ACL**: We provide an Access Control List (ACL) allowing you to specify which IPs or IP blocks have the right to connect to your Database or Analytics instance. By default, no ACL is declared on the service, meaning nobody can connect to your service until you add at least one.
- **End-to-End Encryption**: connections to engines are, by default, encrypted with TLS protocol, providing end-to-end encryption protection. OVHcloud generates an SSL/TLS certificate for each dB instance. Once you establish an encrypted connection between your application and your Database or Analytics instance, your data flows will be encrypted.
- **Data Encryption**: Data is stored on dedicated volumes that are encrypted.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Who can access my Database or Analytics instance initially?</summary>

Once you start a new Database or Analytics instance, the default values are:

- Zero access to IPs or IP ranges. Nothing is authorized (ACL).
- 1 user created (admin) but not configured. You have to reset the password.

Initially, your engine cannot be accessed. It's made on purpose to protect your data from unsolicited connections.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How can I manage authorized access to my Database or Analytics instance?</summary>

Once your Database or Analytics instance is created, you can manage authorized IPs or IP blocks either in the OVHcloud Control Panel or via Terraform or the API. You need at least 1 authorized IP to access your service.

Please read our [Getting Started with Cloud Databases & Analytics](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/20611621210515) guide to get step-by-step instructions.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How can I add new users to my Database or Analytics instance?</summary>

Once your Database or Analytics instance is created, you can manage users either in the OVHcloud control panel or via Terraform or the API. You need at least 1 created and configured user. Please read our [Getting Started with Cloud Databases & Analytics](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/20611621210515) guide to get step-by-step instructions.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is my data encrypted?</summary>

The volumes where the data is stored are encrypted, and the backups are also encrypted before being stored.

</details>

 

### Network

<details>
<summary>Can I connect to my Database or Analytics instance via a public network?</summary>

Yes, all Database or Analytics instances can connect to the public network. It has to be specified and selected during instance creation. If you select Public Network connectivity, you can not be connected through Private Network.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I connect to my Database or Analytics instance via a private network (vRack)?</summary>

Yes, based on the service plan, Database or Analytics instances can connect to a private network (vRack). It has to be specified and selected during instance creation. If you select Private Network connectivity, you can not be connected through the Public Network.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I move my instance from a public to a private network or vice versa?</summary>

No, you cannot. Once the networking type of an instance is set, you cannot switch to the other networking type. However, if your instance already has data and you want to move to the other networking type, you can restore your instance from a backup to a new instance with the other networking type (fork).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I move my Database or Analytics instance from one vRack to another one?</summary>

Currently, you cannot move your engine from one vRack to another. You can follow our [official roadmap](https://github.com/orgs/ovh/projects/16/views/5?filterQuery=main-product%3A%22Managed+Databases%22%2C%22Data+Platform%22%2C%22Data+Processing%22%2C%22Data+Integration%22).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is inbound traffic network included (data pushed TO your Database or Analytics instance)?</summary>

Yes, inbound network traffic is included in our prices and not metered.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is the outbound traffic network included (data pulled FROM your Database or Analytics instance)?</summary>

Yes, outbound network traffic is included in our prices and not metered. It allows you to benefit from predictive pricing without any risks of uncontrolled billing increases.

</details>

 

### Troubleshooting

<details>
<summary>I'm unable to connect to my Database or Analytics instance</summary>

Each Database or Analytics instance is secured by default.

To connect to an instance, please make sure to:

- Have a Database or Analytics instance up and running. Be sure it's not in "maintenance" or "setting up" mode.
- Have at least one user configured (user login and password).
- Have at least one authorized IP. It has to be the IP that will access your engine (your computer, your dedicated server, etc.). For troubleshooting purposes only, you can also add the "0.0.0.0" IP so that it will allow "any" connection. Remove it after your tests are done.
- Know your Database or Analytics instance connection parameters: host address, port, security mode.

Once you are ready, you can test the connection via the DBMS's official command line interface or with classic application code such as Python, PHP, Java, etc. You can find connection examples in various languages in our [Example Repository](https://github.com/ovh/public-cloud-databases-examples).

If you still have connectivity issues, please contact our support.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I need to reset my user credentials</summary>

If you forgot your user credentials or have security concerns, you can modify your user password directly via the OVHcloud Control Panel in the "Users & Roles" section. It will log out existing connections instantiated by this user.

</details>

<details>
<summary>My Database or Analytics instance is running out of storage</summary>

Each Database or Analytics instance has a limited amount of storage space. We recommend that you monitor your remaining storage space constantly.

To manage storage issues, please refer to the [Databases & Analytics - Handling "Disk Full" Situations](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/20731047296659) guide.

</details>

<details>
<summary>My queries are slow</summary>

Queries are operations performed on your Database or Analytics instances, such as READ operations, WRITE operations, or DELETE operations.

These query performances are related to many external factors:

- Your data schema. The organization of your data will strongly impact your performance.
- The amount of data to query. A query on 1 table of 1000 lines is different from a query on 20 tables of 1 million lines.
- The quality of your application code.
- The flavor performance: Each flavor has its own hardware specifications. The more RAM and vCPU you have, the more efficient queries will be.
- The heavy usage of the service (for example, legitimate traffic due to Sales actions).
- An issue with the hardware infrastructure or network.

To troubleshoot your performance, the first step is to check if the service is not affected, i.e., by a [travaux task](https://www.status-ovhcloud.com/). To benchmark a superior flavor, you can duplicate your Database or Analytics instance to a higher flavor model and test the performance again.

Overall, if you are experiencing punctual slowness, you need Database and Analytics skills and system admin skills.

</details>

<details>
<summary>My data is corrupted</summary>

If your data is corrupted, you have two options:

- If your data is important, you can restore a backup where your data is valid. You need to investigate on your own for this step.
- If your data can be deleted, you can delete the Database or Analytics instance service and create a new one, or just follow the official DBMS documentation to delete all the data.

</details>
