# AI Dashboard - Getting started

**Learn how to get started with the OVHcloud AI Dashboard, a consolidated view of your usage of the OVHcloud AI tools designed for building your machine learning projects.**

 

## Requirements

- Access to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/)
- A [Public Cloud project](https://us.ovhcloud.com/public-cloud/) in your OVHcloud account

 

## Instructions

You can access the AI Dashboard is accessible in the OVHcloud Control Panel via the `Public Cloud` and `AI Dashboard` menus.

![manager\_aiDashboard\_shortened.png](/images/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-dashboard-getting-started/40522271050131.png)

 

### Dashboard tab

In this section, you will find information related to your use of AI Tools with easy access to [AI Notebooks](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/34842392686867), [AI Training](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/35104761379219), and [AI Deploy](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/35420368295699).

:::info

**AI Dashboard** is not a product but an overview of the AI Tools you use and an interface where you will find important items such as usage, CLI, users, tokens, and guides (such as this one).

:::

The instances listed are the ones that are in the `RUNNING` and `STOPPED` statuses.

You can also easily access your [Object Storage](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/sections/23498311424275-Object-Storage).

You can find more details on the prices of the AI tools [here](https://us.ovhcloud.com/public-cloud/prices/#ai-&-machine-learning).

You can also see the number of users and tokens created to use your AI Tools.

![manager\_aiDashboard\_full.png](/images/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-dashboard-getting-started/40522251883923.png)

 

#### Command Line Interface

In this section, you will find all the information about installing, authenticating, and using our CLI. You will also find a list of all useful guides on the CLI.

 

#### Documentation

By clicking the `📖 Guides` button or typing cmd-K `⌘ K` on your keyboard, you will find a list of our guides about AI products.

 

### Users & tokens

In this section, you will find all the information about your AI users and tokens.

You can easily create new Public Cloud users with dedicated AI Tools roles (`AI Platform - Operator` or `AI Platform - Read-Only`) and access all your Public Cloud users.

![PaaS\_AI\_general\_AIdashboard03.png](/images/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-dashboard-getting-started/40522271050899.png)

 

### Tokens

You can also manage tokens (create, update, and delete) that you can use with your notebooks, jobs, or apps.

To know how to manage your users and tokens, please read the following guide: [Manage AI users and roles](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/34504142321171).

![PaaS\_AI\_general\_AIdashboard04.png](/images/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-dashboard-getting-started/40522271052947.png)

 

### Docker registries

A Docker registry allows you to store your software images so that you can easily access them during your training or deployments.

![PaaS\_AI\_general\_AIdashboard05.png](/images/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-dashboard-getting-started/40522251894291.png)

 

### Private Git repositories

Private Git repositories allow you to configure all the necessary elements for your AI products to communicate with your GIT projects.

![PaaS\_AI\_general\_AIdashboard06.png](/images/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-dashboard-getting-started/40522271054355.png)

 

### S3 datastores

Datastores allow you to configure all the necessary elements for your AI products to communicate with your S3 containers.

![PaaS\_AI\_general\_AIdashboard07.png](/images/public-cloud/ai-machine-learning/ai-dashboard-getting-started/40522271055123.png)
