# Public VCF as-a-Service - Logging in to your organization

## Requirements

- A web browser
- The welcome email for your new service

 

## Instructions

### First login

Open your web browser and go to the Public VCF as-a-Service login page. Enter your organization name (provided in the welcome email).

![Login organization - Logging](/images/hosted-private-cloud/public-vcf-aas/logging/login-organization.png)

You will now be prompted to enter your username and password in the appropriate fields (this information is provided in the welcome email).

Click the `Log in` button to access Public VCF as-a-Service.

![Login credentials - Logging](/images/hosted-private-cloud/public-vcf-aas/logging/login-credentials.png)

You will be redirected to your organization’s dashboard, which we will describe in our [user interface guide](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/public-vcf-aas/getting-started-dashboard-overview).

![Home page - Logging](/images/hosted-private-cloud/public-vcf-aas/logging/home-page.png)

 

### Change password

Once logged in, click your name in the upper-right corner of the user interface and choose the `Change Password` option from the drop-down menu.

![Interface controls user](/images/hosted-private-cloud/public-vcf-aas/logging/interface-controls-user.png)

You will be prompted to enter your old password and your new password.

Fill in the fields and click `SAVE`.

When you change your password, you should receive a confirmation email.

![Change password - Logging](/images/hosted-private-cloud/public-vcf-aas/logging/change-password.png)
