# Upgrading an on-prem Zerto environment

In this article, we will give you concise steps to upgrade your on-prem Zerto environment to work with OVHcloud's Zerto deployment.

:::warning

**NOTE:** To upgrade your on-prem Zerto instance, please contact OVHcloud customer service or your account manager.

:::

 

### Requirements

- An on-prem Zerto environment and an OVHcloud product with Zerto

 

### Upgrading your on-prem Zerto Environment

To upgrade your on-prem Zerto environment ensure that ports 9071 and 9081 are open. Older versions of Zerto use 9081 and newer ones use 9071 so both will need to be open. Once you have completed this step follow the instructions in this [document](https://s3.amazonaws.com/zertodownload_docs/4.5U4/Upgrading%20Zerto%20Virtual%20Replication.pdf). If you are having trouble completing any of these steps, please reach out to OVHcloud customer support or your account manager for assistance.
