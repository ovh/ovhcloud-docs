# Distributed Firewall Management in NSX

The distributed firewall feature in NSX allows filtering with all elements in your VMware cluster that are on Overlay or VLAN segments. It should be used normally on east-west connections (ovh-T1-gw), but it also works with elements of the VMware cluster that are connected on the north-south gateway (ovh-T0-gw). Filtering applies from the source (VM, segment, network, etc.).

To simplify the administration of NSX, it is possible to place tags on your elements (segments, virtual machines, roles, etc..) and create groups that contain the objects associated with the tags or IP address ranges (this solution should not be preferred).

**Learn how to manage the distributed firewall by creating a rule that blocks traffic between a virtual machine and all virtual machines in another segment.**

## Requirements

- Being an administrative contact of your [Hosted Private Cloud infrastructure](https://us.ovhcloud.com/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/) to receive login credentials.
- A user account with access to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/#/hub).
- Having **NSX** deployed with two segments configured in your NSX configuration, you can use our guide on [Segment Management in NSX](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-segment-management) for more information.

## Instructions

We will isolate communication between a virtual machine and all virtual machines in a segment bi-directionally by performing these operations :

- Create two tags, one on a virtual machine and one on a segment.

- Create two associated groups, one containing the first tag and the other the second.

- Create a policy in the distributed firewall that will contain two rules:

  - A rule that will forbid traffic from the first group to the second.
  - Another rule that will forbid traffic from the second group to the first.

### Creating tags

In the NSX interface...

- Go to the `Networking` tab.
- Click `Segments` to the left in **Connectivity**.
- Click on the `three vertical dots` to the left of the segment you want to tag.
- Choose `Edit` from the menu.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 1](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-01.jpg)

 

To the right of **Tags**, enter `ovsegment` instead of tag and click `Add Item(s) ovsegment` below the input box.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 2](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-02.jpg)

 

Enter `ov1` instead of **Scope** and click `Add Item(s) ov1` below the input box.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 4](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-04.jpg)

 

Click the `+` button to the left of your tag.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 5](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-05.jpg)

 

The created tag is displayed in the bottom right of **Tags**, you can create more tags depending on your needs.

- Click `SAVE`.
- Click `CLOSE EDITING` to complete the markup for your segment.

 

Go to the `Inventory` tab and click `Virtual Machines` on the left in the inventory to view the list of virtual machines.

Then click on the `three vertical dots` to the left of the virtual machine that you want to tag and choose `Edit` from the menu.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 7](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-07.jpg)

 

Enter `vm` instead of **Tag** and click `Add Item(s) vm` below the input box.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 8](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-08.jpg)

 

Enter `ov2` instead of **Scope** and click `Add Item(s) ov2` below the input box.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 9](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-09.jpg)

 

Click the `+` button to the left of your tag.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 10](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-10.jpg)

 

The tag is created, click `SAVE` to save your changes.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 11](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-11.jpg)

 

Stay in the inventory and click `Tags` on the left to see the list of tags.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 12](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-12.jpg)

### Add groups that contain tags

In the inventory, go to `Groups` on the left and click `ADD GROUP` to create a group.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 13](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-13.jpg)

 

Type `g-segment01` below the **Name** column and click `Set` under the **Compute Members** column.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 14](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-14.jpg)

 

Leave `Generic` selected and click `+ ADD CRITERION`.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 15](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-15.jpg)

Choose these settings :

- **Type** : `NSX Segment`.
- **Tags** : Equals `ovsegment`.
- **Scope**: Equals `ov1`.

Click `APPLY`.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 16](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-16.jpg)

 

Click `SAVE`.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 17](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-17.jpg)

 

The group is created. Click `View Members` in the row of your group to display the members list.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 18](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-18.jpg)

 

Click `IP Addresses` to view the IP addresses that are used on your segment and which have been automatically added to your group.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 19](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-19.jpg)

 

Click `NSX Segments` to display the member segment of this group which has been automatically added from the criteria. You can click on `CLOSE` to close this window.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 20](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-20.jpg)

 

Click `ADD GROUP` to create a second group.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 21](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-21.jpg)

 

Type `g-vm` below the **Name** column and click `Set` under the **Compute Members** column.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 22](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-22.jpg)

 

Leave `Generic` selected and click `+ ADD CRITERION`.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 23](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-23.jpg)

Choose these settings :

- **Type** : `Virtual Machine`.
- **Tags** : Equals `vm`.
- **Scope**: Equals `ov2`.

Click on `APPLY`.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 24](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-24.jpg)

 

Click `SAVE`.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 25](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-25.jpg)

 

Click `View Members` in the row of your group to view the members.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 26](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-26.jpg)

 

In the **Virtual Machines** section, you can see the tagged virtual machine that has been automatically added.

Click `CLOSE` to close this window.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 27](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-27.jpg)

### Setting up a distributed firewall rule

We will now create a two-way blocking rule, on the distributed firewall, between the two created groups.

Go to the `Security` tab, select `Distributed Firewall` and click `+ ADD POLICY`.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 28](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-28.jpg)

 

Name your strategy `Isolate vm and segment`.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 29](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-29.jpg)

 

Click the `three vertical dots` to the left of your policy and choose `Add Rule` from the menu.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 30](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-30.jpg)

 

Click the `Pen` icon to the right of **Any** in the **Sources** column.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 31](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-31.jpg)

 

Stay on the `groups` tab, check the `g-segment01` group and click `APPLY`.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 32](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-32.jpg)

 

Click the `Pen` icon to the right of **Any** in the **Destinations** column.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 33](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-33.jpg)

 

Select the `g-vm` group and click `APPLY`.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 34](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-34.jpg)

 

Choose `Drop` to remove packages on this rule and click the `three vertical dots` to the left of your policy.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 35](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-35.jpg)

 

Click `Add Rule` in the menu.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 36](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-36.jpg)

 

Click the `Pen` icon to the right of **Any** in the **Sources** column.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 37](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-37.jpg)

 

Select the `g-vm` group and click `APPLY`.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 38](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-38.jpg)

 

Click the `Pen` icon to the right of **Any** in the **Destinations** column.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 39](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-39.jpg)

 

Select the `g-segment01` group and click `APPLY`.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 40](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-40.jpg)

 

Choose `Drop` to remove packages from this rule and click `publish` to validate the creation of the policy and its two associated rules.

![Distributed firewall management - NSX manage distributed firewall - step 41](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/nsx-manage-distributed-firewall/distfirewallmgmt-41.jpg)

Your rule is active, the traffic between the virtual machine member of the g-vm group and the segment member of the g-segment group is no longer possible.

 

## Conclusion

Having read this guide, you should have been able to manage a distributed firewall to isolate communication between a virtual machine and all virtual machines in a segment bi-directionally, including the creation of tags, associated groups, and a policy.
