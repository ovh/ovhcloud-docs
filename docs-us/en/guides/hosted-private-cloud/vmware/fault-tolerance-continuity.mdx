# VMware Fault Tolerance

The **Fault Tolerance** (FT) functionality of VMware’s vSphere allows you to protect a virtual machine from hardware failures by cloning the VM on two separate hosts.

![Fault tolerance - Fault tolerance continuity - step 1](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/fault-tolerance-continuity/fault-tolerance-1.gif)

 

## Requirements

- vSphere HA activated
- Resource reservation equal to 100% of memory
- VMware Tools installed
- Same generation processors
- 4 vCPUs maximum

 

## Instructions

To activate **Fault Tolerance**, right-click on a VM and click `Fault Tolerance` and `Turn On Fault Tolerance`.

![VM turn - Fault tolerance continuity](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/fault-tolerance-continuity/vm-turn.png)

A configuration window will open, prompting you to make choices for the secondary VM.

1. **Select datastores:** Select datastores to place the secondary VM disks and configuration files.
2. **Select host:&#x20;**&#x53;elect a host for the secondary VM.
3. **Ready to complete:** Review your selections and click `FINISH` to turn on fault tolerance.

:::info

Your virtual machine is now protected by **Fault Tolerance**. Its icon will change, and "primary" will be added to its name.

:::

Several operations are now available when you right-click your VM and choose `Fault Tolerance`.

![Fault tolerance - Fault tolerance continuity - step 7](/images/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/fault-tolerance-continuity/fault-tolerance-7.png)
