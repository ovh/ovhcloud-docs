# How to Manage Licenses for Nutanix

**Find out how to activate or deactivate your licenses in the BYOL offer.**

**NOTE:** Within the Nutanix on OVHcloud portfolio, OVHcloud offers the BYOL (Bring Your Own License) solution, which means that you, as an OVHcloud customer, must provide the Nutanix Cloud Platform licenses on your cluster.\
\
When the Nutanix cluster is ordered within the BYOL offer, the cluster is delivered with a 90-day trial license. As a customer, you must register your Nutanix Cloud Platform licenses on your cluster during this period.\
\
If you want to redeploy a cluster with the BYOL offer, you must first uninstall the licenses on the cluster before launching a redeployment. This guide explains how to add or remove licenses.

## Requirements

- Access to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/#/hub)
- You must have your Nutanix login details on [Nutanix Official Site](https://www.nutanix.com/).
- You must have valid licenses with your Nutanix NIC handle.

## Instructions

There are three types of licenses:

- **Prism Element**
- **Prism Central**
- **Add-ons**

You can install all licenses from **Prism Central**.

### Installation of licenses

#### Retrieving your configuration summary

We will retrieve the **Cluster Summary File (CSF)**, which contains the cluster configuration summary.

In **Prism Central**, click the `gear` icon to go to the settings.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 1](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-01.png)

 

Click `Licensing` on the left.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 2](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-02.png)

 

Click `Update Licenses` on the left.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 3](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-03.png)

 

Click `Download` to retrieve the file containing your cluster’s information.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 4](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-04.png)

 

Click on `Show all downloads` in the top right.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 5](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-05.png)

 

The **CSF** file is in your downloads folder.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 6](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-06.png)

####  

#### Logging in to the Nutanix portal

In **Prism Central**, go to the licenses page and click the `Licenses page` link.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 7](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-07.png)

 

The web browser launches and offers you to connect to the Nutanix portal.

Enter your credentials and click `Log In`.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 8](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-08.png)

####  

#### Create the license for your cluster

In the **Nutanix** Control Panel, click `Manage Licenses`.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 9](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-09.png)

 

Open the **CSF** file generated by **Prism Central** that starts with `ntnx_csf...`.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 10](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-10.png)

 

Scroll through the window to add your cluster licenses.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 11](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-11.png)

 

In the **NCM Ultimate** section, click `Select Licenses`.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 12](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-12.png)

 

Now save on your computer the **License Summary File (LSF)**, which contains the license.

Select your `cluster` and click `Save`.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 13](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-13.png)

 

Click `Next`.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 14](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-14.png)

 

Click `Confirm`.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 15](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-15.png)

 

Click `Save` to save the license file to your computer.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 16](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-16.png)

 

In the license management section of **Prism Central**, click `Upload` to install the license file.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 17](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-17.png)

 

Select the generated file and click `Open`.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 18](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-18.png)

 

Click `Apply License` to complete installation on your cluster.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 19](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-19.png)

 

### Uninstalling licenses

It is sometimes necessary to uninstall licenses to reinstall them on another cluster. If the cluster that contains the licenses is still available you must release your licenses from **Prism Central**.

In **Prism Central** click on the `gear` in the top right corner to access the parameters.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 20](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-20.png)

 

Select `Licensing` to the left and click `Update Licenses`.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 21](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-21.png)

 

Click `Download`.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 22](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-22.png)

 

Click `Save` to save the **CSF** file containing the summary of your cluster configuration with the installed licenses.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 23](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-23.png)

 

Click `Licenses page` to connect to your **Nutanix** license portal.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 24](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-24.png)

 

In the **Nutanix** license portal, click `Manage Licenses`.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 25](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-25.png)

 

Click `Upload File`.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 26](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-26.png)

 

Click `Open`.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 27](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-27.png)

 

Use the scroll bar to reach the bottom of the window.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 28](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-28.png)

 

Click `Unselect License` in the **NCM section**.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 29](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-29.png)

 

Click `Unlicense`.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 30](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-30.png)

 

A confirmation message will appear, click `Next`.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 31](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-31.png)

 

Click `Confirm`.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 32](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-32.png)

 

Click `Save` to save the **LSF** file containing the updated license to your computer.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 33](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-33.png)

 

Return to **Prism Central** license management.

Click `Upload`.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 34](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-34.png)

 

Click `Open` after selecting the generated file from the **Nutanix** portal.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 35](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-35.png)

 

Click `Apply License`.

![Manage licenses Nutanix - step 19](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/manage-licenses-nutanix/manage-license-19.png)

 

The license has been deleted.
