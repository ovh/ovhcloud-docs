## Requirements

- A Nutanix cluster in your OVHcloud account
- Access to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/)
- You must be connected to the cluster via Prism Central
- A connection to an environment other than Nutanix

 

## Nutanix Move Overview

**Nutanix Move** is a tool that allows virtual machine migrations from **VMware ESXI**, **Hyper-V**, **Azure**, and **AWS** to **AHV**.

It can also be used to migrate from Nutanix **AHV** to **AWS**.

This software works on a virtual machine whose sources are available on the Nutanix website with a customer account.

For optimal use, we recommend installing **Nutanix Move** as close as possible to the destination.

The **Nutanix Move** software is the only software that communicates between the source and the destination.

:::warning

**Note:** It is strongly discouraged to use **Nutanix Move** with virtual machines on a Windows Server running **Active Directory** or **Microsoft Exchange**. It is best to migrate as recommended by Microsoft.\
\
For VMs that use databases in **Microsoft SQL**, it is best to stop the database service when completing a migration.\
\
Ensure that the original virtual machine is compatible with the destination Nutanix environment.

:::

 

## Instructions

We will look at how to migrate between a remote environment on Hyper-V to an OVHcloud Nutanix environment.

The source and the destination are on two interconnected private networks through an **IPSEC VPN**.

 

### Preparing the original virtual machines before migration

The virtual machine must be in the list of supported virtual machines in the compatibility matrix for the migration to work.

Log in to the [Nutanix Accounting Matrix](https://portal.nutanix.com/page/documents/compatibility-interoperability-matrix/guestos) site to verify the compatibility of the source machines with the destination environment on Nutanix.

 

#### Specificity of virtual machines on LINUX

Some virtual machines will not start correctly after migration via **Nutanix Move**.\
Check the compatibility list as it is likely that the SCSI driver is not supported and needs to be changed with the PCI driver.\
In this case, you will need to follow this procedure to [change the disk controller of a Virtual Machine](#changediskcontroller) after the virtual machine is migrated.

 

#### Specificity of the Microsoft environment

Microsoft does not provide the SCSI adapter and network card **Virtio** drivers. These drivers must be installed before migration. These drivers are available on the Nutanix website with a customer account.

:::warning

**Note:&#x20;**&#x4D;icrosoft Windows 2008 is no longer supported by Microsoft or Nutanix. However, you can install older **Virtio** drivers and attempt a migration.

:::

Login to the Nutanix website with a customer account to download the [VIRTIO drivers](https://portal.nutanix.com/page/downloads?product=ahv\&bit=VirtIO).

Enter your user name in `Email`, your password in `Password`, and click `Log In`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 1](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-01.png)

Click the `Download` link that suits your needs.

For our example, we will download the driver for **amd64**.

![Move to Nutanix - step 2](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-02.png)

From a Windows virtual machine in the Hyper-V environment, double-click `Nutanix-VirtIO-1.1.7-amd64`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 3](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-03.png)

Read the license terms and conditions, accept them by ticking the box, and then click `Install`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 4](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-04.png)

Click `Finish` to complete the installation without having to restart the virtual machine.

![Move to Nutanix - step 5](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-05.png)

### Installing Move on the Nutanix Cluster

#### Downloading and importing sources

Retrieve the file in **qcow2** format from this site: [Download link for Nutanix Move](https://portal.nutanix.com/page/downloads?product=move).

Sign in with an account registered with Nutanix.

![Move to Nutanix - step 6](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-06.png)

Click `Download` to the right of `Move QCOW2 file for AHV`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 7](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-07.png)

Import the downloaded image from **Nutanix Move** into the cluster. For more information on importing images, see our guide on [importing images](/guides/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/import-iso-images-nutanix-using-prism-central). 

 

#### Installing the **Move** virtual machine

Create a virtual machine from the Move image.

From **Prism Central**, open the main menu by clicking on the button in the top left-hand corner.

![Move to Nutanix - step 8](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-08.png)

Click `VMs` in the menu.

![Move to Nutanix - step 9](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-09.png)

Click `Create VM`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 10](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-10.png)

Type **Nutanix Move** in the `Name` field.

Choose the **2 vCPU**, **2 Cores**, and **8 GB** options in the `VM Properties` fields and click `Next`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 11](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-11.png)

Click `Attach Disk`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 12](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-12.png)

Choose the **Disk**, **Clone from Image**, and **move-4.3.0.qcow2** options and click `Save`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 13](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-13.png)

Click on `Attach to Subnet`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 14](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-14.png)

Choose the network in `Subnet`, make sure it is connected in `Network Connection State`, and click `Save`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 15](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-15.png)

Click `Next`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 16](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-16.png)

Leave the default time zone options and click `Next`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 17](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-17.png)

Click `Create VM` to complete the installation of the **Nutanix Move** virtual machine.

![Move to Nutanix - step 18](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-18.png)

Select the **Nutanix Move** virtual machine and click the `Actions` menu.

![Move to Nutanix - step 19](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-19.png)

Click `Power On` in the `Actions` menu to start the virtual machine.

![Move to Nutanix - step 20](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-20.png)

The virtual machine is started and an IP address is obtained if a **DHCP** server is operational on that network.

![Move to Nutanix - step 21](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-21.png)

This installation of **Nutanix Move** is done on a network that has a DHCP server, but it is possible to configure the virtual machine on a network without a **DHCP server**.

### Configuring **Nutanix Move**

#### Logging in to the web interface and creating environments for migration

Connect to the IP address of the **Nutanix Move** virtual machine through a web browser in HTTPS, as follows: `https://IPaddressNutanixMove/`.

Read the license conditions, accept them and click `Continue`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 22](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-22.png)

Click `OK` to continue.

![Move to Nutanix - step 23](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-23.png)

Select and confirm a password in the fields provided, then click `Set Password` to validate the creation of the Nutanix Move password.

![Move to Nutanix - step 24](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-24.png)

Type the password in `Password` and click `Log In` to log into the **Nutanix Move** web interface.

![Move to Nutanix - step 25](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-25.png)

Click `+ Add Environment`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 26](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-26.png)

Choose `Nutanix AOS`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 27](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-27.png)

Enter the following information:

- **PRISM-CENTRAL-DESTINATION** in the `Environment Name` field
- the private IP address of **Prism Central** in the `Nutanix Environment` field
- a **Prism Central** administration account in the `Username` field
- the account password in the `Password` field

Click `ADD` to add the **Prism Central** environment.

![Move to Nutanix - step 28](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-28.png)

Click `Add Environment`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 29](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-29.png)

Choose `Microsoft Hyper-V`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 30](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-30.png)

Enter the following information:

- **HyperV-SOURCE** in the `Environment Name` field
- the private IP address of the **Hyper-V** server in the `Hyper-V Server/Cluster` field
- a **Hyper-V** administration account in the `Username` field
- the password for this account in the `Password` field

Click `ADD` to add the **Hyper-V** environment.

![Move to Nutanix - step 31](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-31.png)

Both migration environments appear in the top left-hand corner.

![Move to Nutanix - step 32](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-32.png)

 

#### Creating a migration plan

We will create a migration plan from the two environments created previously.

The source will be the **Hyper-V** server, and the destination will be the **Nutanix** cluster.

Click `Create a Migration Plan`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 33](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-33.png)

Choose a name in the `Plan Name` field and click `Proceed`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 34](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-34.png)

Enter the following information:

- **HyperV-SOURCE** in the `Select a Source` field
- **PRISM-CENTRAL-DESTINATION** in the `Hyper-V Server/Cluster` field
- the CLUSTER name in the `Target Cluster` field
- the name of the destination container in the `Target Container` field

Then click `Next` to continue to the next step.

![Move to Nutanix - step 35](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-35.png)

Select the virtual machines you want to migrate from the **Hyper-V** environment to **AOS** by clicking on the `+` icon to the left of the VM.\
Click `Next` to continue creating the migration plan.

![Move to Nutanix - step 36](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-36.png)

Choose the network in `Target Network` and click `Next`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 37](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-37.png)

Click `Next` to confirm the migration.

![Move to Nutanix - step 38](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-38.png)

Click `Save` to save the migration plan without running it.

![Move to Nutanix - step 39](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-39.png)

####  

#### Launching the migration

After the migration plan is created, you can manually launch it.

Check the box next to the desired migration plan.

![Move to Nutanix - step 40](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-40.png)

On the `Actions` menu, click `Start`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 41](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-41.png)

The migration is in progress. The `Status` column gives you information on the status of the migration.

![Move to Nutanix - step 42](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-42.png)

####  

#### Finalizing the migration

Check the status of the migration by hovering over the `In Progress` status.\
A window with the information **Ready to Cutover N** should appear, listing the number of VMs that can be migrated.

Click `In Progress` to start the completion process.

![Move to Nutanix - step 43](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-43.png)

Select all the VMs by ticking the `VM Name` box then click `Cutover`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 44](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-44.png)

Click `Continue`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 45](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-45.png)

Wait a few moments while it completes.

![Move to Nutanix - step 46](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-46.png)

The migration is complete when **Completed** appears in the `Migration Status` column.

![Move to Nutanix - step 47](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-47.png)

Log into **Prism Central** to view the three migrated VMs that are present and running.

![Move to Nutanix - step 48](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-48.png)

#### Changing the Disk Controller on a Linux Virtual Machine

If the virtual machine does not start properly after the migration, it may be due to a bad disk controller in the virtual machine configuration. In this case, it should be changed by following this procedure:

Go to the main menu of **Prism Central** and click on `VMs`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 49](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-49.png)

Make sure the virtual machine is turned off.

![Move to Nutanix - step 50](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-50.png)

Return to the main menu and click `Images`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 51](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-51.png)

Click `Add Image`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 52](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-52.png)

Select **VM Disk** as the `Image Source`. Type a name in `Search by VM Name` and click `+`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 53](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-53.png)

Type a name in the `Image Name` field and click `Next`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 54](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-54.png)

Select `Place image on source VM's cluster` and click `Save`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 55](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-55.png)

The image created from the virtual machine appears after a few moments.

![Move to Nutanix - step 56](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-56.png)

Open the main menu and click `VMs`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 57](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-57.png)

Check the box to the left of the virtual machine.

![Move to Nutanix - step 58](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-58.png)

Open the `Actions` menu and choose `Update`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 59](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-59.png)

Click on the `trash can` icon next to the CDROM drive.

![Move to Nutanix - step 60](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-60.png)

Click on the `trash can` icon next to the disk drive.

![Move to Nutanix - step 61](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-61.png)

Click `Attach Disk`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 62](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-62.png)

Select:

- **Disk** In `Type`
- Select **Clone from image**
- Enter the **name of the image created** in `Image`
- Select PCI as the `Bus type` and click `Save`

![Move to Nutanix - step 63](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-63.png)

Click `Next`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 64](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-64.png)

Click `Next`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 65](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-65.png)

Click `Save`.

![Move to Nutanix - step 66](/images/hosted-private-cloud/nutanix-on-ovhcloud/move-to-nutanix/nutanix-move-66.png)

The disk controller has been modified and the virtual machine boots properly.
