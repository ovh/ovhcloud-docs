# Pricing and management of OVHcloud NSX Edges

## Requirements

- Access to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/) to manage your NSX Edges.
- Use of **VMware NSX 4.1.1** to access customization options.
- Get a basic understanding of VMware concepts and NSX Edge features.

 

## General Overview

### Default Configuration

When creating a VMware environment, OVHcloud automatically provides:

- **2 Medium NSX Edges**:

  - 4 vCPU
  - 8 GB RAM

This default configuration meets most standard requirements for connectivity and network security.

 

### Customization

You can adapt your infrastructure to meet your specific needs:

1. **NSX Edge Sizes**:

   - Medium: 4 vCPU, 8 GB RAM.
   - Large: 8 vCPU, 32 GB RAM.
   - XL: 16 vCPU, 64 GB RAM.

2. **Number of NSX Edges**:

   - Minimum: 2 (default configuration).
   - Maximum: Up to **10 NSX Edges per cluster**.

 

### Limitations

- **1 NSX Edge cluster per vDC**.

- **10 NSX Edges maximum per cluster** (Broadcom/VMware limitation).

- NSX version 4.0.1 does not support:

  - Ordering new vDCs under NSX 4.1.1.
  - Modifying or adding NSX Edges.

 

## Instructions

Log in to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/). Navigate to the `NSX Edge Nodes` tab by clicking `Hosted Private Cloud` > `Managed VMware vSphere` > your PCC > the `Datacenters` tab > your datacenter.

 

### Order new NSX Edges

Click on `Add an NSX Edge`.

![VMware\_NSXedgeNodes\_addNode.png](/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/pricing-and-management-of-ovhcloud-nsx-edges/44815915994643.png)

Then, select the desired size and click `Order`.

![VMware\_NSXedgeNodes\_orderNode.png](/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/pricing-and-management-of-ovhcloud-nsx-edges/44815922625683.png)

All Edges must be the same size. When adding a new Edge, it will automatically match the size of the existing Edges.

 

### Modify the size of existing NSX Edges

Click on `Resize`.

![VMware\_NSXedges\_resize.png](/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/pricing-and-management-of-ovhcloud-nsx-edges/44815916002963.png)

Select the Edge you want to modify.

Choose a new size (Medium, Large, or XL) and apply the changes by clicking on `Confirm`.

:::info

In this example, we are resizing from **Extra large** to **Large**.

:::

![VMware\_NSXedges\_orderResize.png](/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/pricing-and-management-of-ovhcloud-nsx-edges/44815922630035.png)

:::warning

**NOTE:** This process can take quite some time. During the resizing, you will see a mix of node sizes until the process is complete.

:::

 

### Remove unused NSX Edges

At this time, it is not possible to remove NSX Edges from the OVHcloud Control Panel. Please contact our support team for assistance.
