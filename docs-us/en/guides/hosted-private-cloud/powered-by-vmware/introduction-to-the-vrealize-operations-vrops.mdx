# Introduction to the vRealize Operations - vROps

**Learn about the vRealize Operations (also called VMware Aria Operations) interface.**

vRealize Operations is an operations management tool that monitors physical, virtual, and cloud infrastructures and allows automation of maintenance tasks.

 

## Requirements

- be the administrative contact of your [Hosted Private Cloud infrastructure](https://us.ovhcloud.com/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/) to receive login credentials
- have a user account with access to vSphere and vROps (created in the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/))

 

## Instructions

### Interface access

To access vROPS, type in your hosted private cloud address in a browser.\
Click the `vRops` icon.

![HPC\_VMware\_vROps\_intro01\*\*.png](/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/introduction-to-the-vrealize-operations-vrops/36904752204051.png)

Use your vSphere login or the vROps local user that you own.

![HPC\_VMware\_vROps\_intro02\*.png](/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/introduction-to-the-vrealize-operations-vrops/36904728419859.png)

You are now on the vROps Home page with access to all its functionalities.

![HPC\_VMware\_vROps\_intro03.png](/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/introduction-to-the-vrealize-operations-vrops/36904752210323.png)

 

### Data Sources

The `Data Sources` section lists the different spaces monitored by vROps. Your OVHcloud infrastructure is added by default.

![HPC\_VMware\_vROps\_intro04\*.png](/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/introduction-to-the-vrealize-operations-vrops/36904728425875.png)

 

### Environments

The `Environments` section inventories your infrastructure and gives you access to monitoring all your objects. Objects can be Hosts, Network devices, VMs, Applications, etc.

![HPC\_VMware\_vROps\_intro05.png](/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/introduction-to-the-vrealize-operations-vrops/36904752220179.png)

 

### Visualize

The `Visualize` section holds dashboards and reports. Many premade dashboards will give you quick views of your Infrastructure's health.

You can still `+ Create` your own Dashboard to get a more specific view that will cater to your specific needs.

![HPC\_VMware\_vROps\_intro06\*.png](/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/introduction-to-the-vrealize-operations-vrops/36904752222995.png)

You can choose a widget to be displayed.

![HPC\_VMware\_vROps\_intro07.png](/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/introduction-to-the-vrealize-operations-vrops/36904728439699.png)

 

### Troubleshoot

The `Troubleshoot` section is your alert and logs repository. There, you will be able to see and dig into the problems you're experiencing. Contextual links to documentation and fixes are also provided.

![HPC\_VMware\_vROps\_intro08.png](/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/introduction-to-the-vrealize-operations-vrops/36904752230547.png)

 

### Optimize

The `Optimize` section gives you insights and recommendations on the capacity of your infrastructure. The monitoring analysis performed by vROps displays usage and right-sizing info to enable you to scale your environment.

![HPC\_VMware\_vROps\_intro09.png](/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/introduction-to-the-vrealize-operations-vrops/36904752241043.png)

 

### Plan

The `Plan` section gives you trend and capacity management tools so you can future-proof your infrastructure.\
Cost analysis and planning are also helpful for budgeting purposes.

![HPC\_VMware\_vROps\_intro10.png](/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/introduction-to-the-vrealize-operations-vrops/36904728447635.png)

 

### Configure

The `Configure` section defines your metrics and threshold reactions through policies and alerts.\
You will be able to set up groups, profiles, policies and/or alerts, and appropriate responses to tailor your infrastructure needs.

![HPC\_VMware\_vROps\_intro11.png](/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/introduction-to-the-vrealize-operations-vrops/36904728451091.png)

 

### Automation Central

The `Automation Central` section schedules one-time or scheduled maintenance jobs.\
Those jobs can be assigned to single or multiple objects over multiple spaces.

![HPC\_VMware\_vROps\_intro12.png](/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/introduction-to-the-vrealize-operations-vrops/36904728453651.png)

 

### Administration

The `Administration` section pilots vROps access and data collection.\
This is where you can create specific vROps accounts to be used.

![HPC\_VMware\_vROps\_intro13.png](/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/introduction-to-the-vrealize-operations-vrops/36904752248595.png)
