# Associating an email with a vSphere user

You can associate a name, first name, phone number, and email address with a vSphere user in your Private Cloud. The email address allows validation by token.

 

## Requirements

- Access to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://manager.us.ovhcloud.com/manager/)
- A [VMware on OVHcloud](https://us.ovhcloud.com/hosted-private-cloud/vmware/) service in your account
- An active vSphere user (see [this guide](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/18004084382355))

**Navigation**

- Access your [Managed VMware vSphere services list](https://manager.us.ovhcloud.com/#/dedicated/dedicated_cloud/) directly, or
- Click `Hosted Private Cloud` > `Managed VMware vSphere` > `your PCC` > `Users` tab

 

## Instructions

From the `Users` tab, click the *more options* `...` button to the right of the user concerned and choose `Edit`.

![VMware\_usersTab\_moreOptions.PNG](/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/associating-an-email-with-a-vsphere-user/49454076469139.png)

In the following window, you can set your name, first name, phone number, and email address.

This window also allows you to add editing rights to **IP**, **Additional IP**, **NSX Interface**, and the **Token validator** used to approve certain sensitive actions on infrastructures that have the **Advanced Security** option enabled.

Click on `Confirm` to validate the modifications.

![VMware\_usersTab\_edit-user.PNG](/images/hosted-private-cloud/powered-by-vmware/associating-an-email-with-a-vsphere-user/49454061013011.png)
