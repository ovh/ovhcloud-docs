# Public VCF as-a-Service - Logging in to your organization

## Requirements

- A web browser
- The welcome email for your new service

 

## Instructions

### First login

Open your web browser and go to the Public VCF as-a-Service login page. Enter your organization name (provided in the welcome email).

![VCD\_login\_organization.PNG](/images/hosted-private-cloud/cloud-platform/public-vcf-as-a-service-logging-in-to-your-organization/48542975006227.png)

You will now be prompted to enter your username and password in the appropriate fields (this information is provided in the welcome email).

Click the `Log in` button to access Public VCF as-a-Service.

![HPC\_vCD\_loggingIn02.png](/images/hosted-private-cloud/cloud-platform/public-vcf-as-a-service-logging-in-to-your-organization/28539049102355.png)

You will be redirected to your organization’s dashboard, which we will describe in our [user interface guide](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/28330641929491).

![VCD\_homePage.PNG](/images/hosted-private-cloud/cloud-platform/public-vcf-as-a-service-logging-in-to-your-organization/28539049112339.png)

 

### Change password

Once logged in, click your name in the upper-right corner of the user interface and choose the `Change Password` option from the drop-down menu.

![HPC\_vCD\_loggingIn05.png](/images/hosted-private-cloud/cloud-platform/public-vcf-as-a-service-logging-in-to-your-organization/28539064120979.png)

You will be prompted to enter your old password and your new password.

Fill in the fields and click `SAVE`.

When you change your password, you should receive a confirmation email.

![HPC\_vCD\_loggingIn06.png](/images/hosted-private-cloud/cloud-platform/public-vcf-as-a-service-logging-in-to-your-organization/28539064129299.png)
