# Canceling a VPS

**WARNING:** Any additional IPs or licenses attached to your server must be deleted separately. Not doing so will incur the continued cost of using these services, including parking fees for the additional IPs.

 

 

## Requirements

- Access to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/)
- A [VPS](https://us.ovhcloud.com/vps/) service in your account

 

## Topics

- [Canceling a commitment](#canceling-a-commitment)
  - [Undoing a commitment cancellation](#undoing-a-commitment-cancellation)
- [Canceling a service](#canceling-a-service)

 

## Instructions

### Canceling a commitment

You can cancel the commitment on a VPS from the OVHcloud Control Panel. Navigate to the `VPS` section via the `Bare Metal Cloud` menu and select your service from the list.

In the `Home` tab, look at the **My offer** panel. Click `Cancel commitment →`.

![manager\_vps\_cancelCommitement.png](/images/bare-metal-cloud/virtual-private-servers/canceling-a-vps/42208037604627.png)

On the next screen, you can choose a cancellation method.

:::warning

**NOTE:** The chosen method will take effect **after** your current commitment ends.

:::

- `Cancel your commitment and end your service on the expiry date.`
- `Cancel your current commitment and switch to a commitment-free subscription.`

After making your choice, click `Yes, cancel`.

![manager\_vps\_cancelCommitment.png](/images/bare-metal-cloud/virtual-private-servers/canceling-a-vps/42208037609491.png)

 

#### Undoing a commitment cancellation

If you change your mind about your cancellation, you can undo the action from the **My offer** panel.

Click `Undo cancellation →` and confirm the action in the pop-up.

![manager\_vps\_undoCancellation.png](/images/bare-metal-cloud/virtual-private-servers/canceling-a-vps/42208037612179.png)

 

### Canceling a service

You can cancel your VPS service from the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/). Navigate to the `VPS` section via the `Bare Metal Cloud` menu and select your service from the list.

On the `Home` tab, look at the **My offer** panel and click `Cancel →`.

![manager\_vps\_yourVPS\_cancel.png](/images/bare-metal-cloud/virtual-private-servers/canceling-a-vps/42208037614227.png)

In the pop-up window, you can choose a cancellation method.

- `Cancel service on its expiry date`
- `Cancel service immediately`

After making your choice, click `Confirm`.

![manager\_vps\_cancelMethod.png](/images/bare-metal-cloud/virtual-private-servers/canceling-a-vps/42208001052947.png)
