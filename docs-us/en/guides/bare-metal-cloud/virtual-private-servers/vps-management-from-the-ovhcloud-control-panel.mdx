# VPS - Management from the OVHcloud Control Panel

## Requirements

- An active [VPS](https://us.ovhcloud.com/vps/) offer in your OVHcloud Control Panel
- Access to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://manager.us.ovhcloud.com/%20)

Some VPS features mentioned on this page are not available in OVHcloud Local Zones.

Please visit our [VPS page](https://us.ovhcloud.com/vps/#options) for more information.

 

## Instructions

:::warning

**NOTE:** Not all VPS features mentioned on this page are available in OVHcloud Local Zones. Please visit our [Local Zones page](https://us.ovhcloud.com/vps/vps-amd/) for more information.

:::

Log in to your [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://manager.us.ovhcloud.com/%20), go to the `Bare Metal Cloud` section, and select your server under the `Virtual Private Servers` section.

This guide helps you **understand the VPS management interface in the OVHcloud Control Panel**, identify essential information, and use the main available actions (reinstallation, reboot, backup, configuration).

**Content overview**

- [Dashboard](#dashboard)
- [Reboot your VPS](#reboot-your-vps)
- [Reinstall your VPS](#reinstall-your-vps)

 

### Dashboard

The `Home` tab is the **main dashboard** of your VPS.

It centralizes **key information about the service** and provides access to **essential management actions**.

![vps\_dashboard\_numberedPanels.png](/images/bare-metal-cloud/virtual-private-servers/vps-management-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/48813821158547.png)

:::tip

✔ **Use the tabs below to explore each panel available in the VPS dashboard.**

:::

<Tabs>

<Tab label="1 - Your VPS">

**Name**

To customize the name of your VPS, click on the *more options* `...` button and then select `Change the name`. This feature is useful for easier Control Panel navigation when managing multiple VPS services. However, the internal name of the service remains in the format *vps-XXXXX.vps.ovh.us*.

 

**Boot**

The boot mode indicated is either:

- **Normal mode** (*LOCAL*), where the server loads the installed operating system.
- **Rescue mode**, provided by OVHcloud for troubleshooting.

Use the *more options* `...` button to [reboot the VPS](#restarting-your-vps) or boot it into rescue mode if necesary.

You can find further information in our [rescue mode guide](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010553920).

 

**OS/Distribution**

This is the operating system currently installed. Use the *more options* `...` button to [reinstall the same OS or select a different one from the available options](#reinstalling-your-vps).

:::warning

**NOTE:** A reinstallation will erase all data currently hosted on the VPS (except for additional disks).

:::

:::info

If you have ordered a **Windows** VPS, you can only choose a Windows OS for reinstallation. Correspondingly, if Windows was not selected in the order process, it cannot be installed after the VPS is delivered.

:::

Once an operating system is installed, you assume the responsibility to implement security updates. You can find further information [below](#reinstalling-your-vps) and in our guide on [how to secure a VPS](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/4412351365139).

 

**Zone & Location**

These sections provide information on the location of your VPS. This might be useful, for example, to identify potential impacts on your service mentioned in [incident and maintenance reports](https://status.us.ovhcloud.com/).

</Tab>

<Tab label="2 - Your configuration">

**Model**

This item shows the commercial reference which identifies the VPS model corresponding to the [VPS offers on our website](https://us.ovhcloud.com/vps/).

 

**vCores / Memory / Storage**

The current resources of your VPS are displayed here and can be upgraded separately by clicking on the corresponding link. Note that upgrades are limited by the chosen VPS model and may only be available by moving to a [higher range](https://us.ovhcloud.com/vps/).

To learn more about upgrading your configuration, see [this guide](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/38299056076947).

 

**Additional disks**

Add additional disks to your VPS to increase your server’s storage capacity beyond that included in the initial configuration. For example, you can store backup data.

</Tab>

<Tab label="3 - IP">

**IPv4**

The primary public IPv4 address of the VPS is configured automatically at installation. Find out more information about IP management in our guide on [how to configure IP aliasing](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014248820).

 

**IPv6 / Gateway**

Here you will find the public IPv6 address and the associated gateway address. These are automatically attached to the VPS during installation. See our guide on [how to configure IPv6](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406638994067) for more information.

 

**Secondary DNS**

This feature is useful for hosting DNS services. Our guide on [how to configure OVHcloud secondary DNS on a VPS](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010663980) describes it in detail.

</Tab>

<Tab label="4 - Backup">

These options refer to additional VPS services for backing up and restoring your system. To learn more about adding additional disks, see our [product page](https://us.ovhcloud.com/vps/options/) and [this guide](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/4410563713427).

**Snapshot**

A snapshot on a VPS is an instant backup of the server’s state, allowing you to quickly restore the system in the event of a problem. The `Snapshot` option allows you to create a manual snapshot as a singular restore point.

To learn more about snapshots, [click here](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/360012573640).

 

**Automated backup**

A daily backup of the system (excluding additional disks) is created automatically and retained for 24 hours (only applicable to services purchased August 7, 2025, or later). By upgrading to the **Automatic Backup Premium** option, you will have the last 7 daily backups of your VPS, which you can use for mounting and restoring.

Compared to manual snapshots, this feature increases data security by creating several restore points at regular intervals.

To learn more about automated backups, [click here](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/360012678619).

</Tab>

<Tab label="5 - My offer">

These sections show the most important information regarding the billing of your service. If you wish to learn more, please see our other guides:

- [Overview of Billing with OVHcloud US](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002306224)
- [Managing commitments in the OVHcloud Control Panel](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406907355155)
- [Canceling a VPS](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/360013034180)

</Tab>

</Tabs>

 

### VPS function available in the "Home" tab

#### Rebooting your VPS

A reboot might become necessary to apply updated configurations or to fix an issue.

<Tabs>

<Tab label="Via the OVHcloud Control Panel">

However, you can force a "hard reboot" at any time in your [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/). In the `Home` tab, click the *more options* `...` button next to `Boot` in the **Your VPS** section. Select `Reboot my VPS`.

![vps\_getting started05\*.png](/images/bare-metal-cloud/virtual-private-servers/vps-management-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/48813821173267.png)

Click `Confirm` in the window that pops up.

![vps\_getting started06\*.png](/images/bare-metal-cloud/virtual-private-servers/vps-management-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/48813824129299.png)

</Tab>

<Tab label="Via CLI">

When possible, perform a "soft reboot" from the server's GUI (Windows, Plesk, etc.) or via the command line:

```bash
sudo reboot
```

</Tab>

<Tab label="Via OVHcloud API">

You can reboot your VPS via API with the following call:

<Api version="v1" section="/vps" method="POST" route={"/vps/\{serviceName\}/reboot"} regions={["us"]} />

</Tab>

</Tabs>

 

#### Reinstalling your VPS

<Tabs>

<Tab label="Via the OVHcloud Control Panel">

You can carry out reinstallations in your Control Panel. Click the *more options* `...` button to the right of **OS/Distribution**, then `Reinstall my VPS`.

![vps\_getting started03\*.png](/images/bare-metal-cloud/virtual-private-servers/vps-management-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/48813821162643.png)

In the pop-up window, choose an operating system from the drop-down list. The options represent [images compatible with an OVHcloud VPS](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/22141356251027) and are immediately functional after installation.

If you have selected a compatible operating system, you can provide a **public key** to be installed automatically. You have two options:

- Manually copy the key string and paste it into the field `Your Public SSH key`.
- [If you have previously stored](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/38811202776467) a public key in your [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/), select the desired key from the drop-down menu `SSH key to pre-install`.

![vps\_getting started04\*\*.png](/images/bare-metal-cloud/virtual-private-servers/vps-management-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/48813821166355.png)

For more information on this topic, consult our guides:

- [How to create and use authentication keys for SSH connections](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/43353277246995)
- [How to use PuTTY for SSH connections and authentication](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/43358097343891)

If you have selected an SSH key and you do not need a password to log in, activate the option `I do not wish to receive my VPS authentication codes by email`.

:::warning

**NOTE:** The reinstallation will overwrite all data on the server. We recommend taking a snapshot of your VPS before you continue so that you can return to the previous state if you encounter any issues.

:::

:::info

**Licenses**

Some proprietary operating systems or platforms such as Plesk or cPanel require licenses that generate additional fees. Licenses can be managed from the OVHcloud Control Panel: Go to the `Bare Metal Cloud` section, then click on `Licenses` in the left-hand navigation.

To have a **Windows** operating system running on a VPS, it has to be **selected in the order process**. A VPS with another OS installed cannot be reinstalled with Windows in the way described above.

:::

The reinstallation process may take a few minutes.

</Tab>

<Tab label="Via the OVHcloud API">

From the [OVHcloud API console](https://api.us.ovhcloud.com/):

1. Get the service name(s) of your VPS service(s).

   <Api version="v1" section="/vps" method="GET" route={"/vps"} regions={["us"]} />

2) Find the IDs of available images for your service.

   <Api version="v1" section="/vps" method="GET" route={"/vps/\{serviceName\}/images/available"} regions={["us"]} />

3. Find the OS name associated to the image ID.

   <Api version="v1" section="/vps" method="GET" route={"/vps/\{serviceName\}/images/available/\{id\}"} regions={["us"]} />

4) Reinstall your VPS with the new OS distribution.

   <Api version="v1" section="/vps" method="POST" route={"/vps/\{serviceName\}/rebuild"} regions={["us"]} />

   :::info

   Only the **imageId** is required in the Request Body.

   ![vps\_getting started16 API.png](/images/bare-metal-cloud/virtual-private-servers/vps-management-from-the-ovhcloud-control-panel/48813821170707.png)

   :::

The reinstallation process may take a few minutes.

</Tab>

</Tabs>
