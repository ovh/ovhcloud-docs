# Backup Agent - Connect to VSPC

## Requirements

- You must have received VSPC connection credentials by email after ordering your Backup Agent service.
- A compatible web browser.

 

## Instructions

### Access VSPC

:::info

This account is read-only and allows you to access visualizations of your backups and agents.

:::

Go to the VSPC URL: `https://vspc.prod01.us-east-vin.backup.us.ovhcloud.com`.

 

### Log in

Log in using the credentials that were provided to you by email. The login format is usually `vspc-tenant-XXXXXX\user-XXXXXX`.

:::warning

**NOTE:** If you have lost your password, use the `Forgot password?` link on the login page to reset it. If you have lost your username, please [contact our customer support team](/guides/account/account-information/contact-us-support).

:::

 

### View Backup Jobs

Once logged in, click on `Backup Jobs` in the left menu.

![Backup jobs - Vspc presentation](/images/bare-metal-cloud/dedicated-servers/vspc-presentation/backup-jobs.png)

 

#### View successful jobs

Click on `Successful Jobs` for your tenant.

![Backup jobs successful](/images/bare-metal-cloud/dedicated-servers/vspc-presentation/backup-jobs-successful.png)

 

#### View restore points

You can view the available restore points for your backups.

![Backup jobs restore points](/images/bare-metal-cloud/dedicated-servers/vspc-presentation/backup-jobs-restore-points.png)

 

### Access managed agents

To see the list of your installed agents, go to `Managed Computers`.

![Managed computers - Vspc presentation](/images/bare-metal-cloud/dedicated-servers/vspc-presentation/managed-computers.png)

 

### View reports

:::info

At this time, `Reports` are not available in the VPSC console.

:::

 

### View latest alarms

You can view the latest alarms on your agents and backups in the `Active Alarms` section.

![Alarms - Vspc presentation](/images/bare-metal-cloud/dedicated-servers/vspc-presentation/alarms.png)
