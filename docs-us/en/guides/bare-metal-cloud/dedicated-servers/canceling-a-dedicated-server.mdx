# Canceling a dedicated server

**WARNING:** Any additional IPs or licenses attached to your server will need to be deleted separately. Not doing so will incur the continued cost of using these services, including parking fees for the Additional IPs.

 

## Requirements

- Access to the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/)
- A [dedicated server](https://us.ovhcloud.com/bare-metal/) in your account

 

## Topics

  - [Undoing a commitment cancellation](#undoing-a-commitment-cancellation)

## Instructions

### Canceling a commitment

You can cancel the commitment on a dedicated server from the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/). Navigate to the `Dedicated servers` section via the `Bare Metal Cloud` menu and select your service from the list.

In the `General information` tab, look at the **My offer** panel. Click `Terminate my service`.

In the pop-up window, choose a cancellation method.

:::warning

**NOTE:** The chosen method will take effect **after** your current commitment ends.

:::

- `Cancel the service at the end of my subscription.`
- `Keep the service but without commitment.`

After making your choice, click `Yes, cancel`.

 

#### Undoing a commitment cancellation

If you change your mind about your cancellation, you can reverse the action from the **My offer** panel.

Click `Undo cancellation →` and confirm the action in the pop-up.

 

### Canceling a service

You can cancel your dedicated server service from the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/). Navigate to the `Dedicated servers` section in the Bare Metal Cloud menu, then select your service from the list.

On the `General information` tab, look at the **My offer** panel, and click  `Terminate my service`.

In the pop-up window, you can choose a cancellation method.

- `Cancel service now`: Choosing this will cancel your service immediately.

* `Cancel service`: Choosing this will cancel the renewal for your server. It will remain available in your account until the end of the current billing period.

After making your choice, click `Confirm`.
