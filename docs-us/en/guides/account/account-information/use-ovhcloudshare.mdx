# How to use OVHcloudShare

**Learn how to use OVHcloudShare, some use cases for it, and the different options for sharing files.**

OVHcloud is pleased to present [OVHcloudShare](https://files.us.ovhcloud.com), a tool used for file sharing that is available to all customers with an OVHcloud US account.

 

## Requirements

- An [OVHcloud account](/guides/account/account-information/create-account)

 

## Instructions

### Logging in

Use the following link to access the [OVHcloudShare](https://files.us.ovhcloud.com) webpage, enter your OVHcloud account credentials, and click `Login`.

![Account OVHcloud cloud share - step 1](/images/account/account-information/use-ovhcloudshare/account-ovh-cloud-share-01.png)

 

### Terms of service

Before using OVHcloudShare, you must accept the Terms of Use by scrolling down and clicking `I acknowledge that I have read and agree to the above terms of service`.

![Account OVHcloud cloud share - step 2](/images/account/account-information/use-ovhcloudshare/account-ovh-cloud-share-02.png)

 

### Uploading Files

To upload a file, click the `+` button at the top of the screen and select `Upload file` from the drop-down menu. You will be prompted to upload the file from your local machine. Once the file has been uploaded, it will appear on your dashboard.

:::warning

**NOTE:** Files uploaded to OVHcloudShare will be automatically deleted 30 days after they are first uploaded. Files are limited to 10 GB in size.

:::

![Account OVHcloud cloud share - step 3](/images/account/account-information/use-ovhcloudshare/account-ovh-cloud-share-03.png)

 

### Deleting Files

To delete a file, click the *more options* `...` button next to the file in question and select `Delete file` from the drop-down menu.

:::warning

**NOTE:** If you do not delete a file manually, it will automatically be deleted after 30 days.

:::

![Account OVHcloud cloud share - step 6](/images/account/account-information/use-ovhcloudshare/account-ovh-cloud-share-06.png)
