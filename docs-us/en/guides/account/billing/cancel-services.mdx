# How to cancel your OVHcloud services

**Learn how to cancel your OVHcloud services from the OVHcloud Control Panel.**

:::info

This guide follows the layout of menus in the **Beta** version of the OVHcloud Control Panel. To learn how to switch to the Beta version, see [this guide](/guides/account/account-information/control-panel-navigation).

:::

 

## Requirements

- Access to your account in the [OVHcloud Control Panel](https://us.ovhcloud.com/manager/)
- At least one of the services listed below

Expand each universe to see the individual services.
