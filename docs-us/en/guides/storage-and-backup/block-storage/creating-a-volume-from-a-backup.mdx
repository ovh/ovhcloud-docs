# Creating a volume from a backup

You can attach additional disks to your Public Cloud instances based on previously created volume snapshots. This may be useful in the following cases:

- If you want to restore additional disk data.
- If you want to have a highly available, high-performance storage space for your data.
- If you want to move your data to another instance.

 

## Requirements

- A [Public Cloud instance](https://us.ovhcloud.com/public-cloud/) in your OVHcloud account
- A [volume snapshot](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/16372779151507) in the same OpenStack region
- Administrative access (root) to your instance [via SSH or RDP](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002245164#step-4-connecting-to-your-instance)

#### OVHcloud Control Panel Access

- **Direct link:** [Public Cloud Projects](https://manager.us.ovhcloud.com/#/public-cloud/pci/projects)
- **Navigation path:** `Public Cloud` > Select your project

 

## Instructions

### Creating the disk from a backup

In the left-hand menu, under **Storage**, click `Volume Snapshot`.

In the row of the volume concerned, click the *more options* `...` button and select `Create a volume`.

![volumeSnapshot\_snapshotList\_moreOptions.PNG](/images/storage-and-backup/block-storage/creating-a-volume-from-a-backup/48036588132499.png)

Change the default **name** and **volume capacity** if you want, and click `Create the volume`.

It may take a few minutes to create the disk, depending on its size.

![volumeSnapshot\_createVolume.PNG](/images/storage-and-backup/block-storage/creating-a-volume-from-a-backup/48036593736979.png)

 

### Attaching the disk to an instance

Once the disk has been created, you will be redirected to the `Block Storage` area.

In the row of the volume concerned, click the *more options* `...` button and select `Attach to instance`.

![blockStorage\_attachSnapshot.PNG](/images/storage-and-backup/block-storage/creating-a-volume-from-a-backup/48036593743507.png)

In the pop-up window, select an instance and click `Confirm` to attach the disk to it.

Attaching the disk to your instance will begin, which may take a few minutes.

:::warning

**NOTE:** Make sure not to leave the current page in your OVHcloud Control Panel while the disk is being attached. This may interrupt the process.

:::

Once the attachment is complete, you can follow [these steps](https://support.us.ovhcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/20566582694291#configuring-the-new-disk) to configure the disk using Linux or Windows.
