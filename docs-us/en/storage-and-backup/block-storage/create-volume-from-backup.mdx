# Creating a volume from a volume snapshot

You can attach additional disks to your Public Cloud instances based on previously created volume snapshots. This may be useful in the following cases:

- If you want to restore additional disk data.
- If you want to have a highly available, high-performance storage space for your data.
- If you want to move your data to another instance.

 

## Requirements

- A [Public Cloud instance](https://us.ovhcloud.com/public-cloud/) in your OVHcloud account
- A [volume snapshot](/storage-and-backup/block-storage/create-volume-snapshot) in the same OpenStack region
- Administrative access (root) to your instance [via SSH or RDP](/public-cloud/compute/getting-started#step-5-connect-to-the-instance)

#### OVHcloud Console access

- **Direct link:** [Public Cloud projects](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/#/public-cloud/project)
- **Navigation path:** `Public Cloud` > `Projects` > Select your project > `Storage` > `Volume Snapshot`

 

## Instructions

### Creating the disk from a volume snapshot

In the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, click `Volume Snapshot` under **Storage**.

In the row of the snapshot you want to create a volume from, click the `⋮` button, then `Create a volume`.

{/* cp-shot: ![Snapshot list more options](/images/storage-and-backup/block-storage/create-volume-from-backup/snapshot-list-more-options.png) */}

Change the default volume name and capacity if you want, then click `Create volume`.

It may take a few minutes to create the disk, depending on its size.

{/* cp-shot: ![Snapshot create volume](/images/storage-and-backup/block-storage/create-volume-from-backup/snapshot-create-volume.png) */}

 

### Attaching the disk to an instance

Once the disk has been created, click `Block Storage` under **Storage** in the left-hand menu.

In the row of the volume of your choice, click the `⋮` button, then `Attach to instance`.

{/* cp-shot: ![Block storage attach snapshot](/images/storage-and-backup/block-storage/create-volume-from-backup/block-storage-attach-snapshot.png) */}

In the window that opens, select an instance and click `Confirm` to attach the disk to it.

Attaching the disk to your instance will begin, which may take a few minutes.

:::warning

**NOTE:** Make sure not to leave the current page in your OVHcloud Console while the disk is being attached. This may interrupt the process.

:::

Once the attachment is complete, you can follow [these steps](/storage-and-backup/block-storage/create-and-configure-an-additional-disk-on-an-instance#configuring-the-new-disk) to configure the disk using Linux or Windows.
