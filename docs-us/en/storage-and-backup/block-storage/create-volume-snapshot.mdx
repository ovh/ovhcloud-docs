# Creating a volume snapshot

A **volume snapshot** is a recovery point stored within the same storage cluster as the original volume. Operations will be fast, but in the event of an incident on the cluster, the volume and volume snapshot may become unavailable. Volume snapshot creation does not require the volume to be detached from the instance.

This is not to be confused with a **volume backup**, an image created out of your volume, which is stored in the Object Storage cluster where the original volume is located. The level of resiliency is ideal and will allow you to quickly react to any incident on your volume, creating another volume from the backup. Volume backup creation requires the volume to be detached from the instance. For more information on this option, please consult [this guide](/storage-and-backup/block-storage/volume-backup).

A snapshot of an additional volume serves two general purposes:

- You can create backups with a few clicks and keep them as long as needed.
- You can use the snapshot as a template for identical volumes.

 

## Requirements

- A [Block Storage volume](/storage-and-backup/block-storage/create-and-configure-an-additional-disk-on-an-instance) created in your [Public Cloud](https://us.ovhcloud.com/public-cloud/) project

#### OVHcloud Console access

- **Direct link:** [Public Cloud projects](https://console.us.ovhcloud.com/#/public-cloud/project)
- **Navigation path:** `Public Cloud` > `Projects` > Select your project > `Storage` > `Block Storage`

 

## Instructions

:::info

It is not required to detach the volume from its instance first. However, if you wish to detach your volume, please consult the **Detach a volume** section of [this guide](/storage-and-backup/block-storage/create-and-configure-an-additional-disk-on-an-instance#detach-a-volume).

:::

In the left-hand menu of your Public Cloud project, click `Block Storage` under **Storage**.

In the row of the volume concerned, click the `⋮` button and select `Create a snapshot`.

{/* cp-shot: ![Block storage service list more options](/images/storage-and-backup/block-storage/create-volume-snapshot/block-storage-service-list-more-options.png) */}

In the panel that opens, enter a name for the snapshot, then click `Create`.

{/* cp-shot: ![Block storage - Create volume snapshot](/images/storage-and-backup/block-storage/create-volume-snapshot/block-storage.png) */}

You can also click `Volume Snapshot` under **Storage** in the left-hand menu, then `Create a volume snapshot`: select the volume, enter the snapshot name and click `Create`.

The snapshot creation time may take several hours, depending on the amount of data present on the volume, the instance resource utilization at the time of the snapshot, and other host-specific factors.

We would also recommend performing snapshots outside of your production hours.

Other best practices include:

- Avoid creating snapshots during peak hours.
- Install the qemu-guest agent if it is not installed... or try to disable it if necessary.
- Try not to "solicit" the server too much during the snapshot creation phase (I/O limitation, RAM consumption, etc.).

Since a volume snapshot is a clone of the entire disk, it will have the maximum size of the original volume, regardless of the actual disk space allocation.

Your snapshots are listed on the `Volume Snapshot` page, under **Storage** in the left-hand menu. Once the snapshot is created, it is added to this table.

{/* cp-shot: ![Creation - Create volume snapshot](/images/storage-and-backup/block-storage/create-volume-snapshot/creation.png) */}

To delete your snapshot, click the `⋮` button and select `Delete`.

You can also `Create a volume` based on the respective snapshot. You can find further information in [this guide](/storage-and-backup/block-storage/create-volume-from-backup).

{/* cp-shot: ![List more options](/images/storage-and-backup/block-storage/create-volume-snapshot/list-more-options.png) */}
